GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 600 (9 May 46) GD

(SCAPIN-936)

APO 500

9 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Buildings, Facilities and Grounds of the Japanese Armed Forces.

1. Reference:

a. Memorandum General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers file AG 402.5 (24 Sep. 45) GD subject: “Materials, Supplies and Equipment Received and to be Received from the Japanese Armed Forces”, dated 24 September 1945.

b. General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers Directive No. 3 dated 22 September 1945.

c. Memorandum General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers file AG 004 (20 Jan. 46) ESS/GD Subject: “Custody, Control and Protective Maintenance of Japanese Aircraft Plants, Arsenals and Laboratories”, dated 20 January 1946.

d. Memorandum General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers file AG 686 (11 Oct. 45) GD subject: “In Regard to the Cultivation of Air fields other than those used by Allied Forces", dated 11 October 1945.

2. In addition to the materials, supplies, equipment and land areas being returned to the Imperial Japanese Government under the provisions of reference 1a and 1d above, buildings, facilities, grounds and land areas, research establishments and manufacturing plants (formerly the property of the Japanese Armed Forces) no longer required by Occupational Forces and not taken into the custody and control of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers under the provisions of reference 1c above may also be returned to your use. The property referred to above does not include that located in Korea.

3. The buildings, facilities, grounds, research establishments and manufacturing plants referred to above will be returned for the purpose of meeting essential civilian requirements under the provisions of reference 1b.

4. The Home Ministry of the Imperial Japanese Government will be made responsible for keeping complete records of property received. These records will be maintained in such form that any property such as machine tools, generators, furnaces, etc., which are moved from their present location may be traced to the ultimate receiver and location. All records will be made available on demand to authorized representtaives of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers or of the Occupational Force Commanders.

5. The provisions of this directive will not prejudice determination of title and ultimate disposition of any of the properties which may be directed by General Headquartars, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General
