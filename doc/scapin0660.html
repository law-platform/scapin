GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.31 (28 Jan 46) ESS/IE

(SCAPIN-660)

APO 500

28 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Violations of SCAP Directive Number 3. 22 September. 1945.

1. Reference is made to Par. 7. SCAP Directive Number 3, dated 22 September 1945:

“Imports and Exports. No imports to, or exports from, Japan of any goods, wares or merchandise will be permitted, except with the prior approval of this Headquarters.”

2. It has come to the attention of this Headquarters, through official channels, that violations of this order have been and are being committed. The violations have consisted principally of illicit importing (smuggling) of foodstuffs (principally rice) from Korea into Japan, especially to Kyushu and Western Honshu.

3. It is directed that all necessary steps be taken immediately by the Imperial Japanese Government to stop this illicit import trade.

4. It is directed that the Imperial Japanese Government seize and hold all smuggled goods, wares and merchandise.

5. It is further directed that all instances of this violation be reported to this Headquarters within forty eight hours. The report submitted will contain the following information:

a. Location and date of incident.

b. Agency making the arrest.

c. National identity of smuggler apprehended and registry of ship or vessels.

d. Quantities and types of goods, wares or merchandise impounded.

e. Nature of charges made against the apprehended criminals, the scheduled date and location of trials.

f. Any additional information which would be considered pertinent will be included.

6. Disposition of all property and commodities seized will be decided by this Headquarters. The Imperial Japanese Government will then be informed of the specific action to be taken.

7. You are directed to report the specific action taken by the Imperial Japanese Government to enforce this directive.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
