GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 725.1 (6 Sep 46) PH

(SCAPIN-1183)

APO 500

6 September 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Abolition of Licensed Prostitution in Japan.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government

from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers,

AG 726.7 (21 Jan 46) PH, (SCAPIN-642), 21 January 1946, subject: "Abolition

of Licensed Prostitution in Japan."

2. The Imperial Japanese Government has not complied with the provisions

of paragraph 3 of Memorandum referred to above.

3. Compliance in accordance with the terms of paragraph 3 of referenced

memorandum is hereby directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY
Colonel, AGD.,
Adjutant General.
