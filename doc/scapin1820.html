GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 353.1 (15 NOV 47) GC-TNG

(SCAPIN 1820)

APO 500

15 November 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Onohara Shima Bombing and Ground Gunnery Range

1. The islands known as Onohara Shima located at 34° 02' 30" north and 139° 23' east are designated as a bombing and ground gunnery range. Danger areas extend five miles north, five miles south, three miles east and three miles west, all from the center of the terget area.

2. Hours for use of the range will be from 0700 hours to 1700 hours, Item time, Mondays through Fridays. The inhabitants of the Izu islands and the coastal inhabitants from Mamamatsu to Choshi will be notified prior to any period during which the range will be used. When the range is used for periods in excess of fifteen (15) days, warning reminders will be disseminated to the inhabitants concerned. This information will be forwarded through Military Government units to local Japanese civil authorities.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant Ceneral.
