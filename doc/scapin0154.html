GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.9 (17 Oct 45) ESS

(SCAPIN - 154)

17 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Nishina Laboratory, Institute of Physical and Chemical Research

1. Transmitted herewith is a copy of a letter and its inclosure brought to this headquarters by Dr. Y. Nishina, requesting permission for the operation of a cyclotron in the Nishina Laboratory of the Institute of Physical and Chemical Research, Komagome, Tokyo, for investigations of radioactive substances and neutrons in the fields of biology, medicine, chemistry and metallurgy.

2. You are authorized to permit Dr. Nishina to operate his cyclotron, subject to the provisions of paragraph 8 of Directive No. 3, issued by this headquarters 22 September 1945. You are charged with the responsibility of insuring compliance with those provisions.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General
