SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 387. 6 (30 Jan 47) ESS/IN

(SCAPIN - 1499)

APO 500

30 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Inventory Instructions for Machine Tool and Ball and Roller Bearing Plants

1. References are the following memoranda for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, SCAP:

a. File AG 387. 6 (13 Aug 46) ESS/IN, (SCAPIN 1133), dated 13 August 1946, subject: Reparations Selections Within the Machine Tool Industry.

b. File AG 387. 6 (14 Aug 46) ESS/IN, (SCAPIN 1136), dated 14 August 1946, subject: Reparations Selections Within the Precision Bearing Industry.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is notified that certain items of equipment located in specified plants (Inclosures 1 and 2) will be recorded on an inventory form prepared by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

3. There will be made available to the Commanding General, Eighth Army, all personnel, equipment and supplies deemed necessary by him to accomplish the purpose of this memorandum.

4. Within seventy-two (72) hours of the receipt of this memorandum, the Imperial Japanese Government will have qualified representatives report to the Commanding General, Eighth Army, to receive detailed instructions to accomplish the purpose of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
/t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General

2 Incls: 1. List of Machine Tool Plants

2. List of Ball and Roller Bearing Plants

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN 1499)

JZR/NJM/JTN/mlt

10 Jan 47

1. When the original SCAPINS 1133 and 1136 were issued, no inventory instructions were included; it is now time for such instructions to be issued so that cataloging of the equipment in these categories can be accomplished.

2. The accompanying letter gives detailed instructions to Commanding General, Eighth Army, on how the inventory is to be accomplished.

3. The Memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government directs the Imperial Japanese Government to accomplish the inventory under the direction of Commanding General, Eighth Army.

4. Surveillance by Eighth Army required.

5. No concurrences necessary.

6. The Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government will be released on 6 February 1947.

J. Z. R. 2-2574
