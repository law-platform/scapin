GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 423 (29 Jan 46) ESS-IE

(SCAPIN-671)

APO 500

29 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Preparation of Raw Silk for Export.

1. It is directed that the following 1500 cases and 1100 bales of raw silk at Yokohama be prepared by the Imperial Japanese Government for export:

a. 1080 Cases Special AAA White 20/22 Denier.

b. 420 Cases AAA White 20/22 Denier.

c. 690 Bales AA White 13/15 Denier.

d. 410 Bales A White 13/15 Denier.

A list of Japanese Government Conditioning House certificate numbers covering the cases and bales described above is attached hereto.

2. It is directed that an official statement on the availability for shipment be delivered to this Headquarters not later than 7 February 1946. This statement shall be accompanied by the following documents in English:

a. Eight (8) copies of Japanese Government Conditioning House quality and conditioned weight certificates with bales or case marks and numbers indicated on each copy of certificate.

b. Eight (8) copies of packing lists with date, packing list number, bale or case marks and numbers, gross weight and measurements indicated.

c. A statement in eight (8) copies to indicate:

(1) Total number of bales of each grade and size.

(2) Estimated yen value per each ten bale-lot.

(3) Total conditioned weight in pounds of each lot by grade.

(4) Estimated yen value of total bales and total cases prepared for export.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General

1 Incl: List of Japanese Gov Conditioning House Cert Numbers.
