APO 500

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Access to Japanese Scientific and Technical Information in Japan

1. Reference is memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 000.91 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST, SCAPIN 1925, 6 August 1948, subject, same as above.

2. Paragraph 1 of the referenced memorandum is amended by adding "or non-Far Eastern Commission countries at war with Japan subsequent to 7 December 1941. ”

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.01 (23 Jul 48) ESS/ST

APO 500

16 February 1949

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to SCAPIN 1925/1, AG 000.91 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST, subject: Access to Japanese Scientific and Technical Information in Japan, dated 16 February 1949

1. Reference is Memorandum AG 000.91 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST, dated 6 August 1948, subject: Information of General Application Pertaining to SCAPIN 1925, AG 000.01 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST, subject: Access to Japanese Technical and Scientific Information in Japan, dated 6 August 1948.

2. In paragraph 2 of referenced memorandum, the word "directive" shall be changed to "directives" and the word "lists" to "list", and paragraph 2b shall have added the phrase "and of governments at war with Japan subsequent to 7 December 1941” between the word "Commission" and the word "shall".

/s/ R. M. Levy

/t/ R. M. LEVY,

Colonel, A.G.D.,

Adjutant General

(See reverse for SCAPIN)

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1925/1)

HCK/LBC/dc

14 January 1949

1. References are:

a. Memorandum from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers to Far Eastern Commission Member Nations, AG 000.91 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST, dated 6 August 1948, subject: Access to Japanese Scientific and Technical Information in Japan.

b. Radio W 82089 from Department of the Army (JCOS) to SCAP, dated 1 January 1949.

c. Letter from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers to the Commanding General, Eighth Army, AG 000.91 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST, dated 6 August 1948, subject, same as par la.

d. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 000.91 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST, SCAPIN 1925, 6 August 1948, subject, same as par la.

2. Subject memorandum was approved by C/S.

3. Subject radio is directive, Serial Nbr. 98, implementing policy of the Far Eastern Commission, "Access to Japanese Technical and Science Information by Non-FEC Countries at War with Japan.”

4. This action extracts pertinent parts from memorandum (reference la) and forwards this necessary information to all missions in Japan so that action can be taken by them to inform interested non-FEC countries whose interests they represent, and changes SCAPIN 1925 and letter Commanding General, Eighth Army, to conform with latest directive.

5. Thirty-five non-FEC nations were at war with Japan subsequent to 7 December 1941. However, due to termination of period of investigation (31 March 1949), it is believed that only a few non-FEC countries will take advantage of subject policy.

6. This is an administrative matter.

7. Concurrences: G-l - Lt. Col. R. H. Harrison

G-2 - Col. C. S. Myers

CPC - No objection (by telephone from Mr. Blake)

DS - Mr. Ainsworth

LS - A. C. Carpenter

L. H. Cross - 57-8486

H. C. K.
