GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004.06 (26 Jan 48) OGA

(SCAPIN - 1852)

APO 500

26 January 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Submission of Monthly Labor Report.

1. References:

a. AG 091.3 (20 Oct 45) ESS, SCAPIN 260, dated 10 November 1945, subject: Monthly Labor Report.

b. AG 004.06 (19 Jul 47) OGA, SCAPIN 1749, dated 19 July 1947, subject: Change of Submission Date of Monthly Labor Report.

2. References 1a and b are rescinded.

3. The Japanese Government will prepare and submit the following certified data for each of the forty-six (46) Japanese prefectures, for each month beginning with January 1948, and each month thereafter:

a. Actual number of workers furnished to the Allied Forces on Labor Requisition from the first through the last day of the month.

b. Actual number of workers furnished to the Allied Forces on Procurement Demand from the first through the last day of the month.

c. Total amount of cash wages actually paid by the Japanese Government to the laborers supplied to the Allied Forces on Labor Requisition from the first through the last day of the month.

d. Total amount of cash wages actually paid by the Japanese Government to the laborers supplied to the Allied Forces, on Procurement Demand from the first through the last day of the month.

e. Estimated value of wages in kind, such as food, furnished by the Japanese Government, without cost, to laborers working for the Allied Faroes on Labor Requisition from the first through the last day of the month.

f. Estimated value of wages in kind, such as food, furnished by the Japanese Government, without cost, to laborers working for the Allied Forces on Procurement Demand from the first through the last day of the month.

4. On each report shall appear the actual breakdown of each item of data detailed in paragraph 3 above that should be charged to:

United States Army (excluding Air Force)

United' States Air Force

United States Navy

United States Marine Corps

United States State Department

United States Maritime Commission

Japan Oil Storage Company

American Red Cross

Foreign Liquidation Commission

Atomic Bomb Casualty Commission

Far Eastern Commission

Far Eastern Advisory Commission

International Military Tribunal Far East

United Nations National Relief Administration (UNNRA)

Allied Council

International Relief Committee

Australia

Canada

China

Denmark

France

Great Britain (BCOF)

Netherlands

New Zealand

Philippines

Portugal

Spain

Sweden

Switzerland

Russia

All Others not listed hereon

5. The report will be submitted to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, attention: General Accounting Section and is due on the 28th of the month following the period reported.

6. Direct communication between the General Accounting Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Labor Section, Accommodation Division, Special Procurement Board, concerning matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
