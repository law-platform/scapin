GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 072 (8 Apr 49) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN - 1990/2)

APO 500

27 August 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Procedures for Restoration of Patents, Utility Models, and Designs to Allied Nationals.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for Japanese Government, file AG 072 (8 Apr 49)CPC/FP, SCAPIN I990, 8 April 1949, subject as above, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The date in paragraph 6 of memorandum referenced in paragraph 1 above shall be amended to read as follows: "1 September 1949."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
