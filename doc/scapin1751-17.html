GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 387.6 (22 Jan 48) REP

(SCAPIN - 1751/17)

APO 500

22 January 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Transmittal of Allocation Notices.

1. References are:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, File AG 387.6 (22 Jul 47) REP, (SCAPIN 1751), dated 22 July 1947, subject: Responsibilities of the Japanese Government for the Packaging, Transfer, and Delivery of Equipment and Records Allocated to Claimant Nations Under the Reparations Program.

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, File AG 387.6 (2 Dec 47) REP, (SCAPIN 1751/2) dated 2 December 1947, subject: Transmittal of Allocation Notices.

2. Inclosed is a supplemental allocation notice covering items of equipment located in the 1st Tokyo Military Arsenal, Sendai Factory, Haranomachi, Sendai-shi (24-4) which have been accepted to date by the Netherlands, in addition to those previously covered in reference 1b above.

3. Instructions issued in references will apply.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

1 Incls (to addressee only)

1. Alloc. Notice 2-19, Equip.

Alloc Netherlands Plant 24-4 (21 Items)
