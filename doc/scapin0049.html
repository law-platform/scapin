IN THE CLEAR
OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS
SIGNAL CORPS MESSAGE

(SCAPIN -49)

GC-O DBJ/gr 24 September 1945

FROM :SCAP
TO :JAPANESE MINISTRY OF WAR...........OPNL PRIORITY
CINCAFPAC MANILA...................OPNL PRIORITY
CINCPAC............................OPNL PRIORITY
USAFIK.............................OPNL PRIORITY

DESIRE THAT THE KEMPEI TAI ORGANIZATION IN KOREA BE IMMEDIATELY PLACED UNDER THE DIRECT COMMAND OF THE COMMANDING GENERAL JAPANESE SEVENTEENTH ARMY GROUP IN KOREA AND THAT THE HEAD OF THE KEMPEI TAI IN KOREA BE INSTRUCTED TO TAKE ORDERS FROM THE ARMY GROUP COMMANDER PD FURTHER DESIRE THAT REPORT BE MADE TO THIS OFFICE WHEN THIS ACTION HAS BEEN COMPLETED

OFFICIAL:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, US Army,
Adjutant General

APPROVED:

/s/W. E. Chambers
/t/W. E. CHAMBERS
Brigadier General, G. S. C.
Acting Asst. Chief of Staff, G-3
