SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004.03 (7 Feb 47) ESS/TD

(SCAPIN-1512)

APO 500

7 February 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Cotton Textile Capacity.

1. Reference is made to C. L O. Memorandum No.5505 (ECI), dated 18 October 1946, which transmits the Three-Year Textile Industry Rehabilitation Plan.

2. Approval is given for the rebuilding of cotton textile plant capacity to the level of 4,000,000 spindles, and necessary complementary equipment, including looms; and for maximum operation of such machinery.

3. This approval is not to be construed as granting priorities on fuel, power, steel or other materials. Allocations of materials for this reconstruction work will be made as directed by appropriate agencies of the Imperial Japanese Government.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1512)

ESS/TD HST/SCW/mb

27 December 1946

1. Basic: Memo from SCAP to HG, File No. AG 423 ( ) ESS/TD, dated , subject: Cotton Textile Capacity. This directive authorizes the Japanese cotton textile industry to rebuild to a capacity level of 4,000,000 spindles, plus complementary equipment, including looms.

2. Reference is made to Staff Study, dated , subject, Interim Level of Japanese Cotton Textile Production. This staff study shows that 4,000,000 spindles will provide the necessary cotton textiles to meet anticipated export requirements in the next few years; plus a normal level of domestic consumption for industrial and personal and household uses.

3. It is contemplated that the responsible SCAP authorities will exercise supervision over the allocation of scarce materials which will be required for the achievement of this level of capacity.

4. The interests of USAFIK are not concerned.

5. This is considered an administrative matter, and no surveillance by the Eighth Army will be required.
