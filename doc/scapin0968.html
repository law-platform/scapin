GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 231.8 (18 May 46) GD

(SCAPIN - 968)

APO 500

18 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Manning of U. S. Vessels by Japanese Seamen.

1. Circumstances described in C. L. O. No. 2082(CM) of 2 May 1946 have been investigated and the following information has been ascertained:

a. In some instances competent Japanese Masters, Chief Engineers and subordinate officers have not been readily available through the CMMC necessitating the use of U. S. personnel.

b. In some instances more competent seamen have been available through the Yokohama Seamens' Union than through the CMMC.

2. It is conceded that dealing solely with the CMMC would facilitate procurement of Japanese seamen and it is also appreciated that employment of all-Japanese crew would avoid difficulties through differences in customs, language and standardization of wages, food and clothing rations. However, the paramount consideration is operating efficiency of the vessels and to the extent that full efficiency cannot be realized from the employment of seamen procured from the CMMC, crews will be assembled in whatever manner that will ensure such efficiency.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
