GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 800.217 (25 Mar 46) NR

(SCAPIN-847)

APO 500

25 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Whaling Operations Around Bonin (Ogasawara) and Kazan Islands.

Reference: (a) FLTLOSCAP Serial No. 80 dated 27 September 1945.

(b) SCAJAP Serial No. 42 dated 13 October 1945.

(c) SCAJAP Serial No. 587 dated 3 November 1945.

(d) SCAP AG 800.217 (3 Nov. 45) NR dated 3 November 1945, SCAPIN 233.

(e) SCAP AG 323.43 (30 Nov. 45) NR dated 30 November 1945, SCAPIN 371.

(f) SCAP AG 800 (20 Jan. 46) NR dated 20 January 1946, SCAPIN 630.

(g) SCAJAP H 1 - 6, Serial No. 1050 dated 30 January 1946.

(h) COMPATJAP, A 4 - 1, Serial No. P - 1618 dated 5 February 1946.

(i) SCAJAP, H 1 - 6, Serial No. 1086 dated 16 February 1946.

1. Reference is made to memorandum from the Imperial Japanese Government dated 20 March 1946, file C. L. O. No. 1305 (CM), to General Headquarters of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, in which request is made for extension of time for Japanese whaling operations in the vicinity of the Bonin and Kazan Islands to 30 April 1946.

2. Japanese whaling operations will be permitted in the area around the Bonin (Ogasawara) and Kazan Islands, between N. Lat. 24 and 27°30’, and between E. Long. 140°50’ and 143°50’during the period 1 April 1946 to 30 April 1946, both dateds inclusive. This authorization does not establish any precedent for extension of whaling or fishing privileges in any other areas outside of those authorized in references (a), (b), and (c) above, or for any subsequent period of time.

3. Japanese whaling vessels shall not anchor or use any harbor on any of the Bonin (Ogasawara) or Kazan Islands and no personnel from such vessels shall land or have any contact with the above mentioned Islands. Land bases for the whaling operations in the Bonin (Ogasawara) and Kazan Islands area may be established only within the area previously authorized in references (a), (b) and (c) above.

4. The following vessels are permitted to operate in the area outlined in paragraph 2 above.

a. Factory ship: Transport No. 19.

b. Killer boats: Fumi-maru, Seki-maru No. 2, Seki-maru No. 3.

c. Carriers: Banshu-maru No. 25, Banshu maru No. 35, Banshu-maru No. 41, Shinsei-maru, Shinsei-maru No. 2, Shinsei-maru No. 6, Shinsei-maru No. 37, Shinsei-maru 38, Shinsei-maru No. 76.

5. The attention of the Imperial Japanese Government is called to SCAP directive on the whaling industry, reference (d) above.

6. Permission hereby also is authorized for vessels listed in paragraph 4 above to leave, during the period 25 March to 24 April 1946 both dates inclusive, the area proviously authorized in references (a), (b) and (c) above for the purpose of operating in the area hereby authorized. Access to this area will be made through the waters lying between N. Lat. 30° and 27°30’ and E. Long. 140°50’ and 141°50’.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General
