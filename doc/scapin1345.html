GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (19 Nov 46) DS

(SCAPIN-1345)

APO 500

19 November 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Incomplete Report Submitted in Compliance with SCAPIN 958.

1. Reference is made to Paragraph 1a of Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, File AG 014.33 (16 May 46) DS (SCAPIN 958) dated 16 May 1946, subject: “Information Concerning Acts of American Nationals and Certain Japanese Legal Procedures.”

2. Information of non-compliance with directive named in paragraph 1 above has been received. Specific defect is as follows:

a. Cited paragraph 1a concerns all persons who are believed to have had American citizenship and who applied for Japanese nationality. The list submitted, however, contains only the names of those persons whose applications were granted and omits those whose application ware either refused or are pending.

b. As an example, the names of the following persons, who are understood to have made application for Japanese nationality subsequent to 1941, do not appear on the submitted list:

(1) KIRBURN, Elizabeth

(2) WELIS, Florence

(3) FINCHER, Robert Toombs (alias NAI Tamio)

3. Therefore, the Imperial Japanese Government will correct this deficiency by submitting, in duplicate, an alphabetically arranged list of all those believed to have had American citizenship and who applied for Japanese nationality, whether the application was refused or is pending, and with particular attention to the period from 1 January 1941 to date.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
