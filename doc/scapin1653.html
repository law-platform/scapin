MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Declaration of Japanese Merchant Vessels as Total Losses.

1. Appendix "A" contains a list of Japanese merchant vessels which are to be considered as total losses, available for stripping and reduction to scrap for the benefit of the Japanese economy.

2. This memorandum in no way authorizes payment of insurance claims on any of the vessels listed.

3. The period of sixty days from the data of this memorandum is allowed to owners of these vessels to present through the Imperial Japanese Government, Ministry of Transportation to Commander Naval Forces, Japan, any requests for exclusion from the list inclosed of any vessels now under salvage or whose salvage and repair may appear possible.

4. After the sixty-day period no applications for salvage of these ships in their entirety will be approved except for purposes of scrapping, and all vessels will be stricken from the lists of Japanese shipping.

5. A quarterly report of vessels being scrapped under these provisions shall be furnished by the Imperial Japanese Government to Commander Naval Forces, Japan, with whom direct communication is authorized in this matter.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
