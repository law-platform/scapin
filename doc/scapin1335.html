GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.7 (13 Nov 46) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1335)

APO 500

13 November 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Release of German-owned Goods.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to release from the provisions of memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 091.112(13 Sep 45)MG, Scapin 26, 13 September 1945, subject, "Protection of Allied and Axis Property" from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, the following German-owned goods at the locations indicated:

Item Quantity Weight Location

Shellac 57 cases 5,015 kg. Mitsui Bonded Warehouse, Shinko-machi Kobe

Sticklac 187 bags 14,416 kg. Mitsui Bonded Warehouse, Shinko-machi Kobe

Gum Damar 15 cases 1,573.8 kg. Koeki Eidan

Tungsten Ore 1,789 bags and sweepings 54,343.3kg. Koeki Eidan

Tin Containers 2,633 29,621 kg. Koeki Eidan

Tin 676 bars 31,366.4 kg. Koeki Eidan

Tin 1,634 slabs 21,008.8 kg. Koeki Eidan

Selenium 18 cases 620 kg. Koeki Eidan

Molybdenum Ore 146 cases 6,986 kg. Koeki Eidan

Rook Crystals 15 cases 181 kg. Koeki Eidan

Item Quantity Weight Location

Caffeine 96 cases 3,151 kg. Sumitomo Warehouse Kobe

Raw Rubber 6,475 pkgs. 121,295.6 kg. Marimoto Soko Kawanishi Soko, Kobe

Raw Rubber 889 pcs 3,079 kg. Marimoto Soko Kawanishi Soko, Kobe

Menthol Crystals 90 cases 3,953. kg. Japan Warehouse Control Association, Sasayama, Hyogo

Peppermint Oil 30 cases 1,380 kg. Japan Warehouse Control Association, Sasayama, Hyogo

Palm Oil 1 drum 220 kg. Shimbo Soko, Yokohama

2. The above mentioned goods will be relinquished to the Japanese Ministry of Commerce and Industry who will contact the Industry Division of the Economic and Scientific Section of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers as to instructions regarding their disposition.

3. These goods will be distributed in accordance with allocations made as per instructions in paragraph 2 and sold at official government prices.

4. All funds derived from the sale of those goods will be deposited in the Custody Account for the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and a complete report submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

5. The contents of this memorandum shall not be construed to release other goods from the blocking provisions of any other memorandum issued by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander, for the Allied Powers or any ordinance promulgated by the Imperial Japanese Government.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
