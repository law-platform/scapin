AG 413 (2 Dec 48) CPC/PLD

(SCAPIN - 1946/2)

APO 500

20 January 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Centralization of Looted Property.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 413 (2 Dec 48) CPC/LGP, SCAPIN 1946, 2 December 1948, subject, "Centralization of Looted Machinery," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Inclosed is a list of looted machinery and equipment, unidentified as to area of seizure, which are under the custody of the Japanese Government and control of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. The Japanese Government is directed to centralize this machinery and equipment in the Yoga-cho Looted Property Warehouse, Building #11, 2-chome, Tamagawa, Yoga-cho, Setagaya-ku, Tokyo.

3. Instructions outlined in reference 1 above will be followed when centralizing the subject property. The necessary L-3 Transfer Forms are inclosed.

4. A report that the actions directed above have been completed, together with a list of property which has been moved into the designated warehouse in compliance with this directive, will be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers by 23 January 1950.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K. B. BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
