SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 453.3 (26 Jan 46) GD

(SCAPIN-656)

APO 500

26 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Weekly Report on Coal Shipment to Korea.

1. Reference is made to your letter dated 1▲January 1945, file O.L.O. number 223(3.2), subject as above. It is noted on this ▲ report that the shipments of coal to Korea for December total 49,066 tons and to date for January only 6,364 tons.

2. The unloading difficulties which ▲ the of coal at Pusan during the months of October and November have been eliminated and, since early December, that port has been in a position to handle 70/73, 000 tons per month. ▲ will take in ▲ steps to insure full compliance with Memorandum from Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers to the Imperial Japanese Government dated 25 September 1945, file AG 463.3(25 Sep 45) GD, subject: ”Provision of Coal at Pusan, Korea, for use of Commanding General, United States Forces in Korea.”

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. Allen,
Colonel, A. G. D.
Asst. Adjutant General.
