AG 000.5 (18 Mar 46) LS

(SCAPIN - 826)

APO 500

18 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

The Following named Japanese will be apprehended as Suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, EIGHTH Army, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest possible date.

NAME

RANK OR TITLE

DATE, CAMP, ADDRESS, OR ASSIGNMENT

FUJIMOTO, Minoru

Guard

Tokyo Branch Camp No.8, March 1944-August 1945.

FUJIOKA, Hide

Lt. Col.

Member of Kempei Tai, Osaka Hqs. Probable home address: Osaka.

FURUYA, Shonosuke

CI

Was on Luzon, P.I. in Sept. 1945. Sailed for Yokohama, 24 Dec 45, aboard vessel H.B. Stowe. Estimated to have arrived 2 Jan 46. Probable home address: Yamanashi-Ken, Kofushi, Uo-machi, 16 Banchi.

HIBI, Hiroshi

Sgt.

Member of Trans. Corps - Okamoto (Yasuyuki, Lt. Gen.) Unit. Was on Luzon, P.I. Sept. 1945. Sailed for Kure, 3 Dec 45 aboard vessel Maki. Estimated to have arrived 3 Dec 45. Probable home address: Aichi-Ken, Nakahima-Gun, Sogue Cho, Razasogue, Aza, Minami Kawahara, 100 Banchi.

HONDA, Hajime

Civ. Guard

Fukuoka Branch Camp No.1, March 1943-June 1945.

ISHIWARA, Takenori

Lt.

Was on board the Japanese cruiser Tone, during 1944.

ITO, Kensei

Pvt. 2/C

Former member of the Mabuchi (Raid) Co., 1st Bn., 426th Regt. of the Gohoku (147th) Division. Was stationed at Hiyoshi, Chosei Province, Chiba Prefecture, Honshu, Japan, during 1945. Probable home address: Kitamino-Kuni, Joro-Gun, Aiuchi-Mura, Toyoda, Hokkaido.

KAMIURA, Jyunnari

Commander (Navy)

Was stationed on Chichi Jima. Probable home address: Makurazaki-Cho, Kawanabe-Gun, Kagoshima.

MATSUDA, Toru

1st Lt.

MII, Junsuke

Commander

Sup. Pvt.

NAGAI, Kunio

B. M. FITCH
