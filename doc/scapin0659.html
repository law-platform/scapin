28 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Request for Information on the Salaries of the Officials of the Defunct Government-General of Korea Paid by the United States Authorities.

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. Memorandum No. 574(1), dated 15 November 1945, Subject: "Request for Information on the Salaries of the Officials of the Defunct Government General of Korea Paid by the United States Authorities".

2. The request for information contained in reference memorandum is denied.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
