APO 500

6 August 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Use of Japanese Tankers for Lift of Fuel Oil ex Persian Gulf and Saudi Arabia.

1. The Japanese Government is directed to take immediate action to make available until further notice all serviceable Japanese ocean tankers, fully manned, adequately fitted and provisioned for service between Japan and the Persian Gulf for the lifting of petroleum products (Navy Special and/or Diesel Fuel Oil) from Caltex Oil Company refineries at Bahrein Island, Persian Gulf and Ras Tanura, Saudi Arabia.

2. Detailed pertinent instructions covering these lifts are incorporated in the letter from Commander Naval Forces, Far East, to the Ministry of Transportation, dated 29 July 1948, file CNFE/A4/QS, Serial (2467).

3. The master of each vessel will be authorized to receipt for and accept the cargo in the name of Boeki Cho (Board of Trade) at point of loading for transfer of the same to the Petroleum Distribution Kodan upon return to Japan. A report shall be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, within ten (10) days after discharge showing total quantity receipted for and discharged.

4. Quantitative receipts (5 copies) showing the total quantity manifested and received from Bahrein Island and Ras Tanura will be signed and sealed by the officially designated agency of Boeki Cho in the proper space provided for in the receipt. These receipts will be submitted to the local Military Government unit.

5. All Ministries and Agencies of the Japanese Government concerned will assist in the execution of this operation.

6. Direct communication is hereby authorized among Commander, United States Naval Forces, Far East; Commanding General, Eighth Army; General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Economic and Scientific Section, and interested agencies of the Japanese Government to implement the provisions of the Memorandum.

7. No part of the cost of this operation will be charged to the Termination of the War Cost or Occupation Cost accounts of the Japanese Government.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
