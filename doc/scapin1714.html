GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 095 (2 Jun 47) ESS/AC

(SCAPIN - 1714)

APO 500

2 June 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Action Taken on Central Liaison Office Memoranda

1. No objection is offered to the following Central Liaison Office Memoranda:

a. No. 2371 (LO 807/1577) of Yoshiura Shipping Company, Ltd, for permission to pay call on shares.

b. No. 5702 (LO 1891/1107) of Ore Distribution Control Company, Ltd, for removal from the Schedule of Restricted Concerns.

c. No. 5879 (LO 1869/1095) of Grinding Ball Control Company, Ltd, for removal from the Schedule of Restricted Concerns.

d. No. 1150 (2. 1) of Fuji Industrial Company, Ltd, for removal of its subsidiary, Matsuyasu Aero Industry, Ltd, from the Schedule of Restricted Concerns.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. M. Levy
/t/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1714)

WFM/ECW/PFC/mcf

29 May 1947

1. The attached memorandum for the Japanese Government notifies the Central Liaison Office of action taken on Central Liaison Office Memoranda. A complete Memo for Record with all necessary concurrences has been filed in the files of Antitrust and Cartels Division, ESS, for each application covered by this directive.

2. This is an administrative matter and no surveillance by the Eighth Army is required.

3. The interests of USAFIK are not concerned.

4. Concurrences: None required.

E. C. W. 26-6561
