(**) Includes 68 grams remaining in stocks of Nippon Electric Company, 5 as of 31 December 1946, allocated in Third Quarter of 1946.

3. All gold and silver will be obtained from stocks in the possession of the Imperial Japanese Government. 40.495 grams of platinum will be released to authorized representatives of the Ministry of Finance from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan; this platinum will include 10,000 grams of platinum wire allocated to Tanaka Kikinzoku Kogyo K.K. “Platinum family” metals except as indicated by (*), will be obtained from available sources, and reports of such sources will be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. All future requests for the allocation for precious metals for domestic consumption must contain written approval, based upon Japan’s minimum needs, of the interested ministries of the Imperial Japanese Government; application for release of precious metals for manufacture of export items must include the number of the Validated Form 100.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to submit a monthly report to include the following information for each of the firms named in paragraph 2 above:

a. Gold, silver, and platinum on hand as of the first day of the month;

b Gold, silver, and platinum received during the month;

c. Gold, silver, and platinum used during the month;

d. Gold, silver, and platinum on head as of the last day of the month;

e. Products manufactured and disposition of same.

5. Reports will be submitted in quadruplicate, not later than the fifteenth day of the month following that covered by the report. These reports will include data pertaining to the Nippon Dentalist Association.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
