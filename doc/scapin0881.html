GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 430 (17 Apr 46) ESS/IE

(SCAPIN - 881)

APO 500

17 April 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Delivery of U. S. Army Subsistence Stocks

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will take the following action with regard to U.S. Army subsistence stocks, below listed:

a. Arrange with Naval Shipping Control Authority for Japanese Merchant Marine (SCAJAP) for the sending of necessary vessels to Okinawa for the purpose of lifting U.S. Army stocks as follows:

Canned Meats 5,250,000 Lbs. Dry Beans 7,500,000 Lbs.

Canned Vegetables 616,000 Lbs. Jams and Spreads 809,000 Lbs.

Flour 1,520,000 Lbs. Dehydrated Soups 840,000 Lbs.

Stabilized Butter 564,000 Lbs. Spice Nuggets 770,000 Lbs.

Canned Fish 300,000 Lbs. Dehydrated Potatoes 2,550,000 Lbs.

Catsup 79,000 Lbs. 10 in 1 Rations 30,000 Cases

b. Instruct the Ships’ Masters concerned to furnish the Commanding Officer at Okinawa with quantitative receipts for these items at the shipping point, two copies of these quantitative receipts to have a signed verification by the Commanding Officer at Okinawa or his representative and to be retained by the Ships' Masters.

c. Both copies of these quantitative receipts will be verified by the Imperial Japanese Government upon discharge of the supplies at port In Japan and will be given to the Commanding General, Eighth United States Army.

d. Upon arrival in Japan, store the subject stocks close to the cities of Tokyo and Yokohama and inform the General Headquarters of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers by written notice of the points of storage and quantities.

e. Await further instructions from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers before any distribution is made.

2. Terms of payment and accounting will be decided later.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 881)

WFM/HJZ/mlt

8 April 1946

1. Reference is made to message S 40197 GSQMT from CG AFWESPAC to CINCAFPAC advising that a large quantity of excess stocks in Okinawa are subject to spoilage in 60 days; that the quantities cannot be consumed before estimated spoilage date and that the Foreign Liquidation Commission cannot dispose of them. Subject radio asks if the items can be shipped to Japan.

2. Reference is also made to radio CX 59206 from CINCAFPAC to AFWESPAC asking that certain items be shipped to CG 8th Army as Army stocks; that tea is in excess in Japan; and that the remainder be applied against Import program in Japan.

3. The IJG is hereby directed to take delivery at Okinawa of the following stocks and to store them at points close to the Cities of Tokyo and Yokohama:

Canned Meat 5,250,000 Lbs. Dry Beans 7,500,000 Lbs.

Canned Vegetables 616,000 Lbs. Jams and Spreads 809,000 Lbs.

Flour 1,520,000 Lbs. Dehydrated Soups 840,000 Lbs.

Stabilized Butter 564,000 Lbs. Spice Nuggets 770,000 Lbs.

Canned Fish 300,000 Lbs. Dehydrated Potatoes 2,550,000 Lbs.

Catsup 79,000 Lbs. 10 in 1 Rations 30, 000 Cases

4. These stocks are in supplement to our Import Program.

5. This is a policy directive and Army Surveillance is necessary.

6. This problem as it relates to Korea has been considered.

7. Concurrences: NRS - Maj Williamson

G-4 - Trans. - Col. Lind

G-4 - P&P - Col. Ebel

PC&R - Capt. Egekvist and Capt. McLeod

PH&W - Col. Howe

R. A. M.
