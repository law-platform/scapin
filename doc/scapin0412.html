GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (10 Dec 45) LS

(SCAPIN - 412)

10 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Extent of Philippines Command of Lt. General Homma, Masaharu.

The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to furnish this headquarters with an official statements as to the scope or exempt of the command of Lt. General HOMMA, Masaharu, in the Philippines from 24 December 1941 to August 194▲, said statement to included answers to the following questions:

a. Was HONMMA The Supreme Commander of all Japanese Armed Forces, of whatever branch, whenever those Forces were on the land in the Philippines?

b. Did HOMMA have Supreme Command over the Japanese Air Forces in the Philipines?

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
