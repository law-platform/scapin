GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 440 (20 Dec 45) PH

(SCAPIN-465)

20 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Japanese Military and Naval Medical Supplies Received by Home Ministry from Occupation Forces.

1. Memorandum, file AG 402.5 (24 Sept. 45) GD, subject: “Materials, supplies and Equipment Received and to be Received from the Japanese Armed Forces”, dated 24 September 1945, forwarded by this headqparters to the Imperial Japanese Government, directs that the Home Ministry will receive and distribute such supplies and equipment as are to be returned to the Japanese Government.

2. It has been noted from reports received in is headquarters from the Armies and from observation that large quantities of military medical supplies still remain available for distribution through civilian channels. It is known that doctors and hospitals are short of necessary drugs and equipment for proper treatment of the civilian population. This condition must be alleviated by prompt utilization of the existing military stocks.

3. Steps must be taken at once to stimulate the distribution of medical supplies transferred to the Home Ministry and to determine that they reach the consumer. Prompt measures must be taken to assure that:

a. Prefectural officials take vigorous action upon advice that supplies are availlable at a depot or collection point.

b. The prefectural agencies are furnished sufficient transport, labor and warehouse space, and that a minimum of delay is caused by necessary paper work.

c. Diatributing agencies move the supplies to doctors, hospitals and other dispensers expeditiously for the relief of civillian ill.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
