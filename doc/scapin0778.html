GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (27 Feb 46) CCS

(SCAPIN-778)

APO 500

27 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Transfer of Radio Station from Yura to Tanabe

1. Reference is made to letters S859, dated 16 February 1946, 1946 subject “Abolition of Wireless Communications System and “Change of Frequency”, and S891, dated 22 February 1946, subject “Change of Communication Elements and Closing of their Wireless Communication Station”, from Liaison Commission (Tokyo) for the Imperial Japanese Demobilization Ministries.

2. Transfer of the First Demobilization Ministry radio station at Yura, Wakayama Prefecture, to Tanabe, Wakayama Prefecture, and establishment of a circuit between Osaka and Tanabe are not authorized.

3. I accordance with SCAP Memorandum Number (SCAPIN 706), file, AG 311.23(5 Feb 46) CCS, subject, “Radio Communications Operated by Demobilization Ministries”, the station at Yura will be closed and equipment located there surrendered promptly to the occupation forces.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
