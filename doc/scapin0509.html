GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 350 (29 Dec 45) CIE

(SCAPIN - 509)

29 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Conduct of Courses in Physical Education.

1. Reference is made to C. L. O. Memorandum No. 1160 (1.4), dated 17 December 1945, inclosing a copy of Order No. 80 of the Bureau of Physical Education regarding the conduct of courses of physical education under post-war circumstances.

2. The content of Order No. 80 of the Bureau of Physical Education is approved.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
