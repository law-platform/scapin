MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Authorization for retention on Inactive Status of Certain Radio Facilities.

1. Reference is made to memoranda from the Ministry of Communications, File LS 17 and 18, dated 16 September 1946, subject: Applications for Authorization for Retention of Radio Stations.

2. The Japanese Government will preserve intact the radio stations listed in inclosure 1 attached.

3. This memorandum does not authorize the operation of the listed radio stations.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

26 December 1946

MEMORANDUM:

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Memorandum to Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 676.3 (26 Dec 46)CCS, (SCAPIN 1420), dated 26 Dec 46, subject: Authorization for Detention on Inactive Status of Certain Radio Facilities

1. Reference is made to Memorandum to Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 676.3 (29 Aug 46)CCS, (SCAPIN 1166), dated 29 August 1946, subject: Control of Radio Communications. This memorandum as amended authorizes Japanese radio stations, defines the permitted classes of stations and directs the dismantling of all others.

2. The twelve radio stations listed on Inclosure attached, hereto, are not authorized to operate by the reference memorandum. The Japanese Government is directed to preserve these stations intact, thereby exempting them from the requirement that unauthorized radio stations be dismantled.

3. Existing instructions with regard to surveillance of Japanese radio stations are modified to permit the Japanese Government to retain the radio stations listed in attached Inclosure.

R. G. HERSEY

Lt. Col. AGD,

Asst. Adj. Gen.
