GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG410.2 (22 Feb 46) ESS-FI

(SCAPIN-765)

APO 500

22 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Release of Gold, Silver and Platinum for Domestic use.

1. Reference is made to C. L. O. Memorandum No. 131 (2. 1) dated 10 January 1946 subject: “Estimated Quantities of Domestic Consumption of Gold, Silver and Platinum during the first Quarter of 1946.”

2. a. The Imperial Japanese Government may authorize the use of precious metal during the first quarter of 1946 in the following amounts and for the purposes as shown:

Gold Grams

Industrial use 7.000

Industrial art works 10.000

Gold-foil, dust, liquid, chloride 23.000

Total 40.000

Silver

Industrial use and medical use 24.450

Industrial art works 12.000

Total 36.450

Platinum

Industrial use 50.000

Total 50.000

b. No precious metal is authorized by this Memorandum for Dental use. In this connection, refer to this Headquarters Memorandum for Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 091. 33 (18 Jan. 46) ESS - FI, dated 18 January 1946, subject: “Release of Gold, Silver, and Platinum for Dental Work.”

3. The Ministry of Finance may give approval to such transactions in, and transfers of, gold, silver, and platinum as are necessary so that manufactures may acquire the precious metals, the use of which is authorized above. All sources, other than the stocks now impounded in the Bank of Japan, will be drawn upon, before authorizing the use of those stocks. The Imperial Japanese Government will immediately assess all sources of precious metals available for industrial use other than stocks in the Bank of Japan and report to this Headquarters the minimum quantities required for release from the Bank of Japan for the purpose authorized in the Memorandum.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government will submit to this Headquarters on or before the 10th of each month a report showing the amount of precious metals distributed in the previous month, the source from which the metal was obtained, a list of eventual users together with the products manufactured.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN-765)

WFM/ HSO/ cab

1. C. L. O. Memorandum 1188(2. 1) dated 10 January 1946, transmitted a request from the Ministry of Finance for approval by this Headquarters of certain amounts of gold, silver, and platinum for peacetime industrial uses and for indispensable sanitary purposes such as dentists requirements, said amounts to cover the first quarter of 1946.

2. Memorandum for Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 123 (22 Sept 45) ESS, subject: "Control of Financial Transactions," prohibits transactions in gold, silver and platinum without prior approval of this Headquarters.

3. This memorandum authorizes the use of certain amounts of precious metal for industrial and certain medical uses but does not cover dental uses. Precious metal for dental use has already been authorized in Memorandum, file AG 091.33 (18 Jan 46) ESS/FI, subject: "Release of Gold, Silver and Platinum for Dental Work."

4. Amounts approved are as follows:

Gold Grams

Industrial use 7,000

Industrial art works 10,000

Gold-foil, dust, liquid, chloride 23,000

40,000

Silver

Industrial use and medical use 24,450

Industrial art works 12,000

36,450

Platinum

Industrial use 50,000

50,000

5. In order to provide a check, the Imperial Japanese Government was directed to submit to this. Headquarters on or before the 10th of each month a report showing the amount of precious metals distributed, the source from which the metal was obtained, a list of eventual users together with the products manufactured.

6. The Imperial Japanese Government was also directed to immediately assess all sources of precious metals available for industrial use, other than those stocks in the Bank of Japan, and report to this Headquarters the minimum quantities required for release of the Bank of Japan for the purpose authorized in our Memorandum.

7. Inasmuch as a portion of the metal, the use of which is authorized by this memorandum, will probably have to be drawn from stocks in the Bank of Japan, and now in the custody of the Eighth Army, instructions will be prepared authorizing limited releases by the Army for sale to the central agency of distribution (Metal Distribution Control Co.).

8. Verbal concurrences ESS/IN.

C. F. T.
