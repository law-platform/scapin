GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.3 (12 Aug 46) CCS

(SCAPIN-1122)

APO 500

12 August 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Residence Telephone Service for Foreign Nationals and Stateless Personnel Employed by the Occupation Forces.

1. It is directed that the Imperial Japanese Government furnish telephone service to the residences of approved foreign nationals and stateless personnel employed by the Occupation Forces in accordance with the following:

a. All standard rates and charges, including the registration fee and the installation charge will be collected for service rendered.

b. Statements for both local and long distance service will be submitted to the subscribers direct and will not be included in statements of services rendered to the Occupation Force.

c. The Occupation Force Headquarters responsible for approving the installation of local telephone service will be the approving authority.

2. Correspondence between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Communications, regarding technical matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

12 August 1946

MEMORANDUM.

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN - 1122), file AG 311.3 (12 Aug 46) CCS, this Headquarters, dtd 12 Aug 46 subject: "Residence Telephone Service for Foreign Nationals and Stateless Personnel Employed by the Occupation Forces".

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. Instructions covering the procurement of residential telephone service for foreign nationals and stateless personnel employed by the Occupation Forces are in accordance with the following:

a. Request for telephone service will be forwarded through local military channels to the signal representative responsible for obtaining local service for Occupation Force personnel.

b. The Signal Corps representative will investigate to and if no more than 200 meters of telephone plant will need be constructed, the request will be approved and the local representative of the Japanese Communication service will be notified. The applicant will then be notified to file his order for service at the office designated by the Japanese representative.

c. At the time of applying for service, the registration fee and other standard charges, including the installation charge, should be paid.

d. Inasmuch as the contrast for telephone service is a private one, not involving General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, the Japanese Ministry of Communications may, if it deems it advisable, collect a deposit in advance to guarantee payment of bills, and may terminate service if statements are not paid promptly.

e. When approving applications for telephone service under the terms of this memorandum, it should be assured that the service is required for either security purposes or in connection with the individual's duties since it is not intended that any special preference in the connection of service be shown when the need is only for personal convenience.

3. Command instructions pertaining to execution of surveillance are being released simultaneously with this memorandum.

R. G. HERSEY

Lt Col, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
