AG 560 (19 Sep 46) GD

(SCAPIN - 1216)

APO 500

19 September 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Prompt Discharge and Demurrage Charges applicable to the tanker, "Puente Hills".

1. Immediate steps will be taken by the Imperial Japanese Government to insure prompt discharge of the U. S. Navy chartered tanker "Puente Hills" to the Petroleum Distribution Control Company storage tanks at Tsurumi.

2. Demurrage charges of $120 per hour to be payable by the Imperial Japanese Government will be considered as commencing at the time the "Puente Hills" was ready to begin discharge and will continue until this vessel has actually begun to discharge its cargo. Accrual of those demurrage charges will again commence sixty (60) hours after vessel first commences discharge.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
