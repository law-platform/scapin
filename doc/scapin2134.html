(SCAPIN-2134)

APO 500

4 January 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Rationalization of Japanese Police Radio Stations.

1. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.23 (4 May 49) CCS, SCAPIN 2000, as amended, dated 4 May 1949, subject: Japanese Police Radio Stations, is hereby rescinded.

2. It is the responsibility of the Japanese Government to regulate the operation of Japanese police radio stations in accordance with the Provisions of the International Telecommunication Convention (Atlantic City, 1947) and in accordance with Japanese laws as well as current and future directives issued by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
