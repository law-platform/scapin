GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 611 (22 Nov 48) CTS-H

(SCAPIN-1943)

APO 500

27 November 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Five-Year Program for Maintenance and Repair of Japanese Network of Roads and Streets.

1. Reference: Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 091 (15 Sep 45) MG, dated 15 September 1945 (SCAPIN 29), subject: Production in Non-War Plants.

2. Reference 1 above requires the Japanese Government to "initiate, maintain and enforce production and other economic programs that serve the following purposes: (paragraph 3b) the assurance of a just and impartial distribution of available supplies; (paragraph 3d) the restoration of Japanese Economy in order that the reasonable peacetime requirements of the population may be satisfied”; and to “encourage: (paragraph 4g) the production of items required for the operation, maintenance and repair of rail and road transportation and of coastal water transportation within the limitation of directives from the Supreme Commander."

3. The Japanese network of roads and streets, through lack of proper maintenance and repair, is deteriorating to the extent that it is presenting an obstacle to "restoration of Japanese economy in order that the reasonable peacetime requirements of the population can be satisfied”. If road and street deterioration continues unabated, the Japanese people will soon be deprived of one of their valuable physical assets.

4. Extensive construction, maintenance and repair of public roads and streets will no longer be accomplished by procurement instruments and letter directives as a requirement of the Occupation Forces but must be accomplished by the Japanese Government as a part of the Japanese public works program.

5. In order to preserve the existing network of roads and streets, it is necessary for the Japanese Government to develop a comprehensive program which coordinates the activities of the national, prefectural and local governments to the extent necessary for the initiation, maintenance and enforcement of actions which will preserve the existing network of roads and streets for the period of normal life expectancy. The program should include estimates of additional construction and maintenance equipment which must be procured by the Japanese Government and materials and supplies which must be imported, such as asphalt and petroleum products, to accomplish the program.

6. a. (1) Within thirty days from the date of this memorandum, the Japanese Government will submit to Civil Transportation Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, in detail, the program referred to in paragraph 5 above for the remainder of the current Japanese fiscal year. The limited funds available in the current Japanese budget for road works should be used primarily for maintenance and repair of existing roads and streets.

(2) Within sixty days, the program for fiscal year 1949 will be submitted.

(3) Within 180 days, separate programs for fiscal years 1950, 1951 and 1952 will be submitted.

b. These programs will indicate separately:

(1) Production roads necessary for. proper exploitation of agricultural, mineral, timber and industrial resources.

(2) City streets over which scheduled motor transportation services are operated.

(3) Connecting roads between principal traffic centers.

(4) Access roads serving (2) and (3).

and will include but not be limited to:

(1) Number of kilometers of roads and streets by type of surface, required to be repaired and maintained.

(2) Estimated cost based on current price and wage levels.

7. Direct communication within the scope of this memorandum is authorized between Civil Transportation Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and interested agencies of the Japanese Government.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
