GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (22 May 47) LS-R

(SCAPIN - 1700)

APO 500

22 May 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

The following named Japanese will be apprehended as Suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, EIGHTH Army, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest possible date.

NAME RANK OR TITLE PERTINENT DATA

FUKUTE, Yoshihiko Lt Commanded Naval Garrison, Gasmata, 25 July 43-4 Mar 44. Probable home address: #115, Kamigawara, Zaimokusa, Kamakura City, Kanagawa Prefecture.

HARA, Katsuo Cpl Kempei Tai, Ipoh, Malaya, Jan 1943. Probable home address: Hirose-mura, Miyazaki-gun, Fukushima, Miyazaki Prefecture.

HIGA, Shoichi Interpreter Kempei Tai, Alor Star, Malaya, April 1942. Probable home address: Kamihara Yonagusuku-mura, Nakakami-gun, To, Okinawa Prefecture. Age: 27, height:5'4", well built, speaks fluent Malay.

HORAI, Masame Lt Transferred to. #1 Railway Material Supply Depot, 9 Railway Regt, Burma, Siam April 1944.

KAWAI, (FNU) Sub Capt 38 Regt, 38 Div, South China Expeditionary Force, Stanley Jail, Hong Kong, Dec 1941. Was a senior Officer of Tanaka Shoji, or Doi Butai, Hong Kong 25 Dec 1944.

MATSUBARA, Tadao Lt 2 Btn, 3 Co, 9 Railway Regt, Burma Jan -Dec 1944.

TANAKA, Tokizo Civ Foreman Nagoya Area Camp #3, Funatsu, Mar-Aug 1945. Employed by Mitsui Mining Co.

USHIGAI, Sadao Sgt/Maj Kempei Tai, Hong Kong, April 1944-May 1945. Believed to live in Osaka Area. Age: 30-33. Height: - 5'3".

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
