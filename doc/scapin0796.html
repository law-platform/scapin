GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 423 (5 Mar 46) ESS/IE

(SCAPIN-796)

APO 500

5 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Shipment of Mulberry Seedlings to Korea. (XO-19 Mu-1KO)

1. It is directed that the necessary action be taken at once to effect the shipment to Korea of the following listed Mulberry seedlings:

Kenmochi 150,000 pcs.

Misc. Tarieties 50,000 pcs.

2. This shipment of seedlings will be combined with the shipment now being prepared under Directive AG 423 (2 Feb 46) ESS- IE, subject: Shipment of Mulberry Seeds and Seedlings to Korea. The same action will be taken as noted in paragraph 2 and 3 of the above-mentioned directive.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
