GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 131 (12 Dec 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 428)

APO 500

12 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Application of the Daisei Fire and Marine Insurance Company, Ltd., to Transact Insurance Business in Japan.

1. Reference is made to communication of the Ministry of Finance dated 29 November 1945.

2. Reference is further made to paragraph 3(d) of Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386 (14 Nov 45)ESS/FI, subject, "Transactions in Property in Japan Owned or Controlled by Persons Outside Japan."

3. The Imperial Japanese Government may permit the branch offices in Japan of the Daisei Fire and Marine Insurance Company, Ltd., to transact insurance business in Japan provided that:

a. All premium and other income collected on or after 22 September 1945 is segregated in a special account.

b. All payments on claims and for operating overhead expenses are made from this special account.

c. No payments are made from any other assets in Japan.

4. This memorandum shall not be deemed to authorize any transaction which could not be effected without the approval of this Headquarters if the subject company were organized in, and controlled by persons in Japan.

5. An acknowledgement of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
