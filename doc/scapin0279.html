GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 333.6 (14 Nov 45) AG-KD

(SCAPIN - 279)

14 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Effects of Deceased Prisoners of War and Civilian Internees.

1． With reference to Memorandum AG 383.6 of 30 September 1945, above mentioned subject, it is desired that the Prisoner of War Information Bureau deliver forthwith to Major R. S. Williams, Australian Military Forces, against his receipt, the personal effects of all deceased Australian prisoners of war and civilian internees.

2． Report will be made to this office when delivery of such effects is made.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
