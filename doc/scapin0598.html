GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 080 (15 Jan 46) GS

(SCAPIN - 598)

15 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Organization of a Japan Seamen's Welfare and Relief Association.

The request of the Imperial Japanese Government dated 22 December 1945, C. L. O. No.1312 (3.2), for approval of the establishment of the "Japan Seamen's Welfare and Relief Association" is not approved.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
