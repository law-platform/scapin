GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG676.3 (24 Oct 45) CCS

(SCAPIN-187)

24 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Extension of Expeditionary Force Message Service

1. It is desired that Expeditionary Force Message Service be extended to the Osaka-Kyoto area.

2. As facilities become available it is desired that this service be extended to other points in the Empire where United States Armed Forces are stationed. You will be advised of these locations from time to time by the Chief of the Civil Communications Section of this Headquarters.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. Allen,
Colonel, A. G. D.
Asst. Adjutant General.
