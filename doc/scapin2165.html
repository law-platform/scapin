MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Rescission of Memorandum for the Japanese Government SCAPIN 394, dated 6 December 1945

1. Reference is Memorandum for Japanese Government AG 122. 2 (6 Dec 45) ESS/FI, of Remittances and Collections since 15 August 1945.

2. The above referenced memorandum is hereby rescinded.

3. Rescission of the above referenced memorandum does not affect or invalidate the deposit of any related funds heretofore credited to SCAP Custody Account with the Bank of Japan.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ C. C. B. Warden
/t/ C. C. B. WARDEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General
