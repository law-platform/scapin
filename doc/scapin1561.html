GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (8 Mar 47) GA

(SCAPIN - 1561)

APO 500

8 March 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Italian Nationals.

1． Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file and subject as above (SCAPIN 1534) dated 19 February 1947.

2． The nominal rolls of Italian nationals to be repatriated in accordance with above reference are amended by aiding those named on inclosed supplemental list.

3． Current information indicates the SFSTRIERE will arrive Japan in March 1947.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
