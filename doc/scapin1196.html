GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.1 (12 Sep 46) CCS

(SCAPIN-1196)

APO 500

12 September 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Request from Oki Electric Company for Permission to Develop Time Division Pulse-Modulated, Multi-channel Telephone System.

1. Reference is made to memorandum of 15 August 1946 from the Ministry of Communications addressed to the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, requesting permission for the Oki Electric Co. to proceed with research on a multichannel telephone system involving time multiplex and pulse position modulation, as described in an attachment to this request.

2. Permission is granted as requested, subject to the provisions of SCAPIN 984, 25 May 1946, paragraphs 8c and 8e.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

12 September 1946

MEMORANDUM:

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN -1196), file AG 676.1 (12 Sep 46 )CCS, this Headquarters, dated 12 Sep 46 , subject: "Request from Oki Electric Company for Permission to Develop Time Division Pulse -Modulated, Multi-channel Telephone System".

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The present memorandum constitutes an interpretation of SCAPIN 984 and the proposed development is considered to fall within the limitations of this directive.

3. Surveillance will be effected by Occupation Force agencies in accordance with instructions contained in letter, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers to the Commanding General, Eighth Army, file AG 091.3 (25 May 1946)ESS, subject: "Surveillance of Research Activities".

R. G. HERSEY

Lt Col, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
