GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 602 (14 Jan 47) OGA

(SCAPIN-1464)

APO 500

14 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Evaluation of Military Installations Returned to the Japanese Government.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to furnish to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Attention: General Accounting Section, the following information pertaining to surrendered military installations released to Imperial Japanese Government.

a. The name, location, and yen value of each installation released to Imperial Japanese Government up to and through 31 January 1947. Properties will be evaluated upon the price level prevailing in August 1945,

b. For all subject installations released after 31 January, the Ministry of Finance, Bureau of State Property, will receive from Commanding General, Eighth Army or Commander of Naval Activities for Japan. General Accounting Section Forms Number 1, as amended pertaining to surrendered military installations released to Imperial Japanese Government. The Ministry of Finance, Bureau of State Property, will insert the yen value of the installation as of August 1945 in the column “estimated value in yen,” and return these forms to the Commanding General, Eighth Army, Attention Military Government Section, or Commander of Naval Activities for Japan.

2. Direct communication is authorized between General Accounting Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and Eighth Army, and between General Accounting Section and Commander of Naval Activities for Japan, and also between General Accounting Section and Ministry of Finance, Bureau of State Property, Imperial Japanese Government, to implement the provisions of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

APO 500

14 January 1947

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT : Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN 1464), file AG 602 (14 Jan 47)OGA, this Headquarters, dated 14 Jan 1947, subject: Accounting Procedures Pertaining to Surrendered Military Installations Released to Imperial Japanese Government

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The purpose of this memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government is to obtain name, location and yen values as of August 1945, for all military installations released to Imperial Japanese Government up to and including 31 January 1947, and also to notify the responsible agency, Ministry of Finance, Bureau of State Property, that after 31 January 1947, reports of released military installations will be submitted for the insertion of yen values as they are released by either Eighth Army or Naval Activities.

R. G. HERSEY

Lt. Col. AGD,

Asst Adj Gen.
