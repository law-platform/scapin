GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 561 (7 Dec 46) ESS/IN

(SCAPIN - 1382)

APO 500

7 December 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Steel Ship Construction

1. References are the following memoranda for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. File AG 561.4 (21 Apr 46) ESS/IN (SCAPIN-891), dated 21 April 1946, subject: Steel Ship Construction.

b. File AG 560 (23 Aug 46) ESS/IN (SCAPIN-1152), dated 23 August 1946, subject: Conversion Plan of Oil Tankers.

2. The memorandum referred to in paragraph 1a, above, authorized the completion of the 2TL8 Type 10,000 ton oil tanker by the Harima Shipbuilding Company. Reference memorandum, paragraph 1b, above, authorized the conversion of the vessel to a whale factory ship.

3. The authorization for conversion of the 10,000 ton type 2TL8 oil tanker given in paragraph 1b above, is cancelled for the present time. It is directed that the hull be completed as a tanker and held pending final disposition.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
for /t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, A.G.D. ,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1382)

ESS/IN JZR/GEM/ms

26 November 1946

1. Reference is Memo for IJG, SCAPIN 1152, dated 23 August 1945, subject: Conversion Plan of Oil Tankers, in which it was authorized to convert three 10,000 ton oil tankers to whaling processing ships.

2. Two of the above oil tankers have been converted and are at sea on the way to the Antarctic for the 1946-47 Japanese Whaling Expedition. At the time of the authorization for the conversion of the three tankers to whale factory ships, one of the vessels was scheduled to be completed in May of 1947 and was authorized in anticipation of the use of this vessel in either future Antarctic whaling expeditions or expeditions in local Japanese waters.

3. There is no certainty at the present time of any future Antarctic Whaling expeditions and it is not economically advisable to complete and convert this vessel as a whaling factory ship. Also a vessel of this type is not suitable in local Japanese waters.

4. Action taken: Memo to IJG directs the Japanese Government to complete the vessel as an oil tanker hull and hold pending final disposition.

5. Concurrences: N R/Fisheries (Captain Holland).

6. Routine surveillance is necessary on this activity and will be reported in weekly "Occupation Activities Reports".

J. Z. R.
