GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 230 (2 Feb 51) GA

(SCAPIN - 2145)

APO 500

9 March 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
SUBJECT :Foreign Nationals Employed by the United States Occupation Forces.

1. Reference: Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers Memorandum for Japanese Government, SCAPIN 2104, AG 400.12 (26 Jun 50) COM - B, Subject: “Control of Obligations and Expenditures of Funds Appropriated for Requirements of the Occupation Forces”, 26 June 1950.

2. Effective 1 April 1951, the continued employment of foreign nationals currently employed by the United States Occupation Forces and paid from the Funded Yen account, and foreign national employees thereafter recruited locally, will be by Labor Requisition upon the Japanese Government issued by the Commanding General, Japan Logistical Command.

3. All directives of the Supreme Comander for the Allied Powers pertaining to Japanese nationals employed by the Occupation Forces will be made applicable to these foreign nationals without prejudice due to race, religion, or national origin. The Japanese Government will be responsible for administering these foreign nationals in the same manner as Japanese nationals employed by the Occupation Foreces, and in so doing will become the “employer” of these individuals under existing Japanese laws.

4. No foreign national will incur a loss of salary or loss of seniority rights due to this transfer. The Special Procurement Agency will make necessary adjustments in the base salary, above present allowable maximums, of any foreign national whose new gross pay (salary, plus allowances) is less than his former gross pay.

5. A certified list of foreign nationals, including current salary rates, affected by this SCAPIN will be furnished to the Special Procurement Agency six days prior to above effective date by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

6. Direct communication between the appropriate agencies of the Japanese Government and the Economic and Scientific Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, concerning matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
