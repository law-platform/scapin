GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 471.86 (17 Jan 46) ESS/IE

(SCAPIN - 614)

APO 500

17 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Shipment of Explosives to Chingwantao, China.

1. It is directed that 34 tons of explosives, consisting of 800 cases dynamite and 150,000 pieces electric detonators be loaded on board the S/S EIJIN MARU at Miike, Japan. This vessel is expected to arrive at the above port on or about 20 January 1946, and upon completing loading of this cargo will be dispatched direct to Chingwantao.

2. You will advise this Headquarters when the cargo is actually loaded on board, date the vessel sailed and also furnish copy of receipt for items in paragraph 1.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
