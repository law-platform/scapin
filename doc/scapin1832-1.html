GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004.06 (16 Jan 48) GD

(SCAPIN - 1832/1)

APO 500

16 January 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Shortage of Japanese Laborers.

1. Reference your letter, C. L. O. No. 9917 (AG), subject as above, dated 30 December 1947, which forwarded initial report of progress required by paragraph 3, SCAPIN 1832.

2. The labor situation has not yet improved sufficiently, and further vigorous action is necessary, particularly in regard to the distribution of food and clothing rations to laborers. It is desired that such action be taken without delay.

3. An additional report of progress (in duplicate) is desired not later than 10 February 10 February 1946 and thereafter at ten day intervals until further advice from General Headquarters.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
