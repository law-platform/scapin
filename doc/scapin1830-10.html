GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.3 (6 Dec 47) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1830/10)

APO 500

10 November 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Japanese Overseas Radiotelephone Service.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government file AG 311.3 (6 Dec 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1830, dated 6 December 1947, subject same as above, as amended.

2. The division of revenue for calls between Japan and the third area of Argentina, Brazil, and Chile, as shown in inclosure 1 of SCAPIN 1830/3, is hereby amended to conform with inclosure 1 of this memorandum, effective from 1 October 1948. All other provisions for telephone service between Japan and the points named herein remain unchanged.

3. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Ministry of Communications, regarding operating and accounting procedures within the scope of this memorandum, is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

(1) Argentina:

Area I - No extension haul deduction.

Area II - Extension haul deductions of $.75 weekday and $.60 Sunday apply for service with the Provinces of Formosa, Chaco, Salta, Jujuy, Los Andes, Rucuman, Santiago del Estero, Catamarca, LaRioja, San Juan, Mendoza, Neuquen, Rio Negro, Chubut and Misiones.,

Area III - Extension haul deductions of $1.50 weekday and $1.20 Sunday apply for service with the Provinces of Santa Cruza and Tierra del Fuego.

(2) Brazil

Area I - No extension haul deduction.

Area II - Extension haul deductions of $.75 weekday and $.60 Sunday apply for service with the Provinces of Bahia, Goyaz, Matto Grosso, Parana, Santa Catharina, Rio Grande do Sul, Sergipe and Alagoas.

Area III - Extension haul deductions of $1.50 weekday and $1.20 Sunday apply for service with the Provinces of Acre, Amazonas, Para, Maranhao, Piauhy, Ceara, Rio Grande de Norte, Parahyba, and Pernambuco.

(3) Chile:

Area I - No extension haul deduction.

Area II - Extension haul deductions of $.75 weekday and $.60 Sunday apply for service with the Provinces of Antofagasta, Valdivia, Osorno, Llanquihue, Chiloe, and Aysen.

Area III - Extension haul deductions of $1.20 weekday and $90 Sunday apply for service with the Provinces of Tarapaca and Magallanes.
