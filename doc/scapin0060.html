OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.3 (25 Sep 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 60)

25 September 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Provision of Coal at Fusan, Korea for Use of Commanding General, U. S. Forces in Korea.

1. It is directed that necessary action be taken to institute at once measures that will insure monthly delivery at Fusan, Korea of 70,000 to 75,000 tons of coal for use in operating the Korean railways. Coal will be considered to the Commanding General, U. S. Forces in Korea.

2. Delivery will be initiated at the earliest possible date. In order to secure earliest delivery at Fusan, you will consider diversion of ships with suitable cargo now onroute to other destinations, as well as ships now loading. You will apply to Fleet Liaison Officer, SCAP for all necessary modifications of present schedules and for new schedules required.

3. You will inform this office of the first date of delivery and in addition render weekly reports of tonnages delivered under this requisition.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ Harold Fair.
/t/ HAROLD FAIR,
Lt. Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General.
