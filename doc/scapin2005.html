GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 601 (29 Jan 49) DS

(SCAPIN-2005)

APO 500

14 May 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Acquisition of Real Property by Foreign Governments

1. Reference: Circular 9, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, 14 May 1949, copy attached.

2. The above reference permits and sets forth the conditions under which foreign governments may acquire title to or lease real property in Japan.

3. It is desired that the Japanese Government implement the principles set forth in above reference in the following ways:

a. Take the necessary legal steps to assure foreign governments fair treatment in the acquisition and enjoyment of real property.

b. Receive from foreign governments, through General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, applications to acquire title to or lease real property and recommend to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers whether such acquisition is considered acceptable in view of the intended use of the property and the extreme scarcity of available real property in Japan.

c. Reply, through General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, to requests of foreign governments concerning purchase of real property in Japan.

d. Decide, subject to review by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, whether to approve applications by foreign governments for validation of acquisitions of title to or lease of real property in Japan from Japanese nationals and Japanese Government agencies.

e. The Japanese Government, when validating transactions referred to in paragraph 3d above, will state that the acquisition of property thereby validated.

(1) Is considered acceptable in the light of criteria mentioned in paragraph 3b above;

(2) Is not taking place under conditions of fraud, duress, or undue influence assignable in any way to the Occupation, and

(3) In no way constitutes a claim for remittance of foreign exchange or goods outside Japan beyond that authorized by applicable Japanese laws or regulations of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

f. When title to real property is to be acquired by a foreign government and the property is privately owned, procedures should be established whereunder once the private owner is willing to sell and the price has been agreed upon, the Japanese Government will then acquire title to the property from the private owner and transfer title to the foreign government.

g. It is desired that the Japanese Government make the necessary arrangements for compensating sellers or lessors of property in yen in the amount of the agreed purchase price. The Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers will make appropriate disposition of foreign exchange instruments, goods, or services received from the foreign government as its part of the purchase agreement.

4. Direct communication between the appropriate agencies of the Japanese Government and the Economic and Scientific Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, is authorized for the implementation of this directive.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
