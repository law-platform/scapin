GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 400.16 (14 Aug 49) ESS/FTC

(SCAPIN-2061)

APO 500

5 December 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
Subject :Marking of Export Articles

1. References are following Memoranda for Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. AG 400.16 (20 Feb 47) ESS/FT, SCAPIN 1535, 20 February 1947, subject: Marking of Export Articles;

b. AG 072 (9 Sep 49) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 2042, 9 September 1949, subject: Trade-Marks, Trade Names, and Marking of Merchandise in Japan.

2. Reference la. is hereby rescinded.

3. Except as permitted in paragraph 4, all articles prepared for export, the immediate container thereof and the outside package will be marked, stamped, branded or labeled legible English with the words “Made in Occupied Japan,” “Made in Japan” or “Japan.”

4. The above requirement need not be fulfilled in those cases where buyer requests omission thereof and certifies in the contract with the seller that such marking, stamping, branding or labeling is not required by country of destination.

5. All required marking, stamping, branding or labeling shall be made in a conspicuous place and shall be as nearly indelible and permanent as the nature of the article will permit.

6. The requirements listed in paragraphs 3 and 5 are in addition to those set forth in paragraph 8 of reference 1 b., which provides that the Japanese Government take positive steps to:

a. Assure that merchandise manufactured in and/or exported from Japan is not marked or advertised so as to suggest that it is made or processed elsewhere than in Japan.

b Assure that goods made in or exported from Japan are not so marked or otherwise described as to convey a false impression of their quantity, quality or content.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.Bush
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2061)

FEP/JMC/SMM/cu

18 November 1949

1. SCAPIN 1535, 20 February 1947, subject: Marking of Export Articles requires that all exports be marked in legible English with the words "Made in Occupied Japan."

2. Reputable trading houses have stressed that the mandatory requirement of SCAPIN 1535 mitigates against marketing of Japanese goods.

3. DA radio, W 89781, 9 June 1949, advised that FEC Steering Committee was then considering a paper which included the provisions that "steps should also be taken to assure that wherever it is practicable to do so goods which are produced in Japan and exported are clearly marked to show their Japanese origin." (Tab A).

4. Reply to DA radio, W 89781 was made in C-50847, 18 June 1949, to the effect that SCAPIN 1535 already required the marking suggested which was impeding sales of Japanese exports and that numerous requests had been received from buyers to exempt Japanese exports from the marking provision in such cases as goods were destined for a country which did not require such markings. It was further stated that it appeared reasonable to permit exemptions requested if buyers would clarify that goods were destined for countries not requiring such markings and that buyers did not desire such markings. Recommendations were made accordingly and requisite revision in language was submitted. (Tab B).

5. DA radio, W 90593, 25 June 1949, requested that action on radio C-50847 be deferred. (Tab C).

3. DA radio, W 92834, 14 August 1949, included the following paragraphs re marking:

"Steps should be taken to assure that merchandise manufactured in Japan should not be marked or advertised so as to suggest that it is made elsewhere than in Japan."

7. The proposed SCAPIN incorporates the above paragraphs of DA radio W92834, 14 August 1949. The subject paragraphs are included in SCAPIN 2042 dated 9 September 1949.

8. Answer to W 92834, was made in C-52071, 20 August 1949, that wording quoted in paragraph 6 above was interpreted to mean that there was no objection to amending SCAPIN 1535 in the manner recommended in C-50847 and that such amendment would be affected unless the DA objected. (Tab E).

9. Since dispatch of C-52071 on 20 August 1949, no objection has been raised and the appended amended. SCAPIN is therefore submitted for approval.

10. Concurrences: ESS/TEX; ESS/ST

F. E. P.
