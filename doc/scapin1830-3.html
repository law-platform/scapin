AG 311.3 (24 Mar 48) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1830/3)

APO 500

24 March 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Japanese Overseas Radiotelephone Service.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government file AG 311.3 (6 Dec 47)CCS SCAPIN 1830, dated 6 December 1947, subject same as above, as amended.

2. Radiotelephone calls between Japan and the points listed in inclosure 1 attached hereto, via the United States, are authorized, effective 1 April 1948.

3. All provisions of SCAPIN 1830/2 applicable to calls between Japan and the points listed in inclosure 1 thereto will apply equally to calls between Japan and the points listed in inclosure 1 of this memorandum.

4. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section or the General Accounting Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Communications, regarding operating and accounting procedures within the scope, of this memorandum, is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

RATES AND DIVISION OF REVENUE FOR RADIOTELEPHONE SERVICE BETWEEN JAPAN AND OTHER COUNTRIES VIA THE UNITED STATES

The rate for each minute or fraction thereof of overtime will be one-third of the above Initial Period Rates and one-third of the above amounts under Division of Revenue will accrue to each carrier.

The report charge will be $.75 and will be divided in the same manner as the revenue remaining after deductions for extension hauls.

(1) Argentina:

Area I - No extension haul deduction.

Area II - Extension haul deductions of $.75 weekday and $.60 Sunday apply for service with the Provinces of Formosa, Chaco, Salta, Jujuy, Los Andes, Tucuman, Santiago del Estero, Catamarca, LaRioja, San Juan, Mendoza, Neuquen, Rio Negro, Chubut and Misiones.,

Area III - Extension haul deductions of $1.50 weekday and $1.20 Sunday apply for service with the Provinces of Santa Cruza and Tierra del Fuego.

(2) Brazil

Area I - No extension haul deduction.

Area II - Extension haul deductions of $.75 weekday and $.60 Sunday apply for service with the Provinces of Bahia, Goyaz, Matto Grosso, Parana, Santa Catharina, Rio Grande do Sul, Sergipe and Alagoas.

Area III - Extension haul deductions of $1.50 weekday and $1.20 Sunday apply for service with the Provinces of Acre, Amazonas, Para, Maranhao, Piauhy, Ceara, Rio Grande de Morte, Parahyba, and Pernambuco.

(3) Chile:

Area I - No extension haul deduction.

Area II - Extension haul deductions of $.75 weekday and $.60 Sunday apply for service with the Provinces of Antofagasta, Valdivia, Osorno, Llanquihue, Chiloe, and Aysen.

Area III - Extension haul deductions of $1.50 weekday and $1.20 Sunday apply for service with the Provinces of Tarapaca and Nagallanes.
