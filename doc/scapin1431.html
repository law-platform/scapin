GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 451.1 (31 Dec 46) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN - 1431)

APO 500

31 December 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Restitution of International Catholic Hospital's (Shadan Hojin Yamato Hoshikai) Property Wrongfully Transferred.

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386.3 (6 May 46) CPC, SCAPIN 926, 6 May 1946, subject, "Procedure for Returning Property in Japan to Nationals of the United Nations", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386.3 (22 Nov 46) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1354, 22 November 1946, subject, "Restitution of United Nations Nationals' Property Wrongfully Transferred", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

c. Memorandum to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, C.L.O. No, 4484 (PK), 5 September 1946, subject, "Automobile Formerly Belonging to International Catholic Hospital", from Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to take possession of one (1) Ford, Baby, Type C, 1937 model, license No. 7803, by 6 January 1947 preparatory, to restoring the automobile to the International Catholic Hospital (Shadan Hojin Yamato Hoshikai) who owned the property on 7 December 1941.

3. It is further directed that the Imperial Japanese Government will return the automobile to International Catholic Hospital at 670, 2 chome, Shimo-Ochiai, Yodobashi-ku, Tokyo on 6 January 1947 at 1400 hours, under the conditions set forth in "Receipt for Restitution to United Nation National of Wrongfully Transferred Property in Japan", and under the following conditions:

a. A general and a final report by the Japanese Government on the management of the property from 7 December 1941 to the date of restoration, said report to include statement of all financial transactions, will be attached to each copy of the receipt;

b. Receipt will contain a statement as to the condition of the automobile at the time of restoration;

c. Cash and deposits covered by the receipt will be broken down into:

(1) Net proceeds from sale of property (if none so state),

(2) Net proceeds from other transactions (if none so state),

(3) Other (if none, so state).

d. Transfer of title will be made at the District Court having jurisdiction.

4. Four copies of accomplished receipt for the property with documentary evidence of the transfer of title will be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers within ten (10) days of completion of delivery. Receipts and evidence of transfer of title will be submitted in both English and Japanese text with the Japanese translation certified as correct.

5. Copy of this Memorandum will be attached to each copy of the receipts.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
