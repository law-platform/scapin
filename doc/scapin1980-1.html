GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.3 (8 Mar 49) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1980/1)

APO 500

21 April 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Equalization of Radiotelegraph Rates.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.3 (8 Mar 49)CCS, SCAPIN 1980, dated 8 March 1949, subject: Equalization of Radiotelegraph Rates.

2. So much of pages 1 and 3 of inclosure 1 to SCAPIN 1980 pertaining to Press Wireless, Inc.; VLT rates between Japan and "Other Offices U. S. A." is amended as follows:

FROM: ALL POINTS-IN THE UNITED STATES

TO: ALL POINTS IN JAPAN

VLT Thru Rate Japan Radio Press Wireless Outpayment Other Line Share

Other Offices U.S.A. $0.13-1/3 $0.06-2/3 $0.06-2/3

FROM: JAPAN, TOKYO ONLY

TO: UNITED STATES

VLT Thru Rate Japan Radio Press Wireless Outpayment Other Line Share

Other Offices U.S.A. $0.15-1/3 $0.06-2/3 $0.06-2/3 $0.02

3. Direct communication between Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Communications, regarding matters within the scope of this memorandum, is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
