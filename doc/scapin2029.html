(SCAPIN - 2029)

APO 500

18 July 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :International Postal Service for the Ryukyus.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.1 (14 May 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1673, dated 14 May 1947, subject: Establishment of International Mail Service for the Ryukyu Islands.

2. Reference memorandum is hereby rescinded, effective on receipt of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

MEMORANDUM

18 July 1949

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number SCAPIN 2029, file AG 311.1 (14 May 47)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 18 July 1949, subject: International Postal Service for the Ryukyus

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. SCAPIN 1673 was issued upon resumption of International Postal Service by the Ryukyus, and before Japan formally adhered to the Universal Postal Union and Agreements. The Japanese Government was directed to accept and transship any mail received at ports in Japan, addressed to the Ryukyus.

3. Since that time, Japan has been restored to fall membership in the UPU and all matters covered by SCAPIN 1673 are now binding upon Japan under UPU Convention Agreements. The SGAPIN is, therefore, no longer necessary.

R M. LEVY

Colonel, AGD

Adjutant General

DISTRIBUTION

Same as SCAPIN 2029,
