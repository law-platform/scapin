GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 352 (19 Jan 46) ESS-ST

(SCAPIN - 626)

APO 500

19 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Operation of Electrotechnical Laboratory

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. No. 946 (3.3) dated 8 December 1945 and directive this Headquarters AG 352 (24 Dec 45). Subj: Operation of Electrotechnical Laboratory, 24 December 1945.

2. Paragraph 3 of the above directive is amended to add the following:

"g. Radio and radar aids to navigation."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
