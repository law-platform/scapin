SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.31 (1 Apr 49) ESS/PF

(SCAPIN - 1988/2)

APO 500

9 June 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Dried Skim Milk for Nursery Feeding Program

1. Reference SCAPIN 1988/1, dated 8 May 1950, subject: Dried Skim Milk for School Lunch Program.

2. In addition to the use of the dried skim milk for school lunch program as provided in SCAPIN referenced above, the purpose for use of the milk shall be expanded to include the Nursery Feeding Program. Procedure for accomplishing the new program will be the same as outlined in paragraph 3, SCAPIN 1988/1, substituting the Ministry of Welfare for the Ministry of Education.

3. Total funds to be made available from the Counterpart Fund remain unchanged.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ E. C. Gault
/t/ E. C. GAULT,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Acting Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1988/2)

EMR/ECH/ATM/ftk

31 May 1950

1. The attached SCAPIN expands the use of dried skim milk imported with foreign exchange purchased with Counterpart Funds to include the Japanese Nursery Feeding Program. At the time SCAPIN 1988/1 was issued this purpose was inadvertently omitted.

2. Funds to be made available from the Counterpart Fund not to exceed the total amount of ¥1,350,000,000 as provided in SCAPIN 1988/1 was based on a milk tonnage import program approved by Washington that included both the school lunch and nursery feeding program. In order to clearly indicate by directive SCAP intentions regarding the use of the milk in question as the total program affects Counterpart Fund utilization policy and as it is necessary from the Japanese Government legal standpoint for implementation, it is considered desirable that subject SCAPIN be issued.

3. Concurrences: PH&W C. F. Sams Brig. Gen.

ESS/PD F. L. Whittington

ESS/DF __________________

Mr. Middleton, 26-6155
