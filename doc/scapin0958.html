GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (16 May 46) DS

(SCAPIN - 958)

APO 500

16 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Information Concerning Acts of American Nationals and Certain Japanese Legal Procedures.

1. The following will be supplied by the Imperial Japanese Government:

a. Two copies of an alphabetically arranged list of the full names of all persons who were born in the United States or Hawaii or who in some other way are believed to have had American citizenship and who since March 15, 1899 have applied for permission to acquire Japanese nationality, whether under the process known as “Kika” or that known as “Kaifuku”. The following data should be given concerning each person listed:

(1) Date and place of birth.

(2) Whether the application was for “Kika” or “Kaifuku”.

(3) Date of the application.

(4) Nature of action taken on the application and the date thereof.

(5) Last known address and the date thereof.

b. Two copies of an alphabetically arranged list of the full names of all persons who were born in the United States or Hawaii or who in some other way are believed to have had American citizenship and who since January 13, 1941, have entered or served in the Armed Forces of Japan. The following data should be given concerning each person listed:

(1) Date and place of birth.

(2) Branch or arm of the Armed Forces entered.

(3) Dates of entry and of discharge.

(4) Last known address and the date thereof.

c. Two copies of an alphabetically arranged list of the full names of all persons who were born in the United States or Hawaii or who, in some other way, are believed to have had American citizenship and who since January 13, 1941, have accepted, or performed the duties of, any office, post or employment under the Japanese Government, or any political subdivision thereof (including the prefectural and municipal governments). The following data should be given concerning each person listed, with particular attention to item (3):

(1) Date and place of birth.

(2) Title and description of position held.

(3) Whether or not the position was one for which, under Japanese law or regulations, only a Japanese national was eligible.

(4) Dates on which the employment was begun and terminated.

(5) Last known address and the date thereof.

d. A statement as to whether or not at the time of entering the Japanese Armed Forces or of acquiring Japanese nationality or of being employed by the Japanese Government or at any other time a person is ever required to take an oath or make an affirmation or other formal declaration of allegiance to the Japanese State or to the Emperor, and, if so, the exact nature of the oath or affirmation.

e. A brief summary of Japanese laws and regulations concerning the nationality requirements of persons who serve in any capacity in the Japanese Government or political subdivisions thereof (including the prefectural and municipal governments).

2. Appended to each of the above described lists should be a statement that the list completely covers the category or, if not, to what extent it has been impossible to complete the list and the reasons therefor.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
