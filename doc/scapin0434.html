GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 319.1 (13 Dec 45) ESS/R

(SCAPIN-434)

APO 500

13 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Weekly Statistical Data.

1. In order that this Headquarters may be provided with current details on economic conditions in Japan, it is directed that the following agencies which now prepare statistics on a monthly or other basis, shall hereafter make statistics in the fields specified available to this Headquarters on a weekly basis as well as monthly:

a. Bank of Japan: (1) Wholesale and retail price indexes, (2) Bank note issue.

b. Bureau of Statistics, Imperial Cabinet: (1) Wage index, (2) Employment index, (3) Cost of living index.

c. Ministry of Commerce and Industry: (1) KW electric power consumed, classified as hydrogenerated power and thermal generated power, (2) Industrial production index for industries to be specified by this Headquarters.

d. Bureau of Reconstruction: (1) Number and sizes of buildings constructed, classified as to homes or business construction.

e. Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry: (1) Consignment of foodstuffs to Tokyo and Osaka markets.

f. Ministry of Finance (Tokyo clearing house): (1) Bank deposits.

g. Ministry of Communications: (1) Railway freight traffic in tonnage, miles.

h. Ministry of Welfare: (1) Unemployment index.

i. Home Ministry: (1) Strikes index, (2) Tenancy disputes index, (3) Amusements index (paid admissions and Yen value), (4) Black market price index for selected commodities.

j. Japan Warehousing Association: (1) Warehousing index.

k. Department Stores Association: (1) Volume of sales index.

2. Specific instructions will be furnished to representatives of each of the agencies referred to above, regarding details of the required statistics. The weekly statistics referred to above will be made available beginning with the week 1 January 1946 to 7 January 1946.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
