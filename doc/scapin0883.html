GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 140 (18 April 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-883)

APO 500

18 April 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Exempting Accounts of Deposit of Certain Individuals from the Terms of Emergency Measures Imperial Ordinance.

1. Reference is made to Financial Measures Imperial Ordinance No. 83

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to exempt from the terms of reference ordinance the accounts of deposit of those parsons, of non-Japanese nationality, who were imprisoned or whose property was taken, blocked or sold by the Japanese Government or agency thereof between 7 December 1941 and 2 September 1945 because of their being citizens or subjects of countries at war with Japan, or sympathizers with such citizens, subjects or countries.

3. Direct communication between Finance Ministry Bank of Japan and interested Staff Sections of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers is authorized to implement this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 883)

WFM/CWM/jpl

18 April 1946

1. There are at present certain nationals in Japan whose property or persons were put under restraint by the Japanese during the recent war. The majority of these persons are nationals of the United States, Great Britain and the Netherlands.

2. In accordance with Japanese Enemy Property Control Law, issued 23 December 1941, property of nationals of the United States, Great Britain and the Netherlands was seized and sold by the Japanese Government during the war and these nationals were themselves interned.

3. In addition to loss of their property and restraint of their persons, these nationals were deprived of the opportunity of engaging in business or otherwise acquiring assets during the period of the War. Many of them have also suffered personal hardships and indignities at the hands of the Japanese, as a result of which they may presently be in poor physical condition.

4. These nationals, having lost their property and the opportunity of acquiring additional assets during the period of the war, facing the problem of rehabilitating themselves, are also confronted with the restrictions of the Emergency Financial Measures Imperial Ordinances.

5. In view of past hardships, as well as present difficulties, it appears that this group should be exempted from the terms of the Emergency Financial Measures Imperial Ordinances.

6. The interests of USAFIK have been considered. This action does not affect the Korean area.

7. Concurrences: G-2 (W.A.B.) Colonel W. A. Buck

PH&W (G.K.W.) Major G.K. Wyman

Govt Sec. (C.W.) Colonel Robinson for Brig. Gen. C. WHITNEY

8. No surveillance required by occupation forces of this directive.

C. F. T.
