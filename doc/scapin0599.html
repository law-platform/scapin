GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123 (15 Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-599)

APO 500

15 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Restriction on Dividend Payments.

1. Reference is made to letters Ministry of Finance LO 63, 10 December 1945, and LO 103, 21 December 1945, Subject: “Lifting the Restriction on Dividend Payments”.

2. Dividends will be paid only from earnings of the current period.

3. Dividends will not exceed 5% per annum if the dividend paying concern:

a. Has any claims against the Japanese Government for war insurance or Government indemnity, or,

b. Is a restricted concern listed in the inclosure to AG 004 (8 Dec 45) ESS subject: “Establishment of a Schedule of Restricted Concerns”.

4. Dividends will not be paid by the institutions mentioned in paragraph 2 of Ministry of Finance letter LO 103, 21 December 1945.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
