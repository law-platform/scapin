GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 130 (12 Jan 1946) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-586)

APO 500

12 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Borrowing by Government Agencies.

1. Reference is made to memorandum of the Minister of Finance dated 21 December 1945 (LO 110), subject: “Borrowing by Certain Government Agencies and Instrumentalities”, requesting approval for four govetnment-controlled agencies to borrow sums aggregating ¥260,000,000 from the Deposit Funds Section.

2. The request for the Shipping Management Association to borrow ¥ 120,000,000 for payment of its expenses in the operation of merchant shipping is disapproved. This loan is in reality a government subsidy and as such should be paid directly from government appropriations. A direct government grant from the General Account to the Shipping Management Association is authorized for this purpose, in the amount of ¥120,000,000 for December, 1945, and such amounts as may be found necessary for the period January through March 1946.

3. The request for the Overseas Nationals Relief Association to borrow ¥60,000,000 for relief payments to families in Japan of nationals overseas is disapproved. This proposal is not consistent with the memorandum of this Headquarters, dated 8 December 1945, subject: “Relief and Welfare Plans”, (AG 044 (8 Dec 45) GD), which required preparation of one comprehensive plan for relief of all needy persons in Japan during the period January-June 1946. Relief payments or assistance to needy dependents of Japanese nationals overseas will be furnished by the Minister of Public Welfare from general government funds on the same basis as for all other needy persons in Japan.

4. The request for the Housing Bureau to borrow ¥43,000,000 for continuing its program of emergency housing is approved.

5. The request for the Central Insurance Corporation to borrow ¥37,000,000 for payment of interest on past borrowings is disapproved. This is another example of a loan which is in reality a government subsidy. A direct government grant from the General Account to the Central Insurance Corporation is authorized for this purpose to the extent that such interest payments represent interest actually paid or credited through 31 December 1945, to holders of blocked accounts resulting from insurance payments. No interest will be paid or credited to holders of such blocked accounts after 31 December 1945, without prior authorization by this Headquaters.

/s/ W.F. Marguat
/t/ W.F.MARQAT
Major General, U.S.Army
Chief, Econ. & Sc. Sect.
