MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1120)

JZR/GEM/aeb

7 August 1946

1. Reference is C.L.O. No. 3599 (CM) with Inclosure 1, Table No. 2, Construction Plan No. 4 (a) for 211 steel fishing vessels and Inclosure 2 Table No. 3, Construction Plan No. 3 (a) for 17 wooden fishing vessels, dated 23 July 1946, subject: ’’Construction Plan of Fishing Vessels, ” requesting permission to construct the steel and wooden fishing vessels listed on Inclosures 1 and 2.

2. The Division of Fisheries has approved the need of 35, 244 steel and wooden fishing vessels with a total gross tonnage of 337, 087 tons to implement the national food situation in Japan. In the undertaking of this construction program, the Imperial Japanese Government is submitting applications as plans No. 1, 2, 3, 4, etc. Plan No. 1 consisted of 418 steel fishing boats with a total gross tonnage of 48,532 tons and was approved by SCAPIN-949, dated 13 May 1946. Plan No. 2 (a) has not been approved to date. Plan No. 3 (a) and 4 (a) of the reference letter in the above paragraph total a gross tonnage of 26,830 tons. Plan No. 2 (b) is now under consideration for approval and totals 5, 250 gross tons. This makes a total of 80,592 gross tons approved and under consideration for approval of the 337,087 gross tons recommended by the Division of Fisheries.

3. This Memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government and the Command Letter to the Eighth Army authorizes the construction of the 211 steel fishing vessels and the 17 wooden fishing vessels.

4. Routine surveillance is necessary on this activity and will be reported in normal weekly ’’Occupation Activities Reports. ”

5. Concurrence: G-4 (Colonel Breden).

Natural Resources (Capt. Broadbent).

J. Z. R.
