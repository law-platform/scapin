AG 000.5 (1 Dec 45) LS

(SCAPIN-374)

1 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

1. The Following named Japanese are alleged to have committed atrocities and offenses against persons of United Nations Nationals, while confined in Prisoner of War Camps, Internment Camps or Hospitals in Japan. These persons will be apprehended as suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, XI Corps, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest practicable date.

2. The Following named Japanese are alleged to have committed atrocities and offenses against persons of United Nations Nationals, while confined in Prisoner of War Camps, Internment Camps or Hospitals in Korea. Headquarters of 17th Japanese Army reports these parsons to have returned to Japan and to be residing at the address indicated. These persons will be apprehended as suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, XI Corps at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest practicable date.

NAME RANK ADDRESS

MIZOGUCHI, Yasutoshi 2nd Lt. c/o Shunko Hikida, Nakano-Cho.

Zeze, Otsu-Shi, Shiga, Prefecture

IIZUKA, Koza Sgt. Major Ibaragi Prefecture

(Full Address Unknown)

DOND▲, Masaji Sergeant Chiraki-Cho, Fukushima-Ku, Osaka.

SUITA, Kono▲ye Corporal Osaka (Full Address Unknown).

3. The Following named Japanese are alleged to have committed atrocities and offenses against persons of United Nations Nationals, while being transported aboard Japanese Prisoner of War ship ORYOKU MARU between the Philippine Islands and Moji, Japan on the date indicated. These persons will be apprehended as suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, XI Corps at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest practicable date.

NAME RANK POSITION

TOSHINO, 2nd Lt. Commanding Officer of Japanese Army Personel,

Dec 1944-Jan 1945.

WADA, Civilian Interpreter, Dec 1944- Jan 1945.

4. The Following named Japanese, reported to be presently in Japan, is alleged to have committed atrocities and offenses against persons of United Nations Nationals, while acting in the capacity of interpreter for the Japanese Consular General in Manila. This Japanese will be apprehended as suspected War Criminal and delivered to the Commanding General, XI Corps at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest practicable date.

NAME

OKASAKI, Kumao

5. The Following named Japanese, reported to be presently in Japan, is alleged to have committed atrocities and offenses against persons of United Nations Nationals while acting in the capacity of an Interrogator with the Japanese Military Police attached to Fort Santiago, Philippine Islands. The Japanese will be apprehended as suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, XI Corps at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest practicable date.

NAME RANK ADDRESS

TOJO, Tokio Sergeant Chiba-Ken

6. The Following named Japanese are alleged to have committed atrocities and offenses against persons of United Nations Nationals while acting in their official capacity as officials in the Imperial Japanese Army. These persons will be apprehended as suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, XI Corps at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest practicable date.

NAME RANK POSITION OR ADDRESS
