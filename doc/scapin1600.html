GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 423 (4 Apr 47) ESS/TD

(SCAPIN-1600)

APO 500

4 April 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Rayon Textile Capacity.

1. Reference is made to C. L. O. Memorandum No. 5505 (E. C. I.), dated 18 October 1946, which transmits the Three Year Textile Rehabilitation Plan.

2. Authorization is hereby given for the rebuilding of the rayon industry to an annual capacity of 150,000 metric tons of filament yarn and staple fiber.

3. This authorization is not to be construed as granting priorities on fuel, power, steel, or other materials. Allocations of materials for this reconstruction work will be made as directed by appropriate agencies of the Imperial Japanese Government.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1600)

ESS/TD HST/SCW/mb

7 March 1947

1. BASIC: Check Sheet to ESS/Chief 423 (7 March 1947) ESS/TD, dated 7 March 1947, subject, Rayon Program.

2. This check sheet requests approval of Staff Study entitled ''Rayon Program for Japan", dated 28 February 1947. This study contemplates rebuilding of the rayon industry of Japan to a rated capacity of 150,000 metric tons a year, as rapidly as the capacity of the machinery manufacturing industry, and in turn the availability of raw materials, makes possible. The rate of expansion would also be determined by the availability of pulp, coal, and chemicals necessary for the manufacture of rayon.

3. This program emphasizes the proposed distribution of the production of the recommended capacity. The program points out that production of 150,000 metric tons of rayon staple fiber a year would provide a normal level of domestic consumption and a volume of exports which it is anticipated should be realized without difficulty.

4. Concurrences: ESS/PCR (Mr. Kirshenbaum); ESS/FT (Major E. L. Atkins); ESS/IN (Mr. R. Steele);

ESS/AC (Mr. P.W. Byall); ESS/RS (Mr. Emerson Ross); NRS/Forestry (Mr. Murdock)

H. S. T.
