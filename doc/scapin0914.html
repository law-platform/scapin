GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 383.6 (2 May 46) CIS

(SCAPIN - 914)

APO 500

2 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Release of Internees.

1. The following persons, incarcerated in Sugamo Prison on 7 March 1946, were released unconditionally 28 April 1945:

BABA, Masaharu

HONDA, Yutaka

KIKUCHI, Yoshio

HIRAYAMA, Katsutoshi

NAGATA, Yiroshi

SAKAKIBARA, Minoru

2. The Imperial Japanese Government will therefore make the proper notations in its records.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
