27 July 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Cancellation of Authorizations for Construction of Steel Ships

1. References are the following Memoranda to the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, SCAP:

a. AG 560 (13 May 46) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 949, 13 May 1946, subject: Authorization for Construction of Steel Fishing Vessels.

b. AG 560 (10 Aug 46) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 1120, 10 August 1946, subject: Construction Plan of Fishing Vessels.

c. AG 560 (22 Nov 46) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 1356, 22 November 1946, subject: Construction Plan of Fishing Vessels.

d. AG 561 (26 June 47) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 1739, 26 June 1947, subject: Request for Authorization for Changes in Construction Program of Fishing Vessels.

e. AG 561 (14 July 47) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 1745, 14 July 1947, subject: Construction Plan of Steel Fishing Vessels.

f. AG 561 (24 Oct 47) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 1807, 24 October 1947, subject: Change in Construction Program of Fishing Vessels.

g. AG 561 (20 Nov 47) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 1824, 20 Nov 1947, subject: Application for Permission to Construct Steel Fishing Vessels.

h. AG 561.4 (20 Aug 46) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 2033-A, 20 Aug 1946. subject: Authorization for Change in Program of Steel Ship Construction.

2. Authorization granted by reference memoranda for construction of steel vessels listed on attached inclosure 1 is hereby cancelled.

3. Construction work on subject vessels will not be started or resumed unless future authorization for such work is granted.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ A. J. Rehe
for /t/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2033)

GEM/EPS/lp

8 July 49

1. Ref. are Memoranda to the Japanese Government, SCAPIN No. 949, 1120, 1356, 1739, 1807, 1824 and 2033-A, which authorize construction of steel ships.

2. Investigation reveals that thirty-five (35) of the vessels authorized for construction by reference SCAPINS either have not been started or construction work has been stopped on them bacause originally contemplated owners have been unable to secure funds sufficient to cover construction costs and the shipyards concerned have not been able to find new owners.

3. In order to have cognizance at all times of the number of vessels actually under construction and thereby be able to plan and maintain a balanced fleet, it is believed advisable to cancel authorizations for construction of these thirty-five (35) vessels and consider applications submitted for new authorizations if and when buyers are found who are able to finance such construction.

4. Memo to the Japanese Government cancels authorizations for construction of subject vessels and directs that construction work on them will not be started or resumed unless future authorization is granted.

5. This is a technical matter over which ESS exercises supervision by direct communication.

6. Concurrence: NRS - Mr. W. C. Neville (C/N 5 Jul 49)

E. M. Early, 25-8954

S. W. V.
