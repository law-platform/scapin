GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 413 (2 Dec 48) CPC/PLD

(SCAPIN - 1946/1)

APO 500

29 April 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Centralization of Looted Machinery.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 413 (2 Dec 48) CPC/LGP, SCAPIN 1946, 2 December 1948, subject as above, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Inclosed are lists of looted machines under custody of the Japanese Government and control of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers which are unidentified as to area of seizure. The Japanese Government is directed to concentrate the machinery listed in inclosure 1 in the Kurihama Looted Property Warehouse and that listed in inclosure 2 in the Osaka Arsenal Looted Property Warehouse.

3. The instructions outlined in reference above will be followed when centralizing the inclosed lists of property. Inclosed are necessary L-3 Inter Warehouse Transfer forms.

4. A report that the actions directed above have been completed, together with a list of the machinery which has been moved into the designated warehouses in compliance with this directive will be submitted to General Headquarters Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers within thirty (30) days from date of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

12 Incls (For addressee only)

1. Machinery to be Centralized in the Kurihama L/P Whse (in dup)

2. Machinery to be Centralized in Osaka Arsenal L/P Whse (in dup)

3-12. L-3 Transfer Form Nos. 00257 through 00266 (in quad)
