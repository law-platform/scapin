GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (27 Aug 46) GB/CIS

(SCAPIN-1158)

APO 500

27 August 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Sadajiro KATSURA, Japanese National, for Chinese Government.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will immediately apprehend and deliver to the Commandant, Sugamo Prison for incarceration, the following person formerly counselor of the puppet "Manchoukuo" embassy in Tokyo;

KATSURA, Sadajiro.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government will advise General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, when the above individual has been delivered to the authorities of Sugamo Prison. In the event the Japanese Government is unable to make delivery of this individual by 2400 hours, 5 September 1946, it will make a report in detail giving the present location of the individual, and the reason for failure to arrest.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
