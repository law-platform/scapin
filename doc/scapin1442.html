GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (7 Jan 47) GC

(SCAPIN - 1442)

APO 500

7 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Japanese Criminals from Korea.

1. Fifteen (15) Japanese criminals now imprisoned in Korea will be repatriated on a ship to be designated in the near future. Court and prison records will accompany each prisoner.

2. Names of prisoners and offenses follow:

Name Offense

Bunichi Tominaga Embezzlement and forgery of public papers.

Norihito Atsuchi Bribery

Yuzo Nogaski Embezzlement and destruction of public papers.

Jiro Saito Embezzlement and destruction of public papers.

Hishachi Harata Homicide

Yamata Suyeaki Theft

Nobura Yossiaka Theft

Hishihara Katsuyoshi Theft

Nakayama Ichiro Theft

Kokechi Hoyashi Swindling

Kutsu Yubala Forgery and fraud

Tsutsumi Fusachi Embezzlement

Yamamoto Kozo Embezzlement

Araka Sijeo Violation of proclamation

Narita Yoshi Stealing

3. The Imperial Japanese Government will take the necessary action to:

a. Place sufficient Japanese police guards aboard the designated snip to:

(l) Take custody of the individuals mentioned in paragraph 2 above.

(2) Insure their delivery, along with their court and prison records, to the appropriate Japanese officials in Japan.

b. Expedite their processing as repatriates and early removal from the reception center.

c. Incarcerate them upon completion of processing at the reception center.

d. Render a monthly report to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers as to the status of these prisoners.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
