SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

19 June 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
SUBJECT :Rayon Textile Capacity

1. Reference memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 423 (4 Apr 47) ESS/TD, SCAPIN 1600, 4 April 1947, subject: Rayon Textile Capacity.

2. Paragraph 2 of subject memorandum is hereby amended as follows:

“Authorization is hereby given to increase the rayon capacity from an annual capacity of 150,000 metric tons of filament yarn and rayon staple fibre to 169,020 metric tons of filament yarn and rayon staple fibre manufactured by the viscose and cuprammonium processes, 20,000 metric tons of high tenacity yarns (viscose process), 15,000 metric tons of filament yarn and staple fibre manufactured by the acetate process.”

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B. BUSH,
Brigadiar General, USA
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1600/1)

WFM/MMC/MBL/gk

1. Under SCAPIN 1600, dtd 4 Apr 47, subject: Rayon Textile Capacity, authorization was given to the Japanese Government for rebuilding a rayon industry to an annual capacity of 150, 000 metric tons, or 330, 690, 000 pounds, of filament yarn and rayon staple fibre.

2. The reference directive did not specify the amount of filament or rayon staple fibre as related to the total capacity. Production of high tenacity tire cord yarns and acetate filament rayon and staple fibre were not considered in the limitation. To increase production of rayon and other synthetic textile fibres would furnish a domestic source of the yarns which in turn would aid in decreasing import of raw materials such as cotton, wool and best fibres.

3. The rayon production has not reached the limitation of 150, 000 metric tons and it is estimated that this capacity will be reached by December 1952. The capacity registered with the Japanese Government is in excess of the limitation of 150,000 metric tons. The present registered capacity of the rayon industry if 169,020 metric tons per year.

4. This amendment to SCAPIN 1600 will permit full utilization of present registered capacity by company and plant, in addition to producing 20,000 metric tons per year of high tenacity yarns (viscose process) and 15,000 metric tons per year of acetate filament yarn and staple fibre. This amendment does not limit production of synthetics such as vinylon and amilon.

5. Synthetics such as vinylon and amilon are not limited because of type of equipment and raw materials required in addition to end use factors. New limitations are justified due to consideration of the following factors:

a. Critical position of world supply of pulp wood and rayon pulp and the capacity of the Japanese pulp industry with reference to rayon requirements.

b. Loss of many export markets and expansion of world rayon production particularly in countries such as South America, India, Mexico, Australia, Egypt and Australia.

c. New limitation will give Japan a capacity which will be 83% of the pre-war peak of 541,140,000 lbs. (1939).

6. Concurrences: ESS/FTC - By phone, Mr. C.C. Campbell 26-8858; ESS/FTP - By phone, Mr. E. C. Welsh, 26-6561

M. B. Laupheimer 26-8880

M. M. C.
