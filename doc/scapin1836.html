GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 471.86 (29 Dec 47) ESS

(SCAPIN - 1836)

APO 500

29 December 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Manufacture of Industrial Explosives and Accessories

1. References are:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 471.86(10 Jan 47) ESS/IN (SCAPIN-1452), subject: Manufacture of Industrial Explosives.

b. Letter, C.L.O. 9241 (EG), to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, subject: Manufacture and Use of Industrial Explosives and Accessories during Year 1948, dated 2 December 1947.

2. Manufacture of the following types and quantities of explosives, to be used for industrial purposes only, is authorized for the period from January 1948 to 31 December 1948.

Type of Accessory Quantity Authorized

Gelatin Dynamite and Hagi Dynamite 8,200 Metric Tons

Ammonia Dynamite and Ammonium Nitrate Explosives 7,600 Metric Tons

Carlit 1,760 Metric Tons

Reprocessed Military High Explosives 630 Metric Tons

Black Powder 670 Metric Tons

Smokeless Powder 40 Metric Tons

Total 18,900 Metric Tons

A maximum of 10 tons of black powder may be utilized in the production of fireworks and party devices.

3. Authorization is granted to manufacture the following types and quantities of explosives accessories during the period 1 January to 31 December 1948:

Type of Accessory

Fuse 61,000 Kilometers

Blasting Caps for Use with Fuse and Electric Detonators 93,000,000 Pieces

Electric Detonators 31,000,000 Pieces

Percussion Caps 33,000,000 Pieces

Friction Tubes for Whaling 17,000 Pieces

Flame Signal Fuses for Railways 550,000 Pieces

Signal Detonators for Railways 540,000 Pieces

Signal Firing Devices for Ships 15,000 Pieces

Caps for Starting Signal Cap Pistols 20,000,000 Pieces

Sporting Cartridges 300,000 Pieces

4. The Japanese Government will submit to the Commanding General, Eighth Army, application for authority to operate plants necessary for the manufacture of the explosives and accessories authorized by this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ M. J. Noyes
for /t/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1836)

ESS/IN JAR/RCAP/MHPM/dr

15 December 1947

1. In accordance with Directive No. 3, (SCAPIN 47), dtd 22 Sep 45, Requiring submission of applications for the manufacture of essential industrial explosives, the Japanese Government requested authority to manufacture certain quantities of industrial explosives during the calendar year 1947 in 1tr, CLO No. 6704 (ECI) dtd 17 Dec 46, subj: Manufacture and Use of Explosives and Accessories for Period 1 January 1947 to 31 December 1947. After study, authorization was given to manufacture 13,705 tons of explosives plus various quantities of explosives accessories by Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 471.86 (10 Jan 47) ESS/IN, SCAPIN 1452, subj: Manufacture of Industrial Explosives. This authorization expires 3.1 Dec 47. Ltr, CLO 9241 (EG), to GHQ, SCAP, subj: Manufacture and use of Industrial Explosives and Accessories during Year 1948 dtd 2 Dec 47, requests authorization to manufacture and use 21,742 tons of explosives and various quantities of accessories during 1948.

2. Authorization for manufacture of the industrial explosives indicated in this memorandum is based on the following justifications:

a. Intended use will fill important requirements of the Japanese economy, i.e., coal mining, metal mining, quarrying, reconstruction construction of bridges, roads and tunnels, and agricultural purposes such as land reclamation and irrigation. A reasonable quantity of smokeless powder and cartridges is authorized for hunting and whaling. Various accessories necessary to insure transportation safety are also authorized.

b. Amounts authorized are considered minimum requirements.

c. The Japanese system of controls and licensing facilitates any desired checks on the manufacture. A detailed plan, listing the plants to be used and the amount scheduled per plant, has been prepared; this plan is based on a Japanese proposal, modified by this Division.

3. This memorandum authorizes the manufacture of 18,900 tons of explosives, a 37 percent increase over that authorized for 1947, in addition to various quantities of accessories. The principal explosives authorized are based on the following proposed uses:

a. Gelatine and Hagi Dynamite:

Use Quantity Remarks

Coal and lignite mining 2,118 For mining 35,000,000 tons coal and 3,500,000 tons lignite

Mineral mining 2,716 For mining 11,000,000 tons ore

Limestone quarrying 240 For quarrying 10,000,000 tons limestone

Agriculture 882 Waterways, irrigation land reclamation

Public works 733 Tunnels, roads, reclamation

Civil works 723 Harbor development, quarrying of building materials, reconstruction

Miscellaneous 788 Forestry, fishery, hydropower, railroad retail supply

Total 8,200

b. Ammonia dynamite and ammonium nitrate explosives

Use (MT) Remarks

Coal mining 7,600 See paragraph a above

c. Carlit (ammonium perchlorate explosives)

Use (MT) Remarks

Coal & lignite mining 30 See paragraph a above

Mineral mining 768 "

Limestone quarrying 560 "

Agriculture 102 "

Public works 80 "

Command 1tr to 8th Army includes list of approved plants and maximum quantities to be produced by these plants.

4. This matter as it relates to Korea has been considered.

5. Special surveillance by 8th Army to insure adherence to the production plan for the companies indicated, is required.

6. Concurrence: G-4 (Plans & Policy) Lt. Col. Ebel.

7. Refer all questions to Mr. M.H.P. Morand, Te. No. 26-6639

J. Z. R.
