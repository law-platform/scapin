GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 602 (30 Apr 48) ESS/EX

(SCAPIN - 1888)

APO 500

30 April 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Continued Closure of Non-essential Restaurants

1. The attention of the Japanese Government is invited to Directive No. 3, SCAPIN 47, dated 22 September 1945, Office of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, in which the Japanese Government was charged with initiating and maintaining a strict rationing program for essential commodities in short supply, to ensure that such commodities are equitably distributed.

2. The attention of the Japanese Government is further invited to the pending expiration on 30 April 1948 of Cabinet Ordinance No. 118, subject, "The Food Dispensing Business Emergency Measure," which was promulgated on 1 July 1947.

3. In consequence of the continuing world food shortages there is an imperative need for Japan to maximize the utilization of indigenous foodstuffs. The closure of non-essential public restaurants proved an effective measure of food conservation.

4. The Japanese Government is therefore directed to take the necessary action to extend the effectiveness of Cabinet Ordinance No. 118 until 30 April 1949.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. M. Levy
for /t/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General
