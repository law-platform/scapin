GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG095 (24 Oct 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 185)

24 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Funds and Operations of Leybold K. K.

1. Reference is made to CLO Memorandum No. 213, transmitting application of subject enterprise for release of its funds so that it may execute orders for chlorinators placed by the Eighth United States Army.

2. It is directed that expenditures by this enterprise be allowed insofar as necessary to purchase materials and other expenses which are incidental to the production and repair of chlorinators and hot water heaters in accordance with procurement demands of the Sixth and Eighth United States Armies. All expenditures and receipts and production operations of the enterprise will be subject to prior approval of supervisors designated by this Headquarters.

3. You will require monthly reports to be furnished of the operations and transactions of subject enterprise, copies of which will be delivered to this Headquarters and to the supervisors of the enterprise. These reports will contain the following information:

a. Contracts placed with Leybold KK, including date of contract, production schedule, total value of contract, and Army requiring the procurement.

b. Number of units completed each month.

c. Expenditures made during previous month and expenditures authorized for following month, including detailed explanations of each expenditure.

d. Receipts of the enterprise from all sources with detailed explanation of each receipt.

e. All additions and alterations to physical plant and equipment with full explanation of each addition and alteration.

f. All changes in the officers, directors and shareholders of the enterprise with names, addresses and nationalities of individuals concerned.

4. You will also require Leybold KK to furnish the following information within 15 days of the date of this memorandum:

a. Place of organization or incorporation of Leybold KK; length of time the company has been in operation; location of head office and of all branches or sales offices.

b. Description of its business and the chief products dealt in.

c. Detailed description of operations and activities of the business in Japan during the period June, 1941 to August 15, 1945.

d. List of present directors and executive officers, including nationality and present address.

e. List of directors and executive officers as of January 1, 1942, including nationality and address.

f. List of shareholders as of January 1, 1942 and as of September 1, 1945, including names, nationalities and addresses.

5. An acknowledgement of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
