GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 680.2 (10 Jul 50) GA

(SCAPIN-2115)

APO 500

10 July 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Rescission of SCAPINs Authorizing Entry of Personnel into Japan for the Purpose of Visiting Relatives or Friends

1. References:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 095 (22 May 48) GA, SCAPIN 1971, subject: Entry of Personnel into Japan to visit Relatives, 11 February 1949.

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 680.2 (22 May 48) GA, SCAPIN 1971/1, subject: Entry of Personnel into Japan to visit Relatives, 28 February 1949.

c. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 680.2 (22 May 48) GA, SCAPIN 1971/2, subject: Charges for Services Rendered to Personnel Entering Japan to Visit Relatives, 14 March 1949.

d. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 680.2 (22 May 48) GA, SCAPIN 1971/3, subject: Entry of Personnel into Japan to Visit Relatives or Friends, 28 June 1949.

2. Circular 11, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, subject: Entry Requirements and Business Activities in Japan, 16 June 1950, outlines procedures to permit individuals to enter Japan for a period not to exceed 180 days for non-professional or non-commercial purposes.

3. Effective 1 July 1950 the above referenced SCAPINs are rescinded except that the procedures enunciated therein will apply for an interim period in the following manner. Applications for entry of individuals under the 60 day visitors program will be accepted and processed until 31 July 1950. Individuals whose applications for entry are approved on or before 31 July 1950 may enter Japan provided they enter not later than 31 August 1950. No more entries or extensions of stay under this program will be granted after 31 August 1950.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
