OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 452.1 (26 Sep 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 77)

APO 500

1 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Operation of a Limited Facilities, Air Courier, Liaison, and Transport Service.

1. In order to continue for the present, the operation of a limited facilities, air courier, liaison, and transport service in conformity with our radio ZAX 5408 of 14 September, you are authorized to retain following aircraft pending further instructions:

7 Type MC-20

11 Douglas DC-3

6 Shiragiku Single Engine Utility Transports

3 Advance Training Planes

2. The numbers of aircraft authorized above are in accordance with your request.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ Harold Fair.
/t/ HAROLD FAIR,
Lt. Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General.
