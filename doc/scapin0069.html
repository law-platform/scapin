IN THE CLEAR
OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS
SIGNAL CORPS MESSAGE

(SCAPIN -69)

GC-O RWM/dk 28 September 1945

FROM :SCAP
TO :JAPANESE MINISTRY OF THE NAVY.........OPNL PRIORITY
INF :CINCPAC...............................OPNL PRIORITY
COM FIFTH FLEET.......................OPNL PRIORITY
CTF 51................................OPNL PRIORITY
CTF 55................................OPNL PRIORITY

SCAP ZEBRA ABLE ARAY FIVE THREE NAUGHT FORE CMA TERE OBOE OBOE ONE TWO NAUGHT NINE THREE SEVEN IS REFERENCE PD (ZAX 6148) MOVEMENT OF JAPANESE NAVAL VESSELS TO SASEBO DLLAYED BY REFERENCE MAY NOW PROCEED PD INFORM SCAP AND COM FIFTH FLEET OF COMPLETE DETAILS PRIOR TO BEGINNING MOVEMENT

OFFICIAL:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, US Army,
Adjutant General

APPROVED:

/s/ Esher C Burkart
/t/ ESHER C BURKART
for W. E. Chambers
/t/W. E. CHAMBERS
Brigadier General, G. S. C.
Acting Asst. Chief of Staff, G-3
