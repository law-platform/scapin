MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Regulations Affecting Japan Fertilizer Company, Ltd, (Nihon Hiryo K. K.)

1. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to take immediate action to prohibit the Japan Fertilizer Company, Ltd, from making any sale, transfer or other disposition of any capital assets including lands, buildings, major items of equipment and securities except as otherwise authorized by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government will, within fifteen (15) days from date of this Memorandum, submit to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, the following schedules of information concerning the Japan Fertilizer Company, Ltd, in quintuplicate, typed in English on 8" x 11" paer:

a. All movable and immovable assets showing locations, except stores of fertilizer.

b. Outstanding loans and liabilities.

c. All securities and investments.

d. Contrancts and leases of properties and equipment.

e. Estimated borrowings needed for ordinary business purposes for the next thirty (30) days.

3. Nothing herein contained shall be construed to interfere with the ordinary functions of the Company connected with the manufacture and distribution of fertilizer.

4. Direct communication is authorized between the interested Staff Sections of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and agencies of the Imperial Japanese Government to implement all provisions of this Memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1366)

WFM/VHK/FCH/gg

14 November 1946

1. The Japan Fertilizer Company, Ltd, is a joint stock company handling the distribution of all the fertilizer produced in Japan and also acting as a lending agency for fertilizer producers, as well as being a producer itself. This Company is to be dissolved and a Government distribution company established in its place.

2. Pending the dissolution it is desirable to freeze the assets of this Company and also to secure an inventory, a schedule of loans it has made and other information indicating the extent to which the Company controls other fertilizer producers. In certain instances this Company has loaned large sums to other companies without the knowledge or consent of SCAP, has approved expansion of factories not approved by SCAP, and has been used as a foil to evade SCAP policies on fertilizer production.

3. This is an administrative matter, and no surveillance by the Eighth Army is required.

4. The interests of USAFIK are not concerned.

5. Concurrences: ESS/IN (Mr. R.C. A. Purl); ESS/FI (Lt. Col. D. H. Jennings);

NRS (Mr. F. E. Ritchie).
