APO 500

8 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Formosans and Koreans Aboard Vessel Kizan Maru.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, AG 370.05 (5 Mar 46) GC, (SCAPIN-795), subject: "Repatriation of Koreans Aboard Vessel Kizan Maru."

2. Paragraphs 1 and 2 of above reference are rescinded herewith.

3. The Kizan Maru is expected to arrive at Kure about 8 March 1946.

4. On board are 182 Formosan and 16 Korean repatriates.

5. The Imperial Japanese Government will receive and care for the repatriates mentioned in paragraph 4 above, and will transport them to appropriate ports for embarkation on vessels bound for their respective countries.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
