GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 471.86 (6 Feb 46) ESS/IE

(SCAPIN - 705)

APO 500

6 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Shipment of Explosives to Chingwantao, China

1. Reference is made to Directive AG 471.86 (17 Jan 46) ESS/IE, subject: "Shipment of Explosives to Chingwantao, China", dated 17 January 1946. The scheduled sailing of the EIJIN MARU was cancelled and a substitute vessel, S/S TOBATA MARU, has been placed in service to fulfil the original commitments of the EIJIN MARU.

2. It has now been officially reported that the Captain of the TOBATA MARU is reluctant to load the explosives and has put forward objections, asserting that his ship is not properly fitted to carry the same.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to carry out the terms of the original Directive, described in paragraph 1 above, and will load the above mentioned explosive cargo on board S/S TOBATA MARU at MIIKE.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD.
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 705)

WFM/RAM/aav

31 January 1946

1. Reference is made to Directive to the Imperial Japanese Government, AG 471.86 (17 Jan 46) ESS/IE, covering instructions to ship 800 cases dynamite and 150,000 electric detonators to Chingwantao, China, for account of Kailan Mining Administration. The EIJIN MARU was originally fixed to lift this cargo at MIIKE on 20 January 1946, but her sailing was cancelled and the TOBATA MARU was substituted instead.

2. Report was received from SCAJAP that the Captain of the TOBATA MARU had stated objections to loading explosives, claiming that his ship is not properly fitted to carry dangerous cargo.

3. According to SCAJAP and our own conclusions, these objections are not justified. The present memorandum directs the Imperial Japanese Government to carry out the original instructions and load the shipment on board S/S TOBATA MARU, arriving MIIKE on 5 February 1946.

4. Concurrence is not necessary. Information copies furnished to G-4 Supply, G-4 Transportation, SCAJAP and CO Group 55.2.

R. A. M.
