16 August 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Revised Listing of Aircraft Factories, Military and Naval Arsenals and Research Laboratories.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, dated 28 May 1946 (SCAPIN-987), subject: “Custody, Control and Protective Maintenance of Japanese Aircraft Plants Arsenals and Laboratories,” with inclosure, “List of Aircraft Factories, Army and Navy Arsenals, and Laboratories.”

2. Attached as Annexes IV, V and VI are revisions to the list of Aircraft Factories, Army and Navy Arsenals and Laboratories referred to in paragraph 1, above. All plants on Annex IV are removed from the list. Plants appearing on Annex V are added to above mentioned list Corrections are noted on Annex VI.

3. Within seventy-two (72) hours of the receipt of this memorandum, the Japanese Government will have qualified representatives report to the Commanding General, Eighth United States Army, and to the Commander Naval Activities, Japan, to receive instructions with regard to establishment of or releasing from custody and control, installations affected by revisions of the lists referred to in paragraph 1, above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

3 Incls:

1. Annex IV: Installations removed from list of Aircraft Factories, Army and Naval Arsenals and Laboratories.

2. Annex V: Installations added to list of Aircraft Factories, etc.

3. Annex VI: Corrections to list of Aircraft Factories, etc.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1139)

ESS/IN JZR/NFM/kk

12 August 1946

1. Revision of present listings of aircraft factories, Military and Naval arsenals, and research laboratories now under custody and control of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers has been made necessary by submission to this headquarters of information revealing complete war damage to some of the listed installations, transfer of machinery and equipment at others, errors in listing, and the erroneous inclusion or omission of some concerns. Annexes I, II & III to revised list #1 were issued 25 July 46.

2. The attached annexes remove 38 installations from the reparations list, add 12 installations, and correct the listing of 4 others.

3. No concurrences are necessary.

J. Z. R.
