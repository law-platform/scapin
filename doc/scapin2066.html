GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.1 (21 Dec 49) CCS

(SCAPIN-2066)

APO 500

21 December 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Rehabilitation of Postal Savings Branches.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 091.1 (14 Oct 46)CCS, SCAPIN 1268, 14 October 1946, subject, same as above.

2. Reference memorandum is hereby rescinded effective at once, and responsibility for completion of the program of restoration of Postal Savings records is transferred to the Japanese Government.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General

(See reverse for Memo of General Application)

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POKERS

APO 500

21 December 1949

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number SCAPIN 2066 , AG 091.1 (21 Dec 49 )CCS, this Headquarters, 21 Dec 49, subject: Rehabilitation of Postal Savings Branches

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. SCAPIN 1268 was issued for the purpose of securing proper and prompt action in restoring destroyed Postal Savings and related records in Postal Savings Branches.

3. Restoration of the destroyed records is approaching the limit of practical realization and as the Japanese Government is planning to carry on the program, the SCAPIN is no longer considered necessary.

A J REHE

Major, AGD

Asst Adj Gen

DISTRIBUTION

Same as SCAPIN 2066

less Japanese Government

(See Reverse for SCAPIN)
