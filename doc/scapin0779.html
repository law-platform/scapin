GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 122.2 (28 Feb 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 779)

APO 500

28 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Blocking the Bank Account of Seiichi Doi (Toi) and Others.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, this Headquarters, file AG 122.2 (6 Dec 45) ESS/FI, dated 6 December 1945, subject: "Proceeds of Remittances and Collections since 15 August 1945."

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to take immediate action by telegraph to block the bank accounts of the following individuals for the amounts indicated:

a. KIYOSHI TATEISHI 900,000 yen

Deposited with Sumitomo Bank, Wakamatsu, Kyushu

b. SEIICHI DOI (TOI) 250,000 yen

SEIICHI DOI (TOI) 20,225 yen

TAISAKU DOI (TOI) 250,000 yen

TSUMZO OSUGI 250,000 yen

YOSHIMORI YOSHIOKA 250,000 yen

TAKUMI HASHIMOTY 250,000 yen

SHUJO WAGI 200,000 yen

SHUZD WAGI 474,022.26 yen

Deposited with Sanwa Bank, Kokura, Kyushu.

3. It is further directed that at the earliest possible date a detailed report be furnished this Headquarters of action taken under this memorandum which will include the present balance and all transactions in these accounts subsequent to 3 September 1945.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ B. M. Fitch
/t/ B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 779)

WFM/TEB/als

1. Radio TFYMG 449 from Commanding General, USAFIK, received 17 December 1945, assigned to OCCIO for action, requests apprehension of Seiichi Doi under charges for the embezzlement of 3,000,000 yen. Radio TFYMG 627, received 7 January 1946, action OCCIO states that this sum was sent to Japan in the names of nine people. Requests transfer of funds to Bank of Chosen for credit of Mikuni Coal Industries Company. Radio TFYMG 915, received 13 February 1946, action OCCIO, revised to ESS, 14 February 1948, states that prosecution or return of Doi to Korea not desired if money can be restored to Mikuni Coal Industries Company. Amount known sent to Japan 2,710,000 yen. The payees and amounts of the draft sent from Korea in most cases was transferred to other accounts as radio 3, ADJ 467 reveals a new list of names and amounts.

2. Radio 3 ADJ 467 from Commanding General, 32nd Division (G-2) to CINCAFPAC (G-2), received 29 January 1946, assigned OCCIO for action, states that transfer of funds accomplished by a check on the Sanwa Bank, Seoul, Korea to the Sanwa Bank, Kokura Kyushu. The names of the nine individuals, their banks of deposit and amounts of their deposits are enumerated. Total deposits are given as ¥3,094,254.26. A total of the amounts in the individual accounts totals ¥2,844,247.26, which is in excess of amount reported by USAFIK TFYMG 915 known to have been sent to Japan.

3. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, this Headquarters, file AG 122.2 (6 December 1945) ESS/FI, subject: "Proceeds of Remittances and Collections since 15 August 1945" prohibits, without the prior approval of this Headquarters, all transactions in any bank account credited with the proceeds of any remittance from Korea which was credited on or since 15 August 1945. All the accounts which were credited with the proceeds of these remittances from Sanwa Bank, Seoul, Korea are being blocked for the total amount of such remittances in accordance with Memorandum AG 122.2 (6 Dec 45), ESS/FI as the transfer of these funds from Korea was effected on 3 September 1948.

4. Disposition of the blocked funds is not specified in this memorandum. A check sheet from ESS to the Chief of Staff has requested permission to open an account in the Bank of Japan where certain special funds in Japan owed to the Government of Korea or persons in Korea would be deposited for safe keeping and control. As no remittances from Japan to Korea may be effected at the present time, these funds cannot be returned by us to the Mikuni Industries Coal Company in Korea. It is planned to transfer the funds from the accounts blocked by this Memorandum to the SCAP Korean account in the Bank of Japan. It has been requested that OCCIO inform Korea of action being taken here. When facilities for remitting funds to Korea are established it is planned to effect a bloc remittance of funds credited to Korea from the SCAP account in the Bank of Japan.

5. Concurrence Gov. Sec.

C. F. T.

ADDENDUM TO MEMO FOR RECORD:

1. Commanding General USAFIK radio TFYMG 449 refers to DOI also as TOI, designating the name as DOI (TOI). Commanding General USAFIK radio TFYMG 915 also interchanges names of DOI and TOI. As the parties referred to are obviously the same and in order to avoid any possible confusion as to what account should be blocked the names are designated as DOI (TOI) in the memorandum to the Japanese Government.

2. This is an administrative directive. No surveillance is necessary.

C. F. T.
