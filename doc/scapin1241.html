GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123 (1 Oct 46) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1241)

APO 500

1 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Funds Supplied Puppet Governments by the Japanese.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to submit to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, a complete and detailed report concerning all funds supplied to the puppet embassies or government representatives in Japan of Manchukuo (Manchuria), Inner Mongolia, Nanking (China), Burma and India by the Imperial Japanese Government, including bank statements showing all deposits and withdrawals from date of original deposits to the present.

2. It is directed that these date be supplied General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers within thirty days from date of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
