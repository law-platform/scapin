APO 500

3 June 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Reports to be Made by Certain Families.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will immediately take such steps as are necessary effectually to prohibit the sale, gift, assignment or transfer of any movable or immovable property, including securities, and other evidences of ownership, indebtedness or control by the Aikawa, Asano, Furukawa, Kawasaki, Matsushita, Nakajima, Nomura, Okochi, Okura, and Shibusawa families or by any person acting in their behalf.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government shall furnish to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, within fifteen (15) days of the receipt of this Memorandum, a report in quintuplicate, typed in English on 8"x 11" paper listing:

a. All movable or immovable property, securities and other evidences of ownership, indebtedness and control, all cash on hand or on deposit, real estate and Government bonds, in which the members of the families named in paragraph 1, above, had any right, title or interest as of 1 January 1945 and 15 May 1946.

b. All transactions involving movable or immovable property, including securities and other evidences of ownership, indebtedness and control by any member of the famlies named in paragraph 1, above, since 1 January 1945.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government is hereby permitted to authorize such living expenses as are just and reasonable for the families affected by this Memorandum. No amounts will be authorized which can not be attributed directly to living expenses or taxes of the individuals affected.

4. An accurate record will be maintained by the Imperial Japanese Government of each authorization granted. Sufficient justification shall be procured by the Imperial Japanese Government prior to the granting of each authorization. Records of each justification will be kept available for inspection at all times.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 995)

WFM/JMcIH/JCH/gg

25 May 1946

1. a. On 6 November 1945, SCAP Memorandum AG 004 (6 Nov 45) ESS/ADM (SCAPIN-244), subject: "Dissolution of Holding Companies," prohibited the sale, gift, assignment or transfer of any movable or immovable property, including securities and other evidences of ownership, indebtedness or control by members of the "Big Four" Zaibatsu families (Mitsui, Iwasaki, Yasuda and Sumitomo) or by any person acting in their behalf. This action was necessary to prevent these financially influential families from investing in new pursuits that ultimately would materialize in in new monopolistic combines.

b. On 8 December 1945, SCAP Memorandum AG 004 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC (SCAPIN-403), subject: ''Establishment of a Schedule of Restricted Concerns," established a Schedule of Restricted Concerns; SCAP Memorandum AG 300.8 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC (SCAPIN-408), subject: "Regulations Affecting Restricted Concerns," restricted the activities of Restricted Concerns to those incidental to the normal course of business.

c. At present, the business activities of certain families who control the stock of Restricted Concerns are not covered by either SCAP Memoranda (SCAPIN-244) or (SCAPIN-408) referred to above. The former Memorandum covers only the "Big Four" families; the latter Memo restricted the business activities of Restricted Concerns, but not the controlling families thereof. Hence, the business activities of the following named controlling families are presently uncontrolled:

Restricted Concerns Controlling Families

Asano Honsha Asano

Fuji Industrial Co., Ltd. Nakajima

Furukawa Mining Co., Ltd. Furukawa

Kawasaki Heavy Industry Kawasaki

Matsushita Electric Industry Co., Ltd. Matsushita

Nissan and Co. Aikawa

Nomura and Co. Nomura

Okura Mining Company, Ltd. Okura

Riken Industry Co., Ltd. Okochi

Shibusawa Dozoku and Co. Shibusawa

2. a. It has come to the attention of SCAP (reference, Inclosure 1, hereto) that various members of the above listed controlling families are financing new enterprises and, hence, expanding the web of monopoly in Japan. Inclosure 2, hereto, shows the total securities owned by the above listed families, and by each member thereof. These securities include those in non-restricted concerns, as well as in Restricted Concerns. The securities in non-restricted concerns and Government bonds, as well as large personal bank deposits, can at present be used to engage in new enterprises, an example of which is an informal application by a theater owner to remodel a moving picture theater, the funds in the amount of ¥8,648,688 to be furnished by Baron Kishichiro Okura, former Chairman of the Board of Okura and Company. Unless a program restricting the use of the personal assets of the controlling Zaibatsu families is effectuated, the program of dissolving the Zaibatsu will be largely nullified. Accordingly, the Imperial Japanese Government is being directed to take immediate steps to prohibit the sale, gift, assignment or transfer of any movable or immovable property, including securities and other evidences of ownership, indebtedness or control by the families named in paragraph 1c, above, or by any person acting in their behalf.

b. The Imperial Japanese Government is, however, permitted to authorize such living expense as are just and reasonable for the families affected by the attached Memorandum. No amounts are to be authorized which cannot be attributed directly to living expenses or taxes.

3. The interests of USAFIK have been considered. This action does not affect the Korean area.

4. This is considered an administrative matter.

5. Concurrences: Legal Division, ESS (Lt. Col. E. R. Minnich)

Finance Division, ESS (Lt. Col. D. L. Jennings)
