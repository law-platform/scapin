6 April 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Incarceration of Major General ONODERA, Makoto.

The Imperial Japanese Government will make notations in its records to the effect that Major General ONODERA, Makoto will be held at Sugamo Prison from the time of arrival aboard repatriation ship Tsukushi Maru until such time as he may be released.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
