GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 132 (18 Oct 45) ESS

(SCAPIN - 157)

APO 500

18 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Freezing of Accounts of Ando Moku, and Imori Meiji

1. You will immediately freeze all accounts of Ando Moku, Chief of Personnel Section, Imperial Bank of Tokyo, and Imori Meiji, President of the Chosen Saving Bank in Seoul, with any financial institution, including their accounts with Tokyo Branches of the Imperial Bank.

2. You will immediately furnish to this Headquarters, bank statements of all the above accounts from August 1, 1945 to date of freezing which will include all entries made on such account. You will further furnish detailed descriptions and explanations of each entry.

3. You will report immediately to this Headquarters the action taken to comply with these requirements, and the date the required statements, descriptions and explanations will be submitted.

4. An acknowledgement of the receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. Allen
H. W. ALLEN
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 157)

15 October 1945

1. Reference radio MGFIN 13i from CG USAFIK to CINCAFPAC ADV, dated 12 October, 1945 requests cancellation of alleged illegal transfer of 3,000,000 yen from Seoul Branch, Imperial Bank of Tokyo to account of Ando Moku, official of that bank to be held pending instruction from Imori Meiji, President of the Chosen Savings Bank of Seoul.

2. Request that attached letter be forwarded to Japanese Government directing blocking of the accounts of the individuals referred to in Par. 1 and a complete statement of their banking transactions since 1 August, 1945.

3. Request that attached radio be sent to CG USAFIK notifying him of action. Compliance with their request that funds be returned to Chosen Savings Bank deferred until full details are obtained. Radio contains suggestion as to how they may obtain access to funds if vitally necessary to their current operations.

4. Lt. Col. Coudray, Finance Division, concurs.

E. C. Sherbourne

Capt. Ord.

Finance Division

Econ. & Sc. Sec.
