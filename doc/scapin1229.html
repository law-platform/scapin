GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG005 (25 Sep 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-1229)

APO 500

25 September 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Contributions for purposes of National Defense.

1. Reference is made to the following memoranda:

a. Memorandum from the Imperial Japanese Government, CLO No. 4180 (LO 1158 - MS/AC), 22 August 1946, subject: “Application of Asahi Shimbun Sha for Permission to Transfer National Defense Contribution Fund in its Custody to National Treasury,” and attached memorandum. LO 1158 (MS/AC), from Ministry of Finance, 17 August 1946, subject: “Contributions to the Asahi Newspaper.”

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 123 (28 Jun 46) ESS/FI, 28 June 1946, SCAPIN-1040, subject: “Proposed Endowment from National Defense Contribution Fund to Establish an Independent Educational and Cultural Foundation.”

2. The proposal to contribute to the National Treasury ¥7,811,158.96 and accrued interest, representing funds collected by the Asahi Shimbun from subscribers for National Defense and Welfare in accordance with procedure for disposition of other National Defense funds outlined in Paragraph 3, reference 1 b above, is approved.

3. Similar cases referred to the Imperial Japanese Government in the future may be treated in the same manner.

4. It should be understood in this connection that all or any proportion of such funds as are found in second restricted accounts established by the Enforcement Regulations of the Emergency Financial Measures as presently amended, or in the old account of financial institutions as established by the Bill of Emergency Measures Regarding the Accounts of Financial Institutions, shall remain in such accounts subject only to such priority as is given the government by law.

5) Report of all funds contributed to the National Treasury pursuant to Paragraph 3 above will be forwarded to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

6. Direct communication between the Ministry of Finance and interested Staff Sections of this Headquarters is hereby authorized to implement this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1229)

WKL/ATM Ihr

6 September 1946

1. The memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 123 (28 Jun 46) ESS/FI, 28 June 1946, SCAPIN 1040, subject: "Proposed Endowment from National Defense Contributions Fund to Establish an Independent Educational and Cultural Foundation," dealt only with disposition of funds donated voluntarily by the Japanese People directly to the Army and Navy and known to this Headquarters as "The National Defense Fund".

2. Basic request, CLO No. 4180 from the Imperial Japanese Government requests that similar donations received and now held in trust by the Asahi Shimbun (newspaper) and funds of like nature held in trust by other organizations be permitted to be contributed to the National Treasury. This memorandum approves such proposal.

3. The Asahi Shimbun has on deposit in the Sumitomo Bank of Tokyo \4, 527,148.96 and the Sumitomo and Sanwa Bank of Osaka \3,284,010.00, a total of \7,811,158.96. On 20 July 1937, as an advertising scheme the Asahi Shimbun began soliciting donations for the purpose of furthering aircraft production. Later, sums were donated for other national defense and welfare purposes. A summary of transactions from 1937 to date is attached.

4. The balance of the fund now held in trust by the Asahi Shimbun should either be turned back to original subscribers, numbering between fifty to one hundred thousand, or be turned into the general revenue account of the National Government. Paragraph 2 and 4 of subject memorandum approves the request to turn the money into the general revenue account with the understanding that restricted account deposit laws will be strictly adhered to. Approximately \160,000 is included in first restricted account and the balance \7, 351, 158.96 is included in the second restricted account.

5. Representatives of the Asahi Shimbun and the Ministry of Finance stated that other funds of like nature were held in trust by other organizations. Permission to accept such funds as contributions to the National Treasury is given and report of such action to Finance Division, Economic and Scientific Section is directed.

6. Concurrences: None required.

7. This is an administrative matter. No surveillance is necessary. The interests of USAFIK have been considered and are not affected.

1 Incl: 1. Summary of Transactions from 1937 to date.

W. K. L.
