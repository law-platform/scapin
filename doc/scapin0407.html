GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 121.7 (8 Dec 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-407)

APO500

8 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Application of Memorandum regarding Elimination of War Profits and Reorganization of National Finances(AG 121.7 24 Nov 45).

1. Reference is made to letter of 1 December 1945 from the Liaison office, Ministry of Finance (LO 26).

2. The language in paragraph 4 of the subject memorandum “…any claim arising …from war damage…” includes claims arising from measures taken to prevent war damage.

3. The word “…agencies or instrumentalities…” where used in paragraph 8 of the subject memorandum do not include private banks or other private financial institutions. Special banks and the Deposit Fund Section of the Money and Banking Bureau of the Ministry of Finance may continue their normal credit functions insofar as they do not conflict with other portions of the subject memorandum and provided that they shall not be permitted to issue bonds, debentures or any other securities directly or indirectly guaranteed by the government except as authorized by this Headquarters.

4. Paragraph 8 - d of the subject memorandum does not apply to subsidies, tax exemptions, tax allotments, rebates, or any similar benefits granted by existing legislation.

5. Direct communication between the Ministry of Finance and interested staff sections of this Headquarters is hereby authorized to implement the subject memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
