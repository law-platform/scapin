(SCAPIN - 1291)

APO 500

24 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Use of Benzene as Motor Fuel

The Imperial Japanese Government is hereby directed to take the necessary steps to prohibit the use of benzene as a fuel for internal combustion engines.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
for /t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

24 October 1946

Information of General Application pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN-1291), File AG 463. 8 (24 Oct 46) ESS/IN subject: Use of Benzene as a Motor Fuel.

1. With reference to memorandum Number (SCAPIN-1291), from the Supreme Commander For The Allied Powers to the Imperial Japanese Government, this directive is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The critical shortage of benzene needed for the drug, paint, and dyestuffs industry has partially been caused by the use of large amounts of benzene as a motor fuel. The majority of this benzene is used in the plant or area near the coke ovens producing the benzene. Inspection of large coke ovens has shown the majority of the vehicles in the area have been converted to liquid fuel and are using benzene.

3. This directive will prohibit the use of benzene as a motor fuel and will more than double the amount of benzene channeled into the chemical industry.

4. Military surveillance in the form of spot-checks of benzene producers by local military government units is desired to insure adherence to this directive.

/s/ R. G. Hersey

/t/ R. G. HERSEY,

Lt. Col. AGD,

Asst. Adj. General
