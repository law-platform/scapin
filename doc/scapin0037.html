OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.76 (19 Sep 45) CIS

(SCAPIN - 37)

19 September 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Suspension of the Tokyo Newspaper NIPPON TIMES.

1. The Japanese Imperial Government will issue the necessary orders to suspend publication of the Tokyo newspaper NIPPON TIMES.

2. This suspension is to be effective as of 1530 hours this date (19 September 1945) and to continue until 1530 hours 20

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ Harold Fair.
/t/ HAROLD FAIR,
Lt. Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General.
