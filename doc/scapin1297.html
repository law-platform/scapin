GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 451 (28 Oct 46) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1297)

APO 500

28 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Use of Looted Vehicles for Official Business by the Japanese Government.

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 451 (3 Oct 46) CPC/FP, SCAPIN-1248, 3 October 1946, subject “Custody and Storage of Looted Automobiles” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

b. CLO No. 5211 (PK) 7 October 1946, subject “Application for Exemption from Storage of Looted Automobiles in the Custody of Japanese Government Offices.”

2. Pending further instructions, authorization is granted to exempt from the provisions of reference memorandum 1a above the 36 vehicles listed on the attached sheets, now in the custody of the governmental offices designated thereon.

3. The vehicles described above will be held in custody by the offices to which they are assigned at the present time and will be used only for the official business of these offices.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

Incl: List (to addressee only)
