GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004.2 (2 Jul 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-1049)

APO 500

2 July 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Dissolution Plan for the Yokohama Specie Bank.

1. Reference is made to memorandum from the Central Liaison Office, CLO No. 1718 (EF), dated 13 April 1946, subject: “Reorganization Plan of Yokohama Specie Bank Ltd., (LO 427),” and inclosed letter from the Ministry of Finance, same subject.

2. The plan proposed for the reorganization of the Yokohama Specie Bank is approved in principle, but the following provisions, in addition to those set forth in the referenced communications, will be observed in the dissolution of the present Yokohama Specie Bank and organization of a new institution.

a. The name of the new institution will bear no similarity, either in Japanese or in foreign translation, to that of the Yokohama Specie Bank.

b. Directors and executive officers of the new bank will not be persons who are subject to the provisions of Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 091. 1 (4 Jan 46) GS, subject: “Removal and Exclusion of Undesirable Personnel from Public Office.” or who have been associated in similar capacities with the Yokohama Specie Bank since 7 July 1937.

c. The initial paid-in capital of the new bank will be not less than ¥50,000,000.

d. The actual transfer of assets and liabilities from the Yokohama Specie Bank to the new institution will not be effected, except with the prior approval of the Suppeme Commander for the Allied Powers, before announcement by the Imperial Japanese Government of its program for the final disposition of general indemnity and other domestic claims arising out of the war. Any losses to the depositors and other creditors of the Yokohama Specie Bank resulting from the carrying out of the Government’s program will be assessed on the same basis as is prescribed for other financial institutions of a like character.

e. Prior to the effective date of the transfer of assets and liabilites referred to in subparagraph d, above, the Imperial Japanese Government will submit to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, a statement in writing specifically guaranteeing that segregation of the domestic assets of the Yokohama Specie Bank in the new institution will not prejudice the position of foreign creditors of the Yokohama Specie Bank should the foreign assets of the Yokohama Specie Bank prove inadequate to meet its foreign liabilities.

f. The dissolution of the Yokohama Specie Bank and the transfer of its domestic assets to a new institution will be carried out without prejudice to the deposits of foreign governments and their representatives in Japan the deposits of inter-national organizations of a humanitarian or philanthropic character and of those persons whose property the Imperial Japanese Government is required to preserve and protect in accordance with Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 091. 112 (13 Sep 45) MG, dated 13 September 1945, subject: “Protection of Allied and Axis Property.”

3. After segregation of the domestic assets and liabilities of the Yokohama Specie Bank and transfer to the new institution, the Yokohama Specie Bank will be liquidated under the supervision of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers as are those institutions closed by Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers file AG 091. 3 (30 Sep 45) ESS, dated 30 September 1945, subject: “Closing of Colonial and Foreign Banks and Special Wartime Institutions.”

4. Direct communication between the Yokohama Specie Bank, the Ministry of Finance and the interested staff sections of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, is authorized to implement this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1049)

PROBLEM: Dissolution of Yokohama Specie Bank and Organization of New Domestic Bank

FACTS PERTAINING TO THE PROBLEM:

1. The Yokohama Specie Bank during the war was used as an instrument of economic aggression abroad by the Japanese Government. The question of closing the Yokohama Specie Bank came up for discussion early in the occupation when institutions which had engaged in similar activities during the war were closed and ordered to liquidate by directive of GHQ, SCAP. Due to the extent of the domestic business of the Yokohama Specie Bank and also to the valuable information which they proved able to furnish regarding Japanese external assets it was decided not to require them to liquidate at that time. However, the Ministry of Finance officials, as well as officers of the Yokohama Specie Bank, have been verbally requested on numerous occasions since October of last year to submit a plan for the dissolution of the bank.

2. The Japanese submitted a plan for dissolution of the YSB and organization of a new domestic bank through CLO memorandum No. 1718 (EF), subject: "Reorganization Plan of Yokohama Specie Bank Ltd." dated 13 April 1946 and inclosed letter from the Ministry of Finance same subject. The essential features proposed are:

a. That a new bank be organized to assume the domestic non-government business of YSB and operate ten (10) domestic branches as an ordinary bank.

b. The directors and top officials of the YSB are to have no connection with the new bank, and the officers subject to the purge directive will not occupy any position of responsibility in the new bank.

c. The new bank to have ¥50,000,000 capital authorized of which one-quarter is to be paid in initially. Capital is to be raised by offering stock to the public with priority for subscription given to the present stockholders (except Imperial Household) and staff of new bank. No individual or corporation is to own more than 5% of the authorized capital. d. The new bank is to acquire sufficient domestic assets of the YSB to cover its capital and domestic liabilities assumed. Assets to be acquired are to be appraised by representatives of YSB and of the new bank subject to review by the Japanese Government in case of disagreement.

e. Subsequent to the establishment of the new bank and transfer to it from the YSB of domestic assets and liabilities, the YSB will be dissolved.

3. Due to international character of claims which might arise against YSB advice was requested from WARCOS by our radio C-60512 of 2 May 46 which also advised them of the Japanese plan. Radio WAR 88709 22 May 46 from WARCOS directed no action be taken pending recommendation of interested U. S. Government agencies. Radio W89679 30 May 46 from WARCOS concurs in plan presented by Japanese under which complete liquidation will be made of all foreign functions, public and private, as well as all domestic operations on government account. WARCOS radio also suggested that Japanese government guarantee that segregation of domestic assets for new bank does not prejudice the position of foreign creditors in event the foreign assets of YSB prove inadequate, also a different name was suggested for new bank to dissociate it from YSB.

4. The YSB has been out of contact with its foreign branches since the termination of the war and until communication is reestablished there are no means of accurately determining the condition of the bank's external assets or the full effect of unfavorable exchange rates. The YSB acted for and as the agent of the Japanese Government abroad during the war and appears to have claims for certain sums advanced. The disposition of their claims cannot be determined until pending tax measures are settled and an interpretation by the Japanese Government made regarding the disposition of general indemnity and other claims arising out of the war.

CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS:

1. It is considered that the dissolution of YSB and formation of a new bank to take over its sound non-government domestic business is in accordance with sound finance and will tend to improve the general banking situation in Japan.

2. While approving the plan set forth by the Japanese in principle, certain changes are considered essential, namely:

a. The name of the new institution will bear no similarity either in Japanese or in foreign translation to that of the Yokohama Specie Bank.

b. Directors and executive officers of the new bank will not be persons who are subject to the provisions of memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers file AG 091.1 (4 Jan 46) subject: "Removal and Exclusion of Undesirable Personnel from Public Office" or who have been associated in similar capacities with the Yokohama Specie Bank since 7 July 1937.

c. The initial paid in capital of the New Bank will be not less than ¥50,000,000.

d. The actual transfer of assets and liabilities from the Yokohama Specie Bank to the new institution will not be effected except with the prior approval of Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, before announcement by the Imperial Japanese Government of its program for the final disposition of general indemnity and other domestic claims arising out of the war. Any losses to the depositors and to other creditors of the Yokohama Specie Bank resulting from the carrying out of the Government's program will be assessed on the same basis as is prescribed for other financial institutions of a like character.

e. Prior to the effective date of the transfer of assets and liabilities referred to in subparagraph d above, the Imperial Japanese Government will submit to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, a statement in writing specifically guaranteeing that segregation of the domestic assets of the Yokohama Specie Bank in the new institution will not prejudice the position of foreign creditors of the Yokohama Specie Bank should the foreign assets of the Yokohama Specie Bank prove inadequate to meet its foreign liabilities.

f. The dissolution of the Yokohama Specie Bank and the transfer of its domestic assets to a new institution will be carried out without prejudice to the deposits of foreign governments and their representatives in Japan, the deposits of international organizations of a humanitarian or philanthropic character, and of those persons whose property the Imperial Japanese Government is required to preserve and protect in accordance with Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 091.112 (13 Sept 45) MG, 13 September 1945, subject: "Protection of Allied and Axis Property."

3. After segregation of domestic assets and liabilities of YSB and transfer to new bank the liquidation of the old bank will be carried out under the supervision of SCAP as is the liquidation of those banks closed under the 30 Sept 1945 directive.

4. Direct communication between the Yokohama Specie Bank, the Ministry of Finance and the interested Staff Sections of GHQ, SCAP, is authorized to implement this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:
