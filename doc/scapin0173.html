GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG000.5 (22 Oct 45) LS

(SCAPIN-173)

APO 500

22 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Military Government of Batangas Province, Philippine Islands.

It is directed that you furnish this headquarters with the name of the Military Governor of Batangas Province, Philippine Islands during the period of October 1944 to July 1945, the units under his command and the complete name of all subordinate officers

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. W. ALLEN
Colonel, A. G. D.
Asst. Adjutant General.
