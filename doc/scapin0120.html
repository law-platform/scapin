GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.3 (11 Oct 45) ESS

(SCAPIN-120)

11 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Production of Silk.

1. Reference is made to paragraph 3 of memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government, dated 9 October 1945, subject: “Import of Essential Commodities.”

2. You will revoke such orders as have been issued that will result in the reduction of the areas devoted to growing of mulberry trees for the ultimate production of silk except for such areas as will clearly be more valuable for growing food than for growing mulberry trees.

3. You will furnish, by 1 November 1945, a statement containing the following:

a. The quantity of silk, by grade and size, presently available that is suitable for export.

b. Estimate of raw silk production between 1 November 1944 and 30 June 1945.

c. Estimate of raw silk production between 1 July and 31 December 1945.

d. The breakdown of the production forecasted in (a) and (b) above by grade and size.

e. The condition of the raw silk testing and conditioning laboratories and their ability to function. If the laboratories cannot function at this time, state when they will be in a position to do so.

f. Such information as will provide a complete understanding of the present and prospective position of the raw silk industry.

4. You will dissolve:

a. The Nippon Sanshi Tosei Kabushiki Kaisha Inc. (Japan Silk Controlling Company).

b. The Nippon Sanshi Seizo Kabushiki Kaisha Inc. (Japan Raw Silk Manufacturing Company).

c. The Kyoei Sanshi Kumiai (Mutually Prosperious Silk Reeling Company).

5. You will submit to this headquarters for approval, by 1 November 1945, your plans to establish a public agency composed of representatives of the various phases of the sericultural industry, whose function will include but are not limited to:

a. The coordination of technical problems within the industry.

b. The supervision of the testing and grading of silk.

c. Advising the sericultural industry of the quality, types and sizes of silk required for export.

You will prohibit the selection of anyone who has been an active exponent of militant nationalism and aggression for positions on this agency.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN
Colonel, A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General

Distribution:

AG (5)

6th Army (3)

8th Army (3)
