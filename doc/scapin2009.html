SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (26 May 49) CPC/CD

(SCAPIN-2009)

APO 500

26 May 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Acquisition of Precious Metals

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for Japanese Government, file AG 130 (1 Mar 46) ESS/FI, SCAPIN 637-A, 1 March 1946, subject, "Purchase and Sale of Precious Metals," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

b. Memorandum for Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (10 Sep 46) CPC/GP, SCAPIN 1193, 10 September 1946, subject, "Purchase of Precious Metals," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

c. Memorandum for Japanese Government, file AG 386.3 (24 Apr 47) CPC/GP, SCAPIN 3674-A, 24 April 1947, subject, "Precious Metals Confiscated by Japanese Courts," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

d. Memorandum for Japanese Government, file AG 386.3 (24 Apr 47) CPC/CD, SCAPIN 3674-A/1, 2 December 1948, subject, "Amendment to SCAPIN 3674-A, 24 April 1947," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

e. Memorandum for Japanese Government, file AG 130 (22 Sep 45) ESS, SCAPIN 45, 22 September 1945, subject, "Control of Financial Transactions," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. References 1a, 1b, 1c and 1d above, and direct communications relating thereto, are hereby rescinded and superseded by this memorandum.

3. The Japanese Government, through its agent, the Bank of Japan, is directed to purchase all newly-mined precious metals, precious metals produced as by-products of, or from slimes remaining, from ores produced in Japan. Official approved prices will be paid in such transactions, and metals will be purchased by the last day of the month following that in which they were processed.

4. As determined proper, other precious metals may be purchased from Japanese persons, juridical or natural, upon the payment of official prices.

5. The Japanese Government may also retain precious metals, title to which has been acquired by the Japanese Government pursuant to Japanese law.

6. Precious metals, acquired as authorized by paragraphs 3, 4 and 5 above, will be held in safe storage and custody by the Bank of Japan, in vaults in Tokyo and Osaka, or in such other locations as are specifically authorized by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. A minimum amount of processing and other actions incident to the acquisition and proper storage of acquired property is authorized. Other transactions involving precious metals will be as directed by reference 1e above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
