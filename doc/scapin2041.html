GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 523 (18 Feb 49) GD

(SCAPIN - 2041)

APO 500

1 September 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Losses of, or Damage to, United States Government Property Shipped by Japanese Railways.

1. Shipments of United States Government property via railways in Japan have resulted in numerous instances of damage to or shortage of such property. Such discrepancies will be investigated by the United States occupation forces (by a Report of Survey, United States Army WD AGO Form 15-1) and responsibility for the loss or damage will be determined. In those instances in which the railways are found to be responsible for the loss or damage, such reports of investigation will be retained by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers as a basis for applicable action as defined below in effecting reimbursement for the value of the loss or damage sustained.

2. In instances in which the responsibility for loss or damage during rail shipment is attributed to the railway company, the Japanese Government will be hold pecuniarily responsible for such loss. The Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers will maintain a record of the value of such losses and/or damages in dollars or yen and will periodically report the amount and the name of the responsible carrier to the Japanese Government for necessary action as follows:

a. The U.S. dollar value of the loss or damage to U.S. Government owned property, not procured from the Japanese economy, when the responsibility for loss or damage is attributed to a private railway company, will be reported to enable the Japanese Government to effect reimbursement from the responsible carrier. When the responsibility for loss or damage is attributed to the National Railway Corporation, the U.S. dollar value of such loss or damage will not be immediately reported. A record of all such loss and/or damage in U.S. dollars will be maintained by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for future adjudication.

b. The yon value of the loss or damage to Occupation Forces property procured from the Japanese economy when the responsibility for loss or damage is attributable to either the National Railway Corporation or the private railways will be reported to enable the Japanese Government to make deductions from subsequent payments due the responsible carrier for services rendered the United States Occupation Forces.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
