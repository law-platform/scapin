GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 319.1 (29 Nov 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-366)

APO 500

29 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Report of Eleemosynary, Beneficial or Philanthropic Organizations Connected with Institutions Closed by Order of This Headquarters.

1. It is dictated that the Ministry of Finance be instructed to prepare and submit to this headquarters immediately a report which will show with respect to each of the institutions closed pursuant to Memorandum of this Headquarters to the Imperial Japanese government AG 091 .3 (30 Sept 1945), or subsidiary of any such institution, the following information.

a. Name and address of any eleemosynary, beneficial or philanthropic organizations established by or for officers and employees, or in which officers and employees hold membership, and to which the institution has made contribution of total value exceeding 23,000 yen since 1 January 1945.

b. The nature, purpose and scope of operations of such organizations.

c. Name and address of all officers, directors, trustees or other persons who have held any control over disbursal of funds of such societies and organizations since 1 January 1945.

d. Amounts and dates of all contributions to such society or organization of each or other aspects made by the closed institution since 1 January 1945.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 366)

RCK/THH/kns

29 November 1945

1. Memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government directing that Ministry of Finance prepare a report on employee relief societies of institutions closed pursuant to orders from this Headquarters.

2. This Headquarters is in possession of evidence indicating that substantial amounts of money were transferred to such societies by the institutions prior to the issuance of the closing order (30 Sep 1945).

3. No concurrences necessary.

4. Referenced order AG 091.3 (30 Sept 1945) directed the Japanese Government to close all colonization and development companies and special wartime institutions.

/s/ C. F. T.
