GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.7 (28 Oct 45) CIS

(SCAPIN-202)

28 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Establishment of Limited Mail System for Japanese Awaiting Repatriation.

1. The request of the Japanese Government to establish a limited mail system between Japanese awaiting repatriation in formerly Japanese occupied areas and the home islands is approved, subject to the following provisions:

a. Official correspondence will be restricted to matters which pertain to repatriation of Japanese forces and Japanese nationals.

b. Private correspondence will be limited to postcards, and will be of a personal nature only.

c. Commercial and financial communications are prohibited.

d. All mail will be routed through censorship stations at Tokyo, Osaka or Fukuoka.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
