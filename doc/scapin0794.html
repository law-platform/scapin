GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 040 (5 Mar 46) CE

(SCAPIN - 794)

APO 500

5 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Japanese Hydrographic Department.

1. Reference is made to memorandum from Central Liaison Office, Tokyo, dated 14 February 1946, file CLO No.678 (4.1), subject: "Removal of Surveying and Observation Instruments".

2. The Japanese Hydrographic Department is hereby authorized to move all such equipment that has been requisitioned for use by General Headquarters of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, to Tokyo form any branch office in Japan, as requested in reference memorandum.

3. It is desired that the Japanese Hydrographic Department resume its normal peacetime functions with the least practicable delay. Inasmuch as the maintenance of continuous records pertaining to meteorology and oceanography is vitally essential to many post-war industries in Japan and to science in general, authorization is hereby granted for the re-establishing of such facilities and observation stations, including the movement of property and equipment of the Japanese Hydrographic Department pertaining thereto, as are necessary to insure the resumption of these functions at an early date.

4. The provisions of memorandum from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, to the Imperial Japanese Government, dated 26 December 1945, file AG040(26 Dec 45)CE, subject: "Operation of Japanese Hydrographic Department", are continued in effect.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
