GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 684 (11 Oct 48) GC/TNG

(SCAPIN - 1989)

APO 500

4 April 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Yonago Aerial Gunnery Range.

1. The two rectangular over-water areas whose corners are located at the points indicated by the following coordinates are designated as an aerial gunnery range:

Area #1

133° 33' 24" East - 35°49' 06" North

133° 35' 18" East - 35°36' 06" North

134° 17' 48" East - 35°39' 36" North

134° 15' 54" East - 35°52' 36" North

Area #2

131° 54' 18" East - 35° 09' 36" North

132° 06' 36" East - 35° 01' 18" North

132° 33' 24" East - 35° 28' 12" North

132° 21' 06" East - 35° 36' 30" North

2. Area #1 will be used during the months of August through January. Area #2 will be used during the months of February through July.

3. The inhabitants of the ports located in Yamaguchi, Shimano, Tottori and Hyogo Prefectures will be notified prior to each actual use of the range. When the range is used for periods in excess of fifteen (13) days, warning reminders will be disseminated to the inhabitants concerned. This information will be forwarded through Military Government units to local Japanese civil authorities.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
