GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091 (6 Dec 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 397)

6 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Verification of Ownership of Aircraft.

1. References:

a. Your memorandum C. L. O. No. 417 dated 31 October 1945.

b. Memorandum this headquarters AG 402.5 (24 Sep 45) GD, subject, "Materials, supplies and Equipment Received and to be Received from the Japanese Armed Forces" dated 24 September 1945.

2. By reference 1 b above scrap resulting from the destruction of Japanese equipment which is not needed by the Occupational Forces is returned to the Home Ministry of the Imperial Japanese Government for use in civilian pursuits.

3. This command will assume no responsibilities in determining the ownership of Japanese equipment which is being destroyed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
