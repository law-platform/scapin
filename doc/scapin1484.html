MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Revocation of Permit "Payment of Savings and Allotments in Korea of Korean Laborers in Japanese Coal Mines."

1. Reference is Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 248 (29 Oct 45) ESS/FI (SCAPIN 207), dated 29 October 1945, subject: Payment of Savings and Allotments in Korea of Korean Laborers in Japanese Coal Mines.

2. Reference Memorandum will be rescinded and become inoperative effective 28 February 1947. The Special Account established thereunder with the Bank of Japan will be closed and the balance credited to the Custody Account for the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers in accordance with provisions of Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 004.2 (1 Mar 46) ESS/FI, dated 1 March 1946, subject: Accounts in the Bank of Japan for the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government will inform by telegraph all mining companies employing Korean laborers that, effective immediately, no further savings or allotments will be accepted for remittance to Korea.

4. All accumulated savings and allotments accepted from Korean laborers and held by Japanese mining companies will be deposited with the Bank of Japan or with United States Military Government Units prior to 28 February 1947.

5. On 31 March 1947 the Imperial Japanese Government is directed to furnish the General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Civil Property Custodian, a summary report of funds accumulated under authority of reference Memorandum. The summary report will include, in addition to the amounts deposited, the full names and known addresses of the payers and of their designated beneficiaries in Korea.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
for /t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1484)

WKL/TEB/APK/de

10 January 1947

1. Memo for IJG from GHQ, SCAP, AG 248 (29 Oct 45) ESS/FI, dated 29 October 1945, subject: Payment of Savings and Allotments in Korea of Korean Laborers in Japanese Coal Mines, directed that the periodic collection of savings and allotments desired to be remitted to Korea by Korean Nationals working in Japanese coal mines should be deposited in a special account with the Bank of Japan.

2. Memo for IJG from GHQ, SCAP, AG 004.2 (1 Mar 48) ESS/FI, dated 1 March 1946, subject: Account in the Bank of Japan for the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, directs the Bank of Japan to open a Custody Account for SCAP in which will be credited such monies as directed. It is considered that for purposes of more efficient accounting and operating controls, the special account established under Memo to IJG from GHQ, SCAP, AG 248 (29 Oct 45) ESS/FI, should be closed and all future deposits be credited to the Custody Account for SCAP. This is a purely administrative step to simplify and tighten controls over Korean funds in Japan. The Korean Coal Miners account in the Bank of Japan was opened by the Ministry of Finance by Memo referred to in par. 1 before SCAP had authority to open an account for the deposit of funds coming under GHQ custody. Certain funds were deposited in that account but since the opening of SCAP Custody Account all funds to credit of USAMGIK have been deposited in that account. This is desirable as it eases the record keeping by CPC and obviates the danger of accounting errors or omissions. Whether or not new funds for credit of USAMGIK turn up in Japan It is considered desirable that this old account be closed and funds centralized in SCAP Custody Account.

3. Proposed Memo to IJG, from GHQ, SCAP, AG 248 ( ) ESS/FI, dated 22 May 1848, subject: Deposits of Korean Coal Miners in the Bank of Japan, directs that the account established in Bank of Japan to receive collection of savings and allotments desired to be remitted to Korea by Korean Nationals be transferred to the SCAP Custody Account.

4. 13 June 1946, this Memo was referred to CPC for concurrence.

5. 9 September 1946, letter to CG, XXIV Corps, APO 235, AG 386.7 (9 Sep 46) CPC/GP, subject: Funds Consisting of or Pertaining to the Unsettled Accounts of Koreans Who Have Been Repatriated from Hokkaido, was sent by CPC directing the MG, Korea, to pay equivalent of Jap. \2,025,437,16 which is sum collected from various sources, to repatriated Korean beneficiaries, and to advise GHQ, SCAP, of payments so that Custody Account can be debited. Concurrence by CPC was delayed contingent upon receipt of this information. Action was finally taken due to apparent inability of USAFIK to locate beneficiaries in Korea and to prepare covering advice for debit.

6. 23 December 1946, Check Note from CPC to ESS/FI, dated 6 December 1946, subject: Revocation of Permit "Payment of Savings and Allotments in Korea and Korean Laborers in Japanese Coal Mines", was received by ESS/FI, with concurrence on proposed Memo provided:

a. Memo to IJG is amended to accord with CPC re-draft, 6 Dec 46.

b. Letter similar in form and content to CPC draft, is dispatched to EIGHTH ARMY requiring that interested Military Government Units be acquainted with their obligations contingent to revocation of Memo par. 2. and proposed transfer of credit.

7. No objections to re-draft of Memo for IJG. Re-draft specifically rescinds Memo noted in par., 1. , and allows sufficient time for IJG to implement terms of proposed Memo with appropriate instructions to mine operators, branch offices, and agencies of Bank of Japan. 28 February 1947 is set as closure date for operations under Memo rescinded, and provides adequate time to accomplish transfer of accumulated savings and allotments from Japanese coal mines to branch offices or agencies of Bank of Japan. Recommended this as a more feasible means of action.

8. Letter to CG, EIGHTH Army, AG 248 ( ) ESS/FI, subject: Revocation of Permit for Korean Coal Miners to Remit Funds to Korea through Bank of Japan, informs CG, EIGHTH Army of revocation of terms of Memo, par. 1. , and stipulates 7 March 1947 as final date of deposit with Bank of Japan of such savings and allotments as may presently be held by various Military Government Units. Recommend dispatch of this letter as necessary information.

9. This is an administrative matter and requires surveillance of EIGHTH Army.

10. The interests of USAFIK have been considered and provided for in this action.

11. Concurrence: CPC - (P. H. Tansey); Govt Sec - (Lt. Col. C. Darnell)

W. K. L.
