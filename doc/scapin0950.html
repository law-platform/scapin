MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Addition of Showa Electric Industry Company, Ltd., (Showa Denko Kabushiki Kaisha) and Subsidiaries to Schedule of Restricted Concerns.

1. Reference is made to the following Memoranda for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. AG 319.1 (22 Oct. 45) ESS/IE (SCAPIN-177), subject: “Reports to be Made by Certain Business Firms.”

b. AG 004 (31 Oct. 45) ESS (SCAPIN-215), subject: “Sale or Transfer of Securities of Certain Business Firms.”

c. AG 004 (8 Dec. 45) ESS/AC (SCAPIN-403), subject: “Establishment of a Schedule of Restricted Concerns.”

d. AG 300.8 (8 Dec. 45) ESS/AC (SCAPIN-408), subject: “Regulations Affecting Restricted Concerns.”

e. AG 123 (15 Jan. 46) ESS/'FI (SCAPIN-599), subject: “Restriction on Dividend Payments.”

f. AG 319.1 (19 Jan. 46) ESS/AC (SCAPIN-625), subject: "Reports to be Made by Certain Business Firms.”

g. AG 150 (23 Feb. 46) ESS/AC (SCAPIN-767), subject: “War Damage and Government Indemnity Claims of Companies on the Schedule of Restricted Concerns.”

2. The Showa Electric Industry Company, Ltd., and its subsidiaries listed in paragraph 4, below, are hereby added To the Schedule of Restricted Concerns referred to in paragraph 1c, above.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government will take immediate action to assure that the concerns named in paragraph 4, below, shall comply with the provisions of and make reports required by reference Memoranda listed in paragraph 1, above. Wherever reference is made in inclosure 1 and inclosure 2 to Memorandum referred to in paragraph 1a, above, to the year 1944, it shall be amended to read 1945.

4. Subsidiaries of Showa Electric Industry Company, Ltd.:

a. In Japan: Akazawa Coal Mining Co., Ltd.; Himekawa Electric power Co., Ltd.; Hokkaido Carbon Industry Co., Ltd.; Japan Abrasive Material Industry, Ltd.; Japan Coke Industry Co, Ltd.; Japan Combustion Engine Manufacturing Co, Ltd.; Japan-Manchuria Graphite Co., Ltd.; Japan Oil Liquefaction Co., Ltd.; Karafuto Coal Industry Co., Ltd.; North-Japan Industry Co., Ltd.; Oriental Sharpening Grindstone Industry Co., Ltd.; Shoei Industry Co., Ltd.; Showa Carbonic Acid Co., Ltd.; Showa Synthetic Chemical Industry Co., Ltd.; Tohoku Shinko Aluminum Co., Ltd.

b. Outside Japan: Korea Electric Industry Co., Ltd.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 950)

6 May 1946

1. Pursuant to the policy set forth in Joint Chiefs of Staff Directive 1380/15, dated 3 November 1945, investigations are being made of concerns which are not on the Schedule of Restricted Concerns, established by SCAP Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, AG 004 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC (SCAPIN-403), subject: "Establishment of a Schedule of Restricted Concerns."

2. Showa Electric Industry Company, Ltd. (Showa Denko Kabushiki Kaisha) and subsidiaries listed below are placed on the Schedule of Restricted Concerns as a result of investigation disclosing the following information:

a. Description and History of Company:

(1) This Company was organized on 1 June 1939 with an authorized capital of \110,000,000. It resulted from the amalgamation of Japan Electric Industry Company, Ltd. (Nihon Denki Kogyo, K.K.) and Showa Fertilizer Company, Ltd. (Showa Hiryo, K.K.). In October 1943, it amalgamated with the Nichiman Aluminum Company, Ltd., and increased the capital to \122,000,000. In April 1944, the authorized capital was increased to \244,000,000, of which \152,500,000 was fully paid.

(2) Showa Electric Industry Company, Ltd., with total assets of \465,000,000 (unpaid capital of \91,500,000 excluded) is a prominent manufacturer of chemical fertilizer and aluminum. Surveys of the years 1940-1942 show that it produced 15% of Japan's total output of sulphate of ammonia (fertilizer); 22.9% of the calcium cyanamide (fertilizer), and 21.2% of the aluminum. Although accurate figures are not available, it is assumed that this percentage of production was at least maintained throught the remaining war years, as the company's capital was doubled in 1944.

(3) The largest single stockholder is the General Japan Chemical Industry, Ltd. (Dai Nihon Kagaku Kogyo K.) owning 8.23% of the shares, with the Wartime Finance Bank next, owning 8.04%.

(4) Long and short term liabilities amount to \269,423,000. Of this amount \115,000,000 represents debentures, and \109,000,000 short term loans. The Yasuda Bank, Ltd. granted \63,500,000 of the short term loans and the Industrial Bank of Japan \30,800,000.

b. Investments:

(1) As of 30 September 1945, Showa Electric Industry Company, Ltd., had investments totaling \42,529,375, which may be divided into two types:

(a) Investments of \38,411,500, in subsidiary companies.

(b) Investments of \4,117,375, in chemical control associations.

c. Profits - For the period 1 April 1944 to 31 March 1945, the Company had a net profit of \12,306,000.

d. Interlocking Directorates - In some cases the officers of Showa Electric Industry Company, Ltd., are officers of the concerns it controls through its investments.

3. The interests of USAFIK have been considered. This action does not affect the Korean Area.
