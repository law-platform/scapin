GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 464.1 (4 Apr 46) ESS/IE

(SCAPIN - 860)

APO 500

4 April 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Shipment of Grain to Japan

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will take action and assume responsibility as follows with regard to 2,500,000 pounds of grain being, made available for future Japanese consumption:

a. Accept delivery of the 2,500,000 pounds of grain on or about 4 April 1946 at the port of Yokohama from the Commanding General, Eighth United States Army.

b. Furnish the Commanding General, Eighth United States Army with quantitative receipts for the grain at the point of delivery.

c. Store the subject grain so that it is protected against spoilage, pilferage, theft or other hazards, and is under proper custody at all times.

d. Furnish information as to the location of the storage points stating the quantity stored at each location. The grain will be held in storage until official authorization for release is given by the General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Terms of payment and accounting will be decided later.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ B. M. Fitch
for /t/ B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 860)

WFM/WEC/av

2 April 1945

1. Reference is made to radio SPTAE 10052 GO, dated 20 March 1943, from CG, SFPE, to CG. 8th Army.

2. Part 3 of above radio indicates that the balance due on WP-9 is 2,500,000 lbs and that the ETA is 4 April 1946.

3. This directive instructs the IJG to take delivery of the grain at Yokohama. Arrangements for unloading and transportation to storage points will be carried out by responsible agencies of the IJG.

4. It has been decided that the grain will be held in storage under Japanese custody for future release by SCAP due to the critical situation of the world food supply.

5. Letter to the CG, 8th Army, requests supervision of the transfer and that the 2,500,000 lbs is the final quantity to be imported under the approved civilian feeding program WP-9.

6. This problem as it relates to Korea has been considered.

7. This is a policy directive and Army surveillance is necessary. At such times as the Japanese plans for storage and distribution are compiled by this headquarters, detailed instructions will be issued to 8th Army for spot check surveillance of these stocks.

8. Concurrences: NRS - Major Williamson

G-4 Supply - Lt Col Davis

G-4 Transportation - Lt Col Gullion

G-4 Policy - Col Hutchison

PC&R - Captain Smith

R. A. M.
