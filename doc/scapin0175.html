GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 201-Abe, Genki (22 Oct 45) CIS

(SCAPIN - 175)

22 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Abe, Genki.

The Imperial Japanese Government will apprehend ABE, Genki, who held amongst other positions the Portfolio of Home Minister in the Suzuki Cabinet(April to August 15, 1945), and deliver him to the authorities of the Omori Prison Camp for incarceration.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
