APO 500

1 May 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Eleventh Supplemental Request for Permission of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the First Quarter of 1948.

1. Reference is made to C.L.C.O. No. 406 (LO 483-FI/2E), 24 February 1948, subject, "Eleventh Supplemental Request for Permission of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the First Quarter of 1948."

2. Application for permission to use palladium as a catalyzing agent in the manufacture of Eucodal, as requested by reference 1 above, is approved as altered, in the amount of 92 grams of palladium to the Takeda Yakuhin Kogyo K.K. The release will be made from stocks in the possession of the Japanese Government.

3. The use of the metals in manufacturing products, authorized by paragraph 2 above, will extend from the date of publication of this memorandum to 31 December 1948.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
