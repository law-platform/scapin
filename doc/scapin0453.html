MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Applications for Redemption of Serial Bonds

1. References are CLO, LO 41 and 42 dated 6 December 1945, subject: ’’Dissolution of Holding Companies," which request permission to redeem serial debentures listed in applications attached thereto.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
