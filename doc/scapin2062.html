MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2062)

JRA/JCS/WNR/HJR/emb

1 December 1949

1. Reference is Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 004.2 (21 Feb 46) ESS/FI (SCAPIN 759), dated 21 February 1946, subject: Merger of Financial Institutions.

2. Reference Memorandum restricting the merger, consolidation, or amalgamation of financial institutions and the acquisition of additional capital stock or debentures of other financial institutions was issued prior to the establishment of the Law No. 54, entitled "Law Relating to Prohibition of Private Monopoly and Methods of Preventing Fair Trade" dated 12 April 1947.

3. Article 15 of this Law prevents the merger of any company without the permission of the Fair Trade Commission. This Article provides that a merger will not be approved if it causes a substantial restraint of competition in any particular field of trade.

4. Article 11 of this Law prevents any company whose business is financing from owning stocks in a company with which it is competing and which operates in the same field of finance business.

5. Since the Articles cited in paragraph 2 and 3 above establish restrictions which had formerly been established by SCAPIN 759, there is no longer a need for the continuation of referenced SCAPIN.

H. J. Robinson, 57-8810
