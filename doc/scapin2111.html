SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 131 (30 Jun 50) ESS/BFE

(SCAPIN - 2111)

APO 500

30 June 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Surrender of Foreign Exchange Assets

1. Reference memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 131 (29 Nov 46) ESS/FI, SCAPIN 1368, dated 29 November 1946, subject: Surrender of Foreign Exchange Assets.

2. Effective as of 1 July 1950 the above referenced memorandum is hereby rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ K. B. Bush
/t/ K. B. BUSH,
Brigadier General, USA,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2111)

FRJG/WNR/GPW/JEJ/ms

24 June 1950

1. SCAPIN 1368, dated 29 November 1946, subject: Surrender of Foreign Exchange Assets, directs the Japanese Government to require all persons in Japan, except non-Japanese nationals, to surrender to the Bank of Japan all foreign currencies and to declare unlawful the possession of foreign currencies and foreign exchange instruments by such persons.

2. The above referenced SCAPIN is in conflict with the Foreign Exchange and Foreign Trade Control Law (Law No. 228 dated 1 Dec 1949, as implemented by Cabinet Orders and Ordinances and approved by SCAP in that Japanese nationals are authorized to receive foreign means of payment but are required to concentrate the same within ten days of receipt thereof. It is therefore considered necessary that subject SCAPIN be rescinded effective 30 June 1950, which is accomplished by the attached memorandum to JG.

3. Concurrence: CPC (Verbal, K. L. Mayall) 10 June 1950

J. E. Jordan 33-7053

R. R. J. G.
