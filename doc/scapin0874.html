GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.7 (10 Apr 46) ESS/IN

(SCAPIN - 874)

APO 500

10 April 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Resumption of Freon Gas Production.

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. No. 660 (2.3), subject: "Resumption of Frecn Gas Production, " dated 13 February 1946.

2. The application is approved. Only so much of the Osaka Kinzoku Kogyo Kaisha (The Osaka Metal Industry Co. Ltd.) plant is to be operated as is necessary for the manufacture of freon.

3. Approval of this application does not prejudice subsequent decisions relative to disposition of this plant for reparations, nor shall it prejudice any claims based on Allied patent rights for the manufacture of refrigerants.

FOR THE SUFREME COMMANDER:
B. M. FITCH,
Brigadeir General, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.7 (10 Apr 46)FSS/IN

MEMORANDUM:

SUBJFCT : Information of General Application pertaining to

Directive Number (SCAPIN - 674), File AG 463.7 (10 Apr 46)ESS/IN, dated 10 April 1946, GHQ, SCAP, subject: "Resumption of Freon Gas Production."

1. With reference to Memorandum Number (SCAPIN - 874), this Headquarters, to the Imperial Japanese Government, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

a. The reference Memorandum approves the request of the Imperial Japanese Government presented in C.L.O. No. 660 (2.3) subject: ''Resumption of Freon Gas Production," dated 13 February 1946, wherein application was made to resume the manufacture of Freon gas at the Osaka Kinzoku Kogyo Kaisha (Osaka Metal Industry Co.).

b. Only so much of the Osaka Kinzoku Kogyo Kaisha plant is to be operated as is necessary for the manufacture of Freon.

c. For purposes of administration and surveillance the Japanese Nationals concerned will handle all details concerning this matter through the local Military Government Detachment at Osaka.

d. Routine surveillance only will be necessary. Command instructions pertaining to the execution thereof will follow through channels.

/s/ J. W. MANN

/t/ J W MANN

Lt Col, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
