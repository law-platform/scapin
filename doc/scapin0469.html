GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123 (21 Dec 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 469)

APO 500

21 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Approval for proposed printing of currency

1. Reference is made to the letter from the Ministry of Finance, "Request for approval of program to print Bank of Japan notes", (LO 91) dated 17 December 1945; which requests permission to print 6,831 million yen in Bank of Japan notes of the present types during the remainder of December, pursuant to Par. 1 of Memorandum AG 123, 28 Nov 1945.

2. Permission for the above printing is granted.

3. In the future direct communication between the Ministry of Finance and interested staff sections of this Headquarters is hereby authorized to implement the subject memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
