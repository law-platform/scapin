GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (23 Oct 46) GA

(SCAPIN - 1286)

APO 500

23 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Foreign Nationals.

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers File AG 370.05 (7 May 1946) GC (SCAPIN 927) dated 7 May 1946, subject: Repatriation, as amended.

b. Memorandum from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers File AG 014.33 (18 September 1946) GC (SCAPIN 1214) dated 18 September 1946, subject: Repatriation of Japanese from Hawaii, Philippines, Okinawa, the Marianas Islands, and the Caroline Islands.

2. The following nationals will arrive at Uraga on 27 October 1946 aboard the vessel "James King":

Chinese - 3 Indo-Chinese - 7

Formosan - 24 Manchurian - 1

3. Transportation has not yet arrived for repatriation of these nationals. Until such time as transportation is arranged, the Imperial Japanese Government will make necessary arrangements to care for these nationals.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government will be notified when transportation is available for the above named personnel.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
