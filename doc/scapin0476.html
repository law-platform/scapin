MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Petroleum Allocation to Japanese Government January, February, March 1946.

3. It is further directed that on or before the 20th of each month, you will submit to this Headquarters a Diesel Fuel Oil report and a Gas Oil report which will include kerosene under this allocation, for the previous calendar month as follows:

a. Total stocks as of the last day of the month.

b. Receipts from production.

c. Deliveries accepted against releases herein announced.

d. Receipts from other sources.

e. Deliveries to consumers against allocation prescribed above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
