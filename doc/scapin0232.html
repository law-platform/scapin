GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 686 (3 Nov 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 232)

3 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Release of Airfields to the Japanese Government.

1. Release of the following named airfields to the Japanese Government for the purpose of conversion into salt beds is approved:

Noshiro Ego

Iwaki Tamshima

Sado Fukuyama

Toyohashi Bofu

Kawawa Takuma

Fuji Tokushima

Karasu Matsuyama

Shirako Saegi

Kakogawa Takase

Tomakomai Ya▲sushiro

Atsukeshi Tsuiki

Mitohigashi Fukuoka

Fujieda Ibusuki

Sano Sone

2. The other airfields requested in your letter, C. L. O. No. 415, dated 31 October 1945, are being retained by U. S. Forces.

3. It is directed that you contact the Commanding Generals, Sixth and Eighth U.S. Armies concerning details regarding the conversion of airfields listed in Par.1 above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
