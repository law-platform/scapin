AG 121.7 (4 Jun 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-999)

APO 500

4 June 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Transfers and Payments from Blocked Accounts.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 121.7 (24 Nov 45) ESS/FI dated 24 November 1945 from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, subject: “Elimination of War Profits and Reorganization of National Finances.”

b. Memoradum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 132 (2 Jan 46) ESS/FI dated 2 January 1946 from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, subject: “Exemptions and Release of Funds from Blocked Accounts.”

2. Memorandum referred to in paragraph 1b above is rescinded.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government is authorized to transter from accounts blocked by memorandum referred to in paragraph 1a to accounts restricted by the Emergency Financial Measures of the Imperial Japanese Government an amount not to exceed 10,000 yen per person. Any amounts previously withdrawn from these blocked accounts or otherwise received as payment will be deducted from the 10,000 yen authorized herein.

4. Transfers will be made only for payment for restoration of facilities destroyed as a result of the war. Such facilities must be essential for civilian production or personal housing rehabilitation.

5. The Bank of Japan will be directed to submit to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, not later than the 15th of each month, a report of amounts released as provided by this memorandum.

6. Direct communication between the Ministry of Finance, the Bank of Japan and interested Staff Sections of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers is suthorized to implement this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 999)

WKL/JCS/fg

23 May 1946

1. a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 121.7 (24 Nov 45) ESS/FI dated 24 November 1945, GHQ, SCAP, subject: "Elimination of War Profits and Reorganization of National Finances" prevented payments of claims arising in connection with production or supply of war materials, from war damage, or from the construction or conversion of war plants unless such payments were deposited in blocked accounts.

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 132 (2 Jan 46) ESS/FI dated 2 January- 1946, GHQ, SCAP, subject: "Exemptions and Releases of Funds from Blocked Accounts" authorized certain releases and exemptions from provisions of memorandum AG 121.7 (24 Nov 45) ESS/FI. Releases permitted were from claims arising from war damages up to 5,000 yen, claims arising from production of war materials or construction o. conversion of war plants up to 10,000 yen. Also authorized were certain releases for living and household expenses, releases up to 5,000 yen for repair, construction or purchase of a house damaged by war, for payment of taxes and governmental charges to finance reconversion of war plants to peacetime production and for repayment of debts.

2. The various releases and exemptions authorized under the provision of AG 132, as outlined in paragraph 1b above, have resulted in a continual and increasing drain on the accounts concerned with resulting expansion of the currency in circulation.

3. At the present time a plan is being considered to drastically scale down or assess a confiscatory tax on all claims arising from war damage, for production or supply of war materials and from the construction or conversion of war plants. Accordingly, in anticipation of such action the accompanying directive is intended as a basis for equitable release for the purpose of financing legitimate restoration of essential facilities destroyed by war.

4. Inasmuch as 5,000 yen have previously been released from war damage claims and 10,000 yen from claims arising from production of war materials or construction or conversion of war plants, the criteria of up to 10,000 yen releases for individuals for essential house repair and construction and restoration of facilities essential for civilian production is considered equitable. Funds previously received will be deducted to give fair treatment to claimants and war sufferers who have not received a previous payment.

5. The Bank of Japan is made responsible for administration and for necessary reports. At the present time a report is submitted on the 15th of each month on blocked accounts and the new report can be incorporated as a part of the established report.

6. This action does not affect the interests of Korea.

7. This is an administrative memorandum and does not require supervision by the Eighth Army.

W. K. L.
