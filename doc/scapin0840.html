AG 000.5 (25 Mar 46) LS

(SCAPIN - 840)

APO 500

25 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

The Following named Japanese will be apprehended as Suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, EIGHTH Army, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest possible date.

NAME

RANK OR TITLE

DATE, CAMP, ADDRESS OR ASSIGNMENT

BEPPU, Takeshi

Civ. Guard

Employed by Sumitomo Co. from 1943 to 1945 at Niihama, Hiroshima Area, Shikoku, Japan. Probable home address: Niihama-Shi, Hira-Kata-Cho, 226 Go.

ENJO, Genzo

Captain

Was stationed on Chichi Jima.

Probable home address: 1770 Nishikora, Inagakami-Gun, Shiga.

IIJIMA, Mayuki

Ensign (Navy)

Was stationed on Chichi Jima. Probable home address: c/o Odera, Aza Shinden, Fujishima-Mura, Yoshida-Gun, Fukin Prefecture.

ISOGAI, Sunji

Lt. (Navy)

Was stationed on Chichi Jima. Probable home address: 136 Oga, Tateyama.

KAJIYAMA, Shin (Arita)

Captain

Former Captain of ship Brazil Maru. Master of Ship No. 7, Banei Maru, running between Hakodate and Muroran. Employed by Mitsui Steamship Lines, No.2 Mitsui-Kan, 1 Muro-Machi, Nihenbashi-Ku, Tokyo. Crew Dept. No.3, No.1 Section. Probable home address: c/o Shizuka Komura, Ise Cho, Nakanojo-Machi, Azamagun, Gunma-Ken.

KAMBI, Hatsuaki

Supply man at Tokyo Branch Camp No. 1 (Yokohama)until 20 July 44. Was employed in Shibuya District, Tokyo. Probable home address: c/o Yamato Keichi, Tokyo-To, Yodobashi-Ku, Totsuka-Machi, 4 (Yon) Chome 692 or Tokyo-to, Mukojima-Ku, Terajima-Machi, 4 (Yon) Chome 179.

KARIYA, Yasuo

Captain

Was stationed on Chichi Jima. Probable home address: Takasuki, Tayomura, Niihara-Gun, Ibaraki.

KUNORI, Hikaru

Sgt.

Was stationed on Chichi Jima. Probable home address: 508 Tatsue-Mura, Shimoena-Gun, Nagano.

MIYAZAKI, Kenji

Lt. Cmdr. (Navy)

Was stationed on Chichi Jima. Probable home address: Shimonobeta, Shiota-Machi, Fujitsu-Gun, Saga.

MORI, Buhichi

Guard

Ofuna Naval Camp November 1944 - April 1945. Probable home address: Shiozuoka-Ken, Haiban-Gun, Kamigawane-Mura, Sendo No. 1038.

MORIMOTO, Kakichi

Lt.

NAIKI, Hisakichi

Captain

NISHI, Masanori

Guard
