GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 319.1 (11 Dec 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-419)

APO 500

11 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Periodic Reports on Banking Institutions.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government is hereby directed to order the Bank of Japan to furnish this Headquarters periodic reports on the operations of the banking institutions of Japan in accordance with the requirements of the following schedule:

Type of Report Time Limit For Submission

Consolidated Monthly Report of Financial Institutions One month

Bank of Japan - Regular ten-day Report Five days

Bank of Japan - Special ten-day Report Ten days

Deposit Fund Management Bureau - Monthly Report Twenty days

Special Banks - Individual Monthly Reports Twenty days

Certain Private Institutions - Individual Monthly Reports Twenty days

2. The Consolidated Monthly Report of Financial Institutions will consist of two parts:

a. Part I - All special banks, all ordinary banks, all savings banks, and all trust companies.

(1) A consolidated statement of assets and liabilities which will include the following items:

Assets

Cash; Deposits with Domestic Banks; National Government Bonds; Local Government Bonds; Corporate Securities; Other Securities; Call Loans; Bills Discounted; Overdrafts; Loans on deeds; Loans on Stocks and Bonds; Loans on Guarantees; Other Loans on Bills; Unsecured Loans; Foreign Exchange Accounts; Inter-Office Accounts-Foreign Branches; Correspondent Accounts - Foreign Banks; Real Estate Owned; Bank premises including equipment; Capital Uncalled; Other Assets.

Liabilities

Government Deposits; Current Deposits; Ordinary Deposits; National Saving Assoc. Deposits; Ordinary Saving Deposits; Time Deposits; Restricted Deposits; Blocked Accounts of Non-Residents; Other Blocked Accounts; Other Deposits; Foreign Exchange Accounts; Due to Bank of Japan; Other Borrowed Money; Inter-Office Accounts - Foreign Branches; Correspondent Accounts - Foreign Banks, Other Liabilities; Reserve Accounts (itemized) Surplus; Capital Stock; Current Net Income.

This consolidated statement will contain the following separate columns:

Special Banks (Excluding the Bank of Japan); Ordinary Banks, Savings Banks; Trust Companies; Total; Change Compared with Last Month.

The number of institutions reporting will be shown at the top of each columns.

(2) A supplemental statistical section which will include the following items:

(a) A separate report of external assets and liabilities which will include on the asset side:

Due from Foreign Banks; Foreign Securities; Foreign Loans; Other External Assets on the Liability side:

Due to Foreign Banks; Blocked Accounts of Non-Residents; Other External Liabilities.

(b) Number and amount of new loans granted during the month classified as to whether they are loans to industry, loans to commerce, loans to agriculture, loans to finance urban real estate, and other loans.

(c) Number and amount of loan renewals granted during month.

(d) Number and amount of loans past due, loans with interest arrears exceeding three months and other doubtful loans.

(e) Number and amount of Loans to closed war time financing and colonial development companies and banks. Loans in this category will not be reported under sub-paragraphs (c) and (d) above.

(f) Interest or discount rates charged maximum, minimum and average rate.

(g) Rate of interest on deposits classified by type of deposit.

(h) Last dividend declared on capital stock; date of dividend and rate.

b. Part II - Agricultural Credit Cooperatives, Urban Credit Cooperatives and Mutual Loan Companies:

(1) A consolidated statement of assets and liabilities similar to Par 2a (1) above but with the itemization of loan accounts changed to conform with the nature of the operations conducted by credit cooperatives and mutual loan companies. The consolidated statement will contain the following separate columns: Agricultural Credit Cooperatives, Urban Credit Cooperatives, Mutual Loan Companies, Total and Change Compared with Last Month. The number of institutions reporting will be shown at the top of each column.

(2) A supplemental statistics section similar to par 2a (2) but with the second sub-paragraph thereunder changed to read as follows:

“Number and amount of new loans granted during the month classified as to whether they are loans on real estate, loans for purchase of equipment, loans for production purposes, loans for repayment of debts, and loans for consumption purposes.”

3. The special banks required to file individual reports are:

Industrial Bank of Japan; Hypothec Bank of Japan; Yokohama Specie Bank; Hokkaido Colonial Bank; Central Bank for Commercial and Manufacturing Associations; Central Bank for Agriculture and Forestry; Peoples Bank; Pension Bank; National Reconstruction Bank.

4. The private institutions required to file individual reports are:

Teikoku Bank; Mitsubishi Bank; Sumitomo Bank; Yasuda Bank; Sanwa Bank; Nomura Bank; Nippon Savings Bank; Mitsui Trust Co.; Sanwa Trust Co.; Sumitomo Trust Co.; Mitsubishi Trust Co.; Yasuda Trust Co.

5. All reports except those of the Bank of Japan will be dated as of the close of business on the last day of each month. The Bank of Japan’s report will be dated as of the close of business on the tenth, twentieth and the last day of each month. All time limits are calculated from the date of each report.

6. The regular ten-day report of the Bank of Japan will be prepared in accordance with the form now in use. The special ten-day report of the Bank of Japan will be prepared in the same form as the daily statement of assets and liabilities now employed by the Bank of Japan. The monthly report of the Deposit Funds Management Bureau will be in the same form as the present quarterly report issued by that Bureau. The individual monthly reports of the special banks and certain private financial institutions will be in the form of a statement of assets and liabilities and a supplemental statistical section as prescribed in par. 2(a) above.

7. In the compilation of all reports required by Paragraph #1, the Imperial Japanese Government will direct all institutions to exclude from their reports, all assets and all liabilities of branches outside of Japan. The net amounts due from or payable to such branches shall be reported under assets and liabilities, respectively, opposite the heading: Inter-Office Accounts - Foreign Branches.

8. Within ten days this Headquarters will be furnished with copies of the forms of report to be submitted in compliance with this memorandum. The requirements with respect to the ten day report of the Bank of Japan become effective immediately. All other reports will be submitted for the first time as of the close of business 30 November 1945. All reports will be prepared in English and submitted in twelve (12) copies.

9. For purposes of this memorandum the following definitions will apply:

a. “External Assets”: defined in memorandum from this Headquarters, dated 22 September 1945, file AG 130 (22 Sept 1945), subject: “Control of Financial Transactions.”

b. “Foreign Securities”: securities issued by juridical persons incorporated under the laws of, or having more than 25% of their total assets invested in, a country other than Japan.

c. “Foreign Loans”: loans extended to natural persons residing outside of Japan or to juridical persons incorporated under the laws of, or having more than 25% of their total assets, invested in, a country other than Japan.

d. “Restricted Deposits”: deposits and trust accounts subject to the blocking provisions of memorandum from this Headquarters, dated 24 Nov 1945, file AG 121. 7 (24 Nov. 1945) ESS/FI, subject: “Elimination of War Profits and Reorganization of National Finances.”

e. “Blocked accounts of non-residents: deposit accounts of any person residing outside of Japan, regardless of nationality, and regardless of whether such accounts were blocked prior to or subsequent to 15 August 1945.

10. An acknowledgement of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H.W.Allen,
/t/ H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
