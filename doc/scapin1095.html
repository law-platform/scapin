GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (31 Jul 46) CCS

(SCAPIN-1095)

APO 500

31 July 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Discontinuance of Aeronautical Radio Facilities.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will discontinue operation of and will dismantle all Japanese aeronautical radio facilities, including those formerly operated by the Aeronautical Bureau, Ministry of Transportation, except as listed on Inclosure 1 of this memorandum.

2. The dismantling of the stations will be completed within 60 days from the date of this memorandum. All dismantled equipment will be promptly turned over to the occupation forces. A complete report on the disposition of each station will be forwarded to this headquarters by the Japanese Government.

3. The Japanese Government will continue to operate the stations listed on Inclosure 1 in accordance with existing directives.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

31 July 1946

MEMORANDUM:

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN- 1095), File AG 676.3(31 Jul 46)CCS, this Headquarters, Dated 31 July 1946, Subject: "Discontinuance of Aeronautical Radio Facilities."

1. With reference to the subject memorandum the following is published for the infonation of all concerned.

2. The civil aeronautical facilities in Japan were formerly operated by the Aeronautical Bureau of the Ministry of Transportation. However, the Aeronautical Bureau was dissolved by SCAPIN-301. Since certain air navigational aids were being operated by the Aeronautical Bureau for the U. S. Air Forces, it was necessary to transfer by SCAPIN-536 dated 2 January 1946 all radio facilities of the Bureau to the Board of Communications to insure their continued operation. The Board of Communications was allowed to retain all the aeronautical radio equipment until the extent of the need for Japanese equipment could be determined by our air forces.

3. The only Japanese aeronautical radio facilities now required in Japan are those given on Inclosure 1 of the subject memorandum. Therefore, in accordance with the policy of SCAP the unnecessary stations are being ordered dismantled by this directive.

4. It is the responsibility of the Occupation Forces to insure that the dismantling of equipment directed by this memorandum is carried out as directed; that the dismantled equipment suitable for civilian use be turned over intact to the Home Ministry; and that equipment of a military nature be processed in accordance with current directives.

5. Instructions to the Occupation Forces will follow by command channel letter.

R. G. HERSEY

Lt Col, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
