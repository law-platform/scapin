GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.711 (9 Oct 45) GB

(SCAPIN - 112)

APO 500

9 October 1945

TO :Japanese Army and Navy Liaison Committee
FROM :A.C of S., G-2, GHQ, SCAP
SUBJECT :Army Demobilization Reports.

1. In addition to the reports on demobilization of Japanese operational units now submitted weekly by the War Ministry, it is desired that beginning with the report due on 15 Oct 45 and every week thereafter, a separate report be submitted containing a complete list of all non-operational units demobilized together with their strength and date of demobilization as well as a list of all non-operational units not yet demobilized showing strength demobilized and strength remaining. This latter list will include all depot units, railway and shipping units, schools, arsenals, etc.

2. It is requested that the above lists be submitted in the name form, that is, by General Army, area Army and Army, as the lists for the operational units. It is emphasized that the new-type list will be delivered along with and at the same time as the list of the operational units previously submitted.

3. It is also desired that the new-type report include figures for these in the Guazoku class. Figures for this class will be included in parentheses in order that a differentiation may be made between Army personnel and civilian employees.

For the A. C. of S., G-2:

/s/F. P. Munson
/t/F. P. MUNSON
Colonel, GSO
G-2, GHQ, SCAP
