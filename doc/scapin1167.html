GENERAL HEADQUARTERS SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

30 August 1948

MEMORANDUM :

SUBJECT : Information of general application pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN-1167), file AG 561.4 (30 Aug 46) ESS/IN, GHQ, SCAP, dated 30 August 1946, subject: "Wood Ship Construction."

1. With reference to memorandum (SCAPIN-1167) to the Imperial Japanese Government, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The purpose of this directive is to clarify directive (SCAPIN 30) to the central Liaison Committee, dated 15 September 1945, subject: "Repair of inoperative Ships," in which the Imperial Japanese Government was urged to expedite the completion of non-combatant merchant vessels that were under construction at the time of the surrender.

3. This clarification is necessary to establish officially the number, size and location of ships authorized for completion in order to enable the Occupational Forces to maintain the proper supervision over ship construction so that only those vessels authorized will be worked on.

/s/ R. G. Hersey

/t/ R. G. HERSEY,

Lt. Col., AGD

Asst. Adj. General.

Distribution:

Same as (SCAPIN-1167) less Imperial Japanese Government

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1167)

ESS/IN JZR/GEM/rhw

16 August 1946

1. Memorandum to the IJG (SCAPIN ) dated August 1946, subject: "Wood Ship Construction. "

2. The above memorandum contains an official list of all wood vessels of over 100 gross tons for which completion has been authorized, also the size and location.

3. The purpose of this directive to the IJG and letter of command instructions to the Eighth Army is to provide them with an authentic list of authorized wood vessels of over 100 gross tons, and to provide a check on unauthorized construction.

4. This is an administrative matter and requires routine surveillance of the shipyards.

J. Z. R.
