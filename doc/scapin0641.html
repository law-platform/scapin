GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 680.2 (21 Jan 46) GS

(SCAPIN - 641)

APO 500

21 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Applications for Permission to Enter South Korea.

1． Reference is made to:

a. C. L. O. Memoranda 1336 (5.1), dated 24 December 1945, C. L. O. 1443 (5.1), dated 29 December 1945, C. L. O. 86 (5.1), dated 8 January 1946, and C. L. O. 180(5.1), dated 13 January 1946, subjects: "Applications for Permission to Enter South Korea".

b. Memorandum for Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 680.2 (15 Jan 46) GS, dated 15 January 1946, Subject: "Application for Permission to Enter South Korea."

2. Pursuant to the policy set forth in the Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government referred to in paragraph 1b, the applications contained in the memoranda referred to in paragraph 1a are disapproved.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
