GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 383.7 (1 Dec 45) CIS

(SCAPIN - 378)

1 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Japanese Personnel.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will apprehend and deliver to the authorities of the Omori Prison Camp for incarceration not later than 12 o'clock midnight, 12 December 1945, the following individuals:

a. AIKAWA, Yoshisuke, former President of Manshu Jukogyo K.K.

b. ANDO, Saburo, Home Minister 1943-1944

c. AMAU, Eiji, former President of Board of Information

d. AOKI, Kazuo, former Financial Adviser to the Nanking Government

e. ARIMA, Yoriyasu, former President of the Imperial Marine Products Co., Ltd.

f. FUJIWARA, Ginjiro, former Supervisor of Manshurian War Industries

g. FURUNO, Inosuke, President of Domei Tsushinsha

h. GOKO, Kiyoshi, former Director of Imperial Rule Assistance Association

i. GOTO, Fumio, former Vice-President of the Imperial Rule Assistance Association

j. HATA, Hikosaburo, former Chief of Staff of the Kwantung Army

k. HATA, Shunroku, Field Marshal

l. HIRANUMA, Kiichiro, former Premier

m. HIROTA, Koki, former Premier

n. HONDA, Kumataro, former Ambassador to Nanking

o. HOSHINO, Naoki, Chief Secretary to the TOJO Cabinet

p. IDA, Iwakusu, former Director of the Imperial Rule Assistance Association

q. IKEDA, Seihin, former Finance Minister

r. IKEZAKI, Chuko, former Vice-Minister of Education

s. ISHIDA, Otogoro, Major General

t. ISHIHARA, Koichiro, former President of the Ishihara South Seas Industries K. K.

u. KAMISAGO, Masashichi, Major General

v. KAWABE, Shozo, former Commander in Chief in Burma

w. KIKUCHI, Takeo, retired Lieutenant General

x. KINOSHITA, Eiichi, Major General

y. KOBAYASHI, Junichiro, former Director of Imperial Rule Assistance Association

z. KOBAYASHI, Seizo, retired Admiral

aa. KODAMA, Yoshio, Politician

bb. MATSUZAKA, Hiromasa, former Justice Minister

cc. MIZUNO, Rentaro, former Director of Imperial Rule Assistance Association

dd. MUTAGUCHI, Renya, retired Lieutenant General

ee. NAGATOMO, Tsugio, Major General

ff. NAKAJIMA, Chikuhei, former President of Nakajima Aircraft Co., Ltd.

gg. NAKAMURA, Aketo, Lieutenant General

hh. NISHIMOTO, Morimasa, Prince, Field Marshal

ii. NISHIO, Toshizo, retired General

jj. NOMI, Toshiro, Major General

kk. OKABE, Nagakage, Viscount

ll. OKAWA, Shumei, one-time professor of Tokyo Imperial University

mm. OKURA, Kunihiko, former President of Odawara Paper Mills

nn. ONO, Koichi, Major General

oo. OTA, Kozo, former Minister of Education

pp. OTA, Masataka, former Director of Imperial Rule Assistance Party

qq. SAKURAI, Hyogoro, former political adviser to military administration of Burma

rr. SASAKAWA, Ryoichi, President of the Kokusui Taishuto

ss. SATO, Kenryo, former Secretary General of the Supreme War Council

tt. SHIMOMURA, Hiroshi, former Minister without Portfolio in Suzuki Cabinet

uu. SHINDO, Kazuma, former President of the GENYOSHA

vv. SHONO, Suehiko, former Justice Minister and Communications Minister

ww. SHIOTEN, Nobutake, retired Lieutenant General

xx. SHORIKI, Matsutaro, former President of Yomiuri Shimbun

yy. TADA, Hayao, Lieutenant General

zz. TAKAHASHI, Sankichi, retired Admiral

aaa. TAKAJI, Shigeto, Major General

bbb. TANI, Masanobu, Former Foreign Minister

ccc. TOKUTOMI, Iichiro, ▲▲▲

ddd. TOYODA, Soemu, Admiral

eee. TSUDA, Shingo, former ▲▲▲ to Bank of Japan

fff. USHIROKU, Jun, General

ggg. YOKOYAMA, Yui, reported address - Chigasaki

2. In the event that Japanese Government is unable to deliver any of these individuals by the appointed time, it will make a report in detail giving the present location of the individuals not arrested, the reason for failure to arrest, and the date by which deliver can be expected.

3. In the event that individuals ordered apprehended are abroad, last known address will be reported.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
