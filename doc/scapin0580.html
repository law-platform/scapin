GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 334 (11 Jan 46) GD

(SCAPIN-580)

APO 500

11 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Appointment of Civilian Merchant Marine Committee.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government AG 334 (9 Nov 45) GD on the same subject.

2. Misunderstandings have apparently arisen in connection with this Memorandum and the following is forwarded in clarification thereof:

a. The Civilian Merchant Marine Committee will continue to function for the duration of SCAJAP operations.

b. All appointees and employees of the Civilian Merchant Marine Committee become government employees upon appointment or employment. They will be paid by, and be under the exclusive control of the government, and will sever all affiliation with private companies while holding office or positions with the Civilian Merchant Marine Committee. (In this connection the word “affiliation” in paragraph 2 of referenced Memorandum is intended to apply to former affiliations).

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
