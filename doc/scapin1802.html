GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 524 (16 Oct 47) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1802)

APO 500

16 October 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Movement of German Property to United States Eighth Army Warehouses

1. The Japanese Government is directed to inventory, pack, crate, and deliver to United States Eighth Army Warehouses the property of the German concerns and/or organizations listed in Inclosure 1. All inventories will be in English, will list the name of the owner, address of location of the property, each type of property, number of pieces of each type of property, yen value of each item, and total valuation. Each inventory will note thereon SCAPIN 1802.

2. It is further directed that a representative of the Japanese Government centact Headquarters, United States Eighth Army, Yokohama, within three (3) days of receipt of this memorandum in order that this program may be coordinated in an efficient manner. All operations will be under supervision of the United States Eighth Army, and no operations will begin until such time as designated by the United States Eighth Army.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
