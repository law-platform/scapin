MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Additional Application for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in Fourth Quarter of 1947.

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. No. 8219(LO 3747-FB/FP), 21 October 1947, subject, "Additional Application for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in the Fourth Quarter of 1947."

2. Application for permission to use liquid gold, as requested by reference 1 above, is approved in the amounts and by companies listed. Liquid gold stocks in the pooled reserve under custody of the Ministry of Finance will be drawn upon to supply the liquid gold authorized. The firms listed may be used to further process the liquid gold as noted.
