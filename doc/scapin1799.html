GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.7 (13 Oct 47) GD

(SCAPIN - 1799)

APO 500

13 October 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Petroleum Products Required for Japanese Antarctic Whaling Operations in 1947

1. References:

a. Memorandum for Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 463.7 (24 Sep 47)GD (SCAPIN 1781), 24 September 1947, subject, "Petroleum Products Required for Japanese Antarctic Whaling Operations in 1947."

b. Memorandum for Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 800.217 (21 Jun 47/F1)NR (SCAPIN 1737), 21 June 1947, subject, "Japanese Whaling Operations in the Antarctic, 1947-48 Season."

2. Paragraph 3 of reference 1a above is rescinded and the following substituted therefor:

Under authority contained in cited reference, paragraph 2, the Japanese Government is authorized to receive the quantities of petroleum products set forth below for the Antarctic Whaling Expedition 1947-48 season. Unit is barrel, except grease which is long ton. (Figures in parentheses are kiloliter or kilogram.)

Navy 7-0-2 Diesel Navy Special Oil Kerosene Gasoline Lubricating Oil Grease

First Fleet 31,416.7 (4,995.5) 175,434.8 (27.895.5) 377.3 (60) 40.2 (6.4) 1,496.6 (237.97) 2.95 (3000)

Second Fleet 68,952.5 (10,964) 111,664.3 (17,755.5) 377.3 (60) 40.9 (6.5) 2,404.9 (382.4) 2.95 (3000)

TOTALS 100,369.3 (15,959.5) 267,099.1 (45,651) 754.6 (120) 81.1 (12.9) 3,901.5 (620.37) 5.9 (6000)

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
