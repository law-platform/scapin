GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 602 (13 Nov 46) CIE

(SCAPIN-1334)

APO 500

13 November 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Disposition of State-Owned Land Used by Religious Institutions.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum, CLO No. 4842 (PP - E), 20 September 1946, subject: Disposition of State-Owned Land and Forests Used by Shrines and Temples, and to the draft texts of the law and ordinance inclosed therewith.

2. No objection is offered to the proposed law and ordinance, provided that such provisions as are in conflict with the standards set forth in paragraph 3, below, are modified or deleted.

3. In the disposition of State-owned land under the proposed law and ordinance, the Imperial Japanese Government is directed to adhere to the following standards:

a. The title to all public land presently utilized by religious institutions and necessary for their religious functions shall, upon application to the appropriate Japanese Government agency, be given to such institutions free of charge:

(1) Provided the religious institution was in possession of the land prior to 1868 and the State gave no compensation to the religious institution when it assumed title to the land, or,

(2) Provided the land was obtained by the religious institution from non-governmental sources and without the expenditure of public funds.

b. Whether land not necessary for the religious functions of religious institutions but which otherwise comes within the provisions of paragraph a, above, shall be given free of charge shall be determined in accordance with such criteria as may be formulated by the Japanese Government.

c. Title to State-owned forest land in custody of religious institutions shall remain in the State, and the present custodial system shall be terminated. Compensation may be paid to religious institutions for their custodial interest in such land in accordance with the provisions of paragraph e, below.

d. Religious institutions may be permitted to purchase such other land now in their possession or custody at one-half market value if it is essential to religious functions, but in no case if it is primarily revenue-producing land. However, religious institutions may exchange land of equal value in lieu of payment for land which is presently devoted to essential religious purposes.

e. Religious institutions may be compensated for the reasonable cost of improvements made on land of which they will be deprived of possession and custody, but only insofar as these improvements have enhanced the value of such land.

f. The provisions for the transfer to religious institutions of land title shall not apply in the case of military shrines (Yasukuni Jinja, Gokoku Jinja, Shokonsha). Classifications existing on 15 December 1945 shall determine the status of such shrines. 4. The Imperial Japanese Government will submit comprehensive reports to this headquarters monthly describing in detail all actions taken in the disposition of State-owned land presently used by religious institutions.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
