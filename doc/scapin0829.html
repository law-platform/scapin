GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 370.05 (19 Mar 46) GC

(SCAPIN-829)

APO 500

19 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Suspension of Repatriation from Japan of Koreans Residing in Northern Korea.

1.Reference is made to the following:

a.Memorandum from this headquarters file AG 370.05 (17 Nov 45)GC, (SCAPIN-295), dated 17 November 1945, subject: "Repatriation of Non-Japanese from Japan."

b.Memorandum from this headquarters AG 053 (17 Feb 46)GC (SCAPIN-749ﾏﾏ), dated 17 February 1946, subject: "Registration of Koreans, Chinese, Ryukyuans and Formosans."

2.The Imperial Japanese Government upon receipt of this memorandum will:

a.Suspend repatriation from Japan of Koreans whose destination in Korea is north of the 38 degree parallel of north latitude.

b.Keep this suspension in effect until further notification.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
