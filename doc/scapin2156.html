16 June 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Rescission of Memorandums for the Japanese Government.

The following Memorandums for the Japanese Government are hereby rescinded:

a. AG 201-TAKEUCHI, K. (31 Jan 46)CIS, SCAPIN 682, 31 January 1946, subject: Removal from Office of TAKEUCHI, Kisaburo.

b. AG 014.1 (3 May 46)GS, SCAPIN 919, 3 May 1946, subject: Removal and Exclusion from Public Office of Diet Member.

c. AG 091.1 (8 Jun 46)GS, SCAPIN 1007, 8 June 1946, subject: Removal and Exclusion of Mayoral Appointee.

d. AG 091.112 (4 Sep 46)GS, SCAPIN 1175, 4 September 1946, subject: Temporary Retention of Public Official under SCAPIN 550 - Daigoro YASUKAWA.

e. AG 091.112 (4 Sep 46)GS, SCAPIN 1176, 4 September 1946, subject: Temporary Retention of Public Official under SCAPIN 550 - Kogoro UEMURA.

f. AG 095 (26 Sep 46)PH, SCAPIN 1231, 26 September 1946, subject: Removal of Yoshisuke YASUMI, Pharmacy Section, Bureau of Hygiene, Ministry of Health and Social Affairs.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
