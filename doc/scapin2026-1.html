GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.7 (11 Jul 49) GD

(SCAPIN - 2026/1)

APO 500

19 October 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Transfer of Proceeds of Imported Petroleum Products to the Counterpart Fund.

1. Reference: Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 386.7 (11 Jul 49)GD, SCAPIN 2026, 11 July 1949, subject: Transfer of Proceeds of Imported Petroleum Products to the Counterpart Fund.

2. Paragraph 3 of the memorandum referred to in the preceding paragraph is rescinded and the following substituted therefor:

"With reference to paragraph 3e of reference 1a, under present price criteria as established by the Japanese Government Price Board, an allowance has been established to cover the landing costs of petroleum products. Therefore, the International Trade Bureau of the Japanese Government will pay from funds under their control all expenses incurred in landing imported petroleum products consigned to Japanese civil economy. This responsibility shall include payment of expenses incurred by the Japanese Government in the withdrawal or diversion of petroleum products delivered to the Japanese Government from the United States military stocks, afloat or ashore. The assignment of this responsibility to commercial distributors is prohibited.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
