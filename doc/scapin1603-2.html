MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Reactivation of the Coastal Radio Service of the Japanese Ministry of Communications to Handle Ship-Shore Traffic for Allied Merchant Shipping.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 676.3 (25 July 47)CCS, (SCAPIN 1603/1), dated 25 July 1947, subject same as above.

2. Inclosure 3 to the reference memorandum is hereby rescinded and superseded by Inclosure 1 to this memorandum.

3. Direct communications between Civil Communications or other Staff Sections of General Headquarters Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Ministry of Communications, within the scope of this memorandum, are authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

Acceptance Messages, Rates, Accounting Procedures and Settlement of Accounts for Messages to and from British Commonwealth Merchant Shipping

1. Messages of the following types only will be accepted for transmission:

a. Estimated time of arrival of shipping.

b. Control of ship traffic.

c. Official shipping information.

d. Navigation or distress messages from shipping, in accordance with General Radio Regulations (Cairo Revision 1938).

e. Full rate messages from any person on board ships of British Commonwealth registry to any person or organization in Japan,

f. Full rate messages from any person or organization in Japan authorized to possess and use British Armed Forces Special Vouchers (BAFSV) to any person on board ships of British Commonwealth registry.

g. Messages exchanged between ships of British Commonwealth registry and Japanese coastal stations may be in plain language or in code and will be subject to all applicable censorship rules and regulations.

2. Rates for messages between British Commonwealth merchant shipping and Japan will be expressed in sterling and are as follows:

a. The land line charge between Japanese coastal radio station and any point in Japan will be two (2) pence per full rate word.

b. The Japanese coastal radio station charge will be six (6) pence per full rate word.

c. The ship station charge will be four and one half (4 1/3) pence pet full rate word.

3. Messages originating both on board ship and in Japan will be sent prepaid. Payment will be made in Japan in British Armed Forces Special Vouchers, hereinafter referred to as BAFSV's, at rates per word expressed in sterling as set forth in paragraph 2 above.

4. Japan's share in the revenue from the ship-shore service will be the sum of the coastal station and land line changes for both incoming and outgoing messages.

5. All BAFSV revenues collected by the Japanese Ministry of Communications for messages sent prepaid to ships of British Commonwealth registry will be deposited semimonthly with the "Transfer Agent for British Commonwealth Telecommunications Funds" in accordance with instructions given in Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.23 (12 Aug 47)CCS, (SCAPIN 1762), dated 12 August 1947, subject: Osaka- Colombo Radiotelegraph Circuit.

6. Final settlement of the net balance will be effected by the Transfer Agent as set forth in SCAPIN-I762. Such accounts and payments to be kept entirely separate from the Osaka-Colombo accounts and payments in view of the different carriers. The carriers' representative is General post Office, London, through United Kingdom Liaison Mission.

7. Japanese Public Acceptance Office of the Ministry of Communications where prepaid EFM and SCM radiotelegraph messages may be filed for transmission by British Commonwealth Occupation Force military personnel and by civilians authorized to be in possession of British Armed Forces Special Vouchers are those authorize by SCAPIN 1762 and listed in Inclosure 1 to that SCAPIN.

8. The accounting of message tolls will be the responsibility of the Transfer Agent for the British Government and the Ministry of Communications for the Japanese Government. Statements of accounts will be submitted by both agencies through General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

Inclosure 1 to SCAPIN 1603/2
