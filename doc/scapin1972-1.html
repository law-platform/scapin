GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.3 (11 Feb 49) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1972/1)

APO 500

29 July 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Provision of Telephone Services on the Premises of Hotels Operated by the Board of Trade.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.3 (11 Feb 49) CCS, SCAPIN 1972, dated 11 February 1949, subject same as above.

2. Action directed by reference given in paragraph 1 has been accomplished in all essential aspects. Full responsibility for providing adequate communication services for hotels operated by the Board of Trade is hereby transferred to the Japanese Government. The original memorandum is considered completed.

3. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Telecommunications, concerning matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

GEORGE R. CONNOR,
Colonel, AGD,
Acting Adjutant General.
