GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004 (24 May 47) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1703)

APO 500

24 May 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
Subject :Operation of Branches and Agencies in Japan Owned or Controlled by Persons Outside Japan

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 004 (14 Jan 46) ESS/FI, SCAPIN 592,14 January 1946, subject, “Operation of Branches and Agencies in Japan Owned or Controlled By Persons Outside of Japan,” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Paragraph two (2) of the above memorandum is amended to read “with the prior approval of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers” instead of “without the prior approval of this Headquarters.” Henceforth, all individual applications for transactions in the property of branches or agencies in Japan of concerns with the head offices outside of Japan, for the purpose of resuming the normal operations of such branches, or agencies, will be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Civil Property Custodian, for approval.

3. In this connection the Japanese Government is directed to submit to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, by 31 May 1947, a list of all branches or agencies in Japan of firms with head offices abroad which have created or invested in other companies since 14 August 1945.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
