GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (1 Nov 45) ESS-IN

(SCAPIN - 227)

1 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Request for use of Aluminum and Duraluminum scrap at Haneda Airport

1. Reference is made to CLO Memorandum No. 358, transmitting application of the Taiyo Sangyo Company for use of subject materials located at Haneda Airport.

2. Your attention is directed to Par. 3, Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, AG 402.5 (24 Sep 45) GD, Subject: Materials, Supplies and Equipment received or to be received from the Japanese Armed Forces, dated 24 September 1945, which designates the Japanese Home Ministry as the official agency to receive and account for all such materials.

3. The subject application should be directed to the Home Ministry, which should then take the matter up with the Eighth Army.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
