27 August 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Production of Communications Equipment and Parts.

1. Reference is made to the following memoranda for the Japanese Government:

a. SCAPIN 1583, file AG 413.5(26 Mar 47)CCS, dated 26 March 1947, subject: Manufacture and Sale of Office Intercommunicating Systems and Parts.

b. SCAPIN 5896-A, file AG 413.44 (29 Jul 48)CCS, dated 6 August 1948, subject: Production Plan of Radio Receiving Sets and Receiving Vacuum Tubes.

2. Reference memoranda are hereby rescinded effective on receipt of this memorandum:

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
