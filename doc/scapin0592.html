GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004 (14 Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-592)

APO 500

14 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Operation of Branches and Agencies in Japan, Owned or Controlled by Persons Outside of Japan.

1. Reference is made to C. L. O. Memorandum No. 1107 (2. 1) dated 14 December 1945, subject: “Transactions in Property in Japan Owned or Controlled by Persons Outside of Japan.”

2. Subject to the provisions of paragraphs 3 b and 3 c of memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386 (14 Nov 45) ESS/FI, subject: “Transactions in Property in Japan Owned or Controlled by Persons Outside of Japan”, the Ministry of Finance, without the prior approval of this Headquarters, is authorized to approve individual applications for transactions in the property of branches or agencies in Japan of concerns with head offices outside of Japan, for the purpose or resuming the normal operations of such branches or agencies, provided that:

a. Favorable consideration is given only to applications of branches or agencies which manufacture or distribute essential consumer goods, or commodities required for the production of such essential consumer goods or are engaged in services necessary to such production.

b. Transactions are limited to:

(1) The payment of wages, taxes and other expenses ordinarily incurred in the normal conduct of business activities within Japan.

(2) The payment of any expenses necessary for maintaining and preserving the property of such branch or agency.

c. Applications for permission to resume operations by specific branches or agencies are accompanied by the information specified in paragraph 3 of Memorandum file AG 386 (14 Nov 45) ESS/FI, unless previously submitted. One copy in English of applications approved by the Ministry of Finance, will be forwarded to this Headquarters.

3. Any branch or agency which is authorized to resume operations in accordance with this memorandum shall not engage in any transaction which, directly or indirectly, substantially diminishes or impairs the assets of such branch or agency within Japan or otherwise prejudicially affects the interest of the foreign owner in such branch or agency.

4. Where reconversion is considered necessary to the production of essential consumers’ goods by branches or agencies owned or controlled by persons outside of Japan, individual applications for such reconversion will be submitted in accordance with the procedures set forth in Memorandum, file AG 004 (28 Oct 45) ESS/IN, subject: “Reconversion of War Production Plants to Peacetime Production.”

5. This memorandum shall not be deemed to authorize:

a. Any transaction at the request or upon instructions of any person outside of Japan.

b. Any transaction or contract which involves any property located outside of Japan or any contract which is to be performed in whole or in part outside of Japan.

c. Any transaction which could not be effected without the approval of this Headquarters if the applicant were organized under the laws of, and controlled persons in, Japan.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
