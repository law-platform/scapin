GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 541.1 (14 May 46) GC

(SCAPIN- 952)

APO 500

14 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Request for Permission to Sail on Repatriation Vessels.

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. No 2221 (RG), dated 10 May 1946, subject as above.

2. The desires of the Overseas Japanese Relief Association, as set forth in the reference C.L.O. letter, are fully appreciated.

In view of the fact, however, that maintenance of morale of Japanese nationals abroad, as well as keeping them informed of current trends in Japan, is a responsibility of the local Allied Commanders concerned, the request for permission to send committee members to various areas on repatriation vessels is not favorably considered.

3. It is believed that any expression of gratitude on the part of the Japanese people to the local Allied authorities can be covered adequately by means of letters to the commanders concerned. It is not considered desirable for the Imperial Japanese Government to exert any control over Japanese nationals now abroad.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigedier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
