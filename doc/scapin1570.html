GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 423 (14 Mar 47) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN - 1570)

APO 500

14 March 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Property in the Custody of Nippon Yusen Kabushiki Kaisha Which Is Owned by United Nations Nationals.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 091.112(13 Sep 45)MG, SCAPIN 26, 13 September 1945, subject: Protection of Allied and Axis Property, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 130(22 Sep 45)ESS, SCAPIN 45, 22 September 1945, subject: Control of Financial Transactions, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

c. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 600,3(30 Nov 46)CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1370, 30 November 1946, subject: Preservation of Property in Japan Owned by United Nations and Their Nationals, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

d. LO 1243 (SF/FP), 24 August 1946, subject: Delivery to Shipper of Goods Taken Back by S.S. Nako-maru from U.S.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to advise the Nippon Yusen K.K. of the following facts concerning the merchandise referred to in 1d above:

a. Draft for $2,400.17 covering fifteen (15) cases of slide fasteners consigned to order of Bankers Trust Company, notify International Importing Company, was paid on 16 December 1941 by remitting the proceeds to Chase National Bank of the City of New York for credit to the blocked account of Nederlandsche Indische Handelsbank N.V., Kobe, Japan. The shipping documents were delivered to International Importing Company, which desires to recover the goods as soon as private trade transactions of this nature are authorized;

b. Draft for $24,855.31 covering two hundred one (201) cases of cotton and rayon mixed goods consigned to Chase National Bank of the City of New York, notify Mamiye and Hidary, was paid on 12 December 1941 by credit of said amount to the blocked account of Nederlandsche Indische Handelsbank N.V., Kobe, Japan. The shipping documents were delivered to Mamiye and Hidary, who were reimbursed for the amount of the draft by the Insurance Company of North America, which firm now has a financial interest in the merchandise.

3. It is further directed that subject cargoes be preserved and protected in accordance with paragraphs 1a, b and c above until such time as private trade is authorized and the Nippon Yusen K.K. may ship the merchandise to the United States.

4. A report will be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Civil Property Custodian by 15 April 1947 concerning the present storage place and condition of the goods.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
