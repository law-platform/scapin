GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 561.4 (28 Aug 48) ESS/IND

(SCAPIN - 1929)

APO 500

28 August 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Construction of Steel and Wood Vessels

1. Rescission. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 561.4 (22 Aug 46) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 1148, dated 22 August 1946, subject: Construction of Steel and Wood Vessels.

2. The construction and conversion of all steel vessels regardless of size or tonnage and any wood vessel of over 100 gross tons is prohibited without the specific approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

3. Conversion shall be defined as the alteration of the principal dimensions, and/or the design of the vessel.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ A. J. Rehe
for /t/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1929)

GEN/EPS/gep

16 Aug 46

1. Reference is rescission of SCAPIN 1148, dated 22 August 1946, subject: Construction of Steel and Wood Vessels.

2. In view of the increasing number of proposed conversions and the evidenced desire of Japanese officials to call such conversions repairs, it is deemed necessary to issue a directive requiring that permission be obtained from SCAP for such conversion work.

3. The above is required in order to clarify SCAP’s position relating to the control of materials, the rebuilding of the Japanese merchant fleet, and utilization of the shipyards.

4. Action taken: Memorandum to the Japanese Government rescinds SCAPIN 1148 and directs that the construction and conversion of all steel vessels regardless of size or tonnage and any wood vessel of over 100 gross tons is prohibited without the specific approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and further defines conversion as the alteration of the principal dimensions, and/or the changing of the characteristics of the vessel.

5. The interests of Korea are not concerned.

6. Routine surveillance is necessary on this activity and will be reported in normal monthly Occupation Activities Reports.

7. Concurrence: CTS - (Mr. P. E. Richers)

E. P. Sharp, 26-8571

W. S. V.
