GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.3 (5 Nov 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 240)

5 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :INPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Coal for Allies and Neutrals.

1. Coal for governmental and military agencies of allied countries will be requisitioned from the Japanese Government by U. S. agencies and it will be the responsibility of the Japanese Government to make deliveries to the requiring agency.

2. Allied nationals and all neutrals will purchase coal in the civilian market. You will take measures to insure that the needs of the official agencies of neutral countries are net.

3. This Headquarters assumes no obligation for the collection of debits incurred by reason of this directive.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
