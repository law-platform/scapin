MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Application for Permission to Extend Period of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the Second Quarter of 1947.

1. Reference is made to:

a. C.L.O. No. 7930(LO 358O-FI/FF), 10 October 1947, subject, "Application for Permission to Extend Period of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the Second Quarter of 1947;"

b. Memorandum for Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (1 Aug 47) CPC/GP, SCAPIN 1754, 1 August 1947, subject, "Gold, Silver and Platinum for Domestic Consumption in the Second Quarter of 1947," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The companies listed below are authorized to use that part of the precious metals listed, released to them by authority of reference 1b above, from the date of publication of this memorandum to 31 March 1948. The companies authorized by reference 1b may process these precious metals during this same period:

3. The metals whose extended use is authorized by paragraph 2 above, will be used for the purposes shown:

a/ Communication purposes;

b/ Manufacturing compasses and similar instruments;

c/ Manufacturing of medicines;

d/ Platinum nets;

e/ Pyrometers, hydrometers and related measuring instruments;

f/ Medical and Dental use;.

g/ Platinum crucibles and plates.

4. Platinum released to authorized representatives of the Ministry of Finance by reference 1b above, but unused in the amount of 3,126.22 grams, will be retained on hand in the custody of the Ministry of Finance, pending instructions from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
