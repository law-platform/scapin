GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 412.2 (23 Nov 46) ESS/IN

(SCAPIN-1361)

APO 500

23 November 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Inventory, Allocation and Distribution of Pipe and Pipe Fittings.

1. It is directed that immediate action be initiated to accomplish the following provisions with respect to steel and cast iron pipe, eternit and hume pipe and fittings:

a. Obtain complete and exact inventories of all stocks of pipe and pipe fittings, to include:

(1) Name and address of owner or possessor.

(2) Location.

(3) Type, number, dimensions, weight and present condition.

b. Establish measures to prevent the unauthorized transfer, sale or barter of such stocks and provide appropriate penalties for violations.

c. Provide for strict control of allocation and distribution of all stocks, including current production.

(1) Notify all owners and possessors accordingly.

d. A monthly report will be submitted within ten days after the end of each month to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers showing status as follows:

(1) Total stocks available.

(2) Allocations by industry of category.

(3) Deliveries during the month.

2. Companies listed on the schedule of restricted concerns established by Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 004 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC (SCAPIN 403), dated 8 December 1945, subject: “Establishment of a Schedule of Restricted Concerns,” and inclosures and additions thereto may dispose of pipe and pipe fittings to an agency designated by the Imperial Japanese Government pursuant to this memorandum without prior approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. All such sales or other disposition of pipe and pipe fittings will be reported to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, within 60 days after such sale or other disposition, such reports to include amount of purchase price, date of sale or other disposition and name of transferee.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 412.2 (23 Nov 46) ESS/IN

APO 500

23 November 1946

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to SCAP Memorandum AG 412.2 (23 Nov 46) ESS/IN (SCAPIN 1361), subject: Inventory, Allocation and Distribution of Pipe and Pipe Fittings

1. The critical nature of the supply of pipe and pipe fittings requires close supervision of all stocks, production, allocations and distribution border to meet occupation forces requirements and priority requirements of the Japanese industrial and civil economy.

2. a. The Troop and Dependent Housing Programs are being appreciably delayed by the shortage of these materials.

b. Industries, such as coal mining, chemical fertilizer and public works are being seriously handicapped by the short supply, lack of proper distribution and effective control.

3. Military Government Units should cooperate with and support the Imperial Japanese Government allocation program, reporting any apparent irregularities through established military channels.

/s/ R. G. Hersey

/t/ R. G. HERSEY,

Lt. Col. AGD,

Asst. Adj. General.

DISTRIBUTION: Same as SCAPIN 1361 less Imperial Japanese Government

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1361)

JZR/HBO/peh

7 November 1946

1. Subject is issuance of directive to IJG relative pipe and pipe fittings.

2. a. The critical nature of the supply of these materials warrants close supervision of all stocks, production, allocation, and distribution, the needs of the occupation forces being paramount in addition to priority requirements of the Japanese industrial and civil economy.

b. The Troop and Dependent Housing Programs are being delayed appreciably by the shortage of these materials.

c. Industrial needs such as coal mines, chemical fertilizer, engineering, etc., are being handicapped by shortage, lack of proper distribution, and effective control.

3. Directive is being issued to IJG to inventory existing stocks, insure proper utilization and distribution, and report to SCAP of action taken,

4. Concurrences: G-4, Lt. Col. Ebel; OCE, Lt. Col. Tate

5. Military surveillance is not necessary but cooperation of Military Government of 8th Army with Japanese Government Agencies is required for enforcement of the directive.

6. Interests of Korea have been considered.

J. Z. R.
