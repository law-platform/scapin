24 April 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

The following named Japanese will be apprehended as Suspected1 War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, EIGHTH Army, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo detention Camp), at the earliest possible date.

NAME

RANK OR TITLE

PERTINENT DATA

AKAGI, (FNU)

Gunzoku

Labour Dept, Rangoon, Mar 1942 - Dec 1943.

FUJII, Kiyoshi

Lt

Attached to 9 Railway Regt, Sept 1941. Co Commdr of 5 Co, 9 Railway Regt, Oct 1942.

Co Cmmdr Replacement Unit, 2 Railway Regt, Mar 1944.

Was Civilian Railway Engineer. Born at Yamaguchi, 27 Apr 1920.

GOTO, Sadaichi

Capt

4 Bn, 9 Railway Regt, Siam, 1941 - 1943.

Repatriated from Kluang, Malaya, 13 June 1946.

HANAZAKI, Tamotsu

Lt

3 Co, 9 Railway Regt, Siam, 1942 - 1944.

Transferred to Southern Army Field Railway HQ, Mar 1944.

HAYASHI, Masao

Med Segt

Nagoya Area PW Camp #2, Narumi, Honshu, 28 Dec 43 - 14 Oct 44.

Probable home address: Aichi-ken, Seto-shi, Saiwa-cho, #55.

HIRAOKA, Junzo

Col

Recruting Officer, Labour Board, Rangoon, Burma.

Attached to 3 Guards Inf Regt, Dec 1934, HQ Div, Aug 1937, HQ 15 Army, Dec 1941.

HQ Burma Area Army, Aug 1944. Officer of HQ General Army, South Area, Nov 1945.

Repatriated on "Taya Maru," Nov 1946. Born: Hiroshima, 1 Jan 1890.

MARU, Akio

TAKAHASHI, Masahisa

TAKEMOTO, Takeo

WAKUI, Shuji

Lt
