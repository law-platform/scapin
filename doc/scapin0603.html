GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (15 Jan 46) CCS

(SCAPIN - 603)

APO 500

15 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Changes in First Demobilization Ministry Radio Circuits.

1. Reference is made to letter file s609 dated 2 January 1946, subject Opening, abolition and change of wireless communication network".

2. Requests for the establishing of a circuit between Fukuoka and Peiping and for a change of frequency at Taihoku are disapproved.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
