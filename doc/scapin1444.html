MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Importation of Packaged Petroleum Products for the Imperial Japanese Government, January Through June 1947.

1. Arrangements have been made for the sale of imported packaged petroleum products to the Imperial Japanese Government through the Petroleum Distribution Company (or its legally appointed successor) in accordance with the quantities specified in the following schedule.

2. Schedule of approved requirements:

(Unit is gallons)

(1) Transformer (2) Superheated Steam Oyl (3) Machine General Purpose (4) 200 Red Engine Total

January 264,000 174,350 1,176,000 (5) - 1,614,350

February - - - 661,500 (5) 661,500

March - - 793,800 - 793,800

April 264,000 157,850 926,100 661,500 2,009,450

May - - 926,100 - 926,100

June 132,000 157,850 926,100 - 1,215,950

Total 660,000 490,050 4,748,100 1,323,000 7,221,150

Specifications:

(1) Federal (VV-0-401)

(2) Navy (5190)

(3) Saybolt Viscosity - 350/400 Seconds at 100° F, Flash Open Cup - 330° F or above. Pour test " F or below Reaction - Neutral

(4) Saybolt Viscosity - 200 Seconds at 109° F, Flash Open Cup - 370° F or above, Pour test 5° F or below Reaction - Neutral

(5) Product to be drummed. All other deliveries to be in bulk.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government through Petroleum Distribution Company as agent for the Board of Trade is authorized to accept quantity of any one item greater than that specified in any one month provided that the total quantity scheduled for import is not exceeded.

4. Current instructions relative to accounting procedure between the Imperial Japanese Government and occupation forces are continued in force.

5. The Board of Trade as agent for Imperial Japanese Government shall be responsible for insuring that timely and adequate notice of intent to accept delivery is furnished United States Army commands who are responsible for delivery of the products covered by this directive. The Board of Trade shall be responsible that only duly accredited agents shall receipt for deliveries accepted and that such agents present their credentials upon demand of any accredited representative of the occupation forces.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
