AG 004 (26 Jun 50) LS

(SCAPIN-2105/3)

APO 500

18 December 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
Subject :Entry Requirements and Business Activities in Japan

1. Reference is Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 004 (26 Jun 50) FIB, SCAPIN 2105, subject: Entry Requirements and Business Activities in Japan, 26 June 1950, as amended.

2. Referenced memorandum as amended, is hereby further amended as follows:

a. Subparagraph 1a is rescinded and the following substituted: “a. Reference is Circular 15, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, subject: Entry Requirements and Business Activities in Japan, 6 November 1951 as amended by Circular 17, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, same subject, 15 December 1951.”

b. Subparagraph 3i is rescinded.

c. Subparagraph 3l, the words “referenced Circular 11” in this paragraph are deleted and the words “referenced Circular as amended” are substituted.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C. C. B. WARDEN
Colonel, USA
Adjutant General
