GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123 (26 Feb 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 774)

APO 500

26 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Repayment of Uncompleted Remittances by Yokohama Specie Bank.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, this Headquarters, file AG 130 (22 Sep 45) ESS, dated 22 Sep 1945, subject: “Control of Financial Transactions,” which prohibits foreign exchange transactions without prior approval of this Headquarters.

2. You are directed to require the Yokohama Specie Bank, Tokyo, to repay to the Royal Swedish Legation, Japan, the purchase amount of the following remittances:

Amount Date of Remittance Receiver

¥20,100 17 June 1945 K. Larssen, Hongkong

¥70,000 15 August 1945 K. Larssen, Hongkong

¥77,535 15 August 1945 H. Hallgren, Hongkong

The remittances were executed on behalf of the Belgian, Netherlands and Norwegian Governments for the purpose of providing relief money to their nationals in Hongkong.

3. According to telegraphic information received from the Legations representatives in Hongkong, these sums were not received at that end. The Legation has been requested to notify representatives in Hongkong of action being taken here.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 774)

WFM/SM/syk

1. Reference is made to the Note Verbal of 26 January 1946, from Royal Swedish Legation, Tokyo, requesting repayment of Yokohama Specie Bank of amounts being delayed in transfer to Hongkong, originally remitted in behalf of Belgium, Netherlands and Norway for relief to their Nationals.

2. Confirmation has been received from the Yokohama Specie Bank, Tokyo that the following sums were dispatched to its branch in Hongkong. Yokohama Specie Bank did not request that Hongkong payment be confirmed.

Amount Date of Remittance Receiver

¥20,100 17 June 1945 Mr. K. Larssen Hongkong

70,000 15 August 1945 Mr. K. Larssen Hongkong

77,535 15 August 1945 Mr. H. Hallgren Hongkong

¥167,635 Total

Telegraphic information to Swedish Legation, Tokyo, from the Legation's representative in Hongkong reports these sums were not received. The Legation in Tokyo has endeavored to obtain a refundment from the Yokohama Specie Bank but was refused on the ground that the bank has been unable to obtain confirmation of non-payment.

3. Directive to the Imperial Japanese Government this Headquarters, file AG 130 (22 Sep 45) ESS, dated 22 September 1945, subject: "Control of Financial Transactions" prohibits, without prior permission, transactions in foreign exchange. However, this request from the Swedish Legation does not constitute a foreign exchange transaction nor does it affect the external assets of Japan. It is merely a repayment in Japanese yen of a former remittance by the Swedish Legation.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government is instructed to require the Yokohama Specie Bank, Tokyo, to repay the purchase amount of remittances specified in the Note Verbal received from Royal Swedish Legation.

5. It is suggested that the Swedish Legation notify their representatives in Hongkong when repayment is made, in order to guard against possible duplication of payment, in event remittances should reach the receiver at a later date.

C. F. T.
