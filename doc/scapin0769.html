23 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of German Nationals and Nationals Who Claim Austrian or Czechoslovakian Citizenship now in Japan.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. C. L. O. No. 121 (5.1), dated 10 January 1946, subject: "Repatriation of German Nationals in Japan".

b. C. L. O. No.690 (5.3), dated 16 February 1946, subject: "Repatriation of German Nationals in Japan".

2. Memorandum this headquarters file AG370.05 (31 Jan 46) GC (SCAPIN-686), dated 31 January 1946, subject: "Repatriation of German Nationals in Japan", is hereby rescinded.

3. a. It is directed that the Imperial Japanese Government conduct a survey and furnish to this headquarters not later than 10 March 1946 nominal rolls of all German Nationals and nationals claiming Austrian or Czechoslovakian citizenship now in Japan.

b. Rolls will be submitted in duplicate, in form indicated by attached sheets.

c. As indicated by attached sheets, separate rolls will be submitted for following categories of personnel:

(1) Civilians, including those with former diplomatic status, who desire repatriation.

(2) Civilians, including those with former diplomatic status, who do not desire repatriation. The Japanese authorities will inform these nationals that no consideration will be given to their remaining in Japan unless it can be shown conclusively that established permanent domicile in Japan prior to 1 January 1939, that they are able to maintain a livelihood and their presence will add to the economic and social welfare of Japan.

(3) Military, including Naval, personnel.

d. Information to be furnished will be grouped by prefectures and by families within prefectures.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
