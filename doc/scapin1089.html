GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (27 Jul 46) CCS

(SCAPIN 1089)

APO 500

27 July 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Demobilization Board Radio Stations Formerly Operating to North China

1. The Japanese Government will close the radio circuits of the First Demobilization Bureau that link Japan and North China.

2. The equipment at the Japanese terminal of these circuits will be dismantled and surrendered to the Occupation Forces within thirty days following the receipt of this memorandum. A complete report outlining the action taken to comply with this memorandum will be submitted at that time.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

27 July 1946

SUBJECT : Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN 1089), file AG 676.3 (27 Jul 46)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 27 July 1946 subject: "Demobilization Board Radio Stations Formerly Operating to North China."

1. With reference to the subject memorandum the following is published for the information of all concerned:

2. On 5 February 1946, SCAPIN 706 was issued which ordered the closing of all First Demobilization Ministry radio circuits except those linking Japan with North China and all except 22 of the Second Demobilization Ministry stations. However, the China terminals of the Japan to China circuits were closed on July 3 due to the fact that the Japanese troops operating the stations in China are being repatriated.

3. In line with the policy behind the issuance of SCAPIN 706 which closed all First Demobilization circuits except those to North China and the policy of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, to reduce the number of radio stations to a minimum, the Japanese terminals of these circuits are being ordered closed and dismantled.

4. The location and other identifying data, of the two stations to be closed by the subject SCAPIN follows:

a. Tokyo Station:

Location : Number 2, Sonoi-cho, Nakano-ku, Tokyo City.

Operated by : First remobilization Bureau.

Other Identifying Data:

Call Sien | Frequency | Point Contacted

JJI2 | 5206 | Nanking, China

JJI | 9940 | Nanking, China

JJI4 | 5285 | Peiping, China

JJI3 12570 Peiping, China.

b. Fukuoka Station:

Location : Fukuoka City, kyushu, Telephone,Nishi 13.

Operated by : Western Communications Office, First Demobilization Bureau.

Chief Operator : Isamu Egashira.

Othew Identifying Data:

Call Sign | Frequency | Point Contacted

JJK | 7425 | Nanking, China

5. The equipment to be surrendered to the Occupation Forces that is suitable for civilian use in its present form will be turned over to the Home Ministry, All other equipment will be processed in accordance with existing instructions.

6. Command instructions to the Commanding General, Eighth Army to insure compliance with the directive are released simultaneously and will follow through command channels.

P. G. HERSRY

Lt Col, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
