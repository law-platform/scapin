OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 829 (21 Sept 45) GC

(SCAPIN-41)

APO 500

21 September 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Navigation of Japanese Boats in Tokyo Bay

1. Reference is memorandum AG 829 (10 Sept. 45) GC from this office to the Japanese Imperial General Headquarters through the Yokohama Liaison Commission on the subject of navigation of Japanese boats in Tokyo Bay.

2. The reference memorandum authorized the use of boats and vessels of less than 100 tons in Tokyo Bay except for certain restricted areas which were shown on an enclosed chart.

3. You are informed that the areas enclosed by lines joining the following points are removed from the restricted areas previously established in Tokyo Bay.

a. Baker Area: Lat.35-35.16'N Long. 139-47.5'E; Lat. 35 030.72'N Long. 139-48.83'E; Lat. 35-31.3’N Long. 139-47.85’E.

b. Charlie Area: Lat 35°-27.75’N, Long. 139°-41.1’E, along the line of the breakwater to Lat. 35°-30.15’N, Long. 139°-45.85’E; Lat. 35°-29.6’N, Long. 139°-46.65'E.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ Harold Fair.
/t/ HAROLD FAIR,
Lt. Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General.
