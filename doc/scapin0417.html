GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 230 (23 Oct 45) ESS/LA

(SCAPIN - 417)

11 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Employment of Demobilized Personnel

1. This communication is in answer to Central Liaison Office Number 346 dated 23 October 1945 requesting authorization to employ demobilized naval technicians in the Ministry of Transportation for the purpose of expediting repair and reconstruction of transportation facilities.

2. There is no objection to the proposed action provided that the demobilized personnel transferred to the Transportation Ministry does not include any former officer of the Imperial Navy who had been graduated from the Kaigun Heigakko or Kaigun Daigakko or who served in any capacity with the Naval General Staff, Naval Intelligence, or Naval police organizations.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
