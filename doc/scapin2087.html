MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
Subject :Periodic Reports on Banking Institutions

1. Reference is Memorandum for Japanese Government, AG 319.1 (11 Dec 45) ESS/FI, (SCAPIN 419), dated 11 December 1945, subject: Periodic Reports on Banking Institutions.

2. Referenced Memorandum is hereby rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.Bush
Brigadier General, USA,
Adjutant General.

MS MO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2087)

FRJG/JCS/EJR/emb

2 March 1950

1. SCAPIN 419, dated 11 December 1946, subject: Periodic Reports on Banking Institutions, requested the Japanese Government to order the Bank of Japan to furnish Headquarters specific periodic reports on banking institutions in Japan.

2. In compliance with the recent program dealing with relaxation of controls, SCAPIN 419 is being rescinded. Since the periodic reports received in compliance with the SCAPIN are needed by this Headquarters, the Ministry of Finance will continue to submit the reports, such continuance being based upon an oral request.

Mr. Robinson: 33-7052

F. R. J. G.
