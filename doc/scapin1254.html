GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 560 (5 Oct 46) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1254)

APO 500

5 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Salvage, Repair and Refitting of Captured Vessels.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to repair and refit ships of all types and sizes found in Japanese waters which are identified as having been registered in an Allied country at the time of acquisition, seizure or sinking by the Japanese or their agents.

2. The costs of necessary salvage, repair and refitting will be borne by the Japanese Government.

3. Separate directives for each ship will be delivered to the Imperial Japanese Government by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers directing the overhaul, repair, salvage and refitting necessary to permit the return of the vessels in a condition substantially similar to that at the time they came into Japanese hands.

4. Within 30 days after completion of the overhaul, repairs, salvage and refitting directed on each vessel, the Imperial Japanese Government will submit a report of the total costs involved to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
