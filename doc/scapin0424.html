GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.3 (11 Dec 45) GD

(SCAPIN-424)

11 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Additional Requirements to Expedite Coal Production.

1. The action taken by the Imperial Japanese Government to implement paragraph 3 of the directive of this headquarters, subject, “Production of Coal”, AG 463. 3 (6 Dec 45) GD is considered to be inadequate and unsatisfactory.

2. The representatives which the Japanese Government sent to this headquarters at the end of the 48 hour period were not qualified to discuss the overall aspects of the coal production problem. Neither were they in any official position to give authoritative guarantees that positive and aggressive action had been or will be taken by the Japanese Government to insure the coordination of all agencies which the solution of this problem requires.

3. Rather than a summary of positive action taken by the Japanese Government in compliance with the directive AG 463. 3 (6 Dec 45) GD, the report submitted by the representatives of the Japanese Government was a lengthy narrative of programs and interim measures taken or proposed during the past three months. All information contained in this report was already well known to this headquarters. The issuance of the directive AG 463. 3 (6 Dec 45) clearly indicates the position of this headquarters as regards the inadequacy and the unsatisfactory state of current plans and action.

4. As a case in point, the attention of the Japanese representatives was called to the current strike in Hokkaido, They were reminded that on 5 November, the Minister of Welfare issued an order requiring all Prefectual Governors to appoint mediation boards to settle labor disputes. The representatives stated the current strike had not been referred to such a board and that as far as was known no such board existed in the prefectures of this current strike. You are directed to inform this headquarters without delay why such a board was not appointed, why steps were not taken to mediate this strike and what measures have now been taken toward its solution.

5. By separate memorandum, the Imperial Japanese Government is being directed to submit periodic reports on coal production. The firet report, covering the first ten days of December is to be received at this headquarters not later than noon of the 15th day of December. You are directed to have this first report delivered to this headquarters by an official of the Japanese Government of sufficient stature to enable him to discuss the overall aspects of the coal production problem and to give to the officials of this headquarters definite, authoritative guarantees of positive action on the part of the Imperial Japanese Government to insure the required coordination between the various Ministries.

6. A list of the Japanese Government officials selected to deliver this report and to confer on the problems of coal production will be submitted to this headquarters for approval not later than 1200 on 14 December 1945.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
