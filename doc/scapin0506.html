GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.7 (27 Dec 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 506)

APO 500

27 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Sale of Kerosene to Japanese Government.

1. At a conference held 6 November 1945, at which there were present Mr. Y. Yoshida of the Fuel Bureau representing the Home ministry and Mr. N. Ushiki of the Petroleum Distribution Company, this Headquarters agreed to sell to and the Japanese Government agreed to purchase and accept delivery F. O. B. ship Yokohama of approximately 20,000 bulk barrels (42 United States gallons) of kerosene at a tentative price of U. S. O. $5.05 per barrel.

2. Quantitative receipt is held by this Headquarters for delivery Ex. S. S, CANNON BRANCH of 24,738 barrels.

3. Adjusted price is U. S. O. $3.93 per barrel.

4. Value of obligation is U. S. O. $97,230.34.

5. This Headquarters will advise you method of meeting this obligation at a later date.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
