MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Separation Allowances for Government Employees

1. References are:

a. SCAPIN 2003, AG 121 (30 Apr 49) ESS/FIN of 11 May 1949, subject as above.

b. Cabinet Order 264 of 1949, Cabinet Order concerning the Temporary Measures for Separation Allowance under the Execution of the Consolidated Balanced Budget for the Fiscal Year 1949 - 50 issued in compliance with reference 1a.

2. Paragraph 1 of reference 1a is hereby amended to read as follows:

“1. The Japanese Government is directed to take the necessary measures to establish standard allowances to be paid to Government employees separated from Government service consistent with the amount of funds provided for such allowances in the consolidated balanced budget adopted for the fiscal years 1949 - 50 and 1950 - 51 in accordance with the United States Government Interim Directive on economic stabilization.”

3. The standards established in reference 1b will remain in effect but may be amended to provide for:

a. The inclusion of commissioners, members, staff and employees of Reconversion Finance Bank, Holding Company Liquidation Commission, Closed Institutions Liquidation Commission and Securities Coordinating Liquidation Commission as personnel subject to the provisions of reference Cabinet Order.

b. An amendment authorizing payment by public employment security offices of separation allowances, corresponding to the differential amount as established in Article 10 of reference Cabinet Order, to those government personnel who have retired and are entitled to payments during periods of unemployment for one year from the date of their separation. This provision will apply to similar payments to government employees who retired during the fiscal year 1949 - 50 and are entitled to such payments during the fiscal year 1950 - 51.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2003/1)

EMR/JWH/LAH/ftk

7 March 1950

1. References are:

a. SCAPIN 2003, AG 121 (30 Apr 49) ESS/FIN, 11 May 1949, subject, "Separation Allowances for Government Employees."

b. Cabinet Order 264 of 1949, Cabinet Order Concerning the Temporary Measures for Separation Allowance Under the Execution of the Consolidated Balanced Budget for the Fiscal Year 1949-50.

2. Reference 1a directed the Japanese Government to take the necessary measures for establishing standard separation allowances to be paid to Government employees which would be consistent with the amount of funds provided in the budget adopted for the fiscal year 1949-50. Reference 1b is the Potsdam Cabinet Order implementing reference la and was promulgated as temporary legislation. It was the intent that this order remain in effect until sufficient study could be made towards the drafting of permanent legislation concerning the separation allowances of Government personnel.

3. No permanent legislation had been drafted by the Japanese Government at the time of the preparation of the 1950-51 fiscal year budget, therefore, the appropriations for retirement allowances of Government personnel were computed on the basis of the provisions of reference 1b to extend the coverage to the fiscal year 1950-51. This is necessary as reference la directed legislation for the fiscal year 1949-50 only and the Cabinet Order issued thereunder (reference 1b) will expire at the end of the fiscal year 1949-50. Direction of the extension of the present Cabinet Order isolated features of the standard allowances in order to direct the entire attention of the Japanese Government towards the planning and drafting of permanent standards and legislation.

4. The following amendments are also authorized:

a. The inclusion of the officers and employees of the Reconversion Finance Bank, Holding Company Liquidation Commission, Closed Institutions Liquidating Commission and Securities Coordinating Liquidation Commission as personnel subject to the provisions of reference Cabinet Order. This amendment is required as it was considered necessary to make provision in the 1950-51 budget for separation allowances for the personnel of the above agencies which heretofore had not been authorized to pay such allowances to their officers and employees.

b. A change in the procedure of payment to those personnel who have retired and are entitled to payments during periods of unemployment for one year from the date of their separation. The present law provides for payment by the government agency concerned at the location of the former employee's office of employment upon presentation of a certification of unemployment which the former employee has received from the public employment security office most available to him. The proposed amendment provides for certification and payment by the public employment security office most available to the individual as a means of expediting payment and eliminating the travel expenses, etc. often necessary for the collection of the separation allowance at the location of the ex-employee's former office of employment.

*5. Inasmuch as the Japanese Government has been lax in drafting permanent legislation for separation allowances it is considered necessary to direct the presentation at the earliest practicable date of a plan and draft legislation providing for the establishment of a sound and integrated program covering separation allowances so that it may be adopted and implemented in the Japanese Government's budget for the fiscal year 1951-52.

6. The proposed SCAPIN is not to be released until the consolidated budget for the fiscal year 1950-51 has been passed by the Diet.

7. Concurrences: ESS/LAB

GS

L. A. Herbster, 26-6070

E. M. R.

*Note: (regarding Para. 5 above)

In accordance with instructions received from the Chief of Staff the request for permanent legislation will not be directed by SCAPIN, but will be transmitted verbally to the Japanese Government.

E. C. H.
