GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 400 (2 Oct 45) GPA

(SCAPIN-90)

APO 500

2 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Supplies and Services Required for the Occupation Forces.

The following tentative list represents major categories of services and supplies which it is anticipated the Imperial Japanese Government will be called upon to supply to ocupation forces of the Allied Powers. Later additions and modifications will be made as need arises.

SERVICES

1. Use of buildings, docks, petroleum storage, camp areas, airfields, open storage space.

2. Repair and services to occupations installations.

3. Utilities (water, electricity, telephone, etc.)

4. Transportation.

5. Rest areas, resort hotels, athletic installations.

6. Ship repairs and bunkering.

7. Machine and repair shops.

8. Printing.

9. Tourist agencies.

10. Special entertainment (music, dramatics, wrestling, etc.)

11. Laundry and dry cleaning.

12. Processing of film.

13. Bottling plants.

14. Paint shops (signs, repainting, etc.)

15. Shoe repairs.

16. Tailoring and garment repairs.

17. Watch and instrument repairs.

18. Optical repairs and services.

19. Labor, skilled and unskilled.

SUPPLIES

1. Construction materials (lumber, cement, etc., for maintenance and repairs)

2. Fuel (coal, wood, etc.)

3. Lubricating oils and greases.

4. Paper and paper products.

5. Hardware and hand tools.

6. Textiles.

7. Sound and projection equipment.

8. Photographic supplies.

9. Electrical supplies.

10 Drugs and chemicals.

11. Paints, oils, varnishes.

12. Brushes and brooms.

13. Rope and cables (steel or hemp)

14. Medical and dental supplies.

15. Dishware, glassware, silverware, cutlery.

16. Furniture and office equipment.

17. Soap and candles

18. Ink.

19. Insignia, buttons, thread.

20. Surplus fruits and vegetables.

21. Surplus dry foods (tea, coffee, etc.)

22. Locally surplus dairy products, poultry, fresh fish.

23.Ice.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H.W. Allen
/t/ H.W. ALLEN
Colonel, A.G.D.
Asst. Adjutant General
