MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1840)

ESS/IN JZR/GEM/JTV/ms

29 December 1947

1. Reference is C.L.O. letter No. 9198 (CM), dated 29 November 1947, subject: Fishing Boats not to be Licensed for Operation Without Approval, with one inclosure attached.

2. SCAPIN 1807, dated 24 October 1947, subject: Change in Construction Program of Fighting Vessels, authorized for completion of construction of a number of tuna vessels, the keels of which were laid prior to authorization by SCAP directive. The responsible parties who violated SCAP directives by commencing construction of these vessels without prior authorization are presently being prosecuted in Japanese courts. However, in order to conserve critical materials and also to increase needed tonnages of fishing vessels, the completion of these fishing boats was authorized.

3. SCAPIN 1807 prohibited the Japanese Government to license a number of tuna vessels aggregating a total of 840 gross tons. These vessels are to be held in reserve until such time that replacement of other vessels are deemed necessary.

4. The C.L.O. letter referred to in paragraph 1 of this Memo for Record, presents a plan in which the nine (9) vessels aggregating a total of approximately 860 gross tons will replace certain vessels presently operated, but planned to be converted to fish carriers. These vessels are of an equal tonnage of those to be replaced.

5. Action taken: The attached SCAPIN approves the request to put into operation the eight (8) steel tuna vessels upon their completion, and authorizes the conversion of the eight (8) old tuna vessels to fish carriers.

6. The interests of USAFIK are not concerned.

7. Concurrence: Natural Resources Section, Fisheries - Mr. Neville

8. Routine surveillance is required on this activity and will be reported in normal monthly Occupational Reports.

9. Further questions may be directed to Mr. J. T. Valentine, ESS/IN, Shipyard Industries and Harbor Development Branch, Telephone No. 26-8954.

J. Z. R.
