GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 360.31 (23 Dec 45) GD

SCAPIN-484/1)

14 February 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Reactivation of Air Navigational Facilities

So much of memorandum for Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 360.31 (23 dec 45) GD, SCAPIN 484, subject, "Reactivation of Air Navigational Facilities," 23 December 1945, as reads, "The Imperial Japanese Government is hereby directed to place existing air navigational facilities between Tokyo and Kyushu in operation and thereafter operate and maintain such fabilities," is as of 1 April 1949 amended to read, "The Japanese Government is authorized to operate and maintain existing air navigational facilities between Tokyo and Kyushu." A procurement demand will be issued to cover the past expenditures incurred by the Japanese Government in complying with SCAPIN 484, 23 December 1945 to 31 March 1949.

It is considered that the existing air navigational facilities between Tokyo and Kyushu will not be a requirement of the Occupation Forces after 31 March 1949.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M.LEVYen
Colonel,AGD
Adjutant General
