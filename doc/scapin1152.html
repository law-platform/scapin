MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Conversion Plan of Oil Tankers.

1. Reference is C.L.O. No. 3186 (CM) with Inclosure 1, dated 29 June 1946, subject; "Conversion Plan of Oil Tankers," requesting permission to convert three (3) 10,000 ton oil tankers into whale processing ships, two of which are under construction. This request was submitted as plan No. 2A of the fishing vessel construction program.

2. The request for permission to convert the above oil tankers to whale processing vessels as indicated on Inclosure 1 is approved.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY
Colonel, AGD

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1152)

ESS/IN JZR/GEM/rhw

9 August 1946

1. Reference is C. L.O. No. 3186(CM) with Inclosure 1, Construction Plan 2A, dated 29 June 1946, Subject: "Conversion Plan of Oil Tankers," requesting permission to convert three (3) 10,000 ton tankers into whale processing ships, one of which is completed and the other two are under construction. The vessels when converted, are to be used in pelagic whaling operations in the Antarctic.

2. Action on this request has been delayed to date pending a definite decision from Washington as to the State Department's views on this matter. The message from State Department to SCAP, dated 16 May 1946, War SVC (State Serial 308 May 14, 6 PM) gives the views of the State Department and was the basis for AG 800.279 (6 Aug. 46) NR (SCAPIN-1103) 6 Aug. 1946, Subject: Japanese Whaling Operation in the Antarctic." G. H. Q. SCAP WARCOS dispatch Serial C-63799 of 31 July 1946, announced to the State Department that the authorization for the conversion of the three whaling vessels is to be granted. This information was the basis of the Industrial Division's position.

3. By a telephone conference with G-4 (Col. BREDEN) and ESS, Industrial Division (Comdr. Meyer) it was decided to recommend favorable action on the request.

4. Several conferences between the Natural Resources Section, Fisheries Division (Capt. Broadbent) and ESS, Industrial Division (Mr. Valentine) were held and the following points were discussed and are pointed out in this memo for record to qualify the decision made by this division.

a. The Japanese people are in dire need of products derived from the whaling industry.

b. Two of the vessels requested to be converted can be completed and at sea by early November. The most favorable months for whaling in the Antarctic are December, January, February and March. This will give the two vessels four months of whaling operations.

c. These vessels will serve to alleviate the food, soap, fat and other shortages prevalant at this time in Japan. Whale oil has a potential export value and if exported by the Japanese, the product will serve to help stabilize Japan's economy.

d. In the future when a definite policy is set up governing the Japanese fishing industry by the Allied Council, these vessels can be restricted to waters approved by the council or designated authority.

e. By a check sheet from Natural Resources Section, Fisheries Division, concurrence was given on the dispatch Serial 308 May 14 6 PM from the State Department.

5. This division is cognizant of the State Department dispatch Serial 413 July 25 Noon, which states the Norwegian Government's views and the State Department dispatch Serial 426, August 2, 6 PM in which Russia's views were expressed on this matter.

6. Action Taken: The request to convert the three 10,000 ton tankers to whaling vessels is approved.

7. Concurrence: G-4 (Col. Breden); Natural Resources, Fisheries (Capt. Broadbent).

8. Routine surveillance is necessary on this activity and will be reported in normal weekly "Occupation Activities Reports."

J. Z. R.
