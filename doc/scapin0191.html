GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 370.05 (25 Oct 45) GC

(SCAPIN - 191)

25 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Chinese Civilian Internees and Ex-Prisoners of War.

1. This headquarters has received a list of Chinese Civilian Internees and Ex-Prisoners of War in Akita prefecture awaiting movement to China.

2. Passenger list of those destined for North and Central China, listed separately are attached.

3. It is desired that movement of subject personnel to China be included in shipping schedules submitted to SCAJAP for approval.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.

1 Incl- Pass List, North & Central China.
