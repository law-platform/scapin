GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (14 May 47) LS-R

(SCAPIN - 1676)

APO 500

14 May 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Clarification of Status of Former Suspected War Criminal.

1. The following named Japanese listed on Memorandum indicated below is deleted from said list, is released from suspicion as a Suspected War Criminal, is released from Sugamo Prison, and henceforth will be returned to his former status.

NAME MEMORANDUM 10 Jan 47 PAGE NO.

AGATA, Hiroshi Civilian 1

2. All property of above designated individual controlled, impounded, or blocked under Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386.7 (23 Apr 46)CPC, SCAPIN 897, subject: "Control, Impounding and Blocking of Property of Designated Individuals", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, is hereby directed to be released from the provisions of said Memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
