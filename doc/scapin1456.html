AG 000.5 (10 Jan 47) LS-R

(SCAPIN - 1456)

APO 500

10 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

The following named Japanese will be apprehended as Suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, EIGHTH Army, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest possible date.

NAME RANK OR TITLE PERTINENT DATA

ARAKAWA, (FNU) (HARAKAWA) Civ Employed by Furukawa Kogyo, Gng Kijang, NEI. (Rhio-Archipelago). Repatriated from Singapore aboard vessel "Taian Maru," 22 Nov 45.

BANNAI, Matsuji Pvt Kempei-Tai; served under Officer Kawatahara in Tanjong Pinang, Bintan, NEI, Apr 43 - 8 Oct 45. Probably repatriated from Singapore.

FUJII, Y. Served under Capt Ogawa (FNU) Rhio, NEI Garrison, Tanjong Pinang, Bintan, NEI. Probably repatriated from Singapore.

FUKUYAMA, (FNU) Civ Employed by Nihon Keikin-zoku, Gng Kijang, Rhio Archipelago, NEI. Repatriated from Singapore on board "Taian Maru," 22 Nov 45.

GENDA, Sawagi (KENDA) Lt 2nd Gos Div Reece Regt, 25th Army. Landed at Laboean Roekoe (East Coast Sumatra), 12 Mar 1942.

HARIMA, Kiyoshi L/Cpl 47 Line, Communication Garrison, 25th Army, East Coast Sumatra, 1944-45. Evacuated Belawan, 1 Apr 46.

IGARASHI, Narihisa 2/Lt 3rd Bn, 2nd Inf Gd Regt, 2nd Guards Div, Padang, West Coast Sumatra, Aug 42 -Sep 43.
