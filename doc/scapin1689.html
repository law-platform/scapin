GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (20 May 47) CPC/GP

(SCAPIN - 1689)

APO 500

20 May 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Gold, Silver and Platinum for Domestic Consumption in the First Quarter of 1947.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (15 Mar 47)CPC/GP, SCAPIN 1572, 15 March 1947, subject, "Gold, Silver, and Platinum for Domestic Consumption in the First Quarter of 1947," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The following additions and deletions are made to reference memorandum:

a. Nippon Toki K.K. is allotted 510 grams of gold. This gold is to be released from stocks held by the Imperial Japanese Government.

b. 3,150,000 grams of silver allotted to Konishiroku Photo Industrial Company, Ltd., is rescinded.

c. 1,533,000 grams of silver allotted to the Oriental Photo Industrial Company, Ltd., is rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
