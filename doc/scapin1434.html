MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Importation of Petroleum Products for Imperial Japanese Government, January through March 1947.

1. Arrangements have been made for the sale of imported petroleum products to the Imperial Japanese Government through the Petroleum Distribution Company (or its legally appointed successor) in monthly quantities and at the delivery points indicated herein:

Jan Feb Mar Totals

a. Navy special Fuel Oil (Unit is Barrel)

(1) Tsurumi 88,000 88,000 113,200 289,200

(2) Kure 20,000 20,000 18,800 58,800

(3) Sasebo 112,100 112,100 119,500 343,700

220,100 220,100 251,500 691,700

b. Navy 7-0-2 Diesel

(1) Tsurumi 25,100 18,800 Nil 43,900

(2) Shimotsu *12,300 *6,400 *12,600 33,500

(3) Kure 6,400 4,200 Nil 10,600

(4) Sasebo 6,300 6,300 Nil 12,600

50,300 37,700 12,600 100,600

* Shimotsu deliveries will be from Army stock.

c. Range Fuel

(1) Tsurumi 130,000 130,000 130,000 390,000

**86,124

** Of the quantity allocated for Rarch, 86,124 barrels have been previously received. Delivery is authorized for only 130,000 barrels.

d. Kerosene No import during quarter.

2. a. The Imperial Japanese Government shall issue such instructions as may be required to insure that petroleum products delivered in bulk by ocean tanker are made direct from such tankers to land based and/or floating storage as the case may be.

b. The Imperial Japanese Government through Petroleum Distribution Company as agent for Board of Trade, is authorized to accept quantity of any one item-greater than that specified in any one month provided that the total for the three months indicated is not exceeded. This authorization is to provide for contingencies that might arise such as direct discharge from ocean tanker to Petroleum Distribution Company storage.

c. Current instructions relative to accounting procedure are continued in force.

3. The Board of Trade (Boeki Cho) as agent for the Imperial Japanese Government shall be responsible for insuring that timely and adequate notice of intent to accept delivery is furnished United States Army and Navy commands who are responsible for delivery of petroleum products to the Imperial Japanese Government. The board of Trade shall be responsible that only duly accredited agents shall receipt for deliveries accepted, and that such agents present their credentials upon demand by any accredited representative of the Occupation Forces.

4. The quantities set forth in this directive do not include any portion of the 13,000 kilolitres of Navy Special Fuel Oil for use in the production of steel. This additional allocation shall be authorized in subsequent directives.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
