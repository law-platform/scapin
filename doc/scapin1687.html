GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (16 May 47) GA

(SCAPIN-1687)

APO 500

16 May 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Repatriation from Japan to Former Mandated Islands

1. Reference is made to C. L. O. No. 1342 (RF), dated 1 March 1947, subject: “Repatriation to Saipan and Palau,”

2. Future repatriation from Japan to the islands formerly under Japanese mandate will be governed by policies stated below:

a. No Japanese or other non-Micronesian persons will be permitted to enter those islands pending determination of the final status of the islands.

b. Those Micronesian natives and half-breed children who elected to accompany Japanese spouse or parent on previous repartriation from the former mandated islands to Japan may not be repartriated to those islands unless the Japanese spouse or parent is deceased or divorced. Such death or divorce must be legally established by death certificate or divorce decree.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government will:

a. Re-investigate the eligibility of those persons named in above listed reference.

b. Submit a corrected list of those still eligible for repatriation.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
