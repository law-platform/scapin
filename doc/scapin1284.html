GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 560 (22 Oct 46) GD

(SCAPIN - 1284)

APO 500

22 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Charters of the No. 9 KAWAKO MARU and the TAIHEI MARU.

1. Reference is made to C.L.O No.5383 (CM) dated 13 October 1946, subject: Charter of No.9 KAWAKO MARU and TEIHEI MARU.

2. Request for change in charter status of the No.9 KAWAKO MARU is approved. KAWAKO MARU will continue to operate under the general supervision of the Civilian Merchant Marine Committee, and will be included in monthly report of island ferry type vessels submitted by that agency to Administrator, Naval Shipping Control Authority for Japanese Merchant Marine.

3. Request for the change in charter status of the TAIHEI MARU is disapproved at this time.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
