SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

(SCAPIN - 1972)

APO 500

11 February 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Provision of Telephone Services on the Premises of Hotels Operated by the Board of Trade.

1. In order to meet the domestic telephone service requirements of commercial entrants quartered in each hotel in Japan operated by the Board of Trade of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, and of the general public who come into the premises of the hotels, the Board of Trade of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry and the Ministry of Communications will be governed by the provisions and regulations set forth in inclosure 1 hereto, effective 1 March 1949 at 0001 hours.

2. Direct communication between Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Ministry of Communications regarding matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

REGULATIONS COVERING THE PROVISION OF TELEPHONE SERVICES on the Premises of Hotels Operated by the Board of Trade of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry

1. Hotel private branch exchange service will be engineered, installed and maintained to adequately handle requirements of the hotel's guests, and of the hotel's administrative staff for outgoing, incoming and inter-communicating telephone calls. This will involve the provision of an adequate number of private branch exchange switchboard positions, central office trunks and stations (properly distributed to rooms and offices throughout the hotel).

2. An attended public paystation system will be engineered, installed and maintained in a location in the hotel that can be conveniently reached from the lobby of the hotel to meet public requirements for outgoing calls. No agency or firm other than the hotel will be permitted to operate public or semi-public paystations in the promises of the hotel.

3. Any individual line service terminated on the promises of the hotel and subscribed for by either the Board of Trade or its hotel management, or by commercial entrants will be for the exclusive use of the subscriber, and will be subscribed for directly with the Ministry of Communications by the subscriber.

4. The Board of Trade and its hotel management will provide an adequate force of English-speaking operators to cover the private branch exchange switchboard and the attended public paystations system to provide a uniform good grade of service. The Ministry of Communications will assist the hotel management in training the operators for the work to be done, and will send to the hotel, as occasion demands a private branch exchange service supervisor to observe the quality of service being rendered by the hotel's operator force and to advise with the chief of the hotel's operator force.

5. Each outgoing local call completed through the hotel's private branch exchange switchboard and/or the attended public paystation system for the hotel guests and the general public will be paid for in Japanese yen regardless of whether or not the person placing the call is a commercial entrant, a member of the occupation forces or anyone else. The charge per call will be four (4) yen.

6. Each long distance call completed through the hotel private branch exchange switchboard and/ or the public paystation system for the hotel guests and the general public will be paid for in Japanese yen regardless of whether or not the person placing the call is a commercial entrant, a member of the occupation forces or anyone else. Cancellation charges for long distance calls placed but not completed due to no failure on the part of the Ministry of Communications will be paid for accordingly in yen.

7. The following classes of long distance telephone service will be available to anyone placing calls through the hotel's private branch exchange switchboard and/or public paystation system at the rates indicated:

Class Rate Treatment

*a. Ordinary Ordinary rate

*b. Urgent Twice the Ordinary rate

*c. Special Urgent Three times the ordinary rate

*d. Time Appointment For times the ordinary rate

c. "AF Call" Five times the ordinary rate

*(Ordinary, Urgent, Special Urgent and Time Appointment calls will be completed over the regular Japanese long distance facilities only, and will not be handled over the special facilities or by the special long distance operators used for handling "AF Call" ,message service).

The charges for long distance call cancellations will be applied at the regularly established yen rates.

8. No long distance calls originated through the hotel's switchboard, and/or public paystation systems, will be transmitted over the official long distance lines of the occupation forces.

9. The Ministry of Communications will bill monthly and collect from the hotel its regularly established rate of two (2) yen for each outgoing local call completed through the hotel's private branch exchange switchboard and/or public paystation system. Charges for completed long distance calls and for long distance calls cancellations will be billed monthly and collected from the hotel with rate treatment applicable as set forth in paragraph 7 above, less a ten (10) percent credit which will be shown on the bill as a commission to the hotel as compensation for assistance rendered in handling calls.

10. The Ministry of Communications and the Board of Trade will coordinate in issuing instructions to their respective employees which are appropriate and necessary to implement the provisions of all the above regulations.

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive SCAPIN 1972 file AG 311.3 (11 Feb 49)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 11 Feb 49, subject: Provision of Telephone Services on the Promises of Hotels Operated by the Board of Trade

1. With reference to the subject memorandum the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The subject memorandum is issued for the purpose of regulating the provision of adequate domestic telephone facilities in trade hotels operated by the Board of Trade, Ministry of Commerce and Industry which quarter commercial entrants and at times military and accredited civilian personnel of the occupation forces. Further purposes served are the regulation of charges to be made for local and long distance calls handled through the hotel switchboards and public paystation systems for guests of the hotels, and for the general public who come within the promises of the hotels.

3. Military and accredited civilian personnel who on occasion are guests of the hotels are required to pay charges in yen as the hotel managements will not be in a position to determine which calls placed by them could be accepted as being properly chargeable to occupation force expense.

A J REHE

Major, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
