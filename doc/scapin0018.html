GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
UNITED STATES ARMY FORCES, PACIFIC
OUTGOING MESSAGE

(SCAPIN - 18)

Date: 11 September 1945

FROM :THE SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS
TO :THE JAPANESE IMPERIAL GENERAL HEADQUARTERS--PRIORITY
INFO :THE JAPANESE IMPERIAL GOVERNMENT-----ROUTINE
CINCAFPAC----------------------------ROUTINE
WARCOS-------------------------------ROUTINE
SIXTH ARMY---------------------------ROUTINE
EIGHTH ARMY--------------------------ROUTINE
XXIV CORPS---------------------------ROUTINE

PART ONE CLN (ZAX5332) IT IS DESIRED THAT THIS HEADQUARTERS BE FURNISHED A WEEKLY REPORT COMMENCING ONE NOUGHT SEPTEMBER ONE NINE FOUR FIVE CMA TO COVER THE STATUS OF THE DEMOBILIZATION OF THE JAPANESE ARMED FORCES IN JAPAN CMA THE INITIAL REPORT TO INCLUDE THE FOLLOWING CLN ABLE TABULATION OF SPECIFIC UNITS TO INCLUDE INDEPENDENT REGIMENTS OR EQUIVALENT CMA OF THE JAPANESE ARMED FORCES PAREN ARMY AND NAVY PAREN TOTALLY DEMOBILIZED AS OF ONE NOUGHT SEPTEMBER ONE NINE FOUR FIVE PD PART TWO CLN WEEKLY REPORTS BEGINNING ONE SEVEN SEPTEMBER ONE NINE FOUR FIVE PD PART TWO CLN WEEKLY REPORTS BEGINNING ONE SEVEN SEPTEMBER ONE NINE FOUR FIVE INDICATING CLN PAREN ABLE PAREN TABULATIONS OF SPECIFIC UNITS TO IXCLUDE INDEPENDENT REGIMENT OR EQUIVALENT OF THE JAPANESE ARMED FORCES COMPLETELY DEMOBILIZED DURING THE PRECEDING WEEK PD PAREN BAKER PAREN TOTAL NUMBER OF JAPANESE ARMED FORCES PERSONEL DEMOBILIZED DURING THIS PERIOD OMA INDICATED SEPARATELY FOR ARMY AND NAVY

OFFICIAL:

B.M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, U. S. Army,
Adjutant General.

Approved by:

/s/M. J. Gunner
/t/M. J. GUNNER
Brig Gen, GSC,
AC of S, G-1
