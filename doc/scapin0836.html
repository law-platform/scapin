GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 370.05 (23 Mar 46) GC

(SCAPIN-836)

APO 500

23 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Repatriation to Izu Islands.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government AG 370.05 (15 Mar 46) GC (SCAPIN-741-A), subject: “Unauthorized Voyage of Tenkai Maru”, dated 15 March 1946.

2. The Izu Islands north of 30 degrees north latitude and the island Sofu Gan (Lot’s wife) are now included in the Japanese home waters. Clearance for movement of Japanese ships to and from these islands is no longer required.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General
