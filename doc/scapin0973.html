GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (21 May 46) LS

(SCAPIN - 973)

APO 500

21 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Convicted Japanese Criminals.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will apprehend and detain the following named individuals:

KINISHIRO TSUKIYAMA - address unknown

SHOSHAKU TAMURA - address unknown

KITARO MORIMOTO - address unknown

ICHIZO SUGIMOTO - 298, 1st St. Shin Ikedacho, Minatoku, Osaka, Japan

TOKUTARO KASAHARA - address unknown

SEIICHI NISHIKURA - 412, Hihata, Yashikache, Yohfugun, Hyogo Prefecture.

TOSHIHARU YAMASHITA - 679, Nishimachi, Higashiyodogawaku, Osaka, Japan. 891, Higashiishihara, Jizojimura, Tosegun, Kohchi Prefecture.

TAMARU YOSHIMATSU - 1171, Nagabeta, Aichimura Higashimatsuuragun, Saga Prefecture, Japan.

NOBUO HIGA - address unknown

SADAO MORIMITSU - Sako, Yunomura, Tokuyame City, Yamaguchi Prefecture, Japan. 1316 Junancho, Tokuyama, Yamaguchi Prefecture, Japan.

MATSAHIRO OHUGUSHI - address unknown

who were convicted of embezzlement by a United States Military Court in Korea, fined ¥50,000 each and in default of payment thereof sentenced to imprisonment.

2. The above listed individuals are believed to have arrived at Hakata, Japan, on or about 14 February 1946 as crew members of the KOGANE MARU.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government will collect from each of the above named individuals, the sum of ¥21,717.28, representing the unpaid balance of the fine assessed against each of said individuals.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government will deposit the sums collected to the credit of the Military Government of Korea in the "Custody account for the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers" in any branch of the Bank of Japan, as established by Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, AG 004.2 (1 Mar 46) ESS/Finance, SCAPIN (638-A), subject: "Accounts in the Bank of Japan for the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers."

5. The Imperial Japanese Government will deliver a receipt for funds so deposited (including information as to the source of the funds and the credit to be established) to the Civil Property Custodian of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

6. In default of payment of ¥21,717.28 by each of the above named individuals, the Imperial Japanese Government will incarcerate those in default of payment in a Japanese prison for a period of two years.

7. The Imperial Japanese Government will report within thirty days the results of its action taken pursuant to this directive.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
