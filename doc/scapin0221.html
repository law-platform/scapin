GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004 (1 Nov 45) ESS-IN

(SCAPIN - 221)

1 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Transfer of a Set of Magnetron Oscillator

1. Reference is made to your L.O. No. 407 requesting permission to transfer a set of magnetron oscillators "No. 22” from the Naval Research Laboratory, Meguro, Tokyo to Nishina Laboratory of the Institute of Physical and Chemical Research and to operate same for irradiation of seeds.

2. You are hereby authorized to make the above transfer and to permit Dr. Nishina to operate the equipment as specified in C.L.O. No. 407, in compliance with the provisions of Directive No. 3.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
