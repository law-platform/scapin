GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.7 (25 Jan 52) GS

(SCAPIN-2203)

APO 500

17 April 1952

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Property in Japan Formerly Owned by Certain Persons of German Nationality.

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 091.112 (13 Sep 45)MG, SCAPIN 26, 13 September 1945, subject, "Protection of Allied and Axis Property," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 586.7 (13 Oct 49)CPC/PLD, SCAPIN 2051, 13 October 1949, subject, "Notification that the United States, United Kingdom and France are Owners of Certain Categories of Former German Property in Japan," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, as amended

c. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 072 (15 Oct 51)CPC/OD, SCAPIN 2176, 15 October 1951, subject, "Final Disposition of Formerly German-Owned Fatents, Utility Models, Designs, and Pending Applications Therefor," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

d. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.7 (25 Jan 52)CPC/OD, SCAPIN 2195, 25 January 1952, subject, "Property in Japan Formerly Owned by Certain Persons of German Nationality," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

e. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 072 (18 Feb 52)CPC/OD, SCAPIN 7497-A, 18 February 1952, subject, "Final Disposition of formerly German-Owned Trade-Marks and Trade Names," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers will relinquish all responsibilities as Trustee for France, the United Kingdom and the United States of all property coming within the meaning of reference 1 above on the coming into force of the Peace Treaty.

3. It is anticipated that a Tri-Partite Commission consisting of representatives of France, the United Kingdom and the United States will be formed to assume the responsibilities now exercised by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

4. The Japanese Government is advised therefore that on and after the effective date of the Peace Treaty all matters pertaining to subject property should be referred to the Tri-Partite Commission if it is in existence at that time. In the event that said Commission has not yet been formed, these matters should be referred to the representatives in Japan of the three Powers concerned.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C.C.B. WARDEN
Colonel, AGC
Adjutant General
