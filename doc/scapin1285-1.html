GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.3 (7 Jun 47) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1285/1)

APO 500

7 June 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Release of Telephone Exchange and Telephone Equipment from Plants Named for Reparations.

1. References:

a. (SCAPIN 1285), file AG 311.3 (23 October 1946) CCS, subject same as above.

b. Letter, Central Liaison Office, Tokyo to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file CLO No.1741 (RSG-E), dated 14 March 1947, subject same as above.

2. The Ministry of Communications will arrange to assume ownership of the telephone exchanges and telephone equipment listed in inclosure 1, which names fifty-six (56) plants subject to reparations.

3. No communications equipment will be removed from plants in which the exchanges or telephone equipment are being used by the Occupation Forces or for requirements to meet Occupation Force needs.

4. The following named men will be responsible for the removal of any communications equipment in his respective bureau.

Tokyo Hiroya Yoshida

Nagoya Satoshi Kojima

Osaka Yoshitaka Nagata

Hiroshima Kunio Endo

Matsuyama Kikuo Kosuga

Kumamoto Katsutaro Munakata

Sendai Katsuhiko Yoshimura

Sapporo Toshio Murai

Nagano Heio Fujino

Kanazawa Takao Omura

5. The request for additional exchanges which the Ministry of Communications wishes to assume ownership of and which are listed on the inventory of 1 December will be made the subject of a separate request.

6. Direct correspondence between General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Civil Communications Section, and the Ministry of Communications on technical matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

1 Incl

List of 56 Plants Subject to Reparations

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

7 June 1947

MEMORANDUM:

SUBJECT : Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive (SCAPIN 1285/1), file AG 311.3 ( 7 Jun 47 )CCS, this Headquarters, dated 7 June 1947, subject: Release of Telephone Exchange and Telephone Equipment from Plants Named for Reparations

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. Release of the subject telephone and telegraph equipment is required in connection with the provision of communication services for the Occupation Force, or to implement the Occupation mission. In general, the condition of equipment and facilities involved is such that it would not be practical to remove it to points outside of Japan for reinstallation.

3. The fifty-six (56) plants listed in the inclosure to the SCAPIN are a portion of the exchanges and equipment located in aircraft factories, arsenals, privately owned munitions plants and laboratories held in custody for reparations. Further lists will be submitted by the Ministry of Communications for approval of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

4. Command channel instructions regarding permission and surveillance necessary in connection with the assumption of ownership and partial removal of this equipment will be forwarded.

A J REHE

Major, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
