GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 323.31 (9 Dec 47) GC-O

(SCAPIN - 1770/1)

APO 500

9 December 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Closing of Ujina (Kure Area) Reception Center.

1. Reference is made to memorandum from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 323.31 (8 Sep 47)GC-O, (SCAPIN 1770), dated 8 September 1947, subject as above.

2. So much of paragraph 3 c of the reference paragraph 1 above, as reads "to receive and process at Kure, up to 50 Japanese repatriates in one day" is deleted, and the words "to receive and process, in the Kure area, up to 100 Japanese repatriates in one day", are substituted therefor.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
