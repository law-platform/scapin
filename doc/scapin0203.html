GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 800.92 (28 Oct 45) NR

(SCAPIN - 203)

APO 500

28 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Survey of Japanese Fishery Activities in Korea (Chosen) area.

1. You will supply this Headquarters with a detailed report on the following subjects by 15 November 1945. This report should be accompanied by appropriate charts, maps, photographs, drawings, and statistical tabulations.

a. Fishery activities conducted under Japanese auspices in the Korea (Chosen) area during the period 1939-1945, inclusive. This should embody detailed information on:

(1) Location of fishing grounds off Korea where the Japanese conducted operations, with appropriate charts

(2) Species of fish caught

(3) Operational techniques of craft and fishing gear with appropriate photographs and drawings

(4) Statistics on the amount of fish caught by species and localities

(5) Number and types of fishing boats

(6) Number of fishermen engaged

(7) Methods of organizing the fishing enterprises

(8) List of fishing ports where Japanese landed their catches either in Korea or Japan and amounts, y species of fish landed at these ports

(9) Description of any shore facilities used for handling the catch including processing and warehousing plants

(10) Description of techniques for salting, drying, or otherwise processing the catch

(11) Marketing and distribution techniques

(12) Administration and controls exercised over the fishing fleet and shore activities.

b. Information on the ultimate disposition of Japanese fishing boat and fishing nets or other fishing equipment and shore facilities following the withdrawal of Japanese from Korea.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
