MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Results of War Crimes Trials.

Notice is hereby given of the following results of War Crimes Trials by the Permanent Military Tribunal of Saigon as reported by the French Mission.

DESIGNATION

DATE OF SENTENCE

Sentenced to death

7 Oct 46
