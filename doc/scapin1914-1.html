AG 410.2 (28 Jun 48) CPC/CD

(SCAPIN - 1914/1)

APO 500

25 October 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Authority to Use and Consume Pigments Containing Precious Metals.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. C.L.C.O. No. 718 (C.P.B. No. 221), 13 March 1948, subject, "Application for Revision of Treatment of" Gold-containing Colours "Made Partly from Gold (Silver) or Gold Bullions"

b. Memorandum for Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (28 Jun 48) CPC/CD, SCAPIN 1914, 28 June 1948, subject, "Responsibility of Japanese Government for Precious Metal Procedures."

2. As requested by reference la above, approval is granted for the use and consumption of pigments containing precious metals in the pool of commercial products under custody of the Ministry of Finance, Japanese Government, without the prior approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. Released pigments may be used only for:

a. Production of items to be exported

b. Production of items to be sold to Central Purchasing Office

c. Development and production of export designs and samples.

3. The use and consumption of pigments herein authorized will be supervised by the Japanese Government, as detailed in reference lb above. Reports on the consumption of pigments herein authorized for release will be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, as directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
