GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (18 Oct 46) GA

(SCAPIN - 1279)

APO 500

18 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Natives of China and Formosa.

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 370.05 (7 May 46) GC, (SCAPIN-927) dated 7 May 1946, subject: "Repatriation", as amended.

b. Memorandum from the Central Liaison Office, No.4769 (RF) dated 17 September 1946, subject: Repatriation of Chinese and Formosans.

2. Authority is granted to repatriate, in accordance with instructions contained in reference paragraph 1a above, the individuals in attached list A.

3. LST 0043 departing Sasebo on or about 24 October 1946 for Fusan, Shanghai, Canton and Kirun is designated to repatriate these individuals.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government will:

a. Notify the Chinese and Formosans concerned of this authorization to be repatriated.

b. Insure their arrival at the Sasebo Shipping Center prior to 24 October 1946 and their outloading on shipping as designated above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

1 Incl

List of individuals to be repatriated

(to addressee only)
