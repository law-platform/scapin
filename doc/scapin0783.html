GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.12 (1 Mar 46) CIS

(SCAPIN - 783)

APO 500

1 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Concerning the Use of Penal Establishments by the Armed Forces of the Allied Powers.

1. Reference is made to letter from the Imperial Japanese Government, C.L.O. No.690 (1.3). dated 23 November 1945, subject as above.

2. Reference paragraph 1 (a) and (b) of above letter; this headquarters cannot definitely state that other prisons will not be required for use by the Occupation Forces. However, the number required, if any, will be held to the absolute minimum, and a reasonable time be allowed for evacuation.

3. Reference paragraph 2 (a) and (b) of above letter; the Branch Prison at Datemachi, Fukuoka City, is being used for confinement of persons other than members of the Occupation Forces taken into custody by or at the direction of the Occupation Forces. Prisoners in Akita Prison are being held as suspected war criminals and will be prosecuted as such.

4. Reference paragraph 3 of above letter; the request for permission to use the evacuation house within the compounds of the (Tokyo) Sugamo Prison for execution purposes is disapproved.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
