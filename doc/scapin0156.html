GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 388.3 (17 Oct 45) GC

(SCAPIN - 156)

APO 500

17 October 1945

TO :Liaison Committee (Tokyo), For the Imperial Japanese Army and Navy.
SUBJECT :Demobilization of Japanese Military Police.

This is in answer to your letter of 9 October 1945 on the above subject. Instructions regarding demobilization of your Military Police are as follows:

a. Demobilization of the Japanese Military Police Corps will proceed strictly in accordance with the General Army Demobilization Plan recently approved and calling for effective demobilization of this Corps by 1 November 1945 with the minimum of necessary residual activities remaining until 1 December only.

b. At the appropriate time, based on the above, the Japanese Civil Police will be instructed by proper Japanese authority to take over all remaining police duties previously performed by the Military Police.

c. Occupation Force Commanders will comply with your request regarding channels for issuing instructions in connection with demobilization of Japanese Military Police.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen,
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
