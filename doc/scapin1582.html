GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 565 (25 Mar 47) GD

(SCAPIN - 1582)

APO 500

25 March 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Shipment of Limited Amounts of Ryukyuan-Owned Property in Japan.

1. Reference:

a. Memorandum from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 091 (30 Jul 46)GD (SCAPIN 1092), dated 30 July 1946, subject: Shipment of Limited Amounts of Korean-owned Property in Japan.

b. Central Liason Office letter 4976 (RJ) dated 27 September 1946, subject: Request of Ryukyuan to Carry Vermicelli Manufacturing Machine to Okinawa.

2. Permission is hereby granted to Ryukyuans already repatriated to have shipped to the Ryukyu Islands up to 4,000 pounds of property, consisting of light machinery, tools, and business equipment still owned by them in Japan, and amounts in excess of 4,000 pounds of such property upon prior specific approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to perform such necessary functions, of the nature indicated in reference a, as my be allocated to it by the Commanding General, Eighth Army, in connection with the shipment of subject property to the Ryukyu Islands, and to provide necessary shipping space, coordination and liaison as required in reference a.

4. Direct communication is authorized the Imperial Japanese Government in connection with this program with the Commanding General, Eighth Army, and with the Commander Naval Activities, Japan (Naval Shipping Control Authority for Japanese Merchant Marine).

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
