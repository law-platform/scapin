5 May 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
Subject :Applications for Travel of Japanese Nationals Abroad

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 014.331 (5 Jan 50) AG. SCAPIN 2072, subject: Applications for Travel of Japanese Nationals Abroad, 5 January 1950.

2. a. Paragraph 4b is amended to read: “The individual’s completion of the Application for Passport Form or the Application for Certificate of Identity for Travel Within the Far East Command Form in triplicate and supporting documents as specifies.”

b. Paragraph 4e is amended to read: “Issuing passport or Certificate of Identity to individual whose application for travel has been approved by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers (A Certificate of Identity is limited to use within the area of the Far East Command).”

c. Inclosure 6 is added: Application for Certificate of Identity for Travel Within the Far East Command Form.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.Bush
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.

Added 1 Incl
