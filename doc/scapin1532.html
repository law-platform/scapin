19 February 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Application for Discontinuance of Importation of Apples for Consumption by Occupation Forces.

1. Reference letter, Imperial Japanese Government, Central Liaison Office, Tokyo, subject as above, file C.L.O. No. 4807(EA), dated 19 September 1946.

2. Action has been initiated to discontinue at the earliest practicable date the importation of apples for use by the Occupation Forces in Japan for the reasons stipulated in referenced letter.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
