GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (14 Aug 50) CPC/USV

(SCAPIN-2120)

APO 500

14 August 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Rescission of Restrictions Controlling Industrial Diamonds.

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 091.3(30 Dec 45) ESS/FI, SCAPIN 515, 30 December 1945, subject, "Request for Use of Gold, Silver, Platinum and Diamonds," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 095(15 Jun 46) CPC/GP, SCAPIN 1487-A, 15 June 1946, subject, "Release of Diamonds of the Nippon Diamond Company," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

c. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, filo AG 413.1(18 Jun 46)CPC/GP, SCAPIN 1501-A, 18 June 1946, subject, "Release of Diamond Dies," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

d. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386,7(25 Sep 46)CPC/GP SCAPIN 2255-A, 25 September 1946, subject, "Release of Diamond Dies," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

e. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, filo AG 413.1(3 Oct 46) CPC/GP SCAPIN 2303-A, 3 October 1946, subject, "Release of Diamond Dies," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

f. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 413.1(23 Jan 47)CPC/GP SCAPIN 3065-A, 23 January 1947, subject, "Request for Release of Diamond Dies," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2, Reports, directed by reference 1b, 1c, 1d, 1e and 1f above, will no longer be required.

3, Provisions, requiring prior approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for transactions relating to property authorized for release by references cited in paragraph 1 above, are rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
