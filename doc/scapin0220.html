GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (31 Oct 45) CCS

(SCAPIN-220)

31 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Use of 8280 kilocycles frequency prohibited by Japanese Radio Stations.

1. The operation of radio station JCS located in Choshi City, Umigami District, Chiba Prefecture, on a frequency of 8280 kilocycles will be discontinued immediately. No other transmissions on this frequency are authorized by stations operating under the control of the Japanese Government.

2. Until further notice, station JCS may use 8200 kilocycles for fixed and coastal services.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
