GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 121.4 (29 Jan 47) CE

(SCAPIN-1495)

APO 500

29 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Submission of Construction Progress Reports

1. It is directed that one copy of the followiug construction reports, prepared under supervision of the Board of Reconstruction, be furnished every month to the Chief Engineer, General Headquarters Far East Command.

a. Monthly report on states of construction for accommodation for Allied Forces, including Table I (overall), Table II (Dependent Housing), Table III (Troop Housing), Table IV (General Construction).

b. Monthly report on concluded works of construction for occupation Forces, including Table V (overall), Table VI (Dependent Housing), Table VI (Dependent Housing), Table VII (Troop Housing), Table VIII (General Construction).

2. The above reports will be submitted initially for the month of December 1946 by 15 February 1947. Reports for January 1947 and subsequent months will be submitted by the end of the following month.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
