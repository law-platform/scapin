AG 004 (4 Oct 51) ESS/DF

(SCAPIN-2174)

APO 500

4 October 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Rescission of SCAPIN 734.

1. Reference the following Memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. AG 004 (6 Nov 45) ESS/ADM, SCAPIN 244, 6 November 1945, subject: Dissolution of Holding Companies.

b. AG 010 (14 Feb 46) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 734, 14 February 1946, subject: Proposed Legislation Prohibiting International Cartels and the Execution of Restrictive International Contracts.

2. Reference Memorandum, paragraph 1b, above, insofar as it constitutes a basis for Imperial Ordinance No. 33 of 1946, is hereby rescinded; provided, however, that nothing herein shall be construed as in any manner cancelling, modifying or adversely affecting the provisions of paragraph 7 of reference Memorandum, paragraph 1a, above, nor the basic policy and principles stated in Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 004 (3 Jan 46) ESS/LE, SCAPIN 539, 3 January 1946, subject: Disapproval of Plan for Prohibition of Cartels.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C.C.B. WARDEN
Colonel, AGC
Adjutant General
