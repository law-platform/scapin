AG 463.7 (2 Apr 49) GD

(SCAPIN - 1995/1)

APO 500

16 May 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Recovery of Waste Petroleum Products.

1. Reference:

Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 463,7 (2 Apr 49), SCAPIN 1995, 22 April 1949, subject: Recovery of Waste Petroleum Products.

2. Paragraph 5 b of the above reference is rescinded and the following substituted therefor:

"Imported" waste petroleum will include all petroleum products, except aircraft engine oils, procured by the United States Government which have been used or which have become contaminated, and cannot be utilized for their original purpose or as an acceptable substitute for any other refined petroleum product.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
