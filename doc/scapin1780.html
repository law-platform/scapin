AG 311.14 (18 Sep 47) CCS

(SCAPIN-1780)

APO 500

18 September 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Receipt of Japanese Stamp Stock Returned by United States Army Military Government in Korea.

1. The Japanese Government is directed to receive from Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, the Japanese stamp stock listed in Inclosure 1 which has been returned by the United States Army Military Government in Korea, and to execute the required therefor.

2. The value of the returned stamp stock in the amount of ¥ 4,568,021.60 will be credited against accountable stamp stock originally furnished by the Japanese Government to the former Korean Government General.

3. The Japanese Government is authorized to issue the returned stamp stock for sale through usual postal channels if not otherwise prohibited by Memorandum to the Japanese Government, AG 311.4 (13 May 46) CIE, 13 May 1946, (SCAPIN 947) subject: Prohibition of Certain Subjects in Designs of Japanese Postage Stamps and Currency. The returned stamp stock will not be sold for philatelic purposes.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

1 Incl:

List of Returned Japanese Stamp Stock.
