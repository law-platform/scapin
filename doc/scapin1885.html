MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Thirteenth, Fourteenth, Fifteenth Supplemental Requests for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in First Quarter of 1948; and Tenth Supplemental Request for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in Second Quarter of 1948.

1. Reference is made to:

a. C.L.O. No. 560(LO 46-AD/CU), 28 January 1948, subject, "Thirteenth Supplemental Request for Permission of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the First Quarter of 1948;"

b. C.L.O. No. 561 (LO 47-AD/CU), 28 January 1948, subject, "Fourteenth Supplemental Request for Permission of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the First Quarter of 1948;"

c. C.L.O. No. 562(LO 48-AD/CU), 28 January 1948, subject, "Fifteenth Supplemental Request for Permission of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the First Quarter of 1948;"

d. C.L.C.O. No. 355(LO 475 FI/2E), 19 February 1948, subject, "Tenth Supplemental Request for Permission of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the Second Quarter of 1948."

2. The application for permission to use silver, as requested by reference 1a above, is approved in the amounts and by the companies listed below. The released precious metals will be taken from Japanese Government stocks and consumed in the production of items licensed for export by authority order numbers listed. Tokuriki Honten may be licensed to process the released metal.

MANUFACTURER GRAMS SILVER BULLION RELEASED PERCENT TO BE EXPORTED EXPORT ORDER NO.

Daiichi Kinzoku K.K. 5051.25 100 EX 5112

Iriyama Seisakusho 3960 100 EX 3621

Kasahara Seisakusho 3601 100 EX 3646

3. Permission, requested by reference 1b above, is granted for the release of 158.75 grams gold dust to the Yugen Kaisha Taiyo Seisakusho, from the pooled reserve under custody of the Ministry of Finance. This dust will be used in producing articles licensed for export by Authority Order Number 5047.

4, Application for permission to use liquid gold, as requested by reference 1c above, is approved, as altered, in the amounts and by the companies listed below. Liquid gold stocks, in the pooled reserve under custody of the Ministry of Finance, will be drawn upon to supply the liquid gold authorized. The firms listed may be used to further process the pigment as noted.
