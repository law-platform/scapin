MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Withdrawals from Deposit Pass Books issued outside of Japan

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. Memorandum No. 1267 (5.1) dated 21 December 1945, subject: "Withdrawals from Deposit Pass Books brought in by Japanese Repatriates from Manchuria, Korea, Formosa, Okinawa and Saghalien."

2. Request for withdrawal in Japan from bank deposit pass books issued by banks outside of Japan and brought in by repatriates from areas outside of Japan is denied.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
