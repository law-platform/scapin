APO 500

20 February 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Clarification of Status of Former Suspected War Criminal.

The following named Japanese listed on Memorandum indicated below is deleted from said list, is released from suspicion as a Suspected War Criminal, and henceforth will be returned to his former status.

NAME MEMORANDUM 25 Nov 46 PAGE NO.

YOSHIHARA, Kane Lt. Gen. 1

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
