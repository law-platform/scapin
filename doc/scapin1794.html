GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 560 (8 Oct 47) GC-O

(SCAPIN - 1794)

APO 500

8 October 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Processing Japanese Crew Members of United States-Owned Ships Which Have Been Released from Repatriation Service

1. Reference is made to memorandum from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 560 (14 Dec 46) GC, (SCAPIN 1403) dated 14 December 1946, subject as above.

2. Repatriation ship John C. Ainsworth, VH-004, is scheduled to arrive at Yokohama on or about 24 October 1947. Ship is carrying approximately 1,520 crew members of former Japanese operated ships which have been released from repatriation service and returned to the United States.

3. The Japanese Government will process these returning crew members and their baggage and personal possessions in accordance with the provisions of memorandum referred to in paragraph 1 above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

AG 560 (8 Oct 47) GC-O

SUBJECT: Processing Japanese Crew Members of United States Owned Ships Which Have Been Released from Repatriation Service

TO: Commanding General, Eighth Army, APO 343

1. Reference is made to:

a. Letter from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 560 (14 Dec 46)GC, dated 14 December 1946, subject as above, with inclosure.

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 560 (8 Oct 47)GC-O, (SCAPIN 1794) dated 8 October 1947, subject as above, enclosed herewith.

2. Request routine supervision of compliance by the Japanese Government with the instructions contained in memorandum, reference paragraph 1 b above.

3. The Commanding Generals, IX Corps and 1st Cavalry Division have received copies of this letter and inclosure hereto.

BY COMMAND OF GENRERAL MacARTHUR:

A J REHE

Major, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
