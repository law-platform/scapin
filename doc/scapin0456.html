GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.5 (18 Dec 45) SC

(SCAPIN - 456)

18 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Permission to Use Code Books for Communications.

1. This Headquarters has no objection to use of the code books inclosed in Japanese Demobilization Ministry letter SDM No.99 dated 15 December 1945 on and after 1 January 1946.

2. It is desired that six (6) copies of each publication be furnished this Headquarters for censorship purposes.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
