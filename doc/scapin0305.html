GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (19 Nov 45) LS

(SCAPIN - 305)

19 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Lists of Japanese Naval Personnel.

The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to furnish this headquarters, within fifteen (15) days of the receipt of this memorandum, the names of all Army, Navy and Civilian personnel on duty aboard the following named ships during the listed voyages:

VESSEL ROUTE APPROXIMATE DATES

Asaka Maru Singapore to Japan 6 Jun 44 - 28 Aug 44

(Name Unknown) P.I. to Taiwan 2 Oct 44 - 13 Nov 44

Canadian Inventor II Manila to Japan 3 Jul 44 - 1 Sep 44

*Oryoku Maru Manila to Subic Bay P. I. 13 Dec 44 - 15 Dec 44

*Cattle Boat San Fernando, LaUnion to

Takao Formosa 28 Dec 44 -9 Jan 45

*Third Ship Takao Formosa to Moji Japan 12 Jan 45-30 Jan 45

Dai Ichi Maru Singapore to Moji 27 Oct 42 - 30 Nov 42

Fukai Maru Singapore to Fusan, Korea 16 Aug 42 - 24 Sep 42

Hakusan Maru Thailand to Japan 6 Jun 44 - 24 Aug 44

Hawaii Maru Singapore to Moji 2 Apr 43 - 24 Apr 43

Hofuku Maru Thailand to Japan Jan 44 - Sep 44

Honan Maru Singapore, Takan, Moji Oct 42 ---------

Lisbon Maru Hongkong to Osaka 29 Sep 42 - 2 Oct 42

Nissho Maru P. I. to Taiwan 6 May 44 - 4 Aug 44

Nitta Maru Wake I. to China 12 Jan 42 -24 Jan 42

Shingapore Maru Java to Japan 21 Oct 42 - 26 Nov 42

Tacoma Maru Batavia to Rangoon 16 Oct 42 - 7 Nov 42

Tofuku Maru Singapore to Moji 27 Oct 42 - 27 Nov 42

Tottori Maru Manila, Fushong, Osaka 8 Oct 42 - 11 Nov 42

Tsun Maru Hong Kong to Takoa F. 1 Oct 44 - 24 Nov 44

Dai Ichi Maru Manila to Formosa 1 Oct 44 - 8 Nov 44

Nigata Maru Manila to Moji 7 Nov 42 - 26 Nov 42

Sau Maru Manila to Osaka 1 Nov 42 - 20 Nov 42

*Information reveals that the ORYOKU MARU was distressed in Subic Nay, P. I. between 15 Dec 44 and 28 Dec 44, and the passengers transferred to a cattle boat. The cattle boat was distressed at Takao, Formosa between 9 Jan 45 and 12 Jan 45 and the passengers transferred to a third ship.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
