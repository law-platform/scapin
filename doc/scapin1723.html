GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (6 Jun 47) LS-R

(SCAPIN - 1723)

APO 500

6 June 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Clarification of Status of Former Suspected War Criminals.

1. The following-named Japanese listed, on Memorandums indicated below are deleted from said lists, are released from suspicion as Suspected War Criminals, are released from Sugamo Prison, and henceforth will be returned to their former status.

NAME MEMORANDUM PAGE NO.

NAKAMA, Ryohei, Sgt 13 Apr 46 2

SHONO, Eiji, Capt 5 Feb 47 5

TERATO, Hideo, Lt 25 Feb 47 5

YAMANOUCHI, Shoshichi, Pfc 1 Dec 45 3

2. All property of above-designated individuals controlled, impounded, or blocked under Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.7 (23 Apr 46) CPC, SCAPIN 897, subject: "Control, Impounding and Blocking of Property of Designated Individuals", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, is hereby directed to be released from the provisions of said Memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
