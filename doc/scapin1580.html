GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004 (25 Mar 47) CCS

(SCAPIN-1580)

APO 500

25 March 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Liquidation of International Telecommunications Company, Ltd., (Kokusai Denki Tsushin K. K.) and Japan Telegraph and Telephone Construction Company, Ltd., (Nippon Denshin, Denwa Kojii K. K.)

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 386.7 (19 Feb. 47) ESS/AC, (SCAPIN 3268-A), dated 19 February 1947, subject: Regulations Affecting International Telecommunications Company. Ltd., (Kokusai Denki Tsushin K. K.) and subsidiaries.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government will:

a. Repeal the special law creating the International Telecommunications Company, Ltd, Law No. 83 of 11 April 1939, as amended by Laws No. 10 of 26 March 1940 and No. 58 of 29 March 1940, and all ordinances and regulations pertaining thereto.

b. Assume as rapidly as possible actual operation of the communications properties of the International Telecommunications Company, Ltd. and the Japan Telegraph and telephone Construction Company, Ltd.

c. Purchase from the Holding Company Liquidation Commission the communications properties referred to in the preceding sub-paragraph upon completion of appraisal and evaluation of those properties by the Holding Company Liquidation Commission. The appraisal and evaluation will be in accordance with the standards and procedures established by the Enterprise Reconstruction and Reorganization Law, 11 October 1946, as promulgated by Imperial Ordinance No. 500, 30 October 1946. Instructions contained in this sub-paragraph will not delay the assumption of operations as provided in the preceding sub-paragraph.

d. Offer employment to such personnel of International Telecommunications Company, Ltd. and Japan Telegraph and Telephone Construction Company, Ltd. as are required to perform the communications services now being rendered by those companies. The personnel accepting the offer will be transferred to positions of similar grade and classification and in such manner as to receive fair and equitable treatment, retaining seniority benefits equivalent to those earned during their tenure of service with their present companies.

e. Submit, within sixty (60) days from the date of this memorandum, to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, in quintuplicate, typed in English on 8” X 11” paper, the following:

(1) A plan for the assumption by the Imperial Japanese Government of the ownership and operation of all communications facilities pertaining to telephone and telegraph now owned, operated, or both, by the Japan Telephone Equipment Company, Ltd.

(2) A statement explaining in detail the methods and procedures now used by the Ministry of Communications in letting contracts for the construction of buildings in the fields of postal, radio, telephone and telegraph communications.

3. Direct communication is authorized between the interested staff sections of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and agencies of the Imperial Japanese Government concerned to implement all provisions of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

25 March 1947

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT : Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN 1580), file AG 004 (25 Mar 47)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 25 March 1947, subject: Liquidation of International Telecommunications Company, Ltd., (Kokusai Donki Tsushin K. K.) and Japan Telegraph and Telephone Construction Company, Ltd., (Nippon Denshin Denwa Kojii K.K.)

1. With reference to the subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The International Telecommunications Company, Ltd., created in 1930 by special law and active in furthering the Japanese program of economic expansion and aggression, and its subsidiary the Japan Telegraph and Telephone Construction Company, Ltd., are being liquidated. Their communications facilities will be purchased by the Imperial Japanese Government and the communications services now being performed by them will be under direct operation of the Imperial Japanese Government.

3. As the Japan Telephone Equipment Company, Ltd., a subsidiary of the Japan Telegraph and Telephone Construction Company, Ltd., is also performing communications services, the Imperial Japanese Government is instructed to submit to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers a plan for the eventual ownership and operation of those facilities.

4. No command channel letter is being sent to the Eighth Army.

JOHN B. COOLEY

Colonel, AGD,

Adjutant General.
