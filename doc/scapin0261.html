GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 200.2 (10 Nov 45) CIS

(SCAPIN - 261)

10 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Credentials for Authorized Representatives of the SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS.

1. You are hereby directed to make available to authorized representative of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers (SCAP) upon their request, all records and files of government agencies, business enterprises, and organizations of any type. Authenticated Japanese language credentials establishing the identity of such personnel will be presented whenever examinations are made.

2. You are further directed to prepare and supply credentials for authorized representatives of SCAP. Credentials will be pr▲▲▲ Japanese, bear an official seal od the Imperial Japanese Government and an authenticating signature of an appropriate representative of the Imperial Japanese Government in accordance with instructions and sketch attached hereto as EXHIBIT. Three-thousand (3000) of these credentials will be made available to the Office of the Chief Counter Intelligence officer without delay.

3. You are further directed to issue necessary instructions throughout the Prefectures of Japan to the effect that above described credentials will be honored at all times. These credentials will be honored only when counter-signed by Brigadier General E. R. Thorpe, Chief Counter Intelligence Officer.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.

1 Incl:

EXHIBIT I

The Attached sketch will be used as a guide in presentation of credentials for authorized representatives of SCAP. In addition, the following instructions will be observed:

(1) Credentials will be printed on bond paper of approximately the same weight as attached sketch.

(2) Dimensions of credentials will be four and one-quarter inches by two and eleven-sixteenth inches (4-1/4" x 2-11/16").

(3) Credentials will be numbered in sequence from 1 through 3000. Numbers will be in Arabic and will appear I the upper right hand corner (see sketch).

(4) The caption "REPRESENTATIVE S.C.A.P." are the only words which will be printed in English. Japanese translation of these words will appear beneath them. The balance of the credential will be written in Japanese characters only.

(5) Signature of the representative of the Imperial Japanese Government will be placed in lower left hand corner signature will be affixed prior to delivery of credentials to the Office of the Chief Counter Intelligence Office.

(6) The Se▲▲ of the Imperial Japanese Government will be impressed in the lower left hand corner over the signature of above mentioned representative.

(7) Colonel A. W. STUART, Dai Ichi Building, will be consulted for any clarification or further information regarding the printing of these credentials.

(Sketch only on original memo to Jap Gov't)
