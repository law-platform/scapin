GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (27 Aug 46) GC

(SCAPIN-1159)

APO 500

27 August 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Natives of Saipan and Other Islands.

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 370.05 (7 May 46) GC, (SCAPIN-927), dated 7 May 1946, subject: "Repatriation," as amended.

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 370.05 (23 May 46)GC, (SCAPIN-979), dated 23 May 1946, subject as above.

c. C. L. O. number 4055 (RF), dated 16 August 1946, subject: "Repatriation of Natives of Islands Formerly under Japanese Mandate."

2. Libian and Silbas Tekoren, having failed to embark on the S. S. Fentress, which sailed froma Uraga on 22 July 1946, through unauthorized absence from the reception center at Uraga, have forfeited the privilege of repatriation under the provisions of Annex III reference 1a above.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government will:

a. Inform Libian and Silbas Tekeren of the decision in this case.

b. Inform those natives now awaiting repatriation to the former Mandated Islands that shipping is not now available and that they will be notified through the Imperial Japanese Government when arrangements are completed for their transportation.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
