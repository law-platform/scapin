GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (23 Jun 46) LS

(SCAPIN-1036)

APO 500

23 June 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

The Following named Japanese will be apprehended as Suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, EIGHTH Army, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest possible date.

NAME

RANK OR TITLE

PERTINENT DATA

ARAKI, Kaname

Second officer of Lisbon Maru during 1942.

Probable home address: 585 4 Chome Inatsuki-Cho, Oji-Ku, Tokyo-To.

HARADA, Hachiro

Major

Commander, Kempei Tai Koepang during 1942‐43.

HAYASHI, Sennosuke

Sgt/Maj

Guard Commander on Lisbon Maru during 1942,[Member of Hong Kong A.I.M. Dept.

HAYASHI, Yoshihide

M Gen

Chief of Military Administration during Japanese occupation of the Philippine Islands.

Formerly Deputy Chief of Staff of 14th Army.

HIYAMA, Seinoshin

Third Officer of Lisbon Maru during 1942. Probable home address: c/o Shigeru Homma Takaya Mase-Mura, Nishi Kambara-Gun, Niigata-Ken.

JYO, Choesu

Major

Member of Kempei Tai Singapore during 1942.

NAITO, Shinichi

Major

Battalion Commander, 131st Infantry Regiment in 1941. Commanding Officer, Singapore Branch 3rd Army Transport Command Saigon. Sailed to Japan 13 Mar 46 on board vessel Kashima Maru.

SUZUKI, Minami

Colonel

Commanding B.I.A. during 1942. Returned to Japan August 1942.

YOKOTA, Masataka

Lt. Col.

Member of Kempei Tai, Singapore during 1942. Attached to 2nd Field Gendarmerie Tai in 1941.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
