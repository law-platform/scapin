GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 370.05 (7 Nov 45) GC

(SCAPIN-248)

7 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Repatriation of Chinese and Korean Nationals.

1. Transmitted herewith for your information are lists of Chinese and Korean nationals located at Hitachi, whom the Commanding General, Eighth Army has recommended for repatriation at an early date.

2. It is desired that you return this personnel to their respective countries in accordance with the provisions of memorandum to the Japanese Government, file AG 370.5 (1 Nov 45)GC, dated 1 November 1945, subject “Repatriation of Non-Japanese from Japan.” No special priority should be given this group.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. Allen,
Colonel, A. G. D.
Asst. Adjutant General.

2 Inclosures:

Incl. 1-Manifests Koreans.

Incl. 2-Manifesrs Chinese.

(Incl not furnished infor addressees)
