GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

430 (11 Dec 45) ESS/PC

(SCAPIN-421)

11 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Report on Stocks of Staple Foodstuffs.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to inform this Headquarters when the stocks of staple foodstuffs in any city of more than 100,000 persons in the four main islands of Japan fall below ten days’ supply. Staple foods will include; rice, wheat, millet, naked barley, covered barley, sweet potatoes, irish potetoes, soy beans, and other beans.

2. At any time wnen the stocks of staple foodstuffs on hand in any of these cities is below ten days’ supply a daily report of the following information will be submitted to this Headquarters: (1) Actual stocks of staple foodstuffs on hand in terms of days of supply and in terms of metric tons of rice equivalents, (2) daily arrivals of rationed foodstuffs in terms of metric tons of rice equivalents, (3) daily distribution in terms of metric tons of rice equivalents.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
