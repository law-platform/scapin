GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 560 (7 Jan 47) ESS/IN

(SCAPIN - 1441)

APO 500

7 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Construction of Breakwater in the Izu Islands

1. Reference is C. L. O. letter No. 4669 (CM), subject: Conversion of Ex-Naval Vessels into Breakwater of Fishing Base on the Izu Islands, dated 13 September 1946, asking approval of a proposed construction plan of a breakwater in the Izu Islands.

2. The breakwater construction proposal is favorably considered. It is directed that prior to construction of the breakwater, detailed specifications, plans, method of financing and source and quantity of materials required be submitted for approval to the Supreme commander for the Allied Powers, Attention: Chief Economic and Scientific Section.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
for /t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1441)

17 December 1946

1. Reference is C. L. O. letter No. 4669 (CM), dated 13 September 1946, subject: Conversion of Ex-Naval Vessels into Breakwater on Izu Islands, asking permission to construct a breakwater to establish a major fishing base in the Izu Islands.

2. The proposed method of constructing the above breakwater is to sink and anchor one battleship, one destroyer and one coast defense vessel. Therefore, the request was referred to ComNavJap for comment and return. The returned correspondence indicated that the vessels were available for such a project.

3. The request was also referred to Natural Resources, Fisheries and that Section stated in their reply that such a project warranted favorable consideration as there would be considerable benefit derived from the construction of breakwater to establish a fishing base in the Izu Islands. NRS also confirmed the Japanese statement that due to the converging of the warm and cold currents in this area, the fishing operations were very successful as the converging cold and warm currents encouraged a feeding and mating ground for marine life.

4. The Industrial Division is aware that such a method of construction of a breakwater has certain drawbacks due to the possibility of the vessels shifting in a heavy storm which might possibly endanger local water traffic. The location of the proposed breakwater is remote from Allied shipping and any potential hazards are the responsibility of the Imperial Japanese Government. The proposed method of constructing the breakwater is very economical and can be accomplished in a considerably shorter time than the conventional method of constructing coffer dams, driving piles and concrete pourings.

5. Action taken: Since this project will involve the Ministries of Finance, Transportation, Forestry and Agriculture and the Home Ministry, this memo to IJG is issued in order that C. L. O. can coordinate the Ministries involved and submit to SCAP for approval detailed specifications, method of financing, procurement of materials and other pertinent problems involved in the proposed breakwater construction plan.

6. This is an administrative matter and no surveillance by EIGHTH Army is required.

7. Concurrences: Natural Resources, Fisheries (Capt. Ashmore).

J. Z. R.
