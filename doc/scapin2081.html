GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.7 (17 Feb 50) CPC/PLD

(SCAPIN-2081)

APO 500

17 February 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Proposed Payment of Claims Against Certain German Individuals and Juridical Persons.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386,7 (13 Oct 49)CPC/PLD, SCAPIN 2051, 13 October 1949, subject, "Notification that the United States, United Kingdom and France are Owners of Certain Categories of Former German Property in Japan," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The Japanese Government is hereby notified that the United States, United Kingdom and France for whom the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers is trustee will consider the settlement of all valid claims, both secured and unsecured, against property or persons falling within the provisions of reference memorandum 1 above by Japanese nationals now resident in Japan or other nationals resident in Japan since December 1941 to the extent of assets in Japan of their specific debtor.

3. The Japanese Government is therefore directed to order the presentation within a fixed period of ninety (90) days of all claims, matured and unmatured, together with proof or substantiation thereof against property or persons falling within the provisions of reference memorandum 1 above and to establish procedures for the invalidation of claims not submitted within the required period.

4. The Japanese Government is further directed that upon termination of the fixed period all claims will be consolidated and individual reports forwarded to Civil Property Custodian, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for each person and firm for whom claims have been filed. Each report will include recommendations regarding the validity of each claim and sufficiency of the proof thereof.

5. The Japanese Government will prepare and present to Civil Property Custodian, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers by 1 March 1950 such plans for action as may be required to effectuate this directive.

6. Direct communication between Civil Property Custodian, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the appropriate agencies of the Japanese Government is hereby authorized to implement the provisions of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
