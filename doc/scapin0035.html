OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (18 Sep 45) GC

(SCAPIN - 35)

APO 500

18 September 1945

SUBJECT :Discontinuance of Radio Circuit.
TO :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :The Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.

1. The radio circuit between the Headquarters of Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Manila(Call sigh WTA) and the Imperial Japanese Government, Tokyo (Call Sigh JNP) has been discontinued.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is no longer required to maintain the Tokyo Terminal of the above circuit.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ Harold Fair
/t/ HAROLD FAIR
Lt. Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General.
