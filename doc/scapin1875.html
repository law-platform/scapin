PROCESSORS

The cross-index numbers refer to processors, listed under paragraph 2b above, who are authorized to process allocated metals.

3. The following actions will be taken with regard to allocated metals:

a. The approval is for the use of 5,256,000 grams silver already in the stocks of the Fuji Shashin K. K. The extended use of this amount is approved hereby, and no silver will be released to the firm from Japanese Government stockpiles;

b. Unusable cathodes, containing approximately 70,000 grams silver, held by Mitsubishi Kogyo K. K., .will be processed into suitable forms and returned to the Japanese Government;

c. The 1,400 kilograms of 35% silver solder produced by Nippon Kikinzoku Seisakujo and the 400 kilograms of 50% silver solder produced by Ishifuku Kinzoku Kogyo K. K., under authority granted in paragraph 2a above, will be distributed to saw mills according to allocation plan approved by the Ministry of Agriculture, Japanese Government:

d. The 42,120 grams silver scrap and the 4,470 grams silver dross, in storage at the Tokyo Kogaku Kikai K. K. will be processed into suitable forms by the Ishifuku Kinzoku Kogyo K. K. and returned to the Japanese Government;

e. The following companies are licensed to produce transit scales, the parts of which will contain not more than 143 grams silver: Fuji Sokuryoki K. K., K.K. Hitachi Seisakusho, K.K. Kenseisha, K. K. Sokkisha, Nakaasa Sokki K. K., Tokyo Kogaku Kikai K. K., Yamaguchi Sokuryoki K. K., and K. K. Tamaya Shoten. The Tokyo Sokki K. K. is licensed to produce transit scales, the part of which will contain not more than 102 grams silver. These limitations apply to metals approved for release by paragraph 2a above

f. All gold, silver, rhodium, iridium and iridosmium, as well as 3,900 grams platinum will be obtained from stocks in the possession of the Japanese Government;

g. 5,396.2 grams platinum will be released to authorized representatives of the Ministry of Finance, Japanese Government, from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan, Tokyo;

h. 810.5 grams palladium will be released to authorized representatives of the Ministry of Finance, Japanese Government, from the Eighth Army Vaults, Osaka Mint, Osaka;

4. At the time the precious metals are released from custody of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, under the authority of paragraph 3g and 3h above, the Japanese Government will deposit with the Custodian, Eighth Army Vaults, Bank of Japan, Tokyo 525,066 grams of silver. This silver will be in the form of bars of mint or near mint fineness.

5. The Japanese Government is directed to submit monthly reports to include the following information for each of the firms named in paragraph 2a, 2b and 2c above:

a. Silver, gold, platinum, rhodium, iridium, palladium, ididosmium ; silver and gold liquid, dust and foil on hand, as of the first day of the month;

b. Silver, gold, platinum, rhodium, iridium, palladium, iridosmium; silver and gold liquid, dust and foil received during the month;

c. Silver, gold, platinum, rhodium, iridium, palladium, iridosmium ; silver and gold liquid, dust and foil used during the month;

d. Silver, gold, platinum, rhodium, iridium, palladium, iridosmium ; silver and gold liquid, dust and foil on hand as of the last day of the month;

c. Products manufactured and disposition of same.

6. Reports will be submitted in quadruplicate not later than the fifteenth day of the month following that covered by the report. Those reports will include data pertaining to the Ministry of Welfare and the Ministry of Commerce, Utilitarian Merchandise Section, Japanese Government.

7. The period during which the subject allocation of precious metals may be used will begin upon publication and extend to include 30 June 1948.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
