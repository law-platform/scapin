GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 300.6 (27 Jun 49) ESS/FIN

(SCAPIN-2022)

APO 500

27 June 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Amendment of SCAPINS

1. Reference the following memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. AG 121.7 (24 Nov 45) ESS/FI, SCAPIN 337, 24 November 1945, subject: Elimination of War Profits and Reorganization of National Finances;

b. AG 121 (21 Jan 46) ESS/FI, SCAPIN 635, 21 January 1946, subject: Reduction of Government Borrowing and Expenditures, and

c. AG 121.7 (6 Sep 46) ESS/FI, SCAPIN 1186, 6 September 1946, subject: Disposition of Government Property.

2. SCAPIN 337 is hereby rescinded, effective as of the date of publication of this memorandum.

3. SCAPIN 635 is hereby rescinded, effective as of the date of publication of this memorandum.

4. The reports called for in paragraph 2c of reference 1c above are no longer required. Paragraphs 2d, 3 and 5 of reference 1c are hereby rescinded effective as of the date of publication of this memorandum.

5. Approval to dispose of miscellaneous state properties, withheld in paragraph 3 of reference 1c is hereby granted.

6. Those parts of reference 1c not specifically rescinded are not affected by this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2022)

WKL/EMR/ECL/eb

16 June 1949

1. ESS Memorandum for Division Chiefs, dated 2 June 1949, contains a list of reports and controls to be eliminated and directs each division to prepare memoranda for the Japanese Government eliminating those reports and controls pertaining to the particular division.

2. Basic memorandum to the Japanese Government rescinds specified paragraphs and sub-paragraphs of the SCAPINs listed below, each of which prescribed certain controls and reports of a financial nature.

a. SCAPIN 337, dated 24 November 1945, file AG 121.7, subject: Elimination of War Profits and Reorganization of National Finances.

b. SCAPIN 635, dated 21 January 1943, file AG 121.7, subject: Reduction of Government Borrowing and Expenditures, rescinded.

c. SCAPIN 1186, dated 3 September 1946, file AG 121.7, subject: Disposition of Government Property, reports called for in paragraph 2c no longer required; paragraph 2d, 3 and 5 rescinded.

3. Basic states that only those paragraphs of SCAPIN 1186 specifically listed as rescinded are affected.

4. Action taken is part of program to reduce unnecessary paper work by the Japanese Government and SCAP. Supervision of the approved budget of the Japanese Government will provide the requisite information and control previously provided by the rescinded portions of these SCAPINS.

5. No concurrence deemed necessary.

Mr. Leigh. 57-8417

W. K. L.
