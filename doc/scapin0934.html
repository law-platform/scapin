SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 350 (8 May 46) CIE

(SCAPIN - 934)

APO 500

8 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Suspension of SUZUKI, Hisashi. Principal, Hanamaki Middle School, Henuki-Gun, Iwate-Ken.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government, on receipt of this Memorandum, will suspend SUZUKI, Hisashi, from the position of Principal, Hanamaki Middle School, Henuki-Gun, Iwate-Ken, and will prohibit his future employment by all Japanese educational institutions or agencies, either public or private, until such time as he shall have been screened in accordance with provisions of Memorandum AG 350(30 Oct 45)CIE, Subject: "Investigation, Screening, and Certification of Teachers and Educational Officials."

2. SUZUKI, Hisashi, violated Par. 1c of SCAP Memorandum AG 350(30 Oct 45)CIE, Subject: "Investigation, Screening, and Certification of Teachers and Educational Officials" by recommending and hiring as a teacher in November 1945 TAJIMA, Kojiro, a former Naval Lieutenant, in direct violation of orders received from YAMAGUCHI, Zenji, Chief School Inspector for Iwate-Ken. SUZUKI, Hisashi, therefore, falls in the category of educational personnel enumerated as antagonistic to the objectives and policies of the occupation in Par. la of SCAP Memorandum AG 350 (30 Oct 45)CIE, Subject: "Investigation, Screening and Certification of Teachers and Educational Officials".

3. Action taken under this Memorandum will be reported to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers as soon as completed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. K. FITCH;
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
