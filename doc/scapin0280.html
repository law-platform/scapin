GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386 (14 Nov 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-280)

APO 500

14 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Transactions in Property in Japan Owned or Controlled by Persons Outside of Japan.

1. Reference is made to paragraphs 1 and 2 of CLO Memorandum No. 472 (2), dated 5 November 1945, subject: “Request for Approval in Advance of Foreign Exchange Transactions, etc.”

2. Subject to the conditions and exceptions set forth in paragraph 3, the Ministry of Finance may grant general approval to the following transactions by branches or agencies in Japan of concerns with head offices abroad:

a. The payment of wages, taxes and other expenses incurred in the normal operations of the branch or agency prior to 23 September 1945.

b. The payment of any expenses incurred in maintaining and preserving the property of the branch or agency subsequent to 23 September 1945.

c. The collection or payment of any claims or debts owed to or by such branch or agency and accruing by reason of operations prior to 23 September 1945.

3. a. There will be submitted to this Headquarters within 30 days reports on each branch or agency effected by paragraph 2 above. Reports will be submitted three copies in English and one copy in Japanese. Reports will contain the following information:

(1) Name and address of the concern having branch or agency in Japan; address of branch or agency in Japan; principal products manufactured or merchandised in Japan and abroad.

(2) Place of organization or incorporation of the concern and jurisdiction under which organized or incorporated.

(3) Type of business and detailed description of the operations and activities of the concern in Japan and abroad from 1 June 1937 to the present time, including activities since 24 September 1945.

(4) Names, nationalities and addresses of directors and executive officers of the concern as of 31 March 1942 and 24 September 1945.

(5) Names, nationalities and addresses of all shareholders or owners as of 31 March 1942 and 24 September 1945, having interests in the concern in excess of 5%.

b. Approval will not be given to any transactions involving property owned or controlled, directly or indirectly, in whole or in part, by persons subject to the ordinance of the Ministry of Finance on the Preservation of the Property of the Specified Powers (CLOM 49), or to transactions involving the property of such other persons as may have been, or may be specified by this Headquarters.

c. This memorandum shall not be deemed to constitute approval of any transaction which is prohibited by any provisions of any directive issued by this Headquarters other than paragraph 1d of the Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government file AG 130 (22 Sep 45) ESS, subject: “Control of Financial Transactions" and paragraph 1 of Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government file AG 160 (30 Oct 45) ESS/FI, subject: “Contracts of Persons in Japan with Foreign Concerns”.

d. Approval will not be given to the resumption of normal operations of branches or agencies in Japan of concerns with head offices abroad without prior approval of this Headquarters. Applications for permission to approve such resumption of operations by specific branches or agencies must be accompanied by the information specified in paragraph 3a above, if not already submitted prior to application.

4. Subject to the conditions set forth in paragraph 5, the Ministry of Finance may grant general approval for the payment of allowances to families in Japan of persons employed abroad by business concerns which have branch or head offices in Japan.

5. a. If not already submitted in connection with other applications, concerns paying allowances permitted in paragraph 4 will furnish the information specified in paragraph 3a.

b. Arrangements for payment of allowances must have been made prior to 23 September 1945 and allowances must not exceed those paid prior to such date.

c. Payment of allowances shall not be made from any fund or balance created by a transfer of funds to Japan subsequent to 15 August 1945.

6. Any ordinances, regulations or general licenses issued pursuant to this Memorandum must be approved by this Headquarters in advance of issuance.

7. An acknowledgment of the receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
