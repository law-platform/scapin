GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004 (9 May 46) ESS-FI

(SCAPIN-940)

APO 500

9 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Closed Institutions and Time Limits on Legal Action for Recourse.

1. Legal action to enforce contractual obligations, including the collection of checks, drafts, promissory notes, bills of exchange, and realizations on mortgages, pledges, endorsements, guarantees, and other evidence of debt is barred under Japanese law after the expiration of various periods of time subsequent to maturity or for other reasons. Institutions closed, or that may be closed, pursuant to Memorandum of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 091.3 (30 Sep. 45) ESS, dated 30 September 1945, subject: “Closing of Colonial and Foreign Banks and Special Wartime Institutions,” hold large numbers of such instruments and there is danger that delay in the liquidation of these institutions may result in loss to the holding institutions and others. Similarly Japanese statutes, ordinances on prescriptions and like time limits on legal action may affect creditors’ claims against closed institutions.

2. In order to accord the closed institutions and liquidators, appointed or to be appointed as well as the creditors of such institutions sufficient opportunity to enforce their claims in an orderly and unhurried manner and to prevent or minimize loss to the closed institutions and to their creditors, it is directed that the Imperial Japanese Government take all necessary action to suspend the effect of all past and present laws and or

dinances limiting the commencement of legal action on all claims maturing afther 1 January 1931, so that the Statutes of Limitations (prescriptions) cannot be pleaded as a defense or bar to any action brought by any closed institution or by any creditor of such institution of claims maturing after 1 January 1931. Action taken will include provisions to renew and revive any right or claim which may already have expired in consequence of such pleadings.

3. The suspension from regular Statutes of Limitations, ordered in paragraph 2 above, shall not apply to or inure for the benefit of third parties involved in claims of rights of recourse resulting from actions taken by the closed institutions and liquidators in accomplishing liquidation. Action, as necessary, will be taken by the Imperial Japanese Government to provide that the regular Statutes of Limitations affecting the various categories of action available to such third parties shall apply and begin to run as of the date of the commencement of the action by the closed institutions and liquidators, appointed or to be appointed.

4. The institutions at present falling within the purview of this order are listed on Inclosure 1 attached.

5. It is further directed that 3 copies of drafts of the laws or ordinances to be enacted, amended or promulgated in accordance herewith be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, for approval prior to enactment.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 940)

DHJ/WNR/cab

24 April 1946

1. Memo for the Imperial Japanese Government directs suspension of certain elements of Japanese Law with respect to time limitations for legal action in order to preserve the rights of institutions closed by order of GHQ, SCAP as well as certain rights of the creditors of such institutions.

2. Colonial and foreign banks, and certain other institutions whose primary objectives were the coordination of Japan's wartime monetary economy and the economic exploitation of areas occupied by Japan during its co-prosperity sphere phase, were closed at the time of Allied Occupation. These institutions are in process of liquidation by the Finance Division, ESS; and recourse to the makers, endorsers, guarantors, et cetera, o evidences of debt such as checks, drafts, promissory notes, bills of exchange, mortgages and the like, must be preserved in order to effect an orderly and unhurried liquidation of the assets of such institutions. The ramified operations of a number of these institutions considerably ante-date the declaration of war and it is anticipated that there may be claims made dating back as far as 1931. It is suspected that subsidies were paid to certain favored concerns by having loans made to them by the subject institutions and then allowing the period within which collection could be made o expire. In the absence of the proposed legislations such part of the closed institutions will be lost.

3. Govt. Sec. Concurs (H. E.R. for C.W.)

ESS-LE concurs (E. R. M.)

4. The interests of USAFIK have been considered. This action does not affect the Korean area.

5. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, from GHQ, SCAP, file AG 091. 3 (30 Sep 45) ESS, dated 30 September 1945, subject: "Closing of Colonial and Foreign Banks and Special Wartime Institutions."

D. H. J.

ADDENDUM :

Regarding query made by the Deputy Chief of Staff, the date 1 January 1931 has been used in this memorandum in order that possible claims from the Japanese exploitation of Manchuria may be included within the scope of the memorandum.

W. F. M.
