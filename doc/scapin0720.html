MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Application of Nippon Mining Company for Permission to Pay Third Call on Stock of Nissan Ekitai Nenryo Kabushiki Kaisha (Nissan Liquid Fuel Company, Ltd.).

1. Reference is made to the Memorandum from the Ministry of Finance, file LO 148, subject: “Application by Nippon Mining Company for Permission of Paying In of Unpaid Stocks,” dated 15 January 1946, requesting permission to pay the third call on certain stock owned by it of Nissan Ekitai Nenryo Kabushiki Kaisha, the object being the acquisition of capital for the extension of the synthetic petroleum plant of the latter at Wakamatsu, Fukuoka, Kyushu.

2. The above application is denied.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
for /t/ B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 720)

WFM/ITB/zl

1 February 1946

1. CLO by Memorandum LO 148, dated 15 January 1946, forwards the application of Nippon Mining Company to pay the third call amounting to ¥1,250,000 on 100,000 shares of stock of Nissan Ekitai Nenryo Kabushiki Kaisha (Nissan Liquid Fuel Company, Ltd.) owned by it.

2. a. Nippon Mining owns 50% of the stock of Nissan Ekitai Nenryo and exercises almost complete control over it. The other stockholder, Teikoku Nenryo Kogyo, also owns 50% of the stock but exercises practically no management control.

b. The stated purpose of the third call on its stock is the acquisition of capital by Nissan Ekitai Nenryo for the extension of its synthetic petroleum plant at Wakamatsu, Fukuoka, Kyushu.

c. SCAP by AG 300.8 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC, subject: “Regulations Affecting Restricted Concerns,” has established the policy of prohibiting any payment which is not incidental to the normal course of business.

d. Industrial Division, this section, considers any extension of existing facilities of synthetic petroleum plants undesirable because of the uneconomical cost of production, potential demands of such extensions on the iron and steel and other critical industries and the uneconomical use of coal which is the raw material from which synthetic petroleum is made.

3. The above indicates that the proposed extension of its plant by Nissan Ekitai Nenryo, is undesirable. The application is therefore denied.

4. Concurrence: Industrial Division and Finance Division.

S. W. W.
