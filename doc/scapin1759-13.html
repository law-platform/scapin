GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.23 (10 Aug 47) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1759/13)

APO 500

11 January 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service via the United States.

1. Reference is made to Memoranda for the Japanese Government as follows:

a. AG 311.23 (10 Aug 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1759, 10 August 1947, subject: Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service via the United States, as amended.

b. AG 311.22 (29 Sep 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1786, 29 September 1947, subject: Provision of Payment for International Messages Filed by Boeki Cho (Japanese Board of Trade).

c. AG 311.3 (8 Mar 49)CCS, SCAPIN 1980, 8 March 1949, subject: Equalization of Radiotelegraph Rates.

2. The Japanese Government, Ministry of Telecommunications, is authorized, effective upon receipt of this memorandum, to expand radiotelegraph service via the United States to include Full Rate and CDE radiotelegrams between Japan and ships at sea, of United States and other registry, via United States and Honolulu, T. H., coastal radio stations.

3. All messages will be prepaid at the point of origin.

4. Through rates per word, and division of revenue between the Japanese Ministry of Telecommunications and the American carriers, on radiotelegrams from Japan are shown in Inclosure 1. Revenue accruing to the Japanese Ministry of Telecommunications from Full Rate or CDE radiotelegrams incoming to Japan authorized by this memorandum will be $.20 per word and $.13 1/2 per word respectively as shown in Inclosure 1 of SCAPIN 1980. Ship station, coastal station and United States continental landline charges will accrue wholly to the American carriers. Payment of revenue accruing to the Japanese Ministry of Telecommunications and the American carriers on this type of traffic will be handled in the same manner as that currently in effect concerning international telegrams between Japan and the United States and Hawaii.

5. As provided for in Article 41, Section 2, of Radio Regulations (Atlantic City, 1947), the coastal station and ship station charges will follow the radiotelegrams in the regular international telegraph accounts.

6. Direct communication between Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Telecommunications, concerning matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K. B. BUSH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
