GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 602.1 (28 Nov 45) ESS/IE

(SCAPIN-362)

APO 500

28 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Liquidation of National Financial Control Association.

1. Recepit is acknowledged of your memorandum, file number CLO 674 (2. 1), dated 21 November, 1945, enclosing a plan for liquidation of the National Financial Control Association (Zenkoku Kinyu Toseikai).

2. The plan as submitted is approved subject to the following requirements:

a. The Finance Ministry Ordinance governing this liquidation and an English translation thereof shall be furnished to this headquarters.

b. The liquidator immediately upon appointment shall submit to this headquarters a report giving full detaiis concerning the “Special Funds” of \ 377,440.52 shown in the financial statement of this association as of 30 September 1945.

c. Upon completion of the liquidation a complete report on this liquidation shall be submitted to this headquarters.

d. The liquidator shall take possession of and preserve all books and records of the association and permit examination thereof as required by representatives of this headquarters.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
