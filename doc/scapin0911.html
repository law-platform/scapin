GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 464.1 (30 April 46) ESS/IE

(SCAPIN-911)

APO 500

30 April 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Shipment of Grain to Japan.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will take action and assume responsibility as follows with regard to 24,612 long tons of grain being made available for future Japanese consumption:

a. Accept delivery of three shiploads of grain totaling 24,612 long tons at a point designated by the Commanding General, Eighth United States Army. Distribution will be made as shown below:

DATE PORT QUANTITY

1 May 1946 Nagoya 8113 long tons bulk wheat

6 May 1946 Yokohama 7942 〃 〃 〃 〃

334 〃 〃 bagged wheat

8 May 1946 Nagoya 7883 〃 〃 bulk 〃

340 〃 〃 bagged 〃

b. Furnish the Commanding General, Eighth United States Army with quantitative receipts for the grain at the point of delivery.

c. Store the subject grain so that it is protected against spoilage, pilferage, theft or other hazards and is under proper custody at all times.

2. The grain will be held in storage until official authorization for release is given by the General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

3. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 430 (11 Apr. 46) ESS/PC (SCAPIN 960), dated 11 April 1946, subject: “Maintenance of Records of Storage and Distribution of Imported Foodstuffs Accounting and disposition of the subject grain will be in accordance with the foregoing memorandum.

4. Terms of payment and accounting will be decided later.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 911)

RAM/HJZ/jmb

25 April 1946

1. Reference is made to radio SPTAE 10402 GO from SFPE to Commanding General, Eighth Army advising that civilian feeding program WP-13 revised provides for the dispatch of 8 shiploads of grain from West Coast during April.

2. This directive instructs the Imperial Japanese Government to take delivery of 24, 622 long tons of grain on three vessels which are enroute and will arrive on 1, 6 and 8 May 1946.

3. Command letter to Commanding General, Eighth Army requests supervision of the transfer and advises that the 24,622 long tons is part of the approved civilian feeding program WP-13 as revised.

4. This problem as it relates to Korea has been considered.

5. This is a policy directive and Army surveillance is necessary.

6. Concurrences: NRS - Maj Williamson

G-4 - Supply - Maj Mancuso

G-4 - Trans. - Col Lind

G-4 - P&P - Col Ebel

PC&R - Capt. McLeod

R. A. M.
