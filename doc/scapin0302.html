GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.3 (18 Nov 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 302)

18 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Supply of Coal to the British Government.

1. Reference is made to letter your office dated 14 November 1945, file number CLP-No. 576(2), same subject as above. Your inquiries are answered as follows:

a. Coal is to be used for public utilities plants.

b. Reconsideration of sources of supply for the next three liftings is not possible at the present time due next three liftings is not possible at the present time due to the extent which preliminary loading plans have progressed.

c. S. S. "FORT FUFFALO" is scheduled to load at Kakino Ura Shima. It is directed that measures required by you to insure loading of 8,000 tons coal on rhis vessel at this point be undertaken.

2. Specific instructions for the loading of the next two vessels with 8,000 tons coal each will be forwarded as soon as possible.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
