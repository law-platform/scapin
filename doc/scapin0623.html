GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123 (19 Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 623)

APO 500

19 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Release of Blocked Funds

1. Reference is your communication C.L.O. No. 151 (2.1) 11 January 1943, Application from the Yamato Fishing Company, Ltd.

2. You are referred to Ministry of Finance communication LO 96 (19 Dec 45) subject: "Petitions to your Headquarters."

3. The Finance Ministry is at present preparing new ordinances and regulations under which releases of blocked funds will be permitted under provisions of Paragraph 2b (2) of Memorandum, this Headquarters, File AG 132 (2 Jan 45) ESS/FI, dated 2 January 1946, subject: "Exemptions and Release of Funds from Blocked Accounts."

4. The application of Yamato Fishing Company, Ltd. is returned.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
