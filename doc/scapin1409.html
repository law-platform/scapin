APO 500

17 December 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Protection and Preservation of the SS "Gyonan Maru" Ex SS "Hirondello,".

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 560 (5 Oct 46) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1254, 5 October 1946, subject, "Salvage, Repair and Refitting of Captured Vessels", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 560 (4 Nov 46) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1311, 4 November 1946, subject, "Repair and Refitting SS 'Gyonan Maru' ex 'Hirondelle', SCAJAP No. G009", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to halt the repair and refitting of the SS "Gyonan Maru" ex SS "Hirondelle", which were directed in paragraph 2, reference 1b above, pending further instructions.

3. The SS "Hirondelle" is to be placed in the protective custody of the Imperial Japanese Government at the Hakodate Shipyard, Hakodate, pending further instructions. The ship is to be moored and secured, protected from theft and damage and maintained in a seaworthy condition.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
