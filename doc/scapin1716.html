3 June 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Allocation of Gold, Silver and Platinum for Domestic Consumption.

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. No. 6479(LO 2346-FI/FF), 6 December 1946, subject, "Application for Permission to Use Precious Metals in First Quarter of 1947".

2. Allocations of precious metals for use by the Ministry of Education are approved as follows:

a. Tokyo Imperial University, Second Faculty of Engineering is allocated:

(1) 165 grams of gold (to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 7510 grams of silver (5240 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 2270 grams to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(3) 690 grams of platinum (131 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 559 grams to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

b. Tokyo Imperial University. Institute of Science and Technology is allocated:

(1) 43 grams of gold (to be allocated from 260 grams on hand. Surplus gold of 217 grams on hand to be delivered to the Custodian of the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

(2) 238 grams of silver (238 grams to be allocated from 3989.7 grams on hand. Surplus silver of 3751.7 grams to be delivered to the Custodian of the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

(3) 150.375 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

c. Kyushu Imperial University Hospital Attached to the Faculty of Medicine is allocated:

(1) 3010 grams of gold (21.5 grams will be allocated from stocks on hand; 2988.5 grams to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 3170 grams of silver (86 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 3084 grams to be released from the stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(3) 349.5 grams of platinum (35.9 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 313.6 grams to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

d. Tohoku Imperial University, Faculty of Science is allocated:

(1) 121 grams of gold (2 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 119 grams to be released from the stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 706 grams of silver (4 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 702 grams to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(3) 611.1 grams of platinum (8 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 603.1 grams to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

e. Matumoto Medical College is allocated:

(1) 600 grams of silver (to be released from the stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 80 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

f. Toyama Pharmacy College is allocated:

(1) 9 grams of gold (to be released from the stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 985 grams of silver (to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(3) 50 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

g. Gifu College of Agriculture is allocated:

(1) 10 grams of platinum (5 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 5 grams to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

h. Tokyo Pharmaceutical College is allocated:

(1) 1.2 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

i. Physical Institute for Radio Waves is allocated:

(1) 12 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

j. Foundation Fertilizer Institution. Agricultural Department of Tokyo Imperial University is allocated:

(1) 121 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

k. Research Institute of Electric and Magnetic Alloys is allocated:

(1) 26 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

l. Institute of Bearing Research is allocated:

(1) 300 grams of silver (to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

m. Kitasato Institute for Infectious Diseases is allocated:

(1) 10 grams of gold (to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 200 grams of silver (to be released from stocks on hand, held by the Japanese Government).

(3) 14.6 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

n. Japan Baby Nourishment Association is allocated:

(1) 22 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

o. Institute of Scientific and Industrial Research is allocated:

(1) 7 grams of gold (to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 160 grams of silver (to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(3) 557 grams of platinum (90 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 467 grams to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

p. Osaka Imperial University, Microbiology Laboratory is allocated:

(1) 5 grams of platinum (1 gram to be allocated from stocks on hand; 4 grams to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

q. Tokyo Institute of Technology, Chemical Research Laboratory for Natural Resources is allocated:

(1) 56 grams of gold (to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 7300 grams of silver (2000 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 5300 grams to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(3) 180.1 grams of platinum (to be allocated from stocks on hand; surplus of 64.9 grams of platinum on hand to be delivered to the Custodian of the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

r. Chiba Medical University is allocated:

(1) 12 grams of gold (2 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 10 grams to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 1150 grams of silver (150 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 1000 grams to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(3) 45 grams of platinum (2.63 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 42.37 grams to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

s. Kanazawa Medical University is allocated:

(1) 19 grams of gold (to be released from the stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 457 grams of silver (25 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 432 grams to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(3) 6.4 grams of platinum (to be allocated from stocks on hand; surplus of 75.6 grams on hand to be delivered to the Custodian of the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

t. Nagasaki University of Medicine is allocated:

(1) 95.8 grams of platinum (to be-released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

u. Hiroshima University of Literature and Science is allocated:

(1) 24 grams of silver (to be released from the stocks held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 54.6 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

v. Medical Faculty of Okayama is allocated:

(1) 15 grams of gold (to be released from the stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 25 grams of silver (to be released from stocks held by the Japanese Government).

(3) 37 grams of platinum (to be allocated from stocks on hand; the surplus of 250.15 grams of platinum on hand to be delivered to the Custodian of the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

w. Medical School of Keio-Gijuku University is allocated:

(1) 50 grams of gold (to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 1050 grams of silver (to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(3) 397.7 grams of platinum (40 grams to be allocated from stocks on hand; 357.7 to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

x. Kiriu Technical College is allocated:

(1) 30 grams of gold (to be released from stocks on hand hold by the Japanese Government).

(2) 30 grams of silver (to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(3) 53 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

y. Sendai Technical College is allocated:

(1) 6 grams of platinum (to be allocated from stocks on hand; the surplus of 24 grams on hand will be delivered to the Custodian of the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

z. Yamanashi Technical College is allocated:

(1) 57 grams or platinum (to be allocated from stocks on hand; the surplus of 93 grams of platinum on hand to be delivered to the Custodian of the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

aa. Ube Technical College is allocated:

(1) 50 grams of gold (to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 50 grams of silver (to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(3) 150 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

ab. Niihama Technical College is allocated:

(1) 3000 grams of silver (to be released from stocks held on hand by the Japanese Government).

(2) 110 grams of platinum (50 grams to be released from stocks on hand; 60 grams to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

ac. Takaoka Technical College is allocated:

(1) 50 grams of gold (to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(2) 50 grams of silver (to be released from stocks on hand held by the Japanese Government).

(3) 1010 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Amy Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

ad. Muroran Technical College is allocated:

(1) 40 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan).

3. The Japanese Government is directed to submit a monthly report to include the following information for each of the institutions named in paragraph 2, above:

a. Gold, silver and platinum on hand as of the first day of the month.

b. Gold, silver and platinum received during the month.

c. Gold, silver and platinum used during the month.

d. Gold, silver and platinum on hand as of the last day of the month.

e. Disposition of same.

4. Reports will be submitted in quadruplicate not later than the fifteenth day of the month following that covered by the report.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
