IN THE CLEAR
OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS
SIGNAL CORPS MESSAGE

(SCAPIN - 27)

GC-O FHB/dk 14 September 1945

FROM :SCAP
TO :JAPANESE MINISTRY OF THE NAVY
JAPANESE MINISTRY OF WAR
INFO :JAPANESE IMPERIAL GOVERNMENT
CG NIGHTH ARMY
CINCPAC ADV
COM THIRD FLEET
COM FIFTH FLEET
CINCAFPAC MANILA

REFER TO YOKOHAMA LIAISON COMMISSION MEMORANDUM NUMBER ONE THREE THREE OF ONE TWO SEPTEMBER REGARDING OCCUPATION OF THE OMINATO AREA (ZAX5431)

EIGHTH UNITED STATES ARMYWILL OCCUPY THE GENERAL AMORI DASH OMINATO AREA BEGINNING TWO SEVEN SEPTEMBER AS OUTLINED IN RADIO THIS HEADQUARTERS ZEBRA ABLE XRAY FIVE ONE FIVE NINE AS LATER CHANGED BY ZEBRA ABLE XRAY FIVE ONE SEVEN TWO PD PRIOR TO OCCUPATION OF AMORI AREA BY EIGHTH UNITED STATES ARMY THE UNITED STATES NAVAL COMMANDER IN THE OMINATO AREA EXERCISES NECESSARY CONTROL INCLUDING MINOR OCCUPATIONS TO CONTROL THE COASTAL WATERS AND NAVAL ANCHORAGES IN THE SUBJECT AREA PD EFFECTIVE ON OCCUPATION BY BY EIGHT ARMY FORCES THE COMMANDING GENERAL EIGHTH ARMY WILL CONTROL THE ENTIRE AREA EXCEPT FOR THE ACTUAL OMINATO NAVAL BASE AND THE KABAYAMA AIRFIELD WHICH WILL BE UNDER NAVAL CONTROL PD ANY NECESSARY COORDINATION OR NEGOTIATIONS REGARDING THE AMORI DASH OMINATO AREA

EXCEPT FOR MATTERS PERTAINING TO THE OMINATO NAVAL BASE AND KABAYAMA AIRFIELD WILL BE CONDUCTED WITH THE EIGHTH UNITED STATES ARMY

OFFICIAL:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, US Army,
Adjutant General

APPROVED:

/s/S. J. Chamberlin
/t/S.J. CHAMBERLIN
Major General, G. S. C.
Asst. Chief of Staff, G-3
