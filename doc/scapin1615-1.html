GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.6 (18 Apr 47) CPC/PLD

(SCAPIN-1615/1)

APO 500

13 December 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Custody and Storage of Looted Property

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.6 (18 Apr 47) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1615, 18 April 1947, subject, “Custody, Shipment, Storage, and evaluation of Looted Property,” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

b. Memorandum for General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, C. L. O. No. 1931 (RP), 20 March 1947, subject, “Report on Looted Property,” submitting a list of looted property, designated “List 2 (B),” from the Japanese Government.

2. The Japanese Government is informed that paragraph 4 of SCAPIN 1615, reference paragraph 1a, is hereby rescinded. The Japanese Government is directed to release to their previous owners all property reported in list 2 (B), reference 1b above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K. B. BUSH,
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
