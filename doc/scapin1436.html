GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.3 (4 Jan 47) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN - 1436)

APO 500

4 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Restitution of Mr. M.s. Wiersum's Property Wrongfully Transferred.

1. 1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386.3(6 May 46)CPC, SCAPIN 926, 6 May 1946, subject, "Procedure for Returning Property in Japan to Nationals of the United Nations", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386.3(22 Nov 46)CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1354, 22 November 1946, subject, "Restitution of United Nations Nationals' Property Wrongfully Transferred", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

c. Memorandum to Ministry of Finance, Imperial Japanese Government, 386(9 Jul 46)CFC/FP, 9 July 1946, subject, "Property Owned by Mr. M. S. Wiersum on 7 December 1941", from Civil Property Custodian;

d. Memorandum for the Civil Property Custodian, LG 1838 (SF/FP), 11 October 1946, subject, "Report on Property Owned by Mr. M. S. Wiersum on 7 December 1941", from the Ministry of Finance.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to take possession of the land covered by the attached list preparatory to restoring the property to Mr. M. S. Wiersum who owed the property on 7 December 1941.

3. It is further directed that the Imperial Japanese Government will return all property on the attached list to Mr. M. S. Wiersum at the times specified. The land is to be returned on 10 January 1947 at 1000 hours at the address shown on list of property. The movables are to be delivered at such time as the property is returned to the Japanese Government by the Occupation Forces at 309 Omachi, Kamakura. All property on the attached list is to be returned under the conditions set forth in "Receipt for Restitution to United Nation National of Wrongfully Trans-frttrf Property in Japan", and under the following conditions:

a. A general and a final report by the Japanese Government on the management of the property from 7 December 1941 to the date of restoration, said report to include statement of all financial transactions will be attached to each copy of the receipt;

b. If the property has boon leased subsequent to seizure, the Japanese Government will afford the restoree the option either to allow the lease to continue in force orto terminate it at the time of the restoration of the property to him. Where the restoree decides to terminate such lease, the Japanese Government will take the act on necessary to provide him vacant possession;

c. All inventories included in the receipt will contain a statement as to the condition of the property at the time of restoration;

d. Cash and deposits covered by the receipt will be broken down into:

(1) Net proceeds from sale of property (if none, so state),

(2) Net proceeds from other transactions (if none, so state),

(3) Other (if none, so state).

e. Transfer of title will be made at the District Court having jurisdiction.

4. Four copies of accomplished receipt for the property with documentary evidence of the transfer of title will be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers within ten (10) days of completion of delivery. Receipts and evidence of transfer of title will be submitted in both English and Japanese text with the Japanese translation certified as correct.

5. Copy of this memorandum will be attached to each copy of the receipt.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
