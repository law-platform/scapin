MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Trademarks, Trade Names, and Company Names and Marks Used in Common by Companies in the Same Chain of Capital with Designated Holding Companies and Designated Excessive Economic Concentrations.

1. References are:

a. The following memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

(1) AG 004 (6 Nov 45) ESS/ADM, SCAPIN 244, 6 November 1945, subject: Dissolution of Holding Companies.

(2) AG 004 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 403, 8 December 1945, subject: Establishment of a Schedule of Restricted Concerns.

(3) AG 300.8 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 408, 8 December 1945, subject: Regulations Affecting Restricted Concerns.

(4) AG 004 (4 Apr 46) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 859, 4 April 1946, subject: Holding Company Liquidation Commission,

(5) AG 091.3 (23 Jul 46) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 1079, 23 July 1946, subject: Ordinances and Regulations Affecting the Holding Company Liquidation Commission.

(6) AG 095 (26 Nov 46) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 1363, 26 November 1946, subject: Transfer of Zaibatsu Family Properties to Holding Company Liquidation Commission.

b. The following ordinances and law of the Japanese Government:

(1) Imperial Ordinance No. 657 of 1945: Restrictions for the Dissolution of Companies Based upon Imperial Ordinance No. 542 of 1945 on Ordinances or Orders to be Issued as the Result of Acceptance of the Potsdam Declaration, as amended.

(2) Imperial Ordinance No. 233 of 1946: The Holding Company Liquidation Commission Ordinance, as amended.

(3) Imperial Ordinance No. 567 of 1946: On Restrictions of Securities Holdings, etc., by Companies, Based on Imperial Ordinance No. 542 of 1945 on Ordinances or Orders to be issued as the result of Acceptance of the Potsdam Declaration, as amended.

(4) Law for the Elimination of Excessive Concentrations of Economic Power: Law No. 207 of 1947

2. The Japanese Government is hereby directed to take necessary action to accomplish the following:

a. Amend, by Cabinet Order, Article 14 of Imperial Ordinance No. 567 of 1946, by adding thereto express authority on the part of the Holding Company Liquidation Commission to order any designated company, its subsidiary or affiliate, or any other company, and their successors in interest, to take necessary measures to cancel registration of specified trademarks, trade names, or company names and marks, and to refrain from use thereof, and from use of trademarks, trade names, or company names and marks similar thereto, and translations thereof into any foreign language, in the following cases:

(1) Where a trademark, trade name, or company name or mark of a holding company designated under Imperial Ordinance No. 233 of 1946, which is ordered cancelled and its use enjoined by the Holding Company Liquidation Commission, has been used by it in common with its subsidiary and/or affiliate companies; or a trademark, trade name, company name or mark of an excessive economic concentration designated under Law for the Elimination of Excessive Concentrations of Economic Power: Law No. 207 of 1947, which is ordered cancelled and its use enjoined by the Holding Company Liquidation Commission, has been used by it in common with other companies which together constitute or have constituted a combination in the same chain of capital.

(2) Where trademarks, trade names, or company names or marks include any of the following: Mitsui, Mitsubishi, Iwasaki, Sumitomo, Yasuda, Nomura, Okura, Asano, Nissan, Nakajima, Furukawa.

Provided, however, That the foregoing shall not apply to that part of any trademark, trade name, or company name or mark which is a geographical name (Tokyo, Osaka, etc.), or a word of common usage merely descriptive of goods and services or exhibiting no particular identifying characteristics of the companies concerned (Dai Nippon, Toyo, Teikoku, etc.), except where the Holding Company Liquidation Commission, in order to prevent an inequitable distribution of assets in reorganizing a designated excessive economic concentration under said Law No. 207 of 1947, enjoins by final order thereunder the use by such concentration, and its successors, of geographical names and words of common usage as a part of any trademark, trade name, or company name or mark.

b. Enact legislation removing from eligibility for future registration under the Trademark Law (Shohyo-ho), and permitting free use of, any trademark, trade name, or trademarks or trade names similar thereto, and translations thereof into any foreign langnage, the use of which by hitherto registered proprietors or users has been enjoined by the Holding Company Liquidation Commission in a final order issued by it under Article 14 of Imperial Ordinance No. 567 of 1946 (as amended in accordance with paragraph 2 hereof), or the Law for the Elimination of Excessive Concentrations of Economic Power: Law No. 207 of 1947.

c. Enact legislation forbidding future registration, and cancelling registration since the date of issuance of this memorandum, by any person or party under the Commercial Code (Shoho) or Civil Code (Minpo), and prohibiting rights of exclusive use, of any specified company name or mark, or name or mark substantially identical therewith, and translations thereof into any foreign language, the use of which by hitherto registered proprietors or users thereof has been enjoined by the Holding Company Liquidation Commission in a final order issued by it under Article 14 of Imperial Ordinance No. 567 of 1946 (as amended in accordance with paragraph 2 hereof), or the Law for the Elimination of Excessive Concentrations of Economic Power: Law No. 207 of 1947.

3. Pending enactment of the legistlation referred to in paragraphs 2b and c, above, the provisions thereof shall be effectuated by the issuance of a Cabinet Order within ten (10) days from the date of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

k. Law for the Elimination of Excessive Concentrations of Economic Power: Law No. 207 of 1947.

2. Under the Law for Elimination of Excessive Concentrations of Economic Power: Law No. 207 of 1947, the Holding Company Liquidation Commission may, with respect to designated excessive economic concentrations, order specified trademarks, trade names, and company names or marks to be cancelled as a necessary measure in the course of liquidation, reorganization, or other readjustment. Designated holding companies are under the jurisdiction of the HCLC pursuant to Imperial Ordinance No. 233 of 1946. Other companies, subsidiaries and affiliates of the foregoing, which may not be designated organizations under HCLC jurisdiction pursuant to Imperial Ordinance No. 233 or Law No. 207, may together with such economic concentrations and designated holding companies constitute units of a combination in the same chain of capital making common use of trademarks, trade names and company names or marks. Restricted Concerns, which are first line subsidiaries of designated holding companies, and the subsidiaries and affiliates of the former, are already subject to the jurisdiction of the HCLC, however, under Imperial Ordinance No. 567 of 1946 with respect to such matters as stock disposals, elimination of multiple directorships, and discontinuance of restrictive contracts effecting a restraint of competition.

3. The attached SCAPIN directs amendment of Imperial Ordinance No. 567 of 1946 to extend the authority of the HCLC over designated companies, subsidiaries, affiliated and other companies (including designated holding companies), with respect to cancellation of their trademarks, trade names, and company names or marks, and prohibition of use thereof, in such cases where they have been used in common with designated holding companies and excessive economic concentrations.

a. The trademarks, trade names and company names or marks to be dealt with by the HCLC under Imperial Ordinance No. 567 will be those whose use by designated holding companies and excessive economic concentrations is enjoined by the HCLC, and existing registrations ordered cancelled, in proceedings pursuant to Law No. 207 and Imperial Ordinance No. 567, as amended.

b. The HCLC will be empowered to order any company, whether a designated company, subsidiary, affiliate or other company, to discontinue use, as a part of any trademark, trade name or company name or marks, of the following personal names, associated with designated Zaibatsu families whose properties have been restricted under Imperial Ordinance No. 233: Mitsui, Mitsubishi, Iwasaki, Sumitomo, Yasuda, Nomura, Okura, Asano, Nissan, Nakajima, Furukawa.

c. It is expressly provided that the HCLC, in dealing with trademarks, trade names and company names or marks under Imperial Ordinance No. 567, shall not interfere with geographical names, words of common usage merely descriptive of goods or services or not exhibiting any particular identifying characteristics associated with the company involved, except where it is necessary, in order to prevent an inequitable distribution of assets in reorganizing a designated economic concentration under Law No. 207, for HCLC to enjoin, by final order thereunder, the use by such concentration, and its successors, of geographical names and words of common usage as a part of any trademark, trade name for company name or mark.

4. In order to depreciate the value of Zaibatsu trademarks and names, and others which have enjoyed monopolistic advantages or connotations of economic concentration, in the hands of possible new users - and to lessen the likelihood of future use by reliable firms capable of deriving a competitive advantage therefrom - the SCAPIN directs the enactment of legislation preventing future registrability and rights of exclusive user in such trademarks or names. The SCAPIN authorizes concellation of registration and discontinuance of use of such trademarks and names by the hitherto registered holders and users thereof, or their successors in interest, and permits use by others generally, without exclusive rights of user or protectable interests therein. An exception is made in favor of persons or parties not named in the order of the HCLC who are owners of marks or names in actual use registered in a class other than the class of registration of the marks or names ordered cancelled. Registration in such classes will not be disturbed. The owner, however, will not be able to extend his registration into classes other than the one of his existing registration.

5. Pending the enactment of legislation, the SCAPIN authorized the Japanese Government to carry out the provisions thereof by issuance of a Cabinet-Order within ten (10) days from the date of the memorandum.

6. This is an administrative matter and no surveillance by the Eighth Army is required.

7. The interests of USAFIK are not concerned.
