GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 072 (15 Oct 51) CPC/OD

(SCAPIN-2176)

APO 500

15 October 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Final Disposition of Formerly German-Owned Patents, Utility Models, Designs, and Pending Applications Therefor.

1. Reference is made to the following memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. File AG 091.112 (13 Sep 45) MG, SCAPIN 26, 13 September 1945, subject, “Protection of Allied and Axis Property”

b. File AG 386.7 (13 Oct 49) CPC/PLD, SCAPIN 2051, 13 October 1949, subject, “Notification that the United States, United Kingdom and France are Owners of Certain Categories of Former German Property in Japan”

c. File AG 386.7 (13 Oct 49) CPC/OD, SCAPIN 2051/1, 1 August 1951, subject, “Notification that the United States, United Kingdom and France are Owners of Certain Categories of Former German Property in Japan.”

2. It has been decided that subject industrial property, which is now owned by the United Sates, United Kingdom and France, shall be disposed of in the following manners:

a. Release from impoundment directed by reference 1a above

b. Transfer to a new registered owner or owners and release from impoundment directed by reference 1a above

c. Expiration of the statutory period of duration

d. Cancellation, with publication in the case of applications.

3. The Japanese Patent Agency, Ministry of International Trade and Industry, Japanese Government, from time to time will be furnished with lists of specific items of subject industrial property, clearly identified by registration number and name of registered owner, together with instructions detailing the type of disposition to be accomplished. In those cases where the industrial property being disposed of is subject to a non-exclusive, royalty-free license which was granted by Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and is cancellable at will, the instructions will provide for the cancellation of the license simultaneously with the disposition of the industrial property.

4. With reference to dispositions under paragraph 2a above the registered owners may regain control of this industrial property if they so desire but the Japanese Government may require the registered owners to pay all administrative fees in arrears on the date on which the property is released from impoundment. The Japanese Government shall grant the registered owners a grace period of not less than six (6) moths from the date of publication of the release from impoundment, in which to notify the Patent Agency of their intention to regain control of their property and within which to pay the administrative fees which are in arrears. The Japanese Government shall not, however, assess any special fees or charges as penalties for failure of the registered owners to pay the administrative fees while the property was subject to memoranda referenced in paragraphs 1a, 1b and 1c above. If the required fees are not paid within the grace period set by the Japanese Government the industrial property involved shall be subject to cancellation effective the end of the above grace period.

5. With reference to dispositions under paragraph 2b above the Japanese Government may require the new registered owners to pay all administrative fees in arrears on the date of trasfer of ownership to them. A grace period of not less than three (3) months from the date on which notice of the transfer shall be received by the Patent Agency shall be granted the new owners within which to make this payment. The Japanese Government shall not, however, assess any special fees or charges as penalties for non-payment of the administrative fees while the property was subject to memoranda referenced in paragraphs 1a, 1b and 1c above. If the required fees are not paid within the grace period set by the Japanese Government the industrial property involved shall be subject to cancellation effective the end of the above grace period.

6. In the case of dispositions by cancellation under paragraph 2d above, such cancellations shall be made effective sixty (60) days after the publication thereof described in paragraph 10 below. Such publication will include notice that during the interim period appeals against the dispositions may be submitted to the Patent Agency by any persons who believe themselves to be the holders of an interest in the property being disposed of which would prevent its cancellation. Appeals submitted to the Patent Agency will be transmitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers without delay for decisions thereon, and the Patent Agency will defer final disposition of the industrial property pending these decisions.

7. With reference to disposition and publication of applications under paragraph 2d above, no patent, utility model or design shall hereafter be granted covering subject matter disclosed and/or claimed in any of the cancelled applications unless the real or constructive filing date of the application therefor is prior to that of the cancelled application.

8. Administrative fees, referred to above, are to be computed in accordance with the statutory annual and other fees existing on the date on which payment was to be made.

9. In case of disposition of industrial property where the registered German owner retains his right of ownership after disposition, he shall not be permitted to claim royalties and/or damages for infringements for any period for which the annual fees have not been paid. In all other cases the registered German owner shall not be permitted to claim royalties and/or damages for infringements in any event.

10. At the earliest practicable date after the receipt of the instructions described in paragraph 3 above, the Patent Agency shall publish notice of the action directed therein in the appropriate official Patent Agency publication. With reference to dispositions under paragraph 2c it will be sufficient to publish the fact that patents, utility models and designs which have expired by completion of the period of duration have been or are being released from impoundment and may now be freely used by the public.

11. Nothing in this memorandum is to be construed to release the Japanese Government from the provisions of the memoranda referenced in paragraphs 1a, b and c above unless such release is specifically provided in the instructions described in paragraph 3.

12. The Japanese Government is directed to submit to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for approval, not later than twenty-one (21) days from the date of this memorandum, proposed legislation, Cabinet Orders, and/or Ministrial Orders setting out the laws, procedures, and practices intended to carry out the provisions of this memorandum.

13. Direct communication is authorized between interested sections of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the appropriate ministries of the Japanese Government on all matters connected with this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C.C.B. WARDEN
Colonel, AGC
Adjutant General
