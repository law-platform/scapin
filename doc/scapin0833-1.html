GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 684 (22 Mar 46) GC/TNG

(SCAPIN - 833/1)

APO 500

18 July 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Aerial Gunnery Ranges.

1. The name of the Honshu Area Aerial Gunnery Range is changed to the Central Honshu Air to Air Gunnery Range.

2. The coordinates of the range are charged from:

(36°00' N, 140°55' E) (36°00' N, 141°20' E)

(36°30' N, 140°55' E) (36°00' N, 141°20' E)

to:

(36°00' N, 140°55' E) (36°00' N, 141°11' E)

(36°40' N, 140°55' E) (36°40' N, 141°11' E)

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD
Adjutant General.
