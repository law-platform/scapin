GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (19 Feb 47) GA

(SCAPIN - 1534)

APO 500

19 February 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Italian Nationals.

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, AG 091.31 (19 Jan 46)ESS/FI (SCAPIN 622) dated 19 January 1946, subject: Supplemental Instructions Relating to Export

Control.

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, AG 014.33 (9 Jan 47)GA (SCAPIN 1450) dated 9 January 1947, subject: Closing of Uraga Reception Center.

2. The Italian nationals listed on attached nominal rolls desire to be repatriated on the Italian Motorship SESTRIERE, now expected to arrive at Uraga on 25 February 1947.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government will:

a. Postpone the closing of the Uraga Reception Center until completion of repatriation of the Italians aboard the SESTRIERE.

b. Make the necessary arrangements for and provide suitable transportation to the Italian nationals from their homes to Uraga Reception Center. Necessary measures must be taken to insure that the transportation provided for the Italian nationals arrives at Uraga Reception Center early enough on the day scheduled for embarkation to facilitate compliance with paragraph 3d below.

c. Medically examine and inoculate the Italian nationals upon arrival at Uraga Reception Center and process their belongings in accordance with the instructions contained in reference 1a above.

d. Embark Italian nationals on the SESTRIERE on the same day of their arrival at Uraga Reception Center.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government will be notified through the appropriate representative of Central Liaison Office of any change in the expected arrival date of the SESTRIERE.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
