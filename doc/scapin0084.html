IN THE CLEAR
OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS
SIGNAL CORPS MESSAGE

(SCAPIN -84)

2 October 1945

FROM :SCAP
TO :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
INFO :ADFEAF TOKYO
CG EIGHTH ARMY
CG SIXTH ARMY
COMAF FIVE

IN ACCORDANCE WITH PARA FIVE PAREN BAKER PAREN OF GENERAL ORDER NUMBER ONE CMA WHICH STATUS THAT JAPANESE AUTHORITIES WILL INSURE THAT ALL AIDS TO NAVIGATION BE REESTABLISHED AT ONCE C▲▲IT IS DIRECTED THAT TAKE NECESSARY ACTION TO INSURE THAT REQUESTS MADE BY SIXTH AND EIGHTH ARMY COMMANDERS TO BRANCHES OF CLO IN RESPECTIVE ARMY AREAS IN REGARD TO INSTALLATION CMA OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE OF RED OBSTRUCTION LIGHTS ON OBSTACLES SURROUNDING AIRDROMES USED BY OUR FORCES ARE COMPLIED WITH AND REQUIRED INSTALLATIONS ARE ACCOMPLISHED EXPEDITIOUSLY (ZAX 6391)

OFFICIAL:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, US Army,
Adjutant General

APPROVED:

/s/W. E. Chambers
/t/W. E. CHAMBERS
Brigadier General, G. S. C.
Acting Asst. Chief of Staff, G-3
