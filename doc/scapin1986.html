GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 150 (28 Mar 49) DS

(SCAPIN-1986)

APO 500

28 March 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Japanese Applications to United States Office of Alien Property for Return of Vested Property.

1. References:

a. Memorandum for Japanese Government, file AG 130 (22 Sep 45) ESS, SCAPIN 45, dated 22 Sep 45, subject: Control of Financial Transactions.

b. C. L. C. O. 2568 (1G), 13 July 1948.

c. Letter from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, 17 August 1948, subject: Applicability of First War Powers Act to Japan.

d. Letter from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, 18 October 1948, subject: Applicability of First War Powers Act to Japan.

e. Memorandum for Japanese Government, file AG 311 (9 Sep 47) CCS, SCAPIN 1771/3, dated 28 March 1949, subject: Relaxation of International Telecommunications Restrictions.

f. Memorandum for Japanese Government, file AG 311. 1 (28 May 48) CSS, SCAPIN 1900/7, dated 28 March 1949, subject: International Postal Service.

2. The supreme Commander for the Allied Powers hereby authorizes individuals resident in Japan, including Japanese nationals, who claim to have been discriminated against by Axis Governments, to communicate direct with the Office of Alien Property, Department of Justice, United States Government, Washington 25, D.C., in accordance with the procedure described in the inclosure to reference 1 d above for the purpose of filing claims for the return of their property vested in the United States Alien Property Custodian. Documents containing a power of attorney authorizing an agent to receive such property may also be forwarded to the Office of Alien Property as prescribed by the Notice of Claim for Return of Property inclosed with reference 1 d.

3. The Japanese Government is also authorized, in its discretion, to publicize in the press or by other appropriate means the procedure described in the Notice of Claim for Return of Property for the filing of claims by individuals in Japan and designating agents in the United States to receive property.

4. Any dollar proceeds which accrue from the return of property by the Alien Property Custodian to individuals resident in Japan and are remitted to Japan will be transmitted from the United States to Japan through the branches in Japan of one of the following banks:

a. The National City Bank of New York, Tokyo, Osaka and Yokohama.

b. The Chase National Bank, Tokyo and Osaka.

c. Bank of America National Trust and Savings Association, Tokyo, Kobe and Yokohama.

5. Direct communication between the Diplomatic Section and other staff sections of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the appropriate agencies of the Japanese Government is authorized concerning all matters within the scope of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M.LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
