GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.22 (1 May 48) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1851/1)

APO 500

1 May 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Establishment of Optional Deposit Plan for Payment of Charges for International Telegrams.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.22 (22 Jan 48)CCS, SCAPIN 1851, dated 22 January 1948, subject as above.

2. Paragraph 1a of SCAPIN 1851 is hereby rescinded and the following substituted in lieu thereof:

Applicants desiring to utilize this plan will deposit with the Ministry of Communications at the Tokyo Central Telegraph Office or at any other Telegraph Office or Post Office in Japan designated by the Ministry of Communications to handle optional deposits in accordance with this plan, a sum sufficient to offset the charges for their international telegrams for one month, in United States Military Payment Certificates, Foreign Trade Payment Certificates, British Armed Forces Special Vouchers, or Japanese yen, whichever is appropriate.

2. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section and the General Accounting Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Ministry of Communications is authorized concerning matters within the scope of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
