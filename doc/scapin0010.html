IN THE CLEAR
OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS
SIGNAL CORPS MASSAGE

(SCAPIN - 10)

GC MGM/gah 7 September 1945

FROM :SCAP
TO :JAPANESE IMPERIAL HEADQUARTERS
INFO :THE JAPANESE IMPERIAL GOVERNMENT
CG SIXTH ARMY
CG I CORPS
CG TENTH CORPS
CINCPAC ADV
CINCAFPAC MANILA
CG FEAF MANILA

DESIRE THAT THE COMMANDING GENERAL SECOND JAPANESE GENERAL ARMY BE DIRECTED TO ESTABLISH RADIO COMMUNICATION WITH THE COMMANDING GENERAL SIXTH UNITED STATES ARMY ON FREQUENCY OF ETHER ONE FOUR FOUR NAUGHT NAUGHT KILOCYCLES OR ONE FOUR NAUGHT THREE FIVE KILOCYCLES DURING DAYLIGHT HOURS COMMANDING NAUGHT EIGHT NAUGHT NAUGHT SEPTEMBER EIGHT TOKYO TIME PD (ZAX5148) CALL SIGN OF J5EREAT J5E WILL BE USED BY SECOND JAPANESE GENERAL ARMY PD FREQUENCY FOR NIGHT USE WILL BE IN ENGLISH CLEAR THAT USING RADIO TELEGRAPH PD FREQUENCY FOR NIGHT USE WILL BE COMMUNICATED TO SECOND JAPANESE GENERAL ARMY AFTER CONTACT IS ESTABLISHED

OFFICIAL:

/S/W.E. Chambers
/t/W.E. CHAMBERS
Brig General, G. S.C,
G-3, GHQ Advance
