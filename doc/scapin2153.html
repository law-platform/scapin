MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2153)

WFM/RMG, LAR/ld

15 May 1951

1. References are SCAP Memo AG 004 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 403, subject: Establishment of a Schedule of Restricted Concerns, and additions thereto.

2. The companies listed in the above memo were placed on the Schedule of Restricted Concerns, reference para 1, above, because they were first line subsidiaries of designated holding companies.

3. In compliance with Law No. 233 of 1946 the parent company, in all cases transferred the stock it held in its subsidiaries to the UCLC. This prevented the holding company from dissipating the assets of its subsidiaries through management of the company. Since the HCLC has now disposed of a sufficient number of these shares to comply with the standards for removal, none of the companies own any stock in other companies, UN nationals interests are not involved, the companies are not designated (Law No. 207), and, if special accounting companies, their ER&R plans have been approved, there is no longer reason to keep them on the Schedule of Restricted Concerns.

4. Concurrences: None required.

L. A. Randall : 25-6582

R. M. G.
