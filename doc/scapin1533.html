GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091. 31 (19 Feb 47) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 1533)

APO 500

19 February 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Export and Import of Japanese Currency by Crews of Japanese Ships

1. References are the following:

a. Central Liaison Office Memorandum No. 176 (LO 2662-FI/FF), dated 16 January 1947, subject: Application for approval of Export and Import of Japanese Currency Carried by Ships' Crews and Provided for Ships' Supply, which requests that Japanese ships be authorized to carry Japanese Currency for use in foreign ports to meet docking costs and emergency expenses.

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 091.3 (22 Sep 45) ESS, (SCAPIN 44), dated 22 September 1945, subject: Controls over exports and imports of gold, silver, Securities and financial instruments.

2. The application forwarded by reference Central Liaison Office Memorandum is denied.

3. Under no circumstances will special funds in Japanese yen be carried aboard Japanese ships for use in foreign ports to meet docking charges or emergency expenses. All such charges will be referred to the government authorities of the country concerned. Final settlement will be made on an inter-governmental level.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
for /t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD.,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1533)

WKL/TEB/CDY/hg

7 February 1947

1. C. L. O. M. No. 176 (LO 2662-FI/FF), dated 16 January 1947, subject: Application for approval of Export and Import of Japanese Currency Carried by Ships' Crews and Provided for Ships' Supply, requests that Japanese ships engaged in the transport of demobilized personnel and in the shipment of goods be authorized to carry Japanese currency for use in foreign ports to meet pilot charges, wharf dues and emergency expenses.

2. Memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government from SCAP, AG 091. 3 (22 Sep 45) ESS, dated 22 September 1945, subject: Controls over Exports and Imports of Gold, Silver, Securities and Financial Instruments, prohibits the exportation or withdrawal from Japan to any foreign country, or the importation into Japan from any foreign country of gold or silver coin, currency, securities, checks, drafts, bills of exchange, promissory notes, payment instructions, transfer orders, or other financial instruments.

3. It is recommended that application be denied because proposal constitutes a direct violation of SCAP directive referred to in paragraph 2 above. The matter of payment for docking charges and emergency expenses of Japanese ships in foreign ports was coordinated with ESS/ Foreign Trade. This Division stated that all such charges should be referred to government authorities of the country concerned. These governments in turn will forward to SCAP statements of such charges, and final settlement will be made on an inter-governmental level.

4. This matter was also coordinated with COMNAVJAP, Capt. J. D. Leach in charge of operations (Merchant Shipping), who stated that it had been reported to him that in some ports in China, Local government officials have refused to make settlement for wharf and pilot expenses charged Japanese ships, in spite of agreements made between SCAP and the Chinese Government for such settlement. In these instances small personal funds carried by crew members were utilized. Capt. Leach further stated that in view of the fact that COMNAVJAP has no knowledge of the application or the need for it, it should be carefully scrutinized as it might be an attempt to circumvent import and export controls. However, he believed that the carrying of small personal funds by crew members, as presently in practice under COMNAVJAP control, should not be prohibited, in view of difficulties presently being encountered in Chinese ports. This is a memorandum to IJG prohibiting the carrying by Japanese ships of special funds in Japanese yen for use in foreign ports to meet docking charges or emergency expenses.

5. This is an administrative memorandum and does not require the surveillance of the EIGHTH Army.

6. The interests of Korea have been provided for.

7. Concurrences: ESS/FT (S. H. Wright)

W. K. L.
