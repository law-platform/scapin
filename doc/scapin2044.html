GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.8 (13 Sep 49) ESS/IND

(SCAPIN - 2044)

APO 500

13 September 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Rescission of Memorandum for the Japanese Government

1. Reference Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 463.8 (24 Oct-46) ESS/IN, SCAPIN 1291, 24 October 1946, subject: Use of Benzene is rescinded.

2. Reference memorandum is rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ A. J. Rehe
for /t/ R. M. LEVY
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General

Related document原本 →

1. References are:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 463. 8 (24 Oct 46) ESS/IN, SCAPIN 1291, 24 October 1946, subject: Use of Benzene as Motor Fuel.

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 463. 8 (13 Sep 49) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 2044, 13 Sep 49, subject: Rescission of Memorandum for the Japanese Government.

2. Reference la was published at a time when benzene was in critically short supply. This product was urgently needed for the drug, paint, and dyestuffs industries. The shortage was caused partially by the use of large amounts of benzene as a motor fuel.

3. Present supply of benzene to chemical industries is favorable, and no shortage is anticipated. Also, the supply of petroleum products is increasingly more available and at lower costs than those of industrial grades of benzene. In view of the above conditions, enforcement of reference la is no longer required.

4. Concurrence - ESS/IND/RMRB - Mr. A. Chang (verbal).

5. Surveillance by Eighth Army is unnecessary.

W. J. Del Vecchio - 26-8953

W. S. V.
