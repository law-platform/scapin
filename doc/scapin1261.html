GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 440 (11 Oct 46) PH&W

(SCAPIN - 1261)

APO 500

11 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Production and Distribution of Supplies and Equipment for Disease Control Purposes.

1. In the near future certain medical supplies and materials for insect and rodent control purposes will be transferred to the jurisdiction of the Japanese Government, for storage and distribution. These supplies will be received on an import basis and are intended solely to supplement indigenous supplies and equipment to provide minimum requirements for the prevention of disease. In the past supplies of this nature have been distributed directly to Japanese health authorities, by the Occupation Forces, as required for disease control programs. This function will now be assumed by the Japanese Government.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to prepare and submit to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for approval, not later than 25 October, a plan for distribution of subject supplies making provision for the following:

a. Adequate facilities for transportation, warehousing and safeguarding until delivered to the jurisdiction of designated health authorities.

b. Expeditious delivery to each prefectural health officer of sufficient supplies to carry out prescribed health programs.

c. Control of distribution agencies to obviate the possibility of release through unauthorized channels.

d. Establishment and maintenance of adequate reserves to be released only with the concurrence of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

e. Maintenance of sufficient records to provide:

(1) Accurate information on national and regional levels of stocks on hand available for issue.

(2) A basis for computation of future requirements.

(3) A check on the integrity of distribution channels.

3. Direct communication is authorized between the interested Staff Sections of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and agencies of the Imperial Japanese Government concerned to implement all provisions of this Memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

AG 440 (11 Oct 46) PH

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT: Information of General Application pertaining to Memorandum Number (SCAPIN-1261), 11 October 1946, subject: "Production and Distribution of Supplies and Equipment for Disease Control Purposes", to the Imperial Japanese Government.

1. With reference to Memorandum Number (SCAPIN-1261) GHQ, SCAP, to the Imperial Japanese Government, dated 11 October 1946 the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The purpose of the directive mentioned above is to inform the Japanese Government that certain U.S. produced supplies for disease control purposes will be delivered to Japanese agencies under the import program. Supplies include mainly DDT products and medical supplies for venereal disease control. In the past the distribution of such supplies has been handled by Occupation Forces Units It is considered that Japanese agencies have been developed which can now assume this burden.

3. The directive required a distribution plan to be submitted to SCAP not later than 25 October. It is anticipated that this plan will provide for shipment of subject supplies to designated wholesalers who will receive and store the materials. Distribution will be made by these wholesalers only upon request of prefectural health authorities. The detailed plan will, after approval by SCAP, be announced to Occupation Forces Units.

4. Command instructions pertaining to the execution of surveillance required in connection with Memorandum mentioned in paragraph 1 above have been dispatched through command channels. The following is a general statement regarding surveillance to be exercised by Occupation Forces Units.

a. Frequent inspections should be made of wholesale establishments authorized to handle these supplies with a view to determining that adequate storage facilities are available and that the materials are being distributed in accordance with announced policies.

b. Requests submitted by prefectural health authorities should be screened to determine that quantities requested are actually required for established disease control programs.

c. Any irregularities noted should, if possible, be corrected locally; otherwise immediate report to SCAP, through normal command channels, is desired.

5. Before this plan is placed in operation it will be necessary that adequate stocks be furnished Japanese agencies. The effective date will be announced later. In the meantime those supplies will continue to be distributed through Occupation Forces supply channels, in accordance with existing procedures.

R. G. HERSEY

Lt. Col. AGD,

Asst Adj Gen.
