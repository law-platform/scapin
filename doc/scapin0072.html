GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
UNITED STATES ARMED FORCES, PACIFIC

AG 000.7 (30 Sep 45) ESS

(SCAPIN-72)

APO 500

30 Sep 45

SUBJECT :Proposed Press and Radio Release.
TO :Central Liaison Office

1. Reference request by Mr. Uyama, Central Liaison Office, regarding press and radio release on traffic in Tobacco and other commodities.

2. Release has our approval, but we suggest enclosed release be used as it also covers bartering.

/s/ R.C. Kramer
/t/ R.C. KRAMER
Colonel, GSC
Chief, Econ & Sc Sec

2 Incls:

Incl 1- Proposed Released by Japanese

Incl 2- Release Proposed by Economic Section

AttachmentOriginal →

Inclosure 1

Record copy not located. Draft copy is attached. December 1964.

There have lately been an increasing number of cases where Japanese nationals bought cigarettes and other commodities from the Allied soldiers in streets and at railway stations. It is even reported that some persons are reselling these commodities at higher prices. The Allied soldiers are forbidden by the Allied authorities to sell these commodities, and it is impossible for the Japanese Government to tolerate any longer such acts on the part of the Japanese people. It is hereby warned that the Japanese Government with the cooperation of the Allied authorities will take appropriate measures to stop such transactions. Any Japanese who disregard this warning will be severely punished.

For Release to Japanese People:

There has been an increasing number of cases where Japanese nationals have purchased or exchanged cigarettes and other commodities with Allied Forces on the streets and in railway stations. It is reported that some persons are reselling these commodities at even higher prices. Allied Forces are prohibited by the Allied authorities to sell or barter these commodities and it is impossible for the Japanese Government to tolerate any longer such action on the part of the Japanese people. It is hereby warned that the Japanese Government, with the cooperation of the Allied authorities, will take appropriate measures to stop such transactions. Any Japanese who disregard this warning will be severely punished.
