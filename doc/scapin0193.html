GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.4 (26 Oct 45) GS

(SCAPIN - 193)

APO 500

26 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Request Relating to Census.

1. The requests contained in Memorandum C. L. O. No. 251 of 12 October 1945 are not favorably considered.

2. No enlargement of the purposes of the census, facilities for which were requested by Memorandum C. L. O. No.199 of 7 October 1945 (as explained in conference between Mr. Y. Fukunaga of the Imperial Japanese Government and officers of this Headquarters on 17 October 1945), will be permitted, except as directed by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
