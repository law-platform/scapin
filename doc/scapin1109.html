GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (7 Aug 46) LS-A

(SCAPIN-1109)

APO 500

7 August 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Results of War Crimes Trials.

1. Notice is hereby given of the following results of War Crimes Trials as reported by British Authoritiee.

ACCUSED DESIGNATION PLACE OF TRIAL RESULT OF TRIAL

TSURUOKA, Shiroo Cpl., attached Aizawa Coy, Miwa Batt. Singapore Guilty. 18 months Imprisonment.

OGURA, Keiji Lt. Comdr., Paymaster to 14th Naval Garrison. Singapore Guilty. 12 years Imprisonment.

UEDA, Mytshara Capt., OC 14th Naval Garrison. Singapore Guilty. 15 years Imprisonment.

SAKAGAMI, Shigero Lt. Col., OC Ind Arty Bn. Singapore Guilty. 3 years Imprisonment.

SUMI, Toyosaburu Capt., 259th Ind Inf., Bn. Hq. Singapore Guilty. 10 years Imprisonment.

MUNEYUKI, Yasuo Capt., 260 Ind Inf., Bn. #1 Coy. Singapore Guilty. 12 years Imprisonment.

ARAI, Mitsui P.O., 14th Naval Garrison. Singapore Guilty. 10 years Imprisonment.

TORII, Kasuo L/Cpl.t 251 Ind Inf. Bn. Singapore Guilty. 10 years Imprisonment.

NAKAZAWA, Tanakichi L/Cpl., 21st Ind A.A. Bty. Singapore Guilty. 10 years Imprisonment.

ONO, Minoru S/Pvt., 1st Ind FA, Arty., Rear Bn. Singapore Guilty. 10 years Imprisonment.

KOMABA, Kanekichi Civilian, attached to 12th Special Naval Base Force. Singapore Guilty. 10 years Imprisonment.

NAKAMOTO, Hideo Attached Navy, stationed at Port Blair, Andaman Islands on or about 15 Jan 45. Singapore Guilty. Imprisoned for Life.

NONOBE, Yukitaka Attached Navy stationed at 5th F.D. Workshop Army MT Unit at Port Blair, Andaman Islands on or about 15 Jan 45. Singapore Guilty. 15 years Imprisonment.

HORIGUCHI, Yasuo Attached Navy stationed at Port Blair, Andaman Islands on or about 15 Jan 45. Singapore Guilty. 15 years Imprisonment.

TSURUHA, Saneo Sgt./Maj., Kuala Belait, Kempei Tai Singapore Guilty. Imprisoned for Life.

MATSUNAGA, Matao Sgt., Kulal Belait, Kempei Tai. Singapore Guilty. 5 years Imprisonment.

KOJIMA, Hisajiro Attached Navy, stationed at 101 Naval Base Adm. Unit at Nancowry Islands in or about the month of July 45. Singapore Guilty. Imprisoned for Life.

HASEGAWA, Sadeo Sgt./Maj., 37th Army Hq. at Labuan Labuan Guilty. 15 years Imprisonment.

MATSUDA, Osamu S/Pvt., attached Kempei Tai at Labuan. Labuan Guilty. 3 years Imprisonment.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
