GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 680.2 (4 Jan 46) GS

(SCAPIN - 556)

4 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Permission for Certain Japanese to Enter South Korea.

1. Oda Yasume and Ide Isume departed from Seoul, Korea, on 3 January 1946, enroute to Japan in order to repatriate their families and visit the central office of the Japanese Relief Society in Tokyo. The Military Government of Korea, in which they are employed, authorized their travel and desires their return to Korea for further duty after completion of their mission.

2. Permission is hereby granted for Oda Yasume and Ide Isume to return to South Korea.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
