MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
SUBJECT :Provision of Japanese Long Distance Telephone Service for Occupation Force Use

1. Reference:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 311.3 (25 Feb 46)

CCS SCAPIN 773,25 Feb 46, subject as above.

2. Long distance telephone service, at present designated as "AF" Call service furnished through hotels operated by the Ministry of International Trade and Industry shall hereafter be re-designated so as to eliminate the terminology "AF" Call service.

3. The Ministry of Telecommunications is, at its discretion, authorized to:

a. Offer foreign language long distance telephone service, hereinafter called "FL" service, over the switchboard facilities now used for handling AF Calls, to such persons and organizations in addition to the hotels operated by the Ministry of International Trade and Industry as the MOTC may elect. FL service may be provided on a special contract or other suitable basis.

b. Permit the use of the AF switchboard facilities by persons who, because of inability to speak Japanese, are at present unable to use the Japanese toll recording boards.

c. Refer to the AF switchboards calls from public or other telephones where the calling or called parties would be assisted by the English speaking operators at the AF board.

d. Maintain foreign name information telephone directories or to supply Japanese name telephone information service for non-Japanese speaking callers at the AF switchboards.

4. FL service traffic may be handled utilizing the switchboard operators and other local facilities heretofore used for AF call service provided AF calls are not delayed and FL traffic is kept to a level consistent with facilities available. If desired and deemed appropriate, the MOTC may confine FL long distance calls to set time limits to insure equal access to subscribers to this service. Nothing in these instructions will preclude the provision of additional circuit, terminal, and operating facilities at the expense of the MOTC to afford more service to users of FL Service.

5. Protective practices and methods for filing FL calls will be established to provide assurance that no FL calls will be billed to the Occupation Forces and that no portion of the circuits utilized in completing FL calls are my sequestered toll lines.

6. Direct correspondence between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Telecommunications concerning technical and administrative matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorised.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K. B. BUSH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
