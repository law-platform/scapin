GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.93 (1 Dec 45) GC-P

(SCAPIN - 376)

1 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Plan of Radio Weather Messages to Be Issued from the Government Central Meteorological Observatory (G.C.M.O.)

1. Reference C. L. O. letter #531(3), dated 10 November 1945.

Your attention is called to SCAP memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government 000.93 (19 Nov 45) GC-P, subject: "Operation of Japanese Weather Service."

2. Radio frequencies requested have not been given clearance by all governments concerned. Assignment of radio frequencies for meteorological broadcast will be covered by separate correspondence.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
