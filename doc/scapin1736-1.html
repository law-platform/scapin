GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 464.6 (2 Oct 47) ESS/FT

(SCAPIN-1736/1)

APO 500

2 October 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Angaur Phosphate Project

1. Reference is made to paragraph 3 of Memorandum for Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 464.6 (21 June 47) ESS/FT (SCAPIN 1736) dated 21 June 1947, sudject: Angaur Phosphate Project.

2. The following additional instructions will be complied with.

a. The Japanese Government will immediately increase the Japanese Police Force at Angaur from five (5) to ten (10) trained Police.

b. A new survey of the mining area will be made immediately which will positively eliminate any encroachment upon native agricultural areas or essential water shed. New survey monuments will be installed in concrete or other permanent material to clearly define all corners of the mining area. Suitable intermediate markers will be established to materialize the acutal boundary line on the ground and clearly outline the entire perimeter of the mining area.

c. A new site plan will be developed after the field work has been completed, showing new mining perimeters and other installations presently at Angaur, including housing, utilities, roads, drying, conveying, and mining installations. Ten copies of this site plan will be presented to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and other agencies for their use.

d. Arrange a continual flow of, (1) barter goods to Angaur to be used in obtaining fresh fish, vegetables and fruit under the supervision of the local Military Government and Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers Liaison Officer, and (2) such fresh foods as are required.

e. Deliver to Japanese Angaur Superintendent by the Koyei-Maru sufficient fishing boats and gear so that the Japanese workmen can augment the native fish supply.

f. Deliver to the Japanese Angaur Superintendent sufficient blended fertilizer to insure maximum yield of food crops on areas designated for Japanese use by Angaur Native Chief.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1736/1)

FEP/HLM/AJG/HAS/pf

11 September 1947

1. Reference is made to Staff Study ''Angaur Island Rock Phosphate Project", dated 28 July 47. On 11 July, Col. R. J. Butchers, Maj. H. A. Schettler, and Lt. Col. Harry A. Huncilman proceeded to Angaur to investigate the rock phosphate project with the view to determine the true situation. After reviewing the project on the ground, it was concluded that paragraph 3 of SCAPIN 1736, dated 21 June 1947 to the Japanese Govt, subj: "Angaur Rock Phosphate Project" should be amended to include the following:

a. Increase the police force from five to ten police.

b. A new survey of the mining area should be immediately completed.

c. A new site plan to be developed.

d. Arrange continual flow of barter goods.

e. Augment the fishing supplies and equipment at Angaur to insure maximum catch.

f. Deliver sufficient blended fertilizer to obtain maximum vegetable production.

g. Supply larger quantities of fresh vegetables to the Japanese workers at Angaur.

2. These additional instructions are essential to insure that no incident or circumstance inimical to the occupation will arise.

3. Verbal instructions were issued to Mr. Tsugi, Head of the Fertilizer Section of the Fertilizer Food Kodan on 7 August paralleling this action and he assured Foreign Trade that all stipulations outlined in the amendment to SCAPIN 1736 would be immediately fulfilled.

4. This problem as it relates to Korea has been considered.

5. No concurrences necessary.

F. E. P.
