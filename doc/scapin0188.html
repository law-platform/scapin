GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (25 Oct 45) GA

(SCAPIN - 188)

APO 500

25 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Misconduct Among Occupation Troops.

1. Reference C. L. O. No.1 dated 11 September 1945 and YLO No. 44 and 46 dated 14 September 1945, investigation failed to disclose the identity of personnel guilty of alleged offenses.

2. Investigation of all cases of misconduct will be facilitated by following action directed by our letter AG000.5 (3 Oct 45) GA, dated 3 October 1945, subject, "Misconduct Among Occupation Troops."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
