SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

16 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Smallpox Vaccination of Japanese Civilians.

1. The increasing prevalence of smallpox among Japanese civilians requires more concerted control action than is now being taken by responsible Japanese public health officials.

2. It is desired that the Japanese government immediately institute smallpox control measures as follows:

a. Vaccination of all Japanese civilians employed by occupational forces in any capacity, including all civilian personnel in hotels, rest centers and any other place operated for Allied Military Forccs.

b. Vaccination of civilians as required for the immediate and effective control in all areas where smallpox has been reported or may be reported in the future.

c. Re-instatement of the pre-war compulsory vaccination program throughout Japan.

d. Immediate development of facilities required for the production of an adequate supply of smallpox vaccine to carry out and continue the foregoing as required for the eradication of smallpox in Japan.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
