GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (16 Nov 45) CIS

(SCAPIN-285)

16 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Certain Japanese Army Officers.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will apprehend the following Japanese Army Officers who are wanted as war criminals.

Major General Masataka Kaburagi, formerly Chief of Staff, 34th Army, presently Chief of Staff, 55th Army (Shikoku).

Captain Naoto Koike, formerly attached officer of Staff Office, 34th Army (Hankow), presently demobilized in Japan.

2. The above officers will be delivered to the Commanding General, XI Corps, at the XI Corpse Stockade, for incarceration at Omori Prison.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst. Adjutant General.
