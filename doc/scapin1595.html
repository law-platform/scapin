GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (3 Apr 47) GC

(SCAPIN - 1595)

APO 500

3 April 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Quarantine Procedures for Repatriates from Cholera Ports.

1．Reference is made to Memorandum from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 370.05 (7 May 46)GC, (SCAPIN 927), dated 7 May 1946, subject: Repatriation, as amended.

2. Rangoon, Burma, is designated a "cholera port."

3. The British ship Dilwara carrying approximately 1,632 Japanese repatriates is scheduled to arrive at Kure on or about 6 April 1947.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government will apply the quarantine and other procedures prescribed in paragraph 3, Annex V. of reference paragraph 1 above, for repatriated arriving in Japan on shipping from Rangoon, including Japanese repatriated aboard the ship Dilwara upon its arrival at Kure.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
