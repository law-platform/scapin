OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123 (27 Sep 45) ESS

(SCAPIN - 67)

APO 500

27 September 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Funds that may be Brought into Japan by Repatriated Japanese

1. This headquarters has been informed that the Japanese Navy contemplates making payment outside of Japan of discharge allowance to its personnel.

2. Your attention is invited to the fact that the Japanese Navy contemplates making payments outside of Japan of discharge allowances to its personnel.

a. Commissioned Officers - a maximum of 500 yen;

b. Non-commissioned Officers and enlisted men - a maximum of 200 yen.

All other currencies, and all yen currency in excess of the limits set forth above, will be taken up and delivered to this Headquarters.

3. Civilians will be permitted to carry with them into Japan a maximum of 1,000 yen per person. All other currencies, and all yen currencies in excess of this list will be taken up and delivered to this Headquarters.

4. Civilians and military personnel will also be permitted to carry with them their clothing and personal possessions of value only to the owner.

All other property, including securities, financial instruments, and jewelry will be taken up.

/s/ R.C. Kramer
/t/ R.C. KRAMER
Colonel, G.S.C.,
Chief, Economic and Scientific Section
