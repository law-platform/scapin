GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (21 May 48) CPC/CD

(SCAPIN - 1875/1)

APO 500

21 May 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Amendment to SCAPIN 1875, 1 April 1948

1. Reference i3 made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (1 Apr 48)CPC/CD, SCAPIN 1875, 1 April 1948, subject, "Gold, Silver, and Platinum Family Metals for Domestic Consumption in the Second Quarter of 1948." from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2, Allocations of precious metals for use in maintaining transportation facilities are approved, as detailed. The metals approved for release by this amendment will be allocated and used in implementing programs approved by the General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. Those metals arc authorized for use from the date of publication of this memorandum to 30 September 1948.

PROCESSORS

Users listed above are authorized to have metals allocated to them, processed by the indicated companies:

1/ Tokuriki Honten K.K.

2/ Hashimoto Honten K.K.

3/ Ishifuku Kinzoku Kogyo K.K.

3. Precious metals will be taken from stocks in the possession of the Japanese Government, Reports, as required by reference 1 above will be submitted.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
