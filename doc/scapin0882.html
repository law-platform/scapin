GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004 (18 Apr 46) ESS/IN

(SCAPIN - 882)

APO 500

18 April 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Applications for the Conversion of Military Arsenals and Laboratories

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. No. 1021 (1.1) dated 4 March 1948 same subject as above.

2. Applications submitted directly to the Occupation Forces from private individuals, for conversion or removal of machinery and equipment from arsenals or laboratories taken into custody and control by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers will be returned for submission through the Japanese Government.

3. Applications submitted through the Japanese Government will be considered favorably only when accompanying evidence demonstrates that proposed conversion or removal is indispensable for the restoration of a minimum civilian economy.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ J. W. Mann
for /t/ B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 882)

HIMcG/mdb

18 April 1946

1. Reference is made to letter from the Central Liaison Office of the Japanese Government, No. C.L.O. 1021 (1.1), dated 4 March 1946. Letter requests that any application received by GHQ or Hq 8th Army, from private sources for conversion or removal of machinery now in military installations under custody and control of the SCAP, be referred to the Japanese Government for investigation and recommendation. The letter also states that henceforward applications submitted to the Occupation Forces by the Japanese Government will be limited only to those which are deemed indispensable for the restoration of minimum civilian economy of Japan.

2. Establishment of a procedure requiring routing of such applications through the Japanese Government is desirable for reasons given below:

a. The volume of applications now being received is large and this procedure will result in screening out many of them.

b. The requirement imposed on the Japanese Government in justifying applications submitted will result in presentation of sufficient data so that action may be taken with minimum amount of investigation by our own troops.

c. Implementing action has already been taken by Eighth Army insofar as its own responsibility is concerned by issuance of Operational Directive No. 34 (attached as Inclosure 2).

3. Completed action consists of:

a. Memo to I.J.G. which states that applications to the Occupation Forces from private sources for conversion or removal of machinery in military installations will be returned for resubmission through the Japanese Government.

b. Letter to CG, Eighth Army, notifying him of issuance of the memo to I.J.G. and approving action already taken by his Operational Directive Number 34.

4. Concurrence: G-4 (Lt. Col. Nelson)

5. No surveillance is required.

J. A. O'H.
