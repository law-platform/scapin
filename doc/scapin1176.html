GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.112 (4 Sep 46) GS

(SCAPIN-1176)

APO 500

4 September 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Temporary Retention of Public Official under SCAPIN 550 - Kogoro UEMURA.

1. Reference is made to CLO 3892 (PP), dated 8 August 1946, subject: Retention of Kogoro UEMURA.

2. The request contained in reference CLO for the retention in office of Kogoro UEMURA until the end of March 1947 is disapproved.

3. It is directed that, within fifteen days from date of this Memorandum, Kogoro UEMURA be removed from public office and excluded from government service as an undesirable person under the terms of Category G, Appendix A of SCAPIN 550.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
