GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.33 (1 Nov 45) ESS-IN

(SCAPIN- 230)

3 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Shipment of 150,000 Sheets Silkworm Eggs to Korea.

1. It is directed that the necessary action be taken at once to effect the shipment to Korea of 150,000 sheets silkworm eggs.

2. The shipment will be consigned to the Commanding General, United States Forces in Korea.

3. The assembly, crating and shipping arrangements are to be completed at the pert of shipment as soon as practicable. This headquarters is to be advised when all arrangements have beer completed.

4. Acknowledgment is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst, Adjutant General.
