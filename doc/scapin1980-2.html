GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.3 (8 Mar 49) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1980/2)

APO 500

11 July 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Equalization of Radiotelegraph Rates.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.3 (8 Mar 49)CCS, SCAPIN 1980, dated 8 March 1949, subject: Equalization erf Radiotelegraph Rates, as amended.

2. Effective 1 August 1949, so much of page 6 of Inclosure 1 to SCAPIN 1980 as pertains to rate for Ordinary Press messages from Japan to Zone 5, United States, Other offices, is amended to read as follows:

FROM: Japan, Tokyo only

TO: United States, Other offices

Press Through Rate Japan Terminal Japan Radio Outpayment Other Line Share

Zone #5

New York * $0.105 - $0.0425 $0.0425 $0.02

Washington DC .115 - .0425 .0425 .03

Other Places .125 - .0425 .0425 .04

FROM: Japan, Other offices

TO: United States, Other offices

Press

Zone #5

New York * $0.11 $0.005 $0.0425 $0.0425 $0.02

Washington DC .12 .005 .0425 .0425 .03

Other Places .13 .005 .0425 .0425 .04

*Includes Lake Success, N. Y.

3. Direct communication between Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Telecommunications concerning matters within the scope of this Memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
