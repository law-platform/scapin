GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 470.8 (10 Nov 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 264)

10 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Remodeling Japanese Army Tanks

1. Reference is made to letter from First Demobilization Headquarters Liaison Commission, Yokohama file K-120 dated October 29, 1945.

2. Tne request to modify 115 tanks for use as bulldozers and 50 armored cars for tractors as stated in the reference letter, is approved.

3. Before these tanks (tanks to be Model 97 or Model 97 improved Medium Tanks only) and armored cars are placed into operation as bulldozers and tractors the following action will be taken by you to render those items useless as combat vehicles:

a. All armament wiil be removed and destroyed.

b. All fire control equipment and inter-communication equipment will be removed and destroyed.

c. Turrets will be removed and the armor plate of the hull will be cut away as far as can be done without destroying the usefulness of the vehicle for its new purpose. In any case enough mutilation of the hull will be accomplished to make it impossible to replace the turret.

4. Spare parts will be obtained from stocks on hand only. The Commanding General Eighsh U.S. Army will be furnished estimates as to the spare parts needed for two years operation.

5. The Commanding General Eighsh U.S. Army has been charged with the supervision of this project. Direct arrangements will be made with him for the accomplishment of the modifications and clearance will be obtained from him before any of the converted vehicles are put into operation.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
