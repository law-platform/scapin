GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 370.05 (26 Apr 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 902)

APO 500

26 April 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Monies of Deceased Japanese Military Personnel.

1. Reference is made to CLO memorandum No. 1582 (RG) dated

5 April 1946, subject: "Monies of Deceased Japanese Military Personnel."

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is authorized to pay Japanese yen currency of deceased Japanese military personnel, taken up from repatriating nationals at ports of debarkation by Japanese customs officials, to the families of the deceased personnel residing in Japan. The request for the payment of military scripts is disapproved.

e. Yen payments to the families of the deceased personnel will be mado within the limitations prescribed in paragraph 2a (1) of Annex VI of Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supremo Conmandor for the Allied Powers, file AG 370.05 (16 Mar 46) GC (SCAPIN 822) dated 16 March 1946, subject: "Repatriation."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General,AGO,
Adjutant General.
