11 July 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Transfer of Proceeds of Imported Petroleum Products to the Counterpart Fund.

1. References:

a. Ministry of International Trade and Industry L. O. No. 4 to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, 7 June 1949, subject: Transfer of Proceeds of Imported Petroleum Products to the Counterpart Fund.

b. Ministry of Foreign Affairs F.O.M. No. 52 (TIM) to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, 8 June 1949, subject: Transfer of Proceeds of Imported Petroleum Products to the Counterpart Fund.

2. With regard to the request contained in paragraph 3 of letter referred to in paragraph 1a above, the following instructions apply:

a. The Japanese Government will be furnished an estimate of proposed imports of petroleum products considered necessary to sustain the approved economic programs. As requested, this estimate will be furnished quarterly for the quarter of year commencing with January, April, June and September of each year. It is expressly stipulated that the information as to quantity and type of any petroleum product included in this quarterly estimate is furnished to the Japanese Government for planning purposes only, and is subject to change without notice at the discretion of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

b. It is not considered necessary to issue to the Japanese Government a delivery order for each specific delivery of petroleum products. However, arrangements have been made for advance notice on individual imports of petroleum products to be furnished by the Sub-Area Petroleum Officer, Japan, Headquarters, Eighth Army, or his representative at ports of discharge to the Japanese Government representative at the port of discharge. This advance notice will specify name of the vessel, expected time of arrival, type and quantity of products aboard, and port of discharge. Further, the Japanese Government representative at port of discharge will be advised of any changes in advance notices subsequent to their issuance. It must be clearly understood that this advance notice does not constitute authority for representatives of the Japanese Government to make any arrangements for discharge of cargoes with the ship’s captain or chief mate. Such arrangements will be made with the appropriate United States Army representative at each port of discharge.

c. The request that the Japanese Government be furnished a copy of the ship’s manifest or similar document has been evaluated and is approved in principle. The Japanese Government will be furnished, within ten (10) days after completion of discharging cargo, an authenticated copy of a “shipping document” covering the products discharged from each individual vessel. The appropriate agency of the Japanese Government will contact the Sub-Area Petroleum Officer, Japan, Headquarters, Eighth Army, for coordination of this matter.

d. It is considered that the procedure outlined above will preclude the necessity for the submission of any claim as distinguished from adjustment. It is recognized that, for cogent reasons, adjustments will have to be made between the records of the Japanese Government and those of the United States Government. It is expressly stipulated that any adjustment of records will normally be limited to changes in nomenclature only. Any reconciliation of records must be accomplished between the Japanese Government and the Sub-Area Petroleum Officer, Japan, Headquarters, Eighth Army. In the event of failure to arrive at a mutual reconciliation, the decision of the Sub-Area Petroleum Officer, Japan, will be final.

3. With reference to paragraph 3e of reference 1a, under present pricing criteria as established by the Japanese Government Price Board, allowance is made to cover landing costs of petroleum products. Therefore, the International Trade Bureau of the Japanese Government will be responsible for payment of all expenses incurred in landing imported petroleum products consigned to the Japanese civil economy.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
