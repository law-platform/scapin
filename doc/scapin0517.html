GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.3 (30 Dec 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 517)

APO 500

30 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Provision of Coal at Kyushu Ports for British Government.

1. Reference is made to letter this Headquarters, dated 10 November 1945, file AG 463.3 (10 Nov 45)GD, subject: "Provision of Coal at Kyushu Ports for British Government", directing action to insure the shipment of 18,000 tons of coal monthly from Kyushu ports to Hong Kong, movement to be in British controlled ships as nominated from time to time by this Headquarters.

2. Vessels nominated will be sailed to Port Miike for loading pursuant to advice contained in your letter to this Headquarters, dated 23 November 1945, file C. L. O. Number 702 (2.2), subject as above.

3. Vessels nomination the sixth and seventh liftings are as follows:

Capacity Draft ETA Miike

S.S. SA▲LA▲U 8,000 tons 26-1/2 ft. 15 Jan. 1946

S. S. S▲▲ESK 8,000 tons 26-1/2 ft. 28 Jan. 1946

Each vessel will be loaded with 8,000 tons of coal.

4. Method f reporting deliveries will be as previously directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
