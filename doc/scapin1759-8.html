(SCAPIN - 1759/8)

APO 500

9 May 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service Via the United States.

1. Reference is made to memoranda for the Japanese Government as follows:

a. File AG 311.23 (10 Aug 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1759, dated 10 August 1947, subject: Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service Via the United States, as amended.

b. File AG 311.22 (29 Sep 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1786, dated 29 September 1947, subject: Provision of Payment for International Messages Filed by Boeki Cho (Japanese Board of Trade).

c. File AG 311.23 (12 Nov 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1818, dated 12 November 1947, subject: Expansion of Radiotelegraph Prepaid Reply (RP) Service, as amended.

2. Effective 10 May 1949 the Japanese Government is authorized to expand radiotelegraph service via the United States to include private radiotelegrams between Japan and the points listed in inclosure 1.

3. All the provisions of SCAPINS 1759, as amended, 1786, and 1818, as amended, pertaining to the acceptance and handling of all classes of radiotelegrams, settlement of accounts and payment of revenue accruing to the Ministry of Communications and the American carriers will apply in this instance.

4. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Communications, concerning matters within the scope of this memorandum, is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
