APO 500

11 May 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Separation Allowances for Government Employees

1. The Japanese Government is directed to take the necessary measures to establish standard allowances to be paid to Government employees separated from Government service consistent with the amount of funds provided for such allowances in the consolidated balanced budget adopted for the fiscal year 1949-50 in accordance with the United States Government Interim Directive on economic stabilization.

2. Pending establishment of standard allowances in conformity with the requirements of paragraph 1 above:

a. No payments of allowances to separated government employees of any class will be made upon the authority of the Law Concerning Appointment, Removal, etc. of Government Officials Before Application of the Provisions of National Public Service Law Shall Be Made, Law No. 121 of 1947, or the authority of any other statutory ratification of the standards for such allowances set by the Cabinet decision of 29 March 1947; and

b. No payments of allowances to separated employees of any class will be made under Law No. 167 of 1947 to employees who are eligible for allowances under the Pension Law, Law No. 48 of 1923, as amended, the National Public Service Mutual Aid Association Law, Law No. 69 of 1948, or the Unemployment Insurance Law, Law No. 146 of 1947.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2003)

WKL/EMR/JWH/eb

19 April 1949

1. The consolidated balanced budget for Fiscal Year 1949-50 as presented by the Cabinet and as approved by GHQ provides for general reduction in Government employment and includes funds for payment of termination allowances to separated employees equal on the average to three months base salary and allowances.

2. Concurrently with the above action in connection with the budget the Cabinet on 18 March 1949 decided that the separation allowances should be paid according to the provisions of Article 4 of the regulation issued under Cabinet determination of 29 March 1947 provided however that the minimum allowance should b6 four months base salary and allowance.

3. The Cabinet decision of March 18 is inconsistent with the decision of the Cabinet which approved the budget and will result in a budget deficiency.

4. It is essential that this conflict be resolved and furthermore, that separation allowance should be restricted until this conflict is resolved. Separation allowances are also considered to be a matter of a character that should be stipulated by law in accordance with Law No. 72 of 1947.

5. Concurrence by Government Section.

Mr. Harrison, 57-8417

W. K. L.
