GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004 (26 Jun 46) ESS/AC

(SCAPIN-1037)

APO 500

26 June 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Dissolution of Japan Lumber Company Ltd., and Local Lumber Companies.

1. In order to obtain a substantial increase in the production and distribution of available timber lumber and wood products, and to stimulate revival of construction of all types of dwellings, factories and buildings during the present emergency, the Imperial Japanese Government is hereby directed to take the necessary action to:

a. Dissolve the Japan Lumber Company, Ltd., and the Local Lumber Companies; repeal or rescind all laws and regulations giving them legal existence; and preserve complete records of such companies within the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry of the Imperial Japanese Government.

b. Provide for formation of voluntary democratic associations, subject to Government supervision, which will be open to all loggers, saw mill and other woodworking industry operators and owners, and forest owners, on local, prefectural and national levels; and create from such associations an advisory body designed to work with and assist the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry and the Economic Stabilization Board.

c. Integrate into the newly organized Economic Stabilization Board the functions of allocating all materials and planned production. distribution, appropriate rationing and price control for all wood using, processing and producing industries; and provide that the Economic Stabilization Board shall consult with the advisory body specified in subparagraph b, above, on all matters outlined in this subparagraph.

d. Take the necessary and appropriate steps to effectuate; allocation of materials, planned production and distribution, rationing and price control, as specified in subparagraph c, above by transmittal of proper orders, plans decisions or determinations of the Economic Stabilization Board, to the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry, which is hereby charged with responsibility for carrying out and effectuating said orders, plans, decisions or determinations by any additional means deemed advisable, which are not inconsistent with policies of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

e. Provide for the execution by the above specified associations, under supervision of proper governmental agencies, for a period not to exceed one (1) year from date of this Memorandum, of the functions conferred upon them with respect to rationing and price control and allocating of critical materials to all persons and businesses affected thereby, with proper provisions for appeal to the supervising Governmental agency.

f. Liquidate within six (6) months, from the date of this Memorandum, the assets of the Japan Lumber Company, Ltd., and Local Lumber Companies in a manner which will contribute most effectively to increased production of lumber and wood products and to promotion of a more diversified ownership of the means of production, provided, however, that all sales hereunder shall be in accordance with the existing price control regulations.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to put into operation the program outlined in subparagraphs 1a through e, above, within thirty (30) days of the date of this Memorandum and shall furnish a report of action taken, in quintuplicate, typed in English on 8" x 11" paper, to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, within fifteen (15) days thereafter.

3. Direct communication between agencies of the Imperial Japanese Government and interested Staff Sections of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, is authorized to implement this Memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1037)

WFM/JMcIH/GFS/kf

20 June 1946

1. The Japan Lumber Company, Ltd., a joint stock company, was created and is controlled by the Government, but is a privately owned and operated company which enjoys a monopoly in the sale and distribution of timber and lumber. It was created by the Lumber Control Law of 1941 (Imperial Law 66, 12 March 1941).

2. The Japan Lumber Company, Ltd., has authorized capitalization of ¥50,000,000 fully paid up. The principal shareholders include:

Oji Paper Manufacturing Company, Ltd.

Imperial Household

Mitsui Honsha

Mitsubishi Honsha

Nissan Company, Ltd.

Sumitomo Honsha

Kawasaki Heavy Industries Company, Ltd.

Local Lumber Companies

3. It owns extensive blocks of stock in the Local Lumber Companies and advances money and handles the payment of subsidies throughout the industry.

4. Under the Lumber Control Law, the Japan Lumber Company, Ltd., has the following functions and powers of distribution:

a. It is the exclusive distributor of all lumber that crosses prefectural lines.

b. It directs Local Lumber Companies in the production and distribution of lumber within each prefecture.

c. It sells timber to Local Lumber Companies.

d. It purchases all imported timber.

e. It carries out instructions from the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry relative to lumber control and distribution.

5. The Local Lumber Companies, also joint stock companies, were established under the Lumber Control Law, and are the counterpart of the Japan Lumber Company, Ltd., for all lumber business handled within a single prefecture. Regulations provide that they shall:

a. Be the sole buyers of timber products produced in or shipped into their prefecture.

b. Be the exclusive distributors of timber products within the prefecture.

c. Follow directions given them by the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry, the Prefectural Governor or the Japan Lumber Company, on production and distribution.

d. Trade only with the Japan Lumber Company, Ltd., with respect to timber shipped in and out of the prefecture, except for procurement needs of the Army and Navy.

e. Direct the resale of used lumber.

f. Submit quarterly reports to the Japan Lumber Company, Ltd.

6. The Forestry Division of the Natural Resources Section has investigated the status of the entire forestry industry. It has determined that abolition of this private monopolistic control of the lumber industry and granting of necessary control functions to the Government, conducted with the aid of voluntary cooperative associations (which are already in existence in this industry, but have been suppressed), will carry out the SCAP objectives.

7. The attached Memorandum is based upon and in accordance with the policies of SCAP Directive No.3, dated 22 September 1945.

8. The interests of USAFIK have been considered. This action does not affect the Korean area.

9. This is considered an administrative matter.

10. Concurrences: Legal Division, ESS (Lt. Col. E. R. Minnich); Government Section (Col. H. E. Robison); Forestry Division, NRS (Mr. W. S. Swingler); Price Control and Rationing Division, ESS (Capt. W. S. Egekvist); Industrial Division, ESS (Mr. G. T. Walker); Engineer Office (Maj. Gen. H. J. Casey).

J. McI. H.
