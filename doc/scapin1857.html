SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 003 (12 Feb 48) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 1857)

APO 500

12 February 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :New Design Five Sen Note

1. Reference is C.L.O. No. 263 (LO 16/FI/TR) 16 January 1948 forwarding Ministry of Finance Memorandum LO 16 (FI/TR) 12 January 1948, subject: Application for the Approval of the Printing of Bank of Japan 5 Sen Notes.

2. Approval of the new design 5 sen note is granted.

3. Authority is given to print ¥20,000,000 new design 5 sen notes during the calendar year 1948.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. M. Levy
/t/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1857)

4 February 1948

1. Bank of Japan reserves of 5 sen currency notes were exhausted at the end of November 1947. Reserves of 5 sen coins at the same date were ¥4,317,000 and have declined further since that time. There is no flowback of the notes and the coins are hoarded or melted down for various uses. Ration prices are often expressed in units of 5 sen, hence this circulation medium is essential.

2. A new design 5 sen note has been prepared and approved by CI & E as containing no objectionable symbolism.

3. It is intended to print 400,000,000 pieces, totaling ¥20,000,000 during the year 1948.

4. The interests of Korea are not concerned.

5. Concurrences: CI&E - R/D (G.A. Warp) Verbal
