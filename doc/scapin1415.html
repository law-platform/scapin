GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 387.6 (20 Dec 46) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1415)

APO 500

20 December 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Protection by Imperial Japanese Government of War Indemnity Insurance claims and War Indemnity Insurance funds on deposit in banking institutions in the name of German individuals, partnerships, corporations, associations, or non-profit organizations, as exempted from taxation in accordance with provisions of Chapter II, without the filing of a return declaration as required by the provisions of Chapter III of the War Indemnity Special Measures Bill promulgated by the Imperial Japanese Government on 22 September 1946.

1. With reference to War Indemnity Special Measures Bill promulgated on 22 September 1946, the Imperial Japanese Government is directed to exempt all German individuals, partnerships, corporations, associations or non-profit organizations from the penalties as prescribed in Chapter IIX of the Bill for non-compliance with the provisions of Chapter III thereof requiring the filing of a declaration return on or before 14 December 1946.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is further directed to protect to the extent of the exemptions as set forth in Chapter II of the Bill all War Indemnity Insurance funds on deposit in banking institutions and War Indemnity Insurance claims in the name of German individuals, partnerships, corporations, associations or non-profit organizations. The Imperial Japanese Government will have the sole responsibility for having the specified exemptions made. Failure on the part of any German or representative thereof to make the reports or give satisfactory information will not be construed as relieving the Imperial Japanese government of any part of its responsibility.

3. It is further directed that the exempted amount of any German War Indemnity Insurance claim which has not been paid be deposited in a blocked bank account to the credit of the German policy holder.

4. A detailed report shall be submitted in duplicate to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, not later than 14 February 1947, giving the name of each account containing funds representing War Indemnity Insurance, the total amount thereof, the amount of taxation theron and balance remaining in the account.

5. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to order all banking institutions which have on deposit German accounts representing War Indemnity Insurance funds to notify each depositor of the amount of taxes paid and the balance remaining in the account.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
