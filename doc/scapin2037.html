SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 414.3 (16 Jul 49) ESS/FTC

(SCAPIN-2037)

APO 500

11 August 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Release and Sale of Pearls, Natural and Cultured and Articles Containing Pearls.

1. Reference the following memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. AG 386.7 (21 Sep 48) ESS/FTC, SCAPIN 1935, 21 September 1948, subject: Release and Sale of Pearls, Natural and Cultured and Articles Containing Pearls.

b. AG 386.7 (21 Sep 48) ESS/FTC, SCAPIN 1935/1, 29 October 1948, subject: Release and Sale of Pearls, Natural and Cultured and Articles Containing Pearls.

2. The referenced memoranda are rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

Related document原本 →

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2037)

FEP/ABS/RHF/ky

11 Aug 49

1. SCAPIN 1935 and 1935/1, restricting domestic transactions of pearls and pearl articles, were issued to assure the supply of quality pearls to CPO to meet the demands of the occupation personnel.

2. The approval of the Chief of Staff on 22 July 1949, on G-4 Staff Study dated 2 July 49 subject: Disposition of Pearls obtained from Japan Cultured Pearl Company. To have CPO retain quantities of "certain pearls" now on hand will assure the supply of quality pearls to meet occupation forces requirements for at least 18 months.

3. The decontrolling of pearls is in line with the policy of the Committee on Relaxation of Controls.

4. Concurrences: CPO; G-4

F. E. P.

R. Furudera: 26-6476 ADDENDUM: Memo to the Board of Trade, Tokyo from FTCD, ESS, GHQ, 410. 2 (5 Oct 48) ESS/FTC, (BT 48-2875) dtd 5 Oct 1948, subject: Export Procedure for the Sale of Pearls will also be rescinded. This memo established CPO's 10% option on export pearls.
