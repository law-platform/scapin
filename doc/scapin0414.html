APO 500

10 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Request of Schmidt Shoten, Ltd., for Release of Funds

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. Memorandum No. 572 (2) dated 14 November 1945, together with letter from Schmidt Shoten, Ltd., dated 10 November 1945, requesting permission to operate its plant and release of blocked funds,

2. Attention is directed to Memorandum for Imperial Japanese Government, AG 091 (24 Oct 45) ESS/FI, subject: "Application of German Nationals for Release of Property and Other Assets".

3. Application for resumption of operations should be re-submitted in accordance with provisions of that memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
