GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 121.6 (31 Mar 48) GD

(SCAPIN - 1872/5)

APO 500

23 November 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Disbursement of Transportation Expenses of Laborers Directly Employed for the Occupation Forces.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Central Liaison and Coordination Office Letter No. 3766 (LO 4114 AD/IF), 5 November 1948.

b. Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers Memorandum for Japanese Government, file AG 400.12 (31 Mar 48)CE-GD, SCAPIN 1872.

2. It is the responsibility of the Japanese Government to deliver procured labor to the site of employment or to an assembly point if designated by the procurement demand. This responsibility is identical to the responsibility of the Japanese Government for the delivery to the designated point of use or storage other procured requirements of the Occupation Forces; consequently Procurement Demands covering the transport of Japanese workers to and from work locations, or assembly points if designated, will not be issued.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
