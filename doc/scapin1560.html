GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.7 (6 Mar 47) GD

(SCAPIN - 1560)

APO 500

6 March 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Importation of Petroleum Products for Imperial Japanese Government, April Through June 1947.

1. Arrangements have been made for the sale of imported petroleum products to the Imperial Japanese Government through the Petroleum Distributing Company (or its legally appointed successor) in monthly quantities and at the delivery points indicated herein:

Unit is 42 gallon barrel April May June

a. Navy Special Fuel Oil

(1) Tsurumi 148000 138000 138000

(2) Sasebo 117000 113000 113000

285000 251000 251000

b. Navy 702 Diesel

(1) Tsurumi 34700 87700 87700

(2) Sasebo 6300 6300 6300

41000 94000 94000

(Direct discharge from tanker at Tsurumi desired)

c. Range Fuel

(l) Tsurumi 130000 130000 130000

d. Kerosene 25000 25000 25000

(One time delivery of 75000 bbl in April is authorized and desired.)

2. a. The Imperial Japanese Government shall issue such instructions as may be required to permit delivery of petroleum products delivered in bulk by ocean tanker direct from such tankers to land based, or floating storage as the case may be.

b. The Imperial Japanese Government, through the Petroleum Distribution Company as agent for the Board of Trade, is authorized to accept a quantity of any one item greater than that specified above for delivery in any one month provided that the total for the

three months indicated is not exceeded. This authorization is to provide for contingencies that might arise such as direct discharge from ocean tanker to Petroleum Distributing Company storage.

c. Current instructions relative to accounting procedure are continued in force.

3. The Board of Trade (Boeki Cho), as agent for the Imperial Japanese Government, shall be responsible for insuring that timely and adequate notice of intent to accept delivery is furnished appropriate United States Army and/or Navy commands responsible for delivery of petroleum products to the Imperial Japanese Government. The Commander, Service Division 32. United States Navy, is responsible for delivery of Navy fuels and marine lubricants and the Commanding General, Eighth United States Army, is responsible for delivery of all other petroleum products. The Board of Trade shall be responsible that only duly accredited agents shall receipt for deliveries accepted, and that such agents present their credentials upon demand by any authorized representative of the Occupation Forces.

4. The quantities set forth in this directive do not include any portion of the 85000 barrels of Navy Special Fuel Oil requested by the Imperial Japanese Government for use in the production of steel.

5. All previously published directives in conflict with the instructions contained herein are rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
