27 January 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
Subject :Operation of Internal Airline Within Japan

1. Reference Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 004 (26 Jun 50) FIB, SCAPIN 2106, 26 June 1950, subject: Operation of Internal Airline within Japan.

2. Paragraph 2, subparagraph g, is amended as follows:

“g. Subject to approval by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, the licensee may enter into contracts for the transport of mail, freight and passengers within Japan. Such contracts may be expressed in, and licensee may receive in payment therefor, currencies other than Japanese yen, subject to such regulations respecting such currencies as may be applicable.”

3. Paragraph 3 is added as follows:

“3. In addition to the foregoing, the Japanese Government may license any Japanese controlled juridical person to engage in business activities involving internal airline transportation provided neither the Japanese Government nor Japanese nationals participate in the development, manufacture, assembly, ownership or airborne operation of civil aircrfat.”

4. Paragraph 4 is added as follows:

“4. Rates for the transport of mail, freight and passengers shall be fixed by the Japanese Government subject to approval by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.”

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.Bush
Brigadier General, USA,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2106/1)

23 January 1951

1. SCAPIN 2106 informs the JG of conditions under which the SCAP will authorize an internal airline within Japan. Paragraph 2a of this SCAPIN limits such authorization to a foreign owned air line designated or organized by those civil airlines which operated in Japan subsequent to 2 September 1945 and prior to 1 January 1950. (Tab A).

2. Subsequent to issue of this SCAPIN the JG promulgated Cabinet Order 327 in strict conformance therewith and hence excluded any consideration of participation thereunder in any way by a Japanese company. As a result of later informal discussions between the JG and representatives of the foreign owned airlines, it was determined that a more desirable arrangement would be one whereunder a Japanese agency would hire or charter a foreign owned airline to serve as air carrier on a cost plus per mile basis under broad surveillance of SCAP.

3. The substance of the revised proposal was indicated to DA in Radio CX 52031 (para 5) on 25 December 1950. (Tab B).

4. On 10 January 1951, DA 80667 (Tab C) indicated State and Army approval in principle to the revised proposal.

5. Informal negotiations are continuing within the JG and with foreign owned airlines. However, in order that the JG may have a "legal" basis for revising their Cabinet Order to permit participation by a Japanese owned company under appropriate controls with actual air operations being performed by a foreign owned company, it is desired to amend SCAPIN 2106 to accommodate such organization.

6. Recommend approval of the attached amendment to SCAPIN 2106 which contains permissive provision adequate to permit any Japanese controlled juridical person to engage in internal airline business provided such participation does not include development, manufacture, assembly, ownership or airborne operation of civil aircraft.

7. Concurrences: LS, DS, CTS as per attached c/n (Tab D).

Mr. Gregory: 57-8893

C. S. G.
