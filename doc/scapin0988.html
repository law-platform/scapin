GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (29 May 46) LS

(SCAPIN - 988)

APO 500

29 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

The Following named Japanese will be apprehended as Suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, EIGHTH Army, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest possible date.

NAME

RANK OR TITLE

PERTINENT DATA

ARAI, S.

Civilian

Was employed at Nippon Mine Co., at Bukit Besi, Malaya. Lived at Kuala, Pungun, Malaya for several years. Also, employed by Japanese Intelligence Service, Malaya.

ARIMURA, Tsunemichi

Maj. Gen.

Commander in Chief of POW's in Malaya during 1943. Chief of Military Affairs, Western Army.

FUJIWARA, Jutaro

Captain

Officer in charge of Kempei Tai. Sailed from Sumatra for Japan in July 1943. Probable home address: Matsuyama Shi, Gion Machi 2 Chome No. 97

-or-

1409 Serdai, Miuchi-Mura, Onsen Gun, Ehime Prefecture.

HIRAYASU, (FNU)

Lt.

Was Military Governor on Penang during 1942. Left Singapore in Aug. 1943.

KAKIHIRA, (FNU) (KAKIHISA)

Lt.

Adjutant at Camp O'Donnell during 1942. Member of the 61st Service Group.

KAWANO, Hiroaki

Pfc.

Tokyo Branch Camp No. 4 15 May 43 - 31 Aug 45.

KIKUCHI, Kenichi

Mess Sgt.

Hakodate Camp No.3 1 Apr 45 - 31 Aug 45.

KITADA, Kintaro

Was Officer in charge Police District, Port Dickson, Malaya. Left Singapore for Japan on board vessel Tatsutama Maru.

KODA, Zoroku

Lt. Col.

Was Commander of 45th Independent Bn., Kuala Lumpar in October 1942. Returned to Japan in 1943.

KOIKE, Kaneichi

1st Lt.

Member of the Judicial Dept. of the Formosan Army. Probable home address: 764 Goto-Machi, Nagasaki City, Nagasaki Prefecture.

MARUYAMA, Gisaburo

Civilian

Umeda Buncho Camp 24 Nov 43 - 25 Dec 44

Probable home address: Kyoto-Fu, Shimokyo-Ku, Aya-Koji, Higashi Toin, Higashi Hairu

MARUYAMA, Naosuke

2nd Lt.

On board vessel Singapore Maru from 30 Oct to 24 Nov 42, enroute from Singapore to Moji, Japan.

MASUDA, (FNU)

Civilian

Interpreter for Kempei Tai. Sailed from Jesselton 22 Mar for Japan on board vessel Kiji Maru.

MATSUMOTO, Yoshiteru

Koin

Umeda Bunsho Camp 23 Sep 42 - 10 Mar 43 Kobe Branch Camp 11 Mar 43 - May 43 Taisho Branch Camp May 43 - 10 May 45 Chikko Branch Camp 10 May 45 - 15 Aug 45.

MIYAZAWA, Kunio

Probationary Officer

Member of the Judicial Dept of the Formosan Army.

MIZUNO, Tatsuo

Sgt/Maj

Nagoya Branch Camp No. 1 20 Apr 45 - 30 Sep 45 Residence is either Kamioka or Funatsu.

Sgt.

Kawasaki Branch Camp 8 Dec 42 - Mar 43

Amagasaki Branch Camp Mar 43 - Oct 43

Major

Was Infantry Battalion Commander 5th Division at Bukit Kulai Johore during 1942.

NISHIKAWA, Kikuma

Fukuoka Branch Camp No.10 Nov 43 - Aug 45

NISHINA, Yasushi

2nd Lt.

Member of the Judicial Dept of the Formosan Army.

NOGUCHI, Teramichi

Sgt/Maj

OGASAWARA, Makoto

Lt.

On board vessel Singapore Maru from 30 Oct to 24 Nov 42 enroute from Singapore to Moji, Japan. Residence reported to be Tokyo.

OGURA, (FNU)

Sgt/Maj

Member of Toungoo Kempei Tai. Was in Korea in 1943. Left Toungoo, Burma, 27 May 1944.

OUCHI, Tsuneo

Probationary Officer

SHIMODA, Kenji

Probationary Officer

TAGUCHI, Yoshio

Pfc.

TAKITA, Saburo

Lt.
