THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Application for Permission to Use Facilities of Takikawa Chemical Industry Co., Ltd. for Fertilizer Production

1. References are:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG (17 May 46) ESS/IN (SCAPIN 962), subject: Production, Distribution and Use of Fertilizers, dated 17 May 1946.

b. Letter, C.L. O. 3532(ECI) to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, subject: Request for Permission to Supply Mixed Gas Necessary for Production of Ammonium Sulfate at Sunagawa Works of Toyo Koatsu K. K. by Pipe Line to be Laid from Takagawa Plant of Nihon Jinzo Sekiyu, K. K., dated 19 July 1946.

d. Letter, C. L. O. 6816 (ECI) to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, subject: Application for Permission to use Facilities of Takikawa Chemical Industry Co., Ltd. for Fertilizer Production, dated 23 December 1946.

2. Application made in the memorandum referred to in paragraph Id above is approved with the following restrictions:

a. That the additional equipment approved (see paragraph 3 below) be used only to cool gases which are produced by the Takikawa Chemical Industry Co., Ltd. and which are subsequently consumed by the Toyo Koatsu Company in the manufacture of ammonium sulfate fertilizer.

b. That the approval become void on 1 May 1947.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1545)

13 February 1947

1. CLO No. 6816 (ECI) forwards application for permission to use facilities of Takikawa Chemical Industry Company, Ltd., for fertilizer production. Letter requests approval of additional equipment to cope with the difficulties arising from coal shortage and the possible freezing of drains during the winter.

2. At the present time, it is anticipated that 3, 000 cubic meters of coke oven gas per hour will be produced from the facilities of the Takikawa Chemical Company. Ultimately, this production will be about 10, 000 cubic meters per hour. The facilities requested for approval in letter noted above pertain to preparation of gas for transmission through the pipe line, about 12 kilometers long, to the Toyo Koatsu Company at Sunagawa. At the present flow condition, i. e., 3,000 cubic meters per hour, without use of equipment requested, there is danger that freezing or condensed water vapor will cause severe damage to the transmission line and drains. However, at the ultimate flow rate, i. e., 10,000 cubic meters per hour, it can be shown from a heat transfer study that no such danger exists. The equipment requested is only required for the remainder of this winter. In addition to this fact, it is more desirable under the present coal shortage to use electrically-driven compressors (as planned in the Japanese request) rather than the steam-driven compressors which will be used later in full-scale operation.

3. Memo to IJG approves use of certain equipment for the above purpose. This equipment is in addition to that already approved. However, the use has been made void after 1 May 1947.

4. No concurrences necessary.

/s/t/ J. Z. R.
