GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.7 (13 May 48) CPC/CD

(SCAPIN - 1893)

APO 500

13 May 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Application of Financial Institutions Reconstruction and Reorganization Law to 2nd Restricted Deposits of War Criminal Suspects.

1. Reference is made to the following memoranda:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.7, (23 Apr 46)CPC, SCAPIN 897, 23 April 1946, subject, "Control, Impounding, and Blocking of Property of Designated Individuals."

b. L.O. 1794 (AD/FF2), 22 April 1948, subject, "2nd Restricted Deposits that are Designated Property."

2. Application of Article 24 of Financial Institutions Reconstruction and Reorganization Law to Blocked accounts of war criminal suspects is approved.

3. So much of 2nd Restricted Deposits as may be written off to absorb the balance of the final loss of the respective banks is hereby released from the provisions of reference 1a.

4. The remainder, if any, of 2nd Restricted Deposits shall be transferred to the 1st Restricted Deposit of the designated individuals concerned.

5. An itemized report of all releases as provided in paragraph 3 above, and all transfers as provided in paragraph 4 above, will be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Civil Property Custodian, within thirty (30) days after the completion thereof, including the following information:

a. Name of designated person

b. Name of bank or banks

c. Amount or amounts written off

d. Amount or amounts transferred

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
