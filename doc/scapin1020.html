GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004.2 (13 Jun 46) ESS

(SCAPIN - 1020)

APO 500

13 June 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Bank of China, Osaka Branch

1. Reference is made to the following memoranda for Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, SCAP.

a. AG 091.3 (30 Sep 45) ESS, dated 30 Sep 1945, subject: "Closing of Colonial and Foreign Banks and Special Wartime Institutions."

b. AG 123 (20 Oct 45) ESS, dated 20 October 1945, subject: "Appointment of a liquidator of Branches of: 1. Bank of Chosen, 2. Bank of Taiwan, 3. Banque Franco-Japonaise, 4. Chosen Colonization Bank, 5. Deutsche Bank fuer Ostasien."

c. AG 091.3 (18 Jan 45) ESS-FI, dated 18 January 1946, subject: "Appointment of a committee of Conservators for Institutions Closed Pursuant to Memorandum of this headquarters, AG 091.3 (30 Sep 45) ESS."

2. Paragraph 5 of memorandum indicated in 1c above is amended to read: "The Bank of Chosen, the Bank of Taiwan, Dentsche Bank fuer Ostasien, Banque Franco-Japonaise, Chosen Colonization Bank, Banque de l’lndo Chine, Bank of China, Osaka Branch, and the National Financial Control Association are excepted from the provisions of this Memorandum."

3. The provisions of the memorandum indicated in paragraph 1b are hereby declared applicable to the Bank of China, Osaka Branch.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government will take appropriate action to effect the change of status indicated in paragraph 2. Notification of such action will be sent to GHQ, SCAP, the Bank of Japan, the Committee of Conservators for Closed Institutions, and other interested parties.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ J. W. Mann
for /t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1020)

WKL/WNR/mlm

6 June 1946

1. Memo for the Imperial Japanese Government directs that the Bank of China, Osaka Branch, be placed under the direction of the Bank of Japan, rather than the Committee of Conservators for Closed Insititutions, for liquidation purposes.

2. The Bank of China, Osaka, was closed at the time of occupation along with other colonial and foreign banks. It was essentially a small bank of deposit servicing Chinese residents in the vicinity of Osaka. Permission was requested from Washington to effect the payment of depositors; approval is still pending. It is desirable, however, for the Bank of Japan to take the initial steps necessary preparatory for the payment of depositors. The Bank of Japan has facilities in Osaka suited to the type of operation required in handling the affairs of the Bank of China while the Committee has not.

3. No concurrences are necessary.

4. This is considered an administrative matter. The interests of USAFIK have been considered. This action does not affect the Korean area.

5. Direct communication has been authorized.

6. Reference is made to the following memoranda for Imperial Japanese Government, from GHQ, SCAP:

a. AG 091.3 (30 Sep 45) ESS, dated 30 September 1945, subject: "Closing of Colonial and Foreign Banks and Special Wartime Institutions."

b. AG 123 (20 Oct 45) ESS, dated 20 October 1945, subject: "Appointment of a Liquidator of Branches of: 1. Bank of Chosen, 2. Bank of Taiwan, 3. Banque Franco-Japonaise, 4. Chosen Colonization Bank, 5. Deutsche Bank fuer Ostasien."

c. AG 091.3 (18 Jan 46) ESS-FI, dated 18 January 1946, subject: "Appointment of a Committee of Conservators for Institutions Closed Pursuant to Memorandum of this Headquarters, AG 091.3 (30 Sep 45) ESS."

d. Radio W 84974, dated 19 April 1946, from Washington (from Joint Chiefs of Staff) to CINCAFPAC (to MacArthur).

e. Outgoing radio C 59351, dated 29 March 1946, from SCAP to WARCOS (For Joint Chiefs of Staff).

W. K. L.
