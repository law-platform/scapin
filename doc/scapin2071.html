APO 500

4 January 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Rescission of Memoranda to the Japanese Government

1. Reference is made to the following memoranda to the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. File AG 120.01 (12 Sep 47) ESS/FI, SCAPIN 1775, dated 12 September 1947, subject: Reduction of Governmental Expenditures.

b. File AG 091.3 (13 Jan 48) ESS/FI, SCAPIN 1775/1, dated 13 January 1948, subject: Reduction of Governmental Expenditures.

c. File AG 120.01 (12 Sep 47) ESS/FI, SCAPIN 1775/2, dated 10 March 1949, subject: Reduction of Governmental Expenditures.

2. References 1a, 1b, and 1c are hereby rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.Bush
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2071)

JRA/EMR/JEH/eb

17 November 1949

1. The Chief of Staff has suggested that ESS prepare a draft SCAPIN for the purpose of rescinding SCAPIN 1775 and amendments thereto unless compelling reasons can be presented for their continued existence.

2. With the relaxation of controls, it is deemed practicable to rescind SCAPIN 1775 and its amendments which provided for certain restrictions in determining the costs of services and supplies procured by the Japanese Government including those used by the occupation forces. Conditions prevailing in the economy since the issuance of subject SCAPINS have improved sufficiently as to eliminate the urgency of directives of this nature.

3. Sufficient controls to properly govern the procurement of services and supplies now exist or can be incorporated into Japanese laws and regulations by informal suggestions to Japanese Government representatives.

4. Concurrences: ESS/LAB Comptroller

ESS/PD G-4

Mr. Hanna, 57-8417

J. R. A.
