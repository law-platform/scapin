OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS
SIGNAL CORPS MESSAGE

(SCAPIN -65)

GC-O JMB/dk 27 September 1945

FROM :SCAP
TO :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT........OPNL PRIORITY
INFO :COMFEAF...............................OPNL PRIORITY
ADFEAF TOKYO..........................OPNL PRIORITY

AUTHORIZATION HAS BEEN GIVEN FOR FLIGHTS OF AMERICAN AIRCRAFT TO LAND AT ANY AIRDROME IN JAPAN WITHOUT PRIOR NOTIFICATION IN ORDER TO DETERMINE SUITABILITY OF AIRDROMES FOR EMERGENCY USE PD (ZAX 6145) DESIRE SAFE CONDUCT BE ASSURED AND EVERY COURTESY AND FACILITY BE PROVIDED THE PERSONNEL OF SUCH FLIGHTS

OFFICIAL:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, US Army,
Adjutant General

APPROVED:

/s/W. E. Chambers
/t/W. E. CHAMBERS
Brigadier General, G. S. C.
Acting Asst. Chief of Staff, G-3
