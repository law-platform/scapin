MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Revised List No.2 Aircraft Factories, Military and Naval Arsenals and Research Laboratories.

1. Reference is made to Memorandums for the Imperial Japanese Government, dated 20 January 1946 (SCAPIN-629) and 28 May 1946 (SCAPIN-987), subject, “Custody, Control and Protective Maintenance of Japanese Aircraft Plants, Arsenals and Laboratories.”

2. The list of Aircraft Factories, Army and Navy Arsenals, and Research Laboratories attached to reference Memorandum is hereby rescinded and the list attached hereto as inclosure 1 is effective this date.

3. Attached as inclosure 2 is a list of changes to the previous list.

4. Within seventy-two (72) hours of receipt of this memorandum, the Japanese Government will have qualified representatives report to the Commanding General, Eighth United States Army and to the Commander Naval Activities, Japan, to receive instructions with regard to establishing or releasing from custody and control installations affected by revision of the list.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD (SCAPIN - 1263)

WFM/NJM/JZR/vpt

10 October 1946

1. Revised List No. 1 Aircraft Factories, Military and Naval Arsenals and Research Laboratories was issued 28 May 1946. Revision was made necessary by submission to this headquarters of information from the field of complete war damage to some installations, transfer of machinery and equipment for storage at others, errors in listing and information that some concerns do not meet the criteria for reparations claims set forth in Radio WCL 43957 Washington (Joint Chiefs of Staff), dated 31 August 1946. This has been accomplished by five sets of annexes which deleted-266 installations, added 53 installations and corrected the address of 11 others.. Two installations are added and two are deleted at the present time.

2. Revised list No. 2 is a consolidation of above changes. It lists:

a. Aircraft Factories 344

b. Military Arsenals 47

c. Naval Arsenals 44

d. Research Labs 41

Total 476

3. Concurrences by C. R. C.

J. Z. R.
