GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 537 (15 May 47) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-1679)

APO 500

15 May 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Conduct of Commercial Activities by Allied Nationals.

1. Reference is C. L. O. Memorandum No. 2419 (CL), dated 4 April 1947, subject Motor-Truck Transportation Business to be conducted by Allied National, which raises several questions with respect to conduct of commercial activities by Allied Nationals residing in Japan.

2. It is not necessary to obtain the approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for a national of the Allied Powers to conduct a commercial business in Japan provided that such national was resident in Japan prior to, during, and after the war and has not been outside of Japan since the conclusion of the war.

3. Under the foregoing circumstances, an Allied National would be subject to the Japanese laws relating to the conduct of a commercial activity.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1679)

9 May 1947

1. C.L.O. Memorandum No. 2419 (CL), dated 4 April 1947, subject: Motor-Truck Transportation Business to be Conducted by Allied National raises several questions with respect to commercial activities of Allied Nationals residing in Japan. Specific questions posed are:

a. whether or not an Allied National is subject to Japanese law and,

b. whether or not such a national would be required to procure a SCAP license to engage in business in Japan.

2. This application was forwarded to ESS/LE for an opinion. Legal ruled that it is not necessary to obtain SCAP permission for a national of the Allied Powers to conduct a commercial business in Japan provided that such national was resident in Japan prior to, during, and after the war. Under these circumstances, an Allied National would be subject to Japanese law.

3. This does not affect the Korean area.

4. Concurrences: ESS/LE (see attached c/n); Dip Sec (W. J. Sebald)

W. K. L.
