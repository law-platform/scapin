GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004 (24 Dec 45) ESS/AC

(SCAPIN - 490)

APO 500

24 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Application for Transfer of Shares

1. Reference is made to Central Liaison Office Memorandum LO 78 dated 12 December 1945, subject: "Dissolution of Holding Companies," which pertains to the application of the Mitsubishi Trading Company to transfer 4525 shares of their stock.

2. Your attention is directed to paragraphs 3a(1) and (2) of Memorandum this headquarters AG 004 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC, dated 8 December 1945, subject: "Establishment of a Schedule of Restricted Concerns,” which authorized transfers of securities when neither the seller nor the purchaser owns or controls more than 1% of the security issue involved.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ Harold Fair
/t/ HAROLD FAIR,
Lt. Col., A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
