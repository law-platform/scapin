GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 095 (17 Jan 48) ESS/AC

(SCAPIN - 1849)

APO 500

17 January 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Action Taken on Central Liaison Office Memoranda

1. No objection is offered to the following Central Liaison Office Memoranda:

a. No. 9857 (LO 4654/2553) of Riken Mining Company, Ltd, for permission to dissolve, provided:

(1) The applicant will file a petition for liquidation pursuant to the provisions of the Commercial Code of Japan.

(2) All income less current liquidating expenses will be deposited promptly by the receiver until final liquidation of all assets has been completed.

(3) When all assets have been liquidated remaining debts will be paid.

(4) Upon completion of the liquidation the Japanese Government will submit promptly to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, in quintuplicate, typed in English, on 8" x 11" paper, a report thereon.

(5) Distribution of the proceeds will not be made to any shareholder without prior approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

b. No. 9856 (LO 4647/2546) of Kanto Foodstuffs Industry Company, Ltd. for release from the Schedule of Restricted Concerns.

c. No. 9835 (LO 4640/2542) of Kobe Steel Works, Ltd, for permission to expend ¥900,000. from its Free Yen Account for the purchase of three (3) buildings and lease 291.7 tsubo of land at 2-516, Ogurihara-cho, Funabashi, Chiba Prefecture, at ¥40.84 per month provided the lease on the land is terminable at the option of the lessee.

d. No. 9547 (LO 4493/2518) of Nissan Chemical Industry, Ltd. for permission to expend ¥778,800. from its Free Yen Account to purchase thirty-nine (39) steel, and forty (40) wooden hand-trucks.

e. No. 9830 (LO 4674/2563) of Nissan Heavy Industry Company, Ltd, for permission to expend ¥1,157,516.33 from its Free Yen Account to purchase fifteen (15) houses at No. 163 Shinkoyasu-cho, Kanagawa-ku, Yokohama.

f. No. 9334 (LO 4405/2453) of Japan Cement Company, Ltd, for permission to borrow ¥665,000. for purchase of an electric locomotive.

2. Funds will be released in accordance with the Emergency Financial Measures of the Japanese Government and amendments thereto.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ A. J. Rehe
for /t/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1849)

WFM/ECW/PHC/gg

12 January 1948

1. The attached memorandum for the Japanese Government notifies the Central Liaison Office of action taken on Central Liaison Office Memoranda. A complete Memo for Record with all necessary concurrences has been filed in the files of Antitrust and Cartels Division, ESS, for each application covered by this directive.

2. This is an administrative matter and no surveillance by the Eighth Army is required.

3. The interests of USAFIK are not concerned.

4. Concurrences: See paragraph 1, above.
