GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 400.703 (14 Oct 46) OGA

(SCAPIN - 1269)

APO 500

14 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Japanese Manned United States Vessels Used in Transporting United States Surplus Property, Supplies, and Equipment from Iwo Jima to Korea.

1. It is directed that the Imperial Japanese Government:

a. Keep an accurate account for each LST, of all petroleum products used cost in yen of food and provisions, wages paid ship's crew, miscellaneous expenses incurred and number of days each ship is employed in the subject named operation.

b. Submit a report to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, through the Commander Naval Activities of Japan. The report will contain the above information for each LST and will be submitted when its service in this operation is terminated.

2. The attached schedules will be completed and submitted in quadruplicate in accordance with paragraph 1b.

3. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 560 (3 Jan 46) GD, (SCAPIN-547) 3 January 1946, subject, "United States Vessels and Small Craft Operated by Japanese Personnel".

b. Shipping Control Authority for Japan Serial 1005, 3 January 1946 and Inclosure "A" thereto.

(1)For the information of the Imperial Japanese Government, this operation shall in no way abrogate, modify nor limit the liability of the Imperial Japanese Government in respect to vessels employed in the transporting of United States surplus property, supplies and equipment from Iwo Jima to Korea, as defined by the above named references.

4. Direct communication is authorized between the General Accounting Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Shipping Control Authority for Japan to implement all provisions of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

14 October 1946

MEMORANDUM:

SUBJECT : Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN-1269), file AG 400.703 (14 Oct 46)OGA, this Headquarters, dated 14 Oct 46 subject: Accounting Procedures for Maintaining Accounts on the Cost of Transporting Surplus Army Property from IWO Jima to Korea.

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The Purpose of this memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government is to notifiy the responsible agencies and instruct them in the proper procedure for recording and reporting the costs of this operation.

3. It further requests that the Imperial Japanese Government submit reports, based on accurate records, to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

4.

R. G. HERSEY

Lt. Col. AGD,

Asst Adj Gen.
