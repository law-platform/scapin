GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 160 (30 Oct 45) ESS-FI

(SCAPIN-211)

30 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Contracts by Persons in Japan with Foreign Concerns.

1. You are directed to take appropriate action to prevent and prohibit any person in Japan from entering into any financial, commercial or business contract with any concern which is foreign owned or controlled without your permission.

2. You will obtain the approval of this Headquarters before granting any such permission.

3. An acknowledgment of the receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
