MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Rescission of Certain Directives Affecting Restricted Concerns.

Rescissions:

a. The following Memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, are hereby rescinded:

(1) AG 091.3 (20 Oct 45) ESS, SCAPIN 162, 20 October 1945, subject: Dissolution or Liquidation of Major Financial or Industrial Enterprises.

(2) AG 091.3 (20 Oct 45) ESS, SCAPIN 162/1, 9 September 1949, subject: Dissolution or Liquidation of Major Financial or Industrial Enterprises.

(3) AG 319.1 (22 Oct 45) ESS/IE, SCAPIN 177, 25 October 1945, subject: Reports to be Made by Certain Business Firms.

(4) AG 004 (31 Oct 45) ESS, SCAPIN 215, 31 October 1945, subject: Sale or Transfer of Securities of Certain Business Firms.

(5) AG 004 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 403, 8 December 1945, subject: Establishment of a Schedule of Restricted Concerns.

(6) AG 004 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 403/1, 9 November 1948, subject: Disposal and Acquisition of Securities of Restricted Concerns.

(7) AG 004 (24 Dec 45) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 490, 24 December 1945, subject: Application for Transfer of Shares.

(8) AG 004 (31 Dec 45) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 520, 31 December 1945, subject: Dissolution of Holding Companies.

(9) AG 150 (23 Feb 46) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 767, 23 February 1946, subject: War Damages and Government Indemnity Claims on Companies on the Schedule of Restricted Concerns.

(10) AG 004 (4 Apr 46) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 858, 4 April 1946, subject: Addition of Subsidiaries of Nomura and Company, Ltd. to Schedule of Restricted Concerns.

(11) AG 004 (8 Jun 46) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 1008, 8 June 1946, subject: Limitation of Activities of and Requiring of Reports from Certain Concerns.

(12) AG 004.05 (4 Oct 46) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 1250, 4 October 1946, subject: Amendments to Memoranda Affecting Restricted Concerns.

(13) AG 386.7 (22 Oct 47) ESS/FI, SCAPIN 1806, 22 October 1947, subject: Approval to Abrogate Imperial Ordinance No. 243 Concerning Prohibition of and Restriction on Dividend Payments.

(14) Paragraph 3a, AG 004 (10 Mar 49) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 1983, 10 March 1949, subject: Regulations Affecting Restricted Concerns.

(15) AG 004 (10 Mar 49) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 1983/1, 2 May 1949, subject: Amendment to Regulations Affecting Restricted Concerns.

b. The rescission of the above memoranda shall not in any way affect any actions heretofore taken pursuant thereto, nor shall it have any retroactive effect whatsoever; and all references to such memoranda in other directives of the Supreme Commander to the Japanese Government shall from the date hereof be construed to refer to this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 2157) WFM/RMG LNS Id

18 June 1951

1. Referenced memoranda listed in para, a of the attached SCAPIN were issued to implement the provisions of JCS 1380 15 regarding dissolution of industrial and financial combines and introduction of democratic institutions. Certain of these memoranda required the submission of reports and maintenance of status quo until a definitive program could be developed. Those directives are inoperative and are rescinded merely to clear the record.

2. The Schedule of Restricted Concerns, established by SCAPIN 403, and the regulations affecting those concerns, were implemented by Imperial Ordinance No. 657 of 1945 and Imperial Ordinance No. 567 of 1946. The purpose of Ordinance 657 was to require government and SCAP approval for activities of those companies outside the normal course of business until such time as the stock disposal program was complete and new shareholders could exercise normal shareholder control and responsibility. That objective having been accomplished, the directives which constitute a basis for Ordinances 657 and 567 can be rescinded, except as indicated in para. 4, below.

3. One thousand two hundred three (1, 203) companies were placed on the Schedule of Restricted Concerns, the last 69 of which are removed by a SCAPIN currently in process.

4. The only existing directives pertaining to the restricted concerns program which are not being rescinded are SCAPINs 1238 and 1238/1 which are the basis for a provision in Ordinance 567 (Art. 17-2) designed to prevent reformation of the combines by prohibiting capital and management affiliation between companies in the same chain of capital until after the expiration of one year from the date the top company (restricted concern) in each chain of capital is removed from the Schedule. Many of the one-year periods will commence to run with the removal of the 69 companies referred to in para. 3, above. The principle basis for leaving the above two SCAPINs outstanding is to assure that the provisions of Ordinance 567 will be applied uniformly to companies subject to its provisions.

L. N. Salwin 26-6671

R. M. G.
