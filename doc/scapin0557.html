GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG (5 Jan 46) GBLN

(SCAPIN - 557)

APO 500

5 January 1946

TO :Central Liaison Office.

1. Your attention is called to paragraph 8(a) of SCAP Directive No. 3, dated 22 September 1945, requesting "reports of all laboratories, research institutes, and similar scientific and technological organizations, which will include the following information:

(1) Name.

(2) Location.

(3) Ownership.

(4) Description of facilities.

(5) Number of employees.

(6) Detailed list of all projects by agency are currently being studied by these agencies and projects studied since 1940."

2. Review of the reports submitted show that the following institutions were not included.

(1) Imperial Fisheries Experimental Station, Tokyo.

(2) Imperial Fisheries Institute, Tokyo.

(3) Misaki Marine Biological Station (Tokyo Imperial University), Misaki(60 ▲▲ south of Tokyo).

(4) Kominato Marine Biological Laboratory, Kominato bay.

(5) Miyako Meteorological Observatory (Central Meteorological Observatory), Miyako.

(6) Institute of Al▲▲logical Research(Hokkaido Imperial University), Huroran.

(7) Akkoshi Marine Biological Station (Hokkaido Imperial University), Akkeshi-wan.

(8) Seto Marine Biological Laboratory (Kyoto Imperial University), Wakayama.

(9) Imperial Marine Observatory, Kobe.

(10) Institute of Physical Oceanography (Kyoto Imperial University), Kyoto.

(11) Mitsui Institute of Marine Biology, Suzaki (Shikoku).

(12) Amakusa Marine Biological Laboratory(Kyushu Imperial University), Amakusa.

(13) Fisheries Experimental Station, Fusan, Korea.

(14) Surashima Marine Biological Station, Surashima.

(15) Marine Experimental Station(Seapaku Shikenjo), Tokyo.

(16) Science Bureau.

3. It is desire that reports on the above institutions be submitted by 20 January 1945 to this Office.

4. It is also desired that a list of grants made by Scientific Council in the Education Ministry be submitted.

For the A. C. of S., G-2:

/S/ F. P. Munson
/t/ F. P. MUNSON
Colonel, GSC
G-2, GHQ, SCAP
