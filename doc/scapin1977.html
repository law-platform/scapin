GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.1 (27 Dec 48) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1977)

APO 500

2 March 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Use of Japanese Postal Service by Allied Occupation Forces.

1. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.12 (11 Nov 45) GD, SCAPIN 265, dated 11 November 1945, subject: Transmission of Allied Occupation Forces Mail, is rescinded.

2. Effective at once, the following instructions will apply to the use of Japanese Postal Service facilities by organizations and members of the Allied Occupation Forces:

a. Members of the Allied Occupation Forces are permitted to use the facilities of the Japanese Postal Service for mailing letters and post cards only, to points within Japan and other countries, subject to Japanese laws and regulations and to provisions and limitations of the Universal Postal Union.

b. Mail delivered by any Allied Forces Army or Fleet post office to any agency of the Japanese Postal Service addressed to a civil post office in Japan will be delivered by the Japanese Postal Service without cost to the Allied Occupation Forces.

c. Mail deposited in Japanese post offices addressed to an Allied Forces Army or Fleet post office in Japan will be delivered to an Allied Forces Army or Fleet post office for forwarding to the addressee, provided it bears a legible English language address, and Japanese postage at not less than the domestic rate.

3. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section and other staff sections of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Ministry of Communications is authorized concerning all matters within the scope of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

2 March 1949

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive SCAPIN 1977, file AG 311.3 (27 Dec 48)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 2 March 1949, subject: Use of Japanese Postal Service by Allied Occupation Forces

1. With reference to the subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. SCAPIN 265 issued by G-4 on 11 November 1945, in effect, prohibits occupation forces from using Japanese postal facilities by requiring that any mail deposited in Japanese post offices be returned to the nearest Army or Fleet post office. It also provides that mail delivered by an APO or FPO to the Japanese Postal Service, addressed to a civil address in Japan, will be transmitted postage free.

3. FEC postal Memorandum No. 26, dated 29 October 1948, states that for mail purposes, Japan, Korea, etc., are considered as foreign countries and mail from occupation forces addressed to these countries shall be prepaid at the appropriate international rate in U. S. postage, when deposited in an APO. This regulation remains in effect.

4. Subject SCAPIN was issued to provide for:

a. Use of Japanese postal facilities for the mailing of letters and post cards only by members of the occupation forces paying the appropriate Japanese postage rates.

b. Continued handling of official mail from occupation forces addressed to civil post offices in Japan, free of postage and without cost to the occupation forces.

c. Transmission of mail deposited in Japanese post offices addressed to allied occupation forces Army or Fleet post offices.

5. It is not considered advisable to authorize mailing of parcels by members of the occupation forces through Japanese postal system, because of the blackmarket implications.

A J REHE

Major, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
