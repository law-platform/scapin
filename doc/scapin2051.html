GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.7 (13 Oct 49) CPC/PLD

(SCAPIN-2051)

APO 500

13 October 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Notification that the United States, United Kingdom and France are Owners of Certain Categories of Former German Property in Japan

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 091.112 (13 Sep 45) MG, SCAPJN 26, 13 September 1945, subject, “Protection of Allied and Axis Property,” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386,7 (19 Oct 48) CPC/CD, SCAPIN 6112-A, 19 October 1948, subject, “German External Assets in Japan Vested Under Berlin Law No. 5, as Amended by Regulation I, and Procedure to be Followed in Connection Therewith,” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Reference memorandum for the Japanese Government 1b is hereby rescinded and any actions heretofore taken by the Japanese Government inconsistent with the provisions of this memorandum which supersedes 1b above shall likewise be rescinded.

3. The United States, United Kingdom and France for whom the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers is trustee are the owners of all right, title and interest in and to the following categories of property in Japan:

a. Any property in Japan which was owned or controlled before 1 July 1948 by any person of German nationality inside Germany

b. Any property in Japan which was owned or controlled before 1 July 1948 by any person of German nationality outside Germany or by any branch of any business or corporation or other legal entity organized under the laws of Germany or having its principal place of business in Germany.

c. Any property in Japan which was owned or controlled before 1 July 1948 by those persons who were German citizens on or after 1 September 1939 and who at any time have lived abroad (continuously or intermittently) and who aided or attempted to aid Germany or her allies during the war of who assisted Germany or her allies in the preparation for war, but shall not apply to any citizen of any country annexed or claimed to have been annexed by Germany since 31 December 1937.

d. Any property in Japan which was owned or controlled before 1 July 1948 by such categories of persons of German nationality as shall be designated from time to time by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, his agents or successors.

4. Definitions for the purpose of this directive:

a. The term “Person” shall include any natural, collective or juridical person or entity under Public or Private law having legal capacity to acquire, use, control or dispose of property or interest therein; and any German Government, including all political sub-divisions, public corporations, agencies and any instrumentalities thereof. Any juridical person or entity which was organized under the laws of, or has its principal place of business in Germany, shall be deemed to be a person of German nationality within the meaning of paragraph 3a above.

b. The term “Property” shall include all movable and immovable property and all rights and interests in or claims to such property whether matured or not including all property, rights, interests, or claims transferred to or held by third parties as nominees or trustees and all property, rights, interests or claims transferred by way of gift or otherwise or for consideration, expressed or implied, but not including the rights or interest of third parties to a bona fide sale for full consideration, and shall include, but shall not be limited to, buildings and lands, goods, wares and merchandise, chattels, coin, bullion, currency, deposits, accounts or debts, shares, claims, bills of lading, warehouse receipts, all kinds of financial instruments whether expressed in Reichsmarks or in any foreign currency, evidences of ownership of property, contracts, judgments rights in or with respect to patents, copyrights, trademarks, etc. and in general, property of any nature whatsoever.

5. There is excluded from the provisions of this memorandum, property in Japan of those persons of German nationality which has been or may be by specific directives from Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, his agents or successors, released from impoundment directed under the provisions of the memorandum for the Japanese Government reference 1a above.

6. The Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers as trustee for the United States, United Kingdom and France has done or will do all acts which are deemed necessary and appropriate to obtain possession or control, and will exercise complete rights of ownership over all such property.

7. Nothing in this memorandum will be construed as a release from the provisions of the memorandum for the Japanese Government reference 1a above, which provisions, except as modified or amended by subsequent directives to the Japanese Government, remain in full force and effect.

8. The Japanese Government is directed to provide the necessary implementation required to legally transfer, register and/or record under Japanese Law formal title to designated purchasers from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, his agents or successors, of all property coming within the provisions of paragraphs 3a, b, c, and d when so instructed by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

9. A report will be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers within sixty (60) days of the date of this memorandum setting forth action which has been taken in compliance with the above provisions.

10. Direct communication between the appropriate agencies of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Japanese Government is hereby authorized in the implementation of the instructions herein contained.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
