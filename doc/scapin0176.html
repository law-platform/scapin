GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.5 (22 Oct 45) SC

(SCAPIN - 176)

22 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Permission to amend Rikugun Ryakugoo No. 5 currently in use by the Japanese Army.

1. It is appreciated that there is a definite need in Japanese Army wireless communication for the use of code books for the sake of clarity, economy and efficiency of operations. To this end permission has been granted the Japanese authorities to use the following code books:

Rikugun Ryakugoo 4 and 5

Kookuu Rakugoo 4

Sempaku Ryakugoo 3

Tsuushin Tyakugoo 3

Furoku Jiten

And Chinese Telegraph Code.

2. It is further appreciated that these code books, having been constructed in 1944, do not include vocabulary and addresses applicable to the present situation of surrender and demobilization. With this mind, permission is herewith granted the Japanese authorities to amend Rikugun Ryakugoo No.5 so as to include applicable vocabulary and address groups as requested in the attachment to their basic letter of 19 October.

3. Sufficient copies of these amendments to this code book must be deposited to meet the special requirements of censorship.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen,
H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
