AG 123 (8 Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 565)

APO 500

8 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Release of Funds of Italian Government

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 123 (20 Dec 45) ESS/FI subject: “Release of Official Funds of Royal Italian Embassy."

2. It is directed that yen bank accounts belonging to the Italian Government held with the Tokyo Branch of the Yokohama Specie Bank in the name of the Istituto Nazionale per 1 Cambi con l'Estero (Istcambi) be released in accordance with the memorandum referred to in paragraph 1.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General

Related document原本 →

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 565)

OJM/smh

1. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 123 (20 Dec 45) ESS/FI subject: "Release of official Funds of Royal Italian Embassy," authorized the release of the funds of the Embassy held in the Yokohama Specie Bank, Tokyo Branch, for the purpose of paying salaries of Embassy personnel, maintenance of Italian properties and the support of Italian nationals.

2. Mr. Diego Simmonetti, 3rd Secretary of the Embassy now reports that funds for the use of the Embassy are obtained from accounts held in the name of Istituto Nazionale per 1 Cambi con l’Estero (Istcambi) which is an agency of the Royal Italian Government responsible for the custody of Italian official funds abroad.

3. This memorandum releases yen funds held in the name of this agency for use by the Embassy in accordance with our memorandum AG 123 (20 Dec 45) ESS/FI.

4. No concurrences necessary.

C. F. T.
