GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 684 (26 Nov 48) GC/TNG

(SCAPIN - 1948)

APO 500

10 December 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Aerial Gunnery Range.

1. The rectangular over-water area, whose corners are located at the points designated by the following coordinates, is discontinued as an aerial gunnery range:

42° 02' North - 141° 50' East

42° 07' North - 142° 30' East

42° 17' North - 142° 00' East

41° 52' North - 142° 20' East

2. The instructions contained in Paragraph 3, SCAPIN 1908, 10 June 1948, are rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
