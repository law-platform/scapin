GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 061 (31 May 47) CIE

(SCAPIN - 1712)

APO 500

31 May 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Distribution of Japan and World Maps.

1. Reference is made to letter from the Geographical Survey Bureau, Ministry of Home Affairs, dated 1 May 1947, subject: Distribution of Japan and World Maps.

2. General Headquarters, Supreme Commander For The Allied Powers, has no objection to the distribution to educational organizations and institutions of the Map of Japan (scale 1:2,000,000) and the Map of the World (scale 1:22,000,000), copies of which are attached. Reference letter states that these Maps have been published by the Geographical Survey Bureau, Ministry of Home Affairs.

3. This letter does not constitute authorization to print additional maps. Further, it may not be used in support of requests for paper allocation.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

1 Incl

Maps

(to addressee only)
