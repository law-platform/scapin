GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 413.52 (21 Sep 46) IPS

(SCAPIN - 1223)

APO 500

21 September 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Monetary Engraving Plates

1. It is directed that the Imperial Japanese Government deliver the following monetary engraving plates to the International Prosecution Section, SCAP.

a. Tien (10) Gulden Netherlands East Indies

b. Vijf (5) Gulden Netherlands East Indies

c. Fen (1) Gulden Netherlands East Indies

d. Half (1/2) Gulden Netherlands East Indies

e. Ten (10) Pesos Philippine Islands

f. Five (5) Pesos Philippine Islands

g. Fifty (50) Centavos Philippine Islands

h. Ten (10) Centavos Philippine Islands

i. Five (5) Centavos Philippine Islands

j One (1) Centavo Philippine Islands

k. Ten (10) Dollars British Malay, Borneo & Thailand

l. Five (5) Dollars British Malay, Borneo & Thailand

m. One (1) Dollar British Malay, Borneo & Thailand

n. Fifty (50) Cents British Malay, Borneo & Thailand

o. Ten (10) Cents British Malay, Borneo & Thailand

P. Five (5) Cents British Malay, Borneo & Thailand

q. One (1) Cent British Malay, Borneo & Thailand

2. In the event that more than one engraving plate is used, to pulp either of the above denominations, it is directed that complete sets for each denomination be delivered.

3. It is directed that these plates be furnished not later than 23 September 1946.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
