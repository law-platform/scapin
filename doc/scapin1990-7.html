AG 072 (8 Apr 49) CPC/OD

(SCAPIN-1990/7)

APO 500

17 January 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Procedures for Restoration of Patents, Utility Models, Designs and Trade-Marks

1. Reference is made to the following memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. File AG 072 (8 Apr 49) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1990, 8 April 1949, subject, “Procedures for Restoration of Patents, Utility Models, and Designs to Allied Nationals”

b. File AG 072 (8 Apr 49) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1990/2, 27 August 1949, subject, “Procedures for Restoration of Patents, Utility Models, and Designs to Allied Nationals”

c. File AG 072 (9 Sep 49) CPC/FP,SCAPIN 2042, 9 September 1949, subject, “Trade-Marks, Trade names, and Marking of Merchandise in Japan”

d. File AG 072 (8 Apr 49) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1990/3, 21 March 1950, subject, “Procedures for Restoration of Patents, Utility Models, and Designs to Allied Nationals”

e. File AG 072 (8 Apr 49) CPC/OD, SCAPIN 1990/4, 4 August 1950, subject, “Procedures for Restoration of Patents, Utility Models, and Designs to Allied Nationals.”

2. The date in paragraph 6 of memorandum referenced in paragraph 1a above, in paragraph 2 of memorandum referenced in paragraph 1b above, and paragraph 2 of memorandum referenced in paragraph 1e above, shall be amended to read as follows: “31 March 1950.”

3. Paragraph 9 of the memorandum referenced in paragraph 1c above shall be amended by deleting from the first sentence thereof the words, “within one (1) year from the date of the legislation which will carry into effect the provisions of this memorandum,” and substituting therefor the words “not later than 31 March 1951.”

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
