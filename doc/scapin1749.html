GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004.06 (19 Jul 47) OGA

(SCAPIN - 1749)

APO 500

19 July 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Change of Submission Date of Monthly Labor Report.

1. Reference letter Japanese Government, Central Liaison Office, C.L.O. No, 4499 (AL), dated 10 June 1947, subject: Change of Submission Date of Monthly Labor Report.

2. Permission is granted to change the date of submission of the monthly labor reports as required in SCAPIN 260 to the twentieth (20th) of the month following the period covered by the report.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

APO 500

19 July 1947

AG 004.06(19 Jul 47) OGA

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT : Information of General Application Pertaining To Directive Number SCAPIN 1749, file AG 004.06 (19 Jul 47)OGA, this Headquarters, dated 7 July 1947, subject: Change of Submission Date of Monthly Labor Report

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The purpose of this memorandum to the Japanese Government is to grant an extension date for the submission of their monthly labor report required by SCAPIN 260. This extension is necessary due to the revision of the pay period of Japanese Laborers from the last day of the month to the tenth of the following month.

A J REHE

Major, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
