SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.6 (9 Dec 51) CPC/OD

(SCAPIN - 2187)

APO 500

9 December 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
SUBJECT :Looted Property Program

1. The Japanese Government is informed that property brought to Japan from countries occupied; by the former Japanese armed forces and referred to as “looted property” will continue to be the subject of claims by the governments of the countries thus occupied in accordance with the terms of the Peace Treaty.

2. Effective 31 December 1951 the Japanese Government will assume execution of the program for the restitution of such looted property. Requests for restitution of such property will be submitted directly to the Japanese Government by the diplomatic representative in Japan of the country from which such property was removed.

3. Restitution of looted property will be effected in accordance with established policy as set forth in previous instructions issued to the Japanese Government. Annex I lists previously issued related directives with appropriate rescissions and amendments.

4. All actions recommending restitution of looted property after 31 December 1951 and until the coming into force of the Peace Treaty shall have the prior approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

5. Not later than 15 December 1951 a report will be submitted to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers furnishing the following information:

a. Agency of the Japanese Government which will be responsible for the reporting on and the restitution of looted property found in Japan

b. Procedures which will be followed in carrying out all phases of the Looted Property program directly between the Japanese Government and the missions in Japan of the interested Far Eastern Commission nations.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C.C.B. WARDEN
Colonel, AGC
Adjutant General

ANNEX I

The following memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers sets forth some of the procedures governing the restitution of looted property. Except as indicated below, these instructions will remain in force, Rescissions, Group A, and Amendments, Group B, will become effective 31 December 1951.

Group A.

Memoranda to be Rescinded

1. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (7 Apr 48) CPC/LGP, SCAPIN 1878/3, 23 October 1948, subject, "Concentration of Base Metals Impounded Under SCAPIN 1878," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (7 Apr 48) CPC/FLD, SCAPIN 1878/4, 6 May 1949, subject, "Concentration of Base Metals," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

3. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 140 (3 Aug 48) CPC/OD, SCAPIN 5888-A, 3 August 1948, subject, "Establishment of a Uniform System of Records for Warehoused Property," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

4. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 140 (3 Aug 48) CPC/OD, SCAPIN 5888-A/1, 14 August 1948, subject, "Establishment of a Uniform System of Records for Warehoused Property," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

Group B.

Amendments or Changes to be Effected

1. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.3 (19 Apr 46) CPC, SCAPIN 885, 19 April 1946, subject, "Impounding and Reporting of Looted Property," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. Paragraph 2 is amended to read:

"2. Reports of findings of looted property will be compiled by territories or localities from which the property was taken and will include:

a. Complete description of property

b. Quantity

c. Name of owner at time of seizure

d. Disposition of property upon arrival in Japan or in Japanese waters

e. Present title holder and address

f. Present location of property.

Two copies of each report will be furnished the mission in Japan representing the government of the country from which the property was taken and will be submitted in the English language."

2. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.6 (25 Jul 46) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1083, 25 July 1946, subject, "Custody, Shipment and Storage of Looted Property," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. Paragraph 4 and 5 are amended to read:

"4. The Mission in Japan of the government representing the area from which the property was taken will be notified of the location of storage facilities to be used and the condition and location of items which it is considered impracticable to move to such facilities. Items stored will be cataloged and so arranged as to be readily available for inspection. Two copies, in English, of all catalogs will be furnished the foreign mission in Japan which is interested in this property.

"5 Direct communication between the appropriate agency of the Japanese Government and the foreign mission concerned is hereby authorized."

3. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.6 (26 Sep 47) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1783, 26 September 1947, subject, "Safeguarding Property Stored in Looted Property Warehouses in Japan," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. Paragraph 3 is amended to read:

"3. No person, regardless of rank or position, will enter any Looted Property warehouse or inspect any looted property which may have been impounded without a permit from the appropriate agency of the Japanese Government which is responsible for the safeguarding of this property."

Paragraph 4 is rescinded.

Paragraph 5 is amended to read:

"5. No person, regardless of rank or position, will remove any equipment, machinery, materials or other property which has been reported as looted except by written authorization from the appropriate agency of the Japanese Government charges with the responsibility of disposition of this property."

4. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.6 (13 Feb 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1858, 13 February 1948, subject, "Responsibilities of the Japanese Government for the Packaging, Transfer, and Delivery of Property being Restored to Claimant Nations under the Restitution Program," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. Paragraph 6 is amended to read:

"6. Receipt for property restored in restitution will be given by the claimant nation upon the completion of the proper storage of the property aboard the claimant nation's craft, or upon acceptance of the property at the point mutually agreed to by the representatives of the claimant country and of the Japanese Government. After the delivery receipt has been executed, the property will become the entire responsibility of the claimant nation."

Paragraph 7 is rescinded in its entirety.

5. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (7 Apr 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1878,7 April 1948, subject, "Property Produced in Occupied Areas," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

In Paragraph 4, sentence, "In cases where such storage seems impractical application for storage elsewhere must be made specifically to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Civil Property Custodian," is rescinded.

Paragraph 6 is rescinded.

6. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.6 (7 Apr 48) CPO/LP, SCAPIN 1878/2, 4 September 1948, subject, "Concentration of Looted Raw Materials Impounded Under SCAPIN 1878," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. Paragraph 3 and 4 are rescinded.

7. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.6 (13 Feb 48) CPC/PLD, SCAPIN 1858/1, 14 February 1949, subject, "Responsibilities of the Japanese Government for the Packaging, Transfer, and Delivery of Property being Restored to Claimant Nations Under the Restitution Program," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

In Paragraph 3, change the sentence, "Payment will be made into a designated Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers bank account immediately upon presentation of invoices accompanied by necessary documents," to read, "Payment will be made into a bank account mutually agreed to by the representatives of the claimant country and of the Japanese Government immediately upon presentation of invoices accompanied by necessary document."

Paragraph 4e (1) is changed to read:

"(1) The amount it would cost to transport the equipment directly from impounding site to dockside and load aboard ship at the port mutually agreed to by the representatives of the claimant country and of the Japanese Government, in accordance with the provisions of memorandum referred to in paragraph 1b above."

Group C.

Memoranda to Remain in Force

Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 012.2 (31 Oct 50) CPC/OD, SCAPIN 2141, 13 February 1951, subject, "Applicability of Customs and Other Taxes to Looted Property," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.
