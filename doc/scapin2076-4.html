GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 620 (27 Jan 50) CE

(SCAPIN-2076/4)

APO 500

20 February 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
Subject :Provision of Additional Dependent Housing for the Occupation Forces

1. Reference: Memorandum, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 628 (27 Jan 50) ESS/PF, SCAPIN 2076, subject: Provision of Additional Dependent Housing for Occupation Forces, 27 January 1950, as amended by SCAPIN 2076/1, 4 July 1950, SCAPIN 2076/2, 18 July 1950 and SCAPIN 2076/3, 4 August 1950.

2. That portion of paragraph 4, reference 1 above, as amended by SCAPIN 2076/1 which reads, with regard to the phased completion of the housing units in this program:

“Date Accumulative Units

1 January 1951 2,000”

is modified to read as follows:

“Date Accumulative Units

1 January 1951 1,543

1 March 1951 2,003”

3. All phases of construction involved in this program will be completed by 1 May 1951.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.Bush
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
