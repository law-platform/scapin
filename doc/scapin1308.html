GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004 (1 Nov 46) ESS/IN

(SCAPIN-1308)

APO 500

1 November 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Industrial Recovery Corporation.

1. Reference, C. L. O. No. 3965 (ECI - F) proposing the establishment of an Industrial Recovery Corporation and the dissolution of the present Industrial Equipment Corporation.

2. The above proposal in its present form is unsatisfactory.

3. The Japanese Government should modify its proposal for establishment of such Industrial Recovery Corpotation, incorporating the following basic considerations:

a. Provision for the Industrial Recovery Corporation to act as liquidating agent only in its relation to the dissolution of the Industrial Equipment Corporation; but that the Industrial Recovery Corporation shall have the power to allocate facilities in the hands of the Industrial Equipment Corporation by requiring their lease, sale, or other disposition when needed in the execution of the programs of the Economic Stabilization Board.

b. Provision for the Industrial Recovery Corporation to be responsible to the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, subject to the orders, directives and programs of the Economic Stabilization Board.

c. Provision for the Industrial Recovery Corporation’s working funds to be derived from the Reconversion Finance Corporation with respect to each project and subject to the landing regulations of that agency.

d. Provision that all functions of the Industrial Recovery Corporation be exercised within the framework of the economic policy as determined by the Economic Stabilization Board, and that those functions be limited as follows:

(1) The acquisition of industrial equipment and materials and the subsequent lease or sale of such equipment and materials, and the allocation of equipment and materials, belonging to the Industrial Equipment Corporation.

(2) The construction of plant and equipment and the lease or sale of such plant and equipment.

e. Provision that the salaries of the officers and directors be made public.

f. Provision for adequate representation of middle sized and small business on the Board of Directors.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1308)

ESS/IN JZR/rc

26 September 1946

1. CLO No. 3965 (ECI-F) proposes a new government corporation to succeed the present Industrial Equipment Corporation, created during the war as a rough counterpart of the U.S. War Plants Corporation.

2. The present Industrial Equipment Corporation has an extremely unsavory reputation for inefficiency and graft. Its dissolution is considered desirable and should not be delayed by transfer of any of its functions or properties to a successor agency.

3. It is believed that an agency of the Japanese Government, established to buy and sell, or lease, industrial equipment and to build and seller lease industrial plants, is necessary at this time. Certain industrial construction and repair or conversion is vitally important in such industries fertilizer production, coal and others. Under present inflationary conditions, financing such construction and repair cannot be undertaken through normal banking channels due to the risk involved. Although such financing could be accomplished directly by the Japanese Government through the proposed Reconstruction Finance Agency, it is considered that the proposed Industrial Recovery Corporation would be desirable as a facilitating agency.

4. The proposal by the Japanese Government is, therefore, disapproved in its present form, but direction is given for resubmittal in a form to include:

a. No connection with present Industrial Equipment Corporation, except to act as its liquidating agent. This is considered necessary in view of the unsavory character of the Industrial Equipment Corporation and the undesirability of tieing it in any way to its successor agency.

b. Provision for obtaining Working capital from the RFA, considered necessary to bring the proposed agency within the lending regulations of the RFA and prevent a repetition of the Industrial Equipment Corporation's activities in haphazardly issuing debentures.

c. Provision to limit the functions of the proposed agency. Without such limitation, little control could be exercised over its activities.

5. Concurrence: SSS/FI Mr. LeCount

ESS/AC Mr. Henderson

J. Z. R.
