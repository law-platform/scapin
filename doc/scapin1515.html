SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (8 Feb 47) LS-R

(SCAPIN - 1515)

APO 500

8 February 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Release of Suspected War Criminal to Japanese Hospital.

1. FUJITA, Yoshiaki, Sgt, Maj., a Suspected War Criminal, presently interned in Sugamo Prison, Tokyo, will be released therefrom and placed in a reliable Japanese Hospital to receive the necessary medical treatment to return him to a normal physical condition. To effect removal, representatives of the Imperial Japanese Government will contact Legal Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. A report will be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Attention: Legal Section, containing the date subject Japanese was removed from Sugamo Prison, and the name and address of the Japanese institution in which he is placed.

3. Subject Japanese will be held in the Japanese institution until he no longer requires special medical attention, at which time, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers will be contacted for further instructions.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
