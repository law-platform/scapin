GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 152 (30 Nov 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-369)

APO 500

30 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Application of Heinrich Koppers, Tokyo Office, For Release of Blocked Funds.

1. Reference is made to CLC Memorandum No.▲▲▲, dated 16 November 1945, subject: “AG 091(24 Oct 45)ESS-FI” with attached application for release of funds of Heinrich Koppers, Tokyo office.

2. It is directed that immediate steps be taken to liquidate Heinrich Koppers, Tokyo Office. Liquidation expense to be paid may include arrears in rent, miscellaneous expenses and arrears in wages owing employees through 30 November 1945.

3. Any payments made to employees of German nationality will be placed in blocked accounts from which withdrawals may be made in accordance with the Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government AG091.4 (2 Oct 45)ESS, subject: “Authorization No.1, Living Expense Allowances to Axis Nationals Domiciled in Japan.”

4. It is directed that properties of the subject office be disposed of by sale and outstanding claims debts paid within a reasonable time, but not later than 1 January 1946. Process of liquidation over and above authorized liquidation expenses will be placed in a blocked bank account.

5. A report will be submitted to this Headquarters as of 13 December 1945 detailing the progress of liquidation at that date. A report will be submitted at the completion of liquidation proceedings containing a complete statement of.

a. Disposition of the physical assets of the office, including names and addresses of purchasers and amount realized on each item of property disposed of.

b. Payment of arrears in wages and other expenses with name and address of payee in each case.

c. Not proceeds of liquidation proceedings and name of bank and designation of bank account in which funds are deposit.

6. An acknowledgment of the receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 369)

RCK/OJM/kns

30 November 1945

1. Application (undated, received in RSS 17 Nov 1945) from Heinrich Koppers, Tokyo Office, requests release of blocked funds for payment of salaries, rents, and miscellaneous expenses for months of September and October, 1945 in accordance with provisions of Memorandum AG 091 (24 Oct 45) ESS-FI, subject: "Application of German Nationals for Release of Property and Other Assets."

2. This organization is the liaison office between Heinrich Koppers, Essen, Moltkestrasse 29, Germany and Japanese clients in supervision of execution of orders placed by Japanese clients with Koppers Company as well as supervising of erection, starting, and operation of Koppers plants in Japan. Koppers plants include coke over plants, gas plants, gas reforming plants, thylox plants, etc. No manufacturing or fabrication was conducted by the Tokyo office, but it acted as agent in technical engineering advice, as well as ordering special parts for operation and maintenance of such plants.

3. Funds blocked are in the name of R. Clinger, Manager, in the amount of ¥63,621.96 with Yokohama Specie Bank, Ltd., Tokyo.

4. Attached memorandum to CLO directs liquidation of Heinrich Koppers, Tokyo, not later than 1 January 1946.

/s/ C. F. T.
