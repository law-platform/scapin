GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (25 Jul 47) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1603/6)

APO 500

2 August 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Reactivation of the Coastal Radio Service of the Japanese Ministry of Communications to Handle Ship-Shore Traffic for Allied Merchant Shipping.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 676.3 (25 Jul 47)CCS SCAPIN 1603/1, dated 25 July 1947, subject same as above, as amended.

2. Paragraph 6 of inclosure 1 to reference memorandum is rescinded.

3. Instructions covering the provisions of this paragraph have been issued to the special disbursing agents aboard United States Army transports.

4. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the allied Powers and the Ministry of Communications within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

(See Reverse for memo information of general application)

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number SCAPIN l603/6, file AG676.3 (25 Jul 47)CCS, dated 2 August 1948, subject: Reactivation of the Coastal Radio Service of the Japanese Ministry of Communications to Handle Ship-Shore Traffic for Allied Merchant Shipping

2. The instructions rescinded in paragraph 6 of the subject memorandum are superseded by detailed instructions contained in Department of the Army Technical Bulletin TB FIN 30, subject: Accounting for Prepaid Commercial Messages Transmitted from U.S. Army Transports Over Facilities of Japanese Coastal Radio Stations, dated 24 May 1948.
