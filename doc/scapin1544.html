GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (25 Feb 47) GA

(SCAPIN-1544)

APO 500

25 February 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Repatriation of German and Austrian Nationals

1. Reference is made to Memorandum from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 370.05 (23 Feb 46) GA, (SCAPIN-769) dated 23 February 1946, subject “Repatriation of German Nationals and Nationals who Claim Austrian or Czechoslovakian Citizenship Now in Japan”.

2. It is directed that the Imperial Japanese Government conduct a survey and furnish to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, not later than 10 March 1947, nominal rolls of all German and Austrian nationals in Japan, in the form indicated on the attached sheets.

3. Nominal rolls will be arranged in alphabetical order now and submitted in duplicate for the following categories of personnel:

a. Those German and Austrian nationals who desire to remain in Japan. (Incl 1)

b. Those German and Austrian nationls who desire to be repatriated to Germany or Austria in the near future. (Incl 2)

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

As indicated above
