GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.3 (17 Jan 46) GD

(SCAPIN-613)

APO 500

17 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Coal Production.

1. Reference is made to the reports submitted as directed by the memorandum from this Headquarters to the Imperial Japanese Government, AG 463.3 (11 Dec 45) GD, dated 11 December 1945, subject: “Report on Coal Production.” It is noted that there were produced in December 838,900 metric tons of coal. The efforts which obtained this result are commendable.

2. Because December production so exceeded your estimate of 665,000 metric tons, it is desired that the announced estimates of future production, namely:

January 990,000 metric tons

February 1,065,000 metric tons

March 1,250,000 metric tons

be reviewed and that this Headquarters be advised of the revised estimates by 1200 hours, 22 January 1946.

3. This review will take into consideration the degree of achievement obtainable by a concerted and vigorous effort to execute the measures the Imperial Japanese Government announced have been adopted to alleviate the conditions which are adversely affecting the production of coal. Inspections of the coal mining districts of Kyushu and Hokkaido by representatives of this Headquarters have revealed that these measures are not being universally and energetically applied. In view of the known mine capacity, it is expected that this review will also reflect a determination to reach, at an early date, the maximum production of coal consistent with the existing conditions of mines and equipment.

4. A report will be submitted to this Headquarters by 1200 hours, 26 January 1946, giving, by mining districts, the status of fulfillment of each of the following features of the program and the dates on which each will be fully effective at the mines:

a. Increased food nations for miners and their families.

b. Increased pay for miners.

c. Increased clothing distribution to miners.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
