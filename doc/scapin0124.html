GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 132 (10 Oct 45) ESS

(SCAPIN-124)

12 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Freezing Accounts of Toshio Kamiyama

1. You will immediately freeze by telegraph 11 accounts of Toshio Kamiyama, former Assistant Secretary, Accounts Section ▲orean Government, including his accounts with Fukuoka Branch, Yasuda Bank.

2. You will immediately furnish to the Financial Divvision, Economic & Scientific Section, this Headquarters, bank statements of all the above accounts from August 1, 1945 to date of freezing which will include all countries made on such accounts. You will further furnish detailed descriptions and explanation of each entry.

3. You will report immediately to this Headquarters in the action taken to comply with these requirements, and the date the required statements, descriptions and explanations will be submitted .

4. An acknowledgement of the receipt of this memorandum is desired.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. Allen
Colonel, A. G. D.
Asst Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 124)

WFC/dlj

11 October 1945

1. Reference Radio TFY MG Fin 124 from CG USAFIK to CINCAFPAC Adv, dated 5 October 1945: Subject "Freezing of accounts of Toshio Kamiyama and reimbursement of Korean."

2. Reference telegram indicates illegal transfer of funds from Korea to Japan and is in contravention of our radio CA-52133 and unnumbered radio 2 October relative to prohibition of external financial communications. Action is taken to counteract the illegal transfer and reimburse the Korean government.

3. Lt. Col. Coudray and Mr. Alk, Finance Division, concur. No nonconcurrences.

W. F. COUDRAY
