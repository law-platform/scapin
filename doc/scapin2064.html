MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2064)

JRA/JCB/WNR/HJR/fmb

6 December 1949

1. Reference is SCAPIN 407, dated 8 December 1945, subject: Application of Memorandum Regarding Elimination of War Profits and Reorganization of National Finance (AG 121.7, 24 Nov 45). This SCAPIN was issued to clarify SCAPIN 337, subject: Elimination of War profits in Reorganization of National Finances. SCAPIN 337 has been rescinded by SCAPIN 2022 which does not establish any new provisions.

2. Referenced SCAPIN, in addition to clarifying SCAPIN 337, contains the statement "Special Banks and The Deposit Fund Section of the Money and Banking Bureau of the Ministry of Finance may continue their normal credit functions insofar as they do not conflict with other portions of the subject Memorandum and provided that they shall not be permitted to issue bonds, debentures, or any other securities directly or indirectly guaranteed by the government, except as authorized by this Headquarters."

3. Finance Law, Law No. 34 of 1947, provides that all government borrowing shall be authorized in the budget. Any liability which the government assumes must be so authorized through Diet approval. Consequently, no government commitments may be made of a nature described in paragraph 2 above unless they receive the approval of Headquarters.

4. On the basis of the Finance Law, and the fact that the basic SCAPIN which it supplemented has been cancelled, SCAPIN 407 is no longer considered necessary and is therefore rescinded by the basic letter to the Ministry of Finance.

5. Mr. Harrison, ESS/JF/PF, concurs.

Mr. H. J. Robinson, 57-8810

J. R. A.
