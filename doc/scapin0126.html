GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091 (12 Oct 45) GB

(SCAPIN - 126)

12 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Liaison Committee (Tokyo) for the Imperial Japanese Army and Navy.
SUBJECT :Institution for War Records Investigation.

It is desired that the Imperial Japanese War Ministry organize a bureau for investigations concerned with the compilation of sufficient data to obtain a complete historical war record. Sufficient personnel should be retained to allow prompt liaison in all matters concerned with military operations. The organization of this bureau will not operate to delay the demobilization of Japanese Armed Forces as set forth in Japanese submission of 2 October 1945 and approved by Memorandum of 14 October 1945. After 1 December 1945 the personnel of this bureau will be transferred to the Ministry of Demobilization and continue its original function.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
