GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG383.7 (5 Nov 45) CIS

(SCAPIN-239)

5 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of IZUMI, Kiichi, 1st Lt., Japanese Army

The Imperial Japanese Government will up apprehend IZUMI, Kiichi, 1st lieutenant, assigned to the 34 Army in Korea during September, 1945 and formerly attached to Japanese Army Headquarters in Hankow. Lieutenant IZUMI will be delivered to the authorities of the Amori Prison Camp for incarceration.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
