IN THE CLEAR
OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS
SIGNAL CORPS MESSAGE

(SCAPIN -56)

GC-O RWM/rs 25 September 1945

FROM :SCAP
TO :IMPERIAL JAPANESE MINISTRY OF THE NAVY
INF :CINCPAC
COM FIFTH FLEET
COM SEVENTH FLEET
COMINOH
CTF 51
CTF 56

YOUR NAN DOG FIFTEEN OF TWO ONE SEPTEMBER FOUR FIVE REFERS PD (ZAX 6177) JAPANESE NAVAL VESSELS EMPLOYED AS TRANSPORTS TO RETURN NATIONALS TO THE JAPANESE HOME ISLANDS WILL NOT R▲T NOT FLY THE JAPANESE NAVAL NATIONAL ENSIGN PAREN WITH RAYS PAREN BUT MAY FLY THE JAPANESE MERCHANT ENSIGN PAREN WITHOUT RAYS PAREN PLUS INTERNATIONAL EASY WITH TRIANGULAR OUT OUT FROM END PD OFFICERS AND MEN COMPRISING CREWS OF THESE VESSELS MAY WERE NAVAL UNIFORMS ONLY UNTIL SUCH TIME AS OTHER CLOTHING IS AVAILABLE PD SUCH OTHER CLOTHING WILL BE OBTAINED AT THE EARLIEST POSSIBLE TIME

OFFICIAL:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, US Army,
Adjutant General

APPROVED:

/s/W. E. Chambers
/t/W. E. CHAMBERS
Brigadier General, G. S. C.
Acting Asst. Chief of Staff, G-3
