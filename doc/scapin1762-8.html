GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.23 (14 Aug 48) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1762/8)

APO 500

9 August 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service via Osaka-Colombo Circuit.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.23 (14 Apr 48)CCS, SCAPIN 1962/4, dated 14 April 1943, subject same as above.

2. So much of inclosure 1, SCAPIN 1762/4, pertaining to word charges and division of revenue for full rate and British Commonwealth Government radiotelegrams destined to Aegean Islands is amended to read:

FULL RATE Aegean Islands

TOTAL RATE PENCE 40

JAPAN TERMINAL PENCE 4

UK ABD BEYOND PENCE 9

BALANCE EQUALLY DIVIDED PENCE 27

BURITHISH COMMONWEALTH GOVT.

TOTAL RATE PENCE 24

JAPAN TERMINAL PENCE 4

UK ABD BEYOND PENCE 6 1/2

BALANCE EQUALLY DIVIDED PENCE 13 1/2

3. Direct communication within the scope of this memorandum is authorized between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, the General Accounting Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers or the British Transfer Agent, and the Ministry of Communications.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
