GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 560 (22 Mar 46) GC

(SCAPIN-832)

APO 500

22 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Use of Repatriation Vessels for Cargo.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 563.5 (1 Mar. 46) GC, (Scapen-780), dated 1 March 1946, subject: “Cargo Shipped on Repatriation Vessels.”

2. Cargo space on regularly assigned repatriation ships may be allocated by SCAJAP for essential cargo under the following conditions:

a. When suitable cargo ships are not available.

b. When the amount of cargo is so small that the scheduling of a cargo ship therefor is not warranted.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government will continue to arrange with SCAJAP through CMMC for assignment of the necessary space for shipping authorized cargoes.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General
