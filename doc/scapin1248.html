GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 451 (3 Oct 46) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1248)

APO 500

3 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Custody and Storage of Looted Automobiles.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, A. G. 386, 6 (25 Jul 46) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1083, 25 July 1946, subject,“Custody, Shipment, and Storage of Looted Property” from General Headquarters. Supreme Comander for the Allied Powers.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to impound and place in protective custody all motor vehicles which have been identified as looted property, whether or not thay are in use by members of the Imperial Household, the Imperial Japanese Government or private individuals and orgarizations.

3. All the automobiles impounded as a result of this directive will be placed in protective storage immediately in the warehouses which the Imperial Japanese Government has established for this purpose.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government will report to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, that the action directed in this Memorandum has been taken within 20 days from the date of this Memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
