4 June 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Reactivation of Air Navigational Facilities.

1, Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 360.31 (23 Dec 45) GD, SCAPIN 484, subject, "Reactivation of Air Navigational Facilities," 23 December 1945.

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 360.31 (23 Dec 45)GD, SCAPIN 484/1, subject, "Reactivation of Air Navigational Facilities," l4 February 1949.

2. Reference memoranda 1a and 1b are rescinded effective 1 April 1949.

3. The Japanese Government is hereby directed, effective 1 April 1949, to maintain and operate all air navigational aids in Japan except the airways light lines, and those aids presently operated and maintained by the occupation forces.

4. A procurement demand will be issued to cover expenditures incurred by the Japanese Government in complying with this SCAPIN from 1 April 1949 to 31 March 1950.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
