OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 383.6 (30 Sep 45) AGKD

(SCAPIN - 75)

APO 500

30 September 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Effects of Deceased Prisoners of War and Civilian Internees.

1. Paragraph 30, Chapter 4, page 10, "Regulations on the War Prisoners," issued by War Prisoners Intelligence Bureau, states as follows:

"Articles and money of the dead prisoners will be sent to the Prisoners Information Bureau from the Army Troops in charge, the governmental office, the hospital or the dressing station, but articles, the unsuitable for reserve will be sold and the cash will be sent".

2. Report is desired without delay as to what action has been taken by the Imperial Japanese Government to secure the effects of every kink, character and description, of all deceased prisoners of war from every camp.

a. The term "effects," as it pertains to deceased prisoners of war, will include all personal property and chattels, wearing apparel, papers, documents, letters, notebooks, photographs, albums, diaries and articles of probably sentimental value, all funds or evidence of funds, such as credits for money held by or on deposit with any agency or representative of Imperial Japanese Government, and all sums due but not paid to the prisoner of war for labor performed or services rendered prior to the death of such prisoner of war.

3. In the event no action has been taken to secure such effects, it is desired that immediate action be taken to do so, and that such effects, when secured, be marked to show the name, rank, serial number effects, nationality, and branch of service, and the name or other designation of the camp where the deceased prisoner of war was confined.

a. When the foregoing has been accomplished the effects, marked as directed, will be delivered to the headquarters of the major Allied military force occupying the zone or district where the effects were secured.

b. Report will be made to this office when delivery of such effects is made to the headquarters of the appropriate Allied occupying force. A roster will be attached to this report, showing the name, rank, serial number, nationality and branch of service of each deceased prisoner of war and the effects belonging to each as delivered to the appropriate Allied occupation force headquarters.

4. Identical information is desired with regard to the effects of deceased civilian internees. However, it is desired that the effects of civilian internees be held until notification is received from this office as to their disposition.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ Harold Fair.
/t/ HAROLD FAIR,
Lt. Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General.
