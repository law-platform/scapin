GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.3 (22 Apr 48) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1880/3)

APO 500

18 April 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Restoration of Legal Rights and Interests to United Nations Nationals

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.3 (22 Apr 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1880, 22 April 1948, subject, "Procedures for Restoration of Property in Japan to Nationals of the United Nations," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The Japanese Government is hereby directed to employ the following basis in all restorations under the terms of reference 1 above:

a. Restoration of legal rights and interests will be made as they existed on the date of wrongful transfer or seizure within the meaning of reference 1 above. The date of the outbreak of hostilities between the government of the United Nations national claiming restoration and the government of Japan will be considered to be such date of wrongful transfer or seizure unless the facts clearly indicate that a later date is applicable.

b. Restoration of physical property will continue to be made as it exists on the date of restoration, as specified in reference 1 above.

c. All restorations will be carried out in conformity with existing Japanese Law.

d. Directives issued by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers containing special procedures for restoration of specific types of property will take precedence over this directive.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
