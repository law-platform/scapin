GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 132 (30 Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 678)

APO 500

30 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Release from Blocked Bank Account of Doitsu Senryo Gomei Kaisha.

1. Reference is made to application of Doitsu Senryo Gomei Kaisha, dated 21 January 1946, addressed to Mr. Keizo Shibusawa, Finance Minister, for authority to transfer ¥27,784.50 from its blocked bank account to the bank account of Tokyo Marine and Fire Insurance Co. in the same bank for the purpose of paying insurance premiums due on the property of Doitsu Senryo Gomei Kaisha.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to release from the terms of the Ordinance of the Ministry of Finance on the Preservation of the Property of the Specified Powers, dated 20 September 1945, the sum of ¥27,784.50 in blocked bank account in Teikoku Bank, Marunouchi Branch, for purpose of paying insurance premiums due on certain properties of such concern.

3. This memorandum shall not be construed to release the account from the blocking provisions of Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, this Headquarters, file AG 121.7 (24 Nov 45) ESS/FI, dated 24 November 1945, subject: "Elimination of War Profits and Reorganization of National Finances," or from the blocking provisions of any other memorandum issued by this Headquarters, except as directed in paragraph 2 above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
