GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (22 Feb 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 764)

APO 500

22 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Unreported Precious Metals

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, this Headquarters, file AG 123 (6 Oct 45) ESS/FI, dated October 1945, subject: "Reports of Foreign Exchange Assets and Related Matters."

2. This Headquarters has been notified that unreported precious metals are stored in the Sendai Railroad Warehouses, Sendai City, Miyagi-Ken.

3. You are directed to investigate this matter and report the circumstances, names of responsible officials and the reason for failure to comply with the directions of the above mentioned Memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General
