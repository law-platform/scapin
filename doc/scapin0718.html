GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 464.8 (6 Feb 46) ESS/IE

(SCAPIN - 718)

APO 500

6 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Shipment of Mulberry Seedlings to China

1. It is directed that the necessary action be taken at once to effect the shipment to China of the following listed Mulberry Seedlings:

Kenmochi 1,000,000 pcs.

Shimanouchi 300,000 pcs.

Misc. Variety 500,000 pcs.

Total 1,800,000 pcs.

2. The shipment will be consigned to the Chinese Sericulture and Silk Company, care of Chinese Customs, Port of Shanghai.

3. The assembly, packing and transportation arrangements will be completed and consignment delivered to the Port of Shipment as soon as is practicable. This Headquarters will be advised of the date of loading, name of ship, sailing date and Port of Shipment. A copy of the Ocean Bill of lading will be attached to the memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
for /t/ B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 718)

WFM/HJZ/wec

28 January 1946

1. Reference is made to letter to the Supreme Commander from Mr. Chen Yun, Representative of Dr. T. V. Soong, dated 25 January 1946.

2. C.L.O. Memorandum Number 1329 (2.24) dated 26 January 1946, states that a total of 2,210,000 seedlings are now available for export rom Japan. Natural Resources Section states (NR464.8 - 21 Dec 45) there are an additional 300,000 seedlings of the Shimanouchi variety making a total of 2,510,000 seedlings. Of the above total, 710,000 seedlings are now being shipped to Korea. This directive instructs the IJG to ship the remaining amount to China. Total weight will be about 100 tons, involving approximately 700 tons of cubic freight space.

3. Concurrences:

Natural Resources Section - Capt. Belcher

PC & R Division - Capt. Egekvist

G-4 - Supplies - Lt. Col. Davis

G-4 - Transportation - Col. Van Horne

R. A. M.
