GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004 (26 Jun 50) FIB

(SCAPIN-2106)

APO 500

26 June 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
Subject :Operation of Internal Airline Within Japan

1. Reference Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 004 (26 Jun 50) FIB, SCAPIN 2105, 26 June 1950, subject: Entry Requirements and Business Activities in Japan.

2. This memorandum is transmitted for the purpose of informing the Japanese Government of the conditions under which the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers will concur in the issuance of a license to operate an internal airline within Japan. Such conditions are:

a. Authorization will be limited to one airline which may be designated by or organized and financed by interested civil airlines which were licensed and operating into Japan subsequent to 2 September 1945 and prior to 1 January 1950.

b. Licensee shall be subject to all laws and regulations of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, the Commanding General, Far East Air Forces and the Japanese Government.

c. This license shall not in any way waive any other validation, permission or other disposition which may be required under other laws and regulations in regard to the acquisition of materials, facilities or services, or other transactions or actions.

d. Licensee, at all times, shall maintain complete and accurate records of the subject business activity, and such records shall be readily accessible and subject to inspection by duly authorized representatives of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and of the Japanese Government. The facilities and premises shall be open at all reasonable times for inspection by duly authorized representatives of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Japanese Government.

e. The licensee shall make a report to the Foreign Investment Commission of the Japanese Government on commencing or closing the subject business activity.

f. Licensee will not conduct operations from any airfield in Japan except after prior application shall have been made to and express approval shall have been granted by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for use of such airfield.

g. Contracts entered into in Japan by the licensee for the transport of mail, freight, and passengers into and from Japan may be expressed in, and licensee may receive in payment therefor, currencies other than Japanese yen, subject to such regulations respecting such currencies as may be applicable. Rates for the transport of such mail, freight and passengers shall be fixed by the Japanese Government subject to approval by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.Bush
Brigadier General, USA,
Adjutant General.
