GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (4 Dec 46) CCS

(SCAPIN-1379)

APO 500

4 December 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Control of Radio Communications.

1． Reference is made to Memoranda to the Imperial Japanese Government, subject as above:

File AG 676.3 (29 Aug 46)CCS (SCAPIN 1166 ) dated 29 August 1946

File AG 676.3 (20 Sep 46)CCS (SCAPIN 1222) dated 20 September 1946

File AG 676.3 (22 Oct 46)CCS (SCAPIN 1283) dated 22 October 1946

File AG 676.3 (7 Nov 46)CCS (SCAPIN 1323) dated 7 November 1946

2. The Japanese Ministry of Communications has proposed further change to the Master List of Frequencies assigned for use by the Japanese Government as published and amended in the reference memoranda. Additions, deletions and changes subsequent to publication of the last amendment to Master list of Frequencies assigned for use by the Japanese Government are approved and listed in Inclosure 1, to be offensive 30 November 1946.

3. Paragraph 2b4, Inclosure 2 of Memorandum file AG 676.3 (29 Aug 46)CCS, (SCAPIN 1166) dated 29 August 1946, subject: Control of Radio Communications, as amended, is further amended as follows:

Type of Emission Carrier Free. kc Maximum Band Width of Emission, kc

A3 550-1500 10,000

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

1 Incl

Fourth Amendment to Master List of Frequencies Assigned for use by the Japanese Government
