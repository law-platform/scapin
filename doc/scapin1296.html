GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 332.1 (26 Oct 46) GA

(SCAPIN - 1296)

APO 500

26 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Hoisting of National Flag.

1. There is no objection to the display of the Japanese national flag on 3 November 1946 as requested by C.L.O. No. 5573 (PP-K) dated 22 October 1946, subject as above.

2. Occupation troops will be informed in this matter.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
