(SCAPIN - 1338)

APO 500

15 November 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Manufacture of Flame Signal Fuzes and Signal Detonators for Government Railways

1. Reference is letter CLO 5611 (ECI), 24 Oct. 46, subject: Application of Kanto Denki Kogyo K.K. for permission to Manufacture Flame Signal Fuses and Signal Detonators for Government Railways.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government will submit to the Commanding General, Eighth Army a plan for manufacture of these signals and the controls necessary to insure their proper manufacture.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
for /t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1338)

WFM/JZR/EWS/mc

30 October 46

1. Letter from Imperial Japanese Government, CLO 5611, (ECI), 24 Oct 46, subject: "Application of Kanto Denki Kogyo K. K. for Permission to Manufacture Flame Signal Fuses and Detonators for Japanese Railways", forwards request to manufacture 400,000 signal fuses and signal detonators for use on government railways.

2. Manufacture of these signal flares and detonators is necessary for the safe operation of Japanese railways.

3. This directive will authorize the Japanese to manufacture 400,000 pieces of flame signal fuses and detonators. The Japanese are directed to submit to the Commanding General, Eighth Army, plans for manufacture and control of the production of these flares and detonators.

4. It has been the policy for the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers to authorize the Imperial Japanese Government to manufacture the total quantities of industrial explosives. The Eighth U.S. Army grants the actual permission for the individual manufacturers to produce explosives.

5. Routine military surveillance will be required.

6. Verbal concurrence - 3rd MRS. Lt. Col. Lyons, Phone Octogon 2-0944.

J. Z. R.
