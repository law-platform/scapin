GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.3 (21 Nov 47) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1228/6)

APO 500

21 November 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Revised Plan for Receiving Payments Due American Carriers for Messages and Radiotelephone Calls Filed by United States Military and Accredited Civilian Personnel for Transmission to Or Through the United States, Its Territories and Possessions, Prepaid in Japan.

1. Reference is made to memoranda for the Japanese Government as follows:

a. File AG 311.3 (24 Sep 46) CCS, (SCAPIN 1228), dated 24 September 1946, subject as above.

b. File AG 311.3 (17 Jan 47) CCS, (SCAPIN 1228/1), dated 17 January 1947, subject as above.

c. File AG 311.3 (28 Mar 47) CCS, (SCAPIN 1228/2), dated 28 March 1947, subject as above.

d. File AG 311.3 (12 Jun 47) CCS, (SCAPIN 1228/4), dated 12 June 1947, subject as above.

e. File AG 311.3 (28 Aug 47) CCS, (SCAPIN 1228/5), dated 28 August 1947, subject as above.

2. Effective immediately, Inclosure 1 of this memorandum replaces Inclosure 1 of SCAPIN 1228/4. Concurrently, SCAPIN 1228/5 is rescinded.

3. Direct communication between Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Ministry of Communications, within the scope of this memorandum, is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

21 November 1947

MEMORANDUM.

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number SCAPIN (1228/6), file AG 311.3 (21 Nov 47) CCS, this Headquarters, dated 21 November 47, subject: "Revised Plan for Receiving Payments Due American Carriers for Messages and Radio Telephone Calls Filed by United States Military and Accredited Civilian Personnel for Transmission to or Through the United States, its Territories and Possessions, Prepaid in Japan".

1. With reference to subject Memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The subject directive covers desired changes in Inclosure 1 of SCAPIN 1228/4 and as amended by SCAPIN 1228/5, with is an list showing public offices of the Ministry of Communications where radio telegraph messages or radio telephone calls may be filed for transmission, the types and classes of communications service offered, and the amount of Military Payment Certificates authorized for change-making purposes.

3. The changes made by reference memorandum cover the authorization of additional public offices or points for acceptance of SCM and/or EFM telegraph messages and telephone calls, and the establishment for each added public office the amount of Military Payment Certificates authorized for change-making purposes.

4. Public offices of the Ministry of Communications authorized to accept messages and radio telephone calls are made a matter of official record in order that surveillance may be given to the Military Payment Certificates which come into the hands of the Japanese, and the handling of messages to and from the public offices which are named.

5. The manner of execution of surveillance carried on in connection with Inclosure 1, original SCAPIN 1228, and as amended by SCAPIN 1228/1, will be equally and similarly applicable to the public offices and services named in the reference directive.

6. Command instructions pertaining to executing surveillance are being released simultaneously with this memorandum.

M. F. NOYLS

Lt Col, AGD

Asst Adj Gen

DISTRIBUTION

Same as (SCAPIN 1228/6)
