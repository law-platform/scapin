GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.7 (28 Nov 51) CPC/OD

(SCAPIN-2186)

APO 500

6 December 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Custody of German Property and Operation of the Yoga-cho Warehouse at Setagaya-ku.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 091.112 (13 Sep 45)MG, SCAPIN 26, 13 September 1945, subject, "Protection of Allied and Axis Property," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.7 (13 Oct 49)CPC/PLD, SCAPIN 2051, 13 October 1949, subject, "Notification that the United States, United Kingdom and France are Owners of Certain Categories of Former German Property in Japan," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, as amended

c. Memorandum for the Civil Property Division, Reparations Agency, Japanese Government, file 386.7 (28 Nov 51)CPC/OD, 28 November 1951, subject, "Custody of German Property," from Civil Property Custodian, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Effective with the close of business 15 December 1951 the operation of the Yogo-cho German property warehouse at Setagaya-ku Tokyo will become the responsibility of the Japanese Government as outlined in preliminary information supplied to the Civil Property Division, Reparations Agency in memorandum referenced in paragraph 1c above.

3. In order to effect the transfer to a designated agency of the Japanese Government of the German property coming under the provisions of references 1a and b above, and stored in the warehouse, the following procedures will be carried out:

a. The agency designated to operate the warehouse will supply the necessary personnel to take over the administration and recordkeeping functions of the warehouse office and sufficient personnel to complete a joint inventory of the property remaining in the warehouse with the custodial personnel currently assigned the operation of the warehouse. This joint inventory will be undertaken commencing 10 December 1951 and should be completed on or before 15 January

b. On completion of the joint inventory by individual account, officials of the designated agency will sign for each account, thus signifying the correctness of each inventory and formally accepting full custodial responsibility for the property.

c. The designated agency is advised to adopt substantially the same bookkeeping system, with pertinent inventory, receipt, transfer and release forms, as is currently in use by the Office of the Civil Property Custodian. For this purpose the reproduction and use of the forms currently in use are hereby authorized.

4. The property so inventoried will be disposed of in accordance with specific directives to be issued from time to time by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers or, upon the coming into force of the Pease Treaty, in accordance with policies laid down by the Tri-Power Commission composed of representatives of the United States, United Kingdom and France.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C.C.B. WARDEN
Colonel, AGC
Adjutant General
