GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 003 (31 Mar 48) ESS/FI

SCAPIN 1873

APO 500

31 March 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
SUBJECT :New Design Five Sen Bank of Japan Note

1. Reference is Central Liaison and Coordination Office Memorandum No. 868 (LO 1364 FI/TR) 22 March 1948, subject: Printing of New Design Bank of Japan Five Sen Note, with inclosure of Ministry of Finance Memorandum LO 1364(FI/TR) 19 March 1948 on the same subject.

2. Approval is granted to issue the new design five sen notes in conformity with the printing schedule set forth in paragraph 2 of reference memorandum. Further authorization for issuance of five sen notes to conform to the printing schedule up to the approval limit of 400,000,000 pieces for the calendar year 1948 will not be necessary.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1873)

WKL/JCS/ah

25 March 1948

1. SCAPIN-1857, AG 003 (12 Feb 48) ESS/FI, subject: New Design Five Sen Notes authorized printing of a new design 5 sen note up to a maximum of 400,000,000 pieces, value ¥20,000,000 during the calendar year 1948.

2. CLCO Memorandum No. 868 (LO 1364 FI/TR) 22 March 1948 forwarded request from Ministry of Finance to issue subject notes in conformity with the present printing schedule.

3. Printing schedule calls for 60,000,000 pieces of value 3 million yen by the end of March 1948. Proposed Memorandum authorizes issuance in conformity with this printing schedule and also authorizes issuance of the balance up to the approved limit without further approval being required.

4. The interests of Korea are not concerned.

5. No concurrence necessary.

W. K. L.
