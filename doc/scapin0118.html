GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (10 Oct 45) SC

(SCAPIN - 118)

APO 500

11 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Continuation of Ionosphere Measurements and Associated Research in Japan.

1. It is desired that systematic observation of the ionosphere over Japan, and associated research on the propagation of radio waves be continued.

2. A preliminary survey of the status of this work at the termination of the war has been made, and the following list of actions and reforms has been prepared for initial execution.

a. The existing "Physical Institute on Radio Waves" (Dempa-butsuri Kenkyujo) under the Department of Education (located at Tanegawa, Kaminogocho, Setagaya-ku, Tokyo) should from the core of the postwar nonmilitary organization to conduct the work.

b. These research facilities, equipments, and necessary personnel of Naval and Military organizations hitherto contributing to the overall wartime program of work on radio wave propagation, should be transferred to the new nonmilitary organization above.

c. Additional laboratory space should be made available, presumably from military sources, to compensate not only for that space lost as a result of the fire raids, but also to provide for any not increase in size of the final organization which results from consolidation.

d. Hourly virtual height versus frequency measurements (p'f measurements) should commence again in the Tokyo area at the earliest possible date. This work should conform internationally adopted procedures for making and interpreting ionospheric observations now in general use among the Allied Powers.

e. To replace these overseas observing stations no longer under Japanese control, and to form the basis for a permanent peacetime program of routine ionospheric observation, plans should be made for eventual p'f station in the regions of:

(1) Asahigawa(Hokkaido)

(2) Akita -Sendai (Northern Honshu)

(3) Kagoshima (Southern Kyushu)

These now stations should be operated on a continuous basis of hourly measurements, and should conform to those observational procedures prescribed in paragraph "d" above.

f. Steps should be taken to assure that separate magnetic and other geophysical observation and research programs, as well as work on solar physics, which contribute essential data to the ionospheric research, are continued.

3. Tabulation summaries by months of current observations in the internationally adopted standard forms, and copies of now technical reports will be supplied on monthly basis to the Chief Signal Officer of this command for transmittal to interested agencies of the Allied Powers. The necessary headings, labels and abstracts will be in English language.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/S/ H. W. Allen,
/t/ H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
