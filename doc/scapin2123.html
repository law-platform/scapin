GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.77 (18 Sep 50) CCS

(SCAPIN-2123)

APO 500

18 September 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Provision of Broadcasting Facilities.

In confirmation of oral instructions to the Radio Regulatory Commission, it is directed that facilities of the Broadcasting Corporation of Japan be made available for foreign language broadcasting. Expenses in connection with such foreign language broadcasting will be covered by contracts with the Japan Logistical Command.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General

see reverse for Memo of General Application

GENERAL HEADQUATERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

18 September 1950

MEMORANDUM:

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number SCAPIN 2123, file AG 000.77 (18 Sep 50)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 18 Sep 50, subject: Provisions of Broadcasting Facilities

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The purpose of the subject directive is to authorize the Japanese Government end the Broadcasting Corporation of Japan to make available broadcasting facilities of the Broadcasting Corporation of Japan for foreign language broadcasting.

3. The directive will expedite arrangements for use sf these broadcasting facilities.

4. The subject directive provides that all expenses in connection with such foreign language broadcasting will be paid for by the U.S. Government after contracts have been negotiated and entered into with the Japan Logistical Command.

A J REHE

Lt Col, AGC

Asst Adj Gen
