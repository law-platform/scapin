GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (24 Apr 47) LS-R

(SCAPIN - 1630)

APO 500

24 April 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Clarification of Status of Former Suspected War Criminal.

1. The following named Japanese indicated below is released from suspicion as a Suspected War Criminal, is stricken from the Indictment of the Military Tribunal for the Far East, and henceforth will be returned to his former status.

NAME RANK

NAGANO, Osami Admiral

2. All property of above designated individual controlled, impounded, or blocked under Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386.7 (23 Apr 46)CPC SCAPIN 897, subject: Control, Impounding and Blocking of Property of Designated Individuals, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, is hereby directed to be released from the provisions of said Memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
