GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 293 (7 Dec 49) GA

(SCAPIN-2132)

APO 500

30 November 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Payment of All Expenses Involved in the Return of Remains of Japanese Ex-military Personnel to Japan from the Ryukyu Islands.

1. Effective at once and including payment for services rendered to date, the Japanese Government will be responsible for any and all expenses involved in the exhumation, cremation, transportation and delivery to proper agencies in Japan of the remains of Japanese ex-military personnel now interred in the Ryukyu Islands.

2. Reimbursement for services rendered in the Ryukyu Islands will be by deposit of Japanese yen with the Bank of Tokyo of the brunches to the account of the Ryukyus Counterpart Fund. Deposit will be based on properly presented statements of the Military Government of the Ryukyu Islands and will be on the basis of three (3) Japanese yen for one (1) Ryukyuan "B" yen.

3. The Japanese Government will assume primary responsibility for the program. Requests for return of remains will be presented to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and will contain sufficient information upon which the Military Governor of the Ryukyu Islands may be able to carry out that part of the program which must be performed within the Ryukyu Islands.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
