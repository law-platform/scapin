GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 451 (25 Oct 46) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1292)

APO 500

25 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Inventory of Looted Vehicles

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 451 (3 Oct 46) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1248, 3 October 1946, subject: “Custody and Storage of Looted Automobiles,” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. 2. It is directed that physical inventory of each looted vehicle impounded under reference memorandum be made and that a report of such inventory be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers by 23 November 1946. The inventory will contain the following information regarding the vehicles impounded in each warehouse:

a. Model, number of doors and cylinders b. Make c. Year of Model d. Color e. Engine number f. Body number g. Country from which the vehicle was removed h. Photograph (side shot) in triplicate, 8" x 10".

3. The information required in paragraph 2 above will be submitted on every looted vehicle which is taken into custody subsequent to the date of this directive.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
