GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (31 Dec 48) PM

(SCAPIN - 1969)

APO 500

4 February 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Japanese Criminals from Korea.

1. References, a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 014.33 (5 Jun 46) GC, subject, "Repatriation of Japanese Criminals from Korea," SCAPIN 1407-A, 5 June 1946.

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 014.33 (7 Jan 47) GC, same subject as reference a, SCAPIN 1442, 7 January 1947.

2. The reports as required by paragraph 4b, reference a, and paragraph 3d, reference b, are discontinued.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
