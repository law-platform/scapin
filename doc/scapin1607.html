AG 000.5 (12 Apr 47) LS-R

(SCAPIN - 1607)

APO 500

12 April 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

The following namec* Japanese will te apprehended as Suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, EIGHTH Army, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest possible date.

NAME

RANK OR TITLE

PERTINENT DATA

AKABANE, (FNU)

Sgt or WO

OC of PW Escort aboard "DAI NICHI MARU" enroute Singapore to Moji, 28 Oct 42 - 27 Nov 42.

Later posted to Amano Butai in South Burma, Jun 1943.

ITO, Yoshio (Tomio)

A/Pvt

Medical Corps. Stationed at hospital, 10 Kikometre Post, KRA Railway, Siam.

Believed to be in Kuala Lumpur, Malaya during 44.

Was cook for Capt ORI, Masami, Medical Off.

KANNO, Masami

Civ

Guard, Tokyo Camp #11-D (later called 14-B), Nov 44 - 15 Aug 45.

Probable home address: Fukushima-Ken Iwaki-gun, Nakoso-machi, Seikita Minami-machi No. 21.

MITOMI, Hideo

Civ

Chief of Malaya Railway Bureau, KRA Railway, Siam.

NAKAMURA, Yoshitaro

WO

Kempei Tai, Miyazaki Detach, Babelthuap Palau Is, Sep 44.

OGAWA, Eichi

Col

OC, KRA Railway Unit, Sumatra Railway Corps

SHICHINO, Shinobu

Sgt (Medic)

Mitsushima PW Camp, 1 Jan 43 - 10 Jun 43,

TAKAHAGI, Aragi

Civ

Kinjohan Company, KRA Railway, Siam.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
