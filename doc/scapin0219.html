GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 686.3 (31 Oct 45) CCS

(SCAPIN - 219)

APO 500

31 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Japanese Military Radio Stations.

1. It is desired that a report of all radio frequencies listed by circuits being used by the Japanese Army and Navy be forwarded to this headquarters. This report will include the following information in connection with each frequency:

a. Location of transmitting station

b. Location of receiving station or stations

c. Power transmitter.

d. Type of emission

e. Type of transmitting antenna.

2. Any question concerning the technical information desired may be referred to the Chief of the Civil Communications Section of this headquarters.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
