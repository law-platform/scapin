22 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

The following named Japanese will be apprehended as Suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, XI Corps, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest practicable date.

NAME: RANK OR TITLE: DATE, CAMP, ADDRESS, OR ASSIGNMENT:

HARASHIMA, Kanji Captain Probable home address: Kuramochi-Kata, Shobumachi, Minami-Saitama-Gun, Saitama

HAYASHI, Minoru 1st Lt. (Navy) Probable home address: Tohaku, Fujimi-Cho, Higashi-Yashiro-Gun, Yamanashi.

INUZUKA, Koreshige Capt. (Navy) CO of Naval Garrison Unit, P. I. Served under Vice Admiral Oka, Arata, Commander, 3rd Southern Task Force. Returned to Japan Sept 1944. Probable home address: Saga Prefecture, Kyushu.

ITO, Kikuji Lt. Col. Probable home address: 630 Kyodo-Machi, Setagaya-Ku, Tokyo.

IWATAKA, Kenji Capt. (Navy) Graduate of Naval Academy. Was stationed at Truk Is. Naval Base. Former C.O., 103rd Naval Constr. Unit, Marila, P. I. Was also C.O. Oal Base, Nagasaki in 1944.

KATO, Masayoshi Private Was at Yokkaichi Camp (Nagoya Camp #5) during 1945.
