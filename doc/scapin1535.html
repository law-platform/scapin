GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 400.16 (20 Feb 47) ESS/FT

(SCAPIN-1535)

APO 500

20 February 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Marking of Export Articles

1. The Imperial Japaness Government is hereby directed to take immediates steps to insure that evry articles prepared for export after 15 days of receipt of this Directive, the immediate container thereof and the outside package will be marked, stamped, branded or labeled in legible English with the words “Made in Occupied Japan.”

2. All marking, stamping, branding or labeling shall be made in a conspicuous place and shall be as nealy indelible and permanent as the nature of the article will permit.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
