GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.23 (17 Sep 46) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1962)

APO 500

14 January 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Modification of Expeditionary Force Message Service Between Japan and All Points in the British Commonwealth.

1. Reference is made to memoranda for the Japanese Government as follows:

a. File AG 311.23 (17 Sep 46)CCS, SCAPIN 1206, dated 17 September 1946, subject: Establishment of Expeditionary Force and Sender Composition Message Service Between Japan and All Points in the British Commonwealth Over the Osaka-London Radiotelegraph Circuit.

b. File AG311.23 (12 Aug 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1762, dated 12 August 1947, subject: Osaka-Colombo Radiotelegraph Circuit, as amended.

c. File AG 311.23(28 Feb 48)CCS, SCAPIN 1866, dated 28 February 1948, subject: Establishment and Operation of Direct Radiotelegraph Circuit Between Japan and Hong Kong, as amended.

2. Effective immediately, the provisions of the reference memoranda pertaining to the acceptance and handling of Expeditionary Force Messages (EFM) between Japan and all points in the British Commonwealth are modified as follows:

a. Expeditionary Force Message (EFM) Service is authorized and available only between Japan and Australia.

b. This service is discontinued between Japan and all other points in the British Commonwealth.

3. EFM messages filed for transmission from Japan to Australia will continue to be handled in accordance with the provisions of reference memoranda above.

4. Direct communication between Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Ministry of Communications concerning all matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

(See reverse for Memo of General Application)

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

14 January 1949

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number SCAPIN 1962 file AG 311.23 (17 Sep 46)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 14 Jan 49 subject: Modification of Expeditionary Force Message Service Between Japan and All Points in the British Commonwealth

1. With reference to the subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. Notice has been received by Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers from the Civil Communications Advisor to United Kingdom Liaison Mission, Tokyo, to the effect that Expeditionary Force Message (EFM) Service between Japan and British Commonwealth areas, except Australia, is no longer admitted after 31 December 1948.

3. The subject memorandum directs the Japanese Ministry of Communications to modify instructions concerning the acceptance and handling of EFM messages and discontinue this service between Japan and all points in the British Commonwealth, except Australia, effective immediately.

A J REHE

Major, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
