GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 567 (28 Apr 49) CTS

(SCAPIN - 2032/1)

APO 500

29 August 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Japanese Port Operations and Charges for Port Facilities and Terminal Services.

1. References.

a. Japanese law No. 65, 14 May 1549.

b. SCAPIN 2032, 25 July 1549.

2. Reference 1a will be applicable to foreign flag vessels from 1 October 1949.

3. Reference 1c is amended by the addition to paragraph 3c of the following:

"Prices may be increased only when those effect hereunder are not sufficient to cover costs and a reasonable profit. Notice of proposed increases will be published and disseminated not less than 30 days prior to the date on which such proposed increases will become effective. Five copies of such notices will be furnished to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, attention of Chief, Civil Transportation Section."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
