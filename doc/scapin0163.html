GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123 (20 Oct 45) ESS

(SCAPIN - 163)

20 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Appointment of a Liquidator of Branches of:
1. Bank of Chosen
2. Bank of Taiwan
3. Banque Franco Japonaise
4. Chosen Colonization Bank
5. Deutsche Bank Fuer Ostasien

1. It is directed that the Bank of Japan be charged with the responsibility of liquidating the Japanese branches, offices and agencies of the subject institutions.

2. It is further directed that the Bank of Japan take appropriate action to prepare Balance Sheets (Statements of Condition) and other reports as of the close of business on 29 September 1945 with respect to each branch, office and agency of the subject institutions located in Japan. A separate memorandum specifying in detail the reports to be furnished will be transmitted.

3. For the purpose of compiling the above reports and schedules, access to the premises and records of the subject institutions will be granted to personnel accredited by the Bank of Japan. Representatives of this Headquarters will be available at each institution who may be consulted with regard to any problems arising in connection with the preparation of such report.

4. It is also directed that a Plan of Liquidation of the subject institutions be promptly submitted to this Headquarters. Until such a Plan of Liquidation has been submitted to, and approved by this Headquarters, the Bank of Japan is to assume no control over the affairs of any of the subject institutions by virtue of the issuance of this memorandum, except as provided in paragraphs 2 and 3.

5. The Bank of Japan will be subject to directives and instructions relating to policy and procedure which may be issued by this Headquarters.

6. The Imperial Japanese Government will take the necessary action to carry out the provisions of this memorandum.

7. An acknowledgement of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 163)

20 October 1945

JCC/kns

SUBJECT : Memorandum to Imperial Japanese Government regarding appointment of the Bank of Japan as liquidating Agent for Branches in Japan of:

1. Bank of Chosen

2. Bank of Taiwan

3. Banque Franco-Japonaise

4. Chosen Colonization Bank

5. Deutsche Bank fuer Ostasien

1. The subject memorandum directs the appointment of the Bank of Japan as official Liquidating Agent for the purpose stated.

2. Reasons for appointment of Bank of Japan:

a. The Bank of Japan, functioning under the supervision of the Ministry of Finance, operates as the principal financial agency of the Japanese Government.

3. The Bank of Japan as Liquidating Agent, will prepare such financial statements of the subject banks as this headquarters may require.

4. For this purpose the Bank of Japan will be granted access to necessary records, now under our guard, but only under the supervision of our representatives.

5. The Bank of Japan will prepare a Plan of Liquidation which must be approved by this headquarters before proceeding with any of the business of liquidation except the preparation of the financial statements required by us.

6. While it is provided that the Bank of Japan shall be held responsible for the proper conduct of the liquidation, the prerogative of this headquarters to direct policies and procedures is fully preserved.

C. F. T.
