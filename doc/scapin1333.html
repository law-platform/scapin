MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Application for Permission to Designate Niigata Plant of Toyo Gosei K.K. as an Ammonium Sulfate Manufacturing plant

1. References are:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 464.6 (17 May 46) ESS/IN, (SCAPIN 962), 17 May 46, subject: Production, Distribution and Use of Fertilizer.

b. Memorandum from Central Liaison Office, CLO 5389 (ECI), subject as above.

2. Application made in the memorandum referred to in paragraph 1 b, above is approved only as indicated below.

a. The following facilities of Toyo Gosei K. K. :

Facility Quantity

Water gas generator, 40,000 M3/D 1

Water gasholder, 4,000 M3 1

Blow gas holder, 1,000 M3 1

Desulfurization Equipment, 80,000 M3/D 1

CO-gas conversion unit, 80,000 M3/D 1

Mixed gas holder, 1,000 M3 1

C02-gas absorber equipment 80,000 M3/D 1

C02-gas compressor, 1,300 M3/hr 1

Mixed gas compressor, 1,000 M3/hr 3

Methanol synthesis set, 3 T/D 1

Ammonia synthesis set, 6 T/D 1

Methanol distillation plant, 2 T/D 1

Electric power receiving station, 3,000 KW 1

Machine shop 1

Testing laboratory 1

Waste heat boiler, 3 T/hr 3

Direct fired boiler, 1.5 T/hr 2

b. The following facilities of the adjacent plant of the Niigata Ryusan Co. may be purchased or otherwise acquired by agreement between said company and Toyo Gosei, for use only in conjunction with facilities approved in paragraph 2 a, above.

Facility Quantity

Saturator, 50 T/D 1

Centrifuge, 50 T/D 2

Building 1

Warehouse 1

c. The following facilities of the Nippon Chisso Company may be purchased or otherwise acquired by agreement between said company and Toyo Gosei to be used for recirculation of effluent gases from the ammonia synthesis set approved in paragraph 2 a, above.

Facility Quantity

Ammonia synthesis towers 2

Recirculation pumps, 50 M3/hr 2

3. The above items of equipment and their accessories will constitute the Niigata Plant of Toyo Gosei K.K. As defined herein, this plant is added to the approved nitrogen fertilizer plants listed in the memorandum referred to in paragraph 1 a, above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1333)

WFM/JZR/BVH/mc

31 October 1946

1. CLO 5389, (ECI) forwards an application for permission to manufacture ammonium sulfate, synthetic fertilizer by the Toyo Gosei Co., Niigata plant. The plant was originally designed to produce 20, 000 metric tons of ammonium sulfate with methanol as a by-product. During the final phase of the war the plant was not operated, since allocations of materials could not be obtained. Last spring the workers of this plant organized themselves into a union. This union requested the management of Toyo Gosei to invest funds to repair the facilities of the plant in order to produce fertilizer. The management declined, and desired to abandon the plant. The workers took over the plant by force and effected the necessary repairs in spite of objections by the managers. By June the plant was producing fertilizer, and has continued to do so since that time. In August members of this division visited the plant, to find both workers and management still in discord. However, shortly thereafter an agreement was negotiated, and management employee relations have steadily improved. At the time of the plant inspection, tentative plans were made to bring the plant into technically balanced operation. Because of slow action on the part of the management, final plans leading to the present approval have been delayed considerably.

2. Although the original design capacity of the plant was 20,000 tons per year, the present capacity of the plant is now only 7,000 tons per year. This limitation is primarily in the gas producting unit which can produce only enough gas to make 10,000 tons per year. The gas producer is considerably deteriorated, and in addition handicapped by the fact that hand operation is necessary at this time. The other main limitation is the ammonia synthesis system, now operating as a "one pass" system, which wastes enough gas to produce an additional 3000 tons of ammonium sulfate a year. Two compressors and two autoclaves lying idle at Nippon Chisso can be utilized to recirculate present waste gases; this change will bring the production up to 10, 000 tons per year. Approval of additional gas sources at this time is not justifiable since this would require the use of equipment which is still highly critical. However, the addition of this plant to the list of approved nitrogen fertilizer plants is consistent with the present SCAP fertilizer program since the plant is an existing facility now producing fertilizer.

3. The memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government lists specifically what equipment has been approved in this plant and what additional equipment may be acquired.

4. Concurrences - A/C - Mr. Bush

J. Z. R.
