GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 333.5 (1 Jul 48) GD

(SCAPIN - 1916)

APO 500

1 July 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Request by the Procurator- General for Access to Records of the Disposition of Captured Or Surrendered Japanese Supplies and Material.

1. Reference is made to Central Liaison and Coordination Office's letter CLCO Number 1851 (2PA), dated 20 May 1948, subject, "Request for List of Arms and Munitions' to Expedite Investigation on Disposal of Arms and Munitions".

2. The Procurator-General is authorized to examine those records which pertain to the disposition of captured or surrendered Japanese supplies and equipment as were maintained by the occupation forces, and which are now in the custody of units of the Eighth Army. Since these records indicate the quantities of supplies and materials returned to the Japanese Government by area, the Procurator-General should be able to obtain therefrom the information requested.

e. It is desired to emphasize that in many cases the quantities of supplies and materials received by the occupation forces were accounted for on inventories prepared at the time of the surrender by officers of the former Japanese armed forces, and that subsequent release of the major portion or all of these supplies and materials to the Japanese Government was often accomplished by using the same inventory.

4. The Japanese Government is also informed that in a few instances the field reports maintained by some units of the occupation forces were returned to the United States at the time those units were withdrawn from Japan. It is believed that ample data exists to serve the purpose the Procurator-General has in view; however, the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers will endeavor to have those records returned in the event any should prove vital.

5. The Japanese Government will furnish identification cards to the designated representatives of the Procurator-General. The identification cards will be printed in English and Japanese and will state that the bearer is authorized to examine the records as a representative of the Procurator-General. The cards will bear a photograph of the representative on the English text side, together with a specimen of his signature and will be authenticated by the Procurator-General's personally affixed signature.

6. The Commanding General of the Eighth Army has been informed of the foregoing and it is desired that representatives of the Procurator-General report to the Assistant Chief of Staff, G-4, Headquarters, Eighth Army, to arrange such details as may be required.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
