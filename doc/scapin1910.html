15 June 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Price and Distribution Controls Over Fish and Vegetables.

1. Directive number 3, SCAPIN 47, dated 22 September 1945, Office of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, charged the Japanese Government with responsibility for initiating and maintaining a firm control over prices and distribution of essential commodities in short supply.

2. In consequence of the continuing world food shortages there is an imperative need for Japan to secure maximum utilization of indigenous foodstuffs. Since fish and vegetables occupy an integral and indispensables position in the Japanese diet, they are essential commodities in short supply and are within the purview of Directive number 3, referred to above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECOR : (SCAPIN - 1910)

HFA/BFJ/NAS/es

11 June 1948

1. The attached memorandum for the Japanese Government has been prepared for the following reasons:

a. Effective controls over prices and rationing of fish and vegetables are necessary for the duration of the acute food shortage.

b. The anti-blackmarket program initiated on 15 December 1947 achieved impressive results but was in danger of collapsing because of half-hearted support at the Cabinet level.

c. Informal statements by operating personnel of SCAP became insufficient to obtain the whole-hearted support that this program required, in face of Cabinet indifference.

d. It was necessary to formally express as SCAP policy that economic controls over perishable foods are required in order to assure the continued effective implementation by all levels of the government.

2. Since 15 January, the Military Government organization has been effecting very close surveillance over distribution channels largely by means of special surveillance teams drawn from tactical troops. The present tour of duty of these teams ends 1 July 1948. It is the consensus of opinion of this division, Military Government Section of Eighth Army Headquarters and various Military Government Teams that the distribution system has not yet been firmly enough established to permit withdrawal of the special teams and that their withdrawal would cause serious impairment of the system with attendant inflationary evils. Therefore basic letter asks the Commanding General, Eighth Army continue the teams for the remainder of the 1948 calendar year. By that time it is believed that the official distribution channels for fish and vegetables will have become firmly established, thus permitting a reversion to routine surveillance activities.

3. Concurrences: NR/Fisheries (Mr. Neville); NR/Agriculture (Mr. Boulware)

(Approved by Chief of Staff on 3 Jun 1948. See Staff Study attached.)

Refer questions to: N. A. Stitt, 57-8393
