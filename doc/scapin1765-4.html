GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.23 (14 Oct 47) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1765/4)

APO 500

9 August 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service Between Japan and British Commonwealth Areas, via Osaka-Colombo Circuit.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.23 (14 Oct 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1765/3, dated 14 October 1947, subject sane as above.

2. So much of iuclosure 1, SCAPIN 1765/3, pertaining to word charges and division of revenue for full rate and British Commonwealth Government radiotelegrams destined to Zanzibar is amended to read:

FULL RATE BRITISH COMMONWEALTH GOVT.

TOTAL RATE JAPAN TERMINAL UK AND BEYOND BALANCE EQUALLY DIVIDED TOTAL RATE JAPAN TERMINAL UK AND BEYOND BALANCE EQUALLY DIVIDED

PENCE PENCE PENCE PENCE PENCE PENCE PENCE PENCE

Zanzibar (Incl Pemba Island) 57 3 1/2 25 28 1/2 30 3 1/2 12 1/2 14

3. Direct communication within the scope of this memorandum is authorized between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, the General Accounting Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers or the British Transfer Agent, and the Ministry of Communications.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
