GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (9 Jan 47) LS-R

(SCAPIN - 1449)

APO 500

9 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Clarification of Status of Former Suspected War Criminals.

The following named Japanese listed on Memorandums indicated below are deleted from said lists, are released from suspicion as Suspected War Criminals, are released from Sugamo Prison, and henceforth will be returned to their former status.

NAME MEMORANDUM 6 Nov 45 PAGE NO.

KONO, Zenzo M/Sgt 1

MEMORANDUM 15 Dec 45

NISHIKAWA, Yoshio Guard 3

MEMORANDUM 17 Jan 46

FURKAWA, Goro Guard 2

MEMORANDUM 3 May 46

MIYAKAWA, Yoshihisa Capt 2

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
