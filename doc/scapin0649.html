GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (23 Jan 46) CCS

(SCAPIN-649)

23 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Request for Radio Facilities at Ibusuki.

1. Reference is made to letter S 589 31 December 1945, subject: “Application for permission of establishing an agency of communication toward ship”, from Liaison Commission (Tokyo) for the Imperial Japanese Demobilization Ministries.

2. Request for the establishment of a radio station at Ibusuki to communicate with ships entering the harbor is disapproved.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
