SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123 (12 Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 585)

APO 500

12 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Applications for Approval of Transactions in Precious Metals

1. Reference is made to communication from the Ministry of Finance, dated 12 December 1945, transmitting a list of applications for approval of transactions in precious metals completed subsequent to date of issuance of Memorandum for Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 130 (22 Sep 45) ESS, subject: "Control of Financial Transactions."

2. The Ministry of Finance is directed to deny approval to the transaction between Tanaka Precious Metal Industry Co. and the Kanegafuchi Communication Industry Co., involving 2,420,575.8 grams of silver valued at 120,302.61 yen, completed 14 October 1945.

3. It is further directed that the appropriate agency of the Japanese Government acquire this silver from the Kanegafuchi Communication Industry Co. and that it be placed in the proper depository in the Bank of Japan.

4. There is no objection to the approval by the Ministry of Finance of the other transactions on the list referred to in paragraph 1 of this memorandum .

5. Acknowledgement of receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
