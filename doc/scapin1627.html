GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (24 Apr 47) GA

(SCAPIN - 1627)

APO 500

24 April 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Lepers to Ryukyus.

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, AG 370.05 (7 May 46)GC, dated 10 February 1947 (SCAPIN 927), subject, "Repatriation," as amended.

b. C.L.O. No. 1224 (RF), dated 25 February 1947, subject, "Application for Repatriation of Lepers to Okinawa and Amami-Oshima."

2. Existing facilities at Amami-Oshima are not adequate for accommodation of the 201 lepers who desire to return thereto. However, facilities on Okinawa are adequate to accommodate the entire group of 281 lepers who desire to return to the Ryukyus.

3. Authority is hereby granted to repatriate to Okinawa the 281 lepers mentioned in reference 1 b above. The provisions of reference 1 a above apply.

4. The Tachibana Maru, expected to depart Sasebo on 10 May 1947, is designated to transport the lepers to Okinawa.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
