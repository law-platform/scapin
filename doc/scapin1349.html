AG 014.33 (20 Nov 46) GA

(SCAPIN-1349)

APO 500

20 November 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Formosan-Chinese Nationals.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 370.05 (7 May 46) GC, (SCAPIN 927), dated 7 May 1946, subject, "Repatriation", as amended.

2. Enclosed are nominal rolls of 745 Formosan-Chinese nationals to be repatriated on the Chinese vessel SS Tai Nan (expected to arrive at Sasebo on or about 3 December l946).

3. The unaccompanied shipment described in subparagraph 3c (1) (a), (b) and (c) of Annex VI of reference memorandum is not authorized for these repatriates. They will be allowed to carry out of Japan only the following:

a. Yen currency in an amount not to exceed ¥1000 per person.

b. In addition to currency, those financial instruments 1isted in subparagraph 3b, Annex VI, reference memorandum.

c. Clothing, personal possessions and household effects of value only to the owner, limited to 500 pounds per repatriate, and to be transported with each repatriate from his home to the port of Sasebo.

4. Necessary quarters, facilities and supplies, including food, aboard the SS Tal Nan will be furnished by the Chinese Government.

5. The Imperial Japanese Government will:

a. Notify subject nationals and provide suitable rail accommodations to transport them and their baggage to the port of Sasebo, so as to arrive by 1 December 1946. Payment for rail transportation will be made directly to Japanese Government Railways by the individuals concerned.

b. Take necessary action to insure that they and their baggage are properly processed and embarked.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

1 Incl.

Nominal Rolls of Formosan-Chinese nationals

(To addressee only)
