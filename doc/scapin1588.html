GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

AG 463.7 (29 Mar 47) GD

(SCAPIN - 1588)

APO 500

29 March 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Importation of Petroleum Products for Imperial Japanese Government, April through June 1947.

1. Reference. Memorandum for Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 463.7 (6 Mar 47) GD (SCAPIN 1560), dated 6 March 1947, subject: "Importation of Petroleum Products for Imperial Japanese Government, April through June, 1947."

2. So much of the Memorandum referred to in 1 above as pertains to paragraphs 1a, b, c, and d, is rescinded and the following substituted therefor:

Unit is 42 gallon barrel April May June

a. Navy Special Fuel Oil

(1) Tsurumi 120,000 120,000 138,000

(2) Sasebo 100,000 100,000 113,000

220,000 220,000 251,000

b. Navy 7-0-2 Diesel

(l) Tsurumi 62,700 87,700 37,700

(2) Sasebo 6,300 6,300 6,300

69,000 94,000 44.000

(Direct discharge from tanker at Tsurumi desired)

c. Range Fuel

(1) Tsurumi 100,000 NIL 125,000

d. Kerosene

(1) Tsurumi 23,000 25,000 NIL

(One time delivery of 50.000 barrels in April or May is authorized and desired.)

3. Paragraphs 2, 3, 4, and 5 of referenced Memorandum continue in force.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
