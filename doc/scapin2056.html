16 November 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Aeronautical Aids Facilities.

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, flie AG 676.3 (21 Oct 45)CCS, SCAPIN 170, dated 21 October 1945, subject: Communication Control.

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 676.3 (21 Nov 45)CCS, SCAPIN 330, dated 21 November 1945, subject: Japanese Air Navigational Aids (Radio). .

c. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 676 (5 Feb 46)CCS, SCAPIN 701, dated 5 February 1946, subject: Operation Of Additional Air Navigational Aid at O-Shima.

d. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 600.1 (4 Mar 46)CCS, SCAPIN 793, dated 4 March 1946, subject: Installation and Operation of Air Navigational Aids at Kushimoto.

e. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 676.3 (31 Jul 46)CCS, SCAPIN 1095, dated 31 July 1946, subject: Discontinuance of Aeronautical Radio Facilities.

f. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 676.3 (21 Jun 48)CCS, SCAPIN 1095/1, dated 15 July 1948, subject: Discontinuance of Aeronautical Radio Facilities.

g. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 676.3 (21 Jun 48)CCS, SCAPIN 1095/2, dated 24 July 1948, subject: Discontinuance of Aeronautical Radio Facilities.

h. Memorandum far the Japanese Government, file AG 676.3 (11 Jan 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1459, dated 11 June 1947, subject: Discontinuance of Aeronautical Radio Facilities.

i. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 360.31 (23 Dec 45)GD, SCAPIN 2012, dated 1 June 1949, subject: Reactivation of Air Navigational Facilities.

2. The memoranda listed in paragraph 1 are hereby rescinded.

3. Authority for the operation and use of aeronautical aids facilities previously authorized by the memoranda listed in paragraph 1 and listed in Inclosure 1 is hereby reaffirmed.

4. Authorization for additions, changes, or discontinuance of aeronautical aids will be provided by procurement instrument only, in accordance with SCAPIN 1872, as amended, and, when appropriate, in-accordance with SCAPIN 1299, as amended. In the case of radio aeronautical aids, authorization for additions, changes, or discontinuance will also be obtained as provided by SCAPIN 1744, as amended.

5. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Telecommunications, regarding matters within the scope of this memorandum, is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
