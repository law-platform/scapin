MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Defense Contribution Funds for Relief and Welfare Works.

1. Reference is made to memorandum from the Imperial Japanese Government, CLO No. 1203 (2.1), dated 14 March 1946, subject: "Use of National Defense Contributions for Relief and Welfare Works (L0335)," and attached memorandum from Ministry of Finance, dated 6 March 1946, same subject.

2. The proposal to appropriate ¥200,000,000 from the remaining balance of war-time contributions for national defense relief measures is approved. Expenditure of these funds will be made in accordance with provisions of memorandum, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for Imperial Japanese Government, AG 091.4 (27 Feb 46) PH/GS/GA/GD, dated 27 February 1946, subject: "Public Assistance," with particular reference to paragraph 1 a. which states generally that aid will be furnished to all indigent persons without discrimination or preferential treatment.

3. It is understood that the remaining balance of the above mentioned fund after the transfer of ¥200,000,000 is ¥655,763,697 which consists of contributions of ¥7,517,546 for scientific research, ¥91,575,038 for servicemen’s welfare and ¥556,671,113 remaining in the National Defense fund proper. There will be no objection to transferring the above balance to the General Account of the Government, using it for relief purposes and reporting the transfer.

4. In event the Imperial Japanese Government desires to use the remaining balance of these funds for purposes other than relief, a plan for such expenditure will be forwarded for approval as indicated in paragraph 4 of the memorandum from the Ministry of Finance referred to in paragraph 1 above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 898)

WFM/ATM/ajy

24 April 1946

1. This memorandum approves the proposed plan of the Ministry of Finance to appropriate ¥200,000,000 of the national defense contribution fund to carry out urgent relief measures for the needy, provided provisions of memorandum, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for Imperial Japanese Government, AG 091.4 (27 Feb 46) PH/GS/GA/GD, dated 27 February 1946, subject: "Public Assistance," are adhered to. The Government is further directed to transfer the remaining balance of the fund, amounting to ¥655,763,697, to the General Account and to use it for the same purpose. Under the circumstances such action is appropriate and desirable.

3. Transfer of the ¥655,763,697 balance of the Defense Contribution Fund to the General Account would give the Ministry of Finance full Budgetary Control of the entire fund and using it for purposes suggested will provide a substantial fraction of the funds needed to finance the cost of relief for the Fiscal Year 1946-47, thereby reducing the anticipated budget deficit.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government originally proposed to devote ¥500,000,000 of this voluntary contribution fund to endowment for a Scientific Research Foundation. This request was denied by memorandum, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for the Imperial Japanese Government, AG 120 (21 Jan 46) ESS/FI, dated 21 January 1946, subject: "Disposition of Defense Contribution Fund." At that time the government was advised that use of the money for welfare purposes was preferable. This advice was not incorporated in the above cited memorandum but was transmitted orally by verbal direction of the Chief of Staff. Previous memorandum and memo for record are attached. Inasmuch as the Japanese Government waited from 21 January to 14 March before complying with this suggestion, and then only to the extent of one-fourth the total amount, it is believed a direct order at this time is preferable to further suggestions.

5. Concurrence: Public Health and Welfare (G. K. W.).

6. This is an administrative memo. No surveillance is necessary. The interests of USAFIK have been considered. This action does not affect the Korean area.
