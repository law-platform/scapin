GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.3 (20 Dec 45) ESS/LE

(SCAPIN-461)

APO 500

20 December 1945

MEMORANDUM TO :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Establishment of a Public Agency of Sericultural Industry.

1. The plan for the "Establishment of a Public Agency for the Sericultural Industry" submitted with CLO No. 869 (2.4) 4 December 1945 is approved, with the understanding that it is to be considered as a temporary measure and subject to revision by order of the Supreme Commander tor the Allied Powers.

2. The Ministry concerned will submit to this Headquarters 5 days prior to orcmulgation, all Ordinances or Regulations having the force and effect of law which will in any way affect the Public Agency referred to in Par. 1.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
