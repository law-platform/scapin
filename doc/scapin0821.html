APO 500

16 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminal

The following named Japanese will be apprehended as a Suspected War Criminal and delivered to the Prevost Marshal, 8th Army, for incarceration in Sugamo Prison. The apprehension of this individual will be effected not later than 1800 hours, 17 March 1946.

NAME RANK OR TITLE DATE, CAMP, ADDRESS OR ASSIGNMENT

NAGANO, Osami Fleet Admiral Reported Residence: Hancura-Machi, Naka-Gun, Tokushima Prefecture.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
