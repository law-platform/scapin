GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 072 (27 May 49) ESS/ST

(SCAPIN-2100)

APO 500

1 June 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
Subject :Patent Applications

1. Rescissions:

a. AG 072 (22 Sep 45) ESS/ST, SCAPIN 2010, 27 May 1949, subject: Patent Applications.

b. Paragraphs 2 and 3 of AG 072 (22 Sep 45) ESS/ST, SCAPIN 2010/1, 11 July 1949, subject: Patent Rights and Restrictions Concerning Patent Rights.

2. There is no objection to the filing of applications of Japanese Nationals for foreign patent, design and trade mark registrations in those countries, the domestic laws of which allow Japanese Nationals to obtain and hold industrial property rights therein.

3. Foreign exchange funds required for the acquisition of such registrations shall be obtained in accordance with provisions of the Foreign Exchange and Foreign Trade Control Law and appropriate Orders and Ordinances thereunder.

4. Any payments, in whatever form, accruing to an exchange resident by virtue of sale, license or other transaction concerning the rights acquired or to be acquired shall be subject to the provisions of the Foreign Exchange and Foreign Trade Control Law and appropriate Orders and Ordinances thereunder.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.Bush
Brigadier General, USA,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2100)

JWO' B/PET/ECA/DA/vmp

25 May 1950

1. SCAPINs 2010 and 2010/1 set up procedures for and control over applications by Japanese Nationals for patents in foreign countries.

2. The new Foreign Exchange and Foreign Trade Control Law and Regulations remove SCAP control over release of foreign exchange for the purpose of obtaining patents in foreign countries, this responsibility now resting (since 1 Jan 50) with the Japanese Ministry of finance.

3. Consistent with SCAP policy to return controls to the Japanese Government as rapidly as possible, the present time is believed propitious for relinquishing the control over applications by Japanese Nationals for patents in foreign countries, now held under the terms of SCAPIN 2010 as amended by SCAPIN 2010/1.

4. By agreement between the Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of International Trade and Industry, Patent Agency foreign exchange funds will be approved for acquiring foreign patent and trade mark rights by Ministry of finance which will obtain the concurrence before granting a license for the payment. Ministry of Finance will also obtain concurrence of Patent Agency before granting approval to sell or license the property right once acquired.

5. Proposed SCAPIN rescinds 2010 and all of 2010/1 except paragraph 1 which rescinded earlier directives which should remain rescinded; states there is no objection to filing of applications of Japanese Nationals abroad in those countries which will accept them; and that foreign exchange funds required for the purpose or acquired in connection with the assignment, sale, license or use of such rights will be administered in accordance with provisions of the Foreign Exchange and Foreign Trade Control Law and appropriate Orders and Ordinances thereunder.

6. This is an administrative matter.

7. Concurrence: ESS/BFE

Donald Abbey - 26-8066

J. W. O'B.
