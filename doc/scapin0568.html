SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

(SCAPIN - 568)

APO 500

9 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Application of the Fuji Industrial Company to Sell Stock

1. Reference is made to Central Liaison Office Memorandum No. 47, dated 6 December 1945, subject: "Dissolution of Holding Companies," which forwards application of the Fuji Industrial Company for permission to sell its holdings in the Hirata Fishing Manufacturing Company, Ltd.

2. The above application is denied at this time.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 568)

WFM/SWW/las

9 January 1946

1. CLO Memo No. 47, dated 6 December 1945, forwards application of the Fuji Industrial Company for permission to sell 6,000 shares of the Hirata Fishing Manufacturing Company, Ltd., at \200 per share. The suggested sales price is also the price at which it was purchased originally by the Fuji Industrial Company. These shares were purchased by the Fuji Industrial Company when it took over the Hirata Fishing Manufacturing Company for the purpose of using its plant facilities. It is now proposed to sell the shares to the former shareholders.

2. At a meeting held 19 December 1945 at which the following were present, Mr. Thomas (Finance), Mr. Reday (Research), Major Abrams (Legal) land Major Wheeler (Cartels), it was unanimously decided to deny the above application at this time for the following reasons:

a. Policy das already been established by SCAP in Memorandum AG 300.8 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC, subject: "Regulations Affecting Restricted Concerns," which directs that no sale, transfer or other disposition of capital assets, including securities, will be made without prior approval of SCAP.

b. It was considered unwise to establish a policy for reducing security investments where such investments involve the control of the company. The whole problem of dissolving or reducing industrial holding companies will be reviewed with the Edwards Commission which is on its way to this theater. It is expected that with their advice and assistance a procedure can be established to handle such cases as above.

S. W. W.
