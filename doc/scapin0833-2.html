GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 684 (22 Mar 46) GC/TNG

(SCAPIN - 833/2)

APO 500

4 August 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Kyushu Aerial Gunnery Range.

1． Reference: SCAPIN 833, 22 March 1946.

2． The coordinates of the Kyushu Aerial Gunnery Range are changed from:

34°40' North 130° 20' East

34°40' North 130° 40' East

34°10' North 130° 40' East

34°10' North 130° 20' East

to:

34°51' North - 130° 35' 15" East

34°43' 20" North - 130° 52' 10" East

34°08' 40" North - 130° 29' 10" East

34°16' 45" North - 130° 12' 45" East

3. Hours of use of the range will be 0700-1700 hours, local time, Mondays through Fridays. Fishing is permitted in the range danger area during those periods when the range is not in use.

4. The inhabitants of Fukuoka, Yamaguchi and Shimane Prefectures will be notified prior to each actual use of the range. When the range is used for periods in excess of fifteen (15) days, warning reminders will be disseminated to the inhabitants concerned. This information will be forwarded through Civil Affairs Teams to local Japanese Civil authorities.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
