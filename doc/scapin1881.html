APO 500

22 April 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Second Supplemental Request for Permission of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the Second Quarter of 1948.

1. Reference is made to C.L.C.C. No. 163 (LO 446-FI/2E), 9 February 1948, subject, "Second Supplemental Request for Permission of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the Second Quarter of 1948."

2. Application for permission to use gold foil, as requested by reference 1 above, is approved in the amount and by the company stated below. Gold foil in the pooled reserve under custody of the Ministry of Finance will be drawn upon to supply the gold foil authorized.

MANUFACTURER GRAMS GOLD BULLION CONTAINED IN FOIL SHEETS GOLD FOIL PEARCENT TO BE EXPORTED EXPORT AUTHORITY ORDER NO.

Yamanaka Daibutsudo, Osaka 95 5,890 100 EX 5378

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
