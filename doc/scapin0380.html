GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (3 Dec 45) LS

(SCAPIN - 380)

3 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

1. The following named Japanese are alleged to have committed atrocities and offenses against persons of United Nations Nationals, while confined in Prisoner of War Camps and Internment Camps in Japan. These persons will be apprehended as suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, XI Corps, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest practicable date.

HAKODATE AREA

1st BRANCH CAMP, MURORAN, HOKKAIDO

NAME RANK ASSIGNMENT OF DATE

NISHIOKA, Shigeru Civilian Guard 17 June 1943 - 3 Jan. 1944

ASARI, Eiji Sergeant 12 Jan. 1943 - 19 Feb. 1945

MORIYAMA, Work Leader #4 Cement Party, Asano Work Kamiiso,

Prefecture of Otemari 1943 to 1945

TAKASHITA, Toshio Civilian 2 Jan. 1943-13 Jan. 1945

KATISKA, Mess Sgt. August 1943. Later was on Police force

at Muroran until September, 1945.

HIRANO, Work Leader Was at Nisi-Ashibetsu, Prefecture of

Sorachi July, 1945

TAKADA, Work Leader Was at Nisi-Ashibetsu, Prefecture of

Sorachi July, 1945

2. The following named Japanese, while serving in the capacity as Commander of the Pontianak Area, in Dutch Borneo, is alleged to have committed atrocities and offenses against persons of United Nations Nationals. This Japanese will be apprehended as a suspected War Criminal and delivered to the Commanding General, XI Corps, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest practicable date.

Name RANK ASSIGNMENT

UMINO, Major Believed to be now attached to the Western Army District Headquarters in Kyushu

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
