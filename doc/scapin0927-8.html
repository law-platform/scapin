AG 370. 05 (21 Sep 46) GC

APO 500

21 September, 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Repatriation

for the Allied Powers, file AG 370.05 (7 May, 46) GC, (SCAPIN-927), dated

7 May, 1946, subject as above, as amended.

rescinded an the page attached as inclosure will be substituted therefor.

Page 5 (Revised 20 July, 1946), Annex VI.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

O. J. Rehe
