4. a. All gold and silver will be obtained from newly mined stocks in the possession of the Japanese Government.

b. 19,491.20 grams. of platinum, 0.3 grams of iridium and 568 grams of palladium will be released to authorized representatives of the Ministry of Finance from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan, Tokyo.

c. 360.2 grams of rhodium will be released to authorized representatives of the Ministry of Financc from the Eighth Army Vaults, Osaka Mint,Osaka.

d. At the time the precious metals are released from SCAP custody under the outhority of paragraphs 4b and 4c above , the Japanese Government will deposit with the Custodian, Eighth Army Vaults, Bank of Japan, Tokyo, 32,861.157 grams of cold. This gold will be in the form of bars of mint or near mint fineness.

5．Reference Application 1b, a request of behalf of Tokemoto Electric Metal Manufacturing Work and reference application 1g, a request of behalf of Nippon Tojiki Koeki K. K., are disapproved. Reference application 1c is disapproved as it duplicates request considered and approved in reference 1d above.

6. All future requests for the allocation of precious metals for domestic consumption must contain written approval, based upon Japan’s minimum needs, of the interested Ministries of the Japanese Government. Application for releases of precious metals for manufacture of export items must include the numbers of the Validated Form 100. The names of the companies will be submitted in Romaji-Hepburn and requests and all attached papers will be submitted on 8” x 11” paper only.

7. The Japanese Government is directed to submit a monthly report to include the following information for each of the firms named in paragraphs 2a, b, c, and d above:

a. Gold, silver, platinum, palladium, rhodium, iridium; and gold and silver liquid, dust and foil on hand as of the first day of the month;

b. Gold, silver, platinum, palladium, rhodium, iridium; and gold and silver liquid, dust and foil received during the month;

c. Gold, silver, platinum, palladium, rhodium, iridium; and gold and silver liquid, dust and foil used during the month;

d. Gold, silver, platinum, palladium, rhodium, iridium; and gold and silver liquid, dust and foil on hand as of the last day of the month;

e. Products manufactured and disposition of same.

8. Reports will be submitted in quadruplicate not later than the fifteenth day of the month following that covered by the report. These reports will include data pertaining to the Japan Dental Association.

9. The period during which the subject allocation of precious metals may be used will begin upon publication and extend to include the last day of 1947.
