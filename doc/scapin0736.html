GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 440 (14 Feb 46) ESS/IN

(SCAPIN - 736)

APO 500

14 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Tachikawa Army Veterinary Department.

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. No. 102 (1.1) dated 9 January 1946, subject: "Transfer of Tachikawa Army Veterinary Department".

2. The Tachikawa Army Veterinary Department is now used in part by the Occupation Forces. As the existing Materials of the Veterinary Department are removed by the Home Ministry, the remaining buildings will be utilized by the Occupation forces.

3. The transfer of the facilities of the Tachikawa Army Veterinary Department to the Japan Veterinary Society is disapproved.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
for /t/ B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 736)

ESS/IN WLB/rhw

2 February 1946

1. The Imperial Japanese Government in C.L.O. Memo No. 102 (1.1) dated 9 January 1946, subject: "Transfer of Tachikawa Army Veterinary Department" requested SCAP approval, for the transfer of the materials and establishments of the Tachikawa Army Veterinary Department excluding those now being used by the 375th Troop Carrier Group, 54th Troop Carrier Wing and the 5th Air Force.

2. The aims and purposes of the Japan Veterinary Society as well as other pertinent information was requested from the Public Health and Welfare Section. See Check Sheet dated 15 January 1943, file 444.5, subject: "Tachikawa Army Veterinary Department" from ESS to PH, attached.

3. The PH Section in reply to requested information states that:

a. The materials of the Tachikawa Army Veterinary Department are being removed by the Ministry of Home Affairs.

b. The Occupation Forces will utilize all buildings as soon as vacated.

c. Japanese officials concerned with the manufacture of veterinary sera, vaccine, veterinary instruments and equipment expressed a desire to establish laboratories and shops for such purpose at other locations. See Check Sheet, file 444.5 subject: "Tachikawa Army Veterinary Department dated 30 January 1946, PH to ESS, attached.

4. The attached memorandum to the Japanese Government denies its petition for the transfer of the facilities of the Tachikawa Army Veterinary Department to the Japan Veterinary Society.

J. A. O'H

2 Incls:

1. c/n, ESS to PH as indicated.

2. c/n, PH to ESS as indicated.
