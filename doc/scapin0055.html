IN THE CLEAR
OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS
SIGNAL CORPS MESSAGE

(SCAPIN-55)

GC-O TNS/ws 25 Sep 1945

FROM :SCAP
TO :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
CENTRAL LIAISON OFFICE, TOKYO・・・URGENT
INFO :CG USAFIN・・・・・・・・・・URGENT
CG ISNTH ARMY・・・・・・・・URGENT
CG EIGHTH ARMY・・・・・・・ URGENT

COMMANDING GENERAL UNITED STYATES ARMY FORCES IN FUSAN TO JAPAN AT RATE OF FOUR THOUSAND DAILY COMMENCING TWO SEVEN SEPTEMBER USING PART OF AVAILABLE JAPANESE FERRIES NON REGULALLY RUNNING OMA WITH ORMAL PORTS OF REFUGELS TRAFFIC AS DESTIBATION PD (ZAX6055)DESIRE YOU RECEIVE OMA PROGRESS AND CONSUMATE REPATRIATION OF THIS PERSONNELL

OFFICIAL:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, US Army,
Adjutant General.

APPROVED:

/s/W.L. Chambers
/t/W.L.CHAMBERS
Brig. General, G.S.C.,
Acting Asst. Chief of Staff, G-3
