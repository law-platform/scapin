THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Contracts with Foreign Concerns in Japan and with Concerns in Which Foreign Nationals Have an Interest.

1. Reference is Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 160 (2 Jan. 46) ESS/FI (SCAPIN 534) dated 2 January 1946, subject: Contracts with Foreign Concerns in Japan and with Concerns in Which Foreign Nationals Have an Interest.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 534/1)

WKL/JCS/CDY/rmp

22 September 1948

1. Memorandum for Japanese Government from GHQ, SCAP, AG 160 (2 Jan 46) ESS/FIN, dated 2 January 1946, subject: Contracts with Foreign Concerns in Japan and with Concerns in Which Foreign Nationals have an Interest, states that the Ministry of Finance may grant general approval for the execution and performance of certain specified types of contracts between resident Japanese nationals or Japanese owned concerns located in Japan and foreign owned or controlled concerns likewise located in Japan. One such type of contracts are contracts with concerns organized and doing business in Japan which are owned and controlled by nationals of foreign countries which are residing in Japan other than nationals of Germany, Italy, Hungary, Rumania, Bulgaria, Finland and Thailand.

2. Since the above directive was written the status of Italy, Finland and Thailand has changed somewhat. For purposes of restoration of property, nationals of these countries are now treated similar to United Nations nationals. Italy also has an official representative and mission in Japan. Therefore, it is considered that there should be no prohibition against transactions by concerns in Japan which are owned or controlled by nationals of Italy, Finland or Thailand.

3. The attached memorandum amends memorandum referred to in para 1 above by deleting Italy, Finland and Thailand from para 2-a.

4. Surveillance by Eighth Army not necessary.

5. Concurrences: CPC

DS (c/n dtd 4 Oct 48)

LS (c/n dtd 5 Oct 48)

W. K. L.

ADDENDUM TO MEMO FOR RECORD:

WKL/JCS/DCY/km

1. Check Note No. 3 from DS to LS concurs in the proposed memorandum to the J.G. but recommends that Bulgaria, Hungary and Rumania be made additional deletions from par. 2a of reference memorandum since they are also listed as "Special Status Nations" in SCAPIN 1912, 21 Jun 1948.

2. CPC and LS also concur.

3. In accordance with recommendation contained in check note referred to par. 1 above, memorandum for the J.G. has been altered to include Bulgaria, Hungary and Rumania in the countries to be deleted from original memorandum.

W. K. L.
