GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 684 (10 Jun 48) GC/TNG

(SCAPIN - 1907/2)

APO 500

16 July 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Northern Honshu Air-to-Air Range.

1. References:

a. SCAPIN 1907, 10 June 1948.

b. SCAPIN 1907/1, 1 September 1948.

2. An alternate location of the Northern Honshu Air-to-Air Range is approved for use during period 1 July 31 December. Coordinates of danger area are:

40° 38' 20" N - 141° 44' 25" E

40° 44' 50" N - 142° 06' 45" E

40° 10' 50" N - 142° 23' 00" E

40° 04' 35" N - 142° 01' 10" E

3. Present range location will be used 1 January 30 June.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
