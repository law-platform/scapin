3 December 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Revised Listing of Reparations Selections.

1. References are the following memoranda for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. File AG 387.6 (13 Aug 46) ESS/IN, (SCAPIN 1132), dated 13 August 1946, subject: Reparations Selections Within the Sulfuric Acid Industry.

b. File AG 387.6 (13 Aug 46) ESS/IN, (SCAPIN 1133), dated 13 August 1946, subject: Reparations Selections Within the Machine Tool Industry

c. File AG 387.6 (10 Oct 46) ESS/IN, (SCAPIN 1136), dated 14 August 1946, subject: Reparations Selections Within the Precision Bearing Industry.

d. File AG 004 (10 Oct 46) ESS/IN, (SCAPIN 1258), dated 10 October 1946, subject: Revised Listing of Reparations Selections Within Privately-Owned Munitions Plants.

e. File AG 004 (11 Oct 46) ESS/IN, (SCAPIN 1263), dated 11 October 1946, subject: Revised List No. 2 Aircraft Factories, Military and Naval Arsenals and Research Laboratories.

2. Attached as inclosures 1 to 5 are revised annexes which add to or delete from custodial lists under above reference directives.

3. Within seventy-two (72) hours of the receipt of this memorandum, the Imperial Japanese Government will have qualified representaties report to the Commanding General, Eighth Army, and to the Commander Naval Activities, Japan, to receive instructions with regard to establishing or releasing from custody and control of installations affected by revisions of the lists referred to in paragraph 1, above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1376)

JZR/NJMlv

21 November 1946

1. The attached SCAPIN revises present listings of Factories and Arsenals now under custody and control of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. It is made necessary by submission to this headquarters of information from the field of complete war damage to some installations, transfer of machinery and equipment for storage at others, errors in listing, and information that some listed concerns do not meet the criteria for reparations claims set forth in Radio, Washington (Joint Chiefs of Staff) MR2516372, dtd 26 June 1946, while others not listed do meet the criteria for selection for reparations removal under the interim reparations program.

2. The attached SCAPIN with command letters consolidates the changes to date. Changes are as follows:

SCAPIN CATEGORY DELETIONS ADDRESS CHANGES

1132 Sulphuric Acid Ind. 2 2

1133 Machine Tools Ind. 7 8

1136 Precision Bearing Ind. 1

1258 Privately-Owned Munitions 12 2 10

1263 Aircraft, Arsenals and Laboratories 2 4

3. Concurrences from CPC (H. A. Brenn)

4. Request Memo for IJG be held for 10 days after transmittal of letter to Commanding General, Eighth Army.

5. The interests of USAFIK are not concerned.

J. Z. R. 2-5601
