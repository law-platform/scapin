GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 632 (21 Oct 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 169)

APO 500

21 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Use of Japanese Hospitals by Allied Forces.

1. Receipt is acknowledged of letter, N. D. No. 103 dated 7 October, subject, "Continued Use of Naval Hospitals."

2. The need of the Imperial Japanese Government for all available hospital facilities for treatment of civilians including demobilized military and Naval personnel is recognized. It is the policy of this headquarters to take only the minimum facilities actually required to meet the needs of the Allied Forces and to return these facilities for civilian use as soon as they are no longer required. It is not practicable to state definitely that any given facility may not in the future be required for the use of the Allied Forces. Accordingly, the list of facilities attached to letter N. D. No. 103 can not be considered as exempt from future be requirements of the Allied Forces. In the event that it is necessary to take over any of these facilities, the Imperial Japanese government will be given as much advance notice as is practicable.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. ALLEN,
/T/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
