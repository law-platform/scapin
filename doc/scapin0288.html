GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG132 (16 Nov 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-288)

APO 500

16 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Freezing of Accounts of the Manchu Kankei Kikokusha Engokai.

1. You will immediately freeze all the bank accounts of the Manchu Kankei Kikukusha Engokai in Japan.

2. You will immediate furnish this Headquarters bank statements of all the above accounts from the dates of opening the accounts to date of freezing.

3. You will report immediately to this Headquarters the action taken comply with these requirements.

4. An acknowledgment of the receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 288)

RCK/DHB/kns

16 November 1945

SUBJECT : Freezing of Accounts of the Manchu Kankei Kikokusha Engokai

1. It has come to the attention of this office that the Manchurian Relief Association for repatriates from Manchuria, the Manchu Kankei Engokai, was formed in August 1945 by a cash donation of 300 million yen from the Manchurian Heavy Industries Company.

2. This latter company was closed by order of this Headquarters on September 30 because of its wartime activities. There is reason to believe that this Relief Association is not what it pretends to be and may be acting as a cloak for the Manchurian Heavy Industries Company. The information with regard to the formation of the Association and the source of its funds was given verbally by a director of the Association, Mr. S. Uyeda. Until investigation is made and bona fides established, it is considered necessary that the funds of the subject Association be blocked immediately.

3. The attached Memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government directs the immediate freezing of the Association's bank accounts and requires the submission of statements of account.

4. No concurrences are necessary.

C. F. T.
