AG 423 (23 Oct 50) ESS/IND

(SCAPIN - 2128)

APO 500

23 October 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Rescission of Scapins 1600 and 1600/1.

1. Reference the following memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. AG 423 (4 Apr 47) ESS/TD, SCAPIN 1600, 4 April 1947, subject: Rayon Textile Capacity.

b. AG 423 (4 Apr 47) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 1600/1, 19 June 1950, subject: Rayon Textile Capacity.

2. The above SCAPINS pertaining to limitations set on the rayon textile capacity are hereby rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2128)

WFM/MMC/MBL/JCP/gk

17 October 1950

1. Reference SCAPIN 1600/1 gives authorization to increase the rayon capacity from an annual capacity of 150, 000 metric tons of filament yarn and rayon staple fibre to 169, 020 metric tons of filament yarn and rayon staple fibre manufactured by the viscose and cuprammonium processes, 20,000 metric tons of high tenacity yarns (viscose process), 15,000 metric tons of filament yarn and staple fibre manufactured by the acetate process.

2. The critical position of the world supply of cotton will require the Japanese rayon industry to expand, as the present capacity of the rayon industry will not be able to meet the anticipated shortages of cotton.

3. Therefore, subject SCAPINS are being rescinded.

4. Concurrences: ESS/FTC - By phone, Mr. C. C. Campbell 26-8588

ESS/FTP - By phone, Mr. R. M. Gillies 26-6561
