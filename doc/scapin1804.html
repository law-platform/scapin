GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG387.6 (21 Oct 47) ESS/IN

(SCAPIN-1804)

APO 500

21 October 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Destruction of Special Purpose Machinery and Equipment.

1. All special purpose industrial machinery and equipment regardless of ownership or location, functionally limited to use in connection with combat equipment products, are to be destroyed by scrapping or other approved manner within six (6) months of this date. All such machinery and equipment under custody for reparations removal are hereby released for destruction.

2. The Japanese Government will:

a. Coordinate action in compliance with this directive with the Commanding General, Eighth Army and the Commander Naval Forces, Far East.

b. Submit to the Commanding General, Eighth Army and Commander Naval Forces, Far East;

(1) An outline of the overall plan for the accomplishment of this directive.

(2) Monthly reports indicating the status of the program and the estimated date of completion.

(3) Detailed reports for each installation showing:

(a) By individual inventory code number the machines and equipment scrapped, if under custody for reparations removal; otherwise by plant location and general description.

(b) Cost of scrapping.

(c) Tonnage of various grades of ferrous scrap recovered.

(d) Weights of non-ferrous scrap recovered (Brass, Bronze, Copper, Lead).

(e) Proceeds from the sale of scrap and the value of unsold portion.

3. Component parts and auxiliary equipment not special purpose in nature and having independent reparations value will be removed from special purpose tools and equipment under custody for reparations removal, and held pending further instruction.

4. Special purpose elements will be destroyed in such a manner that they cannot be reconstituted into other machines or equipment.

5. Within seventy-two (72) hours of the time of receipt of this memorandum, the Japanese Government will have qualified representatives report to the Commanding General, Eighth Army and the Commander Naval Forces, Far East to receive instructions in regard to specific plants and facilities affected and measures to be taken to effect this destruction.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1804)

ESS/IN JZR/CDR/WT/GD/ls

7 October 1947

1. Reference is radio, War Department (Joint Chiefs of Staff) Number War 84940, dated 26 August 1947, attached as annex 1. This directive transmitted policies adopted by the Far Eastern Commission on 14 August 1947. Paragraph, 1a of reference radio reads: "All special purpose industrial machinery and equipment functionally limited to use in connection with combat equipment-products, should be destroyed."

2. The attached directive to the Japanese Government and Command letters to Commanding General, Eighth Army and Commander Naval Forces, Far East have been prepared to accomplish the destruction of special purpose industrial machinery and equipment. This directive parallels SCAPIN-910, AGO 45.95 (30 Apr 46) Subject: Destruction of Former Japanese Naval Vessels, which sets up the procedure for Scrapping of Naval Vessels.

3. The proposed SCAPIN orders the destruction of all special purpose machines within six (6 months.) The machinery is released to the Japanese Government to be destroyed in such a manner that it cannot be reconstituted into other machines or equipment. Representatives of the Japanese Government will report to Commanding General, Eighth Army and Commander Naval Forces, Far East to receive detailed instructions as to procedures and specific authorizations. The Japanese Government will present an overall plan for the accomplishment of the directive, will make monthly reports showing the progress of destruction and estimated date of completion, and will report for each installation the number of machines and equipment scrapped, the costs of scrapping and the amount and value of scrap salvaged. Eighth Army will consolidate this information and incorporate the government-owned portion in the monthly G-4 Report of Captured and Surrendered Supplies. Auxiliary equipment not special purpose in nature and having reparations value will be removed from reparations machines scrapped and listed under current inventory instructions.

4. This directive is the result of a conference between representatives of the Economic and Scientific Section, Reparations Section and representatives of Eighth Army Military Government Section.

5. Concurrences: G-4 - Lt. Col. Ebel; Reparations Section - Mr. Meiklejohn

6. The interests of Korea are not concerned.

7. Refer questions to Mr. Thurman - phone 26-7842.

J. Z. R.
