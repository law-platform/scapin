GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 602 (5 Jan 51) CPC/OD

(SCAPIN-2135/1)

APO 500

7 February 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Liquidation of Korean Railway Employees Mutual Aid Association.

1. Reference is made to the following memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. File AG 602 (5 Jan 51)CPC/OD, SCAPIN 2135, 5 January 1951, subject, "Liquidation of Korean Railway Employees Mutual Aid Association"

b. File AG 386.7 (7 May 47)CPC/FP, SCAPIN 3766-A, 7 May 1947, subject, "Preservation of Assets of Mutual Aid Association of the Employees of Korean Railway Bureau."

2. The memorandum reference 1b above is rescinded in view of the issuance of the memorandum reference 1a above.

3. Paragraph 5 of memorandum reference 1a above is hereby amended to read as follows: "Any residual assets remaining after payment of the above claims are to be distributed by the liquidator to the Japanese members of the Association in proportion to their previous contributions to the Association."

4. Paragraph 6 of memorandum reference 1a above is hereby amended to read as follows: "The Ministry of Finance, Japanese Government, is to submit a report to the Office of the Civil Property Custodian, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers within sixty (60) days from the date of this memorandum regarding action taken to implement this memorandum. The submission of further reports to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers with respect to the progress of the liquidation will not be required."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
