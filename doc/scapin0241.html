GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.7 (5 Nov 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 241)

5 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Petroleum for Allies and Neutrals.

1. Allied .military authorities have been advised that they will not requisition petroleum products from you. All foreign nationals and governmental agencies will purchase petroleum in the civilian market. You will take measures to insure that the needs of the official agencies of foreign governments are met.

2. This Headquarters assumes no obligation for the collection of debits incurred by reason of this directive.

3. To enable you to comply with this directive it is anticipated that it will be necessary to import products.

For the present these will be imported by this Headquarters and made available to you as required. The procedures for payment will be announced later and will conform to that for other imported commodities.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
