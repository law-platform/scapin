GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 095 (4 Feb 48) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-2091)

APO 500

3 April 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
Subject :Release of Blocked Deposits of Companies with Head Office Abroad

1. Reference is made to following memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. AG 004 (4 Feb 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1856, 4 February 1948, subject, “Preservation of Assets of Branches in Japan of Concerns with Head Office Abroad”

b. AG 602 (17 Nov 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1965, 18 January 1949, subject, “Liquidation of Property in Japan of Branches of Companies with Head Offices Outside Japan”

c. AG 602 (17 Nov 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1965/2, 1 June 1949, subject, “Disposition of Property in Japan of Companies or Persons with Head Offices in Areas Formerly Occupied by the Japanese.”

2. Reference 1a above is hereby rescinded. Bank deposits which have been blocked by reference 1a, above, will be unblocked and will be made available for purposes in conjunction with liquidation of property covered by references 1b and 1c, above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.Bush
Brigadier General, USA,
Adjutant General.
