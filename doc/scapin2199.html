GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.31 (4 Mar 52) ESS/FTC

(SCAPIN - 2199)

APO 500

4 March 1952

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Authority to Sign Payments and Trade Agreements between Japan and Sweden

1. The Japanese Government is hereby authorized to sign and to exchange with the Swedish Diplomatic Representative in Japan, the Payments Agreements, the Trade Agreement and Trade Plan and the side letters for implementation thereof, which were negotiated at the recent Swedish Trade Conference held in Tokyo during the period of 7 December 1951 to and including 28 February 1952 under the chairmanship of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ C. C. B. Warden
/t/ C. C. B. WARDEN,
Colonel, A.G.C.,
Adjutant General
