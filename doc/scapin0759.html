GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 004.2 (21 Feb 46) ESS-FI

(SCAPIN-759)

APO 500

21 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Merger of Financial Institutions.

1. Except as authoized by this Headquarters, the Imperial Japanes Government will not permit the merger, consolidation, or amalgamation of:

a. Any financial institution, including but not limited to banks, trust companies and cooperatives, with any other financial institution or any branch, subsidiary or affiliate thereof.

b. Any financial institution with any of its own subsidiary or affiliates.

2. From this date no financial institution shall be permitted to purchase or otherwise acquire additional capital stock or dabentures of any other financial institution, except as authorized by this Headquarters.

3. Direct communication between the Ministry of Finance and interested staff sectons of this Headquarters is authorized to implement the subject memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN-759)

WFM/ FER/ jpl

1. Among the objectives of this Headquarters specified in the directive from the Joint Chiefs of Staff is the encouragement within Japan of economic procedures and institutions which will contribute to the growth of peaceful and democratic forces in Japan.

2. The merger of small financial institutions into large ones has been one of the devices used by the Japanese to concentrate financial power in the hands of small groups subservient to the military and Zaibatsu leaders.

3. Since 1939 there have been 225 mergers of small financial institutions. Since the initiation of the occupation, eight small institutions have been absorbed by large banks.

4. The directive from the Joint Chiefs of Staff further states that this Headquarters will take measures to break up large financial combines. The first step in the achievement of this objective is to stop the formation of new combines or the extension of those already in existence. This objective will be achieved by the attached directive.

5. It is considered desirable that the merger of institutions be subject to closest scrutiny by this Headquarters and that no such merger be permitted without the permission of this Headquarters.

6. It is therefore recommended that the attached memorandum be forwarded to the Imperial Japanese Government.

7. Direct communication is recommended because it is anticipated that numerous requests will be received from the Japanese Government.

8. ESS- LE concurs ( ).

ESS- AC concurs ( ).

C. F. T.
