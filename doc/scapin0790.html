GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 122.2 (4 Mar 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 790)

APO 500

4 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Blocked Account of Kusumi Seishi

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, this Headquarters, file AG 122.2 (6 Dec 45) ESS/FI, dated 6 December 1945, subject: “Proceeds of Remittances and Collections since 15 August 1945.”

2. It has come to the attention of this Headquarters that on 30 August 1945 the Bank of Chosen, Seoul, Korea transferred ¥15,150,000 to the Bank of Japan, Shimonoseki for the account of Kusumi Seishi. The proceeds of this transfer were subject to blocking by the reference memorandum.

3. It is directed that the Imperial Japanese Government report to this Headquarters the present balance and all payments, transfers or withdrawals effected from the account referred to in paragraph 2, this memorandum, subsequent to 30 August 1945. It is further directed that a full accounting be furnished of all funds paid from that account subsequent to that date.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ Harold Fair
for /t/ B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 790)

WFM/TEB/jrh

1. Radio TFYMG 876 from CG XXIV Corps, received 9 February 1946, requests that ¥15,150,000 transferred by the Bank of Chosen, Seoul, Korea on 30 August 1945 to the Bank of Japan, Shimonoseki for the account of Kusumi Seishi be impounded.

2. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government file AG 122.2(6 Dec 45) ESS/FI, subject: "Proceeds of Remittance and Collections since 15 August 1945" prohibits, without the prior approval of this Headquarters, all payments, transfers or withdrawals from any bank account with the proceeds of any remittance from Korea which was credited on or since 15 August 1945. The proceeds of this transfer, now in the account of Kusumi Seishi, are blocked in accordance with the reference memorandum, as this transfer was made on 30 August 1945 and originated from Korea. In the event that payments had been made from the account of Kusumi Seishi before memorandum AG 122.2 (6 Dec 45) ESS/FI prohibited such payments, the Imperial Japanese Government has been directed to report the present balance and all transactions with respect to this transfer. Steps will be taken to recover any funds paid out from this remittance if the report of transactions effected shows this to be necessary.

3. Concurrences not necessary.

C. F. T.

ADDENDUM TO MEMO FOR RECORD:

This is considered an administrative matter. This directive requires no surveillance by the Eighth Army.
