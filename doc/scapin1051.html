GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123.7 (4 Jul 46) FD

(SCAPIN-1051)

APO 500

4 July 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Supplemental Military Yen Currency Type “A”.

1. It is directed that the Japanese Government enact all necessary legislation to make unlawful the possession of Supplemental Military Yen Currency Type “A” on and after 10 July 1946 by any person or organization except members of the Allied Occupation Forces and those specifically authorized by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. Provisions for the confiscation of Supplemental Military Yen Currency Type “A” in the possession of unauthorized persons or organizations, and appropriate punitive measures shall be included.

2. The Japanese Government shall treat Supplemental Military Yen Currency Type “A” as neither legal tender nor a lawful medium of exchange, and it shall turn over all such currency coming into its possession, custody, or control to the Fiscal Director, General Headquarters, Suprme Commander for the Allied Powers.

3. As a matter of information, Supplemental Military Yen Currency Type “A” is identical in appearance to Supplemental Military Yen Currency Type “B”, now in circulation, except that the letter “A” appears on the face of each note in place of the letter “B”.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
