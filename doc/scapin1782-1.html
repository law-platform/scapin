GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.93 (25 Sep 47) GB/P&E

(SCAPIN - 1782/1)

APO 500

21 August 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Japanese Weather Service.

1. Reference is made to:

a. AG 000.93 (25 Sep 47) GB/P&E, SCAPIN 1782, 25 September 1947, subject: Japanese Weather Service

b. C.L.C.O. No. 2684 (2P), 26 July 1948, subject: Application for Installation of Seismograph at Shimizu Weather Station

2. Application for permission to install a seismograph at Shimizu weather station, as requested in reference 1b above is approved.

3. That part of reference 1a above, which reads:

No Name of Station Location Elevation in feet Services Rendered (CODE) Index Number

42 Shimizu N32 47 E132 57 12 23300 CPT 654

is hereby rescinded, and the following substituted in lieu thereof:

No Name of Station Location Elevation in feet Services Rendered (CODE) Index Number

42 Shimizu N32 47 E132 57 12 23300 CPTS 654

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
