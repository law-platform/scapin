GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 441.1 (2 Nov 45) PH

(SCAPIN-229)

2 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Disposition of Heroin in Japan.

1. The following instructions are issued for the disposition of all heroin in Japan.

a. The possession, use, sale, purchase, gift, receipt, distribution or transportation of diacetylmorphine hydrochloride (heroin), and any salt, compound, preparation, or combination thereof is prohibited.

b. Upon receipt of this memorandum you will confiscate, collect, and deliver into the custody of United States Forces all heroin in Japan. Place and date of delivery will be as designated by the appropriate Army Commanders.

c. You will furnish this headquarters a prompt report of the disposal of each stock of heroin. Each report shall contain the following information:

(1) Owner of heroin.

(2) Date of removal and place from which removed.

(3) Date and place of final delivery into custody of United States Forces.

(4) Name and title of official transferring custody.

(5) Name, Rank, and organization of Officer receiving custody of heroin.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
