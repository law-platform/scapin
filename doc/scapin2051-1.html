GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.7 (13 Oct 49) CPC/OD

(SCAPIN-2051/1)

APO 500

1 August 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Notification that the United States, United Kingdom and France are Owners of Certain Categories of Former German Property in Japan.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.7 (13 Oct 49)CPC/PLD, SCAPIN 2051, 13 October 1949, subject, "Notification that the United states, United Kingdom and France are Owners of Certain Categories of Former German Property in Japan," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Paragraph 8 of memorandum referenced in paragraph 1 above is hereby rescinded and the following substituted therefor:

"8. The Japanese Government is directed to:

a. Provide the necessary legal implementation to transfer, register and/or record under Japanese Law title to designated purchasers from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, his agents or successors, of all property coming within the provisions of paragraph 3 above when so instructed by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

b. Provide the necessary legal implementation to liquidate, dissolve and extinguish such juridical persons coming within the provisions of paragraph 3 above, as may be designated and will do all acts necessary to liquidate, dissolve and extinguish such juridical persons when so directed by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers through specific memoranda

c. Provide the necessary legal implementation to dispose of all property, tangible or intangible, coming within the provisions of paragraph 3 above, as may be designated and will do all acts necessary with respect to such property when so directed by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers through specific memoranda."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Major General, USA
Adjutant General
