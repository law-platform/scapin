GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 411.1 (10 Dec 46) GD

(SCAPIN-1386)

APO 500

10 December 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Delivery of Lumber to Manila, Luzon, Philippine Islands.

1. References:

a. IJG Memorandum, CLO No. 5983(EE), subject as above, dated 13 Nov 1946.

b. SCAP Memorandum AG 411.1 (30 Oct 46)GD (SCAPIN 1302), subject as above, dated 30 October 1946.

c. SCAP Memorandum AG 411,1 (22 Nov 46)GD (SCAPIN 1352), subject as above, dated 22 November 1946.

2. Reference 1b, above, has been rescinded by reference 1c. Attention is invited to par 4 of reference 1c, which states that these shipments of lumber are to meet the needs of the Occupation Forces, in that they are a partial replacement of 4,000,000 board feet borrowed from United States Army stocks in the Philippine Islands during May 1946 to meet urgent needs of the Occupation Forces.

3. The request contained in reference 1a, above, that subject shipment be regarded as a foreign trade export to supplement the foreign exchange of Japan, is not favorably considered.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
