MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Submission of Monthly Labor Report.

1. Reference. Memorandum for Japanese Government, AG 004.06 (26 Jan 48)OGA SCAPIN 1852, subject: Submission of Monthly Labor Report, 26 January 1948, as amended.

2. Effective 1 April 1950 reference SCAPIN and change thereto is rescinded and such reports will be discontinued after the report of the month ending 31 March 1950.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
