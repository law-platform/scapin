GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.711 (9 Oct 45) GB

(SCAPIN - 111)

APO 500

9 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Request for Information on Japanese Units under Control of War Minister.

1. Information available from Japanese Second General Army indicates that certain Japanese shipping, railway, communications and depot units are in 6th U. S. Army area but are not assigned or attached to the Second General Army. No information concerning such units is available from Japanese military authorities in the 6th U. S. Army area.

2. Information received indicates that these units are under jurisdiction and control of War Minister in Tokyo.

3. It is desired that this Headquarters be furnished information reference above units to include designation, location, strength, responsible Japanese Headquarters to contact reference surveillance and inspection of demobilization of these units.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
