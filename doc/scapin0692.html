GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 130.2 (1 Feb 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-692)

APO 500

1 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Request for use of yen bank accounts by German concerns for the purpose of paying salaries and incidental expenses.

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. Memoranda Nos. 1232 (5.3) dated 20 December 1945, 1423 (5.3) dated 28 December 1945 and 227 (5.3) dated to January 1946 inclosing applications from the following German firms for withdrawals of funds for payment of salaries and other office expenses:

Fritzka Kogysho

Winckier & Co.

2. The Ministry of Finance may release from the terms of the Ordinance of the Ministry of Finance on the preservation of the property of the Specified Powers, 20 September 1945, such funds of the above-mentioned firms as are required to pay expenses in the categories hereinafter specified which were incurred by the firms prior to 1 January 1946:

a. Salaries, wages and other remuneration to which employees are entitled under terms of their employment agreements.

b. Rentals of offices, houses, godowns and telephones.

c. Erectric light and gas bills.

Funds will not be released for any other purpose including, but not by way of limitation, traveling allowance, taxes, lawyers fees and incidental office expenses.

3. No retirement allowances, bonuses, or other remuneration will be paid whicn exceed the amounts authorized by laws and/or ordinances in effect at the time such payments become due.

4. This memorandum shall not be construed to release funds from the blocking provisions of any other memorandum issued by this Headquarters or any other ordinance promulgated by the Imperial Japanese Government.

5. All payments to individuals whose bank accounts are blocked will be made into such blocked accounts.

6. Detailed reports as to exact amounts paid under this authorization shall be submitted to this Headquarters.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel,AGD,
Asst Adjutant General.
