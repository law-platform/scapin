25 July 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Japanese Port Operations and Charges for Port Facilities and Terminal Services

1. References.

a. Schedule of Rates for Port Facilities at Japanese Ports, Annex A, Inclosure 1, SCAPIN 1927, 13 August 1948.

b. Memorandum for the Board of Trade (Boeki Cho) Ministry of Commerce and Industry, 569.14 (18 Mar 49) ESS/FC, BT - 49 - 4399, subject, “Procedures for Billing and Reimbursement of Charges for Port Facilities”, 18 March 1949.

c. SCAPIN 1997, 23 April 1949.

2. In accordance with the provisions contained in reference 1c, the Schedule of Rates for Port Facilities at Japanese Ports, reference 1a, is rescinded effective 2400, 24 April 1949.

3. Effective 0001, 25 April 1949, and pending revision of reference 1b:

a. All charges for port facilities furnished to foreign vessels in Japanese ports which are reimbursable in foreign exchange will be computed by applying the single exchange rate, as prescribed in reference 1c, to the prices authorized in applicable Japanese Government Laws, Ministerial Ordinances and central and local Japanese price board notifications which were in effect as of 2400, 24 April 1949, or those local price board notifications which authorize lower maximum rates than those authorized by central price board notifications.

b. The lowest prices and rates as published in laws, ordinances and notifications referred to are maximum prices and no charge in excess thereof will be assessed. All charges for port facilities, services and supplies, regardless of the type of vessel or cargo involved, will be non-discriminatory as to items chargeable and prices thereof. When prices and rates for any facility, service or supplies are not controlled by Ordinances or notifications referred to herein, the prices will be those prevailing as of 2400, 24 April 1949, or will be as agreed between the supplier and user.

c. The following priorities for port facilities anti other services and supplies required to handle the indicated types of cargo arc established:

(1) First priority: Commanding General, Eighth Army, for Occupation Force cargo.

(2) Second priority: Japanese Government, for civilian relief cargo.

d. All indigenous facilities, services and supplies available for the account of commercial vessels and cargo after the priorities in paragraph 3c have been met will be negotiated directly between the Japanese supplier and user. The Japanese Government will insure that those facilities, services and supplies contracted by direct negotiation for commercial account are not required by occupation forces and that the diversion thereof for commercial account will not interfere with the above priorities.

e. Reimbursement for facilities, services and supplies which are classified as exports will be made by the user to the SCAP commercial account in currency acceptable to the Supreme Commander as set forth in paragraph 1b and amended by paragraphs 2, 3a and 3b herein.

4. Reimbursement for facilities, services and supplies which are not classified as exports may be made directly by the user to the Japanese supplier in yen.

5. Direct communication with the Commanding General, Eighth Army in implementation of the above instructions is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
