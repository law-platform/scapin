GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (22 Oct 46) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1283)

APO 500

22 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Control of Radio Communications.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 676.3 (29 Aug 46)CCS (SCAPIN 1166) dated 29 August 1946, subject: Control of Radio Communications, as amended by Memorandum to the imperial Japanese Government, file AG 676.3 (20 Sep 46)CCS (SCAPIN. 1222) dated 20 September 1946, subject: Control of Radio Communications.

2. The Japanese Ministry of Communications has proposed further changes to the master list of frequencies assigned for use by the Japanese Government as published and amended in the reference memoranda. Additions, deletions, and changes approved subsequent to publication of the master list of frequencies assigned for use by the Japanese Government as amended are listed in Inclosurc 1, to be effective 20 October 1946.

3. Paragraph 2 b 4, Inclosure II of the reference memorandum, is amended as follows:

Type of Emission Carrier Freq. kc. Max. width of Frequency Band, Width of Emission, kc.

A3 100 - 550 6,000

500 -1500 10.000

1500 - 6000 6.000

6000 - and above 10.000

A4 Above 48000 120.000

4. Paragraph 1, Inclosure III of the reference memorandum, is amended as follows:

Class of Station Tolerances 1.5 to 30 MC (Per cent) 30 MC and above

Standard Frequency 0.00003 0.00003

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
