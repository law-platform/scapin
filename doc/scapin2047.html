MEMO FOR RECORD (SCAPIN - 2047)

DJC/MAW/av

16 September 1949

1. References are:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Gov't fm GHQ, SCAP, AG 463.3 (10 Mar 49) ESS/EX, SCAPIN 1984, 10 Mar 49, subj: Stabilization of the Coal Industry.

b. Informal memo fm GHQ SCAP to Dir Gen. ESB, 463.3 (24 Aug 49) ESS, sub: Abolition of SFDK and Removal of Price Control on Coal, 24 Aug 49.

2. Reference a. SCAPIN 1984 imposed upon the Japanese Government the responsibility for providing a minimum of 42,000,000 tons of coal during the TFY 1949-50 without increase in the present consumer coal price or subsidy and without resorting to deficit financing. It also imposed certain restrictions regarding wage payments, overtime work, hiring of workers, payments of taxes and suppliers' bills, bookeeping practices and methods of payment to coal operators by the Coal Distribution Kodan as well as requiring reports on actions taken to implement the directive.

3. Reference b. sites the fact that the improvement of coal production and increasing stockpiles make it necessary that the SFKD stop the purchase of coal no later than 15 September 1949 and that it be liquidated no later than 1 April 1950; that all price and allocation controls on coal be removed but that prices be not allowed to rise sufficiently to adversely affect the selling price of essential commodities in the stabilization band and directs that the ESB shall maintain the authority to reinstate price control at any time if necessary.

4. In view of the facts:

a. that SCAP policy as enunciated in SCAP FEC Staff Memorandum #28, AG 300.6 (15 Jun 49) GC, 16 Jul 49, calls for removal of all non-essential controls over administration of Japanese affairs,

b. that there are presently stockpiled in Japan some 4, 000, 000 tons of indigenous coal and,

c. that the coal industry has demonstrated every evidence that it is capable of producing up to full requirements for the Japanese economy without stringent government direction, it is considered advisable at this time to rescind SCAPIN 1984. Such action taken to abolish the SFDK and remove price controls on coal.

5. Inasmuch as coal is now being produced at the rate of 42,000,000 tons per year, which amount is ample to meet Japanese requirement, it is considered that referenced SCAPIN 1984 has served its purpose and should therefore, be rescinded.

6. No surveillance is necessary.

7. Concurrences: ESS/IND M.M. Class; ESS/IRL.H. Moss; ESS/JF J.R. Smith; ESS/LAB R.T. Amis; ESS/PD L. H. Whittington

M. A. Wyatt 26-8501

G. R. R.
