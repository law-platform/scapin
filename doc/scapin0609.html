GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 635 (16 Jan 46) CCS

(SCAPIN - 609)

16 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Operation of Nippon Electric Company Laboratory.

1. Reference C. L. O. 174 (2.3) dated 12 January 1946.

2. Permission is granted for the operation of the research laboratory of Nippon Electric Company, Limited, Located in Ikuta and Inada both Kawasaki, Kanagawa Prefecture.

3. Researches on the following type equipments are prohibited:

a. Radio and other detection and ranging equipment.

b. Radio and radar interference producing equipment.

c. Pulse modulated communications equipment

d. Electronic proximity devices.

e. Voice secrecy systems.

f. Printing secrecy systems.

g. Television

4. Provisions of SCAP directive No. 3 paragraph 8c which requires a monthly report to be submitted on the activities of the laboratory will continue in effect.

5. Acknowledgment of receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
