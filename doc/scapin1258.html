10 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Revised Listing of Reparations Selections Within Privately-Owned Munitions Plants.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, dated 13 August 1946 (SCAPIN 1134), subject: “Reparations Selections Within Privately Owned Munitions Plants.”

2. The list of Privately Owned Munitions Plants attached to the Memorandum referred to in paragraph 1 is hereby rescinded and the list attached hereto is effective this date.

3. Within seventy-two (72) hours of the receipt of this memorandum, the Japanese Government will have qualified representatives report to the Commanding General, Eighth United States Army, and to the Commander Naval Activities, Japan, to receive instructions with regard to establishing or releasing from custody and control installations affected by revision of the list.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
