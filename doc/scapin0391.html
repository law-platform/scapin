GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (4 Dec 45) CCS

(SCAPIN - 391)

4 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Ship-to-shore Navigational Aids (Radio) of the Lighthouse Bureau.

1. The Lighthouse Bureau, Ministry of Transportation, will restore and operate for civilian use as soon as possible the radio beacon and direction finding stations listed in Appendix I of this memorandum.

2. In the restoration, use may be made of similar existing equipment formerly operated by the Japanese Navy provided that the Home Ministry and the Board of Communications allocates such equipment to the Lighthouse Bureau. In case it should prove expedient to take over and operate Japanese Navy navigational aid stations at locations other than those given in Appendix I such action shall be taken only after receiving the permission of this headquarters.

3. All stations will operate in accordance with pre-war standards and recognized International procedures and only on frequencies that have been cleared through the Board of Communications and this headquarters.

4. These stations are authorized to transmit only messages and signals that are necessary in carrying out their function as navigational aids.

5. A complete report giving call sign, frequency exact location, power, and a description of equipment and procedure for each station will be submitted to this headquarters prior to placing it in operation.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.

INCL:

Appendix I-List of Radio Beacon and Direction Finding Stations.
