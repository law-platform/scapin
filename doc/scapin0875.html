MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Amendment to Memorandum to Imperial Japanese Government, AG 410.2 (12 Feb 46) ESS-FI (SCAPIN 732), Subject: Delivery of Gold, Silver and Precious Metals & Stones by Bank of Japan Branches to Eighth Army.

The indented portion of para.2 is amended to read as follows:

"Gold and Silver coins, except silver coins of 50-sen denomination; gold, silver and platinum bullion or alloys in bullion form; diamonds and other precious stones; precious metals; all currency not legal tender in Japan."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
