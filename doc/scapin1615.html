GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG386.6 (18 Apr 47) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1615)

APO 500

18 April 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Custody, Shipment, Storage, and evaluation of Looted Property.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386.3 (19 Apr 46) CPC, SCAPIN 885, 19 April. 1946, subject, “Impounding and Reporting of Looted Property”, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, directing the Imperial Japanese Government to seek out, inventory, and impound all identifiable looted property in Japan.

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386.6 (25 Jul 46) CPC, SCAPIN 1083, 25 July 1946, subject, “Custody, Shipment, and Storage of Looted Property”;

c. C. L. O. No. 1930 (RP), 19 March 1947, subject, “Report on Looted Property, submitting a list of looted property, designated “List 2 (A)”;

d. C. L. O. No. 1931 (RP), 20 March 1947, subject, “Report on Looted Property”, submitting a list of looted property, designated “List 2 (B)”.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to place all the property listed in “List 2 (A)”, reference 1c above, in warehouses or custody in accordanse with the directions contained in reference 1b above.

3. Imperial Japanese Government is directed to evaluate all the property listed in “List 2 (A)”, reference 1c above, in terms of yen prices existing in Japan for similar articles on 7 December 1941, and to submit to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, a list showing such valuations.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to impound the property listed in “List 2(B)”, reference 1d above, only to the extent of requiring the present custodians of the property not to dispose of such property but to retain possession and custody of the property pending further instructions. The present custodians of the property may make normal use of such property provided it is maintained in its present condition.

5. The Imperial Japanese Government will complete the impounding and storage of the property listed in “List 2(A)” and “List 2(B)” and submit a report thereon by 20 May 1947.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
