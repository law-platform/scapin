GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.1 (10 Dec 45) GS

(SCAPIN-413)

10 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Cessation of Communications Between Japanese Government and Its Former Diplomatic and Consular Representatives Abroad.

1. Except as authorized by this Headquarters, the Japanese Government will, from the receipt of this Memorandum, discontinue all communication between itself and former Japanese diplomatic and consular representatives abroad for any purpose other than to effect compliance with the provisions of the memorandum dated 25 October 1945 from this Headquarters, subject, “Transfer of Custody of Diplomatic and Consular Archives and Property,” and for the purpose of informing such representatives of the provisions of this Memorandum.

2. Receipt of this Directive will be acknowledged.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
