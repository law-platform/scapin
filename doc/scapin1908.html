AG 684 (10 Jun 48) GC/TNG

(SCAPIN - 1908)

APO 500

10 June 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Chitose Aerial Gunnery Range.

1. The rectangular over-water area whose corners are located at the points designated by the following coordinates, is discontinued as an aerial gunnery range:

42° 05' north - 141° 40' east

42° 20' north - 141° 50' east

42° 10' north - 142° 20' east

41° 55' north - 142° 10' east

2. The rectangular over-water area whose corners are located at the points designated by the following coordinates, is designated as an air-to-air aerial gunnery range:

42° 02' north - 141° 50' east

42° 07' north - 142° 30' east

42° 17' north - 142° 00'east

41° 52' north - 142° 20' east

3. The inhabitants of all the ports on the south coast of the island of Hokkaido east of 141° 30' east longitude, and west of 143° east longitude, will be notified prior to each actual use of this range. When the range is used for periods in excess of fifteen (15) days, warning reminders will be disseminated to the inhabitants concerned. This information will be forwarded through Military Government units to local Japanese civil authorities.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
