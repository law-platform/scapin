GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.6 (13 Feb 48) CPC/PLD

(SCAPIN-1858/1)

APO 500

14 February 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Responsibilities of the Japanese Government for the Packaging, Transfer, and Delivery of Property being Restored to Claimant Nations Under the Restitution Program.

1. Reference is made to the following memoranda to the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. File AG 387.6 (22 July 47) REP, SCAPIN 1751, 22 July 1947, subject, “Responsibilities of the Japanese Government for the Packaging, Transfer, and Delivery of Equipment and Records Allocated to Claimant Nations Under the Reparations Program”, as amended.

b. File AG 386.6 (13 Feb 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1858, 13 February 1948, subject, “Responsibilities of the Japanese Government for the Packaging, Transfer, and Delivery of Property being Restored to Claimant Nations Under the Restitution Program.”

2. The Japanese Government is informed that claimant nations may execute, through normal foreign trade channels, commercial contracts for the repair of items approved for restitution to them.

3. If the repair work is accomplished at the location where the property is impounded, the contractor will be paid by an agency of the Japanese Government, in accordance with existing foreign trade procedures, the yen contract price of the repair work done pursuant to the contract. In such instances, the cost to the claimant nation will be the contract price, in acceptable foreign exchange of the repair work. Payment will be made into a designated Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers bank account immediately upon presentation of invoices accompanied by necessary documents. If repair work is accomplished at the location mentioned above, the responsibilities of the Japanese Government remain the same as outlined in reference 1b above.

4. If it is necessary to remove restitution items from their present locations in order to accomplish the repair, the following will apply:

a. Dismantling and packaging of restitution items at the impounding site will be accomplished by the packaging contractor in accordance with the provisions of reference 1b above and designated annexes of reference 1a above, and in coordination with the repair contractor, engaged through normal foreign trade channels, so that excess costs due to repair requirements, will be minimized. All costs of dismantling and packaging at such locations will be borne by the Japanese Government.

b. Provision will be made for the claimant nation to execute receipt forms at the impounding site for the property to be repaired, and it will then be delivered to the Japanese contractor for removal from the impounding site. This receipt will release the Japanese Government from further responsibility for the property as restitution items.

c. Costs of transporting the property from its impounding site to repair location, and subsequently, from repair location to dockside and loading aboard ship will be paid initially by the Japanese contractor engaged by foreign trade contractor to execute repair of the restitution items. The repair contractor will be responsible, after completion of repairs, for repackaging the equipment according to provisions of applicable annexes of reference 1a above, utilizing, whenever possible, the package in which the equipment was received.

d. The repair contractor, after movement of the equipment to be repaired from impounding site, will be responsible for safeguarding such equipment from damage, sabotage, deterioration, or other injuries until it is safely loaded aboard ship, and will carry adequate insurance to assure financial responsibility in the event of any such loss.

e. The contractor will be reimbursed in yen, by the appropriate agency of the Japanese Government, as separate items for the following:

(1) The amount it would have cost to transport the equipment directly from impounding site to dockside and load aboard ship at the port designated by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, in accordance with the provisions of memorandum referred to in paragraph 1b above.

(2) Any cost additional to that shown in e (1) for handling and transporting the equipment from the impounding site to the repair location and thereafter from the repair location to dockside and aboard ship, where the contractor’s responsibility ceases.

(3) The contract price for the repair work done pursuant to the contract in accordance with the existing foreign trade payment procedures.

f. The price to the claimant nation will be the contract price, in acceptable foreign exchange, of the items designated in subparagraph e (2) and e (3) above. Payment will be made as provided in paragraph 3 above.

5. The procedures specified herein are to be considered as standard. If other procedures for repair operations are to apply, the Japanese Government will be so informed in each specific case to which the modified procedures apply.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
