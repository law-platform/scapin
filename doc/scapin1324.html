GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 564 (7 Nov 46) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1324)

APO 500

7 November 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repair and Refitting SS "No. 3 Amoi Maru". ex SS "Islas Visayas," SCAJAP No. A027.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 560(5 Oct 46)CPC/FP SCAPIN1254, 5 October 1946, subject, "Salvage, Repair and Refitting of Captured Vessels", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to take immediate steps to repair and refit the SS "No. 3 Amoi Maru", ex SS "Islas Visayas" in Order that the ship may be returned in a condition substantially similar to that at the time she come into the hands of the Imperial Japanese Government.

3. The SS "Islas Visayas" will be repaired at the Otaru Factory, Hakodate Shipyard, Otaru, where she will be sailed at the end of her present voyage.

4. Photographs, size 8" X 10" will be submitted in duplicate showing three views of the subject ship as follows:

a. From either bow showing bridge looking aft.

b. Profile view.

c. From either quarter looking forward.

5. A further memorandum directing delivery in restitution will be issued by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, as soon as the necessary repairs, refitting and trials are satisfactorily completed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
