GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG130 (25 Oct 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 190)

25 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Funds remitted to Japan by Prince Ri's Household.

1. This Headquarters has been advised that on 19 September 1945 the Akasaka Branch of the Imperial Bank transferred the sum of ¥5, 500,000 to the account of Heiichi Yamashito, Secretary of Prince Ri’s Household pursuant to instructions from its correspondent bank in Korea. The approval of this Headquarters to such remittance was not obtained.

2. You are directed to take appropriate action to obtain the cancellation of such credit and the reversal of the entries made.

3. An acknowledgement of the receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 190)

25 October 1945

IGA/kns

1. In MGFIN 135, CG USAFIK requests that this Headquarters cancel a transfer to Yamachito Heiichi in Asoka Branch of Imperial Bank of ¥5, 500,000 and the return of this sum to the Bank of Chosen.

2. Investigation taken here disclosed that on August 19, 1945 the Grandmaster of Prince Ri's Household in Korea initiated a remittance to Heiichi Yamachito, Secretary of Prince Ri's Household of the sum of ¥5, 500, 000. Instructions were received by the Akasaka Branch of the Imperial Bank on 19 September and the entries completing the remittance were effected on that day. The transaction was a foreign financial transaction within the meaning of proclamation No. 3 and was effected without the approval of the CG USAFIK.

3. The most that this Headquarters can do is to cancel the credit. Any further entries or transfers must be effected in Korea.

4. Recommend dispatch of memorandum and radio.

I. G. A.

Approved:

C. F. T.

Radio referred to (MGFIN 135) reads as follows:

"Prince Ri Household trfd 5 and 1/2 million yen 21 Aug to account of Yamashito Heiichi in Asoka Branch of Imperial Bank. Request cancellation of this transfer with return of funds to Bank of Chosen, Seoul with radio advice to Mil Govt Finance here."
