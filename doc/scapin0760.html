21 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Distribution of Winter Clothing Held for Relief Purposes.

1． Reference AG 400 (21 Dec 45)PH, Memorandum for Imperial Japanese Government, subject: "Reserve Surplies Held for Relief Distribution".

2． It is desired to implement the distribution of all winter clothing and blanlets at this time pursuant to provisions of plan approved in reference above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
