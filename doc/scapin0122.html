GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 686 (11 Oct 45) GD

(SCAPIN-122)

APO 500

11 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :In regard to the Cultivation of Airfields Other than those used by the Allied Forces.

1. Receipt is acknowledged of your memorandum, file number are S215, same subject, dated 8 October 1945.

2. The commander of the Sixth and Eighth U. S. Armies have been directed to release airfields for cultivation as expeditiously as possible.

3. In order to expedite the process of release, the land situated on airfields upon which decision is being reached me to be released leaving some cases the disposition of buildings and other installations for final determination at a later date.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/H. W. Allen
/t/H. W. Allen,
Colonel, A. G. D.
Asst. Adjutant General.
