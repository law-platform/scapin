MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :First, Third and Fifth Supplemental Requests for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in the Second Quarter of 1948.

1. Reference is made to:

a. C.L.C.O. No. 2 (LO 419-FI/2E), 3 February 1948, subject, "First Supplemental Request for Permission of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in Second Quarter of 1948;"

b. C.L.C.O. No. 39 (LO 433 FI/2E), 4 February 1948, subject, "Third Supplemental Request for Permission of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in Second Quarter of 1948;"

c. C.L.C.O. No. 19 (LO 430 FI/2E), 4 February 1948, subject, "Fifth Supplemental Request for Permission of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in Second Quarter of 1948."
