GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.8 (10 May 46) CIE

(SCAPIN - 941)

APO 500

10 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Dismissal of NARITOMO, Masaaki, Principal of Akaiwa-Gun Koyomura High School, Okayama-Ken.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government, on receipt of this Memorandum, will dismiss NARITOMO, Masaaki, from the principal-ship of the Akaiwa-Gun, Koyomura High School, Okayama-Ken, and will prohibit his future employment by any Japanese educational or governmental institution or agency.

2. This action is directed for the following reason:

On 21 January 1946, NARITOMO addressed a group of former military personnel in the grammar school at Onoda Village, Akaiwa-Gun, Okayama-Ken. Investigation disclosed that the speech was inflammatory in intent, aimed at reviving militarism and ultra-nationalism in returned soldiers. This is in violation of paragraph 1a. (1) of AG 350(22 Oct 45)CIE, "Administration of the Educational System of Japan".

3. Action taken under this Memorandum will be reported to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers as soon as completed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
