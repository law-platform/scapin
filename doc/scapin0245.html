GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 373.4 (6 Nov 45) GC

(SCAPIN - 245)

6 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Japanese Participation in Air-sea Rescue Operations.

1. In order to provide for the participation by Japanese surface craft in operations designated to rescue aviators forced down in Japanese waters, it is desired that you issue appropriate instruction to accomplish the following:

a. Crews of all Japanese vessels will be on the alert for airmen known ot believed to be forced down in Japanese waters, If the vessel is in a position to effect rescue of airmen in the distress, it will proceed without delay to the scene of the accident and conduct rescue operations.

All personnel rescued will be cared for in the best possible mannaer until they are returned to a unit of the Allied Occupation Forces.

b. Detailed radio reports concerning rescue of airmen forced down at sea will be transmitted without delay to the Japanese Naval Radio Station in Tokyo, or to its successor agencies following the inactivation of the Imperial Japanese Navy Department. Insofar as its functioning for this purpose is concerned, this station will hereinafter be referred to as the "Rescue Communications Center". Suitable instructions will be issued by you to cause such reports to be assigned the highest priority classification, and to be transmitted either directly to the Rescue Communication Center or vie the minimum practicable number of intermediate point to point stations; it is intended that this will be accomplished within the capabilities of existing communications facilities through the use of special procedures and channels to be designated by you and subject to the approval of the Civil Communications Section of this Headquarters.

c. When a vessel not equipped with radio transmitting apparatus effects the rescue of uninjured airmen in distress, it will proceed according to the best judgment of the Master of the Vessel . Personnel requiring medical attention exceeding the facilities of the rescuing vessel, will be delivered to the nearest port possessing suitable facilities for the proper care of the personnel involved. Upon delivering any rescued personnel to a port, the Master of the vessel will promptly report all particulars, through shore based communications facilities, to the Rescue Communications Center.

d. The Rescue Communications Center will telephone to Advance Echelon Far East Air Forces, all reports and information received pertaining to the rescue of airmen, or aircraft in distress.

e. The Rescue Communications Center will broadcast information received from Far East Air Forces that pertains to aircraft known or believed to be lost in Japanese waters. These broadcasts will be scheduled at least six times during the hours of daylight, selected so as to be well distributed throughout the day. The time schedule and frequency channels for these broadcasts will be as designated by you subject to approval by the Civil Communications Section of this Headquarters. You will require all Japanese vessels, and the shore stat ons with which they communicate, to listen in on these broadcasts, and any vessel in a position to effect the rescue of airmen in distress will proceed without delay to the area indicated and conduct rescue operations.

f. The above prescribed broadcast will be supplemented by any other methods of communication between the Rescue Communications Center and ships in Japanese waters that can be arranged within the capabilities of existing communications facilities. These supplementary methods will be employed in cases where excessive delay would be involved in waiting till the next regularly scheduled broadcast period for the transmission of distress traffic.

g. Any Japanese vessel in a position to initiate rescue operations in response to appeals made by either of the methods outlined in e and f above, will immediately transmit to the Rescue Communications Center a report of its position and intentions with respect to conduct of rescue operations.

2. All special communications arrangements will be submitted to the Civil Communications Section of this Headquarters for consideration, and coordinated with Advance Echelon, Far East Air Forces.

3. For your information, it is estimated that the foregoing procedure may be required for the rescue of aircraft lost in Japanese waters approximated once or twice a month, depending upon the weather.

4. Direct communication between Advance Echelon, Far East Air Forces, and the Imperial Japanese Navy Department (or the successor agency which will assume the function of operating the present Naval Radio Station in Tokyo) is hereby authorized on matters specifically relating to the establishment and operation of the rescue procedure prescribed herein.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
