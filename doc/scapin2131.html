GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 015 (31 Mar 50) LS-L

(SCAPIN-2131)

APO 500

18 November 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Accounting for and Payment of Invoices and Claims Against SCAJAP Ships.

1. References:

a. SCAPIN 547, AG 560(3 Jan 46)GD, 3 Jan 46, subject United States Vessels and Small Craft Operated by Japanese Personnel

b. SCAPIN 2086, AG 544(3 Mar 50)CTS, 3 Mar 50, subject: Japanese Merchant Shipping.

2. This memorandum establishes the procedure effective 1 December 1950 for processing, accounting for and payment of invoices for supplies, services, and claims, admiralty or otherwise, against ships under the operational control of Shipping Control Authority for Japanese Merchant Marine (SCAJAP). The scope of this procedure does not include the processing of invoices for supplies, services and claims payable in yen.

3. The Japanese Government will designate an agency of the Japanese Government to handle invoices for supplies, services and claims, admiralty or otherwise, against ships under the operational control of SCAJAP.

4. Invoices for supplies and services furnished to ships under the operational control of SCAJAP will be handled as follows:

a. Agents furnishing supplies and services will forward invoices therefor directly to the shipowner or operator.

b. The shipowner or operator will certify as to the correctness of the invoices and forward them to the Japanese Government.

c. The Japanese Government will review the invoices and make payment in cash or through an open account where applicable.

5. All claims, admiralty or otherwise, against ships under the operational control of SCAJAP will be forwarded to SCAJAP and thereafter dealt with as follows:

a. Claims which do not involve United States owned vessels or property will be recorded by SCAJAP and forwarded by SCAJAP to the Japanese Government for recording and processing. Thereafter, the Japanese Government will make its recommendation and submit the recommendation together with the documented claim file to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for review.

b. Claims involving United States owned vessels or property will be recorded and processed by SCAJAP and thereafter forwarded to the Japanese Government with SCAJAP's recommendation. The Japanese Government will make an independent review and recommendation and forward its recommendation and documentary claim file to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

c. General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers will review and thereafter approve, disapprove or revise the recommendation of the Japanese Government and will thereafter refer the claim to the Japanese Government for final action consistent with the approved action of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

6. Legal Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, is designated as the staff section of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, for reviewing, approving, disapproving or revising claims submitted by the Japanese Government.

7. Direct communication between Legal Section and other staff sections of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Japanese Government, concerning matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
