GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.8 (8 May 46) CIE

(SCAPIN - 935)

APO 500

8 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Dismissal of HIRAMATSU, Kikuo, Head Teacher, Kibi Shurendojo, Kibi-cho, Okayama-ken.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government, on receipt of this Memorandum, will dismiss HIRAMATSU, Kikuo, from the position of Head Teacher at Kibi Shurendojo, Kibi-cho, Okayama-Ken, and will prohibit his future employment by any Japanese educational institution or agency, either public or private.

2. Investigation of HIRAMATSU, Kikuo, has disclosed that:

a. While serving as Head Teacher of Kibi Shurendojo, Kibi-cho, Okayama-Ken, he was an active exponent of militarism and ultra-nationalism. His employment by any Japanese educational institution or agency is in direct violation of paragraph 1b(l) of AG 350(22 Oct 45)CIE, "Administration of the Educational System of Japan".

b. Books of ultra-nationalistic nature were found hidden in his school, the Shurendojo, despite his claims to the contrary.

3. Action taken under this Memorandum will be reported to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers as soon as completed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General.
