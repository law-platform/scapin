GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 600.1 (4 Mar 46) CCS

(SCAPIN-793)

APO 500

4 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Installation and Operation of Air Navigational Aids at Kushimoto.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will install in the shortest possible time a one-kilowatt low-frequency homing beacon and a quadrant-indicating radio range at Kushimoto Honshu 33°28W-135°48’E). After installation it will operate them on a twenty-four hour per day basis.

2. Technical details will be obtained by direct contact with the Commanding officer,

68th Army Airways Communications System Group, NYK Building, Tokyo.

3. A report will be submitted indicating the estimated date and the actual date of commencement of operation.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
