GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123 (23 Oct 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 180)

23 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Funds carried by Chinese Laborers Repatriated from Japan

1. Reference is made to CLOM No. 245 requesting instructions on subject matter.

2. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, AG 091.31 (12 Oct 45), paragraph 3 a, provides that Chinese Nationals repatriated from Japan may take with them yen currency in amount not exceeding ¥1, 000 per person.

3. Paragraph 3 b of Memorandum cited in 2, above prohibits all exchanges of currency for persons leaving Japan.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 180)

OJM/syk

21 October 1945

1. CLO No. 245 (13 Oct 45) requested instructions relative to carrying home of savings by some 32, 000 Chinese laborers being repatriated from Japan to North China and China.

2. Memorandum AG 091.31 (12 Oct 45) paragraph 3. a. permits Korean and Chinese repatriates to take with them yen currency not exceeding ¥1,000 per person.

3. Mr. Aik concurs.

C. F. T.
