GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123 (28 Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-661)

APO 500

28 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Local Finances.

1. Reference is made to following memoranda pertaining to finances of prefectures, cities, towns, and villages:

a. Memorandum from the Ministry of Finance, L. O. 57, dated 8 December 1945, subject: “Issue of Bonds, Extension of Credit, etc., by the Governments Sub-divisions”, and attached memorandum from Ministry of Home Affairs, dated 5 December 1945, same subject.

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, this Headquarters, file AG 123 (16 Jan 46) ESS/FI, dated 16 January 1946, subject: “Local Finances.”

2. a. The issuance of bonds by local government in the amounts and for the purposes shown below is hereby approved.

Education ¥800,000

Sanitation 5,400,000

Industry 16,500,000

Public Works (Calamities) 56,400,000

Public Works (Ordinary) 23,000,000

Electricity & Gas 3,500,000

Rehabilitation Expenses 122,500,000

Budgetary Deficit 445,600,000

All Others 10,640,000

Total ¥684,340,000

This authorization is in addition to that contained in paragraph 3 of reference 1 - b.

b. All other points raised in reference 1-a have been answered in reference 1 - b.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
