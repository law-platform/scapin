GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (15 Jun 46) LS

(SCAPIN-1027)

APO 500

15 June 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

The Following named Japanese will be apprehended as Suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, EIGHTH Army, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest possible date.

NAME

RANK OR TITLE

PERTINENT DATA

AKABORI, Tooichiro

C.P.O.

Medical Corps,4th Naval Hospital, Truk. Sailed from Truk, 2 Nov 45, aboard Japanese transport Kamikaze, Probable home address：325 Izumicho, Tochigi- Shi, Tochigi-Ken.

AKAI, Einosuke

Lt. Cmdr.

Was on Trul during 1944. Left Truk for Hakata Naval Air Base.

Probable residence in Wakayama Prefecture.

ANDO, (FNU)

Civilian

Director, Butshi Haicu Kumai, Penang, Malaya.

Sailed for Japan, 31 Mar 46, aboard carrier Hosho.

ASAI, Masao

2nd Lt.

Lt.

HASHIMOTO, Koji

Colonel

Commander, 1st Railway Material Depot in 1941. Later Commander of 5th Railway Regiment.

HASHIMOTO, Takebaru

Inspector of Police, Penang, Malaya, from 1942 to 1944. Sailed for Kure on board vessel Tatsuta Maru. Estimated to have arrived 3 Mar 46.

HAYAKAWA, Hiroyuki

Lt.

HIRANO, Noboru

Colonel
