5 September 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Application of Nisshin Cotton Spinning Company, Ltd. (Nisshin Boseki K. K.), for Removal of Kokusaku Pulp Company, Ltd. (Kokusaku Pulpu Kogyo K. K.), from Schedule of Restricted Concerns.

1. References are the following Memoranda:

a. Central Liaison Office Memorandum No. 3720 (LO 953/427), 29 July 1946, subject: "Application of Nisshin Cotton Spinning Co., Ltd. for Release of Kokusaku Pulp Co. from Schedule of Restricted Concerns";

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 004 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC, 8 December 1945 (SCAPIN-403), subject: "Establishment of a Schedule of Restricted Concerns";

c. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 004 (8 Jun 46) ESS/AC, 8 June 1946 (SCAPIN-1004), subject: "Addition of Certain Textile Companies to Schedule of Restricted Concerns."

2. Reference Memorandum la, above, forwards application of Nisshin Cotton Spinning Company, Ltd., for removal of its subsidiary, the Kokusaku Pulp Company, Ltd., from the Schedule of Restricted Concerns established by reference Memorandum lb, above, to which it was added by reference Memorandum lc, above.

3. The above application is denied.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
/t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD.,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1182)

WFM/JMcIH/RAM/gg

21 August 1946

1. CLO Memorandum No. 3720 (LO 953/427), 29 July 1946, forwards application of Nisshin Cotton Spinning Company, Ltd., which was added on 8 June 1945 to the Schedule of Restricted Concerns established by SCAP Memorandum AG 004 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC (SCAPIN-403), subject: "Establishment of a Schedule of Restricted Concerns, "for removal of its subsidiary, Kokusaku Pulp Company, Ltd., from said Schedule.

2. Prior to restricting the subject subsidiary, Nisshin Cotton Spinning Company, Ltd., reported Kokusaku Pulp Company, Ltd., as its subsidiary and listed its shareholdings as thirty-two (32) percent of the total shares outstanding. The applicant now states this report was erroneous and that its shareholdings represent only 3.2 percent of the total shares outstanding.

3. Further investigation of the shareholders of Kokusaku Pulp Company, Ltd., revealed that nine (9) textile firms restricted by SCAP on 8 June 1946, control in excess of sixty (60) percent of the shares outstanding of this subsidiary, and of these, two (2) concerns, each own 9.9 percent. In view of the large degree of control by these huge textile firms, it is deemed advisable to retain the Kokusaku Pulp Company on the Schedule of Restricted Concerns despite the fact that control by any one company is slightly lower than ten (10) percent. Hence, the application is denied.

4. This is considered an administrative matter, and surveillance by the Eighth Army is not required.

5. The interests of USAFIK are not concerned.

6. Concurrences: ESS/LE (Major E. R. Minnich); ESS/IN (Mr. G. T. Walker).

J. McI. H.
