GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 383.7 (21 Nov 45) CIS

(SCAPIN - 328)

21 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Colonel Tadashi Kawashima.

The Imperial Japanese Government will apprehend Colonel Tadashi Kawashima, formerly with Japanese 6th Army Headquarters in Hankow, presently believed to be with the General Army, Fukuchiyama Kyoto, and deliver him to the authorities of Omori Prison Camp for incarceration as a war criminal.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
