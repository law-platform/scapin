GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.12 (4 Nov 45) CIS

(SCAPIN-236)

4 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Tonari-Gumi.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government shall:

a. Submit to this Headquarters within ten days after receipt of this memorandum, the name, rank, title and present location of the principal officers in the Ministry of Home Affairs and other ministries, boards and bureaus responsible for, or concerned with, the activities of the Tonari-Gumi.

b. Prepare and submit to this Headquarters by 30 November 1945 a comprehensive report in English describing in detail the organization structure, functions, jurisdiction, channels of command, policies and methods of operation of the Tonari-Gumi. Six copies are requested.

(1) The report shall include, but not be limited to the following specific matters:

(a) Statement describing in detail the control exercised by the civil police, and in the past by the Kempei-Tai and the Tokumu-Kikan, over the Tonari-Gumi and the manner in which, and the purposes for which, that control is and has been exercised.

(b) A detailed explanation of the legal basis for and the legal structure of the Tonari-Gumi, together with exact copies in English of all laws, ordinances, rescripts and administrative orders concerning or affecting the organization, membership, powers and jurisdiction of the Tonari-Gumi, and concerning or affecting the exercise of any measure of control over such associations.

(2) The information requested is not to be limited to the immediate present but shall cover a sufficiently extended period of the past to permit a thorough understanding of the organization and working of the system.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
