GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091 (7 Nov 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-247)

APO 500

14 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Government Subsidies.

1. You will submit within 20 days to this Headquarters, a complete list of all subsidies, grants or subventions which are now being paid, or are payable under present law, to individuals or legal persons in industry or other fields of enterprise except agriculture. You will not include payments to individuals for relief, nor payments to regional, prefectural, city, town or village governments, except when such payments are intended for the above purposes.

2. In each subsidy, grant or subvention listed, you will show the following information; (a) its purpose; (b) to whom it is paid; (c) the amount of payment to each recipient or each subsidized unit if standard, or formula by which the amount is determined if not standard; (d) number of persons to whom payments have been made since 31 March 1945; (e) total amount of payments since 31 March 1945; and (f) total amount due and owing under present law but not yet paid.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 247)

RCK/CSH/kns

7 November 1945

1. The attached memorandum directs the Imperial Japanese Government to submit a complete list of current subsidies to private enterprise, with data on the purpose, amount and recipients of each subsidy.

2. This information is needed for analysis of government budget and expenditures by the Public Finance group. It is also felt that some of these subsidies, though not necessarily directly military, were undertaken essentially to aid the war effort, and, if not forbidden by the surrender terms and subsequent directives, are at least inappropriate and unnecessary to the peacetime economy. This data is also essential to the work of the Price Control and Rationing Division, and the attached directive has been prepared partially at their request. The Oriental Economist has been requested to submit an analysis of the present subsidy system.

3. Concurrence from: Rationing and Price Control, ESS (Lt. Egvekvist); Industry, ESS (Major O'Bearn); Legal, ESS (Maj. Abrams); Natural Resources, Agriculture (Maj. Leonard).

C. F. T.
