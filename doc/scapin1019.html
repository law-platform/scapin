GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.2 (13 Jun 46) CCS

(SCAPIN-1019)

APO 500

13 June 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Handling of Occupation Force Official Business Over Japanese Domestic Telegraph Circuits.

1. It is directed that the Japanese Board of Communications arrange to receive, transmit and deliver official telegraph traffic filed by the Occupation Forces in accordance with instructions which will be furnished by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Full details as to where and how this telegraph traffic is to be filed and received, and other pertinent details will be covered by supplemental memoranda to the Japanese Government or the Board of Communications.

3. Statements of services rendered throughout Japan in accordance with this memorandum shall be consolidated by the Board of Communications and shall be submitted as a single statement. Additional information regarding the preparation of these statements will be furnished by separate correspondence and instructions.

4. Direct correspondence between Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Japanese Board of Communications on technical matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500.

13 June 1946

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT : Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN 1019), file AG 311.2 (13 Jun 46) CCS, this Headquarter dated 13 June 1946, subject: "Handling of Occupation Force Official Business Over Japanese Domestic Telegraph Circuits"

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government (SCAPIN - 1019), file AG 311.2 (13 Jun 46) CCS, dated 13 June 1946, subject as above.

2. Telegraph services to be furnished in accordance with the subject memorandum are required to provide for the handling of official business between Military Government Agencies located in the various prefectures for which military circuits have not been provided. The service may also be used to supplement existing military communications facilities in emergencies.

3. Instructions covering the establishment and handling of traffic to specific points will be forwarded through command channels from time to time as equipment rearrangements are completed and facilities become available.

4. Surveillance required in this connection will be included with the command channel instructions.

J W MAIN

Lt Col, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
