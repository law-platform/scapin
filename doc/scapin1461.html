GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 430 (13 Jan 47) ESS/PC

(SCAPIN - 1461)

APO 500

13 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Report on Stocks of Staple Foodstuffs

1. Reference is Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 430 (11 Dec 45) ESS/PC, (SCAPIN 421), dated 11 December 1945, subject: Report on Stock of Staple Foodstuffs.

2. The memorandum referred to in paragraph 1 above is hereby rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
for /t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, A. G. D. ,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN 1461)

9 January 1947

1. A memorandum was issued to the Japanese Government on 11 Dec 45 calling for a daily report on food stocks in cities over 100,000 when the stock on hand falls below a ten day supply.

2. The attached memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government rescinds the directive of December 1945. A memorandum from ESS/RS to the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry (draft attached hereto) establishes a 10-day report on the supply and distribution of staple foods in fourteen key cities to replace the daily food stock report which is being discontinued. This change will reduce the administrative burden of preparing a daily report and will give this headquarters current data on food rationing in the form best adapted to the requirements of this Division and ESS/RS.

3. Concurrences: ESS/RS, Mr. Levine.

1 Incl: Draft (as indicated above)

/s/t/ W. S. E.
