GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 314.6 (16 May 46) LS

(SCAPIN - 957)

APO 500

16 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Results of War Crimes Trials.

Notice is hereby given of the following results of War Crimes Trials as reported by British Authorities:

ACCUSED DESIGNATION PLACE OF TRIAL RESULT OF TRIAL

TERADA TAKAO S/Sgt., attached 21 Group IPC. Singapore Not Guilty.

HIRAZAWA ATSUSI Warrant Officer, attached 21 Group IPC. Singapore Guilty. Sentenced to death by hanging.

SHIN SHIGETOSHI S/Sgt., attached 21 Group IPC. Singapore Guilty. Sentenced to death by hanging.

HASE RYOSOKU Cpl., attached 21 Group IPC. Singapore Guilty. To be imprisoned for 5 years

MURATA YOSHITARO Pte,, attached 21 Group IPC. Singapore Guilty. To be imprisoned for 14 year

OKAMURA HIDEO Capt., attached "Z" L of C Signals. Singapore Guilty. To be imprisoned for 7 years

OTODA HIROSHI Sgt Maj., Of Imperial Japanese Army, attached 3/2 Gurkha Rifles ALOR STAR Guilty. Sentenced to death by hanging.

YOSHIMURA EKIO Sgt., of the Imperial Japanese Army, attached 4 Ind Fd Regt R.I.A. IPOH Guilty. Sentenced to death by hanging.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
