GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.7 (4 Dec 46) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1378)

APO 500

4 December 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Release of German-owned Dye-stuff.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to release from the provisions of memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 091.112(13 Sep 45)MG, SCAPIN 26, 13 September 1945, subject "protection of Allied and Axis property, " from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, the following dye-stuff belonging to Doitsu senryo Gomel Kaisha at the locations indicated,

Location Weight kg

Horyuji Tomizato-mura Oaza okidome 535, Nara 19,673.1

Ogi, Nishi-Ogi 314 Honjo-mura, Hyogo-ken 29,802.3

Kyoto Nishi Gekko-cho 19 & 22, Nishinokyo 8,860

Kyoto Karasuma, Akinono-cho 524, Karasuma-Nijo saguaru 7,389

Himeji, Takao-cho No. 637 14,685.4

Gose Kubo, Akitsu-mura Aza Saragi, Nara-ken 12,973.9

Gose Furukawa, Akitsu-mura Aza Saragi, Nara-ken 20,500

No. 94 Honcho 3-Chome Isezaki 12,625

No. 37 Honcno Isezaki 9,835

No. 11 Honcho 1-Chome Isezaki 7,686

Location Weight kg

No. 29 Fukusumi-cho Isezaki 6,493.4

Tobu Tsuun K.K. No. 3001 Hirosawa-Machi Kiryu 12,900

K. Yano, No. 3001 Hirosawa-Machi Kiryu 12,980

H. Tamura, No. 60 Tori 2-chome Ashikaga 14,115

No. 397 Sakai-Machi 33,367.7

Yokohama Hatoba 4,850

Ogi Nishi-Ogi No. 314 4,850

2. The above-mentioned goods will be relinquished to the Textile Bureau of the Japanese Ministry of Commerce and Industry which will contact General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Economic and Scientific Section, Industry Division, for instructions regarding their disposition.

3. These goods will be distributed in accordance with allocations made per instructions in paragraph 2 above and will be sold-at prices established by the Japanese Price Board.

4. All funds derived from the sale of these goods will be deposited in the Bank of Japan in the "Custody Account for the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers," and a complete report thereon will be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
