GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.7 (20 Apr 46) GD

(SCAPIN - 888)

APO 500

20 April 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Petroleum Importation for Japanese Government, April, May, June 1946.

1. Reference is made to directive from Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, dated 14 March 1946, file AG 463.7 (14 Mar 46)GD (SCAPIN 812), subject as above, which is amended as follows:

Diesel Oil April May June Total

Yokosuka 4,000 5,700 6,700 16,400

Kobe 3,000 4,000 4,000 11,000

Sasebo 7,000 10,100 10,100 28,200

14,000 19,800 21,800 55,600

2. The increase in quantities of Diesel Oil for May and June are to provide for the fueling of Japanese vessels engaged in minesweeping activities.

3. With the exception of changes indicated, all other provisions of the original directive remain the same. However, on or before the 25th of each month you will specifically report actual quantities of Bunker Diesel Oil delivered by Petroleum Distribution Control Company during the previous month to Japanese minesweeping vessels.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
