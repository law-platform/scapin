MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Application for Permission to Supply Mixed Gas for the Production of Ammonium Sulfate at Sunagawa Works of Toyo Koatsu K. K. by pipeline from Takagawa Plant of Nihon Jinzo Sekiyu K. K.

1. Reference is made to the following memoranda:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 464.6 (17 May 46) ESS/IN (SCAPIN-962), dated 17 May 1946, subject: "Production, Distribution and Use of Fertilizers."

b. Memorandum to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers from the Imperial Japanese Government, through the Central Liaison Office, C.L.O. No. 3582 (ECI), subject: "Request for Permission to Supply Mixed Gas Necessary for Production of Ammonium Sulphate at Sunagawa Works of Toyo Koatsu K. K. by Pipe Line to be Laid from Takagawa Plant of Nihon Jinzo Sekiyu K. K."

2. Application made in the memorandum referred to in paragraph 1b, above, is approved.

3. A portion of the Takagawa Plant of Nihon Jinzo Sekiyu K. K., consisting of the following major items of equipment and their accessories is added to the approved nitrogen fertilizer plants listed in the memorandum referred to in paragraph 1a, above:

Facility Quantity

Coke oven 40 chambers

Gas producer 6

Byproducts plant 1

Water gas generator 5

Methane cracking plant (purifier) 2

Inorganic sulphur purifier 1

Gas compressor 2

Gas mixing plant 1

N2 separator 1

Steam power plant 1

Electric power plant 1

Machine shop 1

Water pumping station 1

Testing laboratory 1

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ A. J. Rehe
for /t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1118)

ESS/IN JZR/RCAP/rhw

31 July 1946

1. Memo from IJG to GHQ, SCAP through CLO, CLO No. 3532 (ECI), subject: "Request for Permission to Supply Mixed Gas Necessary for Production of Ammonium Sulphate at Sunagawa Works of Toyo Koatsu K. K. by Pipe Line to be Laid from Takikawa Plant of Nihon Jinzo Sekiyu K. K., "forwards the application of Nihon Jinzo Sekiyu, in which approval is requested for the use of a portion of their plant to supply mixed gas to the Sunagawa Works of Toyo Koatsu K. K. for the manufacture of ammonium sulphate and by-product coke to fertilizer plants in Honshu.

2. a. Inspection of both plants on 27 June 1946 by members of the Industrial Division disclosed that the Takikawa plant will be able to feed Mixed gas to the Sunagawa Works immediately upon completion of the pipeline. This new gas supply will increase ammonium sulphate production at Sunagawa Works by about 30,000 metric tons annually, and make construction of additional gas generating facilities at the Sunagawa plant unnecessary.

b. Operation of the coke ovens at Takikawa will permit the feeding of good coke to fertilizer plants in northern Honshu, increasing their production and operating efficiency. At present coking capacity exceeds available coal supply but the ovens in question should be among those who operate, for the reasons stated above.

c. The proposed construction constitutes conversion of a nitrogenous fertilizer plant, and therefore required SCAP authorization, as indicated in the fertilizer directive, SCAPIN 962.

3. By memo through CLO, the IJG is advised as follows:

a. Application for permission for the Takikawa Plant of Nihon Jinzo Sekiyu K. K. to supply mixed gas by pipeline to the Sunagawa Works of Toyo Koatsu K. K. is approved.

b. That portion of the Takikawa Plant needed to supply mixed gas to Sunagawa and produce by-product coke is added to the approved nitrogen fertilizer plants listed in the fertilizer directive, SCAPIN 962.

4„ No concurrence is required.

5. This is an administrative matter and no military surveillance is required.

J. Z. R.
