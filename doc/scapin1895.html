APO 500

16 May 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Hoisting of Flags by the Maritime Safety Board.

1. Reference is made to your letter, C.L.C.O. 1499(2P) dated 27 April 1948, subject, "Application for Permission to Hoist National Flag and Maritime Safety Board's Flag."

2. Permission is granted for the Maritime Safety Board to fly the Board's flag on vessels and on buildings belonging to the Maritime Safety Board.

3. The Japanese national flag may be hoisted on the Board's vessels on those dates on which the display of the national flag has been authorized by SCAPIN 1867, 1 March 1948.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
