GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 800.92 (12 Oct 45) ESS

(SCAPIN - 131)

12 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Oceanographic Survey of Philippine Waters and Japanese Fishing Activities in the Philippine Area.

1. You will supply this Headquarters with detailed reports on the following subjects by 25 October 1945. These reports should be accompanied by appropriate charts, maps, photographs, drawings, and statistical tabulations.

a. Results of the oceanographic survey carried out in

Philippine waters by the Japanese Government.

b. Fishery activities conducted by Japanese nationals in the Philippine area during the period 1939-1945, inclusive.

This should embody detailed information on:

(1) Location of fishing grounds where the Japanese conducted operations;

(2) Species of fish caught;

(3) Operational techniques of craft and fishing gear;

(4) Statistics on the amounts of fish caught by species and localities;

(5) Number and types of fishing boats;

(6) Number of fisherman engaged;

(7) Methods of organizing the fishing enterprises;

(8) List of fishing ports where Japanese landed-their catches;

(9) Description of any share facilities used for handling the catch including processing plants;

Memorandum To Imperial Japanese Government, Subject: Oceanographic Survey of Philippine Waters and Japanese Fishing Activities in the Philippine Area, file AG 800.92 (12 Oct 45) ESS, dtd 12 October 1945.

(10) Description of techniques for salting, drying, or otherwise processing the catch;

(11) Marketing and distribution techniques;

(12) Administration and controls exercesed over the fishing fleet and shore activities.

c. Information on the ultimate disposition of Japanese fishing boats and fishing nets or other fishing equipment and shore facilities following the withdrawal of the Japanese forces from the Philippine Islands.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General
