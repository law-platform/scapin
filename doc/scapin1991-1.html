(SCAPIN - 1991/1)

APO 500

28 April 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Removal of Company from the Schedule of Restricted Concerns

1. Reference Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 004 (9 Apr 49) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 1991, 9 April 1949, subject: Removal of Certain Companies from the Schedule of Restricted Concerns.

2. Paragraph 2bh of reference memorandum is rescinded and the following substituted therefor: Soyo Rubber Industry, Ltd. (Soyo Gomu Kogyo K.K.)

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ A. J. Rehe
for /t/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1991/1)

WFM/ECW/CHS/ld

22 April 1949

1. Reference is SCAP Memo AG 004(9 Apr 49) ESS/AC, SCAPIN 1991, subject: Removal of Certain Companies from the Schedule of Restricted Concerns.

2. Reference memorandum inadvertently listed, in paragraph 2bh, Soyo Rubber Works, Ltd. (Soyo Gomu Kosho K.K.) as a company to be removed from the Schedule of Restricted Concerns. The correct name of this company is Soyo Rubber Industry, Ltd. (Soyo Gomu Kogyo K.K.), a subsidiary of Daiwa Industry Company, Ltd. (Daiwa Kogyo K. K.). Attached Memorandum to the JG corrects this error.

3. This is an administrative matter and no surveillance by the Eighth Army is required.

4. Concurrences: None required.

Charles H. Smith - 26-6560

E. C. W.
