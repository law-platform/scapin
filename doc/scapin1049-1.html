28 June 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Dissolution Plan for the Yokohama Specie Bank

1. Reference Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 004.2 (2 Jul 46) ESS/FIN, SCAPIN 1049, 2 July 1946, subject: Dissolution Plan for the Yokohama Specie Bank.

2. As of this date, the trust relationship existing between the Yokohama Specie Bank (a closed institution in the process of liquidation) and the Bank of Tokyo, involving those domestic assets which remained in the old account of the Yokohama Specie Bank, and which were transferred in trust to the Bank of Tokyo, may be terminated, and the remaining domestic assets held in trust may be returned to the Yokohama Specie Bank and may be liquidated according to the Closed Institutions Ordinance (Imperial Ordinance No. 74, 1947).

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY
Colonel, AGD
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1049/1)

WKL/WNR/HJR/vmh

9 June 1949

1. References are:

a. Memorandum to Money and Banking Branch, 25 May 1949, subject: Trust Agreement Yokohama Specie Bank and Bank of Tokyo, from Williams. Brower, Chief Liquidation Branch;

b. SCAPIN 1049, 2 July 1946, subject: Dissolution Plan for the Yokohama Specie Bank;

c. Cabinet Order No. 115, subject: Readjustment of Assets and Liabilities of the Old Account of the Yokohama Specie Bank.

2. In accordance with subject memorandum, it is necessary to amend paragraph 3 of SCAPIN 1049 in order to provide means for the return of e trust assets to the Yokohama Specie Bank so that they can be sold through the Closed Institutions Liquidations Commissions.

3. Following this amendment the Liquidation Branch will modify or repeal Cabinet Order No. 115.

4. Concurrence: ESS/LB/FIN (C. S. Gordon, Jr.) Verbal

W. K. L.
