AG 684 (18 Dec 48) GC/TNG

(SCAPIN - 1992)

APO 500

12 April 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Tori Shima Bombing and Gunnery Range.

1. The three (3) islands known as Tori Shima, located at coordinates 32°15' North - 128° 06' East, have been designated as an Air Force bombing, and gunnery, and Naval surface firing range. The danger area will be that area within a radius of six (6) miles of the islands. Hours of use of the range will be 0700 to 1700 hours, local time, Mondays through Fridays. Fishing is permitted in the range danger area during those periods when the range is not in use.

2. The local Japanese authorities of the seven (7) prefectures on Kyushu will be recipients of range warning notices and reminders of continuous range use, through military government channels. Such notices and reminders will be disseminated to the inhabitants of the seven (7) prefectures on Kyushu, including small islands adjacent to Kyushu which are under the jurisdiction of any of the seven (7) prefectures.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
