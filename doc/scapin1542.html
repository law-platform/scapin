MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Property Owned on 7 December 1941 by Allied Nationals, which has been Designated for Reparations

1. References are the following memoranda for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. File AG 387.6 (24 Oct 46) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 2443-A, dated 24 October 1946, subject: Allied Property Designated for Preparations.

b. File AG 095 (11 Jan 47) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 2995-A, dated 11 January 1947, subject: Property Owned on 7 December 1941 by Ford Moter Company, which has been Designated for Reparations.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is notified that all Allied Nationals property on the attached lists are hereby exclued from reparations removal.

3. This is the initial list of Allied Nationals property to be excluded from reparations removal; other properties will be added by annexes as necessary.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government will make available to the Commanding General, Eighth Army, all personnel, equipment, and supplies deemed necessary by the Commanding General, Eighth Army for accomplishing the mission of this memorandum.

5. Within seventy-two (72) hours of the receipt of this memorandum, the Imperial Japanese Government will have qualified representatives report to the Commanding General, Eighth Army, and to the Commander Naval Activities, Japan, to receive instructions to accomplish the purpose of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1542)

3 February 1947

1. References are:

a. Radio W87058 from (WDSCA ES) dated 6 December 1946 which states:

(1) Standards for looted property set forth in paragraph 4 of Radio WCL 30315, dated 25 July 1946, applicable to property of Allied Nationals owned as of 7 December 1941, are not to be considered.

(2) As an interim measure, machinery, tools, etc. identified as property 100% owned by Allied Nationals as of 7 December 1941 will be excluded from reparations removal to the extent compatible with removing complete plants and production units.

(3) Allied Nationals' property that is withheld from removal with a view to its eventual restoration to its former owner will be preserved, maintained, and protected until such time as it is restored to its rightful owner.

b. Check Sheet from CPC/FP to ESS/IN, subject: Property Owned by Ford Motor Company in Japan, dated 2 January 1947 and check sheet from CPC/FP to ESS/IN, subject: Property Owned on 7 December 1941 by General Motors, Japan Ltd. (American). These check sheets contained:

(1) Lists of property of the companies concerned, to be withheld from reparations removal.

(2) Requests that ESS/IN take necessary action to remove these properties from reparations listing and direct the IJG to preserve and maintain this property until it is returned to its rightful owner.

(3) Requests that CPC be notified of the action taken.

c. The following memoranda to the Imperial Japanese Government from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

(1) File AG 387. 6 (24 Oct 46) CPC/FP, (SCAPIN 2443-A), dated 24 October 1946, subject: Allied Property Designated for Reparations, which informed the IJG that Allied Nationals' property would remain subject to reparations removal.

(2) File AG 095 (11 Jan 47) CPC/FP, (SCAPIN 2995-A), dated 11 January 1947, subject: Property Owned on 7 December 1941 by Ford Motor Company, which has been Designated for Reparations, which amends reference 1c (1).

2. a. It is necessary to issue this memorandum to the IJG because reference la above changes the policy of making available for reparations claim the property of Allied Nationals as set forth in SCAPIN 2443-A, dated 24 October 1946, as amended by SCAPIN 2995-A, dated 11 January 1947.

b. Reference la above provides for the removal from reparations claim certain properties which have been identified as 100% owned by Allied Nationals as of 7 December 1941. It further provides for the care, maintenance, and preservation of such property until it is delivered to its rightful owner.

c. To include these changes in policy, as directed by WDSCA ES in radio W-87058, 6 December 1946, it is necessary to issue the accompanying memorandum to the IJG and a Command letter to CG, 8th Army on the same subject.

3. Concurrence by CPC - General Tansey.

4. Interests of USAFIK are not concerned.

5. This memorandum to IJG will be published 15 February 1947.

6. 8th Army surveillance is required.

J. Z. R. 25601
