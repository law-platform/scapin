(SCAPIN - 1695)

APO 500

20 May 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Request for Permission for Continued Use of Facilities of Takikawa Chemical Co.. Ltd., for Fertilizer Production.

1. References are:

a. Letter, CLO No. 6816 (ECI), subject: Application for Permission to Use Facilities of Takikawa Chemical Industry Co., Ltd. for Fertilizer Production, dated 23 December 1946.

b. Letter, CLO No. 2785 (ECI), subject: Request for Permission for Continued Use of Facilities of Takikawa Chemical Co., Ltd. for Fertilizer Production, dated 15 April 1947.

c. Memorandum for Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 463.4 (25 Feb 47) ESS/lN SCAPIN 1545, subject: Application for Permission to Use Facilities of Takikawa Chemical Industry Co., Ltd. for Fertilizer Production.

2. a. Request for continued use of facilities referred to in 1b above is denied, except as indicated below:

Equipment Quantity

Gas oil cooler (compressor after cooler) 1

Paraffin trap (mist separator) 4

Water separator (mist separator) 2

b. The conditions established in reference 1a above are still applicable.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. J. Rehe
for /t/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1695)

ESS/IN OF/RCAP/BVH/dr

6 May 1947

1. CLO forwards request by Takikawa Chemical Company to permit continued use of facilities approved in SCAPIN 1545. CLO recommends that approval be granted by GHQ, SCAP.

2. For normal operating conditions the equipment requested is not essential. As pointed out in the Memorandum for Record covering SCAPIN 1545 the equipment was temporarily approved to cover a special condition that existed only this year. Compressor after-cooler and mist separators, however, are considered to be accessory equipment to compressors originally approved by SCAPIN 1118.

3. Memorandum to the IJG denies request and points out that SCAPIN 1545 is still in effect.

4. No concurrence necessary.

5. This problem as it relates to Korea has been considered.

6. Refer questions to: B. V. Hettich, 26-6639.

O. F.
