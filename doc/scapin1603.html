GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (5 Apr 47) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1603)

APO 500

5 April 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Reactivation of the Coastal Radio Service of the Japanese Ministry of Communications to Handle Ship-Shore Traffic for Allied Merchant Shipping.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will extend the service of the coastal radio stations of the Ministry of Communications to supply ship-shore radio communication to Allied merchant shipping.

2. This service will begin on 10 April 1947.

3. Acceptable message, rates, accounting procedures and settlement of accounts will be decided upon by the administration requesting the service and the Japanese Government with the approval of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

4. Acceptable messages, rates, accounting procedures and settlement of accounts applicable to messages sent to or received from United States and British merchant shipping are detailed in Inclosures 1 and 2 attached.

5. The Japanese coastal stations authorized to provide this service are listed in Inclosure 3.

6. Direct correspondence between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Communications, regarding technical matters within the scope of this memorandum, is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

3 Incls.

Incl 1 - Acceptance of messages, rates, accounting procedures and settlement of accounts for U.S. merchant shipping.

Incl 2 - Acceptance of messages, rates, accounting procedures and settlement of accounts for British merchant shipping.

Incl 3 - List of the Japanese coastal radio stations.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

5 April 1947

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT : Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN 1603), file AG676.3 (5 Apr 47)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 5 April 1947, subject: Reactivation of the Coastal Radio Service of the Japanese Ministry of Communications to Handle Ship-Shore Traffic for Allied Merchant Shipping.

1. With reference to the subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. Due to the increase of Allied merchant shipping the Japanese Coastal Radio Service is opened for ship-shore communication for Allied merchant shipping.

3. The Japanese Coastal Radio Service now operates thirteen radio stations with the approval of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for Japanese merchant shipping. The coastal radio stations that provide this service and their operators are capable of providing efficient service for Allied merchant shipping.

4. Messages for ship-shore communication with Allied merchant shipping may be sent from any of the domestic telegraph stations of the Japanese Ministry of Communications.

5. It is expected that Allied Powers other than the United States and British Governments will request this service. When rates, accounting procedures and accounting agencies are agreed to by an administration requesting the service and the Japanese Government and approved by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, notice will be given by separate communications.

6. The Occupation Forces are responsible for receiving and disbursing sums paid by the Japanese Ministry of Communications to the "Transfer Agent for Telephone and Telegraph Message Funds". Instructions pertaining to the execution of surveillance will follow through command channels.

JOHN B. COOLEY,

Colonel. AGD,

Adjutant Gen.
