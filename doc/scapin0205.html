GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.112 (29 Oct 45) ESS-FI

(SCAPIN - 205)

29 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Property of Members of the Philippine Embassy

1. It is directed that you take immediate action to prohibit all transactions involving the bank accounts and other property of members and officials of the Embassy of the former Philippine Republic in Japan.

2. The following is a partial list of properties to which the instructions in paragraph 1 apply:

a. Special current account of Jose B. Laurel Jr. in Yokohama Specie Bank, Tokyo Branch.

b. Fixed deposit of Pacenia H. Laurel in Sangwa Bank, Nara Branch.

c. Fixed deposit of Arsenio Laurel, Yokohama Specie Bank, Osaka Branch.

d. Fixed deposit of J. B. Laurel Jr. in Yokohama Specie Bank, Tokyo Branch.

3. It is further directed that within 15 days, detailed report be furnished to this Headquarters of action taken under this memorandum, including a complete listing of the names, addresses and official positions of all persons affected and a full statement of the types and locations of the property thereof.

4. An acknowledgment of the receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 205)

29 October 1945

CFT/OJM/kns

1. A partial list of personal property belonging to members of the Philippine Embassy was transmitted to Finance Division, ESS by Captain W. H. Sayer, 80th CIC Detachment on 26 October 1945.

2. Certain members of the Philippine Embassy are now in process of repatriation from Japan and danger exists that their funds and property may be dissipated unless blocking controls are applied.

3. Concurrences: Mr. Alk and CIC.

C. F. T.
