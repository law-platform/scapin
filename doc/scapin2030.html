SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 130 (19 Jul 49) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-2030)

APO 500

19 July 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Foreign Creditors’ Yen Deposit Account.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 091.12 (21 Sep 45) MG, SCAPIN 39, 22 September 1945, subject, “Protection of Property of Friendly Nations and Their Nationals,” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The Japanese Government is directed to establish a yen account in the Bank of Japan, Tokyo, to be known as “Foreign Creditors’ Yen Deposit Account,”for the purpose of receiving from debtors in Japan, who are liquidating or dissolving, or from other categories of debtors to be designated by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, deposits in favor of the following categories of creditors whose claims were not affected by Japanese Law No. 99, “Hostile National’s Property Control Law,” of 22 December 1941:

a. Non-resident foreigners (including persons of former Japanese colonial or occupied areas).

b. Juridical entities with head offices abroad whose representatives and/or agents or liquidators in Japan have not filed claims for amounts due.

3. The Japanese Government will be responsible for maintenance of the account in accordance with memorandum reference 1 above.

4. The Japanese Government will authorize withdrawals from this account either on direction of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, or on demand of beneficiaries who appear in Japan or their duly appointed agents.

5. The Japanese Government is directed to cause the establishment and maintenance of such records as will enable it, commencing 1 August 1949, to submit monthly reports to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. Sample form for these reports is inclosed herewith.

6. Within fifteen (15) days of date of this memorandum, the Japanese Government will submit to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Civil Property Custodian a plan for implementing this directive.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
