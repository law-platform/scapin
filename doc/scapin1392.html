GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.7 (10 Dec 46) GD

(SCAPIN - 1392)

APO 500

10 December 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Allocation of Petroleum for Rehabilitation of Surplus Civilian Type Military Supplies of United States Government.

1. References:

a. Memorandum, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, to Imperial Japanese Government (SCAPIN 2260A), file AG 463.7 (25 Sept 46)GD, dated 25 September 1946, subject, "Petroleum import for Imperial Japanese Government."

b. Memorandum, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, to Imperial Japanese Government (SCAPIN 2325A), file AG 463.7 (6 Oct 46) GD, dated 6 October 1946, subject, "Petroleum Import for Imperial Japanese Government."

c. Memorandum, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, to Imperial Japanese Government (SCAPIN 2670A) file AG 463.7 (26 Nov 1946) GD, dated 26 November 1946, subject. "Petroleum Impart for Imperial Japanese Government."

d. Memorandum, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, to the Imperial Japanese Government (SCAPIN 1313), file AG 400.703 (5 Nov 46) ESS, dated 5 November 1946, subject, "Sale to Imperial Japanese Government as Surplus of Approved Civilian-Type Military Supplies of United States Government."

e. General Contract, Foreign Liquidation Commission, dated 22 November 1946.

2. a. Arrangements have been completed for supply of the quantities set forth below to accommodate the 2150 vehicles which are to be delivered at Goodyear:

b. Allocation: Gasoline (62 Cetane) 132 KL

lubricating Oil 1.3 KL

Brake Oil 1.3 KL

c. Authority is granted to vary quantities specified not to exceed 10 percent of the total of any one item.

3. Delivery will be made by Petroleum Distribution Company (or their legally appointed successor) to the Board of Trade (Boeki Cho) as the authorized representative of the Imperial Japanese Government. Delivery schedule has been furnished Petroleum Distribution Company.

4. The above listed products are intended to be used in removal of the surplus civilian type military equipment of the United States Government which have been sold to the Japanese Government from United States Army storage to Japanese Government storage, and for the quantities required to conduct "road tests" as may be necessary, to place as many of the vehicles referred to in paragraph 2 above, that can be reclaimed, in operation.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
