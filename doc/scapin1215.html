GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.77 (18 Sep 46) CCS

(SCAPIN-1215)

APO 500

18 September 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Authorization of Radiotelegraph Newscast Service From Japan.

1. The following radiotelegraph newscast services now operating from Japan are approved:

a. Transmissions on frequencies 14595 kc and 18570 kc with maximum power output of 5 and 2.5 kilowatts respectively, using A1 type emission with transmissions beamed in the direction of San Francisco and South China Sea area.

b. Transmissions on frequencies 8175 kc, 12275 kc and 15945 kc with maximum power output of 2.5, 2.5 and 2 kilowatts respectively, using A1 type emission with transmissions beamed to cover East Asia and South China Sea area.

2. No changes will be made in the services set forth above, nor will additional service be established, without formal authorization from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers in accordance with memorandum from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers to the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 676.3 (20 Nov 45)CCS, dated 20 November 1945, subject: "Control of Radio Communications".

3. The radiotelegraph newscast services described in paragraph 1 above will be available to all non-Japanese news agencies which have been licensed by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers to operate in Japan, and to Japanese news agencies which have been specifically authorized by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers to make newscasts from Japan.

4. Newscasts now being made by Kyodo News Agency to Japanese repatriation camps in China and the South China Sea area under an informal authorization granted in1945 are hereby formally authorized.

5. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, SCAPIN 43, AG 000.77(22 Sep 45)CI, dated 22 September 1945, subject: "Radio Code for Japan," and any subsequent memorandum pertaining to broadcast censorship will apply to all newscasts by Japanese news agencies.

6. Direct correspondence between the Ministry of Communications and Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers pertaining to technical details within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

18 September 1946

MEMORANDUM.

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN - 1215), file AG 000.77 (18 Sep 46) CCS, this Headquarters, dated 18 September 1946, subject: "Authorization of Radiotelegraph Newscast Service from Japan".

1. With reference to the subject memorandum the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The purpose of this directive is to formally authorize radiotelegraph newscasting from Japan by news gathering agencies for simultaneous reception at many receiving points by subscribers or representatives of news agencies. Informal authorization was granted in October and November, 1945.

3. At present Associated Press, United Press, Kyodo News Agency, Agence France Presse and Central News Agency of China are using the service.

4. Newscast material transmitted by Japanese news agencies is censored by Civil Censorship Detachment, G-2, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

5. The Eighth Army will provide the necessary surveillance to insure that instructions set forth in the subject memorandum are compiled with.

6. Command instructions pertaining to the execution of surveillance will follow through command channels.

R. G. HERSEY

Lt Col, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
