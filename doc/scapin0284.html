GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 720 (15 Nov 45) ESS/PC

(SCAPIN-284)

APO 500

15 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Collection and Distribution of Perishable Foods.

1. Reference is made to CLO No. 526 (2) requesting permission to revise the present system of collecting, pricing, and distributing perishable foods for consumption in urban areas.

2. The principal points in the proposed plan are:

a. Authorized Agricultural and Fishery Associations and agencies will collect all perishable foods to be sold by producers.

b. Authorized dealers from urban areas will negotiate with Agricultural and Fishery Associations for the bulk purchase of perishable foods.

c. Perishable foods will be distributed from urban markets to licensed retailers for resale to consumers through present rationing organizations.

d. Prices to consumers will be determined by the purchase price from Producers’ associations plus transportation, handling, and service costs. Prices will be supervised by the government and any profiteering by dealers, handlers, or interested agencies will be subject to penalties under existing laws and regulations.

e. Prices and quantities of foodstuffs to be distributed will be publically announced. Committees selected by members of street and neighborhood associations will supervise price and rationing controls over perishable food distribution to insure equal distribution to all citizens.

f. Farmers and fishermen will be provided daily necessities and input requirements at legal prices in proportion to the amounts of perishable foods sold to authorized purchasers.

g. Official prices and restriction of individual transportation of foodstuffs will be removed.

h. Small farming and fishing villages will supply their needs as heretofore.

3. You are directed to put the above plan into immediate operation. The Imperial Japanese Government will insure that the maximum supply of perishable foods is transported to urban areas through authorized dealers.

4. The following monthly reports, by city, for Tokyo, Yokohama, Osaka, Nagoya, Kyoto and Kobe will be submitted to this headquarters by the fifteenth of the following month. The first report will be made by 15 January, 1946.

a. Purchase, wholesale, and retail prices of principal perishable foods.

b. Total quantities of principal perishable foods distributed in each city stated in metric tons.

c. The average daily per capita consumption of principal perishable foods stated in metric weights.

5. Acknowledgement of the receipt of this memoradum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
