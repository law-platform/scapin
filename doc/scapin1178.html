GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 611 (5 Sep 46) CE

(SCAPIN-1178)

APO 500

5 September 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Naming and Marking of Streets in the Tokyo Area.

1. In order to facilitate the travel of Allied Force personnel and in order to provide a basis for the future development of the Tokyo street system, a plan has been prepared for designating streets in the city of Tokyo. Three copies of a map outlining the plan are attached.

2. It is desired that the streets indicated on the attached map be marked with an adequate number of street signs by 1 December 1946. The Commanding General, Eighth Army, will issue necessary instructions to you concerning the preparation and posting of these signs.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

1 Incl: City Map of Tokyo with Street Overlay (3 cy)

(to addressee only)
