GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311 (11 Jan 51) CCS

(SCAPIN-2143)

APO 500

1 March 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Telegraph Delivery of International Radio and Domestic Messages Addressed to Army Post Offices.

1. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311 (1 Aug 50) CCS, SCAPIN 2117, 1 August 1950, subj: Telegraph Delivery of International Radio and Domestic Messages Addressed to Army Post Offices is rescinded.

2. Effective immediately all international and domestic telegrams which contain Army Post Office numbers in the address will be handled as follows:

a. Telegrams received in Tokyo and addressed to an individual or agency in Tokyo will be delivered by messenger to the Army Post Office named in the address of the telegram for ultimate delivery through Army postal channels.

b. Telegrams received in Osaka and addressed to an individual or agency in Osaka will be delivered by messenger to the Army Post Office named in the address of the telegram for ultimate delivery through Army postal channels.

c. Telegrams received in Fukuoka and addressed to an individual or agency in Fukuoka will be delivered by messenger to the Army Post Office named in the address of the telegram for ultimate delivery through Army postal channels.

d. Telegrams received in Japan and addressed to an individual or agency located in a city other than Tokyo, Osaka, or Fukuoka will be transmitted electrically to the telegraph office serving the city, town, or village in which the Army Post Office named in the address is located and there will be delivered by messenger to the Army Post Office for ultimate delivery through Amy postal channels.

3. Effective immediately, any international or domestic telegrams addressed to United Spates Occupation Force military and accredited civilian personnel, which do not include an Army Post Office number in the address will be regularly transmitted and delivered by telegraph messenger to the addressee. If the addressee cannot be located for any reason, the telegram will be transmitted by wire to Yokohama for delivery to Central Mail Directory, APO 503 for handling through Army postal channels.

4. The Adjutant General's Section (Postal Branch) General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, will undertake to immediately furnish directly to the Chief, International Telecommunication Division, Ministry of Telecommunications, information showing city, town, or village locations of all Army Post Offices located in Japan. This information the Chief of the International Telecommunication Division of the Ministry of Telecommunications will make available to the telegraph offices at Tokyo, Osaka, and Fukuoka for telegraph handling purposes only, and dissemination will not be made to unauthorized perons.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

GEORGE R. CONNOR,
Colonel, AGC
Acting Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

MEMORANDUM

1 March 1951

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number SCAPIN 2143, file AG 311 (11 Jan 51)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 1 March 1951, subject: Telegraph Delivery of International Radio and Domestic Messages Addressed to Army Post Offices.

1. With reference to subject memorandum the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The purpose of the subject directive is to instruct the Japanese Government to transmit by telegraph wire to its telegraph office serving a city in which a particular APO number is located, any telegram containing that particular APO number in the address and its delivery by messenger to the APO for delivery to the addressee through Army Postal channels. This handling will substantially decrease the handling time now required for telegrams addressed to any APO located in Japan other than APOs 500, 503 and 925, Telegrams are presently being handled in accordance with this plan for APOs 500, 503 and 925.

3. The AG Section (Postal Branch), GHQ-SCAP, will keep the MOTC informed of the city locations of APOs located in Japan on an up-to-date basis, so that telegrams containing APO numbers in the address may be transmitted to the proper telegraph office serving the city in which a particular APO is located,

4. The subject directive provides that incoming international telegrams and domestic telegrams for United States military and accredited civilian personnel which do not contain APO numbers in the address will be regularly transmitted and delivered by messenger in accordance with international regulations; however if the addressee cannot be located for any reason the telegram will be transmitted by wire to Yokohama, for delivery to the Central Mail Directory, APO 503 for handling through Army Postal Channels.

A.J.REHE

Lt Col, AGC

Asst Adj Gen
