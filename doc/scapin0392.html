GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 130 (3 Dec 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-392)

APO 500

5 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Japan Securities Exchange (Special Account).

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will immediately impound all currency, securities, notes and other assets of the account of the Japan Securities Exchange described as “Japan Securities Exchange (Special Account).”

2. Within one week from the date of this directive a detailed financial statement of this account as of November 30, 1945, together with a list of all securities and other assets in the account shall be delivered to this Headquarters.

3. Such action as is required shall be taken to prevent and prohibit, without prior permission of this Headquarters, any transaction involving the assets of this account.

4. A report will immediately be made to this Headquarters as to the action taken to comply with this directive.

5. An acknowledgment of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
