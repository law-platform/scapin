GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.4 (14 Dec 45) GC

(SCAPIN - 439)

14 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Removal of Specified Property from Japan to Korea.

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. No.965 (5.1), dated 10 December 1945, subject: "Application by a Korean for Permission to ▲▲ Sawing Machine to Korea."

2. This headquarters has no objection of the removal from Japan to Korea by Mr. Keiko Arai, upon occasion of his repatriation ▲▲ this month, of the portable hand sewing machine described in the inclosure to above cited reference. This does not, however, constitute authority to increase the applicant's baggage allowance on space aboard the repatriation vessel.

3. A copy of this memorandum will be furnished Mr. Keiko Arai for presentation to the appropriate port processing authorities as his authority to remove subject sawing machine

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
