GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091 (22 Jun 46) GB/CIS

(SCAPIN-1032)

APO 500

22 June 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Chinese Nationals.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will immediately apprehend and deliver to the Commandant, Sugamo Prison, for incarceration, the following persons, formerly Chinese puppet government officials:

FAN, Han-sen Consul-General, Yokohama

LIN, Wen-shiu Consul, Yokohama

FAN, Chien-sen Vice consul, Yokohama

WU, Feng-wu Vice consul, Kobe

WONG, Wu-sun Vice consul, Kobe

YIN, Tsin-tse Chancellor, Kobe

HUANG, Soong-teh Chancellor, Osaka

MAO, King-fan Consul, Nagasaki

TSENG, Ting-chuen Vice consul, Nagasaki

HSIEH, Chi-kuang Chancellor, Nagasaki

MI, Kuo-ying Chancellor, Nagasaki

TSAO, Nai-ho Chancellor, Nagasaki

KUNG, Hsiang-liang (Major) Asst Naval Attache, Chinese Embassy

FU, Kuo-jui (Capt) Asst Naval Attache, Chinese Embassy

WU, Pao-tseng (Capt) Asst Naval Attache, Chinese Embassy

TIENG, Sou-tseng (Col) Secretary, Naval Attache's Office, Chinese Embassy

SUN, Shih Advisor, Chinese Embassy

SUN, Li-fu Counselor, Chinese Embassy

TAN, Chiao-chen Advisor, Chinese Embassy

YEH, Hsueh-ting Vice consul, Yokohama

2. The Imperial Japanese Government will advise this headquarters when the above individuals have been delivered to the authorities of Sugamo Prison. In the event the Japanese Government is unable to make delivery of any of these individuals by 2400 hours, 15 July 1946, it will make a report in detail giving the present location of the individual not arrested, and the reason for failure to arrest.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
