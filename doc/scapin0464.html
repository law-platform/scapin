GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 240 (20 Dec 45) ESS/DA

(SCAPIN-464)

20 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Control of Salaries and Allowances of Employees.

1. Reference is made to your Central Liaison Office Number 860 (2. 1), dated 4 December 1945, in which you requested approval for the creation of a temporary commodity price allowance and an increase in the rate of the family allowance. Proposals recommended in subject letter are hereby approved following revisions are accomplished therein:

a. The term “government officials” will be stated to apply to all employees of the government.

b. The proviss that no more than fifty percent of the total sum of wages paid at the respective working places will be exceeded in computing the total increase to be paid workers, will be eliminated.

c. The total of increases authorized as a result of either the temporary basic commodity allowance or increase in basic wage, or both, shall not exceed One Hundred Yen (¥100.00) per month.

d. This proposal is approved as a temporary expedient only and will be subject to review and reconsideration by this headquarters at any time.

2. Acknowledgment of receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
