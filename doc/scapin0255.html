GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 829 (8 Nov 45) GC

(SCAPIN - 255)

8 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Transfer of Naval Beacons Under the Control of the Japanese Navy to the Ministry of Transportation.

1. As requested in C. L. O. No. 344, from the Central Liaison Office (Tokyo), dated 23 October 1945, permission is granted to transfer the Naval beacons named in the inclosure to the Lighthouse Bureau of the Ministry of Transportation.

2. Permission is also granted to establish branch offices of the Lighthouse Bureau at Yokosuka, Maizuru, Sasebo, Kure and Ominato for the purpose of servicing the beacons. Authority is granted for the branch office staffs and lighthouse watch boats to enter the above naval port areas for the purpose of carrying out their duties subject to the requirement that permission be obtained from local United States authority prior to entry.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
