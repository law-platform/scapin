GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.31 (9 Aug 48) ESS/FTC

(SCAPIN - 1926/2)

APO 500

17 December 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Return from Abroad of Commercial Quantities of Manufactured Articles for Repair and Return to Owner

Reference is memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 091. 31 (9 Aug 48) ESS/FTC, SCAPIN 1926/1, 19 November 1948, subject: Return from Abroad of Commercial Quantities of Manufactured Articles for Repair and Return to Owner. Paragraph 5. b. is deleted and the following substituted therefor:

"5. b. That for return by international mail of unsealed packages

(1) ' Registration is not possible for packages whose length, breadth and thickness combined exceed 36 inches, and whose weight is in excess of two (2) pounds, three (3) ounces;

(2) Weight limit will be according to the Universal Postal Union Parcel Post Agreement and/or applicable agreement, but not exceeding twenty-two (22) pounds per parcel;

(3) Prohibited articles are listed in Article 16 of the Universal Postal Union Parcel Post Agreement."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ A. J. Rehe
for /t/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General
