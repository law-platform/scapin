APO 500

27 January 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Importation of Goods from Germany and Other Countries Manufactured Under or Carrying Vested Industrial Property Rights.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.7 (13 Oct 49)CPC/PLD, SCAPIN 2051, 13 October 1949, subject, "Notification that the United States, United Kingdom, and France are Owners of Certain Categories of Former German Property in Japan," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The term "former German property," in referenced paragraph 1 above, within the meaning of this memorandum, includes patents, utility models, designs, trade names and trade-marks as property owned ty the United States, United Kingdom and France for whom the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers is trustee.

3. For the purpose of permitting importation to Japan from the Federal Republic of Germany and other countries of goods which carry vested trade-marks or trade-names legally applied in such countries, the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, as trustee for the Three Powers who are the owners of such trade-names or trade-marks in Japan, will, upon application, give consideration to the granting of individual licenses to importers in Japan to import and offer such goods for resale in Japan. The foregoing procedure is also applicable to goods produced lawfully in such countries under inventions or processes which have patent protection in Japan now owned by the above mentioned Three Powers. Such licenses will be non-exclusive, royalty free and revocable at will and will normally be granted provided that:

a. It does not conflict with any effective exclusive agreement to which the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers is a party by virtue of his trusteeship

b. It will not result in the previous German owner of the vested industrial property right obtaining a degree of control, directly or indirectly, over such property right considered undesirable by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

c. The firms outside the Federal Republic of Germany, which use the vested marks on their products, have been either

(1) legitimate manufacturers or users under the marks in their home countries since before 7 December 1941 or

(2) have become legitimate manufacturers or users under the marks since 7 December 1941.

4. Attention is directed to the fact that the license referred to in paragraph 3 above does not grant permission to apply the trademarks or trade-names in Japan nor to make the patented articles nor to use the patented process in Japan, but merely authorizes the importer to use, sell and advertise the imported articles in Japan.

5. Licenses will be granted in accordance with the following procedures:

a. Applications therefore will be made to the Patent Agency, Ministry of International Trade and Industry, Japanese Government and will include the following information:

(1) Regarding patents, utility models and designs

(a) Name, address and nationality of applicant (importer)

(b) Name and address of manufacturer or supplier of goods to be imported

(c) Registration number, including title and classification, name of registered owner (former German owner) and a short description of the invention or process

(d) Description of goods manufactured under such right and the total amount to be imported

(2) Regarding trade-marks and/or trade names

(a) Information required by 5a, (1) (a) and (b) above

(b) Registration number, registered owner, classification and photostatic copies or an exact copy, in duplicate, of the trade-mark or trade-name affixed on the products to be imported

(c) In cases where goods are imported from manufacturers or suppliers in any country other than the Federal Republic of Germany, evidence that such firms have been either

(1) legitimate manufacturers or users under the marks in their home countries since before 7 December 194l or

(2) have become legitimate manufacturers or users under the marks since 7 December 1941.

b. All applications must be typed or clearly printed in English in quadruplicate copies

c. The Patent Agency will transmit such applications, together with a statement certifying the correctness of information contained therein insofar as can be determined from the official records of the Patent Agency, to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, for appropriate action

d. General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers will within a reasonable period of time forward to the Patent Agency for transmittal to the applicant, formal approval or disapproval of the application.

6. The Japanese Government will not be required to take any action under Article 34 of the Trade Mark Law in those cases where the importers are granted permission to import and offer for sale in Japan goods which carry vested trade-marks and/or trade names legally applied in the country from which such goods are imported.

7, The Japanese Government is directed to take necessary steps to advise all importers and foreign exchange banks in Japan of the contents of this memorandum and that import licenses will not be granted in the future for importation of goods described in paragraph 3 above without presentation of:

a. A statement that the articles to be imported have not been manufactured pursuant to a patented invention or process which has patent protection in Japan and/or do not carry trade-marks or trade names that are now owned by the Three Powers or

b. The license from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers to use, sell and advertise such articles in Japan.

8. The Japanese Government is further directed to submit to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers a copy of the filed statement referred to in paragraph 7a above, together with a copy of the corresponding application for the import license.

9. Direct communication between the interested sections of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the appropriate agencies of the Japanese Government is hereby authorized in the implementation of the instructions contained herein.

10. The Japanese Government will submit to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, not later than thirty (30) days from the date of receipt of this memorandum, a report setting out the steps and procedures taken to implement the provisions of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
