AG 000.5 (19 Oct 45) LS

(SCAPIN - 158)

APO 500

19 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Command Exercised by General Tomoyuki Yamashita During the Period from 7 December 1941 to 2 September 1945.

1. It is directed that you furnish this headquarters, within five days, copies of duly authenticated official documents or other official evidence setting forth the following:

a. The geographical extent of the command of General Tomoyuki Yamashita from 7 December 1941 and 2 September 1945.

b. The code names, numbers and other designations of Japanese forces (Ground, Naval and Air), including company level and above, which were under his command between 7 December 1941 and 2 September 1945.

c. The order, or orders, upon which General Tomoyuki Yamashita was given command of the area and forces as set forth in "a" and "b", above.

d. All orders, directives, reports or communications issued to General Tomoyuki Yamashita from higher headquarters concerning the defense or destruction of the city of Manila and provinces within the island of Luzon, Philippine Islands, including the extermination of the civilian population, from January to September 1945.

e. All orders, directives, reports or communications issued to General Tomoyuki Yamashita to subordinate commanders concerning the defense or destruction of the city of Manila and provinces within the island of Luzon, Philippine Islands, including the extermination of the civilian population, from January to September 1945.

f. The biographical and service record of General Tomoyuki Yamashita from 7 December 1941 to 2 September 1945.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
