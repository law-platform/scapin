SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (14 Jul 47) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1744/26)

APO 500

4 August 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Control of Radio Communications.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 676.3 (14 Jul 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1744, 14 July 1947, subject: Control of Radio Communications, as amended.

2. Inclosure 1 is a combined list of all permanent Japanese fixed and land radio stations. Temporary authorizations in general are excluded from this list, however, a few which affect permanent service requirements are added.

3. Inclosure 2 is a list of all permanent Japanese ship, coastal, and marine beacon radio stations.

4. Inclosures 1 and 2 of this memorandum include all the permanent Japanese radio stations that were authorized by the reference memorandum and all additions, deletions, and changes that have been subsequently authorized by memoranda to the Radio Regulatory Commission up to 1 July 1950. The authorizations for the stations listed in these inclosures are reaffirmed by this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K. B. BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General.

2 Incls (Limited Distribution)

1. List of Japanese Radio Stations

2. List of Japanese Ship, and Coastal and Marine Beacon Radio Stations
