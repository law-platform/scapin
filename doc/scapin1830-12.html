GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.3 (6 Dec 47) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1830/12)

APO 500

5 July 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Japanese Overseas Radiotelephone Service.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.3 (6 Dec 47)CCS, SCAP1N 1830, dated 6 December 1947, subject same as above, as amended.

2. Radiotelephone calls routed via the United States and the United Kingdom, between Japan and Eire, are authorized, effective 1 August 1949.

3. All provisions of SCAPIN 1830/2 applicable to calls between Japan and the points listed in Inclosure 1 thereto will apply equally to calls between Japan and Eire.

4. Rates and division of revenue for this service will be as shown in Inclosure 1.

5. Direct communication between Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Telecommunications, regarding operating and accounting procedures within the scope of this memorandum, is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
