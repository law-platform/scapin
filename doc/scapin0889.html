GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 260 (20 April 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-889)

APO 500

20 April 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Refund of Salary Deposits in Pension Fund and Application of General Welfare Annuity Insurance to Ex-Servicemen.

I. Reference is made to Memorandum of Imperial Japanese Government, C. L. O. No. 519 (1.1) dated 2 February 1946 subject: “Pensions and Benefits,'’ and Memorandum to Imperial Japanese Government, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 260 (24 Nov. 45) ESS/FI, subject: “Pensions and Benefits.”

2. Memorandum to Imperial Japanese Government, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 260 (24 Nov. 45) ESS/FI, subject: “Pensions and Benefits” does not preclude refund to ex-servicemen of amounts deducted from their salaries and paid into the pension fund.

3. Refunds of the amounts deducted from the ex-servicemen’s salaries are to be made in cash and are not to be applied as premiums for welfare annuity insurance for the period they were in the service.

4. Copies of any ordinance or instructions prepard to carry out the above authorization should be transmitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for the completion of its records.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 889)

WFM/JEW/fwl

18 April 1946

1. The Japanese Government is proposing extension of its existing system of Welfare Annuity Insurance to ex-servicemen. This step is being taken due to the termination required by GHQ, SCAP, of all payments of pensions emoluments, or benefits of any kind granted by reason of Military Service.

2. All servicemen except for Privates made monthly contributions amounting to 1% of their salaries to the Pension Fund. No objection is seen to the refunding of such payments inasmuch as it was originally part of their ordinary pay.

3. Such refunds are not permitted to be applied as Welfare Annuity Insurance premiums for the period of Military Service. It is considered that this would give ex-military personnel credit for their time in Service; thus running counter to the basic JCS Directive concerning Pensions and Benefits.

4. This is an administrative matter, and no surveillance is required. The interests of USAFIK have been considered and are not affected.

5. Concurrences were received from GS, Public Health and Welfare and ESS/IE for the original draft, and inasmuch as all changes were made in the C/S's Office, no further concurrences are deemed necessary.

D. H. J.
