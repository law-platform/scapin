MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2094)

EMR/JWH/LAH/ftk

26 April 1950

1. References are:

a. SCAPIN 2003, AG 121 (30 Apr 49) ESS/FIN, 11 May 1949, subject, "Separation Allowances for Government Employees."

b. Cabinet Order 264 of 1949, Cabinet Order Concerning the Temporary Measures for Separation Allowance Under the Execution of the Consolidated Balanced Budget for the Fiscal Year 1949-50.

c. Cabinet Order 263 of 1949, Cabinet Order concerning Separation Allowances for the Personnel to Retire in Consequence of the Enforcement of the Law for the Fixed Number of Personnel in the Administrative Organizations.

d. SCAPIN 2003/1, AG 121 (30 Apr 49) ESS/PF, 29 March 1950, subject, "Separation Allowances for Government Employees."

e. Memo for Record of 7 March 1950 concerning reference 1d.

f. Draft bill for Law concerning the Temporary Measures for Separation Allowances for National Public Service Personnel and others.

2. Reference 1a directed the Japanese Government to take the necessary measures for establishing standard separation allowances to be paid to Government employees which would be consistent with the amount of funds provided in the budget adopted for the fiscal year 1949-50. Reference 1b is a Potsdam Cabinet Order implementing reference 1a and was promulgated as temporary legislation to remain in effect until permanent legislation concerning the separation allowances of Government personnel would be drafted by the Japanese Government. Reference 1c is a Potsdam Cabinet Order which provided a higher scale of retiring allowances for government employees who were compelled to retire due to legal reductions in government agencies. Reference 1d extends reference 1a through the fiscal year 1950-51 and reference 1e is the Memo for Record explaining the necessity for reference 1d. Reference If is draft bill submitted by the Japanese Government which supersedes references 1b and 1c and provides for permanent legislation effective 1 April 1951.

3. As outlined in reference 1e, it was necessary for this headquarters to extend SCAPIN 2003 through the fiscal year 1950-51 to continue the basic separation allowances provided by the Cabinet Order issued thereunder (Cabinet Order 264), since the Cabinet took no action to establish separation allowances by legislation. However, the Cabinet of the Japanese Government in their consideration of amendments to the Law for the Fixed Number of Personnel in the Administrative Organs decided that the provisions of reference 1d (Cabinet Order 263) should in all equity be extended through the fiscal year 1950-51 even though said Cabinet Order expired on 30 September 1949. The primary reason for this extension is based on the fact that personnel reductions in certain of the government Kodans were not effected during the period ending 30 September 1949 and are now scheduled to be completed during the fiscal year 1950-51. The Cabinet feels that the employees to be dismissed during 1950-51 fiscal year should receive the same allowances as those dismissed prior to 30 September 1949.

4. In order to correct the inequity mentioned in paragraph 3 above, the Cabinet has drafted a law (reference If) which extends the provisions of Cabinet Order 263 and Cabinet Order 264 through 1950-51 fiscal year and also stipulates that these provisions expire 31 March 1951 and will be replaced by permanent legislation for the 1951-52 fiscal year. As this draft bill provides allowances consistent with the amount of funds provided in the budget for the fiscal year 1950-51 and provides by law for permanent legislation effective 1 April 1951, the intent of the directions of reference 1a is fulfilled and the extension directed by reference 1d is no longer necessary; therefore, the rescission of references 1a and 1d is proposed. A decision by this headquarters concerning the proposed SCAPIN is necessary before concurrence can be given on the attached draft bill (reference 1f).

*5. The proposed SCAPIN is to be released when reference If is promulgated by the Japanese Government.
