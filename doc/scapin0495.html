GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 370.05 (24 Dec 45) GC

(SCAPIN - 495)

24 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Japanese Diplomatic and Consular Members and Others.

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. No.990(5.2), dated 10 December 1945, subject as above.

2. The list of persons for whom repatriation is desired by the Imperial Japanese Government has been forwarded to the United States Government for such action as may be appropriate.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
