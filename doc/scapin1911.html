MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Application for General Permission to Dispose of Properties in Japan Owned by Persons Outside of Japan According to Law Concerning Special Measures for Establishment of Owner Farmers.

1. Reference is made to:

a. C.L.0. No. 5616 (LO 1874 - FI/FF), 18 July 1947, subject, “Application for General Permission to Dispose of Properties in Japan Owned by Persons Outside of Japan According to Law Concerning Special Measures for Establishment of Owner Farmers”;

b. Memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

(1) File AG 130 (22 Sep 45) ESS, SCAPTN 45, 22 September 1945, subject, “Control of Financial Transactions";

(2) File AG 300.6 (18 Feb 48) CPC/CD, SCAPIN 1845/1, 18 February 1948, subject, “Application of Provisions of SCAPIN 26, 13 September 1945, and Ministry of Finance Ordinance No. 78, 20 September 1945, As Amended”;

(3) File AG 386.3 (6 May 46) CPC, SCAPIN 926, 6 May 1946, subject, “Procedure for Returning Property in Japan to Nationals of the United Nations”;

(4) File AG 386.3 (22 Apr 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1880, 22 April 1948, subject, “Procedures for Restoration of Property in Japan to Nationals of the United Nations."

2. The Japanese Government is hereby authorized to purchase and disopse of all agricultural land in Japan subject to the provisions of the Law Concerning Special Measures for Establishment of Owner Farmers except that at this time no action will be taken towards the purchase of land registered as owned on the date of enactment of said law by:

a. Persons subject to the provisions of reference 1b (2);

b. Nationals of one of the United Nations or Neutral Nations who do not in addition possess Japanese nationality;

c. Other non-Japanese persons of areas formerly under the control of Japan who are not as of the date of this directive residents of Japan.

3. Land subject to restitution shall be considered as owned by nationals of one of the United Nations even though registered in the name of another at the date of enactment of the Agricultural Land Reform Law.

4. The Japanese Government is directed to furnish General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Civil Property Custodian, within sixty (60) days of the date of this memorandum, a list of the property covered by the exceptions in paragraph 2 above, the name of the owner, the location and size of his agricultural land holdings.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
