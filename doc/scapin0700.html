GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 201.5 (5 Feb 46) PH

(SCAPIN - 700)

APO 500

5 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Request for Issuance of Certificates of Birth and Death of Japanese under Control of United States Forces.

1. Reference is made to request from Central Liaison Office, C.L. O. No. 1000(5.2), dated 10 December 1945, subject as above.

2. Investigation reveals that United States authorities have established adequate procedures for recording vital statistics data among Japanese under United States control outside Japan and for the transmission of essential information through established channels.

3. In view of the fact that adequate recording and notification measures are already in effect, the request for special reports is not favorably considered.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
