GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 602 (13 Nov 46) CIE

(SCAPIN-1334/1)

APO 500

12 September 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Disposition of State-Owned Land Used by Religious Institutions

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government file AG 602 (13 Nov 46) CIE, SCAPIN 1334, 13 November 1946, subject: “Disposition of State-Owned Land Used by Religious Institutions,” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Paragraph 3f of above reference is hereby rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
