4 Jun 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminal.

Memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government, dated 3 December 1945, file, AG 000.5 (3 Dec 45) LS, wherein the Imperial Japanese Government is directed to deliver one KATISKA to the Commanding General, XI Corps, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest practicable date, is hereby amended to delete said KATISKA's name.

This name appears as the 5th name on page No. 1 of the above mentioned Memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
