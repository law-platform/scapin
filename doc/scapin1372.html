GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.112 (2 Dec 46) GB

(SCAPIN-1372)

APO 500

2 December 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Contact with Japanese Officials.

1. The attention of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, has been called to incidents in which representatives of Foreign Mission make direct contact with officials of the Japanese Government for the purpose of securing informations, inspecting installations or other official business.

2. In compliance with Memorandum for: Chiefs of Foreign Missions in Japan, 9 June 1946, subject: “Official Relations and Contacts with the Imperial Japanese Government, with Staff Sections of General Headquarters, and with United States Occupational Forces,” a copy of which is inclosed, all contact between official representatives of Foreign Governments and the Japanese Imperial Governmant is made through Central Headquarters.

3. In event of a repetition of incidents similar to those referred to in paragraph 1 above, the Japanese Government officials who are contacted will not furnish the foreign officials any information, permit the inspection of any installations, not conduct any other official business unless the request has been cleared through proper channels. The Japanese officials involved will also inform the foreign representatives that any contact should be cleared through the General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. The incident will be reported immediately to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers by the Imperial Japanese Government.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

1 Incl: Memorandum. 9 June 1946.

Memorandum concerning Official Relations and Contacts with the Imperial Japanese Government, with Staff Sections of GHQ, and with United States Occupational Forces.

9 June 1946

1. All official business withe the Imperial Japanese Government, with Staff Sections of GHQ, and with United States Occupational Forces will hence forth be conducted through either the Diplomatic Section or the Foreign Liaison Subsection, G - 2 Section, of this Headquarters.

a. The Diplomatic Section has responsibility in diplomatic matters connected with SCAP and in Japanese Foreign Office relations with foreign governments.

b. The Foreign Liaison Sub-section, G - 2 Section, has responsibility in all other matters.

2. Official business, whenever practicable, will be transacted in writing.

3. Members of the Allied Council for Japan will continue to submit their requests for information through the Secretary General thereof, who will use either the facilities of the Diplomatic Section or the Foreign Liaison Sub-section, G - 2 Section, as indicated.
