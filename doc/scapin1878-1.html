GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (15 Jun 48) CPC/LP

(SCAPIN - 1878/1)

APO 500

15 June 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Suspension of Action Directed in SCAPIN 1878.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 410.2(7 Apr 48)CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1878, 7 April 1948, subject, "Property Produced in Occupied Areas. "

2. The Japanese Government is hereby directed to suspend, until further notice, all action directed in paragraph 4 of the subject memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
