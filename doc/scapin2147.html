MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Petroleum Storage Tank Facilities.

1. The following memoranda are hereby rescinded:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 678 (30 Nov 46) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 1920, subject: Petroleum Storage Tank Facilities, 9 July 1948.

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 678 (30 Nov 46) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 1920/1, subject: Petroleum Storage Tank Facilities, 20 April 1950.

2. Rescission of above memoranda does not affect nor alter the status of petroleum storage facilities presently under reparations custody and control.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B. BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2147)

HBO/ns

19 March 1951

1. Reference is Memorandum for the Japanese Government from GHQ, SCAP, AG 678 (27 Mar 51) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 2417, 27 Mar 51, subject: Petroleum Storage Tank Facilities.

2. Reference memorandum:

a. Recinds the following SCAPINS:

(1) Memorandum for the Japanese Government from GHQ, SCAP, AG 678 (30 Nov 46) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 1920, 9 July 1948, subject: Petroleum Storage Tank Facilities. This SCAPIN directs the Japanese Government to take such action as necessary to prevent construction, major repair, rehabilitation, dismantling, movement or scrapping of any petroleum storage tank facilities in excess of 50 kiloliters capacity and their accessory equipment without prior approval of SCAP.

(2) Memorandum for the Japanese Government from GHQ, SCAP, AG 678 (30 Nov 46) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 1920/1, 20 April 1950, subject: Petroleum Storage Tank Facilities. This SCAPIN amends SCAPIN 1920, authorizing the Japanese Government to approve or disapprove alterations to petroleum storage tank facilities without prior approval of SCAP except those facilities formerly owned by the Japanese army or navy.

b. Informs the Japanese Government that rescission of above memoranda does not effect nor▲alter the status of petroleum storage facilities presently under reparations custody and control.

3. Action taken is based on the following:

a. SCAPIN 1920 was issued for the following reasons which are no longer considered valid and controls exercised are therefore not required:

(1) Storage capacity was considered adequate for requirements and action was required to prevent use of materials and transportation then citically needed for direct use and rehabilitation of other industries.

(2) It was considered necessary to maintain storage capacity at its then current level in-order to assure sufficient tankage for possible reparations.

b. This action is in accord with established policy of relaxing controls wherever possible.

c. Checknote numbers 2 and 3 from CPC and G-4 to ESS, 10 and 15 March 1951 respectively, subject as above. These Sections concur in reference memorandum providing a general statement is made informing the Japanese Government that recission of above memoranda does not affect nor alter the status of petroleum storage facilities presently under reparations custody and control.

d. SCAPIN 1370, subject: Preservation of Property in Japan Owned by United Nations and their Nationals, 30 November 1946, assures protection and preservation of those petroleum storage facilities owned by the United Nations or their nationals.

e. SCAPIN 1263, subject: Revised List No. 2 Aircraft Factories, Military and Naval Arsenals and Research Laboratories, 11 October 1946, assures custody, control and protective maintenance of petroleum storage facilities designated for reparations.

f. SCAPIN 1355, subject: Permits for Conversion and Reconversion of Industrial Plants, 22 November 1946, provides for an established procedure for authorized movement and use of petroleum storage facilities designated for reparations.

4. Surveillance by CAS is not required.

5. Concurrence: G-4 (Pet) by C/N, see paragraph 3c

CPC by C/N, see paragraph 3c
