GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.4 (25 Mar 47) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1581)

APO 500

25 March 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Inventory of all Looted Archaeological Specimens in Japan.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386.3(19 Apr 46) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 885, 19 April 1946, subject: Impounding and Reporting of Looted Property, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386.3 (25 Jul 46) CPC/FP SCAPIN 1083, 25 July 1946, subject: Custody and Storage of Looted Property, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

c. Memorandum to the Central Liaisoh Office, file 000.4(14 Jan 47) CPC/FP, 14 January 1947, subject: Archaeological Material Removed from Hong Kong, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

d. C L O. NO.1038(RP), 18 February 1947, same subject.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to submit an inventory of all looted archaeological specimens now in its custody which have been impounded in compliance with reference memoranda 1a and b above. The inventory is to include the following information regarding the subject specimens:

a. Name and address of place of storage;

b. Country of origin;

c. Place and date of excavation:

b. Type and quantity of specimens;

e. Scientific date as to archaeological period of specimens.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government is further directed to investigate all institutions and establishments in Japan in which looted archaeological material is likely to be located and to submit a report on all such property which has not previously been reported in compliance with reference 1a and b above. The information outlined in paragraph 2 above is also to be furnished on this property.

4. In view of the fact that the required information is partly of a highly scientific and technical nature, it is directed that competent experts be used to the fullest extent in making the investigation and preparing the report.

5. The required inventory is to be submitted to the Civil Property Custodian by 30 April 1947.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
