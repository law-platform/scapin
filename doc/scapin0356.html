GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (28 Nov 45) LS

(SCAPIN - 356)

28 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Japanese Personnel in Philippine Prisoner of War Camps.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to furnish this headquarters at the earliest practicable date rosters of Japanese officers, enlisted men, and civilians on duty at the following named prisoner of war camps in the Philippine Islands during the Japanese occupation.

a. Camp O'Donnell, Capas, Tarlac, Luzon.

b. Prisoner of War Camp 1, 2, and 3, Cabanatuan, Nueva Echija, Luzon

c. Camp Leithley, Mindanao

d. Malaybalay Prisoner of War Camp, Mindanao

e. Old Bilibid Prison, Manila, Luzon

f. Santo Tomas Internment Camp, Manila, Luzon

g. Camp John Hay, Civilian Internment Camp, Baguio Luzon

h. Bacolod, Prisoner of War and Civilian Internment Camp, Negros Island

i. Nichols Field, Luzon

j. Los Banas, Luzon

2. Rosters will show duty and length of service of each individual at each camp by dates.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
