GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.1 (25 May 48) ESS/IND

(SCAPIN-1898)

APO 500

25 May 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Dissolution of the Arms Disposal Committee.

1. Reference the following memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. AG 402.5 (24 Sep 45) GD, SCAPIN 53, 24 September 1945, subject: Materials, Supplies and Equipment Received and to be Received from the Japanese Armed Forces.

b. AG 386.3 (11 Jan 46) GD, SCAPIN 582, 11 January 1946, subject: Disposition of Equipment and Supplies of the Japanese Armed Forces Between the Date of 14 August 1945 and 2 September 1945.

c. AG 402.5 (24 Nov 47) ESS/CCC, SCAPIN 1826, 24 November 1947, subject: Industrial Recovery Corporation (Sangyo Fukko Kodan).

d. AG 402.5 (29 Dec 47) GD, SCAPIN 1835, 29 December 1947, subject: Transfer of Responsibility for Receipt and Accounting for Surrendered Supplies and Equipment Returned to the Japanese Government.

2. Reference C.L.C.O. No. 495(2P) dated 1 March 1948, subject: Application for Approval on Transfer of Functions from Committee for Disposal of Destroyed Implements of War to Industrial Recovery Corporation. Application made in above reference is approved except that the Industrial Recovery Corporation will not pay the Arms Disposal Committee for stocks of material on hand.

3. The Japanese Government is directed to take the following actions:

a. To dissolve the Arms Disposal Committee and all other committees and agencies for the disposal of war material established by the Japanese Government to implement the above references.

b. To take whatever steps are necessary to terminate the contract dated 16 May 1946 between the Home Ministry and the Arms Disposal Committee for the sale of war material by the Arms Disposal Committee.

c. To take all supplies, material, equipment, books, accounts, records, documents and other property now in the custody of, or under the control of, the Arms Disposal Committee or of any other committee or agency dissolved pursuant to sub-paragraph 3 a, above, into custody under the direction and exclusive control of the Procurator General’s office until further notice.

d. To make available to the Procurator General’s Office for examination and inspection all the books, accounts, records and other papers of the affiliated companies or organizations comprising the Arms Disposal Committee.

4. The Industrial Recovery Corporation is designated as the responsible agency to receive, account for, and distribute the following:

a. All supplies, material, or equipment taken into custody by action authorized in 3c, and released from the Procurator General’s office for the rehabilitation of the civilian economy of Japan or for any other program of the Japanese Government pursuant to or in compliance with reference memorandum 1 c.

b. All materials of the same type as formerly handled by the Arms Disposal Committee released by the Occupation Forces to the Construction Board.

5. The Industrial Recovery Corporatinn will report all actions in compliance with paragraphs 4 a and 4 b above to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

6. Direct communication between the responsible agency of the Japanese Government and the Economic and Scientific Section of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers is hereby authorized to implement this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1898)

WSV/LRS/WT/cs

30 April 1948

1. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 402. 5 (24 Sep 45) GD (SCAPIN-53) subject: "Materials, Supplies and Equipment Received and to be Received from the Japanese Armed Forces", designates the Home Ministry to receive all implements are to be returned and to be returned by the Occupation Forces. These implements are to be destroyed and scrapped except parts useable for civilian relief.

2. Joint Ministerial Ordinance of the Japanese Government dated 31 October 1945 issued by the Director of the Research Bureau of the Ministry of Home Affairs and the Director of the Re-Adjustment Bureau of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry orders the below mentioned firms to form a committee (Arms Disposal Committee) in order to receive all material mentioned in SCAPIN 53 for destruction and disposal.

Nippon Steel and Iron Works (Nihon Seitetsu K.K.)

Nippon Steel Tube Works (Nippon Kokan K.K.)

Kobe Steel Works (Kobe Seiko K.K.)

Furukawa Metal Company (Furukawa Kinzoku Kogyo K.K.)

Sumitomo Metal Works (Sumitomo Kinzoku Kogyo K. K.)

(later changed to Fuso Metal Works) (Fuso Kinzoku Kogyo K.K.)

3. The articles of association of the Arms Disposal Committee were published on 31 October 1945. On 16 May 1946 a contract was concluded between the Home Ministry and the Chairman of the Arms Disposal Committee, which stipulated that implements of war returned by the Occupation Forces to the Home Ministry were to be sold to the Arms Disposal Committee without fixing a price. Payment was to be deferred until the implements were scrapped and sold.

4. The Arms Disposal Committee was directly under the supervision of the Japanese Government and was not supervised by Eighth Army. The Committee occasionally submitted reports of its activities to the Eighth Army Military Government Section and to SCAP.

5. The Arms Disposal Committee has frequently been the target of investigation by various committees of the Japanese Diet. Lately, the Hoarded Goods Committee of the Diet has established a sub-committee with the specific object of investigating the actions and personnel of the Arms Disposal Committee, its member firms, and subcontractors. In various instances, the procurators have investigated specific action of member firms and subcontractors. It is evident that certain actions of the Arms Disposal Committee, its member firms, and subcontractors call for a thorough investigation. Apart from that, the dissolution of the Committee will have a great effect on the present Hoarded Goods Program. It is therefore necessary to use the greatest discretion in the method of dissolution. In order to safeguard the books and papers of the Arms Disposal Committee, its seems advisable to use the offices of the public procurators. Since the procurators cannot channel the materials at present in the hands of the Arms Disposal Committee into the Japanese economy, these materials will be released from the procurators' offices to the Industrial Recovery Corporation for utilization in the Japanese economy according to established procedure.

6. Memorandum from S. Kimura, Director of First Division, Central Liaison and Coordinating Office to General Headquarters of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, dated 1 March 1948, subject: "Application for Approval on Transfer of Functions from Committee for Disposal of Destroyed Implements of War to Industrial Recovery Corporation", transmitting a letter from C. Mizutani, Minister of Commerce and Industry to Chief, ESS, dated 29 February 1948 (same subject), requests SCAP's approval. In Check Note No. 1 from ESS to G-4, 602.1 (10 Feb 48) ESS/lN, subject: "Dissolution of Arms Disposal Committee", ESS Requested recommendation or comments. In Check Note No. 2 from G-4 to ESS dated 13 February 1948, same subject, G-4 approved the request of the Japanese Government.

7. The interests of Korea have been considered.
