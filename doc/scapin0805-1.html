GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.93 (28 Jul 47) CCS

(SCAPIN - 805/1)

APO 500

28 July 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Establishment of Hourly Weather Circuits.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, subject same as above, file AG 000.93 (8 Mar 46)CCS, dated 8 March 1946, (SCAPIN 805).

2. The Memorandum given as reference in paragraph 1 provides that the Japanese Bureau of Communications will furnish certain telegraph circuits and services for use in connection with the collection and dissemination of weather information. It will not be necessary to furnish the telegraph circuits and services described therein and the original Memorandum is hereby rescinded.

3. Occupation Force teletypewriters have been installed in certain of the Japanese meteorological observatory offices in connection with furnishing service in accordance with the original memorandum. Arrangements will be made for these teletypewriters and any other Occupation Force telegraph equipment which may have been installed to be removed and returned to Occupation Force agencies. Details in connection with this will be handled by direct correspondence in accordance with paragraph 4 of this memorandum.

4. Direct communication between General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Civil Communications Section, and the Ministry of Communications on technical matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General

(See Reverse for Memo Info of Gen Application)

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

28 July 1947

MEMORANDUM:

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN 805/1), file AG 000.93 (28 Jul 47)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 28 July 1947, subject: Establishment of Hourly Weather Circuits

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. Due to technical inadequacies of the Japanese telegraph system, it has been found impossible to establish telegraph circuits and services covered by the original Memorandum (SCAPIN 805). If it becomes desirable to provide services of the nature originally specified some time in the future, they will be provided by communications orders.

3. Teletypewriters which were provided in Japanese meteorological stations were installed by the 43rd Weather Wing in cooperation with Eighth Army Signal Corps agencies.

4. Command channel instructions regarding the cancellation of the subject telegraph circuits and services and the removal of teletypewriter equipment will be forwarded.

R.G. HERSEY

Lt Col, AGD

Asst Adj Gen

DISTRIBUTION

Same as (SCAPIN 805/1)

less Japanese Government

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

8 March 1946

MEMORANDUM:

SUBJECT : Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN - 805 ) file, AG 000.93 ( 8 Mar 46 )CCS, this Headquarters, dated 8 March 1946, subject: "Establishment of Hourly Weather Circuits."

1. With reference to Memorandum Number (SCAPIN - 805 ), this Headquarters, to the Imperial Japanese Government, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The circuits which the Japanese Government are directed to furnish are to be used by the AACS in the collection and distributing of weather information for the 20th Weather Squadron.

3. No surveillance other than that now exercised by existing Army signal operations personnel will be required and a command directive will not be issued.

HAROLD FAIR,

Lt. Colonel, AGD,

Asst, Adjutant General.

DISTRIBUTION:

Same as SCAPIN 805 except

Imperial Japanese Government.
