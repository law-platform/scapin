GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG383.7 (20 Nov 45) CIS

(SCAPIN-320)

20 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Apprehension of Lt Col Aoshitada Nagatjua

The Imperial Japanese Government will apprehend Lt Col Aoshitada Nagatjua and deliver him to the authorities of Omori Prison Camp for incarceration.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. Allen,
Colonel, A. G. D.
Asst. Adjutant General.
