GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.33 (18 Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-616)

APO 500

18 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Release of Gold, Silver and Platinum for Dental Work.

1. Reference is made to CLO Memorandum No. 1188 (2.1) of 18 December 1945, subject: "Control of Financial Transactions," requesting permission to use gold, silver and platinum for industrial, dental and other uses.

2. (a) The Imperial Japanese Government may authorise the use of precious metals for dental purposes only in the following amounts per month for a three month period starting 1 January 1946:

Gold - 170 Kilograms per month

Silver - 600 Kilograms per month

Platinum - 10 Kilograms per month

(b) The Imperial Japanese Government may also authorize the use by dental schools of one Kilogram of gold and two kilograms of silver per month for three months, beginning 1 January 1946.

3. The Ministry of Finance may give approval to such transactions in, and transfers of, gold, silver and platinum as are necessary so that dentists and cental schools may acquire the precious metals, the use of which is authorized above. All sources, other than the stocks now impounded in the Bank of Japan, will be drawn upon, before authorizing the use of these stocks.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government will submit to this Headquarters on or before the 10th of each month, a repert showing, a complete list of distributers, the amount of precious metals distributed and the source from which the metal was obtained.

5. The system of distribution of precious metals for dental purposes will remain as heretofore under the Bureau of Public Welfare of the Imperial Japanese Government. Records of the allotment end use of these metals will be available for checking at the convenience of this Headquarters.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel,A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
