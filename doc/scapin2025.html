AG 451 (8 Jul 49) CPC/PLD

(SCAPIN - 2025)

APO 500

8 July 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Rescission of Memoranda to the Japanese Government.

1. Reference is made to the following memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. File AG 451 (17 Feb 47)CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1530, 17 February 1947, subject, "Screening and Reporting of Registered Motor Vehicles"

b. File AG 451 (12 Jan 48)CPC/FP, SCAPIN 5133-A, 12 January 1948, subject, "Investigation of Looted Vehicles."

2. Reference 1a and 1b above are hereby rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
