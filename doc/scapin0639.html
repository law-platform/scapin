GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (21 Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 639)

APO 500

21 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Transfer of Silver within Japan

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. Memorandum 156 (2.3) dated 11 January 1946, subject: "Control of Financial Transactions.”

2. The Minister of Finance may approve the transfer of 48.047 kilograms of Silver from the office of Fujita Mizoguchi, No. 25, 1-chome, Imaizumi-cho, Fukuoka, to the office of the Gold and Silver Trading Society, No. 2, 3-chome, Muromachi, Nihonbashi-ku, Tokyo.

3. This memorandum will not be construed to authorize any change in the ownership or other transaction in the silver, the movement of which is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
