GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (3 Oct 46) GC

(SCAPIN - 1245)

APO 500

3 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Ryukyuans Upon Own Transportation.

1. Reference is made to:

a. C.L.O. Number 4927 (RJ), 25 September 1946, subject: "Petition of Ryukyuans for Permission to Repatriate by Their Own Fishing Boat."

b. Memorandum from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 370.05 (7 May 46)GC, (SCAPIN-927) dated 7 May 1946, subject: "Repatriation" as amended.

2. Return of Ryukyuans upon their own transportation as requested in reference of paragraph 1a above is not authorized at this time.

3. Transportation on repatriation vessels of possessions of individuals in excess of quantities mentioned in Annex VI of reference of paragraph 1 b above is prohibited.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
