MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 628)

WFM/OJH/jpl

12 Jan 46

1. C.L.O. Memorandum No. 1482 (5.2) dated 31 December 1945, subject: "Application for Permission to send a Relief Fund to the Japanese Relief Association at Seoul, Korea," transmitted a request that the Japanese Government be permitted to send a Relief Fund of 1,700,000 yen to the Japanese Relief Association in Korea. This amount was said to be sufficient to cover minimum expenses of the association during the four month period from January to April 1946. The function of the Japanese Relief Association was said to be that of providing minimum food, shelter and clothing to Japanese refugees from Northern Korea awaiting repatriation from Southern Korea to Japan.

2. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 130 (22 Sep 45) ESS, subject: "Control of Financial Transactions," prohibits foreign exchange transactions without prior permission from this Headquarters. To date such permissions have not been granted.

3. Military Government authorities in Korea cognizant of the operations of the Japanese Relief Association have not requested any remittances of funds from Japan for the support of activities of this association. The release of any frozen Japanese funds in Korea is the responsibility of the Korean Military Government.

4. Verbal concurrence: Lt. Col. Marsh, Government Section.
