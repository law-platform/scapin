GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 383.7 (31 Jan 46) CIS

(SCAPIN - 684)

APO 500

31 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Currency and Documents held by FUJISHIRO, Tomonobu.

1. Reference is made to Central Liaison Office letter Number 978 (5.1) of 9 December 1945, and subsequent correspondence, advising this headquarters that Mr. FUJISHIRO, Tomonobu, formerly principal of Hibara Primary School, in Keisho-Nando, Korea, was in possession of Eight Thousand Yen (¥8,000) and documents which he had brought with him illegally from Korea on his return to Japan.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government will take possession of the funds and the documents and transfer them to this headquarters on or before 7 February 1946.

3. If the Imperial Japanese Government is unable to make delivery by the appointed time a detailed report of the circumstances will be made to this headquarters.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
