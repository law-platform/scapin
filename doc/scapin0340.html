GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 370.05 (24 Nov 45) GC

(SCAPIN - 340)

24 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Formosans from Japan.

1. Reference is made to the request in C. L. O. No 661(5.1) dated 20 November 1945, subject: same as above.

2. Formosa has not been opened for repatriation. Therefore the repatriation of the Formosans, subject of cited reference, is not favorably considered.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
