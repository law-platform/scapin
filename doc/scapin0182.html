GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 370.05 (24 Oct 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 182)

24 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Request for Permission to Transport Certain Persons to North China

1. Reference is made to CLO Memorandum No. 162 requesting permission for ten Japanese nationals to return to North China.

2. It is directed that this Headquarters be informed as to:

a. Domicile of the persons concerned.

b. Jurisdiction under which the firms cited in your memorandum are organized.

c. Brief description of nature of business in each case.

d. Description of properties to be disposed of in North China with approximate value of plant, names of principal products and monthly production capacities.

e. Number of employees and classification as Japanese, Chinese and other.

f. Domicile of owners of property concerned.

g. Proposed disposition of the properties concerned.

3. Achnowledgement of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 182)

RCK/OJM/syk 21 October 1945

1. By CLO No. 152 request is made that ten Japanese nationals with business positions in North China, but caught in Japan at termination of the war be allowed to return to North China to effect disposal of Japanese properties there.

2. Facts provided in request did not enable identification of properties or individuals in question. This request afforded a means of securing information on Japanese foreign assets.

3. Lt. Col. Coudray and Mr. Aik concur.

C. F. T.
