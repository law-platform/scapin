GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386 (31 Oct 51) CPC/OD

(SCAPIN-2178)

APO 500

31 October 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Property in Japan Belonging to Nationals of the United Nations.

1. Effective 1 February 1952 the Japanese Government will assume execution of the program for restoration of tangible and intangible property in Japan to United Nations nationals. Requests for restoration of property will be submitted directly to the Japanese Government by the diplomatic representative in Japan of the claimant’s government. The Japanese Government will effect the restoration of property to the owner or his duly appointed agent.

2. Restorations will be effected in accordance with established policy as set forth in previous instructions issued to the Japanese Government. Attached Annex I lists previous instructions with appropriate amendments or rescissions.

3. The Japanese Government is authorized to adopt for use any of the forms and procedures which have been developed by the Office of the Civil Property Custodian, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and which are now in use in reporting upon status of property and in effecting restoration.

4. Not later than 15 December 1951 a report will be submitted to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers furnishing the following information:

a. Agency of the Japanese Government which will be responsible for restoration program

b. Copies of all forms to be used in reporting upon the status of property and in effecting restorations.

c. Procedure which will be followed in initiating requests for restoration of property and for effecting restorations.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C.C.B. WARDEN
Colonel, AGC
Adjutant General

ANNEX I

The following memoranda for the Japanese Government from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers set forth the policy governing control, custody and restoration of United Nations nationals property in Japan. Except as indicated below these instructions will remain in force. Rescissions (Group A) and amendments (Group B) will become effective 1 February 1952.

Group A. Memoranda to be Rescinded:

1. File AG 386.3 (6 May 46) CPC, SCAPIN 926, 6 May 1946, subject, “‘Procedure for Returning Property in Japan to Nationals of the United Nations”.

2. File AG 004 (22 May 47) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1702, 22 May 1947, subject, “Relaxation of Control of Property Owned by United Nations Nationals Outside of Japan”.

3. File AG 386.3 (22 Apr 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1880/4, 7 November 1949, subject, “Restoration of Property in Japan to United Nations and Their Nationals.”

4. File AG 386.7 (7 Oct 49) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 6885-A, 7 October 1949, subject, “Applications for Withdrawals from Bank Accounts of United Nations Nationals.”

5. Memorandum for the Bank of Japan, file 130 (19 Aug 1947) CPC/FP, 19 August 1947, subject, “Withdrawals of Funds by United Nations Nationals from the Special Property Administration Account,” from Civil Property Custodian, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, is also rescinded.

Group B. Amendments or Changes to be Effected:

1. File AG 600.3 (19 Apr 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 5519-A, 19 April 1948, subject, “Construction of Buildings on United Nations Property.” Paragraph 3 is changed to read as follows:

“3. The Japanese Government is hereby notified that any benefits or improvements accruing to said property as a result of any construction thereon since 7 December 1941 will, at the option of the United Nations national owner, become part of the property, and will be included in restoration proceedings when said property is restored to the United Nations national.”

2. File AG 386.3 (22 Apr 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1880, 22 April 1948, subject, “Procedures for Restoration of Property in Japan to Nationals of the United Nations.” Paragraphs 9 and 10 are changed to read as follows:,

“9. Restorations will be made in accordance with the following provisions:

a. Receipt in the form of the “Receipt for Restoration to United Nation National of Property in Japan” attached hereto as Annex A and containing all provisions therein will be signed by the restoree and an authorized representative of the Japanese Government. Additional provisions may be included if circumstances require.

b. The Japanese Government will furnish at the time of restoration documentary evidence of transfer of title, a complete inventory of the property being returned and an official final report on the administration of the property from the date of wrongful transfer or seizure to the date of restoration, said report to include a statement of financial transactions and, in the case of industrial and commercial concerns, a closing balance sheet. Copies of documentary evidence of transfer of title, inventory and report on administration will be attached to each copy of the receipt.

c. An agreed statement as to the extent and condition of the property being returned will be drawn up at the time of restoration and will be signed by both the restoree and the authorized representative of the Japanese Government. A copy of this statement will be attached to each copy of the receipt.”

“10. Two copies of the accomplished receipt will be forwarded to the diplomatic representative of the claimant and two copies to the claimant or his agent.”

3. File AG 386.3 (22 Apr 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1880/2, 1 March 1949, subject, “Procedures for Restoration to Nationals of the United Nations of Property under Procurement Demand.” Paragraph 2a is changed to read as follows:

“a. Restoration of title will be effected immediately upon request of the owner.”

4. File AG 010 (1 Jan 50) ESS/FTC, SCAPIN 2070, 1 January 1950, subject, “Foreign Exchange and Foreign Trade Control.” Delete paragraph 3g.

Group C. Memoranda to Remain in Force:

1. File AG 600.3 (30 Nov 46) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1370, 30 November 1946, subject, “Preservation of Property in Japan Owned by United Nations and Their Nationals.”

2. File AG 386 (12 May 47) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1670, 12 May 1947, subject, “Supplemental Instructions Concerning Protection and Preservation of Property.”

3. File AG 095 (8 May 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1880/1, 8 May 1948, subject, “Perpetual Leases.”

4. File AG 095 (9 Aug 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 6007-A, 14 September 1948, subject, “Benefits or Improvements Accruing to Property Belonging to United Nations Nationals.”

5. File AG 386.3 (22 Apr 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1880/3, 18 April 1949, subject, “Restoration of Legal Rights and Interests to United Nations Nationals.”

6. File AG 311.3 (30 Aug 48) CCS, SCAPIN 1964/1, 18 April 1949, subject, “Regulations Covering the Provision of Telephone Services in Japan for Certain Nationals of the United Nations.”

7. File AG 150 (26 May 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1899, 26 May 1948, subject, “Preemptive Rights Accruing to Stock Owned by United Nationals.”

8. File AG 150 (26 May 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1899/1, 3 May 1950, subject, “Preemptive Rights Accruing to Stock Owned by United Nations Nationals.”
