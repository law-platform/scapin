APO 500

17 March 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
SUBJECT :Revised Plan for Receiving Payments Due American Carriers for Messages and Radiotelephone Calls Filed by United States Military and Accredited Civilian Personnel for Transmission to or Through the United States, Its Territories and Possessions, Prepaid in Japan

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.3 (24 Sep 46)CCS, SCAPIN 1228, dated 24 September 1946, subject as above, as amended.

2. This memorandum is issued to cover instructions for the conversion of Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, to Military Payment Certificates of a new issue which may occur at some future date. The effective date of this memorandum will be as of a future day and hour thereof which will be announced verbally to the Ministry of Communications by the Chief, Civil Communications Section, General Hoadquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

3. Upon notification of effective date and hour of this memorandum, Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, will no longer be accepted in payment for telephone and telegraph messages filed by the authorized users of this type of curreney. Each telephone and telegraph office presently authorized to handle telephone and telegraph messages with charges payable in this type of currency will be advised immediately to this effect.

4. All Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, which are in the custody of public offices of the Ministry of Communications, as of the effective date, will be gathered in by the Ministry of Communications by or before 2400 hours on the 20th day following the effective date. Such amounts of Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, as are on hand at the close of each day within the 20-day period of grace in the Treasury of the Ministry of Communications, will be delivered on the following day to the office of the Finance Officer, United States Army, in the Bank of Chosen Building, Tokyo, for conversion to Military Payment Certificates of a new issue, the identification of which will be verbally advised to the Ministry of Communications on the effective date of this memorandum. The last day during which conversion of Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, to Military Payment Certificates of the new issue will be the 20th day following the effective date. The first delivery of Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, to the Finance Officer by the Ministry of Communications for conversion to Military Payment Certificates of the new issue will be during the fifth day following the effective date of this memorandum. Each delivery of Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, will be accompanied by three (3) approved copies of the certificate shown as inclosure 1 hereto.

5. Each telephone and telegraph office which is presently authorized to aocept messages with charges payable in United States Military Payment Certificates will be instructed to:

a. Forward immediately to the Ministry of Communications, Tokyo, all Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, including amounts authorized for making change, which are on hand at the time final instruction is received to discontinue acceptance of Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, in payment for telegraph and telephone messages.

b. Immediately upon the forwarding of all Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, on hand to the Ministry of Communications as called for in subparagraph a above, accept Military Payment Certificates of the new issue in payment of the charges for prepaid messages from United States accredited personnel and other persons or organizations in Japan legally entitled to possess and use United States Military Payment Certificates, and accept prepayment only in Military Payment Certificates of the new issue.

c. Accumulate an equal amount of Military Payment Certificates of the new issue for change-making purposes as was heretofore authorized for each public office where prepayment of messages was made in Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, and where necessary require senders of messages to supply the exact amount of the message charge when change-making funds are not available.

6. All rules and regulations which were applicable to the handling of messages, their payment in, and the handling of Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, are equally applicable to the handling of messages, their payment in, and the handling of Military Payment Certificates of the now issue, unless specific provision for exceptions to or temporary deviation from such rules and regulations is made herein.

7. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section and the General Accounting Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Ministry of Communications, regarding operational interpretations of instructions regarding conversion of currency within the scope of this memorandum, is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUEREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

17 March 1948

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number SCAPIN 1228/9 , file AG 311.3 (17 Mar 48)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 17 March 1948, subject: Revised Plan for Receiving Payments Due American Carriers for Messages and Radiotelephone Calls Filed by United States Military and Accredited Civilian Personnel for Transmission to or Through the United States, Its Territories and Possessions, Prepaid in Japan

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The purpose of this directive is to instruct the Japanese Ministry of Communications as to when and how it will convert Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, which it has on hand to Military Payment Certificates of a new issue.

3. Staff Memorandum No. 7, SCAP & FEC, file AG 123.3 (10 Feb 48)FD, dated 12 February 1948, subject: Conversion of Military Payment Certificates, indicates that a conversion of Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, to Military Payment Certificates of a new series is to be made at some future date.

4. Under authorizations contained in SCAPIN 1228, as amended, the Ministry of Communications receives Military Payment Certificates, from persons and organizations in Japan entitled to possess and use this type of currency, in payment of the charges for prepaid telegraph messages and for radiotelephone calls to or through the United States, and retains in its various telegraph and telephone offices which are authorized to handle such services, certain amounts of Military Payment Certificates for change-making purposes. As of the conversion date of Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, to Military Payment Certificates of a new issue, the Ministry of Communications will have on hand in its various offices scattered throughout the four islands composing Japan, substantial amounts of Military Payment Certificates, Series 471. A 20-day period of time following the official conversion date will be required to get these funds sent into the Ministry of Communications at Tokyo in order to effcct conversion without loss to the Ministry of Communications and the American carriers who are jointly involved in handling commerial telegraph messages and radiotelephone calls which are prepaid in Military Payment Certificates in Japan.

5. The function to be performed by the General Accounting Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, will consist of an audit of the amounts of Military Payment Certificates,

Series 471, delivered by the Ministry of Communications to the Finance Officer for conversion to Military Payment Certificates of the new series within the 20-day period of grace next following the effective date of conversion comprising the sum of the following:

a. The amount of undeposited receipts of Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, on hand at the Treasury of the Ministry of Communications as of the effective date of the conversion.

b. Subsequent receipts of Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, by the Treasury of the Ministry of Communications, Tokyo, during the 20-day period of grace, representing amounts reported by public offices as having been collected and on hand as of the effective date and hour thereof, and as having been collected between the effective date and hour thereof, and the date and hour each Public Office is first officially notified of the effective date and hour of the conversion.

c. Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, on hand at the various overseas radiotelephone offices in Japan representing deposits for uncompleted radiotelephone calls.

d. Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, on hand at the Central Telegraph Office, Tokyo, representing deposits under the optional payment deposit plan.

6. Surveillance with respect to the physical transfer by the Ministry of Communications of Military Payment Certificates, Series 471, to the Finance Officer, United States Army, Bank of Chosen Building, Tokyo, within the 20-day period of grace following the effective day and hour thereof and the proper accomplishment of the certificates required by paragraph 4 of the directive will be the responsibility of the Finance Officer.

7. Command instructions pertaining to extension of surveillance are being released simultaneously with this memorandum.

J. F. BRADSHAW

Major, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
