GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123.7 (5 May 47) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 1656)

APO 500

5 May 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :British Armed Forces Special Vouchers

1. It is directed that the Japanese Government enact all necessary legislation to make unlawful the possession of British Armed Forces Special Vouchers and Australian one penny and one half-penny copper coins on and after 6 May 1947 by any person or organization except members of the Allied Occupation Forces and those specifically authorized by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. Provisions for the confiscation of British Armed Forces special Vouchers and Australian penny and half-penny coins in the possession of unauthorized persons, or organizations, and appropriate punitive measures shall be included.

2. The Japanese Government shall treat British Armed Forces Special Vouchers and Australian penny and half-penny coins as neither legal tender, nor a lawful medium of exchange, and it shall turn over all such currency coming into its possession', custody, or control, to the Chief Paymaster, Headquarters, British Commonwealth Occupation Force, ETA JTMA, JAPAN.

3. As a matter of information it is advised that British Armed Forces Special Vouchers are in sterling values of the following denominations and general description:

DENOMINATION SIZE IN INCHES GENERAL DESCRIPTION

LI (One pound) 5 1/2 x 2 3/4 (Front mainly blue, some pink and green. Back, mainly blue some green and pink)

10/d (Ten shillings) 5 1/2 x 2 3/4 (Front, mainly purple, some orange and red. Back, purple, red and orange)

5/d (Five shillings) 5 x 2 1/2 (Front, mainly green, some mauve and orange. Back, green and brown)

2/6d (Two shillings and sixpence) 5 x 2 1/2 (Front mainly red, some light green. Back, red and purple)

1/d (One shilling) 4 1/2 x 2 1/4 (Front mainly grey, some light green and orange. Back, all grey)

/6d (Sixpence) 4 1/2 x 2 1/4 (Front, mainly brown, some pink and blue. Back, all brown)

/3d (Three pence) 4 1/2 x 2 1/4 (Front, mainly mauve, some orange and green. Back, all mauve)

All notes are printed on a white background.

LI. 10/ --. 5/ --. and 2/6d denominations are serially numbered.

4. Australian copper coins are mostly of one type with the King's head (George VI) on the obverse and a Kangaroo on the reverse together with the word "Australia”. Another type which may be used has the head of King George V on one side and the words "Commonwealth of Australia" on the reverse.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ A. G. Rehe
for /t/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1656)

2 May 1947

1. Check sheet dated 29 April 1947 from the Fiscal Director forwards a letter from CINC, BCOF which outlines a plan to introduce BCOF Special Vouchers to be used in all BCOF establishments after 6 May 1947 instead of Japanese yen. Fiscal Director recommends approval of this Plan. ESS/FI has no objection to this proposal and in view of the Fiscal Director's recommendation, it is considered that the necessary approval should be given.

2. CINC, BCOF requests that IJG be required to enact legislation necessary to make possession of such currencies by Japanese Nationals illegal and forwards a suggested directive to the IJG which would accomplish this. It is considered that the directive as proposed by CINC, BCOF is sufficiently adequate and is, therefore, being dispatched verbatim.

3. This does not affect the Korean area.

4. Concurrences: ESS/LE; FD (see attached C/S)

W. K. L.

Dip Section - No interest Mr. Sebald
