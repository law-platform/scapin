GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG123 (26 Oct 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 194)

26 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Request for Release of Funds

1. Reference is made to CLO No. 331 transmitting three applications submitted by Askania K.K. Company for release of funds subject to the Ordinance of the Ministry of Finance for the Preservation of the Property of the Specified Powers (CLOM No. 49).

2. Your attention is directed to paragraph 4, Memorandum to Imperial Japanese Government, AG 091 (24 Oct 45) ESS/FI, which directs that certain information be obtained prior to forwarding subsequent requests of similar nature.

3. Applications referred to in paragraph 1 above, will be considered upon receipt of the necessary information.

4. Applications for release of assets of C. Raphinphan and P. Woranat are being considered separately by this Headquarters.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 194)

28 October 1945

CFT/JNC/kns

1. CLO No. 331, transmitted three applications of Askania K.K. Company and two applications of former residents of Thailand, for release of funds impounded because they were former enemy nationals (CLOM 49).

2. The three applications of Askania K.K. Company were referred back to Central Liaison Office for submission of additional information required in Memorandum to Imperial Japanese Government, AG 091 (24 Oct 45) ESS-FI. The remaining two applications were referred to Insurance Section, Finance Division for consideration.

C. F. T.
