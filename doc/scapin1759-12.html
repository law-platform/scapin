GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.23 (2 Apr 48) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1759/12)

APO 500

10 December 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service Via the United States.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.23 (2 Apr 48)CCS, SCAPIN 1759/4, dated 2 April 1948, subject as above.

2. So much of inclosure 1 to the reference memorandum as pertains to word charges and divisions of revenue for full rate radiotelegrams from Japan destined to Saudi Arabia is amended to read as follows:

Through Rate .................$1.26

Japanese Terminal............. .▲.0675

Outpayment beyond SF ....... 0.69

American Carrier ............ 0.25125

Japan Radio .................. 0.25125

Credit American Carrier .......... 0.94125

3. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Telecommunications concerning matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K. B. BUSH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
