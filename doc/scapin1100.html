GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.1 (3 Aug 46) CCS

(SCAPIN-1100)

APO 500

3 August 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Establishment of Repatriation Mail Service Between Japan and Northern Korea.

1. Reference is made to letter C. L. O. No. 3389 (CC) of 10 July 1946, subject: “Establishment of Mail System Between Northern Korea and Japan.”

2. Reference is also made to Memorandum, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 311.7 (23 Feb 46) CCS, (SCAPIN 766) subject: Transportation and Handling of Repatriation Mail, authorizing establishment of a limited postal service between Japan and Japanese awaiting repatriation in formerly Japanese occupied areas.

3. Mail exchanges between the two zones in Korea have been temporarily suspended due to cholera outbreak in Korea north of 38°. Repatriation mail to addresses in Korea north of 38° may now be dispatched from Japan to Korea south of 38° north latitude for delivery in Korea north of 38° when mail exchanges are resumed. No assurance can be given that such mail will be delivered to the addresses.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
