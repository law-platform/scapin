GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014 (9 Oct 45) CE

(SCAPIN - 109)

APO 500

9 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Transference of the Hydrographic Office from the Imperial Japanese Navy to the Transportation Ministry.

1. Reference is made to letter from the Imperial Japanese Government file M.D. No. 61, dated 2 October 1945, subject as above , forwarded by the letter from the Central Liaison Office on 3 October 1945. The permission for the transfer of personnel, equipment and establishment of the Hydrographic Office from the Navy Department to the Transportation Ministry is granted.

2. This authority provides for transfer of personnel , equipment and establishments only and in no way modifies prior instruction of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers governing the movement and use of Japanese vessels and activities of Japanese Nationals.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General.
