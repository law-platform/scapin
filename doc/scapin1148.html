AG 561.4 (22 Aug 46) ESS/IN

(SCAPIN-1148)

APO 500

22 August 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Construction of Steel and Wood Vessels.

Construction of all steel vessels regardless of size and any wood vessels of over 100 gross tons is prohibited without the specific approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1148)

ESS/IN JZR/GEM/vpt

12 August 1946

1. Reference AG 561.4 ( ) ESS/IN memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, subject: "Construction of steel and Wood vessels" in that permission of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers must be obtained before construction can be started on any steel vessel regardless of size or on any wood vessel of over 100 gross tons.

2. Reference AG 561.4 ( ) ESS/IN letter to Commanding General, Eighth Army, subject: "Construction of Steel and Wood Vessels," in that the above letter revises paragraph 2 of letter AG 564 (2 Jan 46) ESS/IN, subject: "Definition of Responsibilities Regarding Shipbuilding and Repairs," paragraph 2 of above letter is to read "Your authorization will cover the activating and converting of private shipyard facilities as such" deleting from paragraph 2" and the initiating of construction of wooden ships of not over 100 gross tons."

3. Routine military surveillance will be required.

4. No concurrence necessary.

J. Z. R.
