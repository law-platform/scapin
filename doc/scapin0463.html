GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123 (20 Dec 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 463)

APO 500

20 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Release of Official Funds of Royal Italian Embassy

1. Reference is made to the Ordinance of the Ministry of Finance on the Preservation of the Property of the Specified Powers.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to release from the terms of that ordinance such yen deposits of the Royal Italian Embassy as may be on deposit in the Yokohama Specie Bank and other banks in Japan and which are to be used for the payment of salaries of the Embassy personnel, maintenance of Italian properties and the support of Italian nationals.

3. An acknowledgement of the receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
