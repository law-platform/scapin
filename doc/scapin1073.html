APO 500

20 July 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Limitation of Activities of Trade Corporation (Koeki Eidan) and Japan Miscellaneous Goods Trade Encouragement Company (Nippon Zakka Boeki Shinko Kaisha).

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 323.31 (14 Mar 46) ESS/AC (SCAPIN-815), subject: "Dissolution of Koeki Eidan and Nippon Zakka Boeki Shinko Kaisha."

b. Central Liaison Office Memorandum No. 2499 (ECI-T), dated 23 May 1946, subject: "Business to be Continued by Trade Corporation (Koeki Eidan) after its dissolution."

2. The Imperial Japanese Government will take the necessary action, pending final liquidation of the Trade Corporation, pursuant to reference Memorandum subparagraph la, above, to prohibit the Trade Corporation and its affiliate or subsidiary corporation, the Japan Miscellaneous Goods Trade Encouragement Company, from engaging in any transaction or activity whatsoever without prior written approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, with exception of the following activities set forth in subparagraphs 2a, b and c of reference Memorandum subparagraph 1b, above:

a. Procurement of goods and materials necessary for construction work for the Occupation Forces.

b. Procurement of furniture, electric equipment, and other fixtures and furnishings required for houses and barracks for the Occupation Forces and their families.

c. Business related to the export of raw materials, such as crude rubber, tin, antimony, lead, sulphur, etc., to be carried out without delay in compliance with Memoranda from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government will submit promptly to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, in quintuplicate, typed in English on 9" x 11" paper, within thirty (30) days from the date hereof, the following:

a. Complete inventory as of 1 July 1946 of all property of whatever kind owned or controlled directly or indirectly, in whole or in part, by the Trade Corporation and the Japan Miscellaneous Goods Trade Encouragement Company.

b. List of all property liquidated since 15 August 1945, together with the dates of liquidation thereof and the amount of money or property received therefor.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. Y. Hersey
/t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1073)

WFM/JMcIR/TKW/mcf

15 July 1948

1. a. The Trade Corporation (Koeki Eidan) and its subsidiary, the Japan Miscellaneous Goods Trade Encouragement Company (Nippon Zakka Boeki Shinko Kaisha) were ordered dissolved by SCAP Memorandum AG 323.31 (14 Mar 46) ESS/AC (SCAPIN-851), subject: "Dissolution of Koeki Eidan and Nippon Zakka Boeki Shinko Kaisha.”

b. CLO Memorandum No. 2499 (ECI-T), dated 23 May 1946, subject: "Business to be Continued by Trade Corporation (Koeki Eidan) after its dissolution," petitioned for permission to continue certain types of activity in order to meet the requirements of the Occupation Forces and implement SCAP's import-export program.

c. The Trade Corporation and Japan Miscellaneous Goods Trade Encouragement Company are the two (2) organizations designated by the recently formed Trade Board (Boeki Cho) to execute procurement orders for goods required by the Occupation Forces for dependent housing posts, camps, and station projects.

2. Eighth Army representatives interviewed on the problem of procurement are very well satisfied with the results produced by the Trade Corporation and Japan Miscellaneous Goods Trade Encouragement Company. They are anxious that nothing be done which will cause a slowing down of the present program, the completion date of which, because of the shortage of critical items, has already been retarded some six (6) months.

3. The Government Property Custodian, CPC, expressed concern lest the Companies in question liquidate assets for which that Division is responsible and requested that safeguards be established to prevent disposition, without prior approval of SCAP. Consequently, submission of a current inventory and a list of property liquidated since 15 August 1945 is directed by paragraph 3 of the attached Memorandum.

4. The excepted activities provided in the attached Memorandum will be terminated, and complete and final liquidation of the Trade Corporation and Japan Miscellaneous Goods Trade Encouragement Company accomplished as soon as an efficient substitute acceptable to the Occupation can be established.

5. The interests of USAFIK are not concerned.

6. This is considered an administrative matter and no surveillance by the 8th Army is required.

7. Concurrences: ESS/LE (Lt. Col. E. R. Minnich); ESS/IE (Mr. C. W. Wright); ESS/FI (Mr. W. K. LeCount);

CPC/GPC (Maj. C. T. Ricketts); Engineer Office/SD (Lt. Col. N. L. Tate); GS (Brig. Gen. C. Whitney).

J. McI. H.
