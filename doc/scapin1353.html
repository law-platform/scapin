MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1353)

WKL/JEW/ih

7 November 1946

1. The Imperial Japanese Government has submitted a request for permission to grant an ordinary Civil Service pension to Baron Suzuki on the following grounds:

a. That he "has been always known as a pacificist", and,

b. That he has no means of support.

2. Government Section (Miss Norman) has investigated this matter thoroughly and recommends that the request be disapproved (refer to attached C/S from Government Section to ESS/FI, dated 24 Oct 46). The fact that the Japanese Government considered Baron Suzuki subject to the Purge Directive (SCAPIN 550) and removed him from office on 13 June 1946 contradicts the statement that he is essentially a pacifist. Furthermore, it appears that he is able to support himself, having assets valued at approximately \300,000.

3. On the basis of existing information, it is not believed desirable to exempt Baron Suzuki from the Pension and Benefit Directive (SCAPIN 338) which states that no individual who has been removed from office as a result of SCAP memoranda shall be permitted to receive "pensions, benefits, or other emoluments".

4. This is an administrative matter and no surveillance is required. The interests of USAFIK have been considered and are not affected.

CONFIDENTIAL

W. K. L.
