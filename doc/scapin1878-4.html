APO 500

6 May 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Concentration of Base Metals.

1. Reference is made to the following memoranda to the Japanese Government, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. File AG 410.2 (7 Apr 48)CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1878, 7 April 1948, subject, "property produced in Occupied Areas"

b. File AG 410.2 (7 Apr 48)CPC/LGP, SCAPIN I878/3, 23 October 1948, subject, "Concentration of Base Metals Impounded under SCAPIN 1878. "

2. The Japanese Government is directed to store in looted property warehouses all base metals which wore impounded in compliance with reference par 1a above after the issuance of memorandum 1b above.

3. The above concentration will be accomplished in the same manner as described in reference memorandum 1b above, with the following exceptions:

a. Any now base metals impounded in compliance with reference memorandum 1a above shall be reported on L-2 forms which arc to be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Civil Property Custodian.

b. Prior to the concentration of the subject metals, the Japanese Government shall obtain CPC numbers and L-3 forms authorizing the movement of the subject property to a designated looted property warehouse. The above CPC numbers will be obtained from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Civil property Custodian, Comptroller Division, and request for L-3 forms will be made to General Headquarters, Supreme commander for the Allied powers, Civil property custodian, property Liquidation Division.

4. The Japanese Government is further informed that the above instructions are to be applied to all base metals that will be impounded in the future in compliance with reference memorandum 1a above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
