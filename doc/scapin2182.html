MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Remission of Taxes on Souvenir Items Purchased by Military Personnel.

1. Reference is Memorandum for Japanese Government, AG 012 (24 Dec 45) GD, SCAPIN 491, dated 24 December 1945, subject: Remission of Taxes on Souvenir Items Purchased by Military Personnel.

2. SCAPIN 491, reference 1, is rescinded effective as of the date of the abolishment of the Convertible Account System by the Japanese Government.

3. Subsequent to the abolishment of the Convertible Account System occupation establishments and organizations are authorized to:

a. Purchase indigenous products on a tax free basis, from suppliers in Japan, provided:

(1) Purchases are made by the Central Purchasing Office, or

(2) Each transaction is covered by a Japanese Government export license and payment effected with a foreign exchange instrument.

b. Import direct or through Japanese Government licensed importers commodities or articles on a duty and tax free basis provided:

(1) When items are imported direct via commercial carriers, the importation is covered by a SCAP license form IE:426.

(2) When items are imported through or from licensed importers, the transaction is effectuated through a bonded warehouse upon presentation of a SCAP license form IE 426.

4. Occupation forces and United Nations Command personnel are authorized to:

a. Import items for their personal use on a duty and tax free basis provided that when such items are imported via international mail or commercial carriers, the shipment is consigned to the person authorized to import them and is covered by SCAP license form IE 426.

b. Purchase only automotive vehicles and parts therefor on a duty and tax free basis from Japanese Government licensed dealers operating bonded warehouses or bonded manufacturing warehouses provided that:

(1) The purchase of an automotive vehicle is covered by a SCAP license form IE 426.

(2) Payment for the vehicle and parts is by an acceptable foreign exchange instrument.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C.C.B. WARDEN
Colonel, AGC
Adjutant General
