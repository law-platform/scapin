GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 400 (1 Nov 45) ESS-IN

(SCAPIN - 226)

1 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokvo.
SUBJECT :Rubber seized by the Japanese in Malaya.

1. You are directed to provide this Headquarters at the earliest possible date with a report on disposition made of all stacks of rubber taken in Malaya by the Japanese. The report will include the following:

a. Location and disposition of all rubber stocks taken in Malaya at the outbreak of the war.

b. Detailed information on such stocks as were moved to Japan,

particularly regarding shipment, present storage location, and any markings or labels which might indicate prior ownership.

c. Information as to locations of any stocks which were concealed in Malaya.

d. Other available information useful in locating and establishing ownership.

2. A reply is directed on or before 8 November 1945 stating when the above report will be submitted.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
