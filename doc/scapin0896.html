GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (23 Apr 46) LS

(SCAPIN - 896)

APO 500

23 April 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

The Following named Japanese will be apprehended as Suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, EIGHTH Army, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest possible date.

NAME

RANK OR TITLE

DATE, CAMP, ADDRESS OR ASSIGNMENT

HAMAZAKI, Naoki

Sgt / Maj

Member of the 102nd Div., 77th Mixed Brigade, 174th Inf Bn. Was at Negros, P. I. Sailed from Leyte 16 Dec 45 aboard vessel U.S.S. Jackson.

HATAYAMA, Yoshimi

Lt.

Former member of the Kobayashi (Shimaji) Unit. Was at Batangas Prov., P. I.

Probable home address: Iwate-Ken, Shiwa-Gun, Akaish-Mura, Oaza Inubuchi, Ochiwara 2 Banchi.

INOUYE, Kenichi

Capt.

Was stationed on Truk. Former Chief of Staff, 4th Fleet.

KAWABATA, Shimekichi

Supervisor at Yamane Mine, Hiroshima from Sept 1944 to May 1945.

Probable home address: Nakamura, Nakahagi-Mura, Nii-Gun, Ehime-Ken.

KIMURA, Masayuki

Sgt.

Umeda Branch Camp, Osaka 1943 to June 1945.

MATSUDA, Ichiji

C. I.

Was at Mindanao, P.I. in October 1945.

Sailed from Davao, Mindanao, aboard vessel Hutchen, 27 Oct 45. Probable home address: Okayama-Ken, Okayama-Shi, Oka.

OISHI, Masayuki

Col.

Former member of the Kempei Tai. Probable home address: 1300 Sanjo-Machi, Utsonomiya City, Gumma Prefecture.

OTA, Mamoru

Lt. Cmdr. (Navy)

Probable home address: 26 1 Chome, Tokiwa-Machi, Urawa-Shi, Saitama Prefecture.

SUGI, Masafumi

Civ.

Fukuoka Branch Camp No.7 1944 to 1945. Probable home address: Fukuoka-Ken, Kaho-Gun, Yamada-Machi, Inobana Tanko, Tanko Shatuku.

TANAKA, (FNU)

Civ.

Medical Supply man employed by Murutsu Company at Umeda Branch Camp, Osaka, during 1944.

TOYAMA, Masaichi

Pvt.

Member of the Medical Corps. Was stationed at Tanagawa Branch Camp, Osaka. Probable home address: Aichi-Ken, Toyohashi City, Iwaya-Cho 1-2. Employed at Iwaya Hospital.

UEZONO, Yoshitsugu

Lt. Col.

Former member of the Kempei Tai. Probable home address: 3789 Omuda, Takasaki-Cho, Kitamorogata Gun, Miyazaki Prefecture.

URANO, Gunnosuke

Sailed from Singapore 1 Apr 46 aboard aircraft carrier Hosho for Ohtake Estimated to have arrived 9 Apr 46.

WAKABAYASHI, Seisaku

Vice Adm. (IJN)

Was stationed on Truk. Commanding Officer, 4th Base Force.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
