GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (4 Jun 47) LS-R

(SCAPIN - 1719)

APO 500

4 June 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

The following-named Japanese will be apprehended as Suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, EIGHTH Army, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp), at the earliest possible date.

NAME RANK OR TITLE PERTINENT DATA

MATSUURA, (FNU) Lt 2nd Lt, later Capt. 10 Co, (commanded by Capt Okawa, Kosaburo) 3 Btn, (commanded by Maj Ichikawa Tadashi) 11 Regt, 5 Div, Feb 1942.

MIYAKE, Isamu Lt Comdr Commanding Marine Anti Aircraft Gun Unit, Ballale Island, Solomons Aug 1942 -July 1943. '

KANEHARA Lt Comdr Commanding Marine Anti Aircraft Gun Unit, Ballale Island, Solomons Aug 1942 -July 1943.

KIMOTO, (FNU) Civ Asst Supt Taiping Jail, Taiping Malaya 1944 - Aug 1945.

KOMICHI, (FNU) 2nd Lt 10 Co, (commanded by Capt Okawa, Kosaburo), 3 Btn, (commanded by Maj Ichikawa, Tadashi) 11 Regt, 5 Div, Singapore Feb 1943.

OOKA, Chikara Civ Civilian Irrigation and Drainage Engineer. Formosa Development Co Papar Branch Manager. Jesselton Borneo, 2 months, Beaufort Borneo 5 months, Papar Borneo 1 Mar 44 - Aug 1945. Repatriated from Batavia on Setsu Maru 23 March 1947.

SAGEJIMA, Mangan Lt (Naval) Dobo Detachment of 24 Naval Base Force, Dobo (Aroe Islands) Nov 42 -July 43. Probable home address: Tokorogo-mura, Saihaku-gun, oaza Sueyoshi, Tottori Prefecture.

SUGIYAMA, Kosaburo 1/Cpl 1st Co 553 Independent Inf Btn, Kuching Borneo 20 Oct 42 - Aug 45. Repatriated from Singapore on Choran Maru 8 Jan 47.

TAMAYAMA, Keisan Higher Seaman Guard Ofuna Provisional POW Camp. 1 Apr - 1 July 1945. Previous home address: 225 Kujangri, Yansun-Mun, Ausung Kun, Kyungi-Do, Korea.

TOMA, Seisaku Civ Guard PW Camp #4 Ohasi, Sendai Area, 1 May 43 - 1 Aug 44. Probable home address: 151-1 Oimatsu, Tsuruta, Tsuruta-machi, Kita-Tsugaru-gun, Aomori-Prefecture.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
