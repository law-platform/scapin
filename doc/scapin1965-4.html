GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 602 (17 Nov 48) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1965/4)

APO 500

8 June 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Disposition of Property in Japan of Companies or Persons with Head Offices in Areas Formerly Occupied by the Japanese

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 602 (17 Nov 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1965/2, 1 June 1949, subject, “Disposition of Property in Japan of Companies or Persons with Head Offices in Areas Formerly Occupied by the Japanese,” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers; implemented by Japanese Government Cabinet Order No. 291, 1 August 1949.

2. In order to expedite the liquidation of assets of branches in Japan of foreign corporations with head offices in areas formerly occupied by Japan, the following procedures for the disposition of these properties will become effective as soon as the Inventory report required by Article 16 of Japanese Cabinet Order No. 291 has been submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. Items having a book value of ¥100,000 or less or estimated current value of ¥500,000 or less will be sold by the Special Liquidator on a negotiated sale basis upon reasonable public notice. Items having a book value in excess of ¥100,000 or estimated current value of more than ¥500,000 will be sold by the Special Liquidator under direct supervision of the Ministry of Finance’s Foreign Corporation Liquidation Committee on a sealed bid basis, the right to purchase being awarded to the highest bidder. Sales by sealed bids will be given adequate publicity in the Japanese press. Publicity will include a description and location of the property to be liquidated and will stipulate that:

(1) Property may be inspected and bids submitted during the thirty (30) day period following date of first public notice of sale

(2) All sales will be made for yen on a “where is as is” basis

(3) Sealed bids identified as to name and address of bidder and description of property must be submitted to the Ministry of Finance within thirty (30) days from the first public notice, at the end of which time public opening of the bids will be held and the right of purchase awarded to the highest bidder. The Ministry of Finance will reserve the right to reject all bids and to direct advertising for new bids. Copies of advertisements and lists of bids received will be forwarded to the Office of the Civil Property Custodian, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers immediately after sale is consummated

b. Securities may be sold on any of the major stock exchanges or through security dealers. If sale is made through security dealers, bids will be obtained from at least three dealers and award made to the highest bidder.

3. The assets of all branches not creating new companies will be converted into cash before 30 September 1950. The Special Liquidators for these branches should be directed to submit their plans for disposal of the residual proceeds resulting from the liquidation to the Office of the Civil Property Custodian, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers on or before 31 October 1950. Accompanying each plan for disposal of residual assets will be a statement from the Japanese Government showing the book value of assets and liabilities left abroad at the end of the war. Statements submitted by Special Liquidators indicating that the assets of head offices abroad were more than sufficient to meet liabilities abroad at the end of the war will be guaranteed in each case by the Japanese Government. In cases where satisfactory evidence concerning assets left abroad at the end of the war is not available, all residual proceeds will be deposited in the Foreign Creditors’ Yen Deposit Account until such time as it is possible to get an accurate statement concerning the head offices’ financial condition at the time hostilities ceased.

4. All companies seeking to reorganize by establishing a new company under Japanese law will submit their plans of reorganization to the Office of the Civil Property Custodian, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers on or before 31 October 1950.

5. Direct communication between the Office of the Civil Property Custodian, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the appropriate agencies of the Japanese Government is authorized to implement the terms of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

E.C, GAULT
Colonel, AGD
Acting Adjutant General
