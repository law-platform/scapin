GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 413.44 (28 Jan 46) CCS

(SCAPIN - 663)

APO 500

28 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Production of Radio Receivers.

１． The plan for production and distribution of radio receivers submitted by the Japanese Imperial Government in response to Memorandum from this Headquarters, AG 676.3 (13 Nov 45)CCS, dated13 November 1945, has been reviewed.

２． The prices shall be established in a manner that will permit appropriate quality and maximum availability consistent with SCAP directive No.3.

３． The proposal that set manufacturers be permitted to arrange for the distribution of their products is approved provided this arrangement will not interfere with equitable nation-wide distribution and provided a public agency is made available through which the smaller manufacturers can combine or coordinate their distribution facilities so as to assure fair success to the consumer market.

４． The proposal that the radio sets produced over the amount required to equip an average of 50% of Japanese homes be reserved for export, is not approved at this time.

５． The overall quantity of 3,144,670 home receivers planned for production in 1946 is approved. However, since the productive effect and materials required for the manufacture of all-wave sets and radio phonographs is greater than that required for producing medium-wave receivers, this production shall be limited to a maximum of 200,000 and 80,000 respectively until such time as the attainment of the overall 3,144,670 set production is satisfactorily assured. During any monthly production period, the manufacture of all-wave sets and radio phonographs will not exceed ten percent of the total receiver set production.

６． The plan submitted does not fully comply with the Headquarters Memorandum mentioned in Paragraph 1 above, in that it does not indicate the specific plans of the Japanese Government to allocate materials necessary to accomplish the program. This information will be submitted to this headquarters by 5 February 1946.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
