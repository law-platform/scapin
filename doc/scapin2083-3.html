GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.331 (3 Feb 50) GA

(SCAPIN-2083/3)

APO 500

20 February 1952

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
Subject :Customs, Immigration and Quarantine Operations

1. References:

a. Memorandum for Japanese Government, AG 720.4 (3 Feb 50) GA, SCAPIN 2083, subject: Customs, Immigration and Quarantine Operations, 20 February 1950, as amended by SCAPIN 2083/1, 22 October 1951 and SCAPIN 2083/2, 5 February 1952.

b. Circular No. 14, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, subject: Control of Entry and Exit of Individuals, Cargo, Aircraft and Surface Vessels into from Japan, 22 October 1951.

2. It is the intent of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers to inform foreign governments that the provisions of Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers Circular No. 14 prescribing the procedures for entry of individuals into Japan will be rescinded on the effective date of the Peace Treaty. In order to avoid any interruption in the processing of applications of individuals for permission to enter Japan, it is desired to transmit to foreign governments prior to the effective date of the Peace Treaty complete information relative to the procedures to be followed by individuals in applying for permission to enter Japan.

3. It is requested that the Japanese Government forward to this headquarters no later than 25 February 1952 its procedural plan for the control of entry of individuals into Japan. This procedural plan will be forwarded to forign governments for their information and guidance together with the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers announcement of the rescission of the provisions of Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers Circular No. 14 as it applies to individuals.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C. C. B. WARDEN
Colonel, AGC
Adjutant General.
