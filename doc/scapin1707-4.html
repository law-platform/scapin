GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (8 Jun 48) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1707/4)

APO 500

8 June 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service from Japan to French Indo-China, Via Manila.

1. Reference is made to memoranda for the Japanese Government as follows:

a. File AG 676.3 (27 May 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1707, dated 27 May 1947, subject: Establishment and Operation of Direct Telecommunications Circuits between Tokyo and Manila, as amended.

b. File AG 311.22 (29 Sep 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1786, dated 29 September 1947, subject: Provision of Payment for International Messages Filed by Boeki Cho (Japanese Board of Trade).

c. File AG 311.23 (12 Nov 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1818, dated 12 November 1947, subject: Expansion of Radiotelegraph Prepaid Reply (RP) Service.

2. Effective 15 June 1948 the Japanese Government is authorized to expand radiotelegraph service via Manila to include private, press and government messages between Japan and French Indo-China.

3. Messages from Japan to French Indo-China, with charges paid in United States Military Payment Certificates or Foreign Trade Payment Certificates, will be routed via Manila in conformity with existing traffic distribution regulations and practices.

4. Message classifications authorized from Japan to French Indo-China are:

Full Rate NLT/DLT Government

CDE Urgent Press

LC Press

5. Charges per full rate word and for ordinary press, terminal charges applicable and division of revenue between the Japanese Government and the American carriers, are shown in inclosure 1. Rates for CDE, LC, NLT/DLT classifications are proportionate and will be 60%, 50% and 33-1/3% respectively; of the charges per full rate word. Full rate charges will apply to urgent press messages.

6. Private messages may be sent from Japan by any organization or person authorized to possess and use United States Military Payment Certificates, or Foreign Trade Payment Certificates, by the organization or person in Japan specifically named in a prepaid reply (RP) voucher, and by Boeki Cho under the special arrangements set forth in SCAPIN 1786.

7. Prepaid reply (RP) message service as amended by SCAPIN 1818 is authorized between Japan and French Indo China.

8. Press messages, and government messages, except United States Government messages, will be sent prepaid in both directions. United States Government messages will be handled in accordance with the provisions of SCAPIN 1707.

9. Direct communication between Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Ministry of Communications, concerning matters within the scope of this memorandum, is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

8 June 1948

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT : Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN 1707/4), file AG 676.3 (8 June 48)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 8 June 1948, subject: Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service From Japan to French-Indo China, Via Manila

1. With reference to the subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. Radiotelegraph service is already available from Japan to French Indo-China with message charges payable in British Armed Forces Special Vouchers(RAFSV's).

3. Establishment of radiotelegraph service via Manila connecting with French Indo-China is desirable to accommodate organizations and persons authorized to possess and use United States Military Payment Certificates and Foreign Trade Payment Certificates.

4. The subject memorandum directs the Ministry of Communications to accept and forward messages in the authorized classifications destined for French Indo-China via Manila.

A J REHE

Major, AGD,

Asst Adj Gen.
