AG 091.4 (6 Feb 46) GC

(SCAPIN - 714)

APO 500

6 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Relief of Japanese Nationals in Manchuria.

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. No.516 (5.2), dated 2 February, subject: "Transmission of a Petition for Relief of Japanese Settlers and Youth Corps."

2. The matter of relief and repatriation of Japanese nationals in Manchuria has already been referred to the United States Government for appropriate action.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
