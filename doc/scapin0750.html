GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.3 (18 Feb 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 750)

APO 500

18 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Payment of Interest and Dividends by Teikoku Asshuku Gasu K. K..

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government AG 130 (22 Sep 45) ESS, dated 22 September 1945, Subject: "Control of Financial Transactions".

2. You are directed to grant permission to Teikoku Asshuku Gasu K. K. to pay to the following persons who are resident outside of Japan funds in the specified amounts:

L'Air Liquide S. A. ¥403,052.96

Societe Orient 27,680.48

Edmond Deschars 320.00

Pierre Philippon 320.00

Henri Melchior 16.00

Total ¥431,389.44

3. You will direct the Teikoku Asshuku Gasu K.K. to pay the entire amount into a blocked account in the name of "L'Air Liquide" at the Kobe branch of the Mitsubishi Bank. Mr. H. Aitelli, Representant Au Japon de L'Air Liquide will be permitted to effect withdrawals from this account up to the amount of ¥240,000 over a six months period beginning 1 February 1946 for the purposes of paying salaries and allowances, incidental office expenses and traveling expenses of L'Air Liquide S. A.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 750)

WFM/HSO/jrh

1. Memorandum dated 12 December 1945, was received from P. Depeyre, at that time official representative of French interests in Japan, requesting that permission be granted to Teikoku Asshuku Gasu K. K. to make interest and dividend payments to the following:

L'Air Liquide ¥403,052.96

Societe Orient 27,680.48

Edmond Deschars 320.00

Pierre Philippon 320.00

Henri Melchior 16.00

Total ¥431,389.44

The memorandum also requested that the entire amount be deposited in the Banque de 1'lndo-chine, Tokyo Office, in the name of Henri Aitelli.

2. Permission of this Headquarters was necessary since Memorandum this Headquarters, file AG 130 (22 Sept 45) ESS/FI, dated 22 September 1945, Subject: "Control of Financial Transactions", prohibits any transactions in the property of persons outside of Japan without prior permission of this Headquarters.

3. In memorandum this Headquarters, file AG 095 (27 Dec 45) ESS/FI, dated 27 December 1945, subject: "Application of Teikoku Asshuku Gasu K. K. to make payments to L'Air Liquide and others", to Depeyre, we replied that this Headquarters would authorize payment into blocked account from which withdrawals could be made with the permission of the Ministry of Finance of the Imperial Japanese Government but suggested that another depository be named since the Banque de 1'Indo-China is at present closed insofar as normal banking operations are concerned.

4. Memorandum dated 18 January 1946 was received from L. Jankelevitch, the present Official Representative of French Interests in Japan, requesting that this Headquarters reconsider its decision and allow the funds to be placed in an open account in the Kobe branch of the Mitsubishi Bank under the name of L'Air Liquide.

5. It is not considered desirable to remove all control over these funds and, accordingly, have directed the Imperial Japanese Government to have Teikoku Asshuku Gasu K. K. pay the money into a blocked account in the Kobe branch of the Mitsubishi Bank to the credit of L'Air Liquide and to limit withdrawals from this account to ¥240,000 over a six months period beginning 1 February 1946 and only for purposes of paying salaries and allowances, incidental office expenses and traveling expenses. This amount was established after descussion with H. Aitelli, representative in Japan of L'Air Liquide S. A. Mr. Jankelevitch is notified of our decision and action by the present memorandum.

C. F. T.
