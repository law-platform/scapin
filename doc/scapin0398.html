GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.3 (6 Dec 45) GD

(SCAPIN-398)

A.P.O. 500

6 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Production of Coal.

1. In view of the current serious coal shortage and the need for remedial action to prevent an impending economic crisis, you are directed to take immediate steps to increase the production of coal in Japan.

2. Based on estimates received from agencies of the Japanese Government, this headquarters has authorized limited production of certain essential mine supplies. This production, together with action to solve other problems connected with the mining of coal including transportation and the recruiting of labor, will be accomplished without delay.

3. Within 48 hours of the receipt of this directive, a representative of the Japanese Government will report to this headquarters with information regarding remedial action taken in compliance with this directive and estimates of the increase in coal production which such action will accomplish. Instructions regarding future reports will be issued by this headquarters at that time.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
