GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 323.43 (30 Nov 45) NR

(SCAPIN-371)

30 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government. (Department of Agriculture and Forestry)
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Whaling Operations Around Bonin (Ogasawara) and Kazan Islands.

References:

(a) FLTLOSCAP Serial No. 80 dated 27 September 1945.

(b) SCAJAP Serial No. 42 dated 13 October 1945.

(c) SCAJAP Serial No. 587 dated 3 November 1945.

(d) SCAP AG 800. 217 (3 Nov 45) NR dated 3 November 1945.

1. Reference is made to memorandum from the Imperial Japanese Government (Department of Agriculture and Forestry) dated 8 November 1945, file s. 314, to the Administrator, Naval Shipping Control Authority for Japanese Merchant Marine, (SCAJAP).

2. Japanese whaling operations will be permitted in the area around the Bonin (Ogasawara) and Kazan Islands, between N. Lat. 24°and 27° 30' and between E. Long. 140°50' and 143°50' during the period 1 December 1945 to 31 March 1946, both dates inclusive. This authorization does not establish any precedent for extention of whaling or fishing privileges in any other areas outside of those authorized in reference (a), (b), and (c) above, or for any subsequent period of time.

3. Japanese whaling vessels shall not anchor or use any harbor on any of the Bonin (Ogasawara) or Kazan Islands and no personnel from such vessels shall land or have any contact with the above mentioned Islands. Land bases for the whaling operations in the Bonin (Ogasawara) and Kazan Islands area may be established only within the area previously authorized in references (a), (b), and (c) above.

4. The following vessels are permitted operate in the area outlined in paragraph 2 above.

5. The attention of the Imperial Japanese Government is called to directive from this Headquarters on the whaling industry, reference (d) above.

6. Permission hereby also is authorized for the vessels listed in paragraph 4 above to leave, during the period 1 December 1945 to 25 March 1946 both dates inclusive, the area previously authorized in references (a), (b) and (c) above for the purpose of operating in the area hereby authorized. Access to this area will be made through the waters lying between N. Lat. 30° and 27° 30' and E. Long. 140°50' and 141°50'

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
