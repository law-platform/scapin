SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (9 Jan 48) CPC/GP

(SCAPIN - 1842)

APO 500

9 January 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Additional Application for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in the Fourth Quarter of 1947.

1. Reference is made to:

a. C.L.O. No. 8513 (LO 3766-FI/FF, 29 October 1947, subject, "Additional Application for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in the Fourth Quarter of 1947."

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (22 Nov 47)CPC/CP, SCAPIN 1825, 22 November 1947, subject, "Application for Use of Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption during the Period from Fourth Quarter of 1947 through Third Quarter of 1948," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Application for permission to use liquid gold, as requested by reference 1a above, is approved in the amounts and by companies listed. Liquid gold stocks in the pooled reserve under custody of the Ministry of Finance will be drawn upon to supply the liquid gold authorized. Members of the Kineki Seizo Kumiai granted permission to process liquid gold, by reference 1b above, may further reduce the liquid to consistencies noted below.
