OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.33 (2 October 45) ESS

(SCAPIN - 86)

APO 500

2 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Request for Use of Cotton

1. Reference is made to CLO Memorandum No. 96, requesting permission to use "zabuton".

2. Permission to use "zabuton" is granted subject to the conditions of Directive No. 3 from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers to the Imperial Japanese Government.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H.W.Allen,
/t/ H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General.

1 Incl:

Incl 1: List of "Zabuton" stocks and their location.

MEMORANDUM FOR RECORD : (No. 86)

4 October 1945

1. Letter from Central Liaison Office No. 95 requested release of "Zabuton" (kneeling pads) stocks held by companies listed on enclosure. This cotton is to be used for the manufacture of bedding for needy persons and war refugees.

2. Request granted because these stocks were intended for the Japanese Army and are now serving no useful purpose. This is in accordance with directives issued by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Forces.

3. The action is approved by Military Government Section and General Purchasing Board. G-4 is not interested.

Willard S. Egekvist

1st Lt. GMC
