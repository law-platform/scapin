MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1838)

ESS/IN JZR/GEM/ECW/ms

29 December 1947

1. Reference is C.L.O. letter No. 9028 (CM), dated 21 November 1947 subject: Construction of D-Type Cargo Vessels.

2. The Japanese Government is requesting that permission be granted to construct eight (8) D-type steel cargo vessels, of 1,990 gross tons each. The design is the small three island, will deck type cargo steamer, very suitable for coastwise trade.

3. Steel estimated for the construction of subject vessels amounts to 10,680 metric tons. Each ship will require 1,335 metric tons. Eight thousand two hundred and eighty tons are already available, leaving a shortage of 2,400 metric tons. The Ministry of Transportation stated that 1,000 tons of this shortage would be available by the end of December and the balance of 1,400 tons will be available by the end of March 1948 in new and re-rolled stock.

4. Financing arrangements are as follows: Financing for the construction will be made between Sempaku Kodan and the shipbuilders as a temporary measure, to enable the yards to purchase the necessary materials. This is to be done entirely on the responsibility of the Japanese Government. A co-ownership agreement between Sempaku Kodan and the assigned owners will then be drawn up. At this time, the Sempaku Kodan and assigned owners will make a formal contract with the builders, when actual construction will begin. The question of fuel was investigated and the Ministry of Transportation stated that all of the subject vessels will be built as coal burners and will not draw on the critically short fuel oil supplies.

5. Action taken: Memorandum is being forwarded the Japanese Government approving application to construct eight (8) "D" type steel cargo vessels, as stated and specified on the inclosure.

6. Concurrence: Civil Transportation Section - Mr. Duane.

7. The interests of USAFIK are not concerned.

8. Routine surveillance is required on this activity and will be reported in normal monthly Occupational Reports.

9. Further questions may be directed to Mr. E.C. Wingo, ESS/IN, Shipyard Industries and Harbor Development Branch, Tele. No. 26-8571.

J. Z. R.
