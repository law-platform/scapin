(SCAPIN-1149)

APO 500

22 August 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Correction to Steel Ship Construction List.

1. Reference is memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 561.4 (21 April 46) ESS/IN, dated 21 April 1946, SCAPIN-891, subject: “Steel Ship Construction,” which lists the steel vessels that were under construction at the time of surrender and for which authorization for completion has been given.

2. This memorandum is corrected as shown on the attached list.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1149)

ESS/IN JZR/GEM/aeb

1 August 1946

1. Reference is Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 561.4(21 April 46) ESS/IN, 21 April 1946, SCAPIN-891, subject: "Steel Ship Construction, " which lists the steel vessels that were under construction at the time of surrender and for which authorization for completion has been given.

2. Since the above memorandum was issued, various discrepancies have been reported in the listing of the vessels by shipyard, vessel number and tonnage.

3. It is to correct this listing that this Memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government and Command Letter to the Eighth Army is forwarded.

4. Concurrence: None necessary.

5. This is an administrative matter and routine surveillance of the authorized steel ship construction will be reported in normal weekly "Occupation Activities Reports."

J. Z. R.
