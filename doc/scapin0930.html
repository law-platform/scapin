GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (7 May 46) CPC/GP

(SCAPIN - 930)

APO 500

7 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Application for Release of Gold, Silver and Platinum for Production Use During Second Quarter of 1946.

1. Reference is made to CLO No. 1493 (EF), dated 1 April 1946 and LO 389, dated 26 March 1946, requesting permission to use gold, silver and platinum for production use.

2. Subject to the conditions of Memorandum from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 091 (Sept 45) MG, dated 15 Sept 1945, subject: "Production in Non-War Plants", certain metals are released for planning purposes. The metals are to be used during the second quarter of 1946 for the purposes stated and not to exceed the quantities specified:

GOLD GRAMS

Industry 80,000

Industrial Art Work 25,000

Dental Work 355,000

Gold-foil, dust and liquid chloride 33,000

TOTAL 493,000

SILVER GRAMS

Industry 53,800,000

Industrial Art Work 17,400,000

Dental 1,800,000

Medical and Experimental work 6,000,000

TOTAL 79,000,000

PLATINUM GRAMS

Industry 67,000

TOTAL 67,000

3. The above metals will be released from time to time from sources indicated by the Imperial Japanese Government and only upon specific authorization from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, to designated individuals and companies. Such requests for release will state the name of the applicant, the products to be manufactured and the quantities desired.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
