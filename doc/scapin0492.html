APO 500

24 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Application for approval of Loan to the Toyo Silk Yarn Spinning and Weaving Co., Ltd.

1. Reference is made to Central Liaison Office memorandum LO 43, dated 6 December 1945, subject: "Dissolution of Holding Companies," which forwards application by Mitsui Honsha, Ltd., to loan ¥300, 000 to the Toyo Silk Yarn Spinning and Weaving Co., Ltd. for the primary purpose of assisting former employees and their families who are to be repatriated to Japan from Korea.

2. The above application is denied.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
