GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091 (3 Dec 45) GS-KO

(SCAPIN - 381)

3 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Request for Protection of Lives and Property of Japanese Residing in Southern Korea.

1. Your mermorandum C. L. O. 655 (5.2) dated 20 November 1945 is acknowledged.

2. The request for permission to spend a relief fund for the Japanese nationals in Southern Korea is disapproved.
