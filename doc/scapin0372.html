GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.3 (30 Nov 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 372)

30 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Provision of Coal at Kyushu Ports for British Government.

1. Reference is made to your letter dated 25 November 1945, file C. L. O. No. 702 (2.2) subject as above and memorandum this Headquarters dated 19 November 1945, subject as above.

2. Port Miike, Kyushu, as the point of loading future shipments of coal for the British Government at Hong Kong is approved.

3. The vessel nominated to carry the fourth shipment of 8,000 tons of coal will arrive at Kyushu about 18 December 1945 instead of about 30 November 1945 as originally scheduled. The fifth lifting will be made about 25 December 1945. Vessels nominated for these liftings are the S. S. FORT BUFFALO and S. 3. SAVANAR, respectively. If either or both of those vessels cannot be loaded with coal at Port Miike on or about the dates indicated, this Headquarters will be notified by you to that effect, and at the same time you will designate another loading port.

4. You are directed to make necessary arrangements to supply and. load each of the above named vessels with 8,000 tons of regular, grade coal on the dates of their arrival at Miike. It is further directed that your agent at Miike handling this matter communicate with Sixth Army authorities at that port who will be notified of the action to be taken by you.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
