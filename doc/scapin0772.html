GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 471.66 (25 Feb 46) ESS/IN

(SCAPIN-772)

APO 500

25 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Manufacture of Industrial Explosives.

1. Reference is made to C. L. O. No. 1442 (2. 3) dated 29 December 1945, subject: “Manufacture of Explosives for Civilian Use for the fourth quarter of the fiscal year 1945 and for the fiscal year 1946.”

2. Attention is directed to Memorandum GHQ SCAP AG 471. 86 (10 Feb 46) ESS/IN (SCAPIN-727) Subject: “Manufacture of Industrial Explosives” dated 10 February 1946 which authorized manufacture of certain types of industrial explosives in the period ending 30 June 1946.

3. Additional manufacture of explosives other than authorized in paragraph 2 is prohibited.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN-772)

ESS/ lN RCAP/ mdb

25 February 1946

1. C. L. O. No. 1442 is a request from the Imperial Japanese Government for permission to manufacture certain quantities of explosives for the period January 1946 through March 1947.

2. The data supplied was considered in preparing Memorandum, GHQ, SCAP, AG 471.86 (10 Feb 46) ESS/ lN (SCAPIN-727), subject: "Manufacture of Industrial Explosives", dtd 10 Feb 46 which authorized manufacture of industrial explosives for the period ending 30 June 1946.

3. The present memorandum refers to the Japanese request, and to the SCAP memorandum which constitutes the action of this Headquarters on the request.

4. Informal concurrence by G-4 (Colonel Hutchinson)

J. A. O' H.
