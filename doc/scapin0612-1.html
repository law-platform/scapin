AG 000.8 (17 Jan 46) CIE

SCAPIN 612/1

APO500

18 September 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
SUBJECT :Application of SCAP Directive AG 350 (22 Oct 45) CIE on Administration of the Educational System of Japan

1. Reference : Memorandum, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 350 (17 Jan 46) CIE, SCAPIN 612, subject: Application of SCAP Directive AG 350 (22 Oct 45) CIE on Administration of the Educational System of Japan.

2. That portion of SCAPIN 612 referenced above which pertains to submission in English of all textbooks, teaching manuals, and instructional materials which are approved, sanctioned, or published by the Ministry of Education to this headquarters for review and approval before printing is hereby rescinded.

3. The Ministry of Education shall submit in English such textbooks, teaching manuals, and instructional materials which are approved, sanctioned or published by the Ministry as are specifically requested by this headquarters following publication.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K. B. BUSH,
Brigadier General, USA,
Adjutant General.
