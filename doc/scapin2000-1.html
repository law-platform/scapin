GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.23 (4 May49) CCS

(SCAPIN - 2000/1)

APO 500

21 July 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Japanese Police Radio Stations.

1. Reference is made to SCAPIN 2000, dated 4 May 1949, subject: Japanese Police Radio Stations.

2. The following paragraph and sub-paragraphs of the above reference are hereby changed or rescinded as follows:

a. Change paragraph 4h to read, "Inspect before 1 August 1949 all radio stations listed in Inclosure 5 and 6 to determine which, if any, of these stations are capable of meeting international regulations."

b. Sub-paragraph 4i, rescinded.

c. Paragraph 5, rescinded.

3. Direct communication among Civil Communications Section, and Public Safety Division G-2, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, the Ministry of Telecommunications, and the National Rural Police on matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
