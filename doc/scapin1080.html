SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 111.0 (23 Jul 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-1080)

APO 500

23 July 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Budget Estimates 1946-47.

1. Reference is Memorandum from the Imperial Japanese Government, 6 June 1946, CLO No. 2732 (EF), subject: “Budget Estimates for 1945 - 47” which submitted the proposed General Account Budget for the fiscal year 1946 - 47.

2. The Supreme Commander has no objection to the submission of the general account budget for the fiscal year 1946 - 47 to the Diet. The Supreme Commander neither approves nor disapproves of any item in the budget, but reserves the right at any time to require the Japanese Government to provide funds necessary to carry out policies of the occupation, and to prohibit the expenditure of funds for purposes inconsistent with such policies.

3. Upon its final adoption this budget shall become the sole budget for the general account for the fiscal year 1946 - 47. Any expenditures made or obligation incurred during the 1946 - 47 fiscal year pursuant to any temporary budget previously authorized by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers or pursuant to Article LXXI of the Constitution which made the 1945 - 46 budget effective pending the adoption of this budget shall become a charge against the corresponding items of this budget as though they had been made pursuant thereto and no further expenditure shall be made nor obligation incurred on account of any such temporary budget or through the operation of Article LXXI of the Constitution. This shall not preclude the adoption of supplemental budgets thereafter, provided such budgets are submitted in advance to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

4. The Ministry of Finance shall take steps immediately to:

a. Revise the fiscal procedures and accounting system so that it will currently and accurately reflect the character and object of expenditure by functions, activities and organization units.

b. Adopt uniform terminology to be used throughout the budget and accounting system to avoid the confusion which appears exist among grants, subsidies, relief payments, allowances, etc.

c. Prepare and present simultaneously to the Diet, each year, both the general account budget and the budgets of all special funds and accounts, including a consolidated statement of all government financial operations and the inter-relation among the funds.

5. It is understood that the present budget proposal replaces the so-called “original” budget submitted for approval by memorandum from the Imperial Japanese Government to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers through the Central Liaison Office, CLO No. 276 (2. 1) 18 January 1946, subject: “General Account Original Budget and New Subsidies in the Coming Fiscal Year.” Accordingly no action will be taken on that memorandum.

6. Direct communication between the Ministry of Finance and interested Staff Section of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, is hereby authorized to implement this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1080)

WKL/EMR/lhr

22 July 1946

1. The basic memorandum is in response to a request from the Imperial Japanese Government for approval of the proposed General Account Budget for the 1946-47 fiscal year. In its final form the basic memorandum accomplishes two things:

a. It states that there is no objection to the submission of the budget to the Diet; and,

b. It directs the Imperial Japanese Government to improve their fiscal and accounting procedure in order to present a clearer budget picture in the future.

2. As originally prepared the memorandum would have ordered specific changes in four items of the budget. The question was raised whether this action would constitute an extension of SCAP responsibility for the Japanese economy. On direction from the Chief of Staff those specific orders have been deleted from the present memorandum. It is understood that any failure to comply with SCAP directives through failure to provide funds for the program will be dealt with by separate memorandum in each case the need arises.

3. The memorandum for record which accompanied the earlier draft of the memorandum is attached to indicate the history of the development of this memorandum and also the concurrences thereon. No further concurrences are deemed to be necessary.

4. This is considered to be an administrative matter requiring no surveillance in the field.

5. The interests of USAFIK have been considered and are not affected.

C/S (PJM) has approved.

W. K. L.
