GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 560 (10 Jan 47) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN - 1454)

APO 500

10 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Protection and Preservation of the SS "No, 3 Amoi Maru" Ex SS "Islas Visayas,".

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG560(7 Nov 46) CPC/FP, SCAPIN1324, 7 November 1946, subject, "Repair and Refitting SS' Amoi Maru' ex SS 'Islas Visayas', SCAJAP No. A027", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

b. CMMC No. 3232, 26 December 1946, subject, "Islas Visayas (No. 3 Amoi Maru)"

2. The SS "Islas Visayas" ("No.3 Amoi Maru") is being placed in the protective custody of the Imperial Japanese Government pending further instructions.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to sail the SS "No. 3 Amoi Maru" from the Otaru factory, Hakodate Shipyard, Hokkaido, to the Tokyo-Yokohama area, upon completion of the repairs and refitting directed in reference 1a above, On arrival in the Tokyo-Yokohama area, the ship is to be moored and secured, protected from theft and damage and maintained in a seaworthy condition.

4. General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, will be notified of the date of the vessel's arrival in the Tokyo-Yokohama area and the exact location at which she will be moored and secured while in the protective custody of the Imperial Japanese Government.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
