GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

AG 410.2 (20 Jul 46) CPC/GP

(SCAPIN - 1071)

APO 500

20 July 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Amendment to Memorandum for Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 410.2(12 Feb 46)ESS/FI, (SCAPIN 732) 12 February 1946, subject: "Delivery of Gold, Silver, Precious Metals and Stones by Bank of Japan Branches to the Eighth Army", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. C.L.O. No. 3353, 9 July 1946, and LO 788, 2 July 1946, subject: "Delivery of Gold, Silver and Precious Metals and Stones by Bank of Japan Branches to Eighth Army".

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government file AG 410.2(12 Feb 46)ESS/FI, (SCAPIN 732) 12 February 1946, subject: "Delivery of Gold, Silver and Precious Metals and Stones by Bank of Japan Branches to Eighth Army", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

c. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 410.2(12 Apr 46)CPC/GP, (SCAPIN 875)12 April 1946, subject: "Amendment to Memorandum to Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (12 Feb 46) ESS/FI, (SCAPIN 732) subject: "Delivery of Gold, Silver and Precious Metals & Stones by Bank of Japan Branches to Eighth Army", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The request of the Imperial Japanese Government that silver coins of denominations less than fifty (50) sen be excluded from the provisions of the Memorandum cited in paragraph 1b above, is approved.

3. The memorandum cited in paragraph 1c above, is rescinded.

4. The memorandum cited in paragraph 1b above, is amended so that the indented portion of paragraph 2 will read as follows :

"Gold and silver coins, except silver coins of fifty (50) sen denomination or less; gold, silver and platinum bullion or alloys in bullion form; diamonds and other precious stones; precious metals; all currency not legal tender in Japan;".

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
