APO 500

22 November 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Application for Use of Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption During the Period from Fourth Quarter of 1947 through Third Quarter of 1948.

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. No. 7179(LO 3118-FI/FF), 11 September 1947, subject, "Application for Use of Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption during the Period from Fourth Quarter of 1947 through Third Quarter of 1948."

2. The Japanese Government is authorized to release 696,000 grams gold, 476,000 grams silver, and 1,000 grams rhodium to the Kikinzoku Kyokai for initial processing into plates.

3. Gold and silver will be obtained from newly-mined stocks held by the Japanese Government. 188 grams of rhodium will be obtained from the Yokogawa Electric Company, Tokyo and 812 grams will be released to authorized representatives of the Ministry of Finance from Eighth Army Vaults, Osaka Mint. At the time the rhodium is released, the Japanese Government will deposit with the Custodian Eighth Army Vaults, Osaka Mint, 2,899.36 grams of gold. This gold will be in the form of bars of mint or near mint fineness.

4. After initial processing by Kikinzoku Kyokai, the precious metals will be released as shown below for manufacture into commercial forms:

Manufacturer of End Products Precious Metals to be Released Manufactured

Kimpaku Seizo Kumiai 160,000 grams gold 9,920,000 sheets gold foil

Kimpaku Seizo Kumiai 120,000 grams gold 114,000 grams gold powder

Ishikawaken Hakushokogyo Kyodo Kumiai 400,000 grams silver 8,800,000 sheets silver foil

Kimpun Seizo Kumiai 40,000 grams silver 38,000 grams silver powder

5. The precious metal products produced in accordance with this memorandum will be held under the custody of the Ministry of Finance, and will not be allocated for use without the prior approval of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
