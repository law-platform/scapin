GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.91 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST

(SCAPIN - 1925/2)

APO 500

14 April 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Access to Japanese Scientific and Technical Information in Japan

1. References are:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 000.91 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST, SCAPIN 1925, 6 August 1948, subject, same as above.

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 000.91 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST, SCAPIN 1925/1, 16 February 1949, subject, same as above.

2. Paragraph 1 of reference la is amended to read:

"The Japanese Government is hereby directed to make available from the date of this memorandum through 31 December 1949 all scientific and technical processes having industrial or commercial value of Japanese origin and ownership and developed prior to 31 December 1945 to technical representatives of governments of members of the Far Eastern Commission or non-Far Eastern Commission countries at war with Japan subsequent to 7 December 1941."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ A. J. Rehe
for /t/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General

MEMORANDUM

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to SCAPIN 1925/2, AG 000.91 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST, 14 Apr 49, subject: Access to Japanese Scientific and Technical Information in Japan

1. References are:

a. Memorandum AG 000.91 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST, dated 6 August 1948, subject: Information of General Application Pertaining to SCAPIN 1925, AG 000.91 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST, 6 August 1948, subject: Access to Japanese Scientific and Technical Information in Japan.

i. Memorandum from SCAP to Belgian Diplomatic-Representative, et al, AG 000.91 (2 July 48) ESS/ST, dated 14 August 1949, subject, same as reference lb.

j. Memorandum from SCAP to Head of the Australian Mission in Japan, et al, AG 000.91 (6 Aug 48) ESS/ST, dated 16 February 1949, subject, same as above.

k. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from SCAP, AG 000.91 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST, dated 6 August 1948, SCAPIN 1925, subject, same as above.

l. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from SCAP, AG 000.91 (26 Jul 48) ESS/ST, dated 16 February 1949, SCAPIN 1925/1, subject, same as above.

m. Letter from SCAP to CG, Eighth Army, AG 000.91 (2 Jul 48) ESS/ST, dated 16 August 1948, subject, same as above.

n. Letter from SCAP to CG, Eighth Army, AG 000.91 (6 Aug 48) ESS/ST, dated 16 February 1949, subject, same as above.

2. Reference la states that the Far Eastern Commission has adopted FEC 280/9 allowing access to Japanese scientific and technical information by FEC countries. Reference 1b extends this policy to non-FEC countries at war with Japan. Terminal date of period of access was established as 31 March 1949 for both policies.

3. Reference 1c states Far Eastern Commission is considering extension of FEC 280/9 to 31 December 1949 and requests comments.

4. Reference 1d recommended that because of limited utilization, the period of access be extended only to July 1949.

5. Reference 1f states extension of policies to 31 December 1949 would be in best interest of the United States.

6. Reference 1g stated there were no serious objections to extension of policies to 31 December 1949 and that action was being taken here to effect such extension.

7. Reference 1h, 1k and lm implemented FEC policy 280/9.

8. Reference 1j, 1l and 1n implemented extension of policy FEC 280/9 to include non-FEC countries at war with Japan.

9. This action extends the period of subject policies to 31 December 1949.

10. This is an administrative matter.

11. Concurrences: DS (Capt Hamlin) As per tel conversation with Mr. Kraegel, 9 Apr 49.

L. H. Cross - 26-8057

H. C. K.
