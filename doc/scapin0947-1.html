GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.14 (13 May 46) CIE

(SCAPIN- 947/1)

APO 500

20 February 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Prohibition of Certain Subjects in Designs of Japanese Postage Stamps and Currency

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government file AG 311.14 (13 May 46) CIE, SCAPIN 947, 13 May 1946, subject: “Prohibition of Certain Subjects in Designs of Japanese Postage Stamps and Currency”, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Paragraph 5 of above reference is hereby rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K. B. Bush
Brigadiar General, USA
Adjutant General.
