GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.12 (11 Nov 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 265)

11 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Transmission of Allied Occupation Forces Mail.

1． To facilitate the delivery of official communications of the Allied Occupation Forces, and personal mail of individuals of the Allied Occupation Forces, addressed to offices or individuals located in Japan, the following action is directed:

a. Arrangements will be made immediately to transmit within Japan all such mail postage free. This mail will be received by Japanese Postal Officials from Official Allied Forces Army or Fleet Postoffices which are the only authorized mail depositories for Occupation Forces.

b. Any mail from members of the Allied Occupation Forces which is received by Japanese civilian postal agencies from sources other than Official Allied Forces Army or Fleet Postoffices will be returned to the nearest Allied Army or Fleet postoffice.

c. This headquarters will be informed when the above arrangements have been completed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
