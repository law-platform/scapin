(SCAPIN - 155)

APO 500

17 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Request for reconversion of Sumitomo Kinzoku Kogyo K. K

1. Reference is made to your letter, subject as above, CLO No. 270, 15 October 1945.

2. The request for reconversion of Sumitomo Kinzoku Kogyo K.K. to the production of civilian goods listed in the application is approved.

3. Release of buildings and facilities needed must be obtained from the Commanding General Sixth Army before the reconversion is undertaken.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General
