MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Property of Chow Ring Ppaku (Deceased)

1. Within seven (7) days of the receipt of this memorandum the Japanese Government will accept responsibility for the storage and physical custody of the property itemized after identifying number "6495" in inclosure titled "Property of Undetermined Ownership."

2. Subject property was taken into custody from a safety deposit box in the Mitsubishi Bank, Tokyo on 13 August 1948, and impounded.

The box was rented under the name of Yanosuke Suzuki then residing at No. 110 Seijo-machi, Setagaya-ku, Tokyo, who was agent for Chow Ring Ppaku, a Chinese national. Information secured indicates Chow Ring Ppaku lived in Japan prior to and during World War II, and was alleged to have been affiliated with the Manchukuo Puppet Regime; that he returned to China shortly before the cessation of hostilities and that he was executed after his return by Chinese authorities. A claim for the property has been received from the Government of the Republic of China. Information obtainable to date has been insufficient to determine property rights.

3. Determination of property rights and disposition of the property will be deferred pending the initiation of direct negotiations between the governments concerned after coming into force of the Peace Treaty.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Major General, USA
Adjutant General
