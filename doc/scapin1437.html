GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 560 (4 Jan 47) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN - 1437)

APO 500

4 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Restitution of 17 Korean Fishing Vessels.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 560(2 Dec 46)CPC/FP, SCAPIN 2705-A, 2 December 1946, subject, "Korean Fishing Vessels" from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 560(29 Jun 46)CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1597-A, 29 June 1946, same subject, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

c. C.L.O. 3729(CM), 29 July 1946, same subject;

d. C.L.C. 3857(CM), 5 August 1946, same subject;

e. C.L.O. 4913(CM), 25 September 1946, same subject.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to sail the seventeen fishing vessels listed below to the port of Pusen, Korea, as soon as practicable and to deliver them to an authorized representative of the United States Army Military Government in Korea:

Ebisu Maru No. 2 Shinkei Maru

No. 2 Fuei Maru No.2 Kofuku Maru

Ho Maru Taiyo Maru

Kanhoku Maru No. 5 Gyosho Maru

No. 2 Seishin Maru No.1 Keirin Maru

Toho Maru No. 2 Kaiyo Maru

No. 2 Keirin Maru No. 11 Kyoho Maru

No. 15 Wakatsui Maru No. 11 Chosui Maru

No. 3 Shachi Maru

Prior to the departure of each vessel, clearance will be obtained from the Administrator, Naval Shipping Control Authority for Japanese Merchant Marine (SCAJAP) in accordance with the procedure established for ships leaving Japanese waters.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government will notify General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, as soon as possible, of the approximate dates on which the ships will depart from Japan in order that arrangements can be made for the United States Army Military Government in Korea to receive delivery of the vessels at Pusan, Korea and to repatriate the crews.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government will designate the captain of each vessel as its authorized representative to execute the necessary receipt forms in its behalf. He will have all copies of the receipt forms signed by an authorized representative of the United States Amy Military Government in Korea. Receipt forms for each vessel are to be distributed as follows:

a. Two copies to be returned to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, by the Imperial Japanese Government;

b. Two copies to be retained by the United States Army Military Government in Korea;

c. Two copies to be retained by the Imperial Japanese Government.

The Imperial Japanese Government will be responsible that all the receipt forms are properly executed and distributed as directed above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

17 Incls

17 Receipts,

6 copies each

(To addressee only)
