GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (14 Jul 47) CPC/GP

(SCAPIN - 1747)

APO 500

14 July 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Release of Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in First Quarter of 1947.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 410.2 (15 Mar 47)CPC/GP, SCAPIN 1572, 15 March 1947, subject, "Gold, Silver and Platinum for Domestic Consumption in the First Quarter of 1947", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

b. C.L.O. No. 3632(LO 1297-FI/FF), 12 May 1947, subject, "Application for Release of Rhodium for Domestic Consumption in First Quarter of 1947."

2. Rhodium in the amount of 217 grams approved by 1a, to be used by Yokokawa Denki Seisakusho, Shinegawa Denki Seisakusho, Nippon Koushuha K.K., Hokushin Denki Seisakusho, Yamatake Kogyo K.K. and Shimazu Company Ltd., will be released to authorized representatives of the Ministry of Finance from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Osaka Mint.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
