GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311 (24 Feb 50) SC PO

(SCAPIN-2085/2)

APO 500

20 March 1952

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Rationalization of Japan's International Telecommunications Services.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 311 (24 Feb 50) CCS, SCAPIN 2085, 24 February 1950, subject as above, as amended.

2. Paragraph 14 of reference 1 above is hereby amended to read as follows:

"14. The Handling of Transferred Account (T/A) messages, Receiver-to-pay (RTP) messages and Prepaid Reply (RP) messages, outgoing from Japan or incoming to Japan in accordance with Japanese Government laws, rules, regulations or agreements is authorized."

3. Paragraph 15 of reference 1 above is hereby rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C.C.B. WARDEN
Colonel, AGC
Adjutant General.
