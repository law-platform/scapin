GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.23 (11 May 48) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1762/6)

APO 500

11 May 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
SUBJECT :Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service Via Osaka-Colombo

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.23 (12 Aug 47)CCS, SCAPIN 1762, dated 12 August 1947, subject: Osaka-Colombo Radiotelegraph Circuit, as amended.

2. Effective 15 May 1948, the Japanese Government is authorized to expand radiotelegraph service via Osaka-Colombo to include private, press and government messages between Japan and the points listed in inclosure 1.

3. Charges per word in sterling for full rate, British Commonwealth Government messages and press messages, terminal charges applicable, and division of revenue between the Ministry of Communications and the British carrier are shown in inclosure 1. Rates for CDE, LC, NLT/DLT classifications are proportionate and will be 60%, 50% and 33-1/3%, respectively, of the charges per full rate word.

4. All the provisions of SCAPIN 1762, as amended; SCAPINs 1786, 1810 and 1818, pertaining to the acceptance and handling of all classes of radiotelegraph messages, settlement of accounts, and payment of revenue accruing to the Ministry of Communications and the British carrier, will apply in this instance.

5. Direct communication within the scope of this memorandum is authorized between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, the General Accounting Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers or the British Transfer Agent and the Ministry of Communications.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

1 Incl

Rates and Division of Revenue

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

11 May 1948

MEMORANDUM:

SUBJECT : Information of General Application pertaining to Directive Number SCAPIN 1762/6, File AG 311.23 (11 May 48)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 11 May 1948 subject: Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service Via Osaka-Colombo

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. Radiotelegraph service is already available from Japan to the United States, Central and South American countries, the West Indies and Asiatic points with message charges payable in dollar currencies.

3. Establishment of radiotelegraph service via Osaka-Colombo to the points named in paragraph 2 above is necessary to accommodate organizations and individuals authorized to possess and use British Armed Forces Special Vouchers.

4. The subject memorandum directs the Ministry of Communications to accept and forward messages in the authorized classifications destined for any of the points listed above admitting any of the authorized classifications.

A J REHE

Major, AGD

Asst Adj Gen

Distribution

Same as SCAPIN 1762/6

less Japanese Government.
