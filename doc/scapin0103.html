OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS
SIGNAL CORPS MESSAGE

(SCAPIN -103)

GC-O FGA/ws 8 October 1945

FROM :SCAP
TO :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
INF :ADFEAF TOKYO
COMFEAF NANILA
COMGEN CHINA
COMGEN AAF CHINA
COM PHIL SEA FROW
COM SEVENTH FLEET
CINCAFPAC MANILLA
CINCPAC

SURVEILLANCE AIRCRAFT REPORT THAT ON TWO NINE SEPTEMBER THIRTY TO FORTY AIRCRAFT BEARING JAPANESE AND GERMAN MARKINGS WEST OBSERVED AIRBORNE OVER TAI-OKU CITY IN FORMOSA PD (ZAX 6728) THIS INCIDENT IS IN DIRECT VIOLATION OF TERMS OF DIRECTIVE NUMBER ONE DATED TWO SEPTEMBER CMA WHICH PROVIDE THAT ALL JAPANESE MILITARY CMA NAVAL AND CIVIL AIRCRAFT REMAIN ON THE GROUND CMA ON THE WATER CMA OR ABOARD SHIP CMA UNTIL FURTHER NOTIFICATION OF THE DISPOSITION TO BE MADE OF THEM PD IT IS DESIRED THAT INSTRUCTIONS BE ISSUED IMMEDIATELY TO CAUSE STRICT COMPLIANCE WITH ABOVE CITED DIRECTIVE

OFFICIAL:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, US Army,
Adjutant General

APPROVED:

/s/W. E. Chambers
/t/W. E. CHAMBERS
Brigadier General, G. S. C.
Acting Asst. Chief of Staff, G-3
