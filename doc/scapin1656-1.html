SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123.7 (31 Mar 48) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 1656/1)

APO 500

31 March 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :British Armed Forces Special Vouchers

1. Reference Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 123.7 (5 May 47) ESS/FI, (SCAPIN-1656), 5 May 1947, subject: British Armed Forces Special Vouchers.

2. A second series of British Armed Forces Special Vouchers, in denominations of two shillings and sixpence (2/6), is now in circulation in areas and among personnel authorized to be in possession of such vouchers.

3. These new British Armed Forces Special Vouchers are mainly plum colored with some green and orange. Both the front and back sides are similarly colored. The vouchers all have a vertical line running the complete depth of the note visible only when held to the light. This line is approximately one inch from the left edge viewed through the face.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ A. J. Rehe
for ft/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1656/1)

WKL/TEB/JCS/mlc

25 March 1948

1. Memorandum PY/215/55, 16 Mar 48, from Chief Paymasters office, Headquarters, BCOF advises that present series of two shillings and sixpence (2/6) British Armed Forces Special Vouchers are being withdrawn in favor of a new series. Memorandum requests that JG be advised of description of new note.

2. SCAPIN 1656, 5 May 1947, ESS/FI, subject: British Armed Forces Special Vouchers, advised JG of description of BAFS and directed them that possession by unauthorized persons was to be a punishable offense.

3. Proposed SCAPIN is an amendment to SCAPIN 1656 to include the new series 2/6 notes.

4. The interests of Korea are not concerned.

5. No concurrences necessary.

J. C. Smith 57-8810 W. K. L.
