GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 00013 (15 Oct 45) GC-P

(SCAPIN - 140)

APO 500

15 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Transfer of the Navy Meteorological Posts under the Control of the Imperial Japanese Navy to the Central Meteorological Observatory.

1. Reference letter from Central Liaison Committee (Tokyo), N. D. No. 111, dated 9 October 1945, approval is granted for the temporary transfer to Central Meteorological Observatory of all Naval Meteorological equipment and personnel listed in the inclosure to letter above. Transfer to become effective as of 16 October 1945.

2. The Central Meteorological Observatory will be notified at a later date of the final disposition of personnel and the number and location of the transferred posts to resume operation.

3. Approval is granted to abolish all Naval meteorological posts not listed in the inclosure of the above communication.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
