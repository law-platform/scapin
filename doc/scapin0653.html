GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 132 (26 Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 653)

APO 500

26 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Release of funds of Martin Netke, German national

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. Memorandum No. 1112 (5.3) dated 14 December 1945, inclosing two applications of Martin Netke requesting release of funds in blocked bank account.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to release from the terms of the Ordinance of the Ministry of Finance on the Preservation of the Property of the Specified Powers, dated 20 September 1945, the sum of ¥20,000 in blocked bank account in Teikoku Bank, Tokyo Ginza Branch, for repairing his house and furniture.

3. This memorandum shall not be construed to release the account from the blocking provisions of Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, this Headquarters, file AG 121.7 (24 Nov 45) ESS/FI, dated 24 November 1945, subject: "Elimination of War Profits and Reorganization of National Finances," or from the blocking provisions of any other memorandum issued by this Headquarters.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
