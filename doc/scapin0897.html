GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.7 (23 Apr 46) CPC

(SCAPIN-897)

APO 500

23 April 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Control, Impounding, and Blocking of Property of Designated Individuals.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government is hereby directed to take immediate control of, block, and impound all property, real and personal including, but not by way of limitation, all gold, silver, platinum, currencies, securities, accounts in financial institutions, credits, valuable papers, and all other assets owned or controlled directly or indirectly, in whole or in part, by any person who is now or who hereafter may be detained, arrested, or interned by order of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government will execute within sixty (60) days hereafter, Inclosure No. 1, entitiled, “Information and Inventory Report'”, in all such cases and submit same in triplicate to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

3. To implement execution of same, the Commanding General, Eighth Army is authorized to permit accredited representatives of the Imperial Japanese Government to interview such prisoners, except those held incommunicado.

4. It is further directed that no transaction, whatsoever, shall be made concerning such property without the approval or license of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.

1 Incl:

Information and Inventory Report, Form CPC 2 (To addressee and Eighth Army only)

OFFICE OF CIVIL PROPERTY CUSTODIAN

INFORMATION & INVENTORY REPORT

Property Taken into Custody

1. Name of Owner

2. Address of Owner

3. Name of Property

4. Location of Property

5. Date of Inspection

6. Name of occupant at time of taking into custody

7. Present use of property

8. Name of appointed manager or custodian

9. Address of appointed manager or custodian

10. Description of property

11. Condition of property

12. Value of Property Basis of evaluation

13. Are negotiable securities (Stocks & Bonds) among assets?

14. Account of cash: - On Hand In Bank

15. Name & Address of Bank

Certificate - Information supplied above was made by me through personal inspection and is warranted to be true and correct.

Signed

Inspecting Official

Instructions:

1. Fill out one form CPC-2 (in response to question 4) for each separate piece of property at a different address, if more than one place is owned by owner.

2. Under description of Property, line 10, information should indicate residence, office Bldg, Forest land, factory Bldg, godown, etc.

3. For line 12 basis of valuation should be indicated by “Book Value” “Original Purchase Price”, “Current Market Value” etc.

4. In case property is or was owned by an association full names and addresses of the principal officers should be given on a separate sheet.

5. Inventory of principal assets found on the premises at time of inspection should be attached to this report.

6. If answer to question 13 is yes - a separate list showing details of securities owned will be provided.
