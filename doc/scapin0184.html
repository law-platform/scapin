GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091 (24 Oct 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 184)

24 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Application of German Nationals for Release of Property and Other Assets.

1. Reference is made to CLO Memorandum No. 241 with attached list of applications by German Nationals.

2. It is directed that the following information be furnished to this headquarters with respect to each applicant.

a. Type of business organization (sole proprietorship, partnership, stock company, etc.) and date and place of organization.

b. Names addresses and nationalities of officers, directors and stockholders.

c. Description of the nature of applicant's business since 1937, including products manufactured or merchandised and period engaged in such activity.

d. If the proposed transactions involve payment of debts by applicant, person to whom debt is owed and detailed statement of transaction giving rise to the obligation.

e. If application is for withdrawals from bank deposits of the applicant, name of bank in which account is maintained and present balance in account.

f. If withdrawals are intended for general operating expenses, an itemized statement of these expenses, specifying names, addresses and nationalities of persons to whom payments will be made and, if employees, their positions with the enterprise.

g. If a payment is desired to be made to applicant, the name of the bank into which funds will be paid and full details of transactions that gave rise to the obligation.

h. If transactions relate to insurance payments, the following facts:

(1) Address of property damaged or destroyed.

(2) Brief description (Dwelling, store, factory etc.)

(3) Occupied?

(4) For what purpose?

(5) Owner of record.

(6) In what company or companies was property insured?

(7) Date claim filed.

(8) Date claim approved.

(9) To whom will proceeds be made payable?

(10) Will proceeds be used to repair or replace old structures?

3. Consideration will be given to these applications upon receipt of the foregoing information.

4. It is directed that similar information, whenever applicable, be obtained prior to forwarding subsequent requests for release of property of Powers specified in CLO Memorandum No. 49.

5. If further detail on information required is desired, a representative of the Central Liaison Office should call at Finance Division, Economic and Scientific Section, this Headquarters.

6. An acknowledgement of the receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 184)

24 October 1945

1. CLO No. 241 transmitted applications for release of assets of various types (mostly bank deposits) owned by German nationals.

2. Transactions in property of Specified Powers, including Germany, are prohibited by ordinance of the Ministry of Finance on instructions of this Headquarters. Licenses granted to date limit transactions in such property to payment of certain taxes and certain withdrawals of individuals’ own funds for living expenses.

3. Lt. Col. Locker and Mr. Aik concur.

C. F. T.
