GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.3 (5 May 49) ESS/FIN

(SCAPIN-2004)

APO 500

11 May 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Securities Exchanges

1. References are:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 091.3 (25 Sep 45) ESS, (SCAPIN 59), 25 September 1945, subject: Securities Exchanges.

b. CLCO Memorandum No. 1193 (IL) dated 5 May 1949, subject: Opening of Securities Exchanges, which requests authorization to open the securities exchanges in Tokyo, Osaka and Nagoya at the earliest practicable date.

2. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 091.3 (25 Sep 45) ESS, (SCAPIN 59), subject: Securities Exchanges, is hereby rescinded.

3. The request to open securities exchanges in Tokyo, Osaka, and Nagoya as submitted in reference b above is hereby approved and the opening date of those securities exchanges is specified as 14 May 1949.

4. Transactions on all securities exchanges shall be subject to such controls as are deemed necessary by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

5. The Japanese Government is directed to submit for approval of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, separate requests for the opening or reopening of other securities exchanges, commodities exchanges, or similar institutions at such time as those institutions have demonstrated to the Japanese Government that they are so organized as to comply with the existing laws of Japan.

6. Direct communication between appropriate agencies of the Japanese Government and General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, is authorized for the implementation of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2004)

WKL/JRA/TFMA/mm

5 May 1949

1. SCAPIN 59 (25 Sep 45) instructed the Japanese Government that it would not be permitted to open any stock exchanges, commodity exchanges, or similar institution without prior approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Informal Memorandum for the Minister of Finance from the Economic and Scientific Section, subject: Reopening of Securities. Exchange, dated 1 February 1949, requested the Japanese Government to prepare legislation and perform necessary functions preparatory to opening of securities exchanges and advised that upon completion of such preparatory steps consideration would be given to rescission of the above SCAPIN. CLCO No. 1193 requests permission to open securities exchanges in Tokyo, Osaka and Nagoya at the earliest practicable date.

3. Since the old Japan Securities Exchange which comprised all former exchange has been dissolved and liquidated it was necessary to create entirely new legal entities. Toward this end the Japanese Government has accomplished adequate preparatory steps such as registration of articles of incorporation, court registration, registration with the SEC, etc., of three exchanges in Tokyo, Osaka and Nagoya.

4. It is considered that the above exchanges may be authorized for opening since the following pre-conditions have been met:

a. Appropriate legal registrations as cited above have been accomplished.

b. Physical facilities of the exchanges, space, equipment, communications, are adequate for operation.

c. Sufficient corporations whose shares are suitable for trading have applied to and been listed by the SEC. To date 477 issues have been approved for trading in Tokyo, 356 in Osaka, and 244 in Nagoya. Others are in process of application or examination. The securities exchanges are prepared to operate under and the SEC exercise adequate surveillance over transactions in accordance with the following three salient principles which are considered to be adequate safeguards against illicit security transactions:

(1) Every transaction made on the exchange must be chronologically recorded and records preserved for at least three years.

(2) All transactions in securities listed on the exchange originating in members' firms must be executed on the exchange. (There are legitimate exceptions to this general rule which are covered by exchange regulations but this will prevent the pernicious system of "crossing" transactions on the street or trading against exchange prices without resorting to or recording the transactions on the exchange. It will further preclude fictitious transactions.)

(3) Trading in so-called "stock futures" will be prohibited under the existing SEC laws.

d. The Securities and Exchange Commission's regulations have been premulgated and provide necessary regulation for operation of all securities exchanges.

5. It is considered that the formal opening of legal exchanges will effect a major contribution to the progressive operation of the Japanese economy since it will promote the flow of equity capital into productive channels under a comprehensive system of safeguard designed to eliminate the unscrupulous and haphazard practices which could be effected under curb trading to the detriment of the public. The attached SCAPIN rescinds SCAPIN 59, authorizes the opening of the three separate exchanges cited above and continues the requirement for separate approval by SCAP for the opening of other exchanges in Japan. No surveillance by Eighth Army is necessary for the operation of these exchanges. Necessary surveillance of the operations of these exchanges will be accomplished by appropriate divisions of the Economic and Scientific Section.

6. No concurrences required.

Mr. T. F. M. Adams, 26-6474

W. K. L.
