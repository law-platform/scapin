GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123.7 (4 Jul 46) FD

(SCAPIN-1052)

APO 500

4 July 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Authorization to Use Supplemental Military Yen Currency Type “A”

1. In accordance with paragraph 1, Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 123. 7, (SCAPIN-1051), dated.4 July 1946, subject: “Supplemental Military Yen Currency Type “A”, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, the Japanese board of communication through its offices is hereby authorized to accept Supplemental Military Yen Currency Type “A” in payment for prepaid personal radio, cable and telephone messages which are to be sent to or through the United States of America or its possessions and territories by members of the Allied Occupation Forces.

2. Strict accounting will be made for all Supplemental Military Yen Currency Type “A” received, and the entire amount received will be deposited periodically with a United States Army finance officer or such other agency as the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers may designate.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
