MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Compensation for Losses Occurring from Execution of Construction Works Based on Procurement Demand.

1. Reference: Letter, Japanese Government, Central Liaison and Coordination Office, Tokyo, CLCO Number 3813 (SPB No. 59), subject: Compensation for Losses Occurring from Execution of Construction Works Based on Procurement Demand, 11 November 1948.

2. The Japanese Government is directed to take the following action on receipt of construction procurement demands from the Commanding General, Eighth Army:

a. Prior to initiation of construction projects, ascertain whether the construction work demanded will infringe on property rights other than those of the particular structure or land area included in the procurement demand, such as blocking of roads, property access, damage to stream control works, damage by altering stream flow, damage to adjacent buildings and similar infringements. If the construction demand is found to cause such infringement, the Japanese Government will make a full report of the infringement to Commanding General, Eighth Army, stating in detail, to include yen cost, construction and acquisition required to eliminate infringement. Upon concurrence of Commanding General, Eighth Army, and approval of Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, the construction demand will be amended to provide for the necessary additional construction and property acquisition where property rights of others are infringed.

b. Claims for such infringement of property rights which occur during the progress of construction or at any time after completion of construction will be handled as follows:

(1) A thorough investigation, disallowance of unsubstantiated claims, and recommendations on substantiated claims will be made by the Japanese Government to the Commanding General, Eighth Amy.

(2) Recommendation of action to be taken will be made by Commanding General, Eighth Amy, to Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

(3) Upon approval by Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Commanding General, Eighth Army, will amend the existing procurement demand or issue a new procurement demand.

c. All appeals by injured party to action taken will be made through Japanese Government channels direct to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

3. Action will be taken on the basic request, reference paragraph 1 above, for issuance of procurement demands for compensation for just losses found to be attributable to execution of construction works based on procurement demands, in accordance with the procedure established in paragraph 2 above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
