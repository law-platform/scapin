GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (4 Jan 46) CCS

(SCAPIN - 555)

4 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Frequency for use of Formosa Wireless Station.

1. Reference is made to letter from the Liaison Commission for the Imperial Japanese Demobilization Ministries file S 483 dated 12 December 1945, subject "Data of Communication of Formosa wireless Station".

2. The request to employ frequencies of 7500 and 3820 kilocycles is not approved.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
