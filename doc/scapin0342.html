GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.3 (24 Nov 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 342)

24 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Weekly Report on Coal Shipments to Korea.

1. Reference is made to your letter dated 17 November 1945 subject as above, setting forth actual delivery and proposed schedule of shipment of coal to Korea for the period 6 October to 30 November 1945, inclusive.

2. It is noted that the proposed schedule, paragraph B, contemplates the dispatch of 114,400 tons of coal to Fusan, Korea during the November period. You. are directed to limit the shipment during this month to a total not exceeding 75,000 tons. No backlog accruing up to 1 November 1945 will be shipped unless specifically directed. In this connection your attention is directed to letter this Headquarters dated 19 November 1945, subject: "Provision of Coal at Fusen, Korea for use of Commanding General, U. S. Forces in Korea".

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
