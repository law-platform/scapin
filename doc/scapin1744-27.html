GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (14 Jul 47) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1744/27)

APO 500

25 September 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Control of Radio Communications.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 676.3 (14 Jul 47) CCS, SCAPIN 1744, 14 July 1947, subject: Control of Radio Communications, as amended.

2. Inclosures 1 and 2 of this memorandum are the bimonthly lists of amendments including all additions, deletions and changes to Inclosures 1 and 2 of SCAPIN 1744/26, prepared and submitted in accordance with paragraphs 12 and 13 of reference 1.

3. This memorandum reaffirms the authorization of all amendments authorized by direct communication between Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Radio Regulatory Commission during July and August 1950.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K. B. BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General.

2 Incls (distribution limited)

1. Amendment to List of Japanese Radio Stations

2. Amendment to List of Japanese Ship and Coastal Stations
