GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.1 (14 May 47) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1673)

APO 500

14 May 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Establishment of International Mail Service for the Ryukyu Islands.

1. International mail service will be established between the Ryukyu Islands and all counties of the world effective l5 May 1947. International regular-mail service will be governed by the provisions of the Universal Postal Union Convention of Buenos Aires, 23 May 1939.

2. Naha, Okinawa, has been designated as the international exchange office for the Ryukyu Islands. Closed mails arriving in Japan, labeled to or from Naha, Okinawa, will be handled in accordance with Universal Postal Union regulations covering transit mails.

3. A transit charge of thirty-two centimes per kilogram will be charged countries of origin on parcel post destined to the Ryukyus regardless of the ports of arrival and departure and the overland distance between such ports. Amounts due Japan, for parcel post transit charges will be deposited in the United States Treasury trust fund account "Deposits, Proceeds of Remittances to and Exports from Occupied Territories", Symbol 218,905.1 Japan.

4. Direct communications between Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Ministry of Communications, regarding technical matters within the scope of this memorandum, is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

APO 500

14 May 1947

MEMORANDUM:

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN 1673), file AG 311.1(14 May 47)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 14 May 1947, subject: Establishment of International Mail Service for the Ryuku Islands.

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. Due to limited world shining facilities serving the Ryukyu Islands, a large part of International mail to and from the Ryukyu Islands will be routed via Japanese ports.

3. The purpose of subject memorandum is to authorize the Japanese Government to accept and trans-ship International mail to and from the Ryukyu Islands, also to advise the Japanese Government of the charge due them for handling such transit International mail.

4. Civil Censorship Detachment, G-2, General Headquarters, Far East Command, maintains surveillance over International mail destined to Japan. Surveillance by occupation units of the Eighth Army is not required.

A. J. REHE,

Major, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
