GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 680.2 (13 Feb 52) GD/D

(SCAPIN - 2200)

APO 500

10 March 1952

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
SUBJECT :Relaxation of Controls Over Aircraft Entry, Exit, Passage Over, and Flights Within Japan

1. In accordance with the policy or returning certain controls to the Japanese Government, the following procedures for clearance of aircraft for entry, exit, passage over, and flights within Japan will become effective upon receipt of this letter.

2. Aircraft, other than combatant or those assigned to or serving the United States Armed Forces, the British Commonwealth Forces KOREA and British Commonwealth Occupation Force, or the United Nations Command will not be permitted entry, exit, passage over, or flights within Japan, unless authorized by the Japanese Government.

3. Aircraft flying into, over, or out of Japan will follow standard operating procedures as established by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. Ports of entry are Haneda Air Force Base, Tokyo, and Iwakuni Air Base, Iwakuni.

4. Each aircraft will have as a member of the crew a radio operator able to read and speak English.

5. Requests for authorization of aircraft entry, exit, passage over, or flights within Japan, will be submitted through appropriate diplomatic channels to the Japanese Government at least ten days prior to the date of the proposed flights.

6. Requests will include the following information:

a. Number and type of aircraft.

b. Markings or identification radio call sign.

c. Duration and purpose of stopover.

d. Purpose of flight.

e. Names and positions of crew members.

f. Name of the English-speaking crew member (for other than United States or British aircraft).

g. Names of passengers, if any, and purpose of trip.

h. Description of cargo to be carried.

i. Details or route and timing of flight.

7. Prior to final approval of requests which involve the use of Air Force bases other than Haneda Air Force Base, the Japanese Government will coordinate with the Commanding General, Far East Air Forces.

8. The Japanese Government will inform the requesting agency through diplomatic channels of the action taken on the request. Approval will be valid for five days from the date for which the flight is requested. Twenty-four hours notice will be given of any change in estimated time of arrival or departure.

9. Aircraft will not depart any point in Japan until pertinent Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and Japanese customs regulations have been complied with.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C.C.B. WARDEN
Colonel, AGC
Adjutant General
