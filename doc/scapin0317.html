GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 130 (19 Nov 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-317)

20 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Payment on Incomplete Foreign Exchange Transactions.

1. Reference is made to paragraph 3 of CLO Memorandum No. 472 (2), dated 5 November 1945, subject: “Request for Approval in Advance of Foreign Exchange Transactions, etc.”

2. The Ministry of Finance may grant general approval to the payment of remittances to individuals in Japan and the payment of collection items presented for collection by individuals in Japan, provided that:

a. The remittance or collection item is expressed in terms of the Japanese yen;

b. The remittance bill or notice of completion of collection was received in Japan prior to 23 September 1945;

c. The amount of any one remittance of collection does not exceed 1,000 yen;

d. The paying institution will not be indemnified by the Japanese Government for any loss which might accrue to it by such payment.

3. The plan described in paragraph 4 of CLOM 472 (2), under which the Yokohama Specie Bank would make loans guaranteed by the Imperial Japanese Government against pass books, drafts, bills of exchange and other financial instruments payable in foreign currencies, is not approved.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 317)

RCK/OJM/kns

20. November 1945

1. Paragraph 3, CLOM 472 (2), subject: "Request for Approval in advance of Foreign Exchange Transactions, etc.", requested approval of payments to remittees or trustees of the proceeds of yen remittances and bills of collection which had reached Japan before 23 September 1945 or when notice of collection had been received in Japan before that date.

2. AG 130(22 Sep 45) ESS, subject: "Control of Financial Transactions", prohibited transactions in foreign exchange. Payment on the remittances and bills of collection as requested does not affect the amount of Japanese foreign exchange assets. It is not considered that payments of remittances or collection up to 1,000 yen without guarantee of the payer will involve large amounts or transactions subject to later reversal by this Headquarters. Currency up to 1,000 yen is now admitted into Japan if carried by repatriating Japanese.

3. Paragraph 4, CLOM 472(2) requested permission to effect an arrangement with the Yokohama Specie Bank under which the Bank would make loans to the holders of various instruments payable in foreign currencies up to 300 yen monthly for a limited period. Any losses incurred by the Bank would be made up by payments from Japanese Government funds.

4. Use of foreign exchange assets as collateral for loans by banking institutions is not considered desirable pending reparations decisions. Government guaranties of obviously unsound bank loans are not to be encouraged.

C. F. T.
