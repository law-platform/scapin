GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 095 (23 Nov 46) ESS/AC

(SCAPIN - 1359)

APO 500

23 November 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Cancellation of Certain Contracts Involving the Trade Corporation (Koeki Eidan)

1. References are Memoranda for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. AG 323.31 (14 Mar 46) ESS/AC, dated 14 March 1946, (SCAPIN 815), subject: Dissolution of Koeki Eidan and Nippon Zakka Boeki Shinko Kaisha.

b. AG 004 (20 Jul 46) ESS/AC, dated 20 July 1946, (SCAPIN 1073), subject: Limitation of Activities of Trade Corporation (Koeki Eidan) and Japan Miscellaneous Goods Trade Encouragement Company (Nippon Zakka Boeki Shinko Kaisha).

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to instruct the appropriate Ministry to examine all contracts for the purchase of medicaments for export purposes involving the Trade Corporation in order to determine which of these involve items necessary for domestic use.

3. Paragraph 2 of Memorandum referred to in paragraph 1b, above, is modified to permit the cancellation, by mutual agreement between the Trade Corporation and the manufacturers involved, of contracts described in paragraph 2, above.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government will submit the following to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers in quintuplicate, typed in English on 8" x 11" paper:

a. A list of all contracts described in paragraph 2, above, showing whether or not the items are required for domestic use and any action contemplated under the authority of paragraph 3, above, to be submitted within thirty (30) days of the date of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
for /t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1359)

WFM/VHK/DBO/gg

31 October 1946

1. Reference is Check Sheet from Public Health and Welfare Section to Economic and Scientific Section, 4 October 1946, subject: "Cancellation of Contracts for Medicaments Negotiated between Koeki Eidan and Pharmaceutical Manufacturers for Export Purposes, " requesting that all contracts for medicaments negotiated between the Trade Corporation (Koeki Eidan) and pharmaceutical manufacturers be cancelled, and that the Welfare Ministry of the Imperial Japanese Government be given an opportunity to review each existing contract.

2. The Trade Corporation was ordered to dissolve by SCAP Memorandum AG 323. 31 (14 Mar 46) ESS/AC (SCAPIN-815), subject: “Dissolution of Koeki Eidan and Nippon Zakka Boeki Shinko Kaisha,” and it was forbidden to engage in any but certain specified activities without prior written approval of SCAP by SCAP Memorandum AG 004 (20 Jul 46) ESS/AC (SCAPIN-1073), subject: "Limitation of Activities of Trade Corporation (Koeki Eidan) and Japan Miscellaneous Goods Trade Encouragement Company (Nippon Zakka Boeki Shinko Kaisha)."

3. Prior to the Occupation, the Trade Corporation entered into a number of contracts for the purchase of medicaments from pharmaceutical manufacturers with the intent of exporting them. These medicaments are now acutely needed for domestic consumption. Furthermore, the Trade Corporation is now unable to pay for these goods. Therefore, in many cases both the Trade Corporation and the manufacturer involved desire to cancel these agreements.

4. Where it is now mutually agreeable by all parties involved to cancel a contract, it is not desired to encourage a disregard of the principle of the inviolability of contracts, by ordering these agreements cancelled. In these cases further investigations will be made to determine whether such a step is necessary or desirable.

5. The dissolution of Koeki Eidan will be hastened by the attached Memorandum and no dissipation of assets is involved.

6. This is an administrative matter, and no surveillance by the Eighth Army is required.

7. The interests of USAFIK are not concerned.

8. Concurrences: ESS/FT (Mr. F. E. Pickelle); ESS/LE (Major E. R. Minnich); PHW (Major C. L. Lecker); CPC (Brig. Gen. P. H. Tansey); ESS/RS (Mr. W. G. Hessler).

V. H. K.
