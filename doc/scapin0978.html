GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 437 (23 May 46) ESS/IE

(SCAPIN - 978)

APO 500

23 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Shipment of Salt from Formosa

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will take action and assume responsibility as follows with regard to approximately 200,000 tons of salt being made available for Japanese consumption:

a. Arrange with Shipping Control Authority, for Japanese Merchant Marine, for the assignment of suitable shipping to lift approximately 45,500 tons of salt monthly from May to October, inclusive, 1943 as follows:

30,000 tons at Seou Tao (Kotobuki Shima)

5,500 tons at Chian Chuin Creek (Shogun Creek)

10.000 tons at An Fing (And Hei)

b. Declare ports of discharge in Japan for cargoes of salt at the time shipping is assigned and make all the necessary arrangements to discharge the cargoes at such ports.

c. Submit to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers a forward schedule of the ports of discharge.

d. Instruct each ship master concerned to furnish the official representative of the Chinese National Government at Seou Tao, Chian Chuin Creek and An Ping, as the case may be, with quantitative receipts for the salt at the shipping point. Two copies of these quantitative receipts to be verified by the official representative of the Chinese National Government at the port of loading and to be returned to the ship master.

e. Both copies of the quantitative receipts will be verified by an official representative of the Imperial Japanese Government upon discharge of the salt at ports in Japan and will be transmitted to the Commanding General, Eighth U.S. Army.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is authorized to distribute the subject salt upon arrival in Japan. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 430 (11 Apr 46) ESS/PC (SCAPIN 960-A) dated 11 April 1946, subject: "Maintenance of Records of Storage and Distribution of Imported Foodstuffs." Accounting for the salt will be in accordance with the foregoing memorandum. A report providing the information stipulated in paragraph 1b of the above memorandum will be made to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, covering the distribution of the salt.

3. Terms of payment and accounting will be decided later.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ J. W. Mann
for /t/ B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 978)

RAM/HJZ/ho

15 May 1946

1. Reference is made to message CFB 00499 from CG China to SCAP stating that salt is available for export in Formosa and giving instructions for lifting of same.

2. This directive directs the IJG to lift the said salt in accordance with instructions.

3. A command letter is issued to the 8th Army to supervise this transfer.

4. This is a Policy Directive and Army surveillance is required.

5. This problem as it affects Korea has been taken with consideration.

6. Concurrences: PC&R - Capt Smith

Industry Div - Mr. Saunders
