GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.3 (30 Dec 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-515)

APO 500

30 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Request For Use of Gold, Silver, Platinum and Diamonds.

1. Reference is made to C. L. O. Memorandum No 389 requesting permission to use gold, silver, platinum and diamonds. Such permission is granted as noted below, subject to the conditions of Directive No. 3, dated 22 September 1945, subject: “Economic Problems,” issued by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers to the Imperial Japanese Government.

2. Permission is granted for the melting and refining of silver held by the Ishifuku Metal Industry Co., as follows:

a. About 64 tons

Mikawashima Factory of Ishifuku Co.,

1986, 9-chome, Mikawashima-machi, Arakawa-ku, Tokyo.

b. About 29 tons

11, 2-chome, Kamezawa-cho,

Honjo-ku, Tokyo.

c. About 4.5 tona

11, 2-chome, Kamezawa-cho,

Honjo-ku, Tokyo.

3. Permission is granted for the melting and refining of 4,347 tons of silver products seized by the Allied Forces at 2, 3-chome, Muro-Machi, Nihonvashi Ku, Tokyo. None of the above will be refined until a list of such articles contained therein as are salable be submitted to this Headquarters and further orders are issued as to their disposal.

4. Permission is granted for the appraisal of diamonds and platinum seized at the Tokyo Mezen Industrial School, 170, 4-chome, Nishiokubo Yokobashi Ku, Tokyo and now held at the vaults of Bank of Japan.

5. Permission is granted for the withdrawal of 20 carats of the industrial diamonds seized at the Mitsui Trust Co., Mitsui trust Co., Mitsui Bldg. Muro-Machi Nihonbashi Ku, Tokyo. Such diamonds are to be used only for the production of necessary industrial tools.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
