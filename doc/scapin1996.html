GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 461 (30 Mar 49) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1996)

APO 500

22 April 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Use of Extracts from Occupation Force Telephone Directories in Telephone Directories Published by the Ministry of Communications.

1. Reference is made to letter Japanese Government, file CLCO No. 864 (2P), dated 30 March 1949, subject: Application for Authorization of Inclusion of Extracts of Allied Telephone Directories in Classified English Telephone Directory for Greater Tokyo and Yokohama area.

2. The request for including certain extracts from occupation force telephone directories in the classified English telephone directory for the Greater Tokyo and Yokohama area, which the Central Advertiser K. K. proposes to publish, is not favorably considered. The use of any material contained in any occupation force telephone directory in Japan for inclusion in any telephone directories compiled, published and issued by private persons or organizations is prohibited.

3. The use of any material contained in any occupation force telephone directories in Japan is approved for inclusion in any telephone directories compiled, published and issued under the full control, ownership and supervision of the Ministry of Communications. From such publication and issuance, the net revenues, ensuing from any and all directory service (such as directory sales, extra directory listings, bold face listings, inserts, display advertisements, etc. for telephone subscribers) over and above printing contract costs, shall accrue to the Ministry of Communications. This approval is subject to the provision that the Ministry of Communications will coordinate its use of such material with appropriate directory publication units of the Eighth Amy, to insure that such material when used is as far up to date as is practical, consistent with the publication dates of Ministry of Communications telephone directories.

4. It is believed that an English language directory for the Tokyo-Yokohama area and environs, listing all telephone subscribers connected to Ministry of Communications' central offices within the area covered by the directory, would be useful in improving the handling of telephone calls. However, all telephone directories listing telephones connected to the Ministry of Communications central offices should be published and issued only by and strictly under the full control and supervision of the Ministry of Communications. All material properly belonging in such telephone directories should be copyrighted by the Ministry of Communications and its use should not be permitted by others to insure that all revenues which ensue from properly developed telephone directories s using such copyrighted material will accrue only to the Ministry of Communications. This does not preclude the Ministry of Communications from using the services of printing establishments for printing telephone directories, but all compilation of material, sale of extra listings and advertising space and all dealings with customers with regard to directory listing aid advertising services should be reserved for handling entirely by the Ministry of Communications, as only in this manner pan telephone directories be successfully published from both the standpoint of telephone servicing and revenue considerations.

5. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and the Ministry of Communications regarding matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

APO 500

MEMORANDUM

22 April 1949

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number SCAPIN 1996, file AG 461 (30 Mar 49)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 22 April 1949, subject: Use of Extracts from Occupation Force Telephone Directories in Telephone Directories Published by the Ministry of Communications

1. With reference to the subject memorandum the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The subject memorandum is issued for the purpose of prohibiting use of extracts from any occupation force to telephone directories in any telephone directories compiled, published and issued by private persons or organizations, but to approve the use of any material contained in any occupation force telephone directories in Japan for inclusion in any telephone directories compiled, published and issued under the full control, ownership and supervision of the Ministry of Communications, subject to the provision that the Ministry of Communications will coordinate its use of such material with appropriate directory publication units of the Eighth Army to insure that such material when used is up to date as far as practical consistent with the publication dates of Ministry of Communications telephone directories.

3. Paragraph 4 of the subject memorandum is not to be construed as on order to the Ministry of Communications to publish an English language telephone directory for the Tokyo-Yokohama area. It is included only to convoy suggestions and ideas relative to the content and desirability of the publication of such a directory by the Ministry of Communications.

If the Ministry of Communications should decide to permit a private party or organization to publish such a telephone directory, it is within its province to do so, but such a telephone no directory or any other telephone directories which would be published by private parties or organizations may not include excerpts of material taken from occupation force telephone directories.

J.W. WILLIAMS

Major, AGD

Asst Adj Gen
