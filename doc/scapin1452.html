GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 471. 86 (10 Jan 47) ESS/IN

(SCAPIN - 1452)

APO 500

10 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Manufacture of Industrial Explosives

1. References are:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 471.86 (27 Jun 46) ESS/IN, (SCAPIN-1038), dated 27 June 1946, subject: Manufacture of Industrial Explosives.

b. Letter for General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers from the Imperial Japanese Government, C. L. O. No. 6704 (ECI), dated 17 December 1946, subject: Manufacture and Use of Explosives and Accessories for Period 1 January 1947 to 31 December 1947.

2. Manufacture of the following types and quantities of explosives, to be used for industrial purposes only, is authorized for the period of 1 January 1947 to 31 December 1947:

Type of Explosives and Accessories Quantity Authorized

Gelatine Dynamite and Hagi Dynamite 5,500 Metric Tons

Ammonium Dynamite and Ammonium Nitrate Explosives 5,800 Metric Tons

Carlit 1,385 Metric Tons

Black Powder for Blasting and Fuse 500 Metric Tons

Fuse 45,000 Kilometers

Blasting Caps for Use with Fuse and Electric Detonators 70,000,000 Pieces

Electric Detonators 25,000,000 Pieces

3. Authorization is granted to manufacture the following explosives and accessories to be used for whaling and hunting purposes:

Type of Explosives and Accessories Quantity Authorized 20 Metric Tons

Smokeless Powder 20 Metric Tons

Percussion Primers 11,000 Pieces

Friction Tubes for Whaling 10,000 Pieces

Percussion Caps 25,000,000 Pieces

Sporting Cartridges 500,000 Pieces

4. Authorization is granted to reprocess into industrial explosives, the following military explosives which were turned over to the Japanese Home Ministry:

Military High Explosives 500 Metric Tons

5. The Imperial Japanese Government will submit to the Commanding General, Eighth United States Army, applications for authority to operate plants necessary for the manufacture of the explosives authorized by this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
/t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD, ,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1452)

JZR/EWS/rhw

27 December 1947

1. The Imperial Japanese Government requested authority to manufacture industrial explosives in letter from Imperial Japanese Government, C. L. O. No. 2817 (ECI) dated 11 June 1946, subject: Manufacture of Explosives and Accessories for Civilian Use for the Period from 1 July 1946 to 31 December 1946. This request was studied in detail and a SCAP memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government, AG 471. 86 (27 Jun 46) ESS/IN, SCAPIN 1038, subject: Manufacture of Industrial Explosives was issued authorizing the manufacture and use of specific quantities of explosives. This authorization will expire on 31 December 1946. Letter from the Imperial Japanese Government, C. L. 0. No. 6704 (ECI) dated 17 December 1946, subject: Manufacture and Use of Explosives and Accessories for Period 1 January 1947 to 31. December 1947 requests authorization to manufacture certain quantities of explosives and accessories of civilian use during 1947.

2. The manufacture of explosives shown by the present memorandum is authorized because of the following considerations:

a. The use for which these explosives are intended will fill important needs of the Japanese economy. These uses include coal mining, metal mining, quarrying, reconstruction of war damage, construction of roads, bridges and tunnels, and agricultural uses such as the reclamation of land, and irrigation. Small quantities of smokeless powder and cartridges are authorized for whaling and hunting purposes.

b. Quantities authorized are believed to be minimum needs.

c. Any desired checks on the manufacture can easily be carried out. The Japanese maintain a detailed system of controls and licensing at all levels and a detailed plan for manufacture has been prepared. The plan includes the listing of plants to be used and the amount of production scheduled for each.

3. The detailed plan for the manufacture of the subject explosives is based on a Japanese proposal modified after study by this division. The plan is being forwarded by separate correspondence to the Commanding General, Eighth Army for his use in considering the applications to be submitted by the Imperial Japanese Government in compliance with paragraph 5 of the attached memorandum.

4. Details of the intended use for these explosives are as follows:

a. Gelatine and Hagi Dynamite:

Use Quantity (tons)

Coal Mining 1,200 For use in mining 29,000,000 tons of coal

Metal Mining 1,600 For use in mining 5,000,000 tons of ore

Limestone quarrying 200 For use in Quarrying 6,000,000 tons of limestone

Agriculture 1,000 Waterways, irrigation, and land reclamation

Public works 700 Tunnels, roads, and reclamation

Civil works 600 Quarrying of building materials and war reconstruction

Miscellaneous 200 Repair of hydroelectric plants, railroad repair, and supply for retail dealers

Total 5,500 tons

b. Ammonium and ammonium nitrate explosives:

Coal mining 5,800 See paragraph "a" above.

c. Carlit (ammonium perchlorate explosives):

Coal mining 15 See paragraph "a" above

Metal mining 500 "

Limestone quarrying 300 "

Public works 350 "

Civil works 140 "

Miscellaneous 80 "

Total 1,385 tons

5. Routine surveillance by agencies of the occupational forces will be required. Letter to Eighth Army lists the approved plants and the amount each plant will be permitted to manufacture.

6. Concurrence: G-4
