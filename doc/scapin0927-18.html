GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 370.05 (7 May 46) GC-O

(SCAPIN - 927/18)

APO 500

17 March 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Repatriation.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 370.05 (7 May 46) GC-O, SCAPIN.927, dated 7 May 1946, subject as above, as revised, 9 March 1949.

2. Paragraph 3 of referenced SCAPIN is rescinded and the following substituted:

"3. The Japanese Government will carry out the instructions as contained in annexes to this memorandum."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K. B. BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
