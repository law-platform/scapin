GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 433 (15 Mar 46) ESS/PC

(SCAPIN-818)

APO 500

15 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Delivery of Wheat Flour to Japan.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will take the following action with regard to approximately 7,000,000 pounds of wheat flour being made available for Japanese consumption:

a. Take delivery of the wheat flour at points to be designated by the Commanding General, Eighth United States Army, Yokohama.

b. Furnish the Commanding General, Eighth United States Army, with quantitative receipts for the wheat flour at the points of delivery.

c. Until such time as proper distribution can be made, store the subject flour so that it is protected against spoilage, pilferage, theft or other hazards and is under proper custody at all times.

d. Distribute this wheat flour as soon as practicable.

2. The following information will be furnished:

a. The location of storage points, giving the quantity stored at each location and a statement that the flour is adequately protected.

b. A complete accounting for all imported food supplies to include local area distribution.

3. Terms of payment will be decided later.

4. The Imperial Japanese Gevernment is responsible for the equitable rationing of this wheat flour and will ascertain that distribution is made to areas where shortages are most critical.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General
