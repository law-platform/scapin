GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 451 (28 Dec 46) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN - 1426)

APO 500

28 December 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Return of Vehicles Erroneously Reported as Looted.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386.6 (25 July 46)CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1083, 25 July 1946, subject, "Custody, Shipment and storage of Looted Property";

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 451 (30 Oct 46)CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1248, 3 October 1946, subject, "Custody and Storage of Looted Automobiles";

c. C.L.O. No. 5971(RP) 12 November 1946, subject, "Request for Deletion of Automobiles from Lists of Looted Automobiles";

d. Memorandum to the Central Liaison Office, 451(6 Dec 1946)CPC/FP, 6 December 1946, subject, "Requests for Deletion of Certain Vehicles from List of Looted Vehicles."

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to delete from the List of Looted Vehicles the following three vehicles which were erroneously reported as looted:

a. Ford Motor Bus, Eng. # BB18F5016007, license # Chi6711

b. Ford Motor Bus, Eng. # Chi 178 Formerly BB18F456942, license # Chi6713

c. Chevrolet Motor Bus, Eng. # TR807703, license # Chi607

3. The vehicles described in paragraph 2 above will be returned to the Sodegaura Motor-bus Co. Ltd., Hamano, No. 1130, Mamahama-cho, Chiba-gun, Chiba Prefecture, upon receipt of this memorandum and notification of the action taken will be made to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

4. All vehicles which have boon erroneously reported as looted will be deleted from the looted vehicle list and released to the entity from which they were impounded only after the following stops are taken:

a. Information required in reference 1d above is submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

b. Acceptance of the authenticity of such documentation is indicated by memorandum from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
