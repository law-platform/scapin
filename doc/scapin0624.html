GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 130 (19 Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 624)

APO 500

19 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Application of Omero Principini for Release of Blocked Bank Account Funds

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. Memorandum No. 928 (5.3), dated 7 December 1945, inclosing application of Omero Principini for released blocked bank account funds in excess of the limits set by Memorandum this Headquarters for Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 091.4 (2 Oct 45), dated 2 October 1945, subject: "Authorization No. 1, Living Expense Allowance to Axis Nationals Domiciled in Japan."

2. The application of Omero Principini is denied.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
