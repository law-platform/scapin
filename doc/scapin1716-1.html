GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (29 Sep 47) CPC/GP

(SCAPIN - 1716/1)

APO 500

29 September 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Amendment to Scapin 1716, 3 June 1947.

1. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (3 Jun 47) CPC/GP, SCAPIN 1716, 3 June 1947, subject, "Allocation of Gold, Silver and Platinum for Domestic Consumption," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, is hereby amended as follows:

a. (1) Paragraph 2s (3) is deleted and the following substituted therefor: "6.4 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Rank of Japan, Tokyo)."

(2) Paragraph 2v (3) is deleted and the following substituted therefor: "37 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Amy Vaults of the Bank of Japan, Tokyo)."

(3) Paragraph 2y (1) is deleted and the following substituted therefor: "6 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan, Tokyo)."

(4) Paragraph 2z (1) is deleted and the following substituted therefor: "57 grams of platinum (to be released from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan, Tokyo)."

b. An additional paragraph will be added as follows:

"5. At the time the releases authorized by paragraph 2 above are accomplished, the Japanese Government will deposit with the Eighth Army Custodian, Eighth Army Vaults, Bank of Japan, Tokyo:

"a. 64.9 grams of platinum (from the Tokyo Institute of Technology, Chemical Research Laboratory for Natural Resources):

"b. 217 grams of gold (from the Tokyo Imperial University, Institute of Science and Technology):

"c. 384,924.4 grams of silver (3751.7 grams from the Tokyo Imperial University, Institute of Science and Technology and 381,172.7 grams in substitution for 4,398.745 grams of platinum released)."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
