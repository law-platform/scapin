GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 062.2 (8 Jul 46) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN-1056)

APO 500

8 July 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Census of Foreign Films in Japan.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to take a census of foreign films in Japan as of 15 July 1946.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government will issue an official ordinace, requiring all Japanese exhibitors, distributors, producers or other persons and firms having in their possession any foreign motion picture films to submit detailed information as follows:

(a) Name of Reporter

(b) Address of Reporter

(c) Title of film

(d) Name of Producer

(e) Number and length of reels

(f) Country of origin

(g) Number of prints and whether negative or positive

(h) Location of film

(i) Each reporter or owner will furnish to the Imperial Japanese Government details showing how he acquired possession or ownership of the films and his present right to such possession or ownership.

3. Five copies of this information in tabulated form will be furnished General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and will be submitted in the English language, by 15 August 1946.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government will instruct the possessors of foreign motion picture films who, after investigation, have been found to be illegal owners, to place such films in the custody of the Home Ministry, pending further disposition instructions.

5. The Ordinance will provide that no foreign film will be exhibited until General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers is satisfied that the film is legally possessed. It will also provide adequate penalties for violators. Three copies of the proposed Ordinance both in English and Japanese will be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers within ten (10) days of the date of this directive.

6. Direct communication between the Home Ministry and the Civil Property Custodian, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, is authorized in order to implement the provisions of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY.
Colonel, AGD.
Adjutant General.
