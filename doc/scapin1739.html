MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1739)

1. Reference is C.L.O. letter No. 4443 (CM), dated 7 June 1947, subject: Request for Authorization for Changes in Construction Program of Fishing Vessels.

2. Above reference requests permission to change the construction of four vessels from three shipyards to three other shipyards as shown in list attached hereto.

3. In view of the present work load of the various yards in question as well as the time element and also the material problem, permission for the change is approved by Memorandum to the Japanese Government.

4. The interests of USAFIK are not concerned.

5. Concurrence: None required.

6. Routine surveillance is necessary on this activity and will be reported in monthly Occupation Activities Reports.

7. Further questions may be directed to Mr. E. P. Sharp, ESS/IN, Shipbuilding and Harbor Development Branch, Telephone Number

J. Z. R.
