GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.7 (13 Feb 50) GD

(SCAPIN-2079)

APO 500

13 February 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
Subject :Procedure with Respect to Importation and Distribution of Petroleum Products Procured with United States Aid Funds

1. a. Rescissions:

(1) Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 463.7 (11 Jul 49) GD, SCAPIN 2026, 11 July 1949, subject: Transfer of Proceeds of Imported Petroleum Products to the Counterpart Fund.

(2) Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 386.7 (11 Jul 49) GD, SCAPIN 2026/1, 19 October 1949, subject: Transfer of Proceeds of Imported Petroleum Products to the Counterpart Fund.

b. Reference. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 334 (3 Apr 46) ESS/FTC, SCAPIN 854/1, 4 June 1949, subject: Establishment of Ministry of International Trade and Industry.

2. In order that the Japanese Government may assign to private enterprise all functions relative to receipt, handling and distribution of petroleum and petroleum products (hereinafter referred to as petroleum) procured with United States Aid Funds, the following procedure, retroactive to 1 January 1950, will be followed:

a. The Japanese Government will authorize the commercial companies, i. e., refiner or distributor, foreign or domestic, to which the petroleum has been allocated by the Petroleum Distribution Section. Mining Bureau, Ministry of International Trade and Industry, to receipt for the product to the representative of the United States Armed Forces on behalf of the Japanese Government.

b. The Japanese Government will be furnished, within ten (10) days after completion of discharging cargo, an authenticated copy of a shipping document. In the case of bulk cargoes the shipping document will cover the quantity discharged to the civil economy at ship side as determined by the ship’s gauges. When bulk petroleum is issued from military storage, shore tank gauges will be used in determining quantity issued. In the case of packaged products the shipping document will reflect the quantities indicated on the “Hatch Tally” slips. In the event of failure to arrive at a mutual reconciliation, the decision of the Sub-Area Petrileum Officer, Japan, will be final. The appropriate agency of the Japanese Government will contact the Sub-Area Petroleum Officer, Japan, Headquarters, Eighth Axmy, for coordination of this matter.

c. Quantities indicated on the shipping document will govern both (1) sales to the commercial company and (2) transfers to the counterpart fund. The Japanese Government will bill the refiner or distributor (i. e. the importer) according to quantities shown on the shipping document and at established yen prices. The Japanese Government will then transfer the yen proceeds of such sales (shipping document quantities times the yen CIF costs) to the counterpart Fund.

3. The Japanese Government will not engage in the physical handling of petroleum. All activities relating to physical handling of petroleum, such as transfer from incoming vessels, transportation of product to refiner’s or distributor’s facilities, distribution and sale of products to consumers, etc., will be performed by private enterprise in accordance with regulations established by the Japanese Government.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.Bush
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General.
