GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.3 (3 Jan 47) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN - 1435)

APO 500

3 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Restitution to the Catholic Missionary Corporation of the Sendai Diocese in Japan of Property Wrongfully Transferred.

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386.3(6 May 46)CPC, SCAPIN 926, 6 May 1945, subject, "Procedure for Returning Property in Japan to Nationals of the United Nations", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

b. Memorandum for the imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386.3(22 Nov 46)CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1354, 22 November 1946, subject, "Restitution of United Nations Nationals' Property Wrongfully Transferred", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

c. Letter to Miyagi Military Government Team, Fef. No. SFB-011, 8 August 1946, subject, "Disposition of Buildings and Ground in the former Military Arsenal Area at Harunomachi, East-Sendai, to the Catholic Corporation of Sendai", from Sendai Financial Bureau.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to tales possession of all property covered by the attached list and to assemble all movable property at a safe storage area by 6 January 1947, preparatory to restoring the property to the Catholic Missionary Corporation of the Sendai Diocese in Japan which owned the property on 7 December 1941.

3. It is further directed that the Imperial Japanese Government return all property on the attached list, and any additional movables which may be located and identified, to the Catholic Missionary Corporation of the Sendai Diocese in Japan, 161, Mototerakoji, Sendai City, at addresses shown on list of property to be returned on 6 January 1947 at 1000 hours, under conditions set forth in "Receipt for Restitution to United Potion National of Wrongfully Transferred Property in Japan"; and under the following conditions:

a. A general and a final report by the Japanese Government on the management of the Property from 7 December 1941 to the date of restoration, said report to include statement of all financial transactions, will be attached to each copy of the receipt;

b. If the property has been leased subsequent to seizure, the Japanese Government will afford the restoree the option either to allow the lease to continue in force or to terminate it at the time of the restoration of the property to him. Where the restoree decides to terminate such lease, the Japanese Government will take the action necessary to provide him vacant possession;

c. All inventories included in the receipt will contain a statement as to the condition of the property at the time of restoration;

d. Cash raid deposits covered by the receipt will be broken down into:

(1) Net proceeds from sale of property (If none, so state),

(2) Net proceeds from other transactions (if none, so state),

(3) Other (if none, so state).

e. Transfer of title will be made at the District Court having jurisdiction.

4. Four copies of accomplished receipt for the property with documentary evidence of the transfer of title will be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers within ten (10) days of completion of delivery. Receipts and evidence of transfer of title will be submitted, in both English and Japanese text with the Japanese translation certified as correct.

5. Copy of this memorandum will be attached to each copy of the receipt.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
