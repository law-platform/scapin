GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.3 (23 Oct 46) CCS

(SCAPIN-1285)

APO 500

23 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Release of Telephone Exchange and Telephone Equipment from Plants Named for Reparations.

1. This authorizes the release to the Japanese Ministry of Communications of telephone and telegraph exchanges and telephone and telegraph equipment from aircraft factories, arsenals, privately owned munitions plants, and laboratories held in custody for reparations in accordance with the following memoranda from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, to the Imperial Japanese Government:

a. (SCAPIN-629), file AG 004(20 Jan. 46) ESS/GD, dated 20 January 46, subject: “Custody Control and Protective Maintenance of Japanese Aircraft Plants, Arsenals and Laboratories.”

b. (SCAPIN-1134), file AG 387.6 (13 Aug. 46) ESS/IN, dated 13 August 46, subject: “Reparations Selections Within Privately Owned Munitions Plants.”

2. An inventory of the main items of equipment located in the plants and installations from which the subject equipment is released will be made by the Imperial Japanese Government and furnished to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, by 1 December 46.

3. Communications equipment in any plant or installation covered by this memorandum which is being used by the Occupation Forces will become the property of the Japanese Ministry of Communications but will be continued in service in its present manner and location as long as it is required to meet Occupation Forces’ requirements. The inventory of equipment which is to be made in accordance with paragraph 2 will indicate which installations fall in this category.

4. Direct correspondence between Generel Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Japanese Ministry of Communications on technical matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

GENERAL HEADQUARTERS

APO 500

23 October 1946

MEMORANDUM:

SUBJECT : Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN 1285), file AG 311.3 (23 Oct 46)CCS, this Headquarters, dated 23 Oct 46, subject: Release of Telephone Exchange and Telephone Equipment from Plants Named for Reparations

1. With reference to subject memorandum, the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. The release of the subject telephone and telegraph equipment is required in connection with the provision of communication service for the Occupation Forces or to implement the occupation mission. In general the condition of equipment and facilities involved is such that it would not be practical to remove it for shipment to points outside of Japan for reinstallation.

3. Command channel instructions regarding permission and surveillance necessary in connection with inventory and removal of the subject equipment will be forwarded.

R G HERSEY

Lt Col AGD

Asst Adj Gen
