GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (9 Jan 47) CPC/GP

(SCAPIN - 1448)

APO 500

9 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Gold, Silver and Platinum for Domestic Consumption in the Fourth Quarter of 1946.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. C.L.O. No. 4643(LO 1381-FI/FF), 12 September 1946, subject: "Gold, Silver and Platinum for Domestic Consumption in the Third Quarter of 1946."

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 410.2(10 Dec 46)CPC/GP, SCAPIN 1387, subject: "Gold, Silver and Platinum for Domestic Consumption in the Fourth Quarter of 1946", from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Requests contained in reference C.L.O. 1a, above, were considered, and allocations based thereon were included in the allocations of precious metals approved in reference Memorandum 1b, above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
