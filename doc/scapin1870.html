MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Fourth and Fifth Supplemental Requests for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in the First Quarter of 1948.

1, Reference is made to:

a. C.L.O. No. 9586(LO 4543 - FI/2E), 15 December 1947, subject, "Fourth Supplemental Request for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in the First Quarter of 1948."

b. C.L.O. No. 9587(LO 4549 - FI/FF), 15 December 1947, subject, "Fifth Supplemental Request for permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in the First Quarter of 1948."

2. Application for permission' to Use liquid gold, as requested by reference 1a above, is approved, as altered, in the amounts and by the companies listed below. Liquid gold stocks in the pooled reserve under custody of the Ministry of Finance will be drawn upon to supply the liquid gold authorized. Nippon Kineki K.K., may be used to process the liquid gold.
