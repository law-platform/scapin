MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1356)

ESS/IN JZR/GEM/ms

7 November 1946

1. Reference is C.L.O. letter No. 5590 (CM) dated 23 October 1946, subject: "Construction Plan of Fishing Vessels", with Inclosure 1, Steel Fishing Ships Construction Plan No. 4 (b) asking permission to construct 151 steel fishing vessels, aggregating 19,711 gross tons.

2. This construction plan will bring to date a total of 125, 073 gross tons approved for construction under the proposed fishing vessel construction program of 337,087 gross tons. At a conference between Natural Resources, Fisheries (Capt. Holland) and ESS/EN (Mr. Valentine), the fishing vessel construction program was discussed and favorable action on the construction Plan No. 4 (b) was recommended.

3. At a conference with ESS/Cartels (Mr. Foley) the matter of restricted concerns as owners of a group of the listed fishing vessels was discussed.

4. Action taken: It was decided to approve the application for construction of the 151 fishing vessels.

5. Concurrences: NRS/Fisheries (Capt. Holland).

6. Routine surveillance is necessary on this activity and will be reported in "Weekly Occupation Reports".

J. Z. R.
