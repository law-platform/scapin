GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.31 (23 Jan 46) ESS/IE

(SCAPIN-646)

APO 500

23 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Chinaware Dinner Sets for Export.

1. Reference is made to CLO no. 1224 (2. 2, 3) dated 20 December 1945, which requests the withholding of 7,000 chinaware dinner sets in Nagoya for export purpose.

2. The subject request is hereby denied.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
