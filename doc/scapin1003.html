7 June 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Action to be taken Concerning Personnel of KURASHIKI SHOKO GAKKO, Okayama Prefecture.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government, on receipt of this Memorandum, will investigate the following-named personnel of KURASHIKI SHOKO GAKKO, Okayama Prefecture, with reference to the activities herein described:

a. SASAKI, Kotoichi, Principal of Kurashiki Shoko Gakko, did knowingly secrete military training aids in the storehouse of a nearby Buddist temple, in direct violation of Ministry of Education Document BPE No. 100, dated 26 December 1945, subject: "Thorough Disposal of Weapons and Militaristic Arts used in Physical Education". Among the articles discovered were wooden training swords, complete kendo equipment, cartridge belts and cases, war maps and dummy grenades.

b. SASAKI, Kotoichi; ASHIDA, Norimichi; and KANAMITSU, Harumasa admitted cutting naginata swords in such a manner as to appear to comply with directives but still render the swords suitable for future military use. Such action was ordered by the office of the Prefectural Bureau of Education.

c. TAKEMASA, Satoru and AOKI, (fnu) were charged with the actual handling and disposition of above-listed material.

d. The actions of all personnel concerned were such as to obstruct the progress of an inspection party of CIC and MG personnel.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government will make a report to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, to include results of investigation and recommendation for disciplinary action to be taken in the case of each individual. Report will reach this headquarters not later than two (2) weeks from date of this memorandum.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government will also investigate and report the circumstances concerning the charge that the Prefectural Education Department ordered the alteration of military training aids and their retention by schools.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
