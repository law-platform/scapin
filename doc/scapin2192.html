SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.5 (8 Dec 51) LS

(SCAPIN - 2192)

APO 500

31 December 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Custody and Maintenance of War Criminals.

1. Reference is made to Article 11 of the Treaty of Peace with Japan signed at San Francisco on the 6th day of September, 1951.

2. The Japanese Government is directed to provide sufficient personnel to administer Sugamo Prison and the war criminals therein confined from and after 15 February 1952.

3. The Japanese Government is directed to provide all necessary housing, equipment, services and supplies for the operation of the said prison and the maintenance of the war criminals therein confined from and after the 31st day of March, 1952, or the date the Treaty comes into force as between the United States and Japan, whichever comes first.

4. The Supreme Commander retains the supervision of the said prison and of the war criminals therein confined until the date the Peace Treaty comes into force as between the United States and Japan.

5. The Supreme Commander retains the power to grant clemency, to reduce sentences, and to parole war criminals until his said power is superseded pursuant to the provisions of the said Treaty.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C.C.B. WARDEN
Colonel, AGC
Adjutant General
