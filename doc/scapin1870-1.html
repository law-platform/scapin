GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (13 May 48) CPC/CD

(SCAPIN - 1870/1)

APO 500

13 May 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Amendment to SCAPIN 1870, 23 March 1948

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (23 Mar 48)CPC/CD, SCAPIN 1870, 23 March 1948, subject, "Fourth and Fifth Supplemental Requests for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in the First Quarter of 1948." from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Ito Kinseisha is authorized to act as processor for the following manufacturers:

Nagoya Seito K.K.

Kanetora Seito Sho

Asaji Hayashi Oroshicho (Gifu Ken).

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
