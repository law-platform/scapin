GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.7 (4 Dec 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 390)

4 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Diesel Oil Allocation to Japanese Government - December.

1. Arrangements have been made for the sale to the Japanese Government through the Petroleum Distribution Control Company of Navy Special Fuel Oil in quantities and at points as shown below:

CG, SIXTH ARMY KILOKITERS

Sasebo 6,000

Nagoya 609

Kure 1,000

Wakayama 1,502

CG, EIGHTH ARMY

Hako Saki 2,000

during the month of December, 1945.

2. These sales plus estimated production from indigenous crude will make available 16,306 kiloliters for December consumption. It is directed that this be allocated approximately as follows with the figure for the fishing industry not to be reduced:

KILOLITERS

Fishing Industry 6, 683

Transportation, cargo ships 8, 428

Government Railways 120

Other Government uses 25

Mining and Manufacturing 1,000

Heating and Miscellaneous 50

3. It is further directed that on or before the 20th of January 1946, you will submit to this Headquarters a diesel oil report as follows:

a. Total stocks as of 30 November 1945.

b. Production during December 1945.

c. Deliveries accepted at each point against the releases herein authorized.

d. Deliveries to consumers against allocation prescribed above.

e. Stocks as of 31 December 1945.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
