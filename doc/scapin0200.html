GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 510 (27 Oct 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 200)

APO 500

27 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Movement of Certain Italian Diplomats to Fujiya Hotel, Miyanoshita.

1. This headquarters has directed the Commanding General, Eighth U. S. Army, to arrange for the movement of six members of the Italian Diplomatic Staff, including the Ambassador, from the ▲ara▲uchi Hotel Tokyo, to the Fujiya Hotel, Miyanoshita.

2. The personnel concerned will be quartered and subsisted as outlined in paragraph 4 of Memorandum from this headquarters to the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 091.112(21 Oct 45)GD.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
