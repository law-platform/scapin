GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG383.8 (24 Mar 46) ESS/PC

(SCAPIN-837)

APO 500

24 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Prohibition of Purchase from Allied Military Personnel.

1. Reference is made to C. L. O. Memorandum No. 425 (1.13), 28 Jan. 1946, and the proposed Ministrial Ordinance inclosed therewith concerning the prohibition of purchase of articles from Allied Occupational Forces Personnel.

2. There is no objection to the proposed ordinance but it is suggested that Articles I and II be reworded as follows:

Articles I: “No person or persons shall receive through purchase or exchange, supplies belonging to the United States or any Allied Government, or other articles or supplies intended for use by personnel of the Allied Occupation Forces in Japan which are issued or sold to authorized personnel by agencies of Allied Government or welfare agencies authorized, by such governments to provide supplies to Allied troops.”

Articles II: “No person or persons shall, knowing the supplies have been obtained in contravention of the regulations of the preceding article, sell or purchase, or through exchange, transfer or receive such supplies.”

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN-837)

WFM/ ARR/ sb

21 March 1946

1. The Ministerial Ordinance prohibits persons subject to Japanese law from purchasing or exchanging articles or goods with Allied personnel; the resale by Japanese of such articles is also forbidden. Maximum penalties for violations are three years in jail or a fine of ¥5,000.00

2. The ordinance is approved because it is a rough counterpart of GHQ, AFPAC, Circular Number 80, which forbids Allied Forces' personnel from selling, reselling, or giving away U.S. Government or PX supplies.

3. Concurrences from CIS, Provost Marshal, Government, ESS/ LE, ESS/ LA.

4. This is considered an administrative matter.

W. S. E.

CIS, Col. Creswell; PM, Brig. Gen. Ferrin; Government, Col. Robinson; ESS/ LE Major Abrams; ESS/ LA, Mr. Cohen.
