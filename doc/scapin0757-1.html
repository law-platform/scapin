AG 015 (19 Feb 46) LS

SCAPIN-757/1

APO 500

1 November 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Review of Sentences Imposed Upon Koreans and Certain Other Nationals

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 015(19 Feb 46)LS, subject as above, SCAPIN 757.

2. Paragraph 4 of reference memorandum is rescinded and the following substituted therefor:

"Direct communication between Legal Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and Japanese Governmental agencies concerned, is authorized on matters within the scope of this memorandum."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K, B. BUSH,
Brigadier General, AGD,
Adjutant General.
