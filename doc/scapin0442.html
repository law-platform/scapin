GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 314.4 (14 Dec 45) CIS

(SCAPIN - 442)

14 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Delivery of Documents.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will assemble and deliver to this headquarters before 12 o'clock noon of 19 December 1945 all documents relative the following incidents:

a. The Ketsumeidan Jike (1932)

b. The Shimpei Tai Jiken (1933)

c. The Nagata Jiken (1935)

d. The 2.26 Jiken (1936)

e. The 5.15 Jiken (1937)

f. The July 1940 plot to assassinate Premier Yonai and Minister of the Imperial Household Matsudaira

2. Documents will include records of police and other investigations as well as records of trials of people implicated in the incidents.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
