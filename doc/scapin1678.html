GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.7 (14 May 47) GD

(SCAPIN - 1678)

APO 500

14 May 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Importation of Petroleum Products for the Imperial Japanese Government, April through June 1947.

1. Reference:

a. Paragraph 4, Memorandum from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, to the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 463.7 (6 Mar 47) GD (SCAPIN 1560) dated 6 march 1947, same subject as above.

2. a. Arrangements have been completed for the sale of 208,000 barrels of imported Grade II Fuel Oil, to the Imperial Japanese Government for the exclusive use of the Japanese Iron and Steel Industry during the month of April, May, and June 1947.

b. The Boeki Cho (Board of Trade) shall be responsible that necessary instructions are promulgated to insure that:

(1) The fuel oil will be used as a supplement to coal allocations and deliveries to the iron and steel industry and not as a substitute therefore.

(2) The fuel oil will be used only for the production of critical steel materials, pipe and tubing, silicon steel, tin plate, heavy rail, sheet steel, wire and wire rod, and such other products ▲ may be determined by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

(3) Allocations of fuel oil to the ultimate consumer will be submitted to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for approval prior to delivery of product.

c. The Petroleum Distribution Company (or its legally appointed successor) as agent for the Boeki Cho (Board of Trade) shall be made responsible that distribution of the total amount authorized herein is in accordance with the instructions to be issued by the Boeki Cho (Board of Trade) as directed above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
