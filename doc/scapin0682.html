GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 201-TAKEGUCHI, K. (31 Jan 46) CIS

(SCAPIN - 682)

APO 500

31 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Removal from Office of TAKEUCHI, Kisaburo.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will remove from office TAKEUCHI, Kisaburo, Chief of the Personnel Affairs Section and Chief of the Food Supply Section of the Hiroshima Prefectural Government; and shall exclude him from further government service as that term is defined in Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government dated 4 January 1946, file number AG 091.1 (4 Jan 46) GS, subject: "Removal and Exclusion of Undesirable Personnel from Public Office".

2. A report in English will be submitted to this headquarters when the removal of TAKEUCHI has been accomplished,

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
