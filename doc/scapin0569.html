GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 248 (9 Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-569)

APO 500

9 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Payment of Additional Compensation to Employees of Institutions Closed Pursuant to Memorandum of this Headquarters, AG 091. 3 (30 Sep 45) ESS.

1. It is directed that the Imperial Japanese Government instruct the Ministry of Finance to cause the following payments to be made.

a. To all working employees of the subject institutions in Japan whose employment was continuous between 1 July and 30 September 1945, a payment will be made which is equal to 2 1/4 times basic monthly salary in the position held at 30 September 1945. If employee worked less than the entire period a proportionate payment for the time actually worked will be made.

b. To all such employees who performed work for the institutions subsequent to 30 September 1945 in connection with examination and liquidation, an additional amount will be paid equal to 75% of base per diem salary for each day worked.

2. From the total amount of payments authorized under paragraph 1 (a) and (b) above, the following deductions will be made:

a. The total amount of any bonus payments already made on account of bonus earned in the July-December period, including allowance for hish commodity prices and similar allowances, either by the institution or by other persons or organizations on behalf of the institution.

b. The Total amount of all unsecured debts to the institution.

3. Payments will be made with the maximum accuracy obtainable without access to the records of the subject institutions now under seal, and amounts paid will be subject to later adjustment upon the examination of the accounts of the institutions.

4. No payments will be made under this authorization to any individual removed from office under the privisions of Memorandum of this Headquarters, AG 091. 3 (30 Sept 45) ESS.

5. No payments will be made under this authorization to any individual or the dependents, heirs or assignees or any individual who was not a working employee of the offices in Japan of the subject institutions during the July-December 1945 period.

6. As soon as possible after completion of payments authorized herein, a complete report on such payments will be submitted to this Headquarters.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 569)

WFM/THH/smh

8 Jan 1946

1. The memorandum to the Japanese Government authorizes and directs the payment of additional compensation, in lieu of bonus, to employees of institutions closed pursuant to AG 091.3 (30 Sep 45) ESS.

2. An investigation of compensation methods in Japan discloses:

a. The payment of semi-annual bonuses is an established Japanese business practice, and the bonus has come to be a part of regular compensation.

b. Basic wages and salaries have been kept at an extremely low figure because of the bonus practice. Undue hardship has been relieved by the bonus payments.

c. It has become an accepted practice in Japan to borrow money for living expenses in anticipation of these bonus payments.

d. Bonuses and allowances have become increasingly important with the inflationary increase in the cost of living.

e. In recent years a variation of the bonus system has developed with a monthly payment called "bonus for high commodity prices." The total of these monthly payments are deductible from the regular bonus when it becomes payable.

f. In addition, Japanese employers pay various minor allowances such as: family allowances, allowances for overtime, or expenses incurred in performing overtime or other extra duties.

g. Bonus payments are governed by Imperial Ordinance. The main provisions are as follows:

(1) An annual bonus distributed among officers and employees may be not more than 9 times the total basic monthly salaries of the institution.

(2) An additional bonus equal to 6 times monthly salaries may be distributed provided it can be paid out of earnings and not assets, and is paid in government bonds, or subject to other government saving devices.

h. These authorized funds are distributed among the officers and employees in amounts determined by the management. That is to say, officers and employees do not necessarily share equally in amount or percentage-wise.

3. Minor employees of closed institutions will suffer considerably unless the bonus usually paid in December is paid. This payment will assist in retaining the services of employees for examination and liquidation of (sentence was not finished)

4. While institutions were closed 30 September 1945, the employees, other than directors and higher officials were not discharged because it was anticipated that the services of some of them would be required for investigation and liquidation of the institutions. After selection of those to be held over for the above purpose the rest will be discharged as soon as possible after 31 December 1945.

5. No concurrences necessary.

6. AG 091.3 directs the closing of Colonial and Foreign Banks and Special Wartime Institutions.
