GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (28 Jun 48) CPC/CD

(SCAPIN-1914)

APO 500

28 June 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Responsibility of Japanese Government for Precious Metal Procedures.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 130. (22 Sep 45) ESS, SCAPIN 45, 22 September 1945, subject, “Control of Financial Transactions,” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 091.31 (14 Mar 45) ESS/IN, SCAPIN 814, 14 March 1946, subject, “Export Procedure,” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

c. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (25 Aug 47) CPC/GP, SCAPIN 1767, 25 August 1947, subject, “Procedure for Submitting Requests for Release of Precious Metals,” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

d. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (1 Mar 47) CPC/GP, SCAPIN 3312-A, 1 March 1947, subject, “Processing of Precious Metals,” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Reference 1c above is hereby rescinded and the procedures detailed herein will be used. These procedures are designed to implement the policy of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

3. Effective upon receipt of this memorandum, the following procedures will govern the release of precious metals for domestic use and consumption:

a. The Japanese Government will consolidate precious metals requirements for domestic use and consumption for each three-month period into a paper to be called the “Quarterly Allocation of Precious Metals.” This paper will be submitted to General Headquarter, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Civil Property Custodian, at least two months prior to the first day of the three-month period during which released metals are required;

b. No other requests for the release of precious metals for domestic use and consumption will be submitted except emergency requests, which may be submitted only after informal approval by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. The responsible Ministry of the Japanese Government will obtain this informal approval. After such approval, the request will be submitted to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Civil Property Custodian;

c. The “Quarterly Allocation of Precious Metals” will be presented in such form as may be prescribed by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. Except where companies or juridical persons have firm names properly registered in English or other languages using the Roman lettering, names of all juridical persons will be given in Romaji-Hepburn. Requests not properly submitted will not be favorably considered;

d. In the submission of requests for approval for the release of precious metals, and in the use and consumption of precious metals authorized, each Ministry of the Japanese Government, having jurisdiction or authority will be responsible for:

(1) Reviewing, screening and giving initial approval only to proper requests falling within their jurisdiction;

(2) Determining that the end use for which the metals are requested is proper and that only required amounts are given initial approval;

(3) Disapproving all requests which do not conform to policies established by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

(4) Disapproving all requests which are not of a nature essential to the national requirements of the Japanese people;

(5) Controlling the proper use of released precious metals.

e. Metals released for domestic use and consumption will be fabricated, or processed for use or consumed in producing the articles, equipment or supplies, during the authorized three-month period, except as itemized below. The extended use herein authorized does not diminish the responsibility of the Japanese Government detailed above. Responsible Ministries of the Japanese Government will continue to exercise supervision and control over released metals so that they are not misused, wastefully used or diverted to unapproved use:

(1) Where the recipients of precious metals are municipalities or institutions dedicated to religion, charity and education, the arts and sciences; or where released metals are to be used in producing articles or commercial forms for such institutions or municipalities; the period of use will, without further approval from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, be one of sufficient duration to enable the accomplishment of the objective for which the release was approved. In the case of educational institutions, this use may extend for a school year or more;

(2) Where precious metals are released for public health purposes, the forms and alloys, prepared for medical and dental uses and forms and alloys in distribution channels or in rationed stocks supervised or controlled by the Ministry of Welfare, may be used or consumed without further approval from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

(3) Metal processors, fabricators and other juridical persons who have received allocated metals may, without further approval from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, hold or use these metals to:

(a) Enable the most economical fabrication and processing of precious metals,

(b) Make repairs requiring the use or consumption of precious metals;

(c) Remodel commercial and manufacturing equipment or articles;

(d) Manufacture samples for export or display in sales exhibits in Japan or abroad.

(4) Where, through no fault of the Japanese Government, released precious metals are not received by the actual consumer or processors until after the beginning of the quarter during which their use and consumption is authorized, the Japanese Government without further approval from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, may license the use of such metals for periods of time which will enable proper fabrication or use and consumption. This delayed use of precious metals will be reflected in reducing subsequent requests.

4. Effective upon the receipt of this memorandum, the following procedures will govern where precious metals are required in the production of items for export, and when items are produced in fulfilling contracts with the Central Purchasing Office, Far East Command;

a. A copy of inclosure number 1 will be properly filled out by each manufacturer. This inclosure will be attached to each copy of the application to prepare for export (Specimen Form IE 100). At the time form IE 100, with inclosures, is submitted to the Economic and Scientific Section, Foreign Trade Division for validation, the forms will have attached or stamped thereon, a certificate, statement or stamp which shows that the Ministry of Finance, Japanese Government approves the use of the precious metals required in manufacturing the products itemized therein, and that the approval of General Headquarters, Supreme Commamder for the Allied Powers for the use of proper amounts of precious metals is requested;

b. Commercial from the pool of precious metals, established by authority of reference 1d above, will be released for use as authorized by paragraph 4a above except that specific requests for use of these forms may be included in the Quarterly Allocation where metals are required for domestic use and consumption.

5. Where the Japanese Government is directed to deposit precious metals into the custody of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, such deposits shall be made in the forms of bars of mint or near-mint fineness and in cases where deposits total an amount which cannot be filled by the use of bars of standard size, one small or finger bar will be added to make up the total required. Each bar and finger bar shall be accompanied by four (4) copies of “Precious Metals Weight and Fineness Certificate,” inclosure 2, written on paper eight (8) inches by eleven (11) inches in both Japanese and English and properly completed. No mutilated bars will be deposited. All bars will be marked with standard markings as generally used by the refinery or mint making the casting. These standard markings will in all cases, include trade-mark, fineness and weight.

6. General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers is not concerned with the repairing, remodeling, or remaking of purely private personal property composed of or containing precious metals, where such private property is owned by juridical persons not otherwise restricted, unless such actions taken with regard to this private property will:

a. Consume precious metals previously licensed for use or consumption by General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers;

b. Require the future release of precious metals to replenish articles or stocks destroyed or consumed by such actions.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
