GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 260 (17 Jun 49) ESS/FIN

(SCAPIN - 2018)

APO 500

17 June 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Cancellation of Reports

1. Reference the following memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. AG 260 (19 Mar 46) ESS/FI, SCAPIN 827, 19 March 1946, subject: Pensions and Benefits.

b. AG 260 (5 Nov 46) ESS/FI, SCAPIN 1312, 5 November 1946, subject: Pensions and Benefits.

2. Monthly reports specified in paragraph 6 reference 1a regarding action taken and progress made during the preceding month and reports required by paragraph 3 reference 1b relating to payments made under the provisions of said memorandum are no longer required.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ Clark Marshall
for /t/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 2018)

WKL/EMR/TAM/mm

11 June 1949

1. The responsibility of the Japanese Government under reference memorandum 1a in investigating and clarifying the status of pensions and living aid allowance certificates marked "civil service" has been completed and no further reports are necessary.

2. Reference memorandum 1b and memorandum, file 260, (1 Nov 48) ESS/FIN, subject: Pensions and Benefits, determined the pattern under which pensions and retirement allowances have been made. The progress and extent of conformance to these memoranda have been verfied through required periodic reports on payments forwarded to this headquarters. Detailed surveillance, as reflected in these reports which were essential during the initial stages of development, is no longer necessary. It is recommended, therefore, that reports of payments made as required under paragraph 3 reference 1b be rescinded.

3. No concurrence necessary.

Mr. Miyakawa, 57-8808

W. K. L.
