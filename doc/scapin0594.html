GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123 (14 Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN - 594)

APO 500

14 January 1946

SUBJECT :Approval for Manufacture of New Fractional Coins and Currency
TO :Ministry of Finance, Hypothec Bank Building, Tokyo

1. Reference is made to memorandum from Ministry of Finance, LO 104, dated 20 December 1945, which requests permission to mint new ten sen and five sen coins and to print new fifty sen notes.

2. Approval is granted for the printing of additional fifty sen notes of a new design, and for the minting of subsidiary coins according to the schedule attached to memorandum LO 104.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government will take all necessary security measures in the execution of the program approved herein and will report the steps taken to this Headquarters on or before 1 February 1946.

4. Reports on the progress of this program will be submitted to this Headquarters on the first and fifteenth of each month.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ W. F. Marquat
/t/ W. F. MARQUAT
Maj. General, U. S. Army
Chief, Econ. & Sc. Sect.
