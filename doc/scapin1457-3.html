GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 560 (2 Oct 48) CPC/LP

(SCAPIN - 1457/3)

APO 500

2 October 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Sagami Maru ex British Cargo Vessel SS "Josephine Moller,".

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum to the Japanese Government, file AG 560 (10 Jan 47) CPC/FP, 10 January 1947 3CAPIN 1457, subject, "SS 'Sagami Maru,' ex British Cargo Vessel, SS 'Josephine Moller,'" from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

b. Memorandum to the Japanese Government, file AG 560 (13 Jan 48) CPC/FP, 13 January 1943 SCAPIN 1457/1, subject, same as reference 1a above, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

c. Memorandum to the Japanese Government, file AG 560 (10 Jan 47) CPC/LP, 29 July 1948 SCAPIN 1457/2, subject, same as reference 1a above, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The "Sagami Maru" is scheduled to arrive at Yokohama from Nagasaki on or about 5 October 1943. The Japanese Government is directed to release the ship on 15 October 1948, from its present service as a cable layer under the supervision of the Ministry of Communications, and to have it moored at Yokohama in the protective custody of the General Maritime Bureau, pending further instructions aa to its disposition from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers. The vessel is to be preserved intact as a cable layer, protected from theft and damage, and maintained in a seaworthy condition.

3. It is further directed that upon arrival of the vessel at Yokohama, only the personal possessions of the crew, food supplies, submarine cables, and certain other used cables, reels and drums transported as cargo on the voyage from Nagasaki to Yokohama are to be removed.

4. It is understood that the Ministry of Communications also desires to remove from the vessel certain technical devices and instilments used in connection with cable laying operations, which are not an integral part of the vessel. A list of such items may be submitted for consideration, but are not to be removed without the specific written authority of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
