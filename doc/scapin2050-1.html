AG 333 (22 Jun 46) NR

(SCAPIN-2050/1)

APO 500

31 January 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Japanese Fishery Inspection System

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 333 (22 Jun 46) NR, SCAPIN 2050, subject: Japanese Fishery Inspection System, 10 October 1949.

2. Reference memorandum is amended to delete paragraph 3h and the following new paragraph 3h is substituted:

“3h. Duly appointed officials of the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry who are assigned to the Fishery Inspection Syetem are authorized to exercise the functions of judicial police, as provided in Article 74 of the Fisheries Law, No 267 of 1949, in enforcing the provisions of Memorandum for the Japanese Government SCAPIN 2046.”

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
