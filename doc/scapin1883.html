AG 410.2 (22 Apr 48) CPC/CD

(SCAPIN - 1883)

APO 500

22 April 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Release of Precious Metal Commercial Forms from the Pool under Custody of the Ministry of Finance, Japanese Government; Released Stocks to be Used in Items Manufactured and Processed for Export.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. C.L.O. No. 165(LO 4871-FI/2E), 12 January 1948, subject, "Tenth Supplemental Request for Approval of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the First Quarter of 1948;"

b. C.L.C.O. No. 165(LO 448), 10 February 1948, subject, "Sixth Supplemental Request for Permission of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the Second Quarter of 1948;"

c. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (22 Nov 47)CPC/GP, SCAPIN 1825, 22 November 1947, subject, "Application for Use of Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption during the Period from Fourth Quarter of'1947 through Third Quarter of 1948," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Application for permission to use liquid gold, as requested by reference 1a above, is approved, as altered, in the amounts and by the companies listed below. Liquid gold stocks from the pooled reserve under custody of the Ministry of Finance, Japanese Government, and released by reference 1c above, or by similar allocation will be drawn upon to supply the liquid gold authorized.

MANUFACTURER GRAMS GOLD BULLION CONTAINED IN LIQUID GRAMS LIQUID GOLD PERCENT TO BE EXPORTED EXPORT AUTHORITY ORDER NO.

Nippon Toki K.K. 8571 54093 100% EX 3550,3680,3686,3763,3764,3765,3766,3688,3767

MANUFACTURER GRAMS GOLD BULLION CONTAINED IN LIQUID GRAMS LIQUID GOLD PERCENT TO BE EXPORTED EXPORT AUTHORITY ORDER NO.

Yamaguchi Toki K.K. 181 1650 100% EX 3243,3691

Fuso Kinzoku Narumi Seitosho 47 430 100% EX 3552

Yamaka Toki Gomel Kaisha 62 560 100% EX 3580

Hibino Shinhichi Shoten 29 265 100% EX 3393

3. Application for permission to use silver foil, as requested by reference 1b above, is approved in the amount and by the company listed below. Silver foil stocks from the pooled reserve under custody of the Ministry of Finance, Japanese Government, and released by reference 1c above, or by similar allocations will be drawn upon to supply the silver foil authorized:

MANUFACTURER GRAMS SILVER BULLITON CONTAINED IN FOIL SHEETS SILVER FOIL PERCENT TO BE EXPORTED EXPORT AUTHORITY ORDER NO.

Hara Gomel Kaisha 45,454 1,000,000 100% EX 5583

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
