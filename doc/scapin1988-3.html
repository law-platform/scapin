GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.31 (1 Apr 49) ESS/FIN

(SCAPIN - 1988/3)

APO 500

12 September 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Dried Skim Milk for School Lunch and Nursery Feeding Programs

1. Reference the following Memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. AG 091.31 (1 Apr 49) ESS/EX, SCAPIN 1988, 1 April 1949, subject: Yen Counterpart of GARIOA and EROA Imports.

b. AG 091.31 (1 Apr 49) ESS/PF, SCAPIN 1988/1, 8 May 1950, subject: Dried Skim Milk for School Lunch Program.

c. AG 091.31 (1 Apr 49) ESS/PF, 9 June 1950, SCAPIN 1988/2, subject: Dried Skim Milk for Nursery Feeding Program.

2. References 1 b. and 1 c. above are hereby amended as follows:

a. The rights and obligations relative to the purchase of dried skim milk which were performed by the Foreign Trade Special Account in Japanese Fiscal Year 1950 shall be the responsibility of the Food Management Special Account and payments made relative to this program shall be made from appropriations to the latter account.

b. Total funds to be made available to the Food Management Special Account from the Counterpart Fund shall not exceed five hundred twenty-seven million yen (¥527,000,000) during Japanese Fiscal Year 1951, including the present unused balance for skim milk of five hundred four million, five hundred twenty-seven thousand, eight hundred five yen (¥504,527,805) in the Counterpart Fund.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ C. C. B. Warden
/t/ C. C. B. WARDEN,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General
