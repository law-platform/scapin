GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 463.7 (24 Mar 46) GD

(SCAPIN - 838)

APO 500

24 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Petroleum Importation for Japanese Government, March 1946.

1. Arrangements have been made for the sale from military stocks at Yokohama to the Japanese Government, through the Petroleum Distribution Control Company, of approximately 2900 drums (53 U.S. gallons each) lubricating oil marked N.S. 5190.

2. Price ex military storage Yokohama is U.S. ＄0.86 per U.S. gallon.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General.

Related documentOriginal →

MEMORANDUM FOR: The Record

SUBJECT: Information of General Application Pertaining to Directive Number (SCAPIN‐ 838), file AG 463.7 ( 24 Mar 46) GD, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, dated 24 March 1946, subject: "Petroleum Importation for Japanese Government, March 1946".

1. With reference to Memorandum, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, to the Imperial Japanese Government, Number (SCAPIN - 838), the following is published for the information of all concerned.

2. Surveillance required is indicated in letter, General Headquarters, United States Army Forces, Pacific, to Commanding General, Eighth Army, file AG 463,7 (24 Mar 46) GD, dated 24 March 1946, subject: "Sale of Petroleum to Japanese Government".

3. Area Petroleum Officer, G-4, General Headquarters, United States Army Forces, Pacific, will exercise necessary surveillance to obtain quantitative receipts from Eighth Army and pass same to Economic and Scientific Section, Import and Export Division.

4. Economic and Scientific Section, Import and Export Division, will maintain accounts and notify Japanese Government amount of obligation and method of payment.

HAROLD FAIR,

Lt Colonel, AGD,

Asst Adjutant General

DISTRIBUTION:

Same as SCAPIN - 838 except Imperial Japanese Government.
