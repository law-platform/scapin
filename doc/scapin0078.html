IN THE CLEAR
OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS
SIGNAL CORPS MESSAGE

(SCAPIN -78)

GC-O TWS/gr 1 October 1945

FROM :SCAP
TO :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT......OPNL PRIORITY

IT IS DESIRED THAT YOU FURNISH THIS OFFICE WITH LEAST PRACTICABLE DELAY A REPORT FOR PERIOD TWO SEPTEMBER TO TREE NAUGHT SEPTEMBER ONE NINE FOUR FIVE INCLUSIVE SHOWING NUMBER OF REPATRIATED JAPANESE PAREN LISTING NUMBER OF MILITARY CMA NAVAL AND CIVILIAN PERSONNEL SEPARATELY PAREN RECEIVED IN JAPAN FROM EACH REPEAT EACH PORT OF ORIGIN PD(ZA 6361) A REPORT CONTAINING THE INFORMATION CALLED FOR ABOVE WILL BE SUBMITTED EACH WEDNESDAY CMA BEGINNING ONE NAUGHT OCTOBER COVERING PROGRESS OF REPATRIATION DURING PRECEDING WEEK PAREN MONDAY TO SUNDAY INCLUSIVE PAREN

OFFICIAL:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, US Army,
Adjutant General

APPROVED:

/s/W. E. Chambers
/t/W. E. CHAMBERS
Brigadier General, G. S. C.
Acting Asst. Chief of Staff, G-3
