GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 400 (12 Oct 45) ESS

(SCAPIN - 128)

12 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :C. L. O. No. 167 - 4 October 1945 - Request to Convert Military Stores for Civilian Purposes

1. With reference to the subject letter, use of the materials specified is authorized as shown below.

2. Stocks of caustic soda may be used according to the plan submitted, with priority being given to the production of ammonium sulphate.

3. a. Special steel may be used as shown in the plan submitted except for the item "Provisions", and subject to these conditions:

(1) Repair of ships should receive the highest priority.

(2) In repair of automobiles, truck repairs should receive a higher priority than passenger car repairs.

b. The quantity of steel set aside for "Provisions" will not be released until details of the intended use of the material are supplied to this headquarters.

4. Stocks of lapping cotton may be used as shown in the plan. In this connection, your attention is directed to paragraph 3a of Directive No. 3, this headquarters, which authorizes the Imperial Japanese Government to stimulate immediate maximum production of all essential consumers commodities.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General
