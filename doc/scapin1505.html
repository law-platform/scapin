GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.7 (4 Feb 47) CPC/GP

(SCAPIN - 1505)

APO 500

4 February 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Property of Designated Individuals.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a, C.L.O. No. 5552(RP), 21 October 1946, subject: Applications for Release of Specified Property.

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 386.7(23 Apr 46)CPC, SCAPIN 897 , 23 April 1946, subject: Control, Impounding and Blocking of Property of Designated Individuals, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Applications of the following named persons for release of specified property is denied:

Koto Hashimoto;

Fumiko Matsui;

Katsuyo Ohara;

Tomi Furuyama.

3. Applications of the following named parsons for release of specified property is approved, and the property of these individuals is henceforth released from the provisions of memorandum 1b, above:

Masao Kadowaki;

Masao Fudo.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
