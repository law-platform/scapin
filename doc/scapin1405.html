GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 444. 4 (14 Dec 46) ESS/AC

(SCAPIN - 1405)

APO 500

14 December 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Abandonment of Control over Distribution of Dental Materials and Dissolution of the Dental Materials Control Company

1. References are:

a. Central Liaison Office Memorandum No. 4577 (PK), dated 9 September 1946, subject: Abandonment of Control over Distribution of Dental Materials.

b. Memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government AG 091. 33 (18 Jan 46) ESS/FI, dated 18 January 1946, (SCAPIN 616), subject: Release of Gold, Silver and Platinum for Dental Work.

c. Memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government AG 130 (26 Dec 45) ESS/PC, dated 26 December 1945, (SCAPIN 502), subject: Abandonment of Price and Distribution Controls over Necessary Articles of Life.

2. No objection is offered to the abandonment of control over the distribution of dental materials or to the dissolution of the Dental Materials Control Company provided:

a. That provision for the allocation and distribution of gold, silver and platinum used for dental purposes be made in a manner consistent with the policy stated in the directive referred to in paragraph 1b above.

b. That in the dissolution of the Dental Materials Control Company provision be made for liquidation in conformity with existing price control and currency regulations and for distribution of the stockpiles of its materials so as to make them fairly available to the public and to prevent monopoly.

c. That no other control or control agency be established in this field without prior approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

3. Reports on the action taken will be submitted to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers in quintuplicate typed in English on 8" x 11” paper. These reports shall include the following:

a. Inventory stocks of dental materials on hand as of 1 November 1946 to be submitted thirty days from date of this Memorandum.

b. On 1 January 1947 and every sixty days thereafter, until the conclusion of the liquidation, a report on the progress of the liquidation showing what materials have been disposed of, to whom, and at what prices sold.

c. Final report on the liquidation to be submitted within thirty (30) days after liquidation is completed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
for /t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1405)

WFM/VHK/GFS/lf

25 November 1946

1. References are:

a. CLO No. 4577 (PK), 9 September 1946, subject: "Abandonment of Control Over the Distribution of Dental Materials. "

b. Memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government AG 091. 33 (18 Jan 46) ESS/FI, (SCAPIN 616), subject: "Release of Gold, Silver and Platinum for Dental Work. "

c. Memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government AG 130 (26 Dec 46) ESS/PC, subject: "Abandonment of Price and Distribution Controls over the Necessaries of Life. "

2. This action is based on JCS 1380/15, 5 November 1945, and involves a private control organization.

3. The Dental Materials Control Company was established under the control company ordinance to be the exclusive agent for the purchase and sale of one hundred twenty dental materials.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government has requested permission to abandon the control over the distribution and to dissolve the Dental Materials Control Company on the grounds that they are unnecessary and hamper and restrict the distribution of dental materials.

5. Control over gold, silver and platinum must be maintained in accordance with SCAP monetary policies.

6. This is an administrative matter and no surveillance by the Eighth Army is required.

7. The interests of USAFIK are not concerned.

8. Concurrences: ESS/LE (Mr. H. T. Straitiff); ESS/PC (Mr. W. S. Egekevist); ESS/RS (Mr. E. Ross) GS (Brig. Gen. C. Whitney); PH&W (Lt. Col. B. M. Riordan).
