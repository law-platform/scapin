GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.23 (17 Apr 48) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1759/5)

APO 500

17 April 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service via the United States.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.23 (2 Apr 48)CCS, SCAPIN 1759/4, dated 2 April 1948, subject as above.

2. So much of inclosure 1, SCAPIN 1759/4 as pertains to word charges and division of revenue for full rate radiotelegrams destined for Belgian Congo, Iraq, and Persian Gulf areas are hereby rescinded, and the following substituted in lieu thereof:

Thru Kate Japan Terminal Outpayment beyond SF Radio Tolls American Carrier Share Japanese Share Total Credit American Carrier

$ $ $ $ $ $ $

Belgian Congo 1.21 0.0675 0.64 0.5025 0.25125 0.25125 0.89125

Iraq 1.32 0.0675 0.75 0.5025 0.25125 0.25125 0.90125

Persian Gulf: Bahrain & Muscat 1.30 0.0675 0.73 0.5025 0.25125 0.25125 0.98125

Dubai & Shagrah 1.42 0.0675 0.85 0.5025 0.25125 0.25125 0.10125

3. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Communications, regarding operating and accounting procedures within the scope of this memorandum, is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
