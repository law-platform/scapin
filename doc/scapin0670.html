29 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Application of the Sumitomo Honsha to Transfer Properties to Subsidiaries.

1. Reference is made to Central Liaison Office Memorandum No. 38, dated 6 December 1945, subject: "Dissolution of Holding Companies,” which forwards application of the Sumitomo Honsha, Ltd. for permission to make the following transfers:

a. Mining, forestry and agricultural properties having a net book value of ¥77,140,836 to the Sumitomo Mining Industries Company.

b. Buildings and other immovable properties having a net book value of Y11,416,094 to the Sumitomo Real Estate & Building Company, Ltd.

2. The above application is denied.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
