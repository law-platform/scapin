GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 400.172 (11 Oct 47) GPA

(SCAPIN-1798)

APO 500

11 October 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Allocation of Materials to Meet Procurement Demands.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 400.12 (27 Aug 46) GD, (SCAPIN 1160), subject: “Procurement Demand Procedure,” 27 August 1946.

2. The Japanese Government will take necessary action to insure that in complying with procurement demand procedure as defined in reference cited in paragraph 1:

a. Materials allocated to fulfill Occupation Force procurement demands, are not in excess of quantities required to meet the specifications of those procurement demands.

b. United Nations patented items being manufactured for the Occupation Forces are not being manufactured in excess of the quantities specified in the procurement demands.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
