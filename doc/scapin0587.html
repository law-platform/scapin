GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG248 (12Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-587)

APO 500

12 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Retirement Allowances to Civilian Employees in Military Establishments.

1. Reference is made to memorandum 28 December 1945 from the Central Liaison Office (C. L. O. 1419 - 2. 1) and inclosed memorandum from the Ministry of Finance dated 28 December 1945 (LO 124), subject: “Retirement Allowances to Civilian Employees in Military Factories.”

2. Approval is granted for the release from fixed deposit accounts, of retirement allowances that were received on discharge by civilian workers and laborers in Army or Navy factories or military establishments. Release is not authorized hereunder to officers, enlisted men or civilian officials who worked in military factories or establishments.

3. Copies of any instructions transmitted to financial institutions pursnant to this memorandum shall be sent to this Headquarters for purposes of record.

4. Acknowledgment of receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
