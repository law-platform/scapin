IN THE CLEAR
OFFICE OF SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS
SIGNAL CORPS MESSAGE

(SCAPIN-36)

G-C-O WHR/rs 19 September 1945

FROM :SCAP
TO :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE WAR MINISTRY
INFO :CINCPAC PEARL
COMFEN SIXTH ARMY
COM FIFTH FLEET
CONCAFPAC MANILA
IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.

CITE MY RADIO ZAX FIVE THREE THREE FOUR OF

YOU INFORM COMMANDER JAPANESE SECOND GENERAL ARMY THAT UNITED STATES NAVAL FORCES WILL OCCUPY KURE AT SUCH TIME AS MINE HAZARD HAS BEEN REMOVED.

OFFICIAL:

B. M. FITCH,
BRIGADIER General, US Army,
Adjutant General.

APPROVED:

/s/S. J. Chamberlin
/T/S. J. CHAMBERLIN
Major General, G.S. C.,
Asst Chief of Staff, G-3
