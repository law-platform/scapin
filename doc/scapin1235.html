GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 451 (27 Sep 46) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1235)

APO 500

27 September 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Loan of Vehicles and Trailers for Use in Connection with Maintenance of Occupation Forces Communications Service.

1. Arrangements have been completed for the loan of a limited number of vehicles and trailers for the use of the Japanese Ministry of Communications in connection with the installation of communications facilities required by the Occupation Force and in the maintenance of services now being rendered.

2. Appendix I lists vehicles and trailers which have been approved for loan to the Ministry of Communications, the point to which they are to be delivered, and the Occupation Force unit responsible for their delivery. Partial deliveries can be made at some points at the present time and at the remaining points in the near future.

3. It is directed that a responsible representative of each Operating Bureau Headquarters named in Appendix I be appointed to act for the Ministry of Communications in accepting the subject equipment. Upon appointment, the names, positions and addresses of these representatives will be furnished direct to Signal Office, Eighth Army, through the liaison engineer assigned to that office. A copy of the names of these representatives will also be furnished to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

4. Each representative named in accordance with paragraph 3, will, upon notification of his appointment, report to the Signal Corps unit designated in Appendix I as responsible for issuance of the subject vehicles for instructions as to the date and conditions under which the issue is to be made. These Signal Corpsunits will provide assistance necessary for the training of drivers, the issuance of drivers' permits and will exercise general technical supervision over the use and maintenance of the equipment.

5. If it is desired to ship equipment thus furnished to points other than those to which issued, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers shall be so advised and such arrangements as are necessary will be made.

6. Requests for replacement and repair parts required in connections with the operation of the subject equipment shall be made upon the Signal Corps unit responsible for the issuance and technical supervision of the equipment.

7. Direct correspondence between Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Communications, regarding technical matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
