GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 370.05 (23 Jan 46) GC

(SCAPIN - 648)

23 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Japanese in the Whampoa Concentration Camps, Canton, China.

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. No. 858 (5.1), subject: "Repatriation Vessels for Japanese Residents in Canton", dated 4 December 1945.

2. The request in reference C.L.O. is disapproved.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
