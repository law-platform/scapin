GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 628 (31 Dec 47) ESS/EX

(SCAPIN-1839)

APO 500

31 December 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Accommodations for Semi-Permanent Commercial Representatives and Licensees.

1. The Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers has authorized the entrance into Japan of commercial representatives and has licensed certain firms and individuals to do business in Japan. The Japanese Government is now operating hotels in Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto and Nagoya; however, it will be necessary in the future to provide offices and housing for authorized individuals on a semi-permanent basis.

2. The Japanese Government will designate an agency to assist the individuals described in paragraph 1 above to arrange for necessary accommodations, rehabilitation and upkeep. In cases where the individuals desire to make payment in acceptable foreign exchange, this agency will arrange to reimburse the owners in Japanese yen and to accept foreign exchange from the lessor. Such arrangements will be subject to approval of the Foreign Trade Division of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, Economic and Scientific Section. Funds collected will be deposited in accordance with instructions governing disposition of proceeds of international trade.

3. From time to time the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers will return to the Japanese Government facilities which have been procured for the Occupation Forces. The Japanese Government may make arrangements to utilize such property for rental to commercial representatives and authorized licensees. In such cases, payment of rental will be made in acceptable foreign exchange as indicated in paragraph 2 above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1839)

1. Arrangements are being made to terminate Army logistic support for non-occupational personnel. Commissary privileges will be withdrawn when the facilities of the Overseas Service Store are available. Housing and office space will be withdrawn as other accommodations become available.

2. In order that accommodations may be made available as soon as practicable this memorandum for the Japanese Government directs that an agency be designated to assist authorized individuals in arranging for necessary accommodations, to accept payments therefor in acceptable foreign exchange when so desired by the individual, and to utilize property returned by the Occupation Forces to the JG for rental to commercial representatives and authorized licensees.

3. Concurrences: G-l, G-4 (by C/N).

M. H. H.
