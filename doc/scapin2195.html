GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 386.7 (25 Jan 52) CPC/OD

(SCAPIN - 2195)

APO 500

25 January 1952

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
SUBJECT :Property in Japan Formerly Owned by Certain Persons of German Nationality

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 091.112 (13 Sep 45) MG, SCAPIN 26, 13 September 1945, subject, “Protection of Allied and Axis Property,” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 386.7 (13 Oct 49) CPC/PLD, SCAPIN 2051, 13 October 1949, subject, “Notification that the United States, United Kingdom and France are Owners of Certain Categories of Former German Property in Japan, from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, as amended

c. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 072 (15 Oct 51) CPC/OD, SCAPIN 2176, 15 October 1951, subject, “Final Disposition of Formerly German-Owned Patents, Utility Models, Designs, and Pending Applications Therefor,” from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Effective 1 March 1952 and up to the coming into force of the Peace Treaty, the Japanese Government will assume all the functions of

a. Discovering, preserving and protecting all German assets in Japan coming within the meaning of 1b above.

b. Releasing such property as may be designated from time to time as not coming within the meaning of reference 1a and 1b above

c. Administering and conserving such assets pending their liquidation.

d. Liquidating such assets in accordance with instructions to be issued by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

e. Turning over the proceeds of such liquidation to a depository designated by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers as Trustee for France, the United Kingdom and the United States.

3. In this connection, the Japanese Government will exercise the same responsibility as has been assumed by the Japanese Government under the provisions of Article 20 of the Peace Treaty except that the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers will retain the trusteeship over such assets until the coming into force of the Peace Treaty.

4. In order to transfer effectively the functions as mentioned in paragraph 2 above, the Japanese Government will be required to furnish:

a. An adequate staff, including but not limited to stenographic and clerical personnel

b. A centralized office space including complete equipment and telephone service for:

(1) The assigned staff of the Japanese Government

(2) A small advisory group from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

(3) Members of the Tri-Power Advisory Committee as a conference and meeting room

(4) The storage of active files in such manner that they will be available for daily use by 4b (1), (2) and (3) above.

c. Suitable warehouse space and personnel for the storage and sale of movables.

5. The Japanese Government is further directed to submit to the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, within fifteen (15) days after the receipt of this memorandum, a complete plan of operation in order to comply with the provisions of this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C.C.B. WARDEN
Colonel, AGC
Adjutant General
