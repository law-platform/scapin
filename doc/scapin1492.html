MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1492)

ESS/IN JZR/GEM/ms

9 January 1947

1. Reference is C. L. O. letter No. 6998 (CM), dated 30 December 1946, subject: Application for permission to Construct Miscellaneous Vessels, asking permission to construct three barges, one floating crane, three combination tug and ferry boats, two dredgers, and one water boat.

2. A conference was held between G-2 (Capt. Meals) and ESS/IN (Mr. Valentine), at which time the ferry vessel and auxiliary tug requirements for inter-island ferry port activities were discussed. A conference was also held with Natural Resources Section, Agriculture Section (Mr. L. A. Brown) and favorable action on the proposed construction of the two barges for use in converting lakes into arable lands was recommended.

3. Action taken: Approval for construction of the miscellaneous craft was given by Memorandum to the Imperial Japanese Government.

4. Routine surveillance is required on this activity and will be reported in normal weekly "Occupation Activities Reports".

5. Concurrences: NRS/Agriculture Division (Mr. L. A. Brown); G-2 (Capt. Meals); Transportation Section (Mr. E. F. Powell)

J. Z, R.
