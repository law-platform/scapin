5. a. All silver, gold, rhodium and iridosmine will be obtained from stocks in the possession of the Japanese Government. 22866.5 grams of platinum and 162.2 grams of palladium will be obtained from stocks in the possession of the Japanese Government. Included in the platinum figure is the amount of 3,126.22 grams referred to in paragraph 4, reference 1d above.

b. 1550.8 grams of platinum and 132.6 grams of palladium will be released to authorized representatives of the Ministry of Finance from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan, Tokyo.

c. At the time the precious metals are released from custody of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers under the authority of paragraph 5b above, the Japanese Government will deposit with the Custodian, Eighth Army Vaults, Bank of Japan, Tokyo, 145,355.2 grams at silver. This silver will be in the form of bars of mint or near mint fineness.

6. The Japanese Government is directed to submit monthly reports to include the following information for each of the firm named in paragraphs 2a, 2b, 2c, 2d, 2e, 2f and 5 above.

a. Silver, gold platinum, rhodium, iridium, palladium, iridosmine; silver and gold liquid, dust and foil on hand, as of the first day of the month;

b. Silver, gold platinum, rhodium, iridium, palladium, iridosmine; silver and gold liquid, dust and foil received during the month;

c. Silver, gold platinum, rhodium, iridium, palladium, iridosmine; silver and gold liquid, dust and foil used during the month;

d. Silver, gold platinum, rhodium, iridium, palladium, iridosmine; silver and gold liquid, dust and foil on hand as of the last day of the month;

e. Products manufactured and disposition of same.

7. Reports will be submitted in quadruplicate not later than the fifteenth day of the month followed that covered by the report. These reports will include data pertaining to the Ministry of Welfare and the Ministry of Commerce, Utilitarian Merchandise Section, Japanese Government.

8. The period during which the subject allocation of precious metals may be used will begin upon publication and extend to include 31 March 1948.
