GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 150 (13 Oct 45) CS

(SCAPIN - 133)

APO 500

13 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Charges for Shipment of American Relief Supplies.

1. It is desired that the Japanese Imperial Government submit immediately a final and detailed summary of all expenses incurred in connection with the transportation and distribution of relief supplies picked up at Nakhodka in November, 1944.

2. It is noted that the Japanese Imperial Government has already submitted five separate claims for reimbursement totaling approximately $288,000. These claims have not been detailed and are, therefore, not considered satisfactory. The summery now desired should detail all expenses, including those for which payment has already been made by the United States Government, and should include a statement that it is definitive and that no further claims will be presented.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H.W. Allen
/t/ H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
