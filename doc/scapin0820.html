GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 602 (15 Mar 46) GD

(SCAPIN - 820)

APO 500

15 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Release of Former Japanese Military and Naval Installations in Miyazaki Ken.

1. The following installations are hereby released to the Japanese Government:

Installation Location

(AMS L571-1:250,000)

Kempei Tai Building, Miyakonojo (0592.3--0942.0)

Totoro Torpedo Boat Base (0661.10--1034.75)

Hanagashima Race Track (0631.5--0966.5)

Remount Depot (0642.0--0995.0)

Kawaminami Pasture Land (0640.0--1002.0)

Navy Boat Base (0651.00--1014.80)

Navy Demobilization Office (0630.4--0963.3)

Ningyarai Butai Base (0657.00--1025.85)

Tenth Horse Replacement Depot (0641.5--0995.2)

Tsuno Pasture Land (0640.50--1004.10)

Submarine Base (0624.2--0915.5)

Otsuka Race Track (0609.5--0915.6)

Kobayashi Race Track (1413.0--0973.0)

Miyazaki Regimental District Hdqt. Bldg. (0630.75--0955.75)

2. You are directed to contact Commanding General, Eighth United States Army relative to details of release of above installations.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
