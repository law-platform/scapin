MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Establishment and Operation of a Direct Radiotelegraph Circuit Between Tokyo, Japan and Batavia, Netherlands, East Indies.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 676.3 (3 Apr 48)CCS, SCAPIN 1591/2, 3 April 1948, subject same as above.

2. Effective upon receipt of this memorandum, Inclosure 1 to reference memorandum is rescinded and replaced by Inclosure 1 to this memorandum.

3. Direct communication between Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Telecommunications concerning matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K. B. Bush
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
