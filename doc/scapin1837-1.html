GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 410.2 (28 Jan 48) CPC/GP

(SCAPIN - 1837/1)

APO 500

28 January 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Gold, Silver and Platinum Family Metals for Domestic Consumption in the First Quarter of 1948.

1. Reference is made to memorandum for Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (31 Dec 47)CPC/GP, SCAPIN 1837, 31 December 1947, subject, "Gold, Silver and Platinum Family Metals for Domestic Consumption in the First Quarter of 1948," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. The above reference' is amended as follows:

a. The allocation of 20.6 grams of platinum to Toa Gosei Kagaku Kogyo K.K., processor Tokuriki Honten K.K., is added to approved list in paragraph 2a;

b. Reference paragraph 3, the last two companies listed are changed to read as follows:

GRAMS

USER SILVER GOLD PLATINUM IRIDOSMINE

Yamamori Kikinzoku Kogyo K.K. 2,000 300 1,000

Yamasaki Shoten 1,000 300

c. Reference paragraph 4, note under heading, "Silver" should read, "(includes 5,693,000 grams already on hand. See #1, paragraph 2a). "

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
