GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 471 (9 Nov 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 258)

A.P.O. 500

9 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Receipts for Japanese Munitions Turned over to U. S. Authorities.

1. This headquarters has received a letter dated 31 October 1945 from the Liaison Committee for the Imperial Japanese Army and Navy requesting that the U. S. Forces at Hochijojima be instructed to furnish receipts to the Japanese authorities for munitions received in the course of disarmament.

2. In reply you are advised that no general instructions have been issued to Occupational Forces Commanders to furnish such raceipts nor is it intended to issue such instructions. Whether or not receipts are given is a matter for determination by the Occupational Forces Commanders.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
