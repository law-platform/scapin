GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 471.86 (12 Nov 45) ESS/IN

(SCAPIN - 272)

12 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Manufacture of Explosives for Use in Production of Coal

1. Reference is made to C. L.O. Memorandum No. 380, subject, "Application For Manufacture and Use Of Industrial Explosives."

2. The application concerning explosives for all industrial uses is not favorably considered at this time.

3. You are authorized to manufacture the quantities of explosives listed below, which you are to use for the production of coal and for no other purpose.

4. Manufacture of the following types and quantities of explosives is authorized for the period ending 31 December 1945:

Types of Explosives and Accessories

Gelatine Dynamite and "Hagi" Dynamite

Ammonia Dynamite

Ammonia Nitrate Explosives

Fuse

Blasting Caps for Fuse

Electric Detonators

Quantities Authorized

179 metric tons

190 metric tons

750 metric tons

2, 800 km

2, 550, 000 pieces

4, 130,000 pieces

5. The Japanese Government will submit to the Commanding Generals of the Sixth and Eighth Armies applications for authority to plants necessary for the manufacture of the above authorized explosives.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
