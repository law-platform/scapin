4. a. All gold and silver will be obtained from stocks in the possession of the Japanese Government.

b. 16,632.22 grams of platinum will be released to authorized representatives of the Ministry of Finance from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan.

c. The release of 32.58 grams of iridium is authorized in paragraph 2a above, 25.7 grams will be obtained from stocks in the possession of the Japanese Government; the balance, 6,88 grams, will be released to authorized representatives of the Ministry of Finance from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Bank of Japan.

d. 301.2 grams of rhodium (authorized in paragraph 2a above) will be released to authorized representatives of the Ministry of Finance from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Osaka Mint.

e. Allocation of 765 grams of palladium is approved in paragraphs 2a and 2b above. 479 grams will be obtained from stocks in the possession of the Japanese Government; the balance of 286 grams will be released to authorized representatives of the Ministry of Finance from the Eighth Army Vaults of the Osaka Mint.

f. All future requests for the allocation of precious metals for domestic consumption must contain written approval based upon Japan's minimum needs of the interested Ministries of the Japanese Government. Application for release of precious metals for manufacture of export items must include the numbers of the Validated Form 100.

5. The Japanese Government is directed to submit a monthly report to include the following information for each of the firms named in paragraphs 2a, b, c, and d above.

a. Gold, silver, platinum, palladium, rhodium, iridium, and liquid gold on hand as of the first day of the month;

b. Gold, silver, platinum, palladium, rhodium, iridium, and liquid gold received during the month;

c. Gold, silver, platinum, palladium, rhodium, iridium, and liquid gold used during the month;

d. Gold, silver, platinum, palladium, rhodium, iridium, and liquid gold on hand as of the last day of the month;

e. Products manufactured and disposition of same,

6. Reports will be submitted in quadruplicate not later than the fifteenth day of the month following that covered by the report. Theso reports will include data pertaining to the Japan Dental Association,

7. The period during which the subject allocation of precious metals may be used is extended to include the Third Quarter of 1947.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
