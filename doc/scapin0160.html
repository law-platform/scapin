GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 430.2 (19 Oct 45) GD

(SCAPIN - 160)

APO 500

19 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Personal Rations and Allotments to Demobilized Military Personnel from Overseas.

1. Reference:

a. Letter from the Liaison Committee for the Imperial Japanese Army and Navy, subject: "Application for Permission to Use Part of the Military Supplies and Commodities for the Demobilization of Japanese Troops Overseas," dated 1 October 1945.

b. Letter from the Liaison Committee for the Imperial Japanese Army and Navy, subject: "Personnel, Rations, and Allotments to Demobilized Military Personnel from Overseas," dated 3 October 1945.

2. Food and other necessities for use in transporting demobilized troops from overseas as requested in paragraph 1 (1) of reference 1 a is authorized.

3. Prior to shipment of food, clothing, and medical supplies for use of troops overseas in areas under the control of nations other than the United States as requested in par 1(2) of reference 1a, it is desired that specific requests be submitted to this headquarters for approval. These requests will show location of the troops, number present at each location, quantities of supplies on hand with the troops, a list of the supplies to be shipped and the number of personnel to be returned to Japan in the shipping to be used.

4. Provisions and supplies for individuals of demobilized troops returning from overseas as requested in reference 1b and paragraph 1(3) of reference 1a are authorized within the following limits.

Clothes and equipments:

Beaver 1

Suit (summer or winter) 1

Raincoat 1

Overcoat 1

Underwear (summer & winter) 1

Shoes 1

Chikatabi (rubber soled socks) 1

Socks 1

Gaiter 1

Blanket 1

Mess-tin 1

Kit-bag & Tent 1 (each)

Canteen 1

Glove 1

Standard rations to be supplied:

Cereals (flour) of cracker For 5 days - ( a day's rations)

Cereals - 500g)

Cracker - 690g)

Miso For 5 days (a day's ration 20g)

Soy-bean Sauce - For 5 days (a day's ration 0.015 litre)

Subsidiary Articles of Diet some appropriate articles

Daily necessaries:

Tooth Powder 1

Tooth Brush 1

Soap 1

Pencil 2

Post Card 5

Match Box 1

The amounts that each man is to have in his possession upon discharge shall not exceed the above list.

5. The Home Ministry has been designated as the agency to receive and use for Civilian Relief and other civil pursuits, military supplies and equipment of the Japanese armed forces which are returned to the Imperial Japanese Government. Until supplies and equipment of the types mentioned above been ▲▲▲nd over to U. S. Occupational Forces and returned to the control of the Home Ministry as prescribed by Memorandum this Headquarters, File AG 402.5 (24 Sep 45) GD subject: "Materials, Supplies and Equipment Received and to be Received from the Japanese Armed Forces," dated 24 September 1945, the Japanese Army and Navy are authorized to use supplies and equipment now under their control as authorized by the foregoing paragraphs.

6. After the Home Ministry assume control of the supplies and equipment in question it will be their responsibility to determine the actual quantities which can be made available for individual repatriates or shipment to troops overseas, taking into consideration civilian Reeds. The agency actually to be responsible for supplying repatriated is in the process of determination at the present time. When this agency is finally established it is desired that it make arrangements directly with the Home Ministry for use of supplies in accordance with above instruction.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
