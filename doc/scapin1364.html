SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

APO 500

27 November 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Supplemental Instructions Relating to Guarantee of Deposits.

1. References are the following memoranda to the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. File AG 386.7 (21 Oct 46) ESS/FI, (SCAPIN 1282), dated 21 October 1946, subject: Guarantee of Deposits of United Nations Nationals.

b. File AG 140 (18 Apr 46) ESS/FI (SCAPIN 883), dated 18 April 1946, subject: Exempting Accounts of Deposit of Certain Individuals from the Terms of Emergency Measures Imperial Ordinance.

2. The deposit accounts in financial institutions of all United Nations Nationals as described in reference memorandum 1 - a above and of all persons described in paragraph 2 of reference memorandum 1 - b above, come under the provisions of the guarantee in reference memorandum 1 - a above provided:

a. Such nationals were residing in Japan and its occupied areas during the period 7 December 1941 to 2 September 1945 and were imprisoned, interned, under house arrest, or had their property taken, blocked or sold by the Imperial Japanese Government or Agency thereof, because of their being nationals of countries at war with Japan, or sympathizers with such citizens, subjects or countries.

b. Such nationals were residing outside of Japan and its occupied areas during the period 7 December 1941 to 2 September 1945 and had their property taken, blocked or sold by the Imperial Japanese Government or Agency thereof, because of their being nationals of countries at war with Japan, or sympathizers with such citizens, subjects or countries.

3. The benefits of subject memorandom 1 - a above will also apply to the deposit accounts accrued since 2 September 1945 of persons who are or were in Japan at the invitation or with the permission of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1364)

WKL/TEB/JCS/gwh

18 November 1946

1. a. Memorandum for Imperial Japanese Government, AG 386. 7 (21 Oct 46) ESS/FI, (SCAPIN 1282), subject: "Guarantee of Deposits of United Nations Nationals" ordered guarantee in full of the deposits of United Nation Nationals. This was done to protect United Nation Nationals from United the losses arising out of war indemnity cancellation and reorganization of financial institutions. The intent was to accord preferential treatment to United Nations Nationals whose loyalties were never questioned during the war, who were imprisoned or interned or whose property was taken, sold, or blocked by Imperial Japanese Government as a result of their nationality or sympathies. It was not intended as a blanket guarantee of deposits of anyone of non- Japanese nationality who could lay claim or establish himself as a United Nation National but who remained in Japan during the war, carried on commerce with the Japanese and suffered no restriction of personal liberty, or loss from sale or blocking of property. Ability to be certified, now or later, as a national of a United Nation should not constitute prima facie right entitling such a person to the benefits of SCAPIN 1282. This guarantee should be based on equity, not on accident of birth or fortunate ability to claim citizenship in a United Nation through a technicality or other circumstances. This guarantee should not be considered as a cloak but as definite preferential treatment in compensation for ill-treatment and actual loss. Persons who can technically argue eligibility under SCAPIN 1282 but do not meet the other stipulations should not have the benefits extended to them.

b. Memorandum for Imperial Japanese Government, AG 140 (18 Apr 46) ESS/FI, (SCAPIN 883), 18 April 1946, subject: "Exempting Accounts of Deposit of Certain Individuals from the Terms of Emergency Measures Imperial Ordinance" was designed to give relief to persons non-Japanese nationality who were imprisoned or whose property was taken, blocked or sold by the Japanese Government, or agency thereof, between 7 December 1941 and 2 September 1945 because of their being citizens or subjects of countries at war with Japan, or sympathizers with such citizens, subjects or countries. As a matter of equity, the benefits of the guarantee of deposits should be extended to these persons of non-Japanese nationality as well as to United Nation Nationals. It would be pointless to give the benefits of AG 140 (18 Apr 46) ESS/FI and not extend the benefits of the deposit guarantee as well.

2. The benefits of the guarantee should also be extended to the deposit accounts of persons such as newspaper correspondents, news associations, radio and magazine representatives, and anyone else in Japan at the invitation, or with the permission of GHQ, SCAP for purposes deemed to be in the interest of the occupation. The guarantee here will only apply to the deposit accounts accruing from 2 September 1945.

3. The present memorandum is designed to establish eligibility under SCAPIN 1282 and to eliminate any inequalities which might arise through application and interpretation of the deposit guarantee. The circumstances and qualifications under which the guarantee benefits are extended are thus made quite clear and the true intent of SCAPIN 1282, namely, not to assess a share of the cost of Japanese agression against those persons of non-Japanese nationality who took no part in it and suffered as a result, is accomplished.

4. This action does not affect the interests of Korea.

5. This is an administrative matter and surveillance of the Eighth Army is not required.

6. This action has been coordinated with and concurred in by Diplomatic Section (Mr. U. S. Johnson); CPC (Mr. D. H. Blake); ESS/LE (Maj. E. R. Minnich).

7. Concurrences: DS;CPC;ESS/LE

W. K. L.
