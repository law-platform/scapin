GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 312.1 (3 Nov 45) CIS

(SCAPIN - 242)

6 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Request for Presence of Japanese at Headquarters, Southeast Asia Command.

The Imperial Japanese Government will:

A. Direct Major General Katakura, General Iwakuro, Lt Col Fujiwara, former head of Fujiwara Kikan, Mr. Shuinichu Matsumoto and Mr. Renzo Sawada, former Vice Foreign Ministers, and Minister Ayer, former Minister for Publicity and Propaganda under Chandra Bose, to be prepared to depart from Tokyo on or about 12 November for India.

B. Insure that the above six (6) individuals are in Tokyo ready to depart on above date.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
