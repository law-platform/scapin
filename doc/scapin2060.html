APO 500

3 December 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Reestablishment of Civil Wire Telephone Service Between Japan and Korea.

1. Effective upon receipt of this memorandum, the Japanese Government, Ministry of Telecommunications, is authorized to reestablish civil wire telephone service between Japan and Korea, using the submarine cable facilities via Fukuoka/Pusan.

2. The Ministry of Telecommunications is authorized to make this service available to all persons and organizations in Japan.

3. Initial period rates, overtime rates, and report charges expressed in Japanese yen, on a zone basis from Japan to Korea, as proposed by the Ministry of Telecommunications, are approved by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and are shown in inclosure 1. It is understood that all calls will be prepaid at point of origin.

4. The Ministry of Telecommunications is further authorized to reestablish this service on a non-settlement of accounts, or "sender keep all" basis, and continue on this basis until such time as a settlement of accounts may be mutually agreed upon between the Ministry of Telecommunications and the Korean Ministry of Communications.

5. Routine operating practices and other circuit operating details will be as mutually agreed upon between the Ministry of Telecommunications and the Korean Ministry of Communications.

6. Direct communication between Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Telecommunications, concerning matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
