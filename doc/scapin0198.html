GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 123 (27 Oct 45) ESS-FI

(SCAPIN - 198)

27 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Request for Release of Funds

1. Reference is made to the Ordinance of the Ministry of Finance on the Preservation of the Property of the Specified Powers, dated 20 September 1945 (CLOM 49).

2. It is directed that you take the necessary action to release the sum of ¥8, 000 from the savings account of Oreste Vaccari, in the Yokohama Specie Bank, Tokyo.

3. An acknowledgement of the receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General

MEMORANDUM FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 198) CFT/JNC/tn

27 October 1945

1. An undated letter was received in this office 24 October 1945 from Oreste Vaccari, Karuizawa 525, Nagano Prefecture, requesting the release of 8,000 yen from his savings account in the Yokohama Specie Bank, Tokyo, which had been frozen under ordinance of the Minister of Finance (CLOM 49).

2. Vaccari had received an order for 10,000 copies of his English-Japanese dictionary from Army Exchange Central Purchasing Office to be shipped on 16 November 1945. These dictionaries are for the use of occupation forces and the order was varified with Captain Charles A. Moore, Purchasing Officer.

3. The CIC was consulted and the dictionaries examined by them and formal approval for the printing thereof was granted by Col. Stewart, CIC.

4. Concurrences from GPA and CIC.

C. F. T.
