APO 500

7 October 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Relief Payments to Workers Furnished to the Occupation Forces

1. Reference memorandum from the Ministry of Finance, LO 3359 (AD/2F), dated 30 August 1948, submitted through Central Liaison and Coordination Office, CLCO 3090, dated 1 September 1948, subject: Inquiry on Relief Measures to the Suffered Workers Employed Directly in Works for Occupation Forces.

2. Memorandum to Japanese Government, SCAPIN 1872, dated 31 March 1948, subject: Expenditures from Funds Appropriated for Occupation Force Requirements, does not specify the type or amount of allowances which shall be paid to Occupation workers. No objection is offered to the principle of payment of relief or solatium allowances to such workers provided such payments are made in accordance with officially established allowances for national government employees as a whole.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ A. J. Rehe
for /t/ R. M. LEVY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1872/4)

WKL/EMR/AMM/mm

18 September 1948

1. The Finance Ministry has requested information regarding the authority of the Japanese Government under SCAPIN 1872, dated 31 March 1948, to pay certain relief or solatium allowances to workers furnished to the Occupation Forces by the Japanese Government

2. The particular allowance in question is in the nature of solatium or compensation payments for personal or property damage suffered by such workers in connection with certain fire or earthquake disasters under circumstances that could not be considered damages suffered in line of duty. No such payments are presently authorized for other national government employees.

3. Proposed SCAPIN states that such payments are justified under SCAPIN 1872 only insofar as they are made in accordance with officially established allowances for national government employees as a whole.

4. Surveillance by the Eighth Army is not necessary.

5. Concurrences: G-4; ESS/LAB

A.M. McGlauflin, 57-8807

W. K. L.
