APO 500

14 April 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Release of Precious Metal Commercial Forms from the Pool Under Custody of the Ministry of Finance, Japanese Government Released Stocks to be Used in Items Manufactured and Processed for Export.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. C.L.O. No. 9675(LO 4593-FI/IE), 18 December 1947, subject, "Sixth Supplemental Request for Permission of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the First Quarter of 1948."

b. C.L.O. No. 606(LO 29-FI/2E), 29 January 1948, subject, "Twelfth Supplemental Request for Permission of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in First Quarter of 1948."

c. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 410.2 (22 Nov 47)CPC/GP, SCAPIN 1825, 22 November 1947, subject, "Application for Use of Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption during the Period from Fourth Quarter of 1947 through Third Quarter of 1948," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Applications for the release of gold and silver dust as requested by references 1a and 1b are approved, as altered, in the amounts and by the companies listed. Gold and silver dust stocks from the pool of precious metal commercial products under custody of the Ministry of Finance, Japanese Government, and released for manufacture into dust by reference 1c above, or by similar allocations will be drawn upon to supply the dust authorized. The manufacturers will further process the gold and silver dust into suitable forms in manufacturing items for export as approved by listed Export Authority License Numbers.
