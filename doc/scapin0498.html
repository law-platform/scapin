GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 370.05 (24 Dec 45) GC

(SCAPIN - 498)

24 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Protection and Repatriation of Japanese in Areas Occupied by the Soviet Forces.

1. Reference is made to letter, C. L.O. No. 1183 (5.2), dated 18 December 1945, subject as above.

2. This matter has been referred to the United States Government.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
