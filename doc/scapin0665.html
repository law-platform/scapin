GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 440 (28 Jan 46) PH

(SCAPIN-665)

APO 500

28 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Manufacture of Medical Supplies and Equipment.

1. Reports reaching this Headquarters concerning shortages of medical supplies and equipment indicate an urgent need for increasing production of essential items required for the care and treatment of the sick.

2. The production of an adequate supply of vaccines and sera is particularly important. C. L. O., no. 446 (1), 2 November 1945 reports the quantities that may be produced with available facilities and materials. Recent reports indicate that production of some important items is far below existing capacity.

3. You are directed to submit to this headquarters, not later than 20 February 1946, a plan covering the manufacture of medical supplies and equipment, with particular reference to the production of vaccine and sera. The plan will indicate proposed action for implementation. Consideration will be given the following:

a Full utilization of raw materials, manufacturing equipment and supplies pertaining to the Japanese Army and Navy, which have been returned to the Home Ministry for civilian use.

b Manufacture of essential supplies and equipment, as distinguished from items of doubtful value in the care and treatment of the sick.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
