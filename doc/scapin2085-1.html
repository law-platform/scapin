GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311 (24 Feb 50) SC PO

(SCAPIN-2085/1)

APO 500

28 November 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Rationalization of Japan's International Telecommunications Services.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 311 (24 Feb 50)CCS, SCAPIN 2085, 24 February 1950, subject same as above.

2. Paragraph 13b of reference 1 above is rescind and paragraph 13 is revised to read as follows:

"13. All international telecommunications of a personal or family nature, and business, financial, commercial, and transactional messages, between Japan and any country with which telecommunications services are available, are authorized, subject to the following restriction:

"a. Restriction. Incoming and outgoing international and domestic telecommunications will be made available for review in accordance with instructions issued by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers."

3. Direct communication between Signal Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers and other staff sections of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Telecommunications, concerning matters within the scope of this memorandum is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C.C.B. WARDEN
Colonel, AGC
Adjutant General.
