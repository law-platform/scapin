GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 676.3 (4 Nov 47) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1744/3)

APO 500

4 November 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Radio Communications for the Japanese Whaling Fleet.

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 800.217 (21 Jun 47)NR/FI, (SCAPIN 1737), dated 21 June 1947, subject: Japanese Whaling Operations in the Antarctic in the 1947 - 1948 Season.

b. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 676.3 (14 Jul 47)CCS, (SCAPIN 1744), dated 14 July 1947, subject: Control of Radio Communications.

c. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 458 (17 Sep 47)GD, (SCAPIN 1779), dated 17 September 1947, subject: Radar for Vessels on Antarctic Whaling Expedition.

2. The frequencies recommended by the Ministry of Communications for use by the 1947 - 1948 Japanese Whaling Expedition are authorized as listed in Inclosure 1.

3. The Bureau of Fisheries of the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry hereinafter referred to as the Communications Agent will act as the Communications Agent in Japan for the whaling fleet including the Taiyo Gyogyo (Ocean Fishing Company), the Nippon Suisan (marine Products Company), for those observers accompanying the whaling fleet with the authorization of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and for private messages exchanged between members of the crew of the whaling fleet ships and their families in Japan.

4. Any communications with the whaling fleet will be transmitted or received.by the Japanese coastal radio service only when addressed through the Communications Agent for the whaling fleet. A file copy of all messages sent or received through the Communications Agent will be retained by that Bureau.

5. Communications, including personal messages, to and from authorized Allied observers, will be forwarded to or received from the Natural Resources Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, via the Communications Agent named above, and will be handled without charge.

6. Communications between Japanese nationals including personal messages to and from the whaling fleet, will be sent via the Communications Agent who will relay the message to the company or person addressed subject to all existing rules and requirements of censorship.

7. With respect to the navigation of vessels, meteorological observations and fishing data, the Japanese whaling fleet is authorized radio communication with the following:

a. Other vessels of the authorized Japanese whaling fleet.

b. Radio stations of the Japanese coastal radio service.

c. Whaling vessels of other nations.

8. Messages pertaining to the navigation of Japanese whaling vessels, meteorological observations and fishing data may be sent in the following codes:

a. Nippon Suisan's telegraph code for fishery information.

b. Taiyo Gyogyo's telegraph code for fishery information.

Copies of these codes will be deposited with the Bureau of Fisheries of the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry and the Natural Resources Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

9. The Japanese whaling fleet will answer immediately any communication from the United States Navy or Naval vessels of the other Allied Powers.

10. The Japanese whaling fleet will answer interrogations of non-Japanese coastal stations when so interrogated.

11. Communication is permitted with any radio station when authorized by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers' representatives accompanying the Whaling Expedition.

12. The Japanese whaling vessels may establish contact with other vessels or coastal radio stations to handle distress traffic according to the General Radio Regulations (Cairo Revision 1938).

13. Direct communication between the Civil Communications Section, the Natural Resources Section, or any other interested section of General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Communications and the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry, regarding matters within the scope of this memorandum, is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.

1 Incl

List of Authorized Ship Radio Stations of the Japanese Whaling Fleet and Assigned Frequencies
