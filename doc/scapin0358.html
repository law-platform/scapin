GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.93 (28 Nov 45) GC-P

(SCAPIN - 358)

28 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Discontinuance of Army and Navy Meteorological Services.

1. Reference C. L. O. Letter No 643 (4), 20 November 1945, subject: "Tranmission of Letter of Central Meteorological Observatory", and inclosures.

2. The operation of all Japanese Army and Navy meteorological facilities with the exception of those specifically authorized in this and former S. C. A. P. memorandums, will be discontinued.

3. The following former Army and Navy meteorological stations are authorized to resume operations as civil stations, under control of the Central Meteorological Observatory:

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
