GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 430 (7 Aug 46) PH

(SCAPIN-1112)

APO 500

7 August 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Ration for United Nations’ Nationals, Neutral Nationals and Stateless Persons.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, AG 430 (30 July 46) PH, (SCAPIN-1094), subject as above.

2. The following named countries are added to the list of countries, above reference, paragraph 2b:

Cuba Egypt Salvador

Philippines Brazil New Zealand

Mexico British Commonwealth of Nations

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K. B. BUSH,
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
