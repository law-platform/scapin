AG430.2 (21 April 46) ESS/IE

(SCAPIN-890)

APO 500

21 April 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Release of U. S. Army Subsistence Stocks. (21,331,666 pounds Canned Meats)

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will take action and assume responsibility as follows with regard to 21,331,666 pounds of canned meats being made available for future Japanese consumption:

a. Accept delivery at earliest possible date at points to be designated by Commanding General, Eighth U. S. Army.

b. Furnish the Commanding General, Eighth U. S. Army with quantitative receipt in triplicate for the canned meats at the point of delivery.

c. Store the canned meats so that they will be protected against spoilage, pilferage, theft or other hazards, and under proper custody at all times.

d. Furnish information as to the location of the storage points stating the quantity stored at each location. All of the canned meats will be held in storage until official authorization for release is given by the General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Term of payment and accounting will be decided later

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 890)

RAM/HJZ/ho

10 April 1946

1. Reference is made to Check Sheet from G-4 to Economic and Scientific Section dated 3 April 1946 attaching list of canned meats (21,331,666 pounds), which are excess to the needs of the Eighth Army with recommendation that action be taken to have the Imperial Japanese Government take over the storage of these food stocks. These stocks are now in Tokyo and Kobe. Reference is also made to letter to Commanding General U.S. Army Forces Pacific recommending that authority be granted to sell the excess canned meats to the Imperial Japanese Government at the earliest practicable date.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is hereby directed to take delivery at points designated by the Commanding General Eighth U.S. Army of these stocks and to store them until official authorization for release is given by General Headquarters, SCAP.

3. These stocks will be applied against our Import Program.

4. This is a Policy Directive and Army surveillance is necessary.

5. This problem as it relates to Korea has been considered.

6. Concurrences: G-4 P&P - Col. Ebel

G-4 Supply - Maj. Mancuso

PC&R - Capt. McLeod

PH&W - Col. Howe

NRS - Maj. Williamson

QM - Maj. Bruce

R. A. M.
