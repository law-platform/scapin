AG 091.112 (4 Sept 46) GS

(SCAPIN-1175)

APO 500

4 September 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Temporary Retention of Public Official under SCAPIN 550 - Daigoro YASUKAWA.

1. Reference is made to CLO 3999 (PP), dated 8 August 1946, subject: Retention of Daigoro YASUKAWA.

2. The request contained in reference CLO for the retention in office of Daigoro YASUKAWA until the end of June1947 is disapproved.

3. It is directed that within fifteen days from date of this Memorandum, Daigoro YASUKAWA be removed from public office and excluded from government service as an undesirable person under the terms of Category C, SCAPIN 550.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
