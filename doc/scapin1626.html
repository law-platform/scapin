GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 569.7 (23 Apr 47) GD-P&P

(SCAPIN - 1626)

APO 500

23 April 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Sale and Mortgage of Japanese Merchant Vessels.

1. Reference is made to Central Liaison Office No. 1246 (CM), dated 26 February 1947, subject, "Sale and Mortgage of Japanese Merchant Vessels."

2. The transfer of titles as requested by above reference letter is approved for the following vessels:

1. No. 46 Tatsutaka Maru 8. Eiho Maru

2. No. 29 Tatsutaka Maru 9. Hirugo Maru

3. No. 24 Tatsutaka Maru 10. No. 2 Xoyo Maru

4. No. 47 Tatsutaka Maru 11. No. 57 Chuyo Maru

5. No. 48 Tatsutaka Maru 12. No. 45 Chuyo Maru

6. Choyo Maru

3. The transfer of funds will be in accordance with existing financial regulations of the Imperial Japanese Government.

4. The establishment of a mortgage on the Tuukushi Maru requested by above reference letter is disapproved at this time. Application will be reconsidered upon submission of identification of "newly built vessel" involved in transaction and assurance that approval has been obtained from Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for its construction.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
