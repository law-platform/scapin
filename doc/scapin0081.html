AG 360 (1 Oct 45) GC

(SCAPIN - 81)

APO 500

1 October 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Temporary Air Service Between Osaka and Fukuoka.

1. Reference is made to Central Liaison Office Memorandum No.86 dated 27 September 1945, subject as above.

2. The operation of a temporary air service between Osaka and Fukuoka is not favorably considered.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ Harold Fair.
/t/ HAROLD FAIR,
Lt. Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General.
