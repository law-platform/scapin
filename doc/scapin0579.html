GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 312.4 (11 Jan 46) CIS

(SCAPIN-579)

11 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Mongols at Imperial University, Sapporo, Hokkaido.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will escort to Tokyo and hold at the disposal of this Headquarters, RICHIJAMSO and KURELO, both Mongols, resident in the Imperial University at Sapporo.

2. These men will arrive in Tokyo on or before 2400 (Midnight) 20 January 1946, and this Headquarters will be advised of their araival.

3. If for any reason it is impossible to place these men at the disposal of this Headquarters by the appointed time the Japanese Government will report the reason therefor.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel,A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
