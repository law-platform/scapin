MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Release of Silver from Newly Mined Japanese Government Stocks and Release of Precious Metals Commercial Forms from the Pool under Custody of the Ministry of Finance, Japanese Government; Released Stocks to be Used in Items Manufactured and Processed for Export.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. C.L.O. No. 7708(LO 3195-FI/FF), 2 October 1947, subject, "Additional Application for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in Fourth Quarter of 1947; "

b. C.L.O. No. 8710(LO 4022-FI/FF), 7 November 1947, subject, "Additional Application for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in the First Quarter of 1948;"

c. C.L.O. No. 8711(LO 4023-FI/FF), 7 November 1947, subject, "Additional Application for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in the First Quarter of 1948;"

d. C.L.O. No. 8575(LO 3767-FI/FF), 31 October 1947, subject, "Additional Application for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in the Fourth Quarter of 1947;"

e. Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 410.2

(22 Nov 47)CPC/GP, SCAPIN 1825, 22 November 1947, subject, "Application for Use of Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption during the Period from Fourth Quarter of 1947 through Third Quarter of 1948," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

2. Approval is granted for the release of 74,988 grams of silver from newly mined stocks available to the Japanese Government to the juridical persons, and in the amounts recorded below. The processors listed are hereby authorized to process the released silver into silver nitrate and silver wire. Items produced will be used in products licensed for export under the Export Authority Order Numbers listed:

4. Approval is granted to Shinjiro Kono, of the Ishikawaken Hakusho Kogyo Kyodo Kumiai, to prepare 1,000,000 sheets of silver foil, size 3.75 inches by 3.75 inches, containing 37,500 grams silver by weight, for export approved by Export Authority License Number 2739. This foil is to be prepared from the pool of precious metals commercial products under custody of the Ministry of Finance, Japanese Government, and from silver released for manufacture into silver foil by reference 1e above, or from similar allocations.

5. Approval is granted for the release of 1,000 grams gold dust and 9,400 grams silver dust from the pool of precious metals commercial products under custody of the Ministry of Finance, Japanese Government and released for manufacture into dust by reference 1e above, or by similar allocations, to the Maruni Kogei Shikki Seisakusho K.K. The Aizu Kimpun Kimpakugyo Kumiai may process these dusts into suitable form. The dusts will be used in manufacturing items for export approved by Export Authority License Number 2316.

6. The request for the release of gold liquid to Morimura Shoji K.K. is disapproved. The Japanese Government will resubmit this request in the name of the actual manufacturers, Hyozo Kato and Kumagai Seitosho. This request will include both the quantity of gold liquid needed, its concentration and the quantity of products to be manufactured in filling Export Authority Order Number 3007.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
