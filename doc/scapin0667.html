GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 486.8 (28 Jan 46) CE

(SCAPIN - 667)

28 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Reproduction of Smooth Sheets.

1. Reference is made to letter C.L.O. No.167 (3.2) dated 12 January 1946, concerning the request of the Japanese Hydrographic Department for assistance in obtaining chemical materials reported to be needed to permit compliance with the Allied Powers directive contained in Memorandum AG 486.8 (14 Dec 45) on the above subject.

2. Inasmuch as a subsequent directive has been issued (Memorandum AG 486.8 (14 Jan 46)CE) which specified that smooth sheets (compilation drawings) for areas outside the Japanese main islands be turned over to the Allied Powers, the Hydrographic Department will be required to reproduce smooth sheets for the main islands only. It is therefore desired that the photographic work continue at an accelerated rate for the main islands and that a revised estimate of the date of completion of this work be submitted together with a revised statement of the chemical materials, if any, required to complete the work.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
