IN THE CLEAR
OFFICE OF SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS
SIGNAL CORPS MESSAGE

(SCAPIN-82)

GC-O TNS/1b 2 October 1945

FROM :SCAP
TO :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT
INFO :CINCPAC/POA PEARL
COM FIFTH FLEET
COM SEVENTH FLEET
COM SIXTH ARMY
CG EIGHTH ARMY
CG USAFIK
CONCAFPAC MANILA

REFERENCE YOUR MEMORANDUM SOA NUMBER SEVEN DATED TWO TWO SEPTEMBER AND OUR RADIO ZAX SIX TWO TWO FIVE DATRD THREE NAIGHT SEPTEMBER PD( AX 6334) HAKODATE WILL BE USED AS A PRINCIPLE PORT AND OTARU AS ALTERNATE PORT

OFFICIAL:

B. M. FITCH
BRIGADIER General, US Army, and him
Adjutant General.

APPROVED:

/s/ W. E. Chambers
/T/W. E.S. CHAMBERS
Brig. General, G.S. C.,
Asst. Chief of Staff, G-3
