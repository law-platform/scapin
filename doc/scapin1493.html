AG 014.33 (28 Jan 47) GA

(SCAPIN-1493)

APO 500

28 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Repatriation of Netherlands East Indies Nationals

1. Refererence is made to memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 091.31 (19 Jan 46) ESS/FI (SCAPIN-622), dated 19 January 1946, subject: “Supplemental Instructions Relating to Export Control,”

2. There are approximately one hundred (100) Netherlands East Indies Nationals now in Japan who desire repatriation aboard the SS Tjidabak, expected to arrive Kobe on 4 February 1947.

3. The Netherlands East Indies Nationals concerned have been notified to report to the Hyogo Military Government authorities at Kobe on 5 February 1947 for processing and embarkation.

4. The Imperial Japanese Government will;

a. Provide ambulance service for those persons who are physically incapacitated to such an extent that they would otherewise be unable to travel from their place of residence to the proper railroad station. Verbal instructions will be issued to a representative of the Central Liaison office concerning specific cases for which such abulance service is desired.

b. Medically examine and inoculate all Netherlands East Indies Nationals reporting for repatriation and process their baggage and personal belongings in accordance with instructions contained in memorandum referred to in paragraph 1 above.

5. In the event that additional Netherlands East Indies Nationals desire repatriation in the future, this memorandum, supplemented by appropriate verbal instructions to a representative of the Central Liaison Office, will constitute authority for their movement, processing and repatriation.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
