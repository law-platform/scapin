APO 500

4 April 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Scrapping of Incomplete Wartime Standard Vessels

1. Reference is C. L.O. letter No. 1516 (CM), dated 7 March 1947, subject: Application for Permission to Discontinue Construction of Vessels and Break Up Hulls Thereof, with one inclosure.

2. The Imperial Japanese Government is directed to discontinue construction of and break up hulls of those vessels listed on inclosure attached hereto with the least practicable delay.

3. This directive modifies accordingly Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 561. 4 (21 Apr 46) ESS/IN, (SCAPIN 891), dated 21 April 1946, subject: Steel Ship Construction.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
for /t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1597)

ESS/IN JZR/GEM/EPS/ms

24 March 1947

1, Reference is letter Central Liaison Office, Tokyo, C.L. O. No. 1516 (CM), subject: Application for Permission to Discontinue Construction of Vessels and Break Up Hulls Thereof, dated 7 March 1947, and one inclosure (Table of Vessels), attached thereto.

2. The completion of these vessels was authorized by SCAP Memorandum AG 561. 4 (21 Apr 46) ESS/IN (SCAPIN 891), subject: Steel Ship Construction.

3. A discussion was held with representatives of the Ministry of Transportation, Japanese Government, regarding incompleted vessels authorized by reference of above paragraph 2, and a plan was developed to dispose of the uneconomical wartime standard designed ships.

4. Civil Transportation Section concurs in the cancellation of this construction on the basis of a better overall operating efficiency for long range planning.

5. The Imperial Japanese Government is therefore directed by SCAPIN to scrap the following vessels:
