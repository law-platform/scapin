GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 720.3 (11 June 46) PH

(SCAPIN - 1013)

APO 500

11 June 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Report on Revaccination against Smallpox.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. Memorandum (SCAPIN 921), 4 May 1946, subject: "Vaccination against Smallpox".

b. Letter, C.L.O. 2613 (PP), 29 May 1946, subject: "Report on Revaccination against Smallpox".

c. Ei-Hatsu 381, 30 May 1946.

2. Request by the Welfare Ministry for a delay of thirty (30) days as requested in C.L.O. 2613(PP), is granted.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
