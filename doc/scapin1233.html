AG 383.7 (27 Sep 46) LS-R

(SCAPIN - 1233)

APO 500

27 September 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Release of Chinese Prisoners.

1. The following named Chinese Nationals, presently confined at KOSUGE Prison, Tokyo, will be released to the custody of the Chinese Mission in Japan at the earliest practicable date:

Chou, Wan-Tung

La, Foo-hai.

Ying, Pao-hsing

Wu, Kwei-sun

Whang, Fu-san

2. The above named Chinese were transferred to KOSUGE Prison from AKITA Prison.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
