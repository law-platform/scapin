GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 150 (16 Nov 45) CS

(SCAPIN - 289)

APO 500

16 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Charges for Shipment of American Relief Supplies.

1. Reference is made to the memorandum from this Headquarters, file AG 150 (13 Oct 45), subject as above, dated 13 October 1945 and the reply of the Japanese Government dated 6 November 1945 on the above subject.

2. The Japanese reply does not state that the summary of the changes in question is definitive and that no further claims will be presented. Such a statement will be included in the summary in accordance with the final sentence of the memorandum of 13 October 1945 from this Headquarters.

3. It is assumed that the statement in the first sentence of paragraph 2 of the report submitted by the Japanese Government that "... 1,176,981.23 Yen, or Fr. 1,200,388.84 in Swiss currency", is an error in conversion. The report should be carefully checked to assure that there are no errors.

4. A new summary of the charges in question should be prepared incorporating the desired statement explained in paragraph 2. This report is to be submitted in quadruplicate, at least two copies of which will be signed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
