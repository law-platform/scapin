GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.23 (14 Oct 47) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1765/3)

APO 500

14 October 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service Between Japan and British Commonwealth Areas, via Osaka-Colombo Circuit.

1. Reference is made to memoranda for the Japanese Government as follows:

a. File AG 311.23 (12 Aug 47)CCS, (SCAPIN 1762), dated 12 August 1947, subject: Osaka-Colombo Radiotelegraph Circuit, as amended.

b. File AG 311.23 (20 Aug 47)CCS, (SCAPIN 1765), dated 20 August 1947, subject: Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service Between Japan and British Commonwealth Areas.

c. File AG 311.23 (11 Sept 47)CCS, (SCAPIN 1765/1), dated 11 September 1947, subject: Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service Between Japan and British Commonwealth Areas.

d. File AG 311.23 (26 Sept 47)CCS, (SCAPIN 1765/2), dated 26 September 1947, subject: Expansion of Radiotelegraph Service Between Japan and British Commonwealth Areas, via Osaka-Colombo Circuit.

2. Expansion of radiotelegraph service via the Osaka-Colombo circuit to include private, press and government messages between Japan and the British Commonwealth areas listed in Inclosures 1 and 2, is authorized, effective 15 October 1947.

3. Message classifications authorized between Japan and the British Commonwealth areas listed in Inclosures 1 and 2 of this memorandum are:

Urgent NLT/DLT

Full Rate Urgent Press

CDE Ordinary Press

LC Government

4. Rates per word, terminal charges applicable and division of revenue between the Japanese Ministry of Communications and the British carrier for full rate, press and government messages authorized in this memorandum are shown in Inclosures 1 and 2. Rates for CDE, LC, NLT/DLT classifications are proportionate and will be 60%, 50% and 33-1/3% respectively of the charges per full rate word. The total charges oh each message will be rounded out to the nearest three pence (3d) or multiple thereof.

5. All other provisions of the reference memoranda 1a, 1b, 1c and 1d pertaining to the communications services between Japan and British Commonwealth areas will apply equally to the expansion of service between Japan and British Commonwealth areas listed in Inclosure 1 of this memorandum.

6. Direct communication within the scope of this memorandum is authorized between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, the General Accounting Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers or the British Transfer Agent and the Ministry of Communications.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
