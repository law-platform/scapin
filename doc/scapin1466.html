APO 500

14 January 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Sale of Japanese Merchant Vessels

1. Reference is C.L.O. letter No. 6649 (CM), dated 14 December 1946, subject: Sale of Japanese Merchant Vessels, and inclosures thereto, requesting permission to transfer title of twenty-four (24) vessels.

2. Permission is granted to the sale and transfer of title of subject vessels providing the money transfer is in accordance with Emergency Financial Measures and Amendments of the Imperial Japanese Government.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
for /t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1436)

ESS/IN JZR/GEM/ms

7 January 1947

1. Reference is C.L.O. letter No. 6649 (CM), dated 14 December 1948, subject: Sale of Japanese Merchant Vessels, and inclosures thereto, which requests permission to sell and transfer titles of twenty-four (24) vessels as listed in the inclosure. Reference is also AG 530 (9 Nov 45) GD which requires approval from Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for any transfer of ship's title.

2. The inclosures to the above application go into detail regarding each transfer and state that neither the transferee or transferor is a restricted concern. A check through Anti-Trust and Cartels proves that above statement is true in all but two (2) cases, and those were evidently restricted after the inclosure forms were prepared.

3. Permission for sale and transfer of title of listed vessels is granted provided the money transfer is in accordance with Emergency Financial Measures and Amendments of the Imperial Japanese Government; for the reason that it is to the best interests of the economic program to get subject vessels into operation.

4. Concurrence: SCAJAP - Capt. J. D. Leach, U.S.N.R., 7 January 1947; ESS/Finance - Lt. J. C. Smith, 20 December 1946; Anti-Trust & Cartels - Col. Byall and Mr. Hoyt, 19 December 1946;

J. Z. R.
