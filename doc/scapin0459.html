GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091.4 (19 Dec 45) PH

(SCAPIN - 459)

19 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Relief of Nationals of Countries with Which the United Nations Have Been at War.

1. Reference is made to AG 091.112 (12 Dec 45)CI, dated 12 December 1945, subject: "Restrictions of Nationals of Countries with which the United Nations have been at war", and to AG 386 (19 Nov 45)ESS/FI, dated 20 November 1945, subject: "Release of German Official Funds".

2. All Nationals of Countries with which the United nations have been at war who are residing in Japan and who by reason of blocked accounts or other financial incapacity are unable to provide themselves with the Japanese official ration of food and daily necessities, will be supplies these items by the Japanese Government.

3. Copies in English of all ordinances, regulations, instructions and other enactments promulgated in connection with this directive will be submitted to this Headquarters without delay.

4. Acknowledgement of receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
