GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (4 Apr 47) GA

(SCAPIN - 1598)

APO 500

4 April 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Italian Nationals.

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file and subject as above (SCAPIN 1534) dated 19 February 1947.

b. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file and subject as above (SCAPIN 1561) dated 8 March 1947.

2. The nominal rolls of Italian nationals to be repatriated in accordance with above listed references are amended as follows:

TO BE DELETED

Bocca, Marino Sugamo Prison

Scrazzola, Virginio) Don Bosco Shudion 21 Minami

Alessio, Stefano) Tanakamachi, Itabashiku, Tokyo

TO BE ADDED

Bealessio, Stefano) Don Bosco Shudion 21 Minami

Cecchetti, Albano) Tanakamachi, Itabachiku, Tokyo

Scarano, Francesco Kobe

Abbate, Francesco Kobe

3. Latest information indicates the SESTRIERE will arrive Japan on or about 2 April 1947.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
