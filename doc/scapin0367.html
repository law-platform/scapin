GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 561.4 (29 Nov 45) ESS/IN

(SCAPIN - 367)

APO 500

29 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Completion of Japanese Naval Vessels Under Construction

1. Reference is made to letter N. D. No. 268 of 2 November 1945 to Rear Admiral D. B. Reary, U.S.N., Commander, Japanese Repatriation Group, from the Liaison Committee (Tokyo) for the Imperial Japanese Army and Navy requesting permission to complete certain naval vessels for repatriation use.

2. Permission is granted to complete in demilitarized status for use in repatriation service the following naval vessels:

Type of Ship Name of Ship

Escort Urumi

Escort Murotsu

Escort CD 58

Escort CD 62

Escort CD 78

Escort CD 97

Escort CD 116

Escort CD 142

Escort CD 105

Escort CD 107

Mine Laver Awashima

3. An acknowledgement of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General
