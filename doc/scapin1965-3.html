GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 602 (17 Nov 48) CPC/FP

(SCAPIN - 1965/3)

APO 500

2 August 1949

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Property in Japan of Companies with Head Offices in Areas Formerly Occupied by Japan.

1. Reference is made to:

a. File AG 602 (17 Nov 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1965/1, 8 March 1949, subject, "Property in Japan of Companies with Head Offices Abroad," from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers

b. L0 1527/49 (AD/CU), 25 May 1949, subject, "Reissuance of the Certificates for Capital Stocks Issued by the Domestic Company as Forfeited Overseas by Japanese Repatriates."

2. To implement reference la concerning the liquidation of securities included in the property in Japan of companies with head offices in areas formerly occupied by Japan falling under paragraph 1d of SCAPIN 45 and paragraph 2, Article 2 of Japanese Government Ordinance 88, the issuing domestic Japanese company, or the Japanese Government or subdivision thereof which issued these securities is directed to transfer registration and issue new stock or bond certificates to the branch in Japan and cancel the certificates now located abroad or registered in the name of the head office outside of present day Japan.

3. Companies referred to herein are exclusive of those in process of liquidation under Ordinance No. 74 (Closed Institutions Ordinance).

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
