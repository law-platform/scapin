MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Change of Vessels for Antarctic Whaling Operations.

1. Reference memorandum from Imperial Japanese Government to General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, C.L.O. No 5991 (OM), 13 November 1946.

2. Authority for the substitution of the while killer boat Fumi Maru No. 6 for the Seki Maru as requested in reference memorandum is granted.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
