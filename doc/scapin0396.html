GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 383.7 (6 Dec 45) CIS

(SCAPIN - 396)

6 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Japanese Personnel.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will apprehend and deliver to the authorities of the Omori Prison Camp for incarceration not later than 18 o'clock midnight, 16 December 1945, the following individuals:

a. GODO, Takuo, retired Admiral

b. KIDO, Koichi, Marquis

c. KONOYE, Fumimaro, Prince

d. ODATE, Shigeo, retired Vice-Admiral

e. OGATA, Taketora, former Cabinet minister

f. OKOCHI, Masatoshi, Viscount

g. OSHIMA, Hiroshi, ex-ambassador to Germany

h. SAKAI, Tadamasa, Count

i. SUMA, Yakichiro, ex-ambassador to Italy.

2. In the event that the Japanese Government is unable to deliver any of these individuals by the appointed time, it will make a report in detail giving the present location of the individuals not arrested, the reason for failure to arrest, and the date by which delivery can be expected.

3. If SUMA, Yakichiro, has not returned to Japan by 12 o'clock midnight, 16 December 1945, he will be apprehended and delivered for incarceration upon his arrival back in Japan.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
