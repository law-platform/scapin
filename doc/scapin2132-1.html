17 July 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Payment of All Expenses Involved in the Return of Remains of Japanese Ex-military Personnel to Japan from the Ryukyu Islands.

1. Memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, file AG 293 (7 Dec 49)GA, SCAPIN 2132, 30 November 1950, subject: Payment of All Expenses Involved In the Return of Remains of Japanese Ex-Military Personnel to Japan from the Ryukyu Islands, is amended as follows:

a. So much of line 2 of paragraph 2 reading. "Bank of Tokyo or its branches," is amended to read "National City Bank, Tokyo Branch."

b. So much of line 3 of paragraph 2 reading, "Ryukyus Counterpart Fund," is amended to read "Ryukyus Custody Account."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K.B.BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
