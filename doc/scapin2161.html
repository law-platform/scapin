GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 684 (20 Jul 51) GC-TNG

(SCAPIN-2161)

APO 500

20 July 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Warning Notices of Practice Firing or Bombing

1. Reference is made to the following Memorandums for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. AG684 (22 Mar 46) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 833/1, subject: Aerial Gunnery Ranges, 18 July 1949.

b. AG 684(22 Mar 46) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 833/2, subject: Kyushu Aerial Gunnery Range, 4 Aug 1949.

c. AG684(7 Apr 46) GC, SCAPIN 937-A, subject: Anti Aircraft Artillery Range, 7 Apr 1946.

d. AG684(10 May 47)GC, SCAPIN 1662, subject: Antiaircraft Artillery Range,10 May 1947.

e. AG353.1(15 Nov 47)GC-TNG, SCAPIN 1820, subject: Onohara Shima Bombing and Ground Gunnery Range, 15 Nov 47.

f. AG 684 (26 Jul 46) GC, SCAPIN 1839-A, subject: Aerial Gunnery Range, 26 Jul 1946.

g. AG684(10 Jun 48) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 1907, subject: Misawa Air to Air Gunnery Range, 10 Jun 1948.

h. AG684 (10 Jun 48) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 1907/1, subject: Northern Honshu Air to Air Range, 1 Sep 1948.

i. AG684 (10 Jun 48) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 1907/2, subject: Northern Honshu Air to Air Range, 16 Jul 1949.

j. AG684 (13 Oct 48) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 1945, subject: Air-to-Air Gunnery Range, 29 Nov 1948.

k. AG684 (16 Jun 48) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 1952, subject: Himoko-Jima Air Bombing and Gunnery Range, 28 Dec 1948.

l. AG684 (30 Jun 48) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 1974, subject: Mito Air to Ground Gunnery and Bombing Range, 23 Feb 1949.

m. AG684 (30 Nov 48) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 1976, subject: Misawa Bombing and Gunnery Range, 1 Mar 1949.

n. AG684 (11 Oct 48) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 1989, subject: Yonago Aerial Gunnery Range, 4 Apr 1949.

o. AG684 (18 Dec 48) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 1992, subject: Tori Shima Bombing and Gunnery Range, 12 Apr 1949.

p. AG684 (20 June 47) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 3987 A, subject: Antiaircraft Artillery Range, 20 Jun 1947.

q. AG684 (2 May 49) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 6818-A, subject: Ashiya Air to Ground Gunnery Range, 29 Aug 49.

r. AG684 (4 Jan 51) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 7396-A, subject: Misawa Antiaircraft Artillery Range, 19 Feb 51.

s. AG684 (19 Feb 51) GC-TNG, SCAPIN 7396-A/1, subject: Misawa Antiaircraft Artillery Range, 25 Apr 51.

2. Warning information concerning the use of ranges will be forwarded through the Government Section, GHQ, SCAP to the Japanese Government for dissemination to the local civil authorities in the areas concerned.

3. This directive supersedes the instructions concerning the channels to be observed in the forwarding of such warning information contained in the referenced directives.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K. B. Bush
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General.
