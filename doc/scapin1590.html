AG 000.5 (31 Mar 47) LS-R

APO 500

31 March 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Apprehension of Suspected War Criminals.

The following named Japanese will be apprehended as Suspected War Criminals and delivered to the Commanding General, EIGHTH Army, at Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detertiorn Camp), at the earliest possible date.

NAME

RANK OR TITLE

PERTINENT DATA

Ship Master of "DAI NICHI MARU" (5800 ton cargo vessel), 1935 -43.

Probable home address: Miyazaki-ken, Koyu-gun, Nyuta-mura, No. 4532.

HIRAOKA, Kumeichi

Vice Adm

Cmdr, 9th Naval Base, Penang, Malaya.

Probable home address: Tokyo-to, Meguro-ku, Nakane-cho, No. 1792.

HOKAZONO, Ikuo

Sgt/ Maj

Kowloon District Gendarmerie HQ, Sai Kung, New Territories, (formerly at Austin Road, thereafter at Kowloon), May 44 - Feb 46.

ISHII, Yoshiho

Maj Gen

OC, 32 Division, Morotai, Burneo.
