GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 201- Yoneichi, Uzami (3 Nov 45) ESS-FI

(SCAPIN - 231)

3 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Transfer of Funds by Uzami Yoneichi

1. Information submitted to this Headquarters indicates that on or about 15 September 1945, the account of Uzami Yoneichi, with the Fukuoka Branch of the Imperial Bank, was credited with the sum of ¥1,818, 414.80.

2. In the event that such transfer was effected by debiting the account of a Korean banking institution, you will take immediate action to cancel the credit and to reverse the entries covering such transaction. In the event the credit was established in any other manner, you will immediately block the account and not permit any transfers or withdrawals therefrom without the approval of this Headquarters.

3. You will furnish to this Headquarters within ten days a statement of the action taken by you and complete details, supported by bank account records and statements of all transactions involving the account of Uzami Yoneichi with the Fukuoka Branch of the Imperial Bank which were effected subsequent to the establishment of the credit described in paragraph 1 of this memorandum.

4. An acknowledgement of the receipt of this memorandum is directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H. W. Allen
/t/ H. W. ALLEN,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 231)

29 October 1945

CFT/OJM/kns

1. Radio TFYNG 11 FSRG from CG USAFIK, 27 October 1945, requested necessary action reference Uzami Yoneichi and recovery for Korean Commodity Company of 1,818,414 yen, 80 sen. Uzami Yoneichi collected a bill due Korean Commodity Company, formerly Chosen Food Distribution Company, for rice sold Japanese Navy. A check for 1, 818, 414 yen, 80 sen drawn on his account with Chosen Commercial Bank was given to Korean Commodity Company. The check was rejected by the bank as Yoneichi had transferred his funds to Teikoku Bank, Fukuoka Branch, Japan.

2. Action followed in this case is similar to that of previous cases involving transfer of funds from Korea to Japan during August and. September 1945. If further investigation reveals intent to defraud the Korean Commodity Company, the Japanese Government may be directed to take punitive action.

3. A memorandum has been sent to CIC through C/S and G-2, requesting an investigation of Uzami Yoneichi.

C. F. T.
