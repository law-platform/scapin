GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 014.33 (1 Mar 47) GA

(SCAPIN - 1551)

APO 500

1 March 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office
SUBJECT :Repatriation of Ryukyuan Prisoners.

1. Reference is made to:

a. Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, file AG 014.31 (14 Feb 47)GA, (SCAPIN 1527), dated 14 February 1947, subject: "Repatriation of Non-Japanese".

b. CLO No. 940(RF) dated 14 February 1947, subject as above.

2. The Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers has no objection to repatriation of the Ryukyuan prisoners listed in reference 1b above.

3. Repatriation of Ryukyuan prisoners will be accomplished in accordance with instructions contained in reference 1a above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
