MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 891)

ESS/lN GEM/rhw

19 April 1946

1. Memorandum to IJG (SCAPIN-30) dated 15 September 1945, APO 500, subject: "Repair of Inoperative Ships," directed the IJG to complete construction of non-combat merchant ships that were under construction at the time of the surrender (TAB "A").

2. The above mentioned directive does not contain an official list of vessels that were authorized for completion. The purpose of this directive to the IJG and letter of Command Instructions to the Eighth Army is to provide them with an authentic list of authorized vessels, including merchant ships and demilitarized naval vessels, and to provide a check on unauthorized construction.

3. Concurrence: G-4 (Comdr. Ryan).

4. This is an administrative matter and requires routine surveillance of the shipyards.

J. A. O'H.
