30 October 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Application for Use of Facilities of Miike Gosei Kogyo Co. for Production of Nitrogen Fertilizer

1. Reference is made to the following memoranda:

a. Memorandum from Imperial Japanese Government to The Supreme Commander For The Allied Powers, C. L. O. No. 5219 (ECI), dated 8 October 1946, subject: Request for Permission to Use Facilities of Miike Gosei Kogyo Co. to Expedite Production of Ammonium Sulfate.

b. Memorandum for Imperial Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander For The Allied Powers, AG 464.6 (17 May 46) ESS/IN, SCAPIN-962, subject: Production, Distribution and use of Fertilizer.

2. a. The Miike Gosei Kogyo Co. is authorized to operate the following units of their Omuta plant, Fukuoka Prefecture, for production and purification of gaseous raw materials to be used in ammonium sulfate fertilizer synthesis by the Omuta Works of the Toyo Koatsu Kogyo Co.:

(1) Coke Oven - 2 batteries, 50 chambers

(2) By product plant - 1

(3) Water Gas Generator - 5

(4) Methane Cracking Plant - 2 Towers

(5) Gas Producer - 5

(6) Gas Holder (10, 000 Cu. Meter) - 5

(7) Inorganic Sulfur Purifier - 8 Towers

(8) Organic Sulfur Purifier - 8 Towers (to be moved to Toyo Koatsu Co., Omuta Plant, for use as CO converter)

(9) Steam Power Plant - 1

(10) Electric Power Plant - 1

(11) Machine Shop - 1

(12) Water Pumping Station - 2

b. The above portion of the Miike Gosei Kogyo Co., Omuta Plant, Fukuoka Prefecture, is placed on the approved list of plants for production of nitrogenous fertilizers, as set forth in memorandum, paragraph 1b, above.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ R. G. Hersey
for /t/ JOHN B. COOLEY,
Colonel, A.G.D.,
Adjutant General.

MEMO FOR RECORD : (SCAPIN - 1301)

EFM/JZR/GBB/me

17 October 1946

1. C.L.O. No. 5219 (ECI), subject: Request for Permission to use facilities of Miike Gosei Kogyo Co., to Expedite Production of Ammonium Sulfate, dated 8 Oct 46, requests permission to use a portion of the Omuta plant of Miike Gosei Kogyo Co. to generate gaseous raw materials needed for ammonium sulfate fertilizer synthesis in the Omuta plant of the Toyo Koatsu Kogyo Co.

2. a. The Miike Kogyo Co., Tokyo Koatsu Kogyo Co. and Mitsui Kagaku Kogyo Co. have adjoining plants in Omuta, Fukuoka Prefecture. The three interdependent plants have operated as a unit for a period of years. The Miike plant was a former synthetic oil producer and is to be listed for reparations; the Toyo plant now produces ammonium sulfate fertilizer; and the Mitsui plant produces dyestuffs. While the Miike plant formerly produced synthetic oil, its coke ovens and purification apparatus supply the Toyo Koatsu plant with necessary hydrogen and nitrogen gas, basic raw materials for ammonium sulfate fertilizer. Both the Miike and Mitsui plants produce coke oven gas but Mitsui production is small (12% of Miike) and only Miike has the necessary methane cracking plant and sulfur removing apparatus to produce acceptable nitrogen and hydrogen gas. C.L.O. letter 5219 (ECI) resulted from an informal order by GHQ, SCAP to IJG in which the Japanese were asked to initiate a request for continued use of the necessary parts of the Miike plant. Since that plant is scheduled to be listed for reparations as a former synthetic oil plant, the special authorization will be necessary. The Toyo Koatsu plant is an approved fertilizer plant (SCAPIN 962) and cannot produce ammonium sulfate without the gas production and purification facilities of the Miike plant. The Koatsu plant is presently using nitrogen and hydrogen generated at Miike for the production of fertilizer.

b. Similar authorizations for use of special portions of former synthetic fuel depots have already been granted to IJG as listed below:

(1) Yokkaichi Plant of Second Naval Fuel Depot Mie Pref - SCAPIN 1031

(2) Takikawa Plant of Nihon Jinzo Sekiyu K.K, Hokkaido Pref - SCAPIN 1118

(3) Ube Works of Sanyo Kagaku Kogyo, Yamaguchi Pref - SCAPIN 2327 A

3. Action taken:

a. The IJG is authorized by attached memorandum to continue operation of specified portions of the gas generating and purifying equipment of the Miike Kogyo Co., Omuta Plant, Fukuoka Prefecture. The portion of the plant concerned is also added to the list of approved fertilizer plants set forth in SCAPIN 962, an action required to assure supply of necessary materials to the subject facilities, under existing SCAP directives.

4. No concurrence is required.

5. This is an administrative matter and no military surveillance is required.

J. Z. R.
