AG 410.2 (27 Feb 48) CPC/CD

(SCAPIN - 1865)

APO 500

27 February 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Release of Precious Metals from Japanese Government Stocks for Domestic Consumption and for Use in Items Approved for Export.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. C.L.O. No. 5818 (LO 2364-FI/FF), 26 July 1947, subject, "Application for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in Third Quarter of 1947."

b. C.L.O. No. 9751 (LO 4808-FI/2E), 23 December 1947, subject, "Eighth Supplemental Request for Approval of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the First Quarter of 1948."

c. C.L.O. No. 9752 (LO 4809-FI/2E), 23 December 1947, subject, "Seventh Supplemental Request for Approval of Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the First Quarter of 1948."

2. 3,211 grams gold and 1,376 grams silver from stocks held by the Japanese Government will be released to the Ishifuku Kinzoku Kogyo K.K., for processing into anodes. These anodes will then be used in the. manufacture of fountain pens to be exported by Platinum Sangyo K.K., under Export Authority Order No. 2313.

3. The release of 152,990 grams silver and 309 grams platinum, from stocks held by the Japanese Government, to the companies listed, for the manufacture and repair of equipment to be used in water supply systems in Japanese cities, is approved. The Tanaka Kikinzoku Kogyo K.K., will initially process the silver into plate, wire and tubes, and perform initial processing of the platinum hereby approved for use in the manufacture of 114 chlorinator-type water sterilizers and in the repair of 12 installed sterilizers.

Manufacturer of End Product Precious Metal Contained in Processed Forms Percentage and Use in Japanese Economy

Isomura Sangyo K.K. 93,000 grams silver 100 Public water systems

Suido Kiko K.K. 59,990 grams silver 100 Public water systems

Isomura Sangyo K.K. 309 grams platinum 100 Public water systems

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
