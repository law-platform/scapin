GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 000.77 (26 MAY 48) CCS

(SCAPIN1215/2)

APO500

26 May 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Authorization for Radiotelegraph Newscast Service from Japan.

1. Reference are:

a. Memorandum for Japanese Government, file AG000.77 (18 Sep 46) CCS, SCAPIN 1215, dated 18 September 1946, subject same as above, as amended.

b. Letter from Director of Foreign Telegraph and Telephone Section, Ministry of Communications, file No. 0-38, dated 26 April 1948.

2. The Ministry of Communications is hereby authorized to provide radiotelegraph newscast transmission facilities in accordance with the following provisions:

a. The frequency 18690 kc, now authorized for Program Transmission Service, is hereby authorized on a temporary basis, for the radio-telegraph newscast service

b. authorized herein, using A1 type emission with a maximum power output of 5 kw. An additional frequency for radiotelegraph newscast service will be authorized by direct correspondence if and when clearance is obtained.

c. This authorization is effective for a period of not to exceed thirty (30) days from the date the service authorized herein is first provided, and not to exceed ten (10) hours daily between 2200 GNT and 0800 GNT.

3. Paragraph la of SCAPIN 1215 is rescinded and the following substitutes therefore:

"a. Transmissions on frequencies 14595 and 18570 kc with maximum power output of 5kw on each, using A1 type emission with transmissions beamed in the direction of San Francisco and South China Sea area."

4. Direct Communication between the Civil Communications Section, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, and the Ministry of Communications, regarding matters within the scope of this memorandum, is authorized.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

J.P. BRADSHAW
for GEORGE R. CONNON
Colonel, A. G. D.
Asst. Adjutant General

Received: 28 May 10.00 a.m.

Shukan: 2P

Copy: Chokan
