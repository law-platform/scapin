GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 063.3 (11 Dec 45) GD

(SCAPIN-423)

11 December 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :THE IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Report on Coal Production.

1. Reference is made to memorandum from this headquarters to the Imperial Japanese Government, AG 463. 3 (6 Dec 45) GD, subject, “Production of Coal.”

2. You are directed to render reports on coal production to this headquarters covering each ten day period of the month. The reports will include the following information:

a. Actual production for the ten day period.

b. Stockpiles on the tenth day.

c. Daily average for the ten day period of the number of persons actually engaged in coal mining activity. In addition the net gain or loss in laborers for the ten day period will be shown.

3. Reports are to reach this headquarters not later than noon on the fifth day following the close of each period.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General
