GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 602 (12 Dec 51) CPC/OD

(SCAPIN - 2189)

APO 500

12 December 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Liquidation of Property in Japan Belonging to Branches of Companies with Head Offices Outside Japan.

1. Reference is made to the following memoranda for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. File AG 602 (17 Nov 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1965, 18 January 1949, subject, “Liquidation of Property in Japan of Branches of Companies with Head Offices Outside Japan”

b. File AG 602 (17 Nov 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1965/1, 8 March 1949, subject, “Property in Japan of Companies with Head Offices Abroad”

c. File AG 602 (17 Nov 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1965/2, 1 June 1949, subject, “Disposition of Property in Japan of Companies or Persons with Head Offices in Areas Formerly Occupied by the Japanese”

d. File AG 602 (17 Nov 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1965/3, 2 August 1949, subject, “Property in Japan of Companies with Head Offices in Areas Formerly Occupied by Japan”

e. File AG 602 (17 Nov 48) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 1965/4, 8 June 1950, subject, “Disposition of Property in Japan of Companies or Persons with Head Offices in Areas Formerly Occupied by the Japanese”

f. File AG 130 (19 Jul 49) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 2030, 19 July 1949, subject, “Foreign Creditors’ Yen Deposit Account”

g. File AG 260 (17 Mar 49) CPC/FP, SCAPIN 2030/1, 4 August 1949, subject, “Transfer of Foreign Seamen Beneficiary Funds to the Foreign Creditors’ Yen Deposit Account.”

2. The Japanese Government is informed that effective 15 December 1951 it will assume execution of the program for liquidation of property in Japan of branches with head offices outside Japan.

3. Liquidation of the remaining companies which have not completed liquidation will be effected in accordance with the policy and procedure outlined in previous directives from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, reference paragraphs 1a through 1e above. Supervisory control of the liquidation program will, however, be retained by the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers until coming into force of the Peace Treaty.

4. It is directed that Japanese Government Ordinances which implement the policy and procedure included in reference paragraphs 1a through 1g above be revised and amended to incorporate the changes set forth in paragraphs 2 and 3 above. Changes in such ordinances necessitated by this directive shall be presented to Civil Property Custodian, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers for review prior to 15 December 1951.

5. It is further directed that the Japanese Government authorize withdrawals of yen and securities which are on deposit in the Foreign Creditors’ Yen and Security Deposit Account, Bank of Japan, only on demand of the diplomatic representatives in Japan of the governments of the countries of which the individual beneficiaries of this account are nationals. This demand shall be supported by a certified claim and power of attorney from the individual beneficiary. Such funds and securities were deposited in accordance with reference If and 1g above. A recommended procedure for making these withdrawals shall be presented to Civil Property Custodian, General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers by 15 December 1951 for review and transmission to the missions concerned.

6. Until the coming into force of the Peace Treaty monthly reports of additional deposits made in the Foreign Creditors’ Yen and Security Deposit Account, Bank of Japan, as a result of liquidations completed after 15 December 1951, and from other sources as set forth in references If and 1g above shall continue to be filed with the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers.

7. Since the functions pertaining to operation, control, management and ownership of property in Japan belonging to subject branch offices which were required by the directives listed in Annex A from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers have been completed, such SCAPINs are hereby rescinded.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

C.C.B. WARDEN
Colonel, AGC
Adjutant General
