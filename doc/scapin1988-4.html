APO 500

29 October 1951

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Dried Skim Milk for School Lunch and Nursery Feeding Programs

1. Reference SCAPIN 1988/3, 12 September 1951, subject as above.

2. Total funds to be made available to the Foodstuff Control Special Account (Food Management Special Account) for the School Lunch and Nursery Feeding Programs from the Counterpart Fund shall not exceed ¥1,004,527,805.00 during Japanese Fiscal Year 1951, including the balance of ¥504,527,805.00 carried over from Japanese Fiscal Year 1950, and the ¥22,472,195.00 released 12 September 1951.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ C. C. B. Warden
/t/ C. C. B. WARDEN,
Colonel, A.G.C.,
Adjutant General
