APO 500

20 March 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :The Imperial Japanese Government
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo
SUBJECT :Addition of Subsidiaries of Fuji Industrial Company on Schedule of Restricted Concerns

1. Reference is made to the following Memoranda for the Imperial Japanese Government from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers:

a. AG 319.1 (22 Oct 45) ESS/IE, dated 25 October 1945, subject: "Reports to be Made by Certain Business Firms."

b. AG 004 (31 Oct 45) ESS, dated 31 October 1945, subject: "Sale or Transfer of Securities of Certain Business Firms."

c. AG 004 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC, dated 8 December 1945, subject: "Establishment of a Schedule of Restricted Concerns."

d. AG 300.8 (8 Dec 45) ESS/AC, dated 8 December 1945, subject: "Regulations Affecting Restricted Concerns."

e. AG 319.1 (19 Jan 46) ESS/AC, dated 19 January 1943, subject: "Reports to be Made by Certain Business Firms."

f. AG 150 (23 Feb 46) ESS/AC, dated 23 February 1946, subject: "War Damage and Government Indemnity Claim's of Companies on the Schedule of Restricted Concerns."

2. The subsidiaries of Fuji Industrial Company, listed in paragraph 4, below, are hereby added to the Schedule of Restricted Concerns referred to in paragraph 1c, above.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government will take immediate action to assure that the concerns named in paragraph 4, below, shall comply with the provisions of and make reports required by reference Memoranda listed in paragraph 1, above. Wherever reference is made in Inclosure 1 and Inclosure 2 to Memorandum referred to in paragraph la, above, to the year 1944, it shall be amended to read 1945.

4. Subsidiaries of Fuji Industrial Company:

Daiwa Industrial Co., Ltd. Taisei Gauge Co., Ltd.

Fuji Marine Products Co., Ltd. Taiyo Industrial Co., Ltd.

Harada Manufacturing Co., Ltd. Taiyo Aircraft Industry Co., Ltd.

Hojozan Industrial Co., Ltd. Tanaka Aeronautical Instrument Co., Ltd.

Ibaragi Steel Manufacturing Co., Ltd. Tanaka Spoke Co., Ltd.
