GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 311.1 (6 Oct 47) CCS

(SCAPIN-1792)

APO 500

6 October 1947

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :International Mail Service

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, file AG 311.1 (26 Aug 47) CCS, (SCAPIN 1768), dated 26 August 1947, subject: International Mail Service.

2. The reference memorandum is hereby amended, effective 10 October 1947, by adding the following new category to paragraph 5:

“h. Bibles and all other sacred writings of all religious faiths and sects; and tracts, other pamphlets, books, journals and other publications of which at least 50 percent of the content is devoted to matters generally recognized as religious - (to and from Japan).”

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

R.M. LEVY,
Colonel, AGD,
Adjutant General.
