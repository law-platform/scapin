GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG132 (20 Nov 45) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-319)

APO 500

20 November 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Release of Impounded Funds of the Reichsdeutsche Gemeinschaft.

1. Reference is made to CLO No.534(5), dated 10 November 1945, subject ”AG 091.112(13 Sep 45) MG,” with attached application for release of funds of Reichsdeutsche Gemainshaft.

2. It is directed that application be ▲ the funds be held pending disposition instructions from this Headquarters.

3. An acknowledgment of the receipt of this memorandum is to directed.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ H.W. Allen
/t/H. W. Allen,
Colonel, A. G. D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
