GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 095 (21 Jan 46) ESS/FI

(SCAPIN-633)

APO 500

21 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Eiraku Estate Company.

1. It is directed that the Imperial Japanese Government take such action as may be necessary to prevent and prohibit any transaction by the Eiraku Estate Company (Eiraku Toehi K. K.) in any of its property except on the written permission of the minister of Finance.

2. No such permission will be given without the prior approval of this Headquaters.

3. A report will be submitted to this Headquarters showing action taken pursuant to this memorandum, including a financial statement of the Company, a list of all property hordings, and a list of all stockholders, officers and directors.

5. Direct communication betweeen the Ministry of Finance and interested staff sections of this Headquarters is authorilzed to implement this memorandum.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

H.W.Allen
Colonel,A.G.D.
Asst Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN-633)

WFM/THH/syk

21 January 1946

1. Memorandum to Imperial Japanese Government blocking all transactions of the Eiraku Estate Company (Eiraku Tochi K.K.) except on the authority issued by the Minister of Finance, and approved by this Headquarters.

2. Just prior to closure by order of this Headquarters, the Bank of Taiwan transferred the bank premises of its Yokohama branch to the Eiraku Estate Company--an organization owned and controlled by employees of the Bank of Taiwan. Examination leading to probable recovery of the property is desired.

3. No Concurrences necessary.

4. References: Appendix "A" of Memorandum for the Imperial Japanese Government, this Headquarters, file AC 130 (22 Sep 45) ESS, dated 22 September 1945, subject; "Control of Financial Transactions."

C. F. T.
