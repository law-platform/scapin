(SCAPIN - 1920/1)

APO 500

20 April 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :Japanese Government
SUBJECT :Petroleum Storage Tank Facilities

1. Reference is memorandum for the Japanese Government from General Headquarters, Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, AG 678 (30 Nov 46) ESS/IND, SCAPIN 1920, subject as above, 9 July 1948.

2. Paragraph 1 of reference memorandum is modified to read as follows.

"The Japanese Government is hereby authorized to approve or disapprove all applications for construction, major repair, rehabilitation, dismantling, movement or scrapping of any petroleum storage tanks and their accessory equipment without prior approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers except those pertaining to facilities formerly owned by the Japanese Army or Navy."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ K. B. Bush
/t/ K. B. BUSH
Brigadier General, USA,
Adjutant General

MEMO FOR RECORD: (SCAPIN - 1920/1)

HBC/WT/TMc/lh

3 April 50

1. Reference is memorandum for the Japanese Government from GHQ, SCAP, AG 678 (30 Nov 46) ESS/IND, SCAPIN, 1920/1, subject: Petroleum Storage Tank Facilities, 20 Apr 50.

2. Reference memorandum modifies para 1 of SCAPIN 1920 allowing JG to approve or disapprove applications for alteration of petroleum storage tank facilities regardless of capacity or size without prior approval of the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers except those pertaining to facilities formerly owned by the Japanese Army or Navy.

3. Action taken is based on the following:

a. Controls exerted under previous instructions are no longer considered necessary.

b. This action is in accord with established policy of returning responsibility in such matters to the JG.

c. Check note No. 2 from G-4 to ESS/IND thru CPC and check note No. 3 from CPC to ESS/IND, subject: Proposed Memo to IG Regarding Petroleum Storage Facilities, dated 23 February 1950 and 2 March 1950 respectively, indicate concurrence in the policy of placing responsibility for decisions in these matters with the IG. Although proposed memo has been modified for administrative reasons, the principle remains unchanged; reports requested by G-4 will be obtained by memo.

4. Routine surveillance by CAS is required.

5. Concurrences: G-4 (Pet) see paragraph 3c.

CPC " " "

F. Ito 26-8504
