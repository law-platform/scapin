GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 350 (2 May 46) CIE

(SCAPIN - 917)

APO 500

2 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Reinstatement of TASAKA, Seiki, as Instructor at Niigata First Normal School and Dismissal of KATO, Kakuryo, Principal.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government, upon receipt of this Memorandum, will:

a. Reinstate TASAKA, Seiki to his former position at Niigata First Normal School, Niigata Prefecture.

b. Dismiss KATO, Kakuryo from the position of Principal of the Niigata First Normal School and will prohibit his future employment by all Japanese educational institutions or agencies either public or private.

2. While reserving a position vacancy for a member of the Japanese Armed Forces, Mr. KATO, Kakuryo did not initiate action to reinstate Mr. TASAKA, Seiki who had been dismissed in 1942 because of anti-militaristic teachings. This is in direct violation of Par. 1b(2), Par. 1b(3), and Par. 3, of SCAP Memorandum AG 350(22 Oct 45)CIE, "Administration of the Educational System of Japan", to the Imperial Japanese Government.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH,
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General.
