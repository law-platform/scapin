APO 500

18 May 1948

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Release of Precious Metals to be Used in the Production of Items Licensed for Export.

1. Reference is made to the following:

a. C.L.C.O. No. 659 (LO 1045-FI/2E) 10 March 1948, subject, "Thirteenth Supplementary Application for Domestic Consumption of Precious Metals in the Second Quarter of 1948;"

b. C.L.C.O. No. 698 (C.P.B. No. 215) 12 March 1948, subject, "Sixteenth Supplementary Application for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in Second Quarter of 1948;"

c. C.L.C.O. No. 699 (C.P.B. No. 216) 12 March 1948, subject, "Seventeenth Supplementary Application for Permission to Use Precious Metals for Domestic Consumption in Second Quarter of 1948."

2. Applications for permission to use precious metals, as requested by reference 1a above, are approved in the amounts and by the companies stated below. The metals will be used in the manufacture of products licensed for export:

a. To the Yamanaka Shoji K.K.: 78,745 grams gold dust and 9,375 grams silver dust from the pool of commercial products under custody of the Ministry of Finance. Released dust is to be used in manufacturing products produced for export under Export Authority Order Numbers EX 5739 and EX 5205.

b. To Makino Brothers and Co.: 25,500 grams silver, to be processed into silver plate by Katsumi Yasukura & Sadao Tsuji, and used in manufacturing products produced for export under Export Authority Order Number EX 5753. This silver will be obtained from stocks held by the Japanese Government.

3, Application for permission to use liquid gold, as requested by reference 1b above, is approved, in the amounts and by the companies stated. Liquid gold in the pooled reserve under custody of the Ministry of Finance will be released.

The released liquid will be used in the manufacture of articles licensed for export by the stated Export Authority Order Numbers.
