9 May 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Transfer of War Criminals.

The following named Japanese, presently held as War Criminals by the XXIV Corps, are being transferred from Korea to the custody of the Commanding General, EIGHTH Army, and will be placed in Sugamo Prison (Tokyo Detention Camp) upon arrival in Japan.

NAME RANK

NOGUCHI, Yusuru Colonel

OKAZAKI, Kojuro Lt Col

ARIIZUMI, Shigeru Captain

GOTO, Isamu Captain

ISOBE, Takae Captain

TAKADO, Takeo Captain

UCHIDA, Goro Captain

KAWAKURE, Yohachi Lieutenant

MOROTOMI, Hiromasa Lieutenant

SUMI, Tatsuro PO

SAITO, Gihee Sgt/Maj

TAKUMA, Shotaro Sgt

KITAOKA, Iwamatsu Cpl

SONG, Kao Chin

SITO, Risaburo Cpl

USHIHARA, Tatshumi Cpl

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B. M. FITCH
Brigadier General, AGD
Adjutant General
