AG 311.1 (27 Dec 48) CCS

(SCAPIN - 1977/1)

APO 500

3 November 1950

MEMORANDUM FOR :JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
SUBJECT :Use of Japanese Postal Service by Allied Occupation Forces.

1. Reference is made to Memorandum for the Japanese Government, AG 311.1 (27 Dec 48) CCS, SCAPIN 1977, 2 March 1949, subject: same as above.

2. Effective at once, reference Memorandum is amended by changing paragraph 2a to read as follows:

"a. Members of the Allied Occupation Forces are permitted to use the facilities of the Japanese Postal Service, subject to provisions of Japanese laws and regulations governing domestic and international postal services."

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

K. B. BUSH
Brigadier General, USA
Adjutant General
