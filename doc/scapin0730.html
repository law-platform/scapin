GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 464.1 (11 Feb 46) ESS/IE

(SCAPIN-730)

APO 500

11 February 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
Subject :Delivery of 2,000,000 Pounds of Wheat Flour.

1. The Imperial Japanese Government will take the following action with regard to 2,000,000 pounds of wheat flour being made available for Japanese consumption:

a. Take delivery of the 2,000,000 pounds of wheat flour from the Commanding General, Eighth United States Army at a point to be designated by him.

b. Furnish the Commanding General, Eighth United States Army with quantitative receipts for the wheat flour at the delivery point.

c. Store the subject flour so that it is protected against spoilage, pilferage, theft or other hazards and is under proper custody at all times.

d. Notify this Headquarters of the location of storage points, giving the quantity stored at each location.

2. Terms of payment and accounting will be decided later.

3. The Imperial Japanese Government is responsible for the equitable rationing of imported food supplies and will, therefore, ascertain that distribution is made to areas where shortages are most critical. This Headquarters will be furnished with a complete accounting of all imported food supplies to include the distribution of final consumers.

4. Direct communication is hereby authorized between the interested staff sections of this Headquarters and the responsible agencies of the Imperial Japanese Government to implement the objectives of this directive.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

B.M.FICHI
Brigadiar General,AGD,
Adjutant General
