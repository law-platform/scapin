GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
SUPREME COMMANDER FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 680.2 (15 Jan 46) GS

(SCAPIN-604)

APO 500

15 January 1946

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GOVERNMENT.
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Tokyo.
SUBJECT :Application for Permission to Enter South Korea.

1. Reference is made to C.L.O. No. 1204(5.3), dated 19 December 1945; C.L.O. No. 1285(5.1), dated 22 December 1945, and C.L.O. No. 1298(5.1), dated 22 December 1945, Subjects: "Applications for Permission to Enter South Korea".

2. The applications contained in reference memoranda are disapproved.

3. For your future guidance, applications for permission of Japanese nationals to enter South Korea will be disapproved unless this Headquarters has received from the U.S. Military Government in Korea a request for the entry of the particular persons. You will be notified by this Headquarters of such requests. This should obviate the forwarding by you to this Headquarters of applications which you may receive from Japanese nationals desiring permission to enter South Korea.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

HAROLDd FAIR,
IT Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst Adjutant General.
