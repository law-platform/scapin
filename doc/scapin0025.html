OFFICE OF THE SUPREME COMMANDER
FOR THE ALLIED POWERS

AG 091 (13 Sep 45) CS

(SCAPIN - 25)

13 September 1945

MEMORANDUM FOR :IMPERIAL JAPANESE GENERAL HEADQUARTERS
JAPANESE MINISTRY OF WAR
JAPANESE MINISTRY OF NAVY
THROUGH :Central Liaison Office, Yokohama.

1． It is directed that upon the abolition of the Imperial Japanese General Headquarters, pursuant to the Memorandum from the Supreme Commander for the Allied Powers, dated 10 September 1945, the functions charged to the Imperial Japanese General Headquarters will be executed by the respective Ministries concerned.

2． It is further directed that the existing Liaison Commission continue to function, but with respect to the Ministries of War and Navy instead of Imperial Japanese General Headquarters.

3． Acknowledgment of receipt of this directive is desired.

FOR THE SUPREME COMMANDER:

/s/ Harold Fair.
/t/ HAROLD FAIR,
Lt. Colonel, A.G.D.,
Asst. Adjutant General.
